### Collect potato cacti option


#### If the player has completed at least the medium Desert achievements

- **Weird Old Man:** Oh, them. Not got none of them. Got some paper, though. Here, you can have it… before they get it.
- (receives=potato cacti)

##### If the player has already claimed their potato cacti for the week

- **Weird Old Man:** What? Not got none of them. They probably eat them. I'll have more in [X] days.

#### If the player has not completed the medium Desert achievements

- **Weird Old Man:** What? Not got any of them for you. You might be one of them.
