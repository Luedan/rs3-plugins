# Wandering soul

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Wandering soul](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AWandering%20soul)
- **Letter**: `W`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawn

- A wandering soul has appeared on the ritual site!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - A wandering soul has appeared on the ritual site!

### Dismiss

- You dismiss the wandering soul from the ritual site.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You dismiss the wandering soul from the ritual site.
  - (continue)
- The following reward is added to the ritual chest: 1 x [Item]

### Despawn

- The wandering soul abandons the ritual site.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The wandering soul abandons the ritual site.
