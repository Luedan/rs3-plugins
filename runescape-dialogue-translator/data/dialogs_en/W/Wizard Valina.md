## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Wizards' Tower

- **Wizard Valina:** Welcome to the Wizards' Tower, adventurer.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What can I do here?
    - **Wizard Valina:** It was wizards of this tower who discovered the Rune Mysteries - the secret of creating runes out of rune essence. Archmage Sedridor will teleport adventurers to the essence mine. His office is on the second floor.
    - **Wizard Valina:** Recently a wizard called Finix discovered an alternative runecrafting method. Speak to him on the roof if you're interested.
    - **Wizard Valina:** If you'd like to practice combat magic, you might want to attack the spellwisps outside the tower. I believe there's a cluster of them to the west of here.
    - (Condition: If Imp Catcher is not completed:)
      - **Wizard Valina:** Also, I think Wizard Mizgog is looking for an adventurer to help him with something. He's on the first floor if you want to ask him what the matter is.
  - [Choice]: I'm fine, thanks.
    - (end)
