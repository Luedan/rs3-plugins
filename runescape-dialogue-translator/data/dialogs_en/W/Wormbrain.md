## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Port Sarim jail


#### Talking to him

- **Wormbrain:** Whut you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you in for?
    - **Player:** What are you in for?
    - **Wormbrain:** Me not sure. Me pick some stuff up and take it away.
    - **Player:** Well, did the stuff belong to you?
    - **Wormbrain:** Umm...no.
    - **Player:** Well, that would be why then.
    - **Wormbrain:** Oh, right.
  - [Choice]: Sorry, thought this was a zoo.
    - **Player:** Sorry, thought this was a zoo.

#### Attacking him


##### Before Dragon Slayer

- The goblin is already in prison. You have no reason to attack him.

##### After Dragon Slayer

- You have already taken Wormbrain's map piece. There is no use in beating him up further.
