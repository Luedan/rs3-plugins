# Wilhelm

- **Letter**: `W`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Wilhelm:** Hallo.
- Select an option
  - Who are you?
    - **Wilhelm:** I'm Wilhelm, I've travelled from a far off land. It's a land known for its tulips and windmills. We also have a strange liking to the colour orange. To celebrate these past 10 years of prosperity, I have brought with me a delicacy known as stroopwafels. I will stay around for a week handing out boxes of stroopwafels.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What are these stroopwafels?
    - **Wilhelm:** Oh they are so delicious, you just can't stop eating them. A stroopwafel is two thin waffles with a flavoured filling in the middle. Ever since coming to these lands I've made some experiments on the fillings. You should give all types of flavours a taste.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Can I have some stroopwafels?
    - *(If the player has not already been given the box of stroopwafels:)*
      - *(If the player doesn't have space in their backpack:)*
        - **Wilhelm:** You don't have enough room in your inventory for this package of delicious stroopwafels.
      - *(If the player has a free slot in their backpack:)*
        - Wilhelm hands you a box of stroopwafels.
        - **Player:** Thank you.
    - *(If asked again after the player has already been given the box of stroopwafels:)*
      - **Wilhelm:** I have run out of stroopwafels, please come back tomorrow for more.
  - Goodbye
    - **Wilhelm:** Tot ziens.
