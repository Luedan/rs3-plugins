# Wilough

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Gertrude's Cat

- **Player:** Hello youngster!
- **Wilough:** I don't talk to strange old people.
- **Player:** Hey who you calling old?! And strange?!
- **Wilough:** You obviously. Old, strange and stupid.
- **Player:** Whatever.

### Normally

- **Player:** Hello again.
- **Wilough:** You think you're tough do you?
- **Player:** Pardon?
- **Wilough:** I can beat anyone up.
- **Player:** Really?
- The boy begins to jump around with his fists up. You wonder what sort of desperado he'll grow up to be.

### During Zodiac Festival


#### Without the envelope

- **Wilough:** Hey, you're old right?
- **Player:** What? No! I'm not old!
- **Wilough:** You're pretty old...
- **Player:** No!
- **Wilough:** Ugh, mum said I have to write a letter for an old person for this Zodiac Festival thing.
- **Wilough:** Who am I going to give this to now?
- **Player:** How about the Wise Old Man?
- **Wilough:** Oh yeah! I think he's older than the dawn of time!
- Wilough hands you an envelope
- **Wilough:** Thanks for offering to deliver it for me too!
- **Player:** I never offered to...
- **Wilough:** Thanks!
- *(Continues with normal chat.)*

#### With the envelope

- **Wilough:** Hey, why haven't you delivered my letter to the Wise Old Man yet?
- **Player:** Urgh... I'm on it.
- *(Continues with normal chat.)*

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Wilough:** My brother reckons I can't solve this puzzle! I can't have him laughing at me! Do me a favour and solve it for me.
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Wilough has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Wilough:** Sorry but you haven't solved the puzzle yet.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Wilough has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Wilough:** Thanks mister!
