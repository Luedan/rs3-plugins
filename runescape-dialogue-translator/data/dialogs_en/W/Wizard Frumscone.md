# Wizard Frumscone

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Wizard Frumscone](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AWizard%20Frumscone)
- **Letter**: `W`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Wizard Frumscone:** Do you like my magic Zombies? Feel free to kill them, there's plenty more where these came from!
- *If the player has completed Swan Song:*
  - **Player:** I spoke to the necromancer like you suggested...
  - **Wizard Frumscone:** Please - let's not talk about it. Distentor and Zavistic would get very annoyed if they found I'd sent you to a necromancer.
  - **Player:** Ok, fair enough.

### Attempting to open the gate

- **Wizard Frumscone:** You can't attack the Zombies in the room, my Zombies are for magic practice only and should be attacked from the other side of the fence.

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Wizard Frumscone:** Oh, you'll be here for this, correct?
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Wizard Frumscone has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Wizard Frumscone:** That is not the answer that I was looking for. Please solve the puzzle first.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Wizard Frumscone has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Wizard Frumscone:** Wonderful! There you go!
