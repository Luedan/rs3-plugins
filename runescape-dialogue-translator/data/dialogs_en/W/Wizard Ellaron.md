## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Rune Mysteries and Rune Memories

- **Wizard Ellaron:** What can I do for you on this glorious day, adventurer?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Wizard Ellaron:** I am the Wizard Ellaron. Grand Master of the Order of Saradomin; Initiate into the Inner Circle of the Rune Mysteries; Heir to the Sacred Tradition of the Staff of Fire.
    - **Wizard Ellaron:** Impressive, no? We wizards love to invent titles for ourselves. All it really means is that I'm one of the senior wizards of the tower.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)
