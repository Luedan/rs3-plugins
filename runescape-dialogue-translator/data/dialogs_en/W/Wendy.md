## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Swept Away

- **Wendy:** Oh, hello.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Wendy:** Me? I'm Wendy. I'm learning the ways of witchcraft from Maggie here. She's very clever, you know...and very patient.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: What are you doing?
    - **Player:** What are you doing?
    - **Wendy:** I'm travelling with Maggie, trying to learn the ways of witchcraft.
    - **Player:** And what are you learning at the moment?
    - **Wendy:** I'm learning all about - um, hold on...let me just think...
    - **Wendy:** It was something really quite fascinating, I'm sure...
    - **Wendy:** Uh...
    - **Wendy:** Hmm...
    - **Player:** Look, don't worry about it, really.
    - **Wendy:** Sorry, I can't quite recall at the moment, but I'm sure it will come back to me, sooner or later.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: You look a little lost.
    - **Player:** If you don't mind my saying so, you look a little lost.
    - **Wendy:** It's funny - I get that a lot! I don't think I'm lost...not at the moment, at least.
    - **Wendy:** Keeping track of where one is isn't a problem, is it? I think it's more that I keep forgetting where I'm going.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do you mean?
        - **Player:** What do you mean?
        - **Wendy:** Well, it's just that there are so many interesting things to do and see, really!
        - **Wendy:** It's all well and good to make plans for myself, but I always seem to get distracted along the way.
        - **Player:** Distracted? By what?
        - **Wendy:** Oh, well, you know. A pretty flower. A little impling flying by. A fluffy cat that looks like it needs a scratch under its chin.
        - **Wendy:** I see things like that and my best-laid plans just seem to fly right out of my head!
        - **Player:** Hmm, I can see how they might.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: So where are you going?
        - **Player:** So where are you going?
        - **Wendy:** Well, Maggie has been teaching me the basics of witchcraft.
        - **Wendy:** I'm sure she sent me out to collect something but, come to think of it, I can't quite remember what it was.
        - **Player:** Well, perhaps it was something for a potion? Or a spell?
        - **Wendy:** Oo, that sounds possible! Do you remember what it might have been?
        - **Player:** Well, given that I wasn't there for the conversation, I have no way of knowing that, I'm afraid.
        - **Wendy:** Oh. Sad.
        - **Wendy:** Wait, what were we talking about again?
        - **Player:** Look, don't worry about it, okay?
        - (initial)

#### Using a regular cat on her

- **Player:** Could you make this cat purple for me?
- **Wendy:** I can, but I should warn you: I can make cats purple, but I don't know how to change them back. It's a permanent change! Is that okay?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please make my cat permanently purple.
    - **Player:** Yes, please make my cat permanently purple.
    - (removes=cat) (receives=purple cat)
    - **Wendy:**  There you go! Oh, such a beautiful colour.
    - **Player:** Thank you.
  - [Choice]: No, I think I'll hold off for the time being.
    - **Player:** No, I think I'll hold off for the time being.
