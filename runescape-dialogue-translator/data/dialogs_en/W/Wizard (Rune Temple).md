## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Wizard:** Hello, fellow runecrafter. How can I help you?
- **Player:** What are you researching?
- **Wizard:** Well, the Runecrafting wizards have got themselves into a bit of a state over a thesis published by a student.
- **Wizard:** We're also researching a new branch of Runecrafting at the moment: weapon imbuing.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What did this paper say?
    - **Player:** What did this paper say?
    - **Wizard:** Well, in short, it states that one day the altars like this one could run out of runic energy!
    - **Player:** That's terrible! How will humans perform magic if we cannot make runes?
    - **Wizard:** Well, it is still only a theory. Hardly any of the Runecrafting Guild wizards believe it to be true.
    - **Wizard:** Still, we have not proved Wizard Finix's theories to be incorrect yet, and - if they're true - this is very worrying. Wizards have been dispatched to all the known altars to investigate.
    - **Player:** So, what can we do if Wizard Finix's theories are true?
    - **Wizard:** Well, there is a new method being explored, which was detailed in Wizard Finix's thesis. He is the best person to explain it to you; he is currently in the Wizards' Tower, if you want to speak to him.
    - [Menu]: Travel to the Wizards' Tower?
      - [Choice]: Yes
        - {{Qact|The wizard casts a spell on the player and they are teleported to the top of the Wizards' Tower, in front of Wizard Finix.}}
      - [Choice]: No
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: What is weapon imbuing?
    - **Player:** What is weapon imbuing?
    - **Wizard:** Well, if you bring an inert magic weapon to an altar, you can use rune essence to imbue the weapon instead of binding runes.
    - **Wizard:** This process saturates the weapon with runic energy and thus increases its power. This way, even regular wood can be made into menacing magical armaments!
    - **Wizard:** In theory, that is. During our studies, we only ever managed to imbue eternal magic wood, perhaps due to the strong inherent magical properties that it possesses.
    - **Wizard:** Our fellow wizards aren't proficient enough to properly imbue the wood either. None were able to get more than trace amounts of runic energy to settle in it.
    - **Wizard:** Why don't you try it for yourself sometime? Just fletch some eternal magic wood into a staff, wand or orb and bring it to an altar with some essence.
    - **Wizard:** If you're more skilled than us, you might even confirm our theories and actually produce some usable weapons!

### Upon crafting runes for the first time after the 110 Runecrafting update

- **Wizard:** Hello, fellow runecrafter! The Runecrafting Guild recently discovered that altars can be used to make magic weapons. If you're interested, I'll tell you all about it!
