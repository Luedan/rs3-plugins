## Dialogue Transcript


### At the entrance to the Werewolf Agility Course


#### Talking to him


##### Without the ring of Charos equipped

- **Player:** What's beneath the trapdoor?
- **Werewolf:** That's none of your business, human, and I'll never tell.
- **Player:** Oh, come on - I'm only curious.
- **Werewolf:** If it wasn't my duty to stand here and guard our agility course from the likes of you, I would be relieving you of your life right now.
- **Player:** So it's an agility course, then?
- **Werewolf:** No ... yes ... oh blast - you didn't hear me say anything, right?
- **Player:** No problem. Can I come in?
- **Werewolf:** No, human - it's werewolves only.

##### With the werewolf outfit equipped

- **Player:** What's beneath the trapdoor?
- **Werewolf:** That's none of your business, human, and I'll never tell.
- **Player:** I'm not a human, I'm a werewolf.
- **Player:** You can tell by my...errr....stunningly fluffy tail.
- **Werewolf:** You smell of human...and your tail is attached by a peg.
- **Player:** Erm...woof?
- **Werewolf:** Har har har.
- {{Qact|The werewolf attacks the player, knocking them out. Screen fades to black momentarily.}}
- **Player:** I guess I deserved that.

##### With the ring of Charos equipped

- **Player:** What's beneath the trapdoor?
- **Werewolf:** It's an agility course designed for lycanthropes like ourselves, my friend.
- **Player:** Can I come in and use it?
- **Werewolf:** Certainly. The cavern contains two courses: on the west side is a level 60 Agility Course, and on the east side is a level 25 Skullball Course.

#### Attempting to enter the trapdoor while not wearing the ring of Charos

- **Werewolf:** You can't go down there, human. If it wasn't my duty to guard this trapdoor, I would be relieving you of the burden of your life right now.

#### Attempting to enter the trapdoor while wearing the ring of Charos

- **Werewolf:** Good luck down there, my friend. Remember, to the west is the main agility course, while to the east is a skullball course.
