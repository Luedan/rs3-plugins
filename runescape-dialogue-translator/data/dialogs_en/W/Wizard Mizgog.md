## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Wizards' Tower


#### Before Imp Catcher

- **Wizard Mizgog:** My beads! <br>Where are my beads?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Can I help you?
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** Wizard Grayzag next door decided that he didn't like me for some reason, so he enlisted an army of imps.
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** The imps stole all sorts of things. Most of them were things I don't really care about like eggs, balls of wool, things like that...
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** But they stole my magic beads! There was a red one, a yellow one, a black one, and a white one.
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** The imps will have spread them all over the kingdom by now. Could you get my beads back for me?
    - (see|Imp Catcher)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

#### After Imp Catcher

- **Wizard Mizgog:** What can I do for you, adventurer?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Do you have any more amulets of accuracy?
    - (opens=Mizgog's Amulets of Accuracy)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

#### After Love Story

- **Wizard Mizgog:** What can I do for you, adventurer?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: You didn't do well against Zenevivia.
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** I'm sorry, I'm no battle wizard! I just came here to unravel the mysteries of the universe.
    - **Player:** Is that working for you?
    - **Wizard Mizgog:** Yes, I'm making good progress.
  - [Choice]: Do you have any more amulets of accuracy?
    - (opens=Mizgog's Amulets of Accuracy)
