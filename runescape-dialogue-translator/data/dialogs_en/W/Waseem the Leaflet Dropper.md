## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Feud

- **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** I don't have time to talk right now! My boss is paying me to hand out these flyers.

### After The Feud

- **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** I don't have time to talk right now! Ali Morrisane is paying me to hand out these flyers.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who is Ali Morrisane?
    - **Player:** Who is Ali Morrisane?
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Ali Morrisane is the greatest merchant in the east!
    - **Player:** Were you paid to say that?
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Of course I was! You can find him in the north edge of town.
  - [Choice]: What are the flyers for?
    - **Player:** What are the flyers for?
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Well, Ali Morrisane isn't too popular with the other traders in Al Kharid, mainly because he's from Pollniveach and they feel he has no business trading in their town.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** I think they're just sour because he's better at making money than them.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** The flyer advertises the different shops you can find in Al Kharid.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** It also entitles you to money off on your next purchase in any of the shops listed on it. It's Ali's way of getting on the good side of traders.
    - **Player:** Which shops?
    - (Condition: If the player doesn't have a flyer in their backpack:)
      - (receives=Al Kharid flyer)
      - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Here! Take one and let me get back to work.
    - (Condition: If the player already has a flyer in their backpack:)
      - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Are you trying to be funny or has age turned your brain to mush? Look at the flyer you already have!
  - [Choice]: What is there to do
    - **Player:** What is there to do round here?
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** I'm very busy, so listen carefully! I shall say this only once.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** To the south is the busy and wondrous market of Al Kharid, and to the south are the ruins of the Duel Arena, now known as Het's Oasis.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** If you're here to make money, there is a mine to the south. Watch out for scorpions, though, they'll take a pop at you if you go too near them.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** To avoid them, just follow the western cliff as you travel south.
    - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** If you're in mood for a little rest and relaxation, there are a couple of nice fishing spots south of the town.
    - **Player:** Thanks for the help!

### Take-flyer option

- (Condition: If the player doesn't have a flyer in their backpack:)
  - (receives=Al Kharid flyer)
  - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Here! Take one and let me get back to work. I still have hundreds of these flyers to hand out, I wonder if Ali would notice if I quietly dumped them somewhere?
- (Condition: If the player already has a flyer in their backpack:)
  - **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Are you trying to be funny or has age turned your brain to mush? You already have a flyer!

### Using a flyer on Waseem

- **Player:** I don't want this! It's out of date!
- **Waseem the Leaflet Dropper:** Then why would I want it? Keep moving, I have to hand all these flyers before I get paid.
