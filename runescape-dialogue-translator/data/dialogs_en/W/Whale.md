# Whale

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Whale](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AWhale)
- **Letter**: `W`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

*(The following dialogues do not appear for the whale itself, but instead for the event regarding the whale.)*
- color=#ff8800

#### Misty

*(The following dialogues appear above Misty and is displayed color=#00a652 in the chat box.)*
- His mouth is full of fish, cast your lines!
- Captain, there be whales here!
- That's one giant whale!
- Don't fall in. You wouldn't want to get swallowed by that one!

#### Fisherman

*(The following dialogues appear above the Fisherman and is displayed color=#3278be in the chat box.)*
- Ugh! Give me a hand, he's swallowed me whole!
- Ughhhhh! HELP!
- Get him to spit me out!
- AHHHHHHHHHH! It's REALLY wet in here!

#### End


##### If the player succeeds

  - As the whale departs, water splashes the area.
  - Boost active: A 10% increase to your fishing catch rate (30 minutes)
  - The whale has departed...

##### If the player fails

  - Fisherman: Ugh! Nooooooooooo, we needed more help!
  - The whale departs feeling unsatisfied.
  - Unfortunately the encounter was not completed in time. Completing encounters successfully rewards deep sea fishing buffs.
  - The whale has departed...
