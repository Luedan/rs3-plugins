## Dialogue Transcript


### In Port Sarim


#### Before Pirate's Treasure

- **Wydin:** Welcome to my food store! Would you like to buy anything?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (opens=Wydin's Food Store)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
  - [Choice]: What can you recommend?
    - **Player:** What can you recommend?
    - **Wydin:** We have this really exotic fruit all the way from Karamja. It's called a banana.
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: Hmm, I think I'll try one.
        - **Player:**Hmm, I think I'll try one.
        - **Wydin:** Great, you might as well take a look at the rest of my wares as well.
        - (opens=Wydin's Food Store)
      - [Choice]: I don't like the sound of that.
        - **Player:**I don't like the sound of that.
        - **Wydin:** Well, it's your choice, but I do recommend them.

##### Attempting to enter the back room

- **Wydin:** Hey, you can't go in there. Only employees of the grocery store can go in.
- **Player:** Sorry, I didn't realise.

#### After Pirate's Treasure

- **Wydin:** Is it nice and tidy round the back now?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, can I work out front now?
    - **Player:** Yes, can I work out front now?
    - **Wydin:** No, I'm the one who works here.
  - [Choice]: Yes, are you going to pay me yet?
    - **Player:** Yes, are you going to pay me yet?
    - **Wydin:** Umm... No, not yet.
  - [Choice]: No, it's a complete mess.
    - **Player:** No, it's a complete mess.
    - **Wydin:** Ah well, it'll give you something to do, won't it.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy something please?
    - **Player:** Can I buy something please?
    - **Wydin:** Yes, of course.
    - (opens=Wydin's Food Store)

##### Attempting to enter the back room without an apron on

- **Wydin:** Can you put your white apron on before going in there, please?
