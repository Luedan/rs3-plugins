#### Claim option


##### With the medium Ardougne tasks or above

- **Wizard Cromperty:** As you've done so well in impressing not only the citizens of Ardougne, but also those of Vanille, I'll give you [700/1050] essence. Happy runecrafting!
- (receives=700 or 1050 noted pure essence)

##### Without the medium Ardougne tasks or above

- **Wizard Cromperty:** Sorry, old chap. I haven't gotten anything for you.
- You must complete the Medium or Hard Ardougne tasks to earn Cromperty's rewards.

##### Having already claimed for the day

- **Wizard Cromperty:** As much as I like you, old chap, you've already had your essence. Come back in [X] days/tomorrow.
