## Dialogue Transcript


### Before using the guidance

- **Wolf hunter:** Heard the news from Rellekka? Rumour is that Hati wolf has returned and Sköll may be joining him shortly.
  - Can you show me how to get there?
    - **Player:** Can you show me how to get there?
    - **Wolf hunter:** Certainly. I can guide you there as soon as you're ready to go.
    - Travel to Rellekka?
      - Let's go.
        - **Player:** Let's go.
        - **Wolf hunter:** Happy hunting!
        - {{Qact|The player gets teleported to the gate of Rellekka.}}
      - I've changed my mind.
        - **Player:** I've changed my mind.
        - **Wolf hunter:** Ah. Well, happy hunting wherever your feet take you, then.
  - Sounds dangerous...
    - **Player:** Sounds dangerous...
    - **Wolf hunter:** Pah! Look at you, scared of a wolf! Away with you!
  - *(If the player has killed Hati:)*
    - Hati's already dead.
      - **Player:** Hati's already dead; I killed him myself.
      - **Wolf hunter:** Well, according to what I've heard, he's alive and well in the woods. Maybe you didn't kill him well enough?
  - *(If the player has killed Sköll:)*
    - But I killed Sköll already.
      - **Player:** But I killed Sköll already.
      - **Wolf hunter:** He is still alive and biting, haven't you heard? Maybe you should try again.
