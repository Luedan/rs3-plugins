## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Shield of Arrav, or as a Black Arm Gang member


#### Attempting to climb up the ladder

- **Weaponmaster:** Only members of the Phoenix Gang are allowed up here. Find your own weapon stash.

### As a Phoenix Gang member

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Weaponmaster:** Hello, fellow Phoenix! <br>What do you seek?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm after a weapon or two.
    - **Weaponmaster:** No problem. Feel free to look around.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I'm looking for treasure.
    - **Weaponmaster:** Aren't we all? We've not got any up here. Go mug someone somewhere if you want some treasure.
    - (initial)
