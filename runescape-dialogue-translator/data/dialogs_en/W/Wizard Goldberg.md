## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Members world

- **Wizard Goldberg:** Good day to you, Player!
- [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=initial-options
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Why, my boy, I am Wizard Goldberg: world-renowned inventor! Give me a lever long enough, a fulcrum on which to place it, some bellows and maybe a few tubes, and I could assemble a machine to move the world!
    - Goldberg seems to lose himself in this thought.
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** As long as the peacock kept hold of the spanner...
    - **Player:** Um... Wizard Goldberg?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Oh, right, yes... This is my latest invention: the Rune Goldberg Machine! Which I truly believed had the potential to help us unravel the mysteries of magic!
    - **Player:** Had?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Well, yes... It turns out she wasn't quite the success I'd dreamt her to be, so I've taken her out of action.
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - [Choice]: What's this machine?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** This is my latest invention: the Rune Goldberg Machine! Before I took her out of action, she was able to turn unremarkable runes into purest vis wax! She was always a bit temperamental... Some runes worked better than others. Anyway, she's no longer in use, sorry.
  - [Choice]: Bye!
    - (end)

#### Free-to-play world

- **Wizard Goldberg:** Good day to you, Player! I've been looking for fellow scientists to help me with my experiments! But it's turning out to be harder than I thought.
- This feature can only be used on a members' server.

### Using runes on him

- **Wizard Goldberg:** [Type] runes, eh? Fascinating... Should I recommend those to other for their experiments?
- [Menu]: [Type] runes: do you recommend these to other players?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Splendid! I'll share this knowledge with the world.
  - [Choice]: No.
    - (end)

### Using an [[expired spin ticket]] on him

- **Wizard Goldberg:** The sign!
- **Wizard Goldberg:** Take this brother, and guard it well.
- (removes=the expired spin ticket)
- (receives=a nearly boiled egg)
- Wizard Goldberg snatches up your ticket and hands you an egg.

### Historical dialogue


#### Standard dialogue

- **Wizard Goldberg:** Good day to you, Player!
- Select an option{{anchor|initial-options}}
  - Who are you?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Why, my boy, I am Wizard Goldberg: world-renowned inventor! Give me a lever long enough, a fulcrum on which to place it, some bellows and maybe a few tubes, and I could assemble a machine to move the world!
    - Goldberg seems to lose himself in this thought.
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** As long as the peacock kept hold of the spanner...
    - **Player:** Um... Wizard Golberg?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Oh, right, yes... This is my latest invention: the Rune Goldberg Machine! Which I believe has the potential to help us unravel the mysteries of magic!
    - **Player:** So how does it work?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Well, I haven't quite unravelled the mysteries of the machine itself yet, so I'm letting everyone take a crack at it.
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - What's this machine?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** This is my latest and most fabulous invention: the Rune Goldberg Machine! It will take your ordinary, unremarkable runes and turn them into purest vis wax!
    - Select an option
      - Vis wax?
        - **Wizard Goldberg:{{anchor|wax}}** Wax! Simply place three runes into the Rune Goldberg Machine and watch them be transformed! She's a bit temperamental, that machine. Some runes work better than others. You might need to do some experimentation.
        - (initial|initial-options)
      - Go on...
        - (above|wax)
  - What can vis wax be used for?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** I'm sure it has plenty of uses, my boy! I'm told it can make your auras last longer, if that's something you have trouble with. It's highly prized by challenge masters for its rich waxiness. They'll let you change or extend your challenges in exchange for a good dollop of the stuff. It can even be used to increase the amount you can gather from divine locations - really! Using one simple trick discovered by a housewife! That is to say, my wife.
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - Can you help me with the machine?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Hrrrrm... She's a fickle machine, so what worked for your friends yesterday may not work for you today. I do know that the top slot seems to work the same for everyone... And there's a fair chance you know someone who'll have the same experience with the middle as you... But the bottom is a mystery that you alone can solve.
    - *Unknown condition 1:*
      - **Wizard Goldberg:** If you're stuck, my good friend, ((A Player Name)), had an interesting hypothesis. Hmmm... What was it now? Ah, yes! '((A Rune)) runes may be worth investigating.' That was it. If you'd like to dispute ((A Player's Name))'s findings, do show me a different rune and I'll pass your advice along.
      - **Player:** You don't really seem to know how your own machine works.
      - **Wizard Goldberg:** Oh, heavens no! I was trying to invent an automatic egg boiler!
      - (initial|initial-options)
    - *Unknown condition 2:*
      - **Wizard Goldberg:** I'm afraid I can't help you anymore than that. But if you figure that works well, show me a rune and I'll suggest it to anyone that gets stuck.
      - (initial|initial-options)
  - How did you know my name?
    - **Wizard Goldberg:** Why, I used my Name Goldberg Machine, of course!
    - Goldberg indicates a series of seemingly ordinary screwdrivers that have been tied together with fishing line. He offers no further explanation.
    - (initial|initial-options)
