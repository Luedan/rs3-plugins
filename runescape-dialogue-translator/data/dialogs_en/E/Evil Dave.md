#### After Evil Dave's Big Day Out

- **Evil Dave:** Welcome to my BASEMENT OF DOOM!

  - [Choice]: 'Evil Dave's Big Day Out'.
    - **Player:** Hello Dav-uh Doris?
    - **Evil Dave:** Look who it is, The Troublemaker.
    - **Player:** How are you finding being Dave?
    - **Evil Dave:** At first I thought this could be catharsis. I could be the Dave that he never was. I could show him how much better of a Dave I was than him. But soon, it became obvious to me. I could make Dave a better person. I could show Dave what it was like to be Dave. I could make Dave pay.
    - **Player:** How did that work out for you?
    - **Evil Dave:** He doesn't care. He just loves being me. But I learned something. Now I know why Dave wants be evil. Any manner of COLD REPRIEVE is preferable to living out such a MEANINGLESS EXISTENCE.
    - **Player:** Um, I'm going to go. Catch you later?
    - **Evil Dave:** GOODBYE DEARIE. GOODBYE.
