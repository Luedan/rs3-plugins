## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### While not on Kili's tier 90 tank task or before having talked to Kili about ScapeRune

- Conversation 1
  - **Player:** Let me out of here!
  - **Evil Bob:** I will never let you go, Player!
  - **Player:** Why not?
  - **Evil Bob:** Uhm... Because I say so! And, also because I can never have enough servants! Now catch me some fish, I'm hungry.
  - **Player:** What fish, where?
  - **Evil Bob:** Talk to my other servants and hurry it up!
- Conversation 2
  - **Player:** Let me out of here!
  - **Evil Bob:** I will never let you go, [Player Name]!
  - **Player:** What's it all about?
  - **Evil Bob:** Sit down and I'll tell you. It's a question of servant shortage.
  - **Player:** Go on.
  - **Evil Bob:** You are a skilled worker. A human like you is worth a great deal as a slave.
  - **Player:** A slave? I will have nothing to do with you. Is that clear? Absolutely nothing.
  - **Evil Bob:** Now, be reasonable, little human. It's just a matter of time before you do everything I ask of you. Play along and this could be a very nice place.
  - **Player:** I will not make any deals with you. I'm a human. I will not be pushed, numbered, meowed at, teleported or enslaved. My life is my own.
  - **Evil Bob:** Is it?
  - **Player:** Yes. You won't hold me.
  - **Evil Bob:** Won't I? Let me assure you that there's no way out. Just ask my servants.
- Conversation 3
  - **Player:** Let me out of here!
  - **Evil Bob:** I will never let you go, [Player Name]!
  - **Player:** How is it possible that you're talking?
  - **Evil Bob:** How is it possible that you're not meowing?
  - **Player:** Meowing? Why would I be meowing?
  - **Evil Bob:** Most humans do; that's why I'm wearing this Amulet of Manspeak. I do try to train my staff in civilised speech. Ah, but I suppose things are slightly different in your world. What a dreadful place that must be.
  - **Player:** I think I'm getting a headache...
- Conversation 4
  - **Player:** Let me out of here!
  - **Evil Bob:** I will never let you go, Player!
  - **Player:** What did you do to Bob?
  - **Evil Bob:** Bob? I am Bob! Oh, you mean the Bob in your world? Nothing. I am the incarnation of Bob here on ScapeRune.
  - **Player:** What, you mean this is...like some kind of mirror land?
  - **Evil Bob:** Something like that. Somewhere, in ScapeRune, there must be a human servant called Player. But you work just as well for me. Now get to work, human. Fish for me!

#### While on Kili's tier 90 tank task and after having talked to Kili about ScapeRune


##### Only on the first time

- **Player:** Hi Evil Bob. I'm Player.
- **Evil Bob:** Ah, a new prisoner!
- **Player:** Not quite. I was hoping to get some blueberries, please.
- **Evil Bob:** Hmm. Normally I wouldn't help a human with something they need.
- **Evil Bob:** But, I am bored. And you did say please.
- **Evil Bob:** You must get me something in return first. Something shiny.
- **Player:** Something shiny? There's a lot of shiny things.
- **Evil Bob:** Not just anything shiny. Something to keep me entertained.
- **Evil Bob:** Now, out of my sight.
- Evil Bob's eyes glaze over, as he mentally leaves the conversation.
- **Player:** ...
- **Player:** Something entertaining for a cat, and shiny. A mouse... A ball of wool, maybe?

##### Afterwards

##### Without golden wool

- **Evil Bob:** Where is my shiny and entertaining item?
- **Player:** I don't have it yet.
- **Evil Bob:** I don't want to see you until you do.

##### With golden wool

- **Player:** I have something shiny for you.
- **Evil Bob:** Yes? Give it here.

- **Evil Bob:** This is just what I need. Now, off with you.

##### After having given Evil Bob golden wool


##### Before having received blueberries

- **Player:** Er... Can I have the blueberries now?
- **Evil Bob:** Ugh. You again. I suppose a deal is a deal.
- Evil Bob beckons his servant forward.
- **Servant:** Here you go. Homegrown by the prisoners!
- File:Blueberries.png Evil Bob's servant hands you some blueberries.
- (receives=blueberries)
- **Player:** Thanks. I think.

##### After having received blueberries without having lost them or a variant of the pie

- **Evil Bob:** Get me some fish for my supper, slave.

##### After having received blueberries but having lost them or a variant of the pie

- **Player:** I lost the blueberries. Can I have some more?
- ((Without golden wool:))
  - **Evil Bob:** I'm not a charity. I'm evil! Bring me more golden wool and you can have more blueberries.
- ((With golden wool:))
  - Evil Bob beckons his servant forward.
  - **Servant:** Here you go.
  - (removes=golden wool) (receives=blueberries)
  - Evil Bob servant takes the golden wool from you, and gives you some more blueberries.
