## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Kennith's Concerns

- **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Well hello there; welcome to our little village. Pray, stay awhile.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** I supply the local fishermen with the tools and bait they need to do their job.
    - **Player:** Interesting. Have you been doing it long?
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Why yes, all of my life.<br />I took over from my father, who inherited the business from his father and so on.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** In fact, there have been Lovecraft's selling bait for over ten generations.
    - **Player:** Wow, that's some lineage.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Oh yes, we have a long and interesting family history. For one reason or another the Lovecraft's have always been bait sellers or writers.
    - **Player:** Hmm, yes well. I'm sure it's all fascinating, but I...
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Oh very, very fascinating, for instance my great grandfather Howard...
    - **Player:** Yawn. Oh, I'm sorry but I really must be getting on. I think my giraffe needs feeding.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Your?
    - **Player:** Giraffe. Sorry, but he gets cranky without enough sugar. Bye now!
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Oh right, goodbye.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy some fishing supplies please?
    - **Player:** Can I buy some fishing supplies please?
    - (opens=Lovecraft's Tackle)
  - [Choice]: You are a bit too strange for me. Bye.
    - **Player:** You are a bit too strange for me. Bye.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Sniff. Yes, everyone says that.
  - [Choice]: cond=If the player has progressed far enough in The Slug Menace:|You know, you sound just like the mayor?
    - **Player:** You know, you sound just like the mayor?
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Really? That's the nicest thing anyone has ever said. I admire him a great deal. He works so hard for this town.
    - **Player:** You look like him as well?
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Hmm, I don't think I do.
    - **Player:** No really, what with the unhealthy skin tone and bulging eyes. The resemblance is uncanny.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Well, I haven't spoken to him in a while. I don't get out much.
    - **Player:** I can see why.
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Maybe the Mayor has finally seen how much I admire him and has grown to admire me?
    - **Player:** Anything's possible I suppose. Wait a minute, you mean you've always looked that odd?
    - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Hey, I resent that!
    - **Player:** I'm sorry, it's just... Nevermind.

### In the mines below Witchaven, after Kennith's Concerns

- **Player:** Are you not going home, Ezekial?
- **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Oh, I will soon; I'm just taking in the atmosphere...
- **Player:** The 'atmosphere'? This place has brought us nothing but trouble.
- **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Absolutely, but that just increases my determination to rid the world of those sea slugs once and for all. Anger is an energy, Player.
- **Player:** I'm a bit worried about you, Ezekial. I think you ought to go home and relax.
- **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Maybe you're right. I'll go soon, just give me a little more time.

### After Kennith's Concerns and before Salt in the Wound

- **Player:** Hello, Ezekial.
- **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Why, hello, Player! I'm so pleased to see you again. Life has been very dull in this quiet little village ever since our last adventure.
- (Condition: With rubium in inventory:)
  - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** I see you have some rubium - would you like to sell it to me?
  - **Player:** Well, that depends! How much would you give me for [amount] pieces?
  - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** For that amount of rubium I can offer you [10 gp per rubium].
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Okay, it's a deal!
      - **Player:** Okay, it's a deal!
      - (removes=all rubium in inventory) (receives=10 coins per rubium)
      - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** It's a pleasure doing business with you.
    - [Choice]: I'm sorry, Ezekial. I think I'm going to keep hold of it myself for now.
      - **Player:** I'm sorry, Ezekial. I think I'm going to keep hold of it myself for now.
      - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Come back later if you change your mind. I'm going to need all that I can get hold of if we're going to finally beat the sea slugs.
- (Condition: Without rubium in inventory:)
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: So, no sign of any more sea slug activity?
      - **Player:** So, no sign of any more sea slug activity?
      - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** No, it's all very quiet around here at the moment. maybe a little too quiet...
      - **Player:** I see. Let's hope it stays that way!
      - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Oh, I don't know about that. I'm looking forward to going on another adventure with you sometime.
    - [Choice]: Can I buy some fishing supplies please?
      - **Player:** Can I buy some fishing supplies please?
      - (opens=Lovecraft's Tackle)
    - [Choice]: I need to get going; see you later.
      - **Player:** I need to get going; see you later.
      - **Ezekial Lovecraft:** Okay, Player. Pray, don't be a stranger.
