## Dialogue Transcript

- **Eliza:** Have you met Violet? She's so cool! I want hair like hers!
- **Player:** I helped her get here. You wouldn't believe the adventure we had together.
- **Eliza:** You know Violet? Can you ask her if she'll be my best friend?
- **Player:** I'll see what I can do.
