## Dialogue Transcript


### On board the ''Lady Zay''

- **Player:** What do you do for fun on this ship? You know, when you're not doing pirate stuff.
- ** 'Eagle-eye' Shultz:** All sorts! Hide and seek, pin the patch on the pirate, walk the plank!
- **Player:** What a life! Wait a minute. 'Walk the plank'? Surely that's a bit dangerous?
- ** 'Eagle-eye' Shultz:** Well of course, but where's the fun without a few deaths?
- **Player:** I think I'll stick to Runelink.
