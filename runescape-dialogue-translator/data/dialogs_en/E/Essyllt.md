## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Mourner Headquarters


#### After Mourning's End Part I

- **Player:** Do you have the key to the excavation site yet?
- **Head Mourner:** No the guard is being a bit slow, but we should have it soon.
- **Player:** Alright, I'll report in again tomorrow.

#### After Mourning's End Part II and before Plague's End

- **Head Mourner:** Any news yet on our missing dig team?
- **Player:** No, I have looked everywhere, but I can see no sign of them.
- **Head Mourner:** This is not good news, did you find anything else down there?
- **Player:** Nothing other than the beasts you spoke of.
- **Head Mourner:** I must ask you to return to the search and remember if you see anything elven-made down there you must let me know. After all that is what we are here for.
- **Player:** I'll come to you as soon as I dig anything up.
