#### With both hands free

- {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. Player is on Lake Mnemosyne in a rowboat following Ed.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* Welcome to Ed's boat tours in the City of Um. Today we'll be rowing around Lake Mnemosyne, while I tell you some fun facts about the sights.
- {{Qact|The player and Ed begin rowing.}}
- {{Qact|The player and Ed stop at the back of the Well of Souls. The camera pans and focuses on the well.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* First stop, the Well of Souls. A masterpiece of architecture, this vessel harbours all of the souls that live here, in Um.
- **Ed:** It was created in the First Age, not long after the City itself, and boasts stone carvings of skulls and Death himself.
- **Ed:** If you look close enough, you can even see the individual souls floating about in its bowl.
- **Player:** It really is an impressive structure.
- **Ed:** Indeed. I wouldn't get too close if I were you though - there's quite a drop surrounding it.
- {{Qact|The camera focuses on the player. The player and Ed begin rowing.}}
- {{Qact|The player and Ed stop at some shipwrecks on which the camera focuses.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* For our second stop, just some of the many shipwrecks in Mnemosyne.
- **Ed:** Imagined into existence by the many sailors and pirates alike, these wrecks scatter themselves across the rocky outcrops.
- {{Qact|The camera focuses on the player. The player and Ed begin rowing.}}
- **Player:** Have you met many famous beings in Um?
- **Ed:** Great question. With Um having seen many residents over the years, I have met my fair share of notable Gielinorians.
- **Player:** Like who?
- **Ed:** Well, to name a few, Queen Glarial of the elves, Obi'Sooth of the aviansie, and the infamous Count Draynor.
- **Ed:** Most of them pass through to the afterlife, but some, such as the legendary fisherwoman Tavia, choose to hang around in Um for a while.
- **Ed:** No gods, though, as beings such as them - as well as Mahjarrat and demons - do not pass through the Underworld.
- {{Qact|The player and Ed stop to view Rasial's citadel. The camera turns to face it.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* The third stop on our tour is the dastardly Rasial's citadel.
- **Ed:** Whilst not in the City of Um, the citadel sits high in the sky - with views over a vast array of the Underworld.
- **Ed:** I haven't personally visited, but I hear it has an impressive central chamber, complete with an antique organ.
- {{Qact|Thecamera returns to the player.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* I don't get fazed easily... But looking up at that citadel for too long sends a rattle down my old bones.
- {{Qact|The player and Ed begin rowing.}}
- **Ed:** If you're lucky, you might come across some fellow boaters when exploring Mnemosyne, such as our locals Sammy and Deano.
- {{Qact|The player and Ed stop by the graveyard. The camera pans and focuses on it.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* Our penultimate stop on the tour is the graveyard and market of Um.
- **Ed:** Rather than honouring the dead, the shrines in the graveyard honour the living. Many residents light candles within them to look over their living loved ones, while they wait for them to pass.
- **Player:** How thoughtful.
- {{Qact|The camera pans to the market.}}
- **Ed:** The market is home to a variety of traders, who come from all over to sell their wares on the docks of Um.
- {{Qact|The camera focuses on the player. The player and Ed begin rowing.}}
- {{Qact|The player and Ed stop below the bridge. The camera focuses on the centre of the city.}}
- **Ed:** *grumble* And our final stop on this tour is the centre of the City itself.
- **Ed:** The City of Um was created by Death in the First Age, and has since seen a vast variety of beings pass through - humans, trolls, elves, and more.
- **Ed:** Residents that pass through here create the features of the city through their memories. This leaves most of the structures to deteriorate over time as the residents pass on, and their memories are forgotten.
- **Ed:** However, one of the most notable and longest-standing features of Um is its giant hourglass.
- **Ed:** This is one of the few surviving original features since the City's inception, having been kept in memory due to the frequent visits by Death.
- **Ed:** Now, does anyone have any questions before we end the tour?
