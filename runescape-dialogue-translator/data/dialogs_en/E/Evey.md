## Dialogue Transcript


### Celebration of Fire

- **Evey:** Uh... Hello there.
- **Player:** What are you doing here?
- **Evey:** Chad said that they needed some help running the celebration this year so I tagged along.
- **Player:** What's going on around you then?
- **Evey:** Oh! We're summoning pretty fire spirits using special fire stones.
- **Player:** That sounds like a lot of fun.
  - *(If the fire stone hasn't been claimed:)*
    - **Player:** Can I have a go?
    - **Evey:** Here, take this fire stone. You can use it to summon one of the fire spirits you see around us.
    - **Player:** Thanks, I'll make use of this.
  - *(If the fire stone has been claimed:)*
    - **Player:** Have you got any more fire stones?
    - **Evey:** Sorry, I've not got any on me, but I did give some supplies to Alice.
    - **Player:** Ok, thanks.
