## Dialogue Transcript


### Going Like Clockwork


#### Talking to


##### During the event

- *(If there are clockwork pieces to hand in:)*
  - *(Dialogue from handing in clockwork pieces occurs, then continues below.)*
- **Eira:** Hello! I'm looking for people to help save Christmas. Would you be able to help?
- Talk about...
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - **Eira:** My name is Eira. I'm Santa's head elf.
      - **Eira:** Or at least I am at the moment!
      - **Eira:** We're having a few issues at the workshop and I need to get it all sorted before Santa gets back from his holidays.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What as happened?
      - **Eira:** Santa is having his yearly holiday, just before the Christmas rush, but this year the snow implings aren't happy about it at all.
      - **Eira:** They all want a holiday too, and because they can't have one they've decided to be naughty and steal all of the clockwork pieces from the workshop.
      - **Player:** Clockwork pieces?
      - **Eira:** Yes, they are used to make Santa's new toy for this year; a clockwork gnome pet.
      - **Player:** So you can't make any more?
      - **Eira:** Not until we get some of the clockwork pieces back, which is where you come in, I hope.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - How can I help?
      - **Eira:** The snow implings have deposited the clockwork pieces all throughout Gielinor.
      - **Eira:** You'll likely find them while you are doing your day to day activities.
      - **Player:** What should I do with them when I find them?
      - **Eira:** If you could bring them back to me, that would be a huge help!
      - **Eira:** I'll be able to get you some rewards for doing it too.
      - **Eira:** I'm hoping to get the whole thing cleared up before Santa comes back.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - Anything else I should know?
      - **Eira:** You might come across some of the naughty snow implings themselves.
      - **Eira:** If you catch them they should have a pile of clockwork pieces on them.
      - **Eira:** I think if they run out of pieces that will head back to the workshop and get back to work.{{Sic}}
      - **Eira:** At least, I hope that's what will happen.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What are the rewards?
      - **Eira:** Once we've got our stocks replenished I can fashion the clockwork pieces into some rewards for you.
      - **Eira:** There will be a clockwork shield and a clockwork crown, both very fetching.
      - **Player:** Anything else?
      - **Eira:** Yes, there will be a clockwork lemon. Flighty little thing. You can wind it up and watch it fly around your head!
      - **Eira:** Adorable but strange.
      - **Eira:** And then there is, of course, the clockwork gnome pet.
      - **Eira:** Considering you'll be helping us be able to make these for all the children this year, you should get one too.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only after asking something else:)*
    - Bye.
      - **Eira:** May you have lots of luck with all of your gathering!

##### After the event

- **Eira:** Thanks for helping me collect up all of the clockwork pieces. I still have some time here to thank you for your efforts.

#### Handing in clockwork pieces


##### Without clockwork pieces

- You do not have anything to hand in.

##### With clockwork pieces and rewards to unlock

- Handing in event currency will increase your progress for this event unlocking unique rewards. This will remove currency up to the amount that is required to complete the event.
- Do you wish to continue?
  - No
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
  - Yes
    - You hand in [X] Clockwork pieces. You need to hand in [Y] more to complete the event.
    - *(Dialogue from claiming rewards occurs.)*

##### With all rewards claimed

- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.

#### Claiming rewards


##### Without rewards to claim and none claimed

- **Eira:** You haven't handed in enough clockwork pieces for me to give you any rewards.

##### With rewards to claim

- *(If a customisation is unlocked:)*
  - **Eira:** There you are!
  - *(The following is repeated for all the unlocked rewards:)*
    - Congratulations, you have unlocked the [Clockwork Shield/Clockwork Crown/Clockwork Gnome Pet].
    - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If an item is rewarded:)*
  - *(If the inventory is full:)*
    - You do not have enough inventory space for some of the rewards. Please make some space and try again.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - **Eira:** There you are!
    - *(The following is repeated for all the unlocked rewards:)*
      - Congratulations, you have been awarded [an event mystery box/the Clockwork Lemon item].
      - *(Continues below.)*
- You have unlocked [X]/6 of the rewards for the Going Like Clockwork event.

##### Without rewards to claim and some claimed

- **Eira:** You have more rewards to unlock. Collect more clockwork pieces to get them.
- You have unlocked [X]/6 of the rewards for the Going Like Clockwork event.

##### With all rewards claimed

- **Eira:** You've already claimed all of the rewards I have to offer.
- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining clockwork pieces into consumable rewards.

### Reindeer-terrorbird racing

- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - How does the Terrorbird Racing work?
    - **Eira:** These races are an extra part of the Christmas festivities for this year. They're very exciting.
    - **Eira:** You can race once an hour and the idea is to get round all of the 8 gates in the quickest time possible.
    - **Eira:** The quicker you are the greater your reward will be at the end of the race.
    - **Eira:** As you go through each gate you'll be told where the next gate can be found.
    - **Eira:** Remember, you'll need to be wearing a Terrorbird mount to be able to take part. It adds to the fun!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - Where can I get a Terrorbird costume?
    - **Eira:** You can wear your own Terrorbird mount costume but there is a special Christmas themed one available for free in Solomon's Store.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - How do the hi-scores work?
    - **Eira:** The top 10 quickest times are stored and can be seen by checking on any of the race gates.
    - **Eira:** Hopefully you'll make the top 10!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- Bye for now.
  - **Eira:** Happy racing!
