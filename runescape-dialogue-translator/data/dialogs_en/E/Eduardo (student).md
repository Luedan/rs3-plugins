## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Dig Site


#### Talk to option

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Eduardo (student):** Oh, hi. I'm studying hard for an exam.
- **Player:** What exam is that?
- **Eduardo (student):** It's the Earth Sciences exam.
- **Player:** Interesting....

#### Using an animal skull on him

- **Player:** I am not sure why I am giving this to the student...
- Nothing interesting happens.

### After The Dig Site


#### Before unlocking research

- **Eduardo (student):** Oh, hi again. News of your find has spread fast. You are quite famous around here now.

#### After unlocking research


##### Before joining research team

- **Player:** I'm setting up a research team and wondered if you'd want to be a part of it...
- **Eduardo (student):** I'd love to, but only interns and about can work on research teams. The only way I can become an intern is if I impress Acting Guildmaster Reiniger with an artefact find.
- **Eduardo (student):** It would have to be an artefact related to my specialisation too, which is animal skulls and equipment.
- (Condition: If the player doesn't have a ceremonial unicorn ornament:)
  - **Player:** Would it fast track the process if I just brought you a restored artefact that fits?
  - **Eduardo (student):** You'd do that just so I can work on your research team? That's so kind.
  - **Player:** Well, we did study for our exams together - you helped me out a lot, so I'm only returning the favour.
  - **Eduardo (student):** Tell you what - if you can bring me a restored ceremonial unicorn ornament from Everlight, I'll report for duty ASAP!
  - **Player:** I'm on it!
- (Condition: If the player has a ceremonial unicorn ornament:)
  - **Player:** I have a restored ceremonial unicorn ornament from Everlight...
  - [Menu]: Give ceremonial unicorn ornament to Eduardo?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - **Eduardo (student):** Fantastic! Reporting for duty, boss!
      - Eduardo has joined your research team!
    - [Choice]: No.
      - **Player:** Let me get back to you on this.
      - **Eduardo (student):** Whenever you're ready is good.

##### After joining research team

- **Eduardo (student):**Ready for research whenever you need me, boss!
