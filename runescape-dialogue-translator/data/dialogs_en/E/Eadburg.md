## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there. What's in the pot?
- **Eadburg:** The stew for the servants' main meal.
- **Player:** Can I have some?
- **Eadburg:** Well not unless you become a servant. There's a war on, you know, so food is scarce.
- **Player:** Good point. I should probably leave you to it.
- **Eadburg:** Bye then.
