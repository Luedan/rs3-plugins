## Dialogue Transcript


### At Het's Oasis


#### Normally

- ((Only on first time:))
  - **Etta Stone:** Hello there!
  - **Player:** What's going on? Last time I was here, everything was intact!
  - **Etta Stone:** Ah, well, you see... There was seemingly an earthquake. The whole Duel Arena, just destroyed!
  - **Player:** How awful!
  - **Etta Stone:** Indeed. My team and I are helping out the locals to recover and restore what we can.
  - (continue)
- ((Only after the first time:))
  - **Etta Stone:** Hello there, Player.
  - (continue)
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What's going on here again?
    - **Player:** What's going on here again?
    - **Etta Stone:** Unfortunately a devastating earthquake occurred around what was the Duel Arena, turning it into rubble and ruin.
    - **Etta Stone:** Everyone in the area is chipping in to help recover what we can, and to restore the area into an oasis of life and beauty.
    - (previous)
  - ((Only during the Oasis Restoration event:))
    - What can I do to help?
      - **Player:** What can I do to help?
      - **Etta Stone:** At the moment, we're trying to unlock that hefty chest over there... We came across it whilst scouring the ruins for artefacts. But it's proving to be a bit more difficult than we anticipated.
      - **Etta Stone:** So in the meantime, please look around to see what you can help with! There's all sorts that need to be doing each day.
      - **Player:** Like what?
      - **Etta Stone:** Well, to start with, there's a number of activities around the oasis you might be able to use your skills for.
      - **Etta Stone:** The water is horribly infested with scarabs, so you can use your hunting abilities to help clear them out.
      - **Etta Stone:** With the destruction also came a blooming of new flower species. You might be able to use your farming abilities to cultivate these, for further studying.
      - **Etta Stone:** And lastly, you can use your agility to explore the area. Let me know if you come across anything interesting!
      - **Etta Stone:** I've also got a task list here with me if you need anything else to do.
      - **SELECT AN OPTION**
        - Open tasks.
          - (The task interface opens.)
        - No thanks.
          - (previous2)
  - Tell me about yourself.
    - **Player:** Tell me about yourself.
    - **Etta Stone:** Not much to say, really! My mother and father were archaeologists, so I've been around the profession my whole life. It was only natural that I'd grow up to be one, too.
    - **Etta Stone:** I was born and bred in Varrock, but my work has taken me all over Gielinor. It's what I love about it!
    - (previous)
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Etta Stone:** Farewell.

#### During the Oasis Restoration event


##### Before receiving research items or having lost them

- **Etta Stone:** Ah, World Guardian. How good it is to see you. I wonder, if it's not too much of an inconvenience, could you do me a small favour?
- **Etta Stone:** With so much of the arena now being...rather forcibly {{Sic|uncoverered}}, there are a number of historical artefacts that have appeared.
- **Etta Stone:** The museum is helping out with the restoration of these artefacts for Al Kharid. But we rather lack the facilities here.
- **Etta Stone:** As you're the most trustworthy person I'm aware of, would you be so kind as to drop these off at the museum for me?
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - Sure, I'll take them to the museum.
    - **Etta Stone:** Fantastic, you'll just need to hand them over the curator.{{Sic}}
    - ((Without space in backpack:))
      - **Etta Stone:** Oh dear, it appears you're already overburdened. When you've got some space free in your backpack come talk to me again.
    - ((With space in backpack:))
      - (receives=research items)
      - You receive a package full of broken bits of pottery.
      - **Etta Stone:** Thank you ever so much!
  - Not right now.
    - **Etta Stone:** Oh, I totally understand. You must be a very busy person. Well, I'll be here if you change your mind.
    - (above|options)

##### After receiving research items

- **Etta Stone:** Have you delivered the research items yet?
- **Player:** No, not yet. I'll get on that.
- (above|options)
