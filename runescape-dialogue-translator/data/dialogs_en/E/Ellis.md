## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid


#### Achievement diary dialogue

- **Ellis:** Greetings, friend. I am a manufacturer of leather.
- **Ellis:** I see you have started your Lumbridge and Draynor achievement diary. Would you like a needle and thread for Crafting?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - (receives=a needle and some thread)
    - Ellis hands you a needle and thread.
    - **Ellis:** Here you are then. If you ever find yourself in need of another set, you can always buy needles and thread from most Crafting shops.
    - (below|Standard dialogue)
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Ellis:** Very well then.
    - (below|Standard dialogue)

#### Standard dialogue

- **Ellis:** Greetings, friend. I am a manufacturer of leather.
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Can I buy some leather then?
    - **Player:** Can I buy some leather then?
    - **Ellis:** I make leather from animal hides. Bring me some cowhides and I'll tan them into soft leather for you.
  - [Choice]: Leather is rather weak stuff.
    - **Player:** Leather is rather weak stuff.
    - **Ellis:** Normal leather may be quite weak, but it's so easy to craft that anyone can work with it.
    - **Ellis:** Alternatively you could try hard leather. It's not so easy to craft, but I only charge 3 gp per cowhide to prepare it, and it makes much sturdier armour.
    - **Ellis:** I can also tan snake hides and dragonhides, suitable for crafting into the highest quality armour for rangers.
    - **Player:** Thanks, I'll bear it in mind.
  - [Choice]: What can you tell me about Ali Morrisane?
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about Ali Morrisane?
    - **Ellis:** I wouldn't mind him setting up shop, but he's brought others in.
    - **Ellis:** Two of them came here and said they were going to put me out of business if I didn't listen to them!
    - **Ellis:** Hah, as if they think they could threaten me with a hammer and a pair of scissors.
    - **Player:** What will you do about the threats?
    - **Ellis:** The other shopkeepers and I won't give in to their threats. Osman's also told us that he'll make sure we're protected.
    - **Ellis:** I'll keep tanning leather for people like you to make into clothes and armour.
