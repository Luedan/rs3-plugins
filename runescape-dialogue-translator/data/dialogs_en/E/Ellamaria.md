## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock Palace Garden


#### Without the requirements for Garden of Tranquillity

- **Player:** What's going on here? I see a lot of farming patches with nothing growing in them.
- **Ellamaria:** No, one has just had them installed. One has had the most marvelous idea to bring renewed happiness to one's own dearest husband.
- **Player:** One? I'm not sure I understand you-
- **Ellamaria:** Oh dear - the common classes, oh how they fill one with intolerable levels of exasperation, I swear to be most true.
- **Player:** Ri-ight ... if you say so, my lady. I'll be off then, if you don't mind-
- **Ellamaria:** Yes, be off with you, before I call the guards.
- **Player:** No problem, sorry to have bothered you.
- **Ellamaria:** Peasant - WAIT!!!
- **Player:** Are you speaking to me?
- **Ellamaria:** Who else would I be speaking to, you funny little [man/woman]? Of course you.
- **Player:** Well for a moment there I thought there was a dog around here somewhere...
- **Ellamaria:** Oh yes, sarcasm - how amusing. No, no, peasant - I have a question to ask of you, and you are required to answer.
- **Player:** Well, seeing as you put it so nicely...
- **Ellamaria:** Do you know of anyone who might have some degree of horticultural experience?
- (Condition: Without level 25 Farming - this takes priority over non-completion of Creature of Fenkenstrain:)
  - **Player:** If you're asking me do I know a gardener, then I'm afraid the answer is no. And with that, I would kindly request that I can take your leave now, m'lady.
  - **Ellamaria:** Yes, hurry off peasant - you are of no use to me.
  - [The Queen is looking for somebody with at least level 25 Farming -  you may need to practise your Farming skills a little bit more.]
- (Condition: With level 25 Farming and non-completion of Creature of Fenkenstrain:)
  - **Player:** If you're asking me do I know a gardener, then yes I do. I happen to be quite handy at farming myself, as it happens.
  - **Ellamaria:** Well, that is as may be. However, this task may also involve some knowledge of the skills of diplomacy and persuasion. From the manner of your speech, these are skills I doubt that you possess.
  - **Player:** Hmmm. I could say that it takes one to know one...
  - **Ellamaria:** Be off with you, peasant. You are mightily fortunate that I do not call the guards this instant, after which you may never see another sunrise.
  - [You may find that completing the quest 'Creature of Fenkenstrain' may set you upon the path you need to follow in order to learn the skills that Queen Ellamaria requires from you.]

#### After Garden of Tranquillity

- **Ellamaria:** What, you want to dig up the plants that took one so long to grow? Don't be be ridiculous, you silly little [man/woman].
- **Player:** All right then, sorry I asked.
- **Ellamaria:** Impertinent peasant! Oh, if you promise to leave one alone then feel free to pick fruit from the White Tree. One is told that they are a great natural energy restorative.
