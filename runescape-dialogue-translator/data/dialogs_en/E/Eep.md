    - **Eep:** Notably, we uncovered some kind of archaeological site just to the west of me, as well as various ancient remains we've found all across the Hollow.

    - **Eep:** I can also impart a bit of my knowledge of prayer on you, as well as some more Spooky tokens, in exchange for any ancient remains you find while working within the Hollow.
