## Dialogue Transcript


### Without tea in backpack

- **Elsie:** Hello dearie! What can old Elsie do for you?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: What are you making?
    - **Player:** What are you making?
    - **Elsie:** I'm knitting a new stole for Father Lawrence downstairs. He could do with something to keep his neck warm, standing in that draughty old church all day.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me a story?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me a story?
    - **Elsie:** Maybe I could tell you a story if you'd fetch me a nice cup of tea.
    - **Player:** I'll think about it.
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me how to get rich?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me how to get rich?
    - **Elsie:** Well, dearie, I'm probably not the best person to ask about money, but I think the best thing would be for you to get a good trade. If you've got a trade you can earn your way, that's what my old father told me, Saradomin rest his soul.
    - **Elsie:** I hear people try to get rich by fighting in the Wilderness north of here but that's no way for honest folks to earn a living! So get yourself a good trade, and keep working at it. There's always folks wanting to buy ore and food around here.
    - **Player:** Thanks for the advice.
    - (other)

### With tea in backpack

- **Elsie:** Ooh - that looks like a lovely cup of tea, dearie. Is it for me?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Yes, you can have it.
    - **Player:** Yes, you can have it.
    - (removes=cup of tea)
    - (Condition: If this is the first time giving Elsie a cup of tea:)
      - {{RSFontAchievement|Jackanory}}
      - (continue)
    - **Elsie:** Ahh, there's nothing like a nice cuppa tea. I know what, I'll tell you a story to thank you for the lovely tea...
    - **Elsie:** A long time ago, when I was a little girl, there was a handsome young man living in Varrock. I saw him here in the church quite often. Everyone said he was going to become a priest, and we girls were so sad about that.
    - **Elsie:** But young Dissy - that was the young man's nickname - he was a wild young thing. One night he gathered some lads together, and after the evening prayer-meeting they all put on masks and sneaked down to the evil temple in the south of the city, the evil one.
    - **Elsie:** The next day, there was quite a hubbub. The guards told us that someone had painted 'Saradomin pwns' on the wall of the Zamorakian temple!
    - **Elsie:** Now, we'd always been taught to keep well away from that dreadful place, but it really did us all good to see someone wasn't afraid of the scum who live at that end of town. Old Father Packett was furious, but Dissy just laughed it off.
    - **Elsie:** Dissy left town after that, saying he wanted to see the world. It was such a shame, he had the most handsome shoulders...
    - **Elsie:** A young man came here looking for stories about Dissy - of course, that's not his proper name, but his friends called him Dissy - and I told him that one. He said Dissy had become a really famous man and there was going to be a book about him.
    - **Elsie:** Well, that's all good, but I do wish Dissy had just come back to Varrock. I did miss him so much... well, until I met my Freddie and we got married, but that's another story.
    - **Player:** Thank you. I'll leave you to your knitting now.

    - **Player:** No, keep your hands off my tea.
