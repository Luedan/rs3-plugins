### In East Ardougne


#### After Biohazard and before Regicide

- **Player:** Hello Elena.
- **Elena:** Hey, how are you?
- **Player:** Good thanks, yourself?
- **Elena:** Not bad, let me know when you hear from King Lathas again.
- **Player:** Will do.

#### After Regicide and before Mourning's End Part I

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Elena:** Hello Player. It's been a long time. What can I do for you?
- **Player:** Just passing by.

#### After Mourning's End Part I, before speaking to Arianwyn in Lletya

- **Player:** Hey ya Elena.
- **Elena:** Hello Player, so have you found out what those Mourners are up to yet?
- **Player:** Sort of, they seem to be looking for an old temple buried deep under the mountain. This temple is meant to give them access to great magical power, but I have no idea what that is yet.
- **Elena:** Well let me know when you find out.
- **Player:** I will.

#### After Mourning's End Part I and before Plague's End

- **Player:** Hey ya Elena.
- **Elena:** Hello Player, so have you found out what those Mourners are up to yet? <ref>Dialogue ends at this point. Probably an oversight by Jagex</ref>

#### After Plague's End

- **Elena:** It's hard to believe it's all over, Player!
- **Elena:** Now that the Mourners are gone from West Ardougne, I'm going to focus on my studies.
- **Elena:** I think I'll travel to the elven lands and meet your new friends. I suspect they have an understanding of the sciences that is quite different from ours - perhaps I can learn from them.
