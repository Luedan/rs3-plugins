# Emerald Benedict

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Emerald Benedict](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AEmerald%20Benedict)
- **Letter**: `E`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### If the player speaks to Emerald Benedict while standing directly one tile from him

- **Emerald Benedict:** Got anything you don't want to lose?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - ((Bank account opens.))
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - ((Bank PIN settings open.))
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see my collection box.
    - ((Collection box opens.))
  - [Choice]: Yes thanks, and I'll keep hold of it, too.
    - **Player:** Yes thanks, and I'll keep hold of it, too.
    - (end)

### If the player speaks to Emerald Benedict while standing diagonally or over one tile from him

- **Emerald Benedict:** Good day. How many I help you?
- ((If the player has items waiting for them in the Grand Exchange:))
  - **Emerald Benedict:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - ((Bank account opens.))
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - ((Bank PIN settings open.))
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see my collection box.
    - ((Collection box opens.))
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Emerald Benedict:** This is a branch of the Bank of Gielinor. We have branches in many towns.
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: And what do you do?
        - **Emerald Benedict:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - [Choice]: Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Emerald Benedict:** Yes we did, but people kept on coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.
