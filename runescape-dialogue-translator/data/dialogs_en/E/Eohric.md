## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Eohric:** Hello. Can I help?
- **Choose an option:**
  - What is this place?
    - **Eohric:** This is Burthorpe Castle, home to His Royal Highness Prince Anlaf, heir to the throne of Asgarnia.
    - **Eohric:** No doubt you're impressed.
    - **Select an option**
      - Where is the prince?
        - **Eohric:** I cannot disclose the prince's exact whereabouts for fear of compromising his personal safety.
        - **Eohric:** But rest assured that he is working tirelessly to maintain the safety and wellbeing of Burthorpe's people.
          - (initial)
      - Goodbye.
        - (end)
  - That's quite an outfit.
    - **Eohric:** Why, thank you. I designed it myself. I've always found purple such a cheerful colour!
      - (initial)
  - Where did the council go?
    - **Eohric:** They have all returned to their respective kingdoms.
    - **Eohric:** With the war over, there's no need for a great council any more.
    - **Player:** I thought the alliance might last a bit longer.
    - **Eohric:** That doesn't seem likely.
  - Goodbye.
    - (end)
