## Dialogue Transcript

- **Guthix:** The Blade seemed almost drawn to this tiny moon. I later learned they shared a long history.
- **Guthix:** I discovered rents in the dimensional fabric...wounds in the weave between worlds that will not heal.
- **Guthix:** Those threads allowed me to find my way to many worlds that would otherwise have remained forever hidden from the Blade.
- **Guthix:** Dimensionally speaking, Zanaris is a nexus of planar threads. Its radials spiral into a network of near-infinite ravaged planes: husks of dead worlds, drained of every ounce of life.
- **Guthix:** I was alarmed by this revelation.<br>What interdimensional predator could wreak such devastation?
- **Guthix:** Was this the work of Tuska, the great beast that devoured my kin on Naragun? Could its appetite be so voracious? Or worse, was Tuska but one insatiable entity in a great swarm of devastation?
- **Guthix:** I could only hope this parasite was long since deceased. The alternative - that it lingered in the dark void between worlds, and could strike at any moment - haunted my dreams for many centuries.
- **Guthix:** ...Each gap between worlds cut by the Blade is like a rip in clothing, and they fray, as such, with each use.
- **Guthix:** I must take care to hem each edge I have carved.<br>Only the Blade keeps them stable.<br>Many pieces must be sacrificed...
- **Guthix:** Calamity!<br>I underestimated the ability of the Blade.<br>I have sundered a planet.
- **Guthix:** It was a world of divine beauty and fabulous magic, a realm of wild nature where four ruling courts of the seasons were in constant conflict, yet maintained a tumultuous balance of power.
- **Guthix:** Yet, at the core of this world of light, I witnessed a great darkness. A corrupt, ravenous presence; a bloated parasite that fed voraciously on the living, whose appetite could never be satisfied.
- **Guthix:** Arrogantly, I sought to cut that devouring corruption from its host. Instead, I destroyed the planet, shattering its fragments across the cosmos.
- **Guthix:** Worst of all, I fear my actions have released corruption from its prison.
- **Guthix:** Its tendrils entwine the threads of mortality.<br>It has taken root in pathways I have opened.
- **Guthix:** There are many places I now dare not travel, for fear that corruption may probe deeper into the cosmos, and find a new host.
- **Guthix:** I have sought out the elements of the sundered world. Some of them endure. A land of perpetual light and one of eternal winter, and a periphery where neither can endure the other.
- **Guthix:** But there are large fragments of Renmark I have been unable to locate.<br>I can only hope that some of its inhabitants may have survived my hubris.
