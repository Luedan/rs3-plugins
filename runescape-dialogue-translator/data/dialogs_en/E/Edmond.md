### Treasure Trails

- (Condition: Only the first time:)
  - **Edmond:** Can I help you?
  - **Player:** Possibly. Do you have a challenge for me?
  - **Edmond:** Yes I do, as a matter of fact.
- (receives=challenge scroll (medium))
- [Edmond has given you a challenge scroll!]

##### When talked to again

- **Edmond:** Please enter the answer to the question.
- Enter amount:<br>[Input field]
- (Condition: When given the wrong answer:)
  - **Edmond:** Is that your final answer? Because it sounds unlikely to me.
- (Condition: When given the correct answer:)
  - (removes=the clue scroll (medium) and challenge scroll (medium))
  - (Condition: If the trail continues:)
    - (receives=a new clue scroll (medium))
    - [Edmond has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (Condition: If the trail ends:)
    - (receives=reward casket (medium))
    - [You've found a casket!]
    - **Edmond:** Sounds good!
