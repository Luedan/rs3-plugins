### Easter events since 2024


#### Talk to


##### Normally

- **Easter Bunny:** Happy Easter, Player, and welcome to the Blooming Burrow!
- **Easter Bunny:** Our celebrations are in full swing - there's plenty to explore and help out with!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: cond=Before completing Great Egg-spectations:|Talk about 'Great Egg-spectations'.
    - (see|Great Egg-spectations)
  - [Choice]: cond=After completing Great Egg-spectations and before starting Corporate Egg-spionage:|Do you have a quest for me?
    - (see|Corporate Egg-spionage)
  - [Choice]: cond=With Corporate Egg-spionage in progress:|Talk about 'Corporate Egg-spionage'.
    - (see|Corporate Egg-spionage)
  - [Choice]: cond=With Corporate Egg-spionage completed:|What's new this Easter?
    - **Player:** Is there anything new to do in the Burrow this spring?
    - **Easter Bunny:** Ayah has spotted some more spies above the burrows to the east. Speak to her just past the beehives if you want to help investigate.
    - **Player:** Consider me {{Sic|intruiged}}!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What's going on here?
    - **Player:** What's going on here?
    - **Easter Bunny:** Many things! You can find my numerous nephews scattered around the burrow, each offering a different activity.
    - **Easter Bunny:** My nephew Fizzy Bunny needs some help wrangling some escaped chocolate bunnies. You can also help out my factory, the Egg Plant, by mining some chocolate, and smithing some foil.
    - **Easter Bunny:** More recently, my brother Rich has been looking for eager sailors to test out his new carrot boats, and my other brother, Sticky, is looking after the Burrow's bees.
    - **Easter Bunny:** Stewy, Gooey, and Chewy, could also use a hand... But I'd need you to help me out first before I grant you access to the factory line.
    - **Easter Bunny:** The activities will reward you some skilling experience and spring tokens. You can exchange spring tokens with Gummy Bunny, for some lovely springtime rewards!
    - **Easter Bunny:** My nephew Nougat Bunny has also set up a bunch of egg hunts for you adventurers this year! You should talk to find out more about it.
    - **Player:** Lovely! It sounds like there's a lot to help out with, then.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place? It's beautiful!
    - **Easter Bunny:** Thank you! My evergrowing family and I settled here a little while back.
    - **Easter Bunny:** You remember my old burrow and factory?
    - **Player:** It's been a while since I last visited, but yes, I do.
    - **Easter Bunny:** Well, with the rate my nephews and nieces were popping into this world at, we soon outgrew that place!
    - **Easter Bunny:** It took a lot of bunny-power, but we lugged all of our heavy factory equipment out of the old burrow and into this new, more spacious, one.
    - **Easter Bunny:** All of my niblings get a hole of their own, and I get my own office. Everyone's happy!
    - **Player:** Is there a spare hole that I could come and live in? I'd be happy to stay here all the time.
    - **Easter Bunny:** I'm afraid not. Any empty holes are reserved for future bunlings.
    - **Player:** Oh well.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - **Player:** Bye for now!
    - **Easter Bunny:** Have a happy Easter!
