### In Prifddinas


#### After Plague's End, before The Light Within

- **Player:** Hello, Eluned. What's it like being back in Prifddinas?
- **Eluned:** You might find this hard to believe... but this is my first time in the city. I love it - it's so beautiful. From my earliest memories, the city - or, at least, what remained of it - has always been held by the Iorwerth.
- **Player:** So, where were you born, if you don't mind me asking?
- **Eluned:** I was born in... huh. That's funny, I don't recall.
- **Player:** Didn't your parents ever tell you?
- **Eluned:** Oh. Er... I'm an orphan. I don't know anything about my parents either.
- **Player:** Oh, I'm sorry.
- **Eluned:** It's okay. Well, I mean, it's not okay... but I don't mind you asking. I was taken in by the Ithell and raised as one of their own. They're all the family I could ever want. I'm always happy to enchant something for you. Islwyn's taught me how to sing weapons now, too!
- *If you have any uncharged crystal teleport seeds in your inventory*:
  - **Eluned:** I understand you agreed to help us! This is good news indeed. Do you need me to re-enchant your crystal teleport seeds?
    - *(Same as above)*
- *If you do not have any uncharged crystal teleport seeds or crystal items to repair in your inventory*:
  - **Eluned:** Oh, you don't have any seeds I can't enchant, nor crystal items for me to repair. I'll be here whenever you do.
