## Dialogue Transcript


### Elder Guard at the door

*You can only talk with the Guard while wielding any type of monkey greegree.*

#### Talk when entering

- **Elder Guard:** Grrr ... What do you want?
- **Player:** I must speak with Awowogei on a subject of great import.
- **Elder Guard:** As you wish.

#### Talk when leaving

- **Elder Guard:** Grrr ... What do you want?
- **Player:** I would like to leave now.
- **Elder Guard:** As you wish.

### Elder Guard by the side

- **Elder Guard:** Move!
