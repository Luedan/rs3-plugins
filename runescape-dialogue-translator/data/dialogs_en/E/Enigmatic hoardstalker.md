## Dialogue Transcript


### While Dungeoneering


#### Talking to the Enigmatic hoardstalker

- **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** Wanderer, your movements are as uncertain as a fledgling in first-flight. Are you lost?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Do you have a riddle for me?
    - **Player:** Do you have a riddle for me?
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** I do. Here it is:
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** [Riddle.]
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** Give me the answer not in words but with an object.
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** Like all gorajo, I have no name; instead, I have a path. I have chosen to be a naabe hoardstalker.
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** My tribe required a mental trial to be given at this door. I am to stop the unclear of mind from passing into danger.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Can you open the door for me?
    - **Player:** Can you open the door for me?
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** I am capable of opening the door, ahnanai.
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** ...
    - **Player:** Well, go on then!
    - **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** Ah, you wish to skip to the end, like a wolf stalking prey. Naabe, it is not that easy. You must prove your clarity of thought with a trial.
    - (previous)

#### Get-Riddle

- (above|Riddle)

#### Solving the riddle

- **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** Your mind is as clear as a mountain lake, ahnanai! You may pass, but do so with caution. Your thinking is sharp, but so are the claws and teeth beyond.
- All the doors in the room are now unlocked.

#### Talking to the Enigmatic hoardstalker afterwards

- **Enigmatic hoardstalker:** You have proven to be wise, brah, and you would make your sachem proud. Pass in peace.
