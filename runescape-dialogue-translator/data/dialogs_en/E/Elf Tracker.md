### Task System

- (Condition: If the Easy Tirannwn achievements have been completed and the rewards not claimed:)
  - **Player:** I've completed all of the Easy tasks in the Tirannwn set.
  - **Elf Tracker:** Ah, that's a relief. You can wear the Tirannwn quiver now. I have one for you.
  - [The Elf Tracker hands you a quiver and an old lamp.]
  - **Elf Tracker:** This is a powerful quiver. It will help you through leaf traps, make fighting mourners easier as well as acting like a chime.
  - **Player:** Thanks!
  - **Elf Tracker:** Now that you've completed the Easy task set you can also sell your limestone to Meredith, claim free swamp tar in the Lletya general store and lots more.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player has lost their Tirannwn quiver 1:)
  - **Elf Tracker:** You again? How did you get on with the quiver?
  - **Player:** I lost it.
  - **Elf Tracker:** Lost? Well, it just so happens I found another. Take better care of this one.
  - [The Elf Tracker hands you a quiver.]
  - (receives=Tirannwn quiver 1)
  - (continue)
