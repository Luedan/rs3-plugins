## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Black Knights' Base

- **Isaura:** Who are you and what are you doing here?
- (Condition: Before completing the Archaeology tutorial:)
  - **Player:** I'm not sure why I'm here, but my name is Player.
  - **Isaura:** Well, Player, if you were an archaeologist, I would be asking you about collections.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: After completing the Archaeology tutorial:)
  - **Player:** My name is Player, and I'm an archaeologist! I'm here because apparently you are interested in collecting artefacts?
  - **Isaura:** Ah yes. That is correct. Excellent.
  - (continue)
- **Player:** Why do the Black Knights want to collect all this old stuff?
- **Isaura:** That is none of your concern. We are paying for your artefacts and for you not to ask questions.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Isaura:** I am Chief Requisitions Officer Isaura Wabub. You may call me precisely that.
    - **Player:** That's an unusual name. Are you sure you're a Black Knight, and not a White Knight in disguise?
    - **Isaura:** Are you suggesting the name my parents gave me is a silly pun or joke?
    - **Player:** Wait, no, sorry, that's not what I meant. I just me-
    - **Isaura:** Haha! I'm just yanking your chain. I've heard all the jokes, don't worry.
    - **Isaura:** But, yes, of course I'm a Black Knight...though I would hardly reveal it to a random archaeologist if I were a spy, now, would I?
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are you collecting?
    - **Isaura:** Take a look at my requirements, unless you're one of those types who prefers - urgh - talking...
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: View collections.
        - ((Cult-ural Appropriation interface opens.))
      - [Choice]: Talk about collections.
        - **Isaura:** Well, I am a Black Knight, so I will take anything Zamorakian! The order is very interested in whatever you might unearth at the Infernal Source. It's part of our organisation's history and heritage.
        - **Player:** Is Movario not connected with your organisation? How do you feel about him managing the site?
        - **Isaura:** As I understand it, we have worked with him in the past, but he is more of a freelancer. I also understand he is mostly in it for himself, so I would put my trust in you over him.
        - **Isaura:** The Kinshra can make it worth your while.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are your collection rewards?
    - **Isaura:** For a start, we will trade you chronotes for anything you bring to us. They hold value to archaeologists for a number of reasons.
    - **Isaura:** Though, for each of my collections, I have some specific and unique items up for trade.
    - ((If the player hasn't obtained abyssal thread yet:))
      - **Isaura:** Hmm, I suppose you can have this abyssal thread I've found no use for?
      - **Player:** Ooh, what's that?
      - **Isaura:** I think it's the stringy bits of abyssal creatures spun together.
      - **Player:** Eww, no thanks.
      - **Isaura:** Don't be so hasty! The ZMI's dark mage at the centre of the Abyss says you could use it on a giant Runecrafting essence pouch, and it would permanently stop your essence pouches from degrading.
      - **Player:** Actually, that sounds alright, thanks!
      - (continue)
    - ((If the player has obtained abyssal thread:))
      - **Isaura:** Did you use that abyssal thread to protect your rune essence pouches yet?
      - ((If the player hasn't yet made Threads of Fate:))
        - ((If the player still has the abyssal thread:))
          - **Player:** Not yet.
          - (continue)
        - ((If the player has lost the abyssal thread:))
          - **Player:** Um, do you have any more abyssal thread? I lost mine before using it.
          - **Isaura:** Fine, but don't lose this one!
          - [Menu]: Take abyssal thread from Isaura?
            - [Choice]: Yes.
              - (receives=abyssal thread)
              - (continue)
            - [Choice]: No.
              - (end)
        - ((If the player has made Threads of Fate:))
          - **Player:** Yes, I put it to good use, thanks.
          - (continue)
    - **Isaura:** I have a couple of tetracompass pieces you can have for completing my middle two collections.
    - ((If the player hasn't obtained Ariadne's Diadem yet:))
      - **Isaura:** I have a relic - some headwear called Ariadne's Diadem. It makes you better at support skills, helps you train them slightly faster.
      - (initial)
    - ((If the player has obtained Ariadne's Diadem:))
      - **Isaura:** How's that head support? Good? Good. No need to talk more about it.
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Bye.
    - **Isaura:** Dismissed!
