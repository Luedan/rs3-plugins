## Dialogue Transcript


### In Prifddinas


#### Talking to him

- **Ianto:** Hi there!
- **Player:** Hi, Ianto.
- (Condition: Only on the first time:)
  - **Ianto:** I was doing some cleaning and found some things which I'd like you to have!
  - **Player:** Oh! Really!?
  - **Ianto:** Yeah! Got some gnomeballer's gear, a large gnomeball, some coronets, a godless ceremonial set and an astromancer set!
  - **Player:** Thanks Ianto!
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Without having collected and opened a Golden cracker:)
  - **Ianto:** The makers of the world have won an award! Have a cracker, with our compliments!
  - [Menu]: Would you like a golden cracker?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - (receives=golden cracker)
      - [Ianto gives you a golden cracker.]
      - **Player:** Thanks, Ianto!
      - **Ianto:** Pull it with another person and you'll get a special surprise!
      - (continue|main)
    - [Choice]: No.
      - **Ianto:** Feel free to collect it later!
      - (continue|main)
- **Ianto:** Now, what can I do for you? Want to see my spinning plates or kites? Want to check out my range of party items? Or did you want an item back?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Spinning plates?
    - **Player:** Spinning plates?
    - **Ianto:** There was a mix-up some years back with a man who lives in Draynor - something about 'dragon plates' being confused for armour.
    - (opens=Ianto's Toy Store)
  - [Choice]: Wow, a kite!
    - **Player:** Wow, a kite!
    - **Ianto:** You're not the first to say that...
    - **Player:** Can I have one, please?
    - **Ianto:** Well, I suppose I did order more than I need... It's yours for the bargain price of 100 coins.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: That's a bargain! I'll take one.
        - **Player:** That's a bargain! I'll take one.
        - (opens=Ianto's Toy Store)
      - [Choice]: No, thanks, I don't want one.
        - **Player:** No, thanks, I don't want one.
  - [Choice]: Party items, you say?
    - **Player:** Party items, you say?
    - **Ianto:** Yes, I have all sorts of stuff - confetti, bubble markers, fireworks, firecrackers and some mugs! Take a look!
    - (opens=Ianto's Toy Store)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my items, please.
    - **Ianto:** Sure thing, let me just see what you're missing.
    - ((Diango's Item Retrieval interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: Can you check my bank for items?
    - **Ianto:** Sure thing, let me see what I can take off your hands.
    - **Ianto:** I'll look through items that I will hold onto, items that could go into a costume room in your house and junk items.
    - ((Item Cleanup interface opens.))

#### Redeem code

- **Player:** I'd like to redeem an item code, please.
- **Ianto:** Of course. Here you go.
- ((Redemption code interface opens.))
