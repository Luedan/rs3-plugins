# Impling collector

- **Letter**: `I`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Menaphos


##### With less than tier 4 reputation

- **Impling Collector:** Have you seen any implings around here?
- **Player:** I haven't.
- **Impling Collector:** That's a shame. I hear if you work hard in Menaphos you'll start to find more of them.
- Reaching tier 4 in Menaphos city reputation will unlock a chance to roll for implings and Guthix butterflies while skilling in Menaphos.

##### With tier 4 or greater reputation

- **Impling Collector:** Have you seen any implings around here?
- **Player:** Yeah, I keep seeing them while I work round the city.
- **Impling Collector:** Of course! If you disrupt their environment they're bound to appear. Sometimes you don't find the ones you're looking for though - if you're interested I could teach you how to stop those implings and butterflies noticing you?
- [Menu]: Toggle impling and butterfly settings?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - *(Gathering Settings opens)*
  - [Choice]: No.
    - **Impling Collector:** Okay, see you around.
