## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock


#### Normally

- **Iffie:** Hello, dearie! Were you wanting to collect a costume, or is there something else I can do for you today?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I've come for a costume.
    - **Iffie:** I may be able to find your lost ghostly costumes, or I have my costume store. Which do you want?
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: One of my ghostly costumes.
        - ''(Reclaim interface opens.)
      - [Choice]: A costume from your store.
        - **Iffie:** Some of these costumes even come with a free emote!
        - **Iffie:** Just buy one piece of the mime or zombie costumes and I'll show you all of the relevant moves.
        - (opens=Iffie's Random Costume Shop)
  - [Choice]: Aren't you selling anything?
    - **Player:** Aren't you selling anything?
    - **Iffie:** Oh, yes, but only costumes. Thessalia sells some other clothes and runs the makeover mage service.
  - [Choice]: I just came for a chat.
    - **Player:** I just came for a chat.
    - **Iffie:** Oh, I'm sorry, but I'll never get my knitting done if I stop for a chit-chat with every young [lad/lass] who wanders through the shop!
    - **Iffie:** You should go and see my sister, Elsie, upstairs in Varrock Church; she's always ready for a good natter.
  - [Choice]: cond=If the player is eligible for the classic cape:|I'm here to talk about what you are knitting.
    - **Player:** I'm here to talk about what you are knitting.
    - **Iffie:** This? I've been knitting all sorts of things, mainly things that remind me of the good ol' days.
    - (Condition: Condition unknown:)
      - **Iffie:** You might be younger than me, but perhaps you want a cape that reminds you of the old days too?
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: Condition unknown:)
      - **Iffie:** And I see you are a veteran of the old days - would you like a cape that reminds you of the old days?
      - (continue)
    - **Iffie:** My knitting comes at a price - it's a fair bit of effort and materials. Does 50,000 coins sound okay to you, m'dear?
    - [Menu]: Pay 50,000 for a Classic cape?
      - [Choice]: I'll take one!
        - **Player:** I'll take one!
        - (Condition: If the player does not have enough coins:)
          - **Iffie:** That's good, but you don't have enough money for the cape.
        - (Condition: If the player does not have space in backpack:)
          - **Iffie:** That's good, but you've got no space in your inventory for the cape and hood.
        - (Condition: If the player has enough coins:)
          - (removes=50,000 coins) (receives=classic cape and classic hood)
          - **Iffie:** Thanks. Enjoy!
      - [Choice]: No, thanks.
        - **Player:** No, thanks.

    - **Iffie:** It takes quite a lot though, dearie, depending on the costume you want.
    - **Iffie:** You could also exchange ghostweave for special Hallowe'en chocolates that have interesting effects.
    - **Iffie:** After the event you can bring any unused ghostweave back to me and I will exchange it for Crafting XP.
    - *(During the main promotion:)*
      - **Iffie:** Perhaps later I'll buy any ghostweave you've got left, but you should try making costumer or chocolates with it now.
    - *(After the main promotion:)*
