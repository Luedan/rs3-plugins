## Dialogue Transcript


### Senntisten entrance


#### Without having completed City of Senntisten

- **Intern Jimmy:** You're free to access Senntisten through here, but if you want to learn more about it, you should talk to Dr Nabanik. I believe he's up in the old Guildmaster's office.

#### Having completed City of Senntisten

- **Intern Jimmy:** You are cleared for access. Feel free to enter the city at any time.

### Pontifex shadow ring teleport (before [[Update:This Week In RuneScape: Elder God Wars Dungeon - The Nodon Front|The Nodon Front update]])

- (The player teleports next to the ancient door)
- **Player:** Wait, it doesn't even teleport me into the cathedral? This ring is lame.
- **Intern Jimmy:** Oh, yeah, about that... We're working to create an exception so your ring can teleport you past the wards straight into the cathedral. But it's going to take us at least a few weeks. I promise we'll make that ring more useful for you or my name's not James.
- **Player:** Don't you go by 'Jimmy'?
- **Intern Jimmy:** Look, gimme a chance here, we're working on it... Please, be patient.

### Senntisten Dig Site

- **Jimmy:** Hello.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Why are you the site manager here?
    - **Jimmy:** Well, since Senntisten is effectively your discovery, I would say you're the site manager really.
    - **Jimmy:** But since you've got a lot of stuff on your plate already, I'm here to assist you as best I can.
    - **Jimmy:** I'll handle the minutiae of the site management and you can focus on uncovering ancient secrets from the Second Age.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What is this dig site about?
    - **Jimmy:** This is the ancient city of Senntisten, the centre of power for the vast Zarosian Empire of the Second Age.
    - **Jimmy:** Right now it's a war zone. With the forces of the Elder Gods gathering against us, it's hard to perform the delicate work that an archaeological dig requires.
    - **Jimmy:** But there's still so much to discover here! We can try to understand what life was like for the citizens of the Empire.
    - **Jimmy:** We can perhaps learn about the rituals and trappings of the ancient Zarosian religion.
    - **Jimmy:** We might even uncover forgotten techniques and secrets to improve our daily lives.
    - **Jimmy:** The possibilities are endless.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I want to talk about mysteries.
    - (continue|Mysteries)
  - [Choice]: Bye.
    - **Jimmy:** See you around, boss.
    - (end)
