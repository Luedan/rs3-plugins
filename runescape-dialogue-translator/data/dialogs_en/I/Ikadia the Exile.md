## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley Dungeon

- **Ikadia the Exile:** Praise Zamorak for conflict, friend. Will you not test the strength of my chaos druids? I fear they grow weak and deserve to be cut down.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Ikadia the Exile:** I am Ikadia the Exile, leader of these druids. I wear the title in honour of my ancestor Urval, who was exiled by the druids above for preaching the truth about Guthix and Zamorak.
    - **Ikadia the Exile:** Zamorak is Guthix's most perfect incarnation! He brings balance to the world through chaos!
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Haven't you heard? Guthix is dead!
        - **Player:** Haven't you heard? Guthix is dead!
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** Of course I've heard, you insolent little man!
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** Like all druids, I felt the shiver run through the world. Unlike them, however, I rejoiced! Guthix has shed his old form, just as Urval foretold. Now he returns in the form of Zamorak, to bring balance through chaos!
        - (other)
      - [Choice]: Saradomin defeated Zamorak at the Battle of Lumbridge.
        - **Player:** Saradomin defeated Zamorak at the Battle of Lumbridge.
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** Fool! You see only the surface and are blind to the true meaning!
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** When Saradomin and Zamorak began to fight, Lumbridge became a war zone, did it not? A place of conflict and destruction - a place of chaos!
        - (other)
      - [Choice]: That's an interesting interpretation.
        - **Player:** That's an interesting interpretation.
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** I'm glad to meet someone who's willing to think independently, rather than blindly accept popular beliefs.
      - [Choice]: Your beliefs are ridiculous.
        - **Player:** Your beliefs are ridiculous.
        - **Ikadia the Exile:** I am not here to win converts. Believe what you will.
  - [Choice]: I'd rather test *your* strength.
    - **Player:** I'd rather test *your* strength.
    - (Condition: Before Birthright of the Dwarves:)
      - **Ikadia the Exile:** Maybe someday, little one - when I have a reason to fight you.
    - (Condition: After Birthright of the Dwarves:)
      - **Ikadia the Exile:** You have tested my strength once before and defeated me. It was a worthy combat!
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
