    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Group Ironman allows you to enjoy the Ironman experience as a group of 2 to 5 friends. You know, when you want to get away from it all, but do it with someone.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** As you work together and grow, you can complete Journey Achievements.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Journey achievements are a set of objectives for the group to complete as individuals and as a group - you will need to level your skills, complete quests, kill bosses and explore all Gielinor has to offer.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Completing Journey Achievements will unlock rewards such as group storage, an upgradable cosmetic armour and unique pose emotes.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Or you can chart your own path and set your own goals as you experience a brand new type of adventure. The choice, as they say, is yours.

  - [Choice]: How do I create or join a group?
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** It's quite simple really! There are two options: you can either start your own group or be invited to an existing group.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** If you want to create your own group, simply click the 'Create Group' option and then choose which ruleset you would like your group to be.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Once that's decided, you can then invite anyone you want to join your group. Each group can be between 2 and 5 players in size.
    - You can invite players to your group by clicking the {{Contrast|c=#036|t='Invite to Group'}} button and typing the player's name, or by right-clicking players and selecting {{Contrast|c=#036|t='Invite'}}.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Each group can have their own unique name so pick one that best represents you.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Then you're ready to go! Confirm your group, and head over the bridge to the portal which will take you to Lumbridge to begin your adventure!
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Don't forget to collect a set of armour from the stand next to me; it will provide you some initial protection from any enemies you may face.
    - (previous|initialOpts)
  - [Choice]: What are the different group rulesets?
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** There are two different rulesets that you can choose from: Competitive and Regular.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Competitive is a strict ruleset where you can only play with your group; this means all your progress and accomplishments come from within the group only.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** In Competitive, a group can only be formed with those fresh to Gielinor so you can set out as a group to prove yourself as the best of the best.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Regular is a more relaxed ruleset that allows you to play with others outside your group, and there are no restrictions on leaving and joining other groups in the future.
    - **Iroh, the Group Ironman tutor:** Pick whichever mode best represents your group. Competitive groups can choose to become a Regular group later; however a Regular group can never become a Competitive group once the group has been finalised.
