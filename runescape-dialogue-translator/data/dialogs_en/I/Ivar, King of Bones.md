## Dialogue Transcript


### Overworld


#### Talk to

- **Ivar, King of Bones:** I am Ivar, King of Bones! Who dares challenge my throne?
- **Player:** Hi, I'm Player.
- **Ivar, King of Bones:** I don't need to know your name. I learn what I need through the fight!
- **Ivar, King of Bones:** I serve the Lord of Bones, but none are highter in the mortal realm than me.
- **Player:** Wait. Wouldn't it be the other way around? Lords serve kings.
- **Ivar, King of Bones:** Do not try to trick me, squishy one! Come, test your bones against my might!
- **Ivar, King of Bones:** Defeat me, and you might earn my club or crown.
- **Player:** I don't think they'd fit me like you have them! And what if I lose?
- **Ivar, King of Bones:** Then your bones will find a place of honour in my temple!
