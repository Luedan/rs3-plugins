## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before completing the Secrets of Amberfell quest

- (see|Secrets of Amberfell)

#### After completing the Secrets of Amberfell quest

- **Inanna:** Greetings, Player.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about Amberfell.
    - **Player:** How is Amberfell faring?
    - **Inanna:** For now, it is well.
    - **Inanna:** I remain close to reassure them. While I am here, they are safe.
    - **Inanna:** Sorrel's transgressions have been forgiven and the bonds to Wendlewick have been mended. They are all stronger for it.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Ask about the Sanguine.
    - **Player:** Has the Sanguine spread closer?
    - **Inanna:** Yes. Each day, it consumes more of the east.
    - **Inanna:** The monsters that attacked us at the temple were a vanguard. A sign of what is to come.
    - **Inanna:** Amberfell itself is safe for now. We will have to take the fight east soon, though, if we are to survive.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Ask about the Moonrise temple.
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about the temple to the north of here?
    - **Inanna:** It is called Moonrise.
    - **Inanna:** Once, long ago, it was a site of great power. This was before I came to be. I do not know what it hides.
    - **Inanna:** I seldom travel there myself. It is watched over by another. One I wish to avoid.
    - **Inanna:** Perhaps it will soon reveal its secrets to you.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Attempt to pet.
    - **Player:** May I?
    - **Inanna:** You may.
    - **Inanna:** Briefly.
    - {{Qact|The player pets Inanna.}}
    - **Inanna:** You have my trust, Player. Do not squander it.
  - [Choice]: cond=Without the Anzagar pet following|Goodbye.
    - **Inanna:** May the moonlight favour you.
  - [Choice]: cond=With the Anzagar pet following|Introduce Anzagar.
    - **Player:** I thought you might like to meet Anzagar.
    - **Anzagar:** ...
    - **Inanna:** I sensed it. But I could scarcely believe it.
    - **Inanna:** My brother has been freed from his eternal torment. You have succeeded where I never could.
    - **Inanna:** Thank you, my friend.
    - **Inanna:** Your fate is a blessed one, little Anzagar. You will grow to be a mighty colossus one day.
    - **Anzagar:** ...
    - **Anzagar:** Player...
    - **Player:** He spoke!
    - **Inanna:** He is rather fond of you and the adventures you have shared, I see. Do not squander this trust. For a colossus' favour is precious beyond measure.
    - **Inanna:** Go now, with my blessing. And may moonlight forever guide your path.
