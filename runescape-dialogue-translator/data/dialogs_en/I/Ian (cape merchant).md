## Dialogue Transcript

- **Ian:** Hello there! Are you interested in buying one of my special team capes?
- What do team capes do?
  - If you and your friends all wear the same team cape, you'll be less likely to attack your friends by accident, and you'll find it easier to attack everyone else.
  - They're very useful in the Wilderness and other player-vs-player combat areas where you might come across friends you don't want to harm. So would you like to buy one?
- Yes please!
- I'd like to access my bank please.
- No thanks.
