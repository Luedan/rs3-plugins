## Dialogue Transcript


### In the King's Axe Inn in Keldagrim

- **Inn Keeper:** Welcome to the King's Axe inn! What can I help you with?
- **Player:** Can I have some beer please?
- **Inn Keeper:** Go to the bar downstairs. I only deal with residents.
- **Player:** Residents? People live here?
- (Condition: Before The Giant Dwarf:)
  - **Inn Keeper:** No, just guests that stay the night. Like the gnome delegation that's come in recently. I believe they've come to talk to the Red Axe.
  - **Player:** Where have they come from, do you know?
  - **Inn Keeper:** That's a rather impertinent question, one I certainly don't ask of my guests.
- (Condition: After The Giant Dwarf:)
  - **Inn Keeper:** No, just guests that stay the night.
