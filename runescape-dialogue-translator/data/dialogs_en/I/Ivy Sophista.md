## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley


#### Talking to her normally

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: cond=After While Guthix Sleeps|Ask about 'While Guthix Sleeps'.
    - **Player:** Hello Ivy, how's everything going?
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Oh Player! I've heard of your exploits from Thaerisk...not everything of course, but he was saying how incredibly brave you were during the whole mission! Well done!
  - [Choice]: Ask about the chest.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** That chest? Oh, it's simply here for safekeeping. We keep it here as a reward for determined treasure-seekers who find both key halves and assemble them into the crystal key which can open the chest!
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Also...I have heard rumours of a great treasure even harder to obtain than this one. Spread around the world are fragments of the elusive crystal triskelion key.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Find them, and use the key to locate the hidden chest, and the treasure is yours!
  - [Choice]: Ask about herself.
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Ah, well, I'm keeping an eye on things. Making sure nothing unexpected happens.
    - **Player:** That's reassuring.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** One day I hope it will be.

#### During the fifth task of The Need for Mead event

- **Player:** Hello, Ivy! I need to tell you something.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I was forced to steal a book from you.
    - **Player:** I'm really sorry but I was forced to steal a book from you by someone named Leslie.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Ah. I used to know Leslie very well. I'm not surprised to hear they were the one to take it.
    - **Ivy Sophista:**Have you brought it back for me?
    - **Player:** Sadly not. I believe it's somewhere at the bottom of the River Lum now.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** They destroyed it?
    - **Player:** Maybe you can fish it out of the river?
    - **Ivy Sophista:**That's highly unlikely.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** I won't stand for this! Thank you for telling me though.
  - [Choice]: I know where your missing book is.
    - **Player:** I was in Lumbridge earlier and I witnessed someone throwing a book into the River Lum.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** And why are you telling me about this?
    - **Player:** They shouted your name as they threw it, so I thought it might have something to do with you.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Strange. Give me a second.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** ...
    - **Ivy Sophista:** NO!
    - **Ivy Sophista:** You may be right. It looks like an extremely rare book is missing from my collection.
    - **Ivy Sophista:** Thank you for telling me. I'll send a group there right away to try and find it. It's worth an awful lot.
