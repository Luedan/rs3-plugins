### In Prifddinas, after The Light Within


#### Talk to

- **Player:** Hello Islwyn.
- **Islwyn:** Well met Player.
- **Player:** Where's Eluned? Isn't she normally here?
- (Condition: If the player received the Eluned's letter and has not yet destroyed the letter:)
  - **Islwyn:** The letter should cover everything.
- (Condition: If the player has not received, or has destroyed Eluned's letter:)
  - **Islwyn:** Yes ... she wanted me to give you this. It explains everything.
  - [Islwyn hands you a letter from Eluned.]
  - **Islwyn:** Prifddinas is quite the sight. It certainly lives up to expectations.
  - **Player:** Have you spoken to your grandfather yet?
  - **Islwyn:** No … not yet… I'm trying to work out what to say.
  - **Player:** He probably feels the same maybe you should … er … both fail to talk together?
  - **Islwyn:** That … yeah … that really didn’t make much sense.
  - **Player:** Indeed not … so …crystal singing?
  - **Islwyn:** Show me a crystal seed or crystal item, and I'll see what I can do with it.
