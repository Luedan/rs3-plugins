- **Letter**: `Q`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Royal Trouble

- **Queen Sigrid:** Hello.
- **Player:** Er...Hello, Your Majesty.
- **Queen Sigrid:** Have you come for a particular reason? I am a busy Queen, you know. I had to attend the opening of a new orphanage this morning.
- **Player:** Yes, I...
- **Queen Sigrid:** And this afternoon I shall be receiving the Embassador of Karamja and accompanying him on his week-long tour of Etceteria.
- **Player:** Er, I...
- **Queen Sigrid:** Then this evening we shall be putting on a grand ball! All of the kingdom's nobility will be present!
- **Player:** If I m...
- **Queen Sigrid:** Oh, who am I kidding...nothing ever happens here. I always wanted to be a princess when I was a girl. If I'd been told it would be like this, I wouldn't have bothered.
- **Player:** Your Majesty!
- **Queen Sigrid:** Yes? Did you want something?
- **Player:** ...actually, no.

### After Royal Trouble

- **Queen Sigrid:** Thank you for all your help, [Fremennik Name]. It's good to know that the Regent of Miscellania is willing to help his neighbours.
- **Player:** You're welcome, Your Majesty.

### After Blood Runs Deep

- **Queen Sigrid:** Thank you for everything, [Fremennik name]. It was hard to move out of my beloved castle, but worth it to be with my husband.

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Queen Sigrid:** Ah, this puzzle is yours, I take it?
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Queen Sigrid has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Queen Sigrid:** Is that the best you can do? Try harder!

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Queen Sigrid has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Queen Sigrid:** Great; there is the reward.
