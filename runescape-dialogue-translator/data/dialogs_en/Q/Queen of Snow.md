## Dialogue Transcript


### In Christmas Village


#### Talk to after Cold Front


##### When the Christmas Village is inactive

- **Queen of Snow:** The snow tournament draws to a close. I look forward to seeing you again next Wintumber, noble traveller!

##### When the Christmas Village is active

- **Queen of Snow:** Thank you for helping me enjoy the 'great' snow tournament for what it is, and take the festivities less seriously.
- [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=initial-options
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Queen of Snow:** I am the Queen of Snow. The all-wise Guthix placed me in command of the forces of snow and ice that dominate the winter of each year.
    - **Queen of Snow:** To what extent, as monarch of this land, I remain my mother's daughter and successor to my sister is an interesting philosophical question.
    - **Queen of Snow:** Does the head fit the crown or the crown fit the head?
    - **Queen of Snow:** In becoming the personification of winter, have I ceased to be a young ice sprite named Lumi?
    - **Queen of Snow:** Ultimately, the question is irrelevant. I am what I am, and I shall remain irrevocably transformed, whether by the weight of responsibility or the role I inhabit, until I, too, return to snow or vacate the throne.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=who-are-you
      - [Choice]: What is an ice sprite?
        - **Queen of Snow:** Ice sprites trace our lineage to the King of Roses and Queen of Snow. Direct descendants are usually potential candidates to perpetuate the monarchy.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Given their political importance, ice sprite princesses and princes are typically not allowed to leave the Land of Snow unless an appeal is granted.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Ice sprites are mortal, like you humans, but longer-lived.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Reductively speaking, we can also play with cold and water.
        - (previous|who-are-you)
      - [Choice]: You've met the god of balance?
        - **Queen of Snow:** Well, not personally.<br>But so my father declared (with much conviction) when I ascended the throne at my coronation.
        - **Queen of Snow:** I am obligated to perpetuate that declaration.<br>Such beliefs can be comfortably reassuring.
        - (previous|who-are-you)
      - [Choice]: I have a different question...
        - (previous2|initial-options)
  - [Choice]: What is the Land of Snow?
    - **Queen of Snow:** The Land of Snow is where snowmen dance and never melt, celebrating winter all year long.
    - **Queen of Snow:** This land was created aeons ago by Guthix, as part of his balancing of your world.
    - **Queen of Snow:** The cold of the Land of Snow counteracts the perpetual heat of its counterpart, the Land of Sunlight, ensuring the correct and comfortable balance of hot and cold in Gielinor.
    - **Queen of Snow:** Here in the Land of Snow, it is winter all year 'round, with bright clear stars overhead and crisp snow underfoot. To us, there is no 'last Christmas' or 'next Christmas'. It is always Christmas.
    - **Queen of Snow:** This is the realm where winter weather, and the very idea of snow, originates. During winter, your world is aligned to the Land of Snow; close enough to visit, for a short time.
    - **Queen of Snow:** Every visitor to the Land of Snow, every snowball thrown and snowman made, sends echoes of Christmas cheer through every world.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=land-of-snow
      - [Choice]: Tell me about snow and snow imps.
        - **Queen of Snow:** When your world and the Land of Snow are close, I send my snow imps to Gielinor to share the gift of snow.
        - **Queen of Snow:** The snow my imps bring you has a little of the Land of Snow's magic... amplified by every heart the snow touches.
        - **Queen of Snow:** All snow that falls in other worlds is a reflection of the eternal substance of the Land of Snow.
        - (previous|land-of-snow)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about snowmen.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Snowmen are the eternal substance of snow, shaped into the form of man. When you build a snowman, you form an instance of a snowman in the Land of Snow; a crude and temporary copy. A vessel for an idea.
        - **Queen of Snow:** On Gielinor, winter is but a season in your year. When a snowman melts, its essence returns to the Land of Snow.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Here, winter never ends. Snow never melts. This joining of form and essence are eternal. So in the Land of Snow, snowmen are immortal.
        - (previous|land-of-snow)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the snow tournament.|land-of-snow
        - **Queen of Snow:** Traditionally, the Land of Snow's midwinter celebration features a snowman tournament.
        - **Queen of Snow:** A great snow tournament, where immortal snow warriors, snow rangers and snow mages battle to see which is best.
        - **Queen of Snow:** Since each style has counter-balanced advantage against and weakness to another, the whole arrangement honours Guthix.
        - (previous|land-of-snow)
      - [Choice]: I have a different question...
        - (previous2|initial-options)
  - [Choice]: Happy Christmas, your majesty.
    - {{Qact|The player bows (mirroring the farewell dialogue option from the Queen of Snow's debut in the 2007 Christmas event).}}
    - **Queen of Snow:** Happy Christmas, noble visitor.
