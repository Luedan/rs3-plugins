## Dialogue Transcript


### When trying to access the store with something equipped

- **Qaseem:** I don't feel comfortable helping you try on shoes when you are wielding something. Please remove what you are holding first.

### When trying to access the store with nothing equipped and a feet override active

- **Qaseem:** You'll have to take what you're wearing on your feet off first.
- You will need to remove anything worn on your feet and also disable any feet overrides before you can change your shoes.
