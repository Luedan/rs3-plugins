## Dialogue Transcript


### In Pollnivneach


#### Before The Feud

- **Hakeem the Mayor:** Welcome adventurer to the town of Pollniveach, the gateway to Menaphos and Al Kharid. My name is Hakeem and I'm the mayor of this town. I hope you enjoy your stay here.
- **Player:** Thank you. That is one of the warmest welcomes I've ever had! People generally treat travelling adventurers with suspicion.
- **Hakeem the Mayor:**All are welcome here. Now more than ever I suppose.
- **Player:** What do you mean by that?
- **Hakeem the Mayor:** There's trouble in town, and a lot of villagers have left as a result. Things are looking dire.

#### After The Feud

- **Hakeem the Mayor:** Thank you once again. Have you spoken to Ali Morrisane yet?
- **Player:** Yes, I spoke to him already. That tourism idea of yours was really quite good.
- **Hakeem the Mayor:** Well not to be immodest, but it was all my idea. Well I have things to do, good luck adventurer.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about the Task System.
    - (Condition: If the Hard desert achievements have been completed and the rewards not claimed:)
      - **Player:** I've completed all of the Hard Tasks in the Desert set.
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** So you have. I have a special amulet kept aside for you.
      - (receives=Desert amulet 3 and an antique lamp)
      - [The Mayor hands you an amulet and a lamp.]
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** There you go. It's a pretty trinket, but I no longer need it. You'll find it helps you when - ah - exploring tombs.
      - **Player:** Thanks! What else does it do?
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** Well, the mages in the arena will give you further discounts. I've had a word with Ayesha and Zahra and they'll now protect your cacti for you, so you should get better harvests, too.
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** Your work in the desert has also made the phoenix near the dominion tower more docile - you should be able to gather multiple feathers from it with more ease.
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** Last, but not least, you may find that the Keris inflicts vicious blows more often while fighting kalphites, as the amulet helps you become more dexterous with it.
      - **Player:** That sounds good.
      - **Hakeem the Mayor:** There's more, just check the list.
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player has lost their desert amulet 3:)
      - **Hakeem The Mayor:** Ah, the amulet-wearer! How are you finding it?
      - **Player:** I lost it.
      - **Hakeem The Mayor:** Oh, no!
      - **Hakeem The Mayor:** Never mind, I have another. Here...
      - [Hakeem the Mayor hands you an amulet.]
      - (receives=Desert amulet 3)
      - (continue)

        - **Hakeem the Mayor:** Some also give items that will help complete other achievements, and many count as progress towards the set for the area they're in.
        - [Menu]: Choose an option:
          - [Choice]: Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
            - **Player:** Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:**  For completing the Desert set, you are presented with an amulet.
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:** This amulet will become increasingly useful as you complete greater difficulty levels.
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:** When you are presented with your rewards, you will be told of their uses.
            - You may also see what rewards a set item provides by hovering over the Rewards icon for its Exploration Achievement set in the Achievements interface.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: How do I claim these rewards?
            - **Player:** How do I claim the rewards?
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:** You need to complete all Tasks within a particular difficulty set, then you can claim your reward.
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:** Some of the Desert Tasks are simple, some will require certain skill levels, and some might require quests to be started or completed.
            - **Hakeem the Mayor:** To claim your Desert Task rewards, speak to Hassan in Al Kharid Palace, Zahur in Nardah, the Golem in Uzer, or myself.
