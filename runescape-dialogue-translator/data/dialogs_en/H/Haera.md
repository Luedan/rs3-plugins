## Dialogue Transcript


### In western Keldagrim

- **Haera:** What are you doing in our home?
- **Player:** I just came to ask you a question.
- **Haera:** Hegir, why did you let this human into our home?
- **Hegir:** I didn't let [him/her] in, [he/she] let [himself/herself] in!
- **Haera:** Now what do you want, human? Speak up, speak up!
- **Player:** I ehm, just wanted some information.
- **Haera:** Well go ask someone else instead of barging into people's homes!
- **Hegir:** There's no need to be rude to the human, Haera dear.
- **Haera:** Now don't you dear me. I'll do whatever I please.
- **Player:** I think this is my cue to leave...
