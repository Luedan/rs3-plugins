### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Who are you, and what are you doing here?
- **Hollowtoof:** Gimme yer stuff!
- **Player:** You know, I don't think I will.
- **Hollowtoof:** We is raidin'! {{Sic|so}} you gimme yer stuff or I'll stick yer...in da gizzards!
- *If the player has a low combat level:*
  - **Player:** Hey, you can't talk to me like that!
  - **Hollowtoof:** Gimme stuff or shove off!
- *If the player has a high combat level:*
  - **Player:** No, you give me YOUR stuff. {{Sic|&nbsp;}}This is a raid!
  - **Hollowtoof:** Ulp! Um, sorry dere boss. Here, here, I get you some stuff.
  - *If the player has not claimed all of the raider items and does not own the raider items that are unclaimed:*
    - *If the player has not claimed the raider chainbody:*
      - **Hollowtoof:** Here, take armour! It metal, and tough!
    - *If the player has not claimed the raider axe:*
      - **Hollowtoof:** Here, take axe! It's good! I nicked it ages ago!
    - *If the player has not claimed the raider shield:*
      - **Hollowtoof:** Here, take shield! Just don't punch my face!
    - **Hollowtoof:** Dat's it! I got nuffin else!
    - **Player:** Well, I suppose this will do for now.
  - *If the player has claimed all of the raider items or owns the raider items that are unclaimed:*
    - **Hollowtoof:** I've got nuffin for you! Nuffin! Just don't kill me!
    - **Player:** Well, I'll let you off...this time!
