## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue after Swan Song

- **Player:** Hiya Hermie!
- **Herman Caranos:** Ah, Player! How's the fishing going?
- **Player:** Still working on it. How's the Colony?
- **Herman Caranos:** Flourishing, now that the colonists are coming back! Soon all the finest restaurants will be serving monkfish!
- **Herman Caranos:** It's a pity those skeletons you brought here haven't gone away again, though. They're in the dormitories, which is rather annoying! So please take a little time to kick 'em around a bit.
- **Herman Caranos:** Oh, and please don't call me Hermie.
- **Player:** Whatever you like, Hermie!
