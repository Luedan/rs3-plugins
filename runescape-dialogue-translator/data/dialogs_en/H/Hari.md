## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Hari:** Hello.
- Choose an option
  - Who are you?
    - **Hari:** My name is Hari.
    - **Player:** And what are you doing here Hari?
    - **Hari:** Like most people who come here to Edgeville, I am here to seek adventure in the Wilderness. I found a secret underground river that will take me quite a long way north.
    - **Player:** Underground river? Where does it come out?
    - **Hari:** It comes out in a pond located deep in the Wilderness.
    - **Hari:** I had to find a very special type of canoe to get me up the river though, would you like to know more?
    - Select an option
      - Yes
        - {{anchor|teach}}**Hari:** It's really quite simple to make. Just walk down to that tree on the bank and chop it down. When you have done that you can shape the log further with your hatchet to make a canoe.
        - *If the player has a woodcutting level higher than 57:*
          - **Hari:** Your skills rival mine friend. You will certainly be able to build a Waka.
          - **Player:** A Waka?  that?
          - **Hari:** A Waka is an invention of my people, it's an incredible strong and fast canoe and will carry you safely to any destination on the river.
          - **Player:** Any destination?
          - **Hari:** Yes, you can take a waka north through the underground portion of this river. It will bring you out at a pond in the heart of the Wilderness. Be careful up there, many have lost more than their lives in that dark and twisted place.
        - *If the player has a woodcutting level between 42 and 57:*
          - **Hari:** You seem to be an accomplished woodcutter. You will easily be able to make a Stable Dugout They are reliable enough to get you anywhere on this river, except to the Wilderness of course. Only a Waka can take you there.
          - **Player:** A Waka? What's that?
          - **Hari:** Come and ask me when you have improved your skills as a woodcutter.
        - *If the player has a woodcutting level between 27 and 42:*
          - **Hari:** You are an average woodcutter. You should be able to make a Dugout canoe quite easily. It will take you 2 stops along the river.
          - **Player:** Can I take a dugout canoe to reach the Wilderness?
          - **Hari:** You would never make it there alive.
          - **Player:** Best not to try then.
        - *If the player has a woodcutting level between 12 and 27:*
          - **Hari:** I can sense you're still a novice woodcutter, you will only be able to make a log canoe at present.
          - **Player:** Is that good?
          - **Hari:** A log will take you one stop along the river. But you won't be able to travel into the Wilderness on it.
        - *If the player has a woodcutting level lower than 12:*
          - **Hari:**  Well, you don't look like you have the skill to make a canoe. You need to have at least level 12 woodcutting. Once you are able to make a canoe it makes travel along the river much quicker!
