# Hobnob

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hobnob](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHobnob)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Hobnob:** What do you want, human?
- **Player:** Who are you?
- **Hobnob:** I'm Hobnob - mightiest of the hobgoblins. You must be Zanik's trained attack beast.
- **Hobnob:** Zanik must be a really jammie dodger to have a human stand in for her.
- **Player:** That's rich talk coming from someone with such crumby armour.
- **Hobnob:** I'm ready to take every down. This tournament will be a slam dunk.
- **Player:** Well if we meet in battle, I'll chew you up and spit you out.
- **Hobnob:** I'll crunch you into cream!
- **Player:** When I break your legs, you'll need wagon wheels to get around!
- **Hobnob:** We'll see!
