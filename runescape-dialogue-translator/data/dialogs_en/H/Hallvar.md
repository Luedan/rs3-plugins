# Hallvar

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hallvar](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHallvar)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Hallvar:** Greetings.
- **Player:** What are you here for then?
- **Hallvar:** Well, as you're a great explorer, I guess I can tell you. I exchange ore for certificates.
- **Player:** So, if I hand you pieces of ore, you'll give me...
- **Hallvar:** Paper.
- **Player:** Okay.
- **Hallvar:** Would you like me to look through your pack for suitable ores?
- *Select an option*
  - Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
      - ''(if the player does not have any suitable ores in their inventory)
        - **Hallvar:** You don't have any suitable ores.
        - (end)
      - ''(if the player has suitable ores in their inventory)
        - *(the ore in the inventory is replaced with notes)*
        - **Hallvar:** There you go.
        - (end)
  - No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - (end)
