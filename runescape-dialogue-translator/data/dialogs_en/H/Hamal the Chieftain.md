  - **Hamal the Chieftain:** You have my thanks, in any case.
  - (continue)

  - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Greetings once again, honourable [Fremennik name/Player].

- **Hamal the Chieftain:** No, I've decided to set up a permanent camp here.
- **Hamal the Chieftain:** There is no need to move now with our new supply of food. Also, there is a protective spirit looking after us now.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Well, I must be off again.
    - **Player:** That's all very interesting, but I must be off again.
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Safe journey, outerlander.
  - [Choice]: What exactly happened to your daughter?
    - **Player:** What exactly happened to your daughter?
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** I cannot exactly say. I believe some of her spirit must have fused with the pool she loved so much. She is no longer my daughter, but the daughter of the mountain.
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** We will stay here, under her protection. With our new food supplies we will be able to stay here for many years more.
    - (initial|options)
  - [Choice]: I thought you didn't believe in magic?
    - **Player:** I thought you didn't believe in magic?
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** How little do you understand our ways. Of course we believe in magic, we merely believe it is not for the living to wield it.
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Many spirits are connected with the earth or the water. There is the Draugen, for instance, a {{Sic|malovelent}} spirit.
    - (Condition: Without the Fremennik Trials completed:)
      - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Then there is an ancient water spirit that the Fremennik call the Fossegrimen.
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: With the Fremennik Trials completed:)
      - **Player:** Oh yeah, I ran into him.
      - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Have you? We do not approve of the Fremennik hunt, the Draugen should be left alone, evil though it is.
      - **Hamal the Chieftain:** And perhaps you have also come across the Fossegrimen?
      - **Player:** Yes, I have.
      - (continue)
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** Now that is a spirit that is quite different from the Draugen... she is, perhaps, not entirely dissimilar to the Mountain Dottir.
    - **Player:** The mountain what?
    - **Hamal the Chieftain:** You would call her mountain daughter. Asleif is no longer my daughter, she belongs to the mountains now.
    - (previous|options)
