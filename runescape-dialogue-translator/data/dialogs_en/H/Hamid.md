## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue, in the Worker District of Menaphos

- **Player:** Hi!
- **Hamid:** Hello, traveller. How may I be of assistance?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's a monk doing in a place like this?
    - **Player:** What's a monk doing in a place like this?
    - **Hamid:** Menaphos is a wondrous place full of beauty and splendour, but its beauty is tarnished by its misguided faith.
    - **Hamid:** The people here worship the Menaphite Pantheon and not the divine radiance of the glorious Saradomin.
    - **Hamid:** So I am here to save their souls, by bringing them the wisdom of Saradomin.
    - **Player:** Really?
    - **Hamid:** It beats being stuck in the monastery!
  - [Choice]: Which monastery do you come from?
    - **Player:** Which monastery do you come from?
    - **Hamid:** I belong to the monastery north of Falador.
    - **Player:** You're a long way from home.
    - **Hamid:** Yeah, I miss the guys.

### Historical dialogue, at Duel Arena

- **Player:** Hi!
- **Hamid:** Hello, traveller. How may I be of assistance?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you heal me?
    - **Hamid:** You'd be better off speaking to the nurses.
    - **Hamid:** They are so...nice, after all.
  - [Choice]: What's a monk doing in a place like this?
    - **Hamid:** Well, don't tell anyone, but I came here because of the nurses.
    - **Player:** Really?
    - **Hamid:** It beats being stuck in the monastery!
  - [Choice]: Which monastery do you come from?
    - **Hamid:**  I belong to the monastery north of Falador.
    - **Player:** You're a long way from home.
    - **Hamid:** Yeah, I miss the guys.

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Hamid:** Hello! Please complete this for me!
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Hamid has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Hamid:** I’m afraid that the puzzle is wrong.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Hamid has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Hamid:** Excellent! Good work!
