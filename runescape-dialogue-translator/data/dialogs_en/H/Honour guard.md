# Honour guard

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Honour guard](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHonour%20guard)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### In the town

- **Honour guard:** Hail, [Fremennik name]!
- **Player:** Good day! How are you doing?
- **Honour guard:** Pretty good. It's nice to have a day off from troll-killing duty.
- **Player:** Have you killed many trolls?
- **Honour guard:** Of course. It's all part of growing up around here. You get used to it.
- **Player:** See you later.

#### Outside of the town

- **Honour guard:** Hail, [Fremennik name]!
- **Player:** Good day!
- **Honour guard:** I hope you're not heading north. There are lots of trolls, very dangerous.
- **Player:** Thanks for the warning. I'll keep my eyes peeled.

#### On the walls and island to the north

- **Honour guard:** Hail, [Fremennik name]!
- **Player:** Good day!
- **Honour guard:** Are you here to help with the trolls?
- **Player:** I'm afraid not, I have other things to do.
- **Honour guard:** Well, watch yourself. Those rocks that the trolls throw can take your arm off.

### While wearing the silly jester costume


#### Inside and outside the town

- **Honour guard:** Good day, jester! Got any new jokes?
- **Player:** No. I only use old, recycled jokes. It's better for the environment.
- **Honour guard:** Haha!

#### On the walls and island to the north

- **Honour guard:** Hey jester, this area is not a suitable place for civilians to wander about in. Trolls everywhere.
- **Player:** Right you are. I'll be careful.
