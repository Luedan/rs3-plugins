## Dialogue Transcript


### Before doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Hello? Ah you're new here, aren't you?
- **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):{{anchor|brugsen}}** I suggest you speak with Brugsen Bursen or the Grand Exchange Tutor near the entrance for a lesson. Brugsen will give an interesting, deeper lesson on the Grand Exchange and the Tutor will give a briefer, plain lesson.

#### Info-combat option

- **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Huh? Oh, not till you've had training.
- (continuea|brugsen)

### After doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** What? Oh, hello. I was deep in thought. Did you say something?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: You seem a bit flustered.
    - **Player:** You seem a bit flustered.
    - **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Sorry, I'm just deep in thought. I'm waiting for many deals to complete today.
    - **Player:** What sort of things are you selling?
    - **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Good old armour and weapons! My people - dwarves, you understand - are hoping I can trade with humans. Are you selling items here also?
    - **Player:** I have already, yes.
    - **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Then good luck with your future bids!
  - [Choice]: Do you know much about the prices for armour and weapons?
    - **Player:** Do you know much about the prices for armour and weapons?
    - **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** Most certainly. My area of expertise.
    - ((Guide Prices: Armour and Weapons opens up.))
  - [Choice]: I didn't say anything at all.
    - **Player:** I didn't say anything at all.
    - **Hofuthand (armour and weapons):** I thought you at least said, 'Hello'. I must be going mad. Do you think I'm going mad?
    - **Player:** Oh, most definitely. You should see a doctor before it's too late.
