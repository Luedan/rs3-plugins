## Dialogue Transcript


### In Keldagrim marketplace


#### Standard dialogue

- **Hervi:** Greetings, human… can I interest you in some fine gems?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Show me the wares!
    - **Player:** Show me the wares!
    - (opens=Green Gemstone Gems)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from his stall

- **Hervi:** {{RSChat|Hands off those gems!}}

#### Trading or speaking to him after stealing from his stall

- **Hervi:**{{RSChat|shadow=yes|colour=yellow|You're the thief! Stop!}}
- **Hervi:**{{RSChat|shadow=yes|colour=yellow|Guards, get this filthy human!}}

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Hervi:** Hello! I’ve got a little quest for you, are you interested?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, I love quests!
    - **Player:** Yes, I love quests!
    - **Hervi:** That’s the spirit! Now, what I need is [item]. And I need them within [number] minutes as well.
    - **Player:** Not much of a quest, but I’ll do it. Just [item] in [number] minutes.
  - [Choice]: No, quests are dull and boring.
    - **Player:** No, quests are dull and boring.
    - **Hervi:** Don’t be so dismissive, you didn’t even hear what I wanted to ask you!
    - **Player:** I don’t want to! I just want to kill things!

##### Talking to Hervi without the item

- **Hervi:** Is/are my [item] arriving anytime soon?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Not anytime soon. Or at all.
    - **Player:** Not anytime soon. Or at all. I’ve given up on looking for them.
    - **Hervi:** Couldn’t you decide that in advance? You’re certainly lacking in manners!
  - [Choice]: Soon, yes, I hope so.
    - **Player:** Soon, yes, I hope so.
    - **Hervi:** Good, soon can’t be soon enough for me!

##### Talking to Hervi with the item

- **Player:** Good news, I’ve got you [item]!
- **Hervi:** And not a minute too late! Great! Do you want me to pay you in gold or would you like me to show you some smithing tricks?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Show me the gold!
    - **Player:** Show me the gold!
    - (receives=coins)
  - [Choice]: Please teach me that.
    - **Player:** Please teach me that.
    - {{Qact|Screen fades in and out}}
    - (receives=experience)
    - You feel more experienced.
