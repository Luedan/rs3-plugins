- [You feel very strange, as if Hazelmere has started poking around in your head.]

- **Player:** Well, that's probably as close to a compliment I'm ever likely to get from you, so thanks!

##### Using a small crystal seed on him

- [You feel very strange, as if Hazelmere has started poking around in your head.]
- **Hazelmere:** So you want to know what your crystal seed does?
- **Player:** How did you...? Oh I forgot about this mind meld thing.
- **Hazelmere:** The seed is a piece of elven magic, all elven magic comes from the manipulation of seeds of crystal. The elves give it divine properties. Elves sing to their crystals and they form into the desired shape.
- **Player:** So how do I turn it into something useful?
- **Hazelmere:** Either talk to an elf or find some other way to sing to the crystal. If this came from Oaknock, he must have found a way...
- **Player:** Thanks, I'll have a look round Oaknock's old workshop. I can have a chat with Brimstail while I'm at it! Thanks!

##### Using a crystal saw on him

- [You feel very strange, as if Hazelmere has started poking around in your head.]
- **Hazelmere:** So you want to know what your crystal saw does?
- **Player:** How did you...? Oh I forgot about this mind meld thing.
- **Hazelmere:** Well, I do remember seeing Oaknock using the saw. He said it allowed him to build devices more complicated than even he was capable of constructing. The device he built must have been very complicated indeed.
- **Player:** I'd say! Anyway, the saw looks like it could be pretty useful. Thanks.
- [The saw will add three levels to your effective Construction level when you are building something that requires a saw.]

##### First time only

- **Hazelmere:** What a silly thing to say. Of course I'm glowing, I'm a ghost! Also because owl poop...
- **Player:** Hazelmere! You're all glowing!
- **Hazelmere:** Probably forgot all about me soon as I died.
- **Player:** Oh, right. I forgot your ability to read minds. It's very confusing.
- **Hazelmere:** Yes, yes, they told me. They do just stand outside, you know? Good job honouring them, I suppose.
- **Player:** I've met the other heroes who fell trying to stop Lucien.
- **Hazelmere:** Not really, but I knew what I was getting into. The others knew the dangers too
- **Player:** I'm sorry about what happened to you all. Lucien was stopped, if that's any consolation.
- **Hazelmere:** I know. But I didn't save you so you could take up the dark art of necromancy.
- **Player:** I've had to turn to necromancy to take on this new threat. It's a danger to you, the others...everyone in Um!
- **Hazelmere:** Oh, fine, I'll let you off the hook. Just don't turn evil on us.
- **Player:** I was strongarmed into it! Rasial MADE me his new apprentice; it wasn't my choice!
- **Hazelmere:** *sigh* And I suppose you want to rope us all into dealing with this new threat.
- **Player:** You know...it's going to take a LOT to defeat Rasial...
- 150 souls fly over to power the vessel.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - 150 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - (continue)
- **Hazelmere:** Fine, we'll help.
- **Player:** So what do you say? Are my heroic friends up for one more mission to stop an evil necromancer?
