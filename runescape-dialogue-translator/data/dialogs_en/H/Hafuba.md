# Hafuba

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hafuba](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHafuba)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *If the player is not using an amulet of monkeyspeak:*
  - **Hafuba:** Ook Ah Uh! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ah Ook!
  - You cannot understand what he is saying.
- *If the player is not holding a greegree:*
  - **Hafuba:** I don't know what you think you are doing here human, but I do know that you will not live long enough to leave this place.
- *If the player is holding a greegree:*
  - **Hafuba:**  Welcome to the temple of Marimbo, child. In less busy times I would bless you, but I have not the luxury of free time at the moment. However, feel free to stay here as long as you need.
  - **Player:** Thank you.
