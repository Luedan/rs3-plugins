#### Having lost the ice gloves from Heroes' Quest

- **High Priest:** Many greetings. Welcome to our fair island.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Have you seen a pair of ice gloves?
    - **High Priest:** By which you mean the pair of mythical gloves you stole from the cold dead body of the Queen of the Ice?
    - **Player:** Er...
    - **High Priest:** The gloves that you acquired to aid you in plucking the Entranan firebird? Another victim of your murderous nature, I should add.
    - **High Priest:** The ice gloves that, from context, I would assume you have lost, if not carelessly discarded?
    - **Player:** Those ice gloves, yes.
    - (receives=ice gloves)
    - [The high priest of Entrana shivers as he hands you your lost ice gloves.]
    - **High Priest:** One of my monks found these gloves. If only to spare this world from further carnage, I return them to you.

#### Historical dialogue

- **High Priest:** Many greetings. <br>Welcome to our fair island.
- **High Priest:** You are standing on the holy island of Entrana. It was here that Saradomin first stepped upon Gielinor.
- **High Priest:** In homage to Saradomin's first arrival, we have built a great church, and devoted the island to those who wish peace for the world.
- **High Priest:** The inhabitants of this island are mostly monks who spend their time meditating on Saradomin's ways.
- **High Priest:** Of course, there are now more pilgrims to this holy site, since Saradomin defeated Zamorak in the battle of Lumbridge.
- **High Priest:** It is good that so many see Saradomin's true glory!

### Using an item on the high priest
