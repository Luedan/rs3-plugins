## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Hermit Cave, after Hermit Permits

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you imbue another crab amulet for me?
  - (Condition: With a crabulet in backpack, bank or in a cramulet:)
    - **Hermit:** I sense that you already have a crabulet. This is a precious gift, not to be given out to anyone and everyone. One is plenty.
  - (Condition: Otherwise:)
    - (Condition: Without a crab amulet in backpack:)
      - **Hermit:** You don't have a crab amulet to imbue.
    - (Condition: With a crab amulet in backpack:)
      - (removes=crab amulet) (receives=crabulet)
      - **Hermit:** There. Another crabulet enchanted for you.
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: How did you learn crabspeak?
    - **Player:** How did you learn crabspeak?
    - **Hermit:** My mother was a crab. She taught it to me from birth.
    - **Player:** Really? That sounds like a medical miracle.
    - **Hermit:** Hah! I wish, but no.
    - **Hermit:** In my past life I was a sailor. After a nasty incident with a school of sharks, I ended up stranded. Eventually washed up on the shores of Wendlewick, starving and cold.
    - **Hermit:** Managed to crawl my way into a whale carcass, where some warming-but-rotting whale blubber saved me from the brink of death.
    - **Player:** Rotting whale blubber would probably send me to the brink of death.
    - **Hermit:** The crabs then brought me some fresh sardines to eat. Maybe they thought I was one of theirs because of my hair? I don't know.
    - **Hermit:** Anyway, I followed them into this cave here and just fell in love. All the isolations you could dream of! No taxes! The simple life.
    - **Hermit:** All the seafood I could imagine, but I never once ate crab. They eventually learned to trust me, and granted me the gift.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do the villagers know you live here?
    - **Player:** Do the villagers know you live here?
    - **Hermit:** Eh, some of them. The smell mostly keeps them away.
    - **Hermit:** Except Esther! Oh, Esther. Lovely woman. She was very intrigued by my situation.
    - **Player:** Not to be rude, but have you considered bathing?
    - **Hermit:** I bathe! With the crabs of course.
    - **Player:** Right. So you bathe in rancid cavewater, full of crabs.
    - **Hermit:** Exactly. Clean as a whistle!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** See you around, Hermit.
    - **Hermit:** Goodbye, Player.
