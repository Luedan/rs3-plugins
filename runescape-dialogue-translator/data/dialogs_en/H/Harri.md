# Harri

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Harri](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHarri)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - Who are you?
    - **Harri:** Greetings to you. My name is Harri. It's my job to look after the egg shy and hook-a-duck stalls.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - How does the egg shy work?
    - **Harri:** We'd normally have a coconut shy, but as it's the right time of the year, we've gone for an egg shy for this fayre.
    - **Harri:** All you have to do is choose the type of ball that you want to throw and try to knock off the eggs. The balls have different properties. You get 10 balls at a time to throw.
    - The standard balls have an average chance to hit and give an average amount of Ranged experience.
    - The cabbages have a lower chance to hit but give higher experience and the chinchompas have a higher chance to hit but give lower experience.
    - Choose whichever type of ball you like for each set of 10 throws.
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - You will only get charged tokens for the times that you hit the eggs.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - You will gain Ranged XP from this attraction.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - How does the hook-a-duck work?
    - **Harri:** All you have to do is choose the type of rod that you want to use and try to catch the ducks. You get 10 goes at a time. The rods have different properties.
    - The light-weight rod has an average chance to catch a duck and gives an average amount of Hunter experience.
    - The fast-action rod has a lower chance to catch a duck but gives higher experience and the magnetic rod has a higher chance to catch but gives lower experience.
    - You can choose whichever type of rod you like for each set of 10 tries.
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - You will only get charged tokens for the times that you catch a duck.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - You will gain Hunter XP from this attraction.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - How are things going?
    - **Harri:** Swimmingly, thank you for asking.
    - **Harri:** It's a great life, travelling from place to place and meeting all sorts of different people, and seeing the joy on their faces when they play with the different attractions.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
