## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Harmony pillar farmer:** Welcome to the home of the greatest potion-making secrets of Clan Meilyr!
- **Harmony pillar farmer:** We freely share knowledge among ourselves. Outsiders, however, must trade.
- (Condition: Only on the first time:)
  - **Harmony pillar farmer:** Before we proceed, we have some of our own knowledge to share with you.
  - **Harmony pillar farmer:** Within the old lands - your people refer to them as Daemonheim - knowledge of our clans past remains out of our reach.
  - **Harmony pillar farmer:** Our clan has recently acquired the means to translate the ancient elven tongue. Should you find anything which looks like it matches this in your travels, bring it to us and we shall share this knowledge.
  - [The shopkeeper provides a journal, which will automatically hold anything you find in Daemonheim.]
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Do you have anything to sell?
    - **Farmer:** Yes, I may provide seeds to grow harmony moss, or recipes for combination potions.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Seeds, please.
        - (opens= Meilyr Clan Store)
      - [Choice]: Recipes, please.
        - (opens= Recipe Shop)
      - [Choice]: I'd like to ask something else.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Bye.
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: What can you tell me about these pillars?
    - **Harmony pillar farmer:** The inner workings of harmony pillars are a mystery. Our research indicates that they're powered by the skills of whoever plants a harmony moss seed, which I can provide you with - for a price.
    - **Harmony pillar farmer:** The skill a pillar relates to changes each day, or when it is fully sated and harvested. Once a seed is placed, it is irrevocable and you must finish the harvest before it will change.
    - **Harmony pillar farmer:** The moss will not grow over time. Only as you grow shall it grow.
    - <span style="display: inline-block; text-align: center;">Harmony pillars do not grow like regular farming patches.<br><br>You must gain 50,000 XP in the skill it is attuned to in order to advance a growth stage. A pillar has 4 growth stages.</span>
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: How much harmony moss have I harvested?|cond=After having asked about the pillars at least once before:
    - (Condition: If less than or equal to 50 moss:)
      - **Harmony pillar farmer:** Hmm, what was the name again? Ah - yes. There we go. By my count you've harvested [number] units.
    - (Condition: If more than 50 moss:)
      - **Harmony pillar farmer:** You've harvested a lot! I stopped counting after you reached 50 units.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Bye.
    - (end)

### Interacting for the first time after reaching level 99 in all skills

- **Harmony pillar farmer:** Clan Meilyr respects those who master all that can be mastered. These potions are offered to those who manage such feats.
- (receives= perfect juju agility potion, perfect juju dungeoneering potion, perfect juju farming potion, perfect juju herblore potion, perfect juju mining potion, perfect juju prayer potion, perfect juju smithing potion and perfect juju woodcutting potion)
