## Dialogue Transcript


### In Dorgesh-Kaan, before The Chosen Commander

- **Merchant Walton:** What do you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Merchant Walton:** I'm selling 'exotic' food to these goblins: milk, eggs, bread, rice...everything from the surface fascinates them.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy something?
    - **Player:** Can I buy something?
    - **Merchant Walton:**  Oh, no. I'm only selling to the cave goblins. Besides, you're used to this food, so you wouldn't buy it for the prices I'm asking.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
