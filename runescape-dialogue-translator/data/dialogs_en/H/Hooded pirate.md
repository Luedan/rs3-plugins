# Hooded pirate

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hooded pirate](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHooded%20pirate)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without the [[Crumpled note (stained)|stained crumpled note]]

- The figure reaches out a hand towards you. Something is clenched between his eerily grey fingers. Do you accept his gift?
- Select an option
  - Yes.
    - As he opens his fingers, you could swear that you hear the bones crack. Something writhing and small falls mercifully through your fingers. A scrunched-up piece of paper lands in your hands.
    - {{Qact|Crumpled note is placed in your inventory.}}
  - No.
    - The figure lowers his hand, but says nothing. Beneath his cowl a rasping noise can be hear.

### With a [[crumpled note]] or after obtaining the [[rotten journal]]

- The figure says nothing. Something crawls beneath the rags in a manner that makes the hairs on the back of your neck stand on end.

### With [[black coin]] in inventory

- {{Qact|Black coin is removed from your inventory, and crumpled note is placed in your inventory.}}
- With a single movement the disturbing figure takes the coin and drops another crumpled note into your hands. Briefly your fingers touch and you resist the urge to vomit.

### After having lost the [[Crumpled note (perfumed)|perfumed crumpled note]]

- {{Qact|Crumpled note is placed in your inventory.}}
- The mysterious figure gives you a crumpled piece of paper.
