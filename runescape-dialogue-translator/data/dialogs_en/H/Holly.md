# Holly

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Holly](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHolly)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Open Christmas Spirit Shop

- (opens=Christmas Spirit Shop)

### Talk to

- **Holly:** Get your festive wares here! Accepting Christmas spirit only!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What do you sell?
    - **Player:** What do you sell?
    - **Holly:** Many old festive rewards. You name it, I've got it.
    - **Player:** Santa hats? Christmas crackers?
    - **Holly:** I haven't procured those yet. But, I can do you one better...
    - **Player:** Yes?
    - **Holly:** With a wand that shoots Christmas puddings!
    - **Holly:** As well as icy-themed weapons, a snowman outfit, and more!
    - [Menu]: Open shop?
      - [Choice]: Yes please.
        - (opens=Christmas Spirit Shop)
      - [Choice]: No thank you.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: cond=Only during a Christmas event|How can I earn Christmas spirit?
    - **Player:** How can I earn Christmas spirit?
    - **Holly:** Many ways. You can help out with some of the activities around the village, such as making snowballs, or chopping fir logs.
    - **Holly:** You might also see some of my teeny siblings around the world. Grabbing them out the air will give you some too.
    - **Player:** Thanks!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Maybe another time.
    - **Holly:** Hurry back! They're available for limited time only!
