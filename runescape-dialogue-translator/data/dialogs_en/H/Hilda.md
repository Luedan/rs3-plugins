## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Torsteg at the Wilderness side entrance to Daemonheim.*
- **Player:** Hello.
- **Hilda:** Can we help you with anything?
- What would you like to say?
  - Can I go through the gate?
    - **Player:** Can I go through the gate?
    - **Hilda:** If you want, we will let you pass into the Wilderness. Be warned that other players might attack you there, and you won't be allowed through the gate while you're in combat.
    - What would you like to say?
      - I am not afraid.
        - **Player:** I am not afraid.
        - **Hilda:** Your speech is brave. Travel south-west, and you should reach the city of Varrock, if you don't die first. Now, can we help you with anything else?
        - What would you like to say?
          - What are you doing here?
            - **Player:** What are you doing here?
            - **Hilda:** We are guarding this gateway against the vicious fighters who roam the Wilderness.
            - **Player:** Good luck with that.
            - **Hilda:** Thank you. Now, can we help you with anything else?
              - (previous)
          - Is there any other way out of here?
            - **Hilda:** Oh yes. Go back up the hill to the campsite, then head east to the docks. There is a ferry that will take you south to the so-called civilised lands.
            - **Hilda:**It's far safer than trying to walk through the Wilderness. Now, can we help you with anything else?
              - ((Shows initial options with "the gate" switched to "that gate" but no difference in dialogue))
          - No thanks.
            - **Player:** No thanks.
      - Forget it, I'm not going through there.
        - **Player:** Forget it, I'm not going through there.
        - **Hilda:** That is wise. Now, can we help you with anything else?
          - ((Same options as above))
  - What are you doing here?
    - (above)
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.

### Prior to the release of Dungeoneering

- **Player:**  Hello there. What’s-
- **Torsteg:**  HALT! Come no further!
- **Hilda:**  Calm down, Torsteg. Sorry, traveller. He takes his job very seriously. As for you, traveller, I suggest you turn back. The Fremennik have fortified this area, and we aren’t letting anyone in.
- **Player:**  What is this place?
- **Torsteg:**  It is none of your concern.
- **Hilda:**  Quiet, Torsteg! They came all this way, they deserve an explanation. A while back, our seers felt a strange power wash over the land. It was unlike anything they’d ever experienced. They were able to pinpoint the source of this strange power, and we quickly mobilized to contain the unknown threat. Our brothers are risking their lives to this end as we speak.
- **Player:**  Why can't I come in?
- **Torsteg:**  Because we have our orders!
- **Hilda:**  Forgive our caution, but we Fremennik aren’t known for trusting outsiders. We have orders to stop anyone getting in, so that’s how it’s going to be.
- **Player:**  But I’m not an outsider, I’ve completed your trials.
- **Hilda:**  Regardless, you are on that side of the fence, so that’s where you must stay.
- **Player:**  Oh, come on, please let me pa-
- **Torsteg:**  YOU SHALL NOT PASS!
- **Player:**  Okay okay. I get the picture.
- **Player:**  I’m done with my questions.
- **Hilda:**  Very well, traveller, be on your way.
