## Dialogue Transcript


### Without a full [[Shark outfit (disambiguation)|shark outfit]] on

- **Howie:** Howie Howie!
- **Player:** You seem like an excited little thing!
- **Howie:** How HOWIE Howie!
- **Player:** A VERY excited little thing. What's your name?
- **Howie:** HOWIE!
- *(If player has the list on them and hasn't completed the task yet:)*
  - Howie grabs your task list and crosses off a task. He also hands you 2 Tribal mask stamps.
- *(If player lacks the list and hasn't completed the task yet:)*
  - The tiny tribal idol seems confused that you don't have your Tribal task list with you. He pouts sadly.

### With a full [[Shark outfit (disambiguation)|shark outfit]] on

- The tiny tribal idol shivers in fear at the sight of a shark! It seems like he doesn't like them.
