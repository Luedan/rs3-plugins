# Harrison

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Harrison](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHarrison)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** What are you doing here?
- **Harrison:** I usually hang around this place in case anyone shows up.
- **Harrison:** Although it's not often you see many people around here!
- **Player:** So you just wander around?
- (Condition: With Hard Wilderness Tasks done:)
  - **Harrison:** Well I can see you've done quite a few tasks around here, so I can offer some help to you. If you have any noted items, feel free to give them to me and I'll swap them out for usable objects. I won't be able to help you out if you're under attack though, so be careful! Oh, and I won't swap your items back into notes either. It's a one-way deal, I'm afraid.
  - **Player:** Thanks, I'll keep that in mind.
- (Condition: Without Hard Wilderness Tasks done:)
  - **Harrison:** I'd say I do a bit more than that, but for now I just gather items people leave behind after they're done killing each other.
  - **Player:** Alright then, if you say so.
