## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Heckel Funch:** Good day to you, my friend, and a beautiful one at that. Would you like some groceries? I have all sorts. Alcohol also, if you're partial to a drink.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
    - **Heckel Funch:** Ahh well, all the best to you.
  - [Choice]: I'll have a look.
    - **Player:** I'll have a look.
    - **Heckel Funch:** There's a good human.
    - (opens=Funch's Fine Groceries)

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Heckel Funch:** Please do puzzle for me human!
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Heckel Funch has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Heckel Funch:** No, that's wrong I'm afraid.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Heckel Funch has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Heckel Funch:** Well done, human!
