## Dialogue Transcript


### 24th—25th Anniversary celebrations after 4 January


#### Overhead lines

- ((One of the following is seen randomly every 15 seconds:))
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Enjoy balloon drops at lodestones every 30 minutes today!}}
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Hear ye, hear ye! RuneScape turns [24/25] this month!}}
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|We're celebrating the [24th/25th] RuneScape anniversary!}}

#### When talked to

- **Player:** What's going on?
- **Herald:** RuneScape turns [24/25] this month! There are balloon drops every 30 minutes around most lodestones to celebrate!
- **Player:** This sounds fun!
- **Herald:** Aye. Enjoy the celebrations!
- **Player:** Thanks I'll check it out.{{Sic}}

### Christmas 2018–2019


#### Overhead lines

- *(One of the following is seen randomly every 15 seconds, and sometimes the herald also throws a snowball at the player while saying the line:)*
  - *(Only in 2018:)*
    - **Herald:** {{RSChat|A portal to the Land of Snow has appeared on White Wolf Mountain!|color=#FFFF00|bg=#0A1720}}
  - *(Only in 2018:)*
    - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Adventurers wanted! Someone needs help on White Wolf Mountain!|color=#FFFF00|bg=#0A1720}}
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Check out the giant cracker at the Grand Exchange!|color=#FFFF00|bg=#0A1720}}
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Head to the Grand Exchange to help build the biggest cracker of all time!|color=#FFFF00|bg=#0A1720}}
  - **Herald:** {{RSChat|Hear ye, hear ye! Santa is looking for adventurers' assistance at the Grand Exchange.|color=#FFFF00|bg=#0A1720}}

#### When talked to

- **Player:** What's going on?
- **Herald:** Oh it's my favourite time of the year! Santa has returned!
- **Player:** Santa's here?
- **Herald:** Yes! You can find him at the Grand Exchange and this year he's building a giant cracker and needs everyone's help.
- **Herald:** You can find cracker paper all around Gielinor so if you see any, I recommend handing it in because I hear Santa has some lovely presents for those that help!
- **Player:** Thanks I'll check it out.
