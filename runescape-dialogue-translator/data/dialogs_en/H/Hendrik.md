# Hendrik

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hendrik](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHendrik)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Hendrik:** Saradomin's blessings upon you, my friend.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What can you tell me about this place?
    - **Hendrik:** Since it was founded, Ashdale has become something of a refuge; a place for people who want some peace and quiet.
    - **Hendrik:** Gielinor can be a dangerous place. The people who live here know they don't have to worry about what tomorrow will bring.
    - **Hendrik:** Most of the time, anyway.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can you give me any tips on fishing?
    - **Hendrik:** It's like most things, really - practice makes perfect. Just keep at it, and you'll soon start to see an improvement.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do you have any fishing bait?
    - ''(If the player already has fishing bait in their inventory:)
      - **Hendrik:** Don't you already have some? <br>Try looking in your backpack.
      - (initial)
    - ''(If the player dropped the fishing bait on the ground:)
      - **Hendrik:** Don't you already have some? <br>Try looking on the floor.
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: Otherwise:)
      - **Hendrik:** Of course - here you go. Let me know if you need any more.
      - (receives=10 Fishing bait)
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Saradomin? Who's that?
    - **Hendrik:** Saradomin is one of the beings who first brought life into Gielinor.
    - **Hendrik:** Most of us who live in Ashdale have taken Saradomin as our patron. He stands for truth, justice, and - above all - order.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Hendrik:** Saradomin watch over you, my friend.
