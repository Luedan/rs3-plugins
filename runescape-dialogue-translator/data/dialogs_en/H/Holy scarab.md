## Dialogue Transcript


### Interactions


#### Interaction 1

- **Player:** Hello!
- The scarab does not respond.
- **Player:** HELLOOO!
- The scarab seems to be ignoring you.
- **Player:** HELLLOOOOOOOOOOO?
- **Holy scarab:** This better be worth breaking my vow of silence for. What do you want?
- **Player:** What do you call a psychic scarab?
- **Holy scarab:** *sigh* I don't know. What do you call a psychic scarab?
- **Player:** A for-chitin teller!
- **Holy scarab:** Really. Disrupting my peace and quiet for that?
- **Player:** Well, I thought it was shell-arious.
- The scarab goes back to ignoring you.
- **Player:** Tough crowd.

#### Interaction 2

- **Player:** What are you doing?
- The scarab has its eyes closed.
- **Player:** Are you listening to me?
- The scarab quietly hums. They seem to be meditating.
- **Player:** You know, some candles would really help set the mood. They're very relaxing.
- **Player:** Oasis Breeze would be the perfect scent for you. We could head over to the Candle Shop and buy one right now!
- The scarab opens one eye to glare at you.
- **Player:** Maybe not then.
