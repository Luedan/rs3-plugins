# Hailong

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Hailong](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AHailong)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without all the potions

- **Hailong:** Oh you're here about the potions, let's see what you have.
- **Hailong:** Sorry, you don't have all the things I need yet.
- **Hailong:** Bring me a Strength Potion (4),{{Sic}}a Restore Potion (4) and an Agility Potion (4).

### With all the potions

- **Hailong:** Oh you're here about the potions, let's see what you have.
- **Hailong:** Great! You brought me all the potions!

### After bringing him the potions

- **Hailong:** You have already completed this task.
