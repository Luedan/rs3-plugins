## Dialogue Transcript


### First time

- **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Hello human, and welcome to our amazing Gnomecopter Tours. What can we do for you?
  - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
    - [Choice]: What's Gnomecopter Tours?
      - **Player:** What's Gnomecopter Tours?
      - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** The gnomecopters are something Sasquine and I invented. Using a unique blend of gnomic engineering and human magic, we've launched a whole new kind of tour experience.
        - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
          - [Choice]: How did you make them?
            - **Player:** How did you make them?
            - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Oh, it's very clever. Sasquine and I saw some men riding around on big magic carpet thingies in the desert south of Al Kharid and we couldn't resist 'borrowing' one or two.
            - **Player:** You 'borrowed' some magic carpets?
            - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Well, we didn't exactly ask permission but they're for research purposes. That's allowed! Anyway, we're going off topic.
            - **Player:** Alright, so you stole some magic carpets. What did you do then?
            - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** We managed to interface the carpets with the latest in gnome clider technology. My brother's an expert flight engineer; he helped a lot. The result is what you see here: the gnomecopter.
            - **Player:** It doesn't look much like a gnome glider or a magic carpet.
            - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** The rotors are mostly made from the fabric of the carpets. Our expert gnomic engineering makes it far easier to handle than the original carpet and its magic allows the gnomecopters to do things that normal gliders
            - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** can't.
            - **Player:** What exactly can these things do?
            - (continue)
          - [Choice]: What exactly can these things do?
            - **Player:** What exactly can these things do?
              - (On a free-to-play world)
                - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** We're using them to show members' areas to people on non-members' worlds.
                - **Player:** So I could have a look at some of the places members get to visit?
                - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Yes, and we're offering our service completely free of charge at the moment, so you can't lose out. Chat to Sasquine to get going.
                - **Player:** I'll think about it.
                - (end)
              - (On a members world)
                - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Well, this may sound strange but we're using them to show members' areas to people on non-members' worlds.
                - **Player:** Hang on, we're on a members' world here! I could just go to these places myself.
                - **Hieronymus Alvafrim:** Yes, you probably could. That said, we're currently offering our service for free and you can't beat that price! Chat to Sasquine to get going.
                - **Player:** I'll think about it.
                - (end)
    - [Choice]: Have you got anything good for sale?
      - (dialogue missing)
    - [Choice]: Nothing at the moment, thank you.
      - **Player:** Nothing at the moment, thank you.
        - (end)
