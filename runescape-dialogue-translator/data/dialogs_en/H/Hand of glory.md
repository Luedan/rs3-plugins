## Dialogue Transcript


### Picking up


#### Without space in backpack

- **Player:** I don't have room to take this...which I'm almost thankful for.

#### With space in backpack

- You take the hand of glory.

### Combining


#### First hand of glory

- **Aris:** Who is this? Who is messing with things they don't understand again?
- **Player:** What just happened? I just looked at this hand and then your voice was in my head?
- **Aris:** I'm using the Player Contact spell. This is Player, isn't it? I should have known. This is Aris, by the way.
- **Player:** What? What's a player? I'm confused?
- **Aris:** Perpetually. And you are also messing around with a hand of glory! Those can be dangerous in the wrong hands...
- **Player:** ...
- **Aris:** You know what I meant!
- **Player:** Okay, so what can I use this hand for and how can I make it safe?
- **Aris:** The safest use for a hand of glory is to use a Ring of Luck on it. Anything else and the universe could explode. I mean, it could anyway.
- **Player:** Add any luck ring, got it!
- **Aris:** Did I say 'any'? No, I specifically said 'Ring of Luck'. You have to take baby steps with these things.
- **Player:** Universe. Exploding. Gotcha.
- **Aris:** If you find multiple hands of glory, then maybe you can start to slowly build up which tier of ring you use...
- *If the player does not have a Ring of Luck in the inventory:*
  - **Player:** Well, I guess I'd better get hold of a luck ring for this hand of glory.
- *If the player has a Ring of Luck in the inventory:*
  - Place luck ring on hand of glory?
    - Yes.
      - Are you sure - one or both items are tradeable?
        - Yes.
          - (The hand of glory and Ring of Luck are removed from the player's inventory.)
          - (receives=Hand of Glory (Ring of Luck))
        - No.
          - (end)
    - No.
      - (end)

- **Aris:** You need to take this slowly - you used a ring of luck last time, and this time you should use a Ring of Wealth - fully charged, of course.
- **Player:** Right. Universe exploding, have to be careful...
- **Aris:** What? No, just don't be cheap.
- ((If a fully charged Ring of Wealth is not in the inventory:))
