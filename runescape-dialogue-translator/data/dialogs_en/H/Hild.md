#### If the player has lost their catspeak amulet

- **Player:** I've lost my enchanted Amulet of Catspeak.
- **Hild:** This can be rectified, simply bring me another Amulet of Catspeak and five death runes and I will enchant it for you.
- (Condition: If the player does not have five or more death runes:)
  - **Player:** I don't have the five death runes on me.
  - **Hild:** Come back to me when you have them.
- (Condition: If the player has five death runes but not the catspeak amulet:)
  - **Player:** I have the runes but don't have the amulet on me.
  - **Hild:** Bring me the amulet and I will enchant it for you.
- (Condition: If the player has both five death runes and the catspeak amulet:)
  - **Player:** I have the death runes with me now.
  - **Hild:** Good. Give me the amulet so that I may perform the enchantment.
  - [You pass Hild the amulet.]
  - {{qact|Hild casts a spell and a magic sound effect is heard.}}
  - (removes=catspeak amulet and five death runes) (receives=catspeak amulet (e))
  - [Hild gives you the enchanted amulet.]
  - **Hild:** Using the enchanted amulet is easy; open up the amulet and you'll see a cat's face, use the whiskers to rotate the cat's nose. You'll see the nose move and the new direction highlighted. When the nose points in the direction that Bob
  - **Hild:** is in, the eyes will light up and the mouth will open. You'll need to head in that direction and then check the direction again. If you are very close to Bob then the eyes and mouth will animate no matter which way the nose is pointing. That's all there is to it, good luck!
  - **Player:** Thanks!
