## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Spirits of the Elid

- **PLAYER:** Good day to you.
- **Habibah:** Hello.
- **PLAYER:** You don't look too happy.
- **Habibah:** True. We've not fallen on the best of times here.
- **PLAYER:** Any way that I can help?
- **Habibah:** Possibly. I'd go talk to Awusah the Mayor of Nardah. He's in the big house on the east side of the town square.

### After Spirits of the Elid

- How's life now the curse has been lifted?
  - **Habibah:** Much better thanks to you. We're all very impressed.
