# Horvik

- **Letter**: `H`

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- **Horvik:** Hello, do you need any help?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Do you want to trade?
    - (opens=Horvik's Armour Shop)
  - [Choice]: No thanks, I'm just looking around.
    - **Player:** No thanks, I'm just looking around.
    - **Horvik:** Well, come and see me if you're ever in need of armour!

### In New Varrock

- **Horvik:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you want to trade?
    - (opens=Horvik's Armour Shop (New Varrock))
  - [Choice]: What's it like being a zombie?
    - **Horvik:** As you'd expect. Constant hunger for brains, a bit of shambling, body parts falling off. C'est la mort.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: No thanks, I'm just looking around.
    - **Horvik:** Well, come and see me if you're ever in need of armour!
