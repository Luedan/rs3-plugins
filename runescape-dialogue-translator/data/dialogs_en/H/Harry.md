## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Harry:** Welcome! You can buy Fishing equipment at my store. We'll also give you a good price for any fish that you catch.
- Select an option
  - Let's see what you've got, then.
    - (opens=Harry's Fishing Shop)
  - *(If the player has a Fishbowl (water) or Fishbowl (seaweed))* Can I get a fish for this bowl?
    - **Player:** Can I get a fish for this bowl?
    - ''(If the player has a Fishbowl (water)):
    - **Harry:** Sorry, you need to put some seaweed into the bowl first.
    - **Player:** Seaweed?
    - **Harry:** Yes, the fish seem to like it. Come and see me when you have put some in the bowl.
    - ''(If the player has a Fishbowl (seaweed)):
    - **Harry:** Yes, you can. I can see that you have a nicely filled fishbowl there to use, and you can catch a fish from my aquarium if you want. You will need a special net to do this though - I sell them for 10 gold.
      - I'll take it.
        - **Player:** I'll take it.
        - **Harry:** Here you go.
        - (receives=tiny net)
        - (removes=10 coins)
        - Harry sells you a tiny net.
      - No, thanks; later, maybe.
        - **Player:** No, thanks; later, maybe.
  - *(If the player has a Fishbowl (water), Fishbowl (seaweed) or pet fish)* Do you have any fish food?
    - **Player:** Do you have any fish food?
    - **Harry:** Sorry, I'm all out. I used up the last of it feeding the fish in the aquarium. I have some empty boxes, though - they have the ingredients written on the back. I'm sure if you pick up a pestle and mortar you will be able to make your own.
    - (receives=empty box (fish))
    - **Harry:** Here. I can hardly charge you for an empty box.
  - Sorry, I'm not interested.
    - (end)
