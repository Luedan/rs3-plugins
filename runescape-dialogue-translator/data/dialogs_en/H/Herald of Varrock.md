## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Herald of Varrock:** Welcome to Varrock, my [fine fellow/good lady]. How might I be of service?
- **Player:** Hello; a pleasure to meet you. What do you do here?
- **Herald of Varrock:** I'm glad you asked, for I have the distinct honour to be the Varrock herald.
- **Herald of Varrock:** It is my solemn duty to uphold and maintain the standards and practices of the royal court, whether it be royal events or herald capes.
- ((If you don't have a Varrock herald cape:))
  - **Player:** Herald cape? What's that?
  - **Herald of Varrock:** There is nothing more prized and sought after than a Varrock herald cape! Given only to those who are trusted to maintain the honour and duty of Varrock, someone such as yourself, of course.
  - **Herald of Varrock:** You may even customise the cape if your deeds within the realm have been of help to the city!
  - **Herald of Varrock:** Would you care to take one of these fine capes?
  - **Accept this Varrock herald cape?**
    - Yes
      - **Player:** Sure, I'll take it!
      - **Herald of Varrock:** Excellent! Let's take a look at the customisations we can do...
      - (opens=Customisation)
    - No
      - **Player:** No thanks.
      - **Herald of Varrock:** As you wish. I'll be here should you come to your senses.
- ((If you have a Varrock herald cape:))
  - **Player:** Oh, I already have one of those.
  - **Herald of Varrock:** Why, of course you do! And might I add there is none finer in all the land. Would you perhaps like to further customise your cape?
  - **Customise you Varrock herald cape?**
    - Yes
      - (opens=Customisation)
    - No
      - (end)
- ((If you have a herald cape from another city:))
  - **Player:** I already have a herald cape from another city, though.
  - **Herald of Varrock:** Quite. I see that other cities are, again, following in Varrock's bold footsteps.
  - **Herald of Varrock:** Can I not interest you in the honour of this Varrock herald cape instead?
  - **Change your herald cape to Varrock?**
    - Yes
      - **Player:** Sure, I'll take it!
      - **Herald of Varrock:** Excellent! Let's take a look at the customisations we can do...
      - (opens=Customisation)
    - No
      - **Player:** No thanks, I'll keep the cape I have.
      - **Herald of Varrock:** My, my; such sad times we live in. I'll be here should you come to your senses.
