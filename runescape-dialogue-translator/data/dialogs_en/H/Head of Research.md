## Dialogue Transcript

- **Head of Research:** Welcome!
- Choose an option:
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Head of Research:** The name's Engelbert, I'm the Head of Research here at the Archaeology Guild.
    - (previous|Options)
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Head of Research:** I manage the researchers, making sure that we're out in the field often to keep up to date with our history.
    - (previous|Options)
  - ((If you have completed the research tutorial:))
    - Ask about research management.
      - Select an option
        - Can you tell me what research is about?
          - **Player:** Can you tell me what research is about?
          - **Head of research:** Now that you've received the Assistant qualification, you're able to manage a research team. As a qualified archaeologist you're able to manage a team of researchers. You can pick your research team and send them out into the field to conduct research for you. You'll have to pay in Chronotes though, of course! You definitely won't see many people working for free these days. Once the deed is done, your team will return with a report of their findings. They might even return with various materials and artefacts!
          - (previous|ResearchManagement)
        - Can you explain researchers to me?
          - **Head of research:** Research contracts will take various amounts of time to complete, and the researchers within your team will affect this. Some researchers can get the work done much faster, but they'll probably cost you a little extra. You'll find that some researchers won't work with you unless you've gained their trust first. You may want to seek them out and persuade them otherwise.
          - (previous|ResearchManagement)
        - Can you tell me about special research?
          - **Head of research:** Whilst out in the field you may find points of interest that could do with additional study time. Take some notes and then you'll be able to send out a team to do some more thorough investigations.
          - (previous|ResearchManagement)
        - Ask about something else.
          - (previous2|Options)
