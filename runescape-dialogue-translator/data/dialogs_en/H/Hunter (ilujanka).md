## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Hunter:** Welcome, fellow dreamer.
- ((Before having built the hunting lodge:))
  - **Hunter:**  If you could provide me some resources from your base camp, I could create a hunting lodge for you to train at within this dream, and teach you something of Ilujankan hunting techniques.
- ((After having built the hunting lodge but before having made progress:))
  - **Hunter:** Thank you for the vines. If you keep supplying me with more, I can teach you much about Ilujankan hunting techniques.
- ((After a small amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Hunter:** You are learning much of our ways. Keep on as you are, and I will share a story with you.
- ((After a great amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Hunter:** You have learned much. I shall soon share my tale with Paskeeper Sharrigan.
- ((After having unlocked the story:))
  - **Hunter:** I have recounted my tale to Pastkeeper Sharrigan. Thank you for all of your efforts. Perhaps you should train at one of the other stations for a time.
