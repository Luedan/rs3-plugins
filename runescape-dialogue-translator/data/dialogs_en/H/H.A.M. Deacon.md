# H.A.M. Deacon

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:H.A.M. Deacon](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AH.A.M.%20Deacon)
- **Letter**: `H`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **H.A.M. Deacon:** We are the righteous ones in his eyes alone.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** Let them not infest our cities and towns...
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** And lo, we become the power, indeed the force to stop these monsters in their tracks.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** Verily I urge you, my friends to take up your spades and farm your farms to feed our people in this blessed sanctuary.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** If thine monsters visage does frighten thee, then tear it off I say... tear it off!
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** For Saradomin will guide our sword arms and smash the enemies of humans till their bones become dust.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** And let us smite these monsters unto their deaths.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** For they are not the chosen ones in Saradomin's eyes.
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** Where do we go for safety from these monsters... here, my brethren!
- **H.A.M. Deacon:** And let there be no cave or shelter for their spawn until the end of days.
