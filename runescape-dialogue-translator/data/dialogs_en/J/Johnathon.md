# Johnathon

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Johnathon](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AJohnathon)
- **Letter**: `J`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Family Crest

- **Johnathon:** I am so very tired... Leave me be... to rest...

### After Family Crest


#### With the unimbued family gauntlets

- **Johnathon:** Hello again. My family now considers you a hero.
- **Player:** Your father tells me you can improve these gauntlets for me...
- **Johnathon:** And right he is at that. Although my skill with Death Rune magiks was not great enough to defeat Chronozon, I am adequately skilled at Chaos Rune magiks. If you give me your gauntlets I will bestow upon them some of my power, and make any bolt spells you wish to cast more effective.
- Select an option
  - That sounds good to me.
    - **Player:** That sounds good to me!
    - Johnathon takes your gauntlets from you, and begins a low chant over them. You see them begin to glow and sparkle, before he returns them to you.
    - (removes=family gauntlets) (receives=chaos gauntlets)
  - I shall see what options your brothers can offer me first.
    - **Player:** I think I'll check my other options with your brothers before committing myself.
    - **Johnathon:**I doubt they will offer anything as useful as my magical boost, but as you wish.

#### With the imbued family gauntlets or without any of the gauntlets


##### Talk-to option

- **Johnathon:** Hello again. My family now considers you a hero.
- **Player:** Maybe because… I am.
- **Johnathon:** Yes well... Is there anything I can help you with?
- Choose an option:
  - Can you change my gauntlets for me?
    - **Player:** Can you change my gauntlets for me? {{anchor|change-gauntlets}}
    - *(If the player has no gauntlets:)*
      - **Johnathon:** You don't have any gauntlets I can imbue.
    - *(If the player currently has chaos gauntlets in inventory:)*
      - **Johnathon:** Your gauntlets are already imbued with my powers. You don't have any gauntlets I can imbue.
    - *(If the player has the gauntlets:)*
      - **Johnathon:** Yes certainly, though it will cost you 25,000 coins?
      - Select an option
        - Great thanks!
          - *(If the player has at least 25,000 coins:)*
            - (removes=family gauntlets and 25,000 coins) (receives=chaos gauntlets)
            - **Johnathon:** Your gauntlets are already imbued with my powers.
          - *(If the player has less than 25,000 coins:)*
            - **Johnathon:** Unfortunately you don't have enough coins on you, come back with 25,000 and I'll change those gauntlets for you.
        - No that's ok thanks.
          - (end)
  - *(After having the family gauntlets enchanted for the first time:)*
    - Does your family own any more gauntlets?
      - **Player:** Does your family own any more gauntlets? I really appreciate the skill and effort you and your brothers have put into those gauntlets, and to prove it, I would like to own one of each pair.
      - **Johnathon:** I own a pair my father gave me, but they are a family heirloom and I’m not supposed to part with them. However if my dad saw fit to give you his, I could be convinced.
      - **Player:** How much would you want for them?
      - **Johnathon:** 1 million coins.
      - Select an option
        - I would like to buy them for 1 million.
          - **Player:** I would like to buy them for 1 million.
          - **Johnathon:** Are you sure? That's a lot of money!
          - Select an option
            - **Player:**Yes!
              - (removes=1,000,000 coins) (receives=chaos gauntlets)
            - **Player:**I suppose not.
              - (end)
        - I suppose you had better keep them.
          - **Player:** I suppose you had better keep them.
          - **Johnathon:** Thank you!
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
