## Dialogue Transcript

- **Jakob:** Welcome to NovtumberFest! Cooking is great!
- Select an option
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - **Player:** Who are you?
      - **Jakob:** My name is Jakob, I'm in charge of watching over the grills.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - How do the grills work?
      - **Player:** How do the grills work?
      - **Jakob:** It's dead easy!
      - **Jakob:** Just head over to the grill and get those sausages sizzling!
      - You will receive Cooking XP for cooking sausages.
      - **Jakob:** Best of all you can keep any which you cook.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - Can you tell me about your sausages?
      - **Player:** Can you tell me about your sausages?
      - **Jakob:** You could at least buy me dinner first...
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Bye.
    - **Jakob:** It's been nice to meat you!
