## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- Jonny the beard is not interested in talking.

### In New Varrock


#### Before Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav

- **Jonny the Beard:** I'd give my left arm to join your band.
- **Ping:** Pong, he's back again.
- **Pong:** Tell him we don't want groupies.
- **Jonny the Beard:** I just want to be Jonny the Bard! <br>I play a mean flute!

#### After Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav


##### If the player sided with the Black Arm Gang

- **Jonny the Beard:** My dreams of being Jonny the Bard are over. <br> You can't play a flute wit' one arm.
- **Player:** Jonny the Bard?
- **Jonny the Beard:** All my life - and zombie life - I been dreamin' of playing in taverns.
- **Jonny the Beard:** I were gonna play the flute like a demon, I tells ya. <br>Ne'er mind. Back to a life of crime...

##### If the player sided with the Phoenix Gang

- **Jonny the Beard:** A pox on Katrine and her recruitment process.<br>One of her Black Arm initiates beat me up and stole my arm.
