## Dialogue Transcript


### Day 1

- **John:** Where did it come from?
- **Player:** I hear it came from the well. Hey, that's a cute dog! Does he have a name?
- **John:** No.
- **Player:** ... Okay.

### Day 2

- <nowiki>*</nowiki>Dog is barking beside the well<nowiki>*</nowiki>
- **John:** What's that boy? Joey fell down the well!?
- **Player:** ... How on Gielinor did that happen?
- **John:** He probably lost his glasses. And without them he just didn't see well.

### Day 4

- **John:** Dog. Stay.

### Day 6

- **John:** Well, that's it over.
- **Player:** It sure is!
- John looks down to Dog
- **John:** It's okay. Let's go home.
- Dog barks happily
