## Dialogue Transcript


### In Edgeville

- **Jeffery:** Keep it quick. What do you want?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: *(After Gunnar's Ground)* Who was that love poem for?
    - **Jeffery:** It, er, it didn't work out well. I don't want to talk about it! Leave me alone!
  - [Choice]: I want to use the furnace.
    - **Player:** I want to use the furnace.
    - (Condition: On free-to-play worlds:)
      - **Jeffery:** You want to use my furnace? No one can use my furnace! Only I can use my furnace!
    - (Condition: If the player has not completed the easy Varrock tasks:)
      - **Jeffery:** You want to use my furnace? I only let exceptional people use my furnace. You don't look exceptional to me.
      - **Player:** How do I become exceptional?
      - **Jeffery:** Exceptional people have earned exceptional items; earning Varrock armour would impress me.
      - **Player:** Alright!
    - (Condition: If the player has completed the easy Varrock tasks:)
      - **Jeffery:** You seem exceptional enough. Go ahead.
      - **Player:** What can I make here, exactly?
      - **Jeffery:** Well, depending on your skill as a blacksmith, you can use this furnace to smelt ore into metal bars.
      - **Player:** Oh, I see. What's so special about this furnace, then?
      - **Jeffery:** If you smelt at this furnace while wearing your Varrock armour the enchantment on the armour will give you a small chance of smelting two bars instead of one.
      - **Player:** I see. So, which metal will I be able to obtain more of when smelting with the armour I'm wearing?
      - (Condition: If the player has their Varrock armour equipped:)
        - **Jeffery:** While wearing the Varrock armour, you will have a chance of smelting an extra bar of any metal up to, and including, adamant.
        - **Player:** Oh, that's useful. That should save me a fair bit of time. Thanks very much.
        - **Jeffery:** Stay exceptional!
      - (Condition: If the player does not have their Varrock armour equipped:)
        - **Jeffery:** Try coming back when you're actually wearing your Varrock armour.
        - **Player:** Oh, right. Yes, I see. Okay, thanks.
  - [Choice]: Er, nothing.
    - **Player:** Er, nothing.

#### Attempting to use his furnace when not allowed

- **Jeffery:** Keep away from that! It's dangerous!
