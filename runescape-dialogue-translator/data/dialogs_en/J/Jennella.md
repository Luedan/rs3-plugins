# Jennella

- **Letter**: `J`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to her

- **Jennella:** I cannot speak to you outerlander! Talk to Brundt, the Chieftain!

#### Pickpocketing her

- **Jennella:** {{RSChat|You stay away from me outerlander!}}
- They are too suspicious of you for you to get close enough to steal from them.

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello.
- *Jennella will greet the player with any of the following at random, before continuing with the dialogue:*
  - *Greeting 1:*
    - **Jennella:** How are you, [Fremennik name]? I hope all fares well with you this day!
    - **Player:** I'm fine, thanks, how are you?
    - **Jennella:** All is well with me also!
  - *Greeting 2:*
    - **Jennella:** Hello again, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. What need you of me this day? You can always rely on my assistance!
    - *If the player hasn't completed Hero's Welcome:*
      - **Player:** Nothing thanks. I just fancied a chat.
      - **Jennella:** My door is always open should you ever need my help [Fremennik name].
  - *Greeting 3:*
    - **Jennella:** Hello. Did you want something, [Fremennik name]?
    - *If the player hasn't completed Hero's Welcome:*
      - **Player:** No, not really.
      - **Jennella:** Well... okay then!
  - *Greeting 4:*
    - **Jennella:** [Fremennik name], it has been too long since last we met!
  - *Greeting 5:*
    - **Jennella:** Welcome back, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. It is always a pleasure to see you here!
  - *Greeting 6:*
    - **Jennella:** Hello to you, too!
  - *Greeting 7:*
    - **Jennella:** Always good to see you, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name].
- *(If the player has completed Hero's Welcome, the dialogue continues below.)*

#### After Hero's Welcome

- Select an option
  - Can I ask you about V?
  - *Jennella may reply to you with any of the following at random:*
    - *Reply 1:*
      - **Jennella:** He died saving you, [Fremennik name]. Make sure you use his final gift wisely and live a life that we can all be proud of.
    - *Reply 2:*
      - **Jennella:** He was a great hero to our people. Why couldn't they have gone for one of the bad gods, like Zamorak or Saradomin?
    - *Reply 3:*
      - **Jennella:** Those cowards. They didn't even have the decency to face him as a warrior.
    - *Reply 4:*
      - **Jennella:** It's sad that he's gone, but he will live on as an inspiration to countless heroes across countless worlds.
    - Goodbye.
      - (end)
