## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet

- *Jasim the Camel and the player say one of the following at random:*
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Player:** I'm not going to pet that! I might get fleas or something else that nasty creature might have.
    - (Condition: If the player is wearing a camulet:)
      - **Jasim the Camel:** You're fairly nasty yourself, human. Poking and prodding me like a lump of meat.
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Player:** I'm not going to pet that! It will probably bite my hand off, that filthy beast.
    - (Condition: If the player is wearing a camulet:)
      - **Jasim the Camel:** Watch who you're calling a filthy beast, human.
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - **Player:** That beast would probably bite my fingers off if I tried to pet it.
    - (Condition: If the player is wearing a camulet:)
      - **Jasim the Camel:** If you want me to bite your fingers off, you're going the right way about it.
  - *Dialogue 4:*
    - **Player:** Mmmm... Won't you make the nicest kebab?
    - (Condition: If the player is wearing a camulet:)
      - **Jasim the Camel:** How would you like it if you were destined for the kebab shop when you finally keeled over?
