## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Jiminua:** Welcome to Jiminua's Jungle Store. Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please. What are you selling?
    - **Jiminua:** Take a good look.
    - (opens=Jiminua's Jungle Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Jiminua:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - [Choice]: Can you un-note any of my items?
    - **Jiminua:** I can un-note pure essence, but nothing else. Just give me the notes you wish to exchange.
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)
