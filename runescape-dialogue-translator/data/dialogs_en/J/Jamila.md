## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hi. Are you one of the returned townspeople?
- **Jamila:** Why, yes. You must be the adventurer Maisa told us about.
- **Player:** I guess so.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: I'd like to shop, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like to shop, please.
    - **Jamila:** Certainly!
      - (opens=Jamila's Craft Stall)
  - [Choice]: How is business?
    - **Player:** How is business?
    - **Jamila:** Slow at the moment, so slow. Maybe time will make things better.
  - [Choice]: Any tasks for me that you can think of?
    - **Player:** Any tasks for me that you can think of?
    - **Jamila:** I have no plans that would require your mighty services...at least, not yet.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye for now.
    - **Player:** Goodbye for now
    - **Jamila:** Come back soon!
