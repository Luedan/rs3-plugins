## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hi. You look quite important. What do you do here?
- **Junior Cadet Finda:** Shhh, they're watching!
- **Player:** What? Who?
- **Junior Cadet Finda:** There are secret agents everywhere. Trust no-one!
- **Player:** Why, what's the matter?
- **Junior Cadet Finda:** Such a conspiracy like you wouldn't imagine. I believe this place is the source, as I shall soon find out.
- **Player:** Okay...
- **Junior Cadet Finda:** I've said too much. Please - go on your way.
