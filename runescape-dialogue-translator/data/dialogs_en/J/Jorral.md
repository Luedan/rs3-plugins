## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Jorral:** Why is it that whenever that enchanted key re-appears, you soon come visiting?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: How's the archaeology going?

    - **Jorral:** Good. I'm looking more into the background of that enchanted key; hopefully, we'll be able to time travel with it again in future.
    - **Player:** Don't you mean the past?
    - **Jorral:** Yes, travel to the past, in our future. I hadn't even considered trying to travel into the future!
