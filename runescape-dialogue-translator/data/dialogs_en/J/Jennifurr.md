## Dialogue Transcript


### Without catspeak

- **Jennifurr:** Meow, meow meow.

### With catspeak

- **Jennifurr:** Don't mind me, I'm just checking on the mummies' enchantments. Don't want them falling apart mid-game, do we?
- **Player:** Their enchantments?
- **Jennifurr:** Oh, yes. The mummies are kept animated by powerful enchantments passed down from cat to cat.
- **Jennifurr:** My family line has kept these mummies going for centuries. It's quite the honour.
