## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Jade:** Greetings warrior, how may I help you?
- (Condition: If the player has items waiting for them in the Grand Exchange:)
  - **Jade:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - **Player:** I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(Bank Pin Settings interface opens)*
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see my Collection box.
    - **Player:** I'd like to see my Collection box.
    - (opens=Collection box)
  - [Choice]: How long have you worked here?
    - **Player:** How long have you worked here?
    - **Jade:** Oh ever since the Guild opened. I like it here.
    - **Player:** Why's that?
    - **Jade:** Well.. what with all these warriors around, there's not much chance of my bank being robbed is there!
    - (previous)
