### {{Anchor|Assignment advice}}Assignment advice


#### Bats

- **Jacquelyn:** Bats are rarely found on the ground, so you'll have to fight them while they're airborne. You can find some flying around in the Lumbridge Catacombs.

#### Birds

- **Jacquelyn:** Birds aren't the most intelligent of creatures, but watch out for their sharp, stabbing beaks. Even the chickens in Lumbridge are after your blood, so get them before they get you.

#### Cave bugs

- **Jacquelyn:** Cave bugs are like cave crawlers, except smaller and easier to squish; although, they still have a fondness for plants. They lurk in the dark beneath Lumbridge Swamp.

#### Cave slime

- **Jacquelyn:** Cave slimes are the lesser cousins of jellies. Don't be fooled, though: they can still be dangerous, as they're often poisonous. Killing the ones beneath Lumbridge Swamp might be a good start.

#### Cows

- **Jacquelyn:** Cows are bigger than you, and they'll often hit hard but react fairly slowly. You can slaughter the ones in Lumbridge - I bet they are up to no good.

#### Frogs

- **Jacquelyn:** Frogs will attack and move faster than the average human, but on the whole an experienced warrior can deal with a lot of them quite quickly. You can find them in the swamp to the south.

#### Ghosts

- **Jacquelyn:** The last Ghost I killed I found thrown weapons took them down effectively.
- Ghosts are undead, so not the easiest thing to find in populated areas. Try looking in the abandoned Draynor Manor.

#### Goblins

- **Jacquelyn:** Goblins are mostly just annoying, but they can be vicious. Watch out for the weapons they sometimes carry. You can find them on the east side of the river here in Lumbridge.

#### Rats

- **Jacquelyn:** Rats come in different sizes if you want a challenge or not. You can find the normal variety lurking around the castle grounds or head south into the swamp for giant rats.

#### Skeletons

- **Jacquelyn:** Skeletons are undead, so Magic is your best bet against them. Skeletons can be found in the Lumbridge Catacombs.

#### Spiders

- **Jacquelyn:** Spiders are often venomous and many varieties are camouflaged, too. I've seen plenty to the south-west of Lumbridge.

#### Zombies

- **Jacquelyn:** Zombies are undead, so be careful. You can find a variety of corpse fighters in the Lumbridge Catacombs or zombies in the sewers under Draynor village.
