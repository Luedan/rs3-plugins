# Jimmy Dazzler

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Jimmy Dazzler](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AJimmy%20Dazzler)
- **Letter**: `J`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Rat Catchers

- **Jimmy Dazzler:** What is your name and what is your business here?
- **Player:** My name is Player, and I have a couple of questions for you.
- **Jimmy Dazzler:** No.
- **Player:** What do you mean by no?
- **Jimmy Dazzler:** That just isn't the ticket. I don't know you nor will I answer any questions. Now begone from here, you'll attract unwanted attention to me.
- **Player:** As if those 'clothes' you're wearing wouldn't do that already.

### After Rat Catchers

- **Jimmy Dazzler:** Ah, Player, aren't you just the ticket?
- [Menu]: Can you tell me where I could find...
  - [Choice]: Grimesquit and Phingspet
    - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find Phingspet and Grimesquit?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** You can find those vile harpies in the sewers of Varrock.
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** ~ Jimmy shudders when he thinks of them. ~
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** If you ask me, it's too good a place for them.
  - [Choice]: Hooknosed Jack
    - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find Hooknosed Jack?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** He should be in the roughest area of Varrock, knowing him. He likes a good scrap does Jack.
  - [Choice]: Smokin' Joe
    - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find Smokin' Joe?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** You won't find him around here. They wouldn't let a filthy old dwarf like him into a civilised town like this.
    - **Player:** Well, if he's not here, where would I find him?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** If the old rascal's lungs haven't given up yet, you should find him in Keldagrim, the city of dwarves.
  - [Choice]: The Face
    - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find 'The Face'?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** Old Pugly? My oh my, she got her comeuppance alright!
    - **Player:** Old Pugly?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** Pug ugly.
    - **Player:** That's really nasty.
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** Believe me, she deserves everything she gets!
    - **Player:** What did she do?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** I couldn't possibly tell you, but be careful around her.
    - **Player:** Will you please tell me where I could find her?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** She'll be in or around Port Sarim I reckon.
  - [Choice]: Felkrash
    - **Player:** Can you tell me where I would find Felkrash?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** The Boss? You'll find her in Port Sarim.
    - **Player:** She's your boss?
    - **Jimmy Dazzler:** Well, sort of. She's the unofficial head of the rat catchers.
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - (end)
