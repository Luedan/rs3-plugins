## Dialogue Transcript


### In the H.A.M. Hideout

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What kind of organisation is this?
    - **Player:** What kind of organisation is this?
    - **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** We're a proactive organisation working towards the cessation of monsters in normal civilised human society. There seems to be no backbone in this land so we're stepping up to the challenge.
    - **Player:** Hmm, how do you propose to do that? I mean, I see people every day killing goblins around here!
    - **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** We're mobilising people and we're starting our own society...cleaning out the caves once inhabited by these foul creatures and defending them so that they can never again shelter sub-human species.
    - **Player:** That sounds kind of strange, but, hey, it's your choice.
    - (initial|standard)
  - [Choice]: Who are you and what do you do here?
    - **Player:** Who are you and what do you do here?
    - **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** My name is Johanhus and I lead these glorious people on a courageous mission called 'Humans Against Monsters'. We mean to make this land free of monsters, so that we can all live in peace.
    - (initial|standard)
  - [Choice]: Okay, thanks.
    - **Player:** Okay, thanks.

#### First time speaking to him after The Chosen Commander

- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Sad news, brother. Sigmund’s group has been destroyed by a goblin attack.
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** This just goes to show the danger of taking action directly… All that Sigmund’s attacks achieved was getting some good humans killed.
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Besides, the cave goblins aren’t much of a threat. They may not be human, but they’re no worse than the dwarves or gnomes. From now on, we’ll leave them alone, and focus on occupying caves and lobbying the government.
- (continuea|standard)
