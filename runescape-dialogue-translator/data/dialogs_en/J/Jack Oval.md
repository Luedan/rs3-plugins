## Dialogue Transcript


### In Burthorpe

- **Jack Oval:** You need to make things. Leather. Pottery. Every piece you make will increase your skill.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I need Crafting supplies.
    - (opens=Jack Oval's Shop)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about Crafting and other artisan skills.
    - **Jack Oval:** Crafting is the most versatile skill by far. You can make leather armour, pottery, jewellery and even glasswork.
    - **Jack Oval:** If you ever get as good as me, you'll be able to perform extraordinary feats like making armour from the hides of dragons!
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Tell me about Fletching.
        - **Jack Oval:** Fletching is about making bows and arrows. The better you are at it, and the better the quality of your materials, the better the result.
        - **Jack Oval:** Every bow and every arrow you make will increase your skill.
        - **Jack Oval:**  If you expect to spend any time as an archer you'll want to ensure you have the arrows you need.
        - **Jack Oval:** Most people have no appreciation for how many arrows an army goes through every day. We could really use your help re-supplying them.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about Smithing.
        - **Jack Oval:** Smithing is all about making weapons. And armour! The better you are at smithing, the higher the quality of the metal you can work with and the better the resulting items are.
        - **Jack Oval:** Every bar of metal you smelt and every metal item you smith will improve your abilities.
        - **Jack Oval:** As well as being able to make gear directly, some of the most powerful ancient relics need an expert smith to reassemble their fragmented parts.
        - **Jack Oval:** Burthorpe's militia need to be kept well supplied with arms and armour. It falls to smiths to meet that need.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: (back)
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the skills menu.
    - {{Qact|Skills infobox opens.}}
    - You have many skills you can train. The more you practice a skill, the better it gets. Higher skill levels unlock new activities: equipment to craft, locations to explore, quests to complete, and much more.
    - Perform actions associated with a skill to earn experience points (XP) in that skill. Earn enough XP and you'll gain a level in that skill.
    - Most skills start at level 1, and you unlock all their benefits by leveling the skill to 99. (Some skills have unlocks up to level 120.)
    - The bottom number next to a skill icon is your base level in that skill (un-modified by buffs or debuffs). It is used for most requirement checks.
    - The top number is your modified skill level (temporarily boosted or drained by skill buffs and debuffs - such as potions or spells). It is used for most skill checks.
    - Achieve 99 in all your skills to earn the coveted Max Cape!
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
