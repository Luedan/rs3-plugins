# Kjut

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Kjut](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AKjut)
- **Letter**: `K`

## Dialogue Transcript


### In eastern Keldagrim

- **Kjut:** Kebabs! Get your kebabs here! One gold each!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Sure, one kebab please.
    - **Player:** Sure, one kebab please.
    - (Condition: If the player has at least one coin:)
      - (removes=one coin) (receives=a kebab)
      - You buy a kebab.
    - (Condition: If the player has no coins:)
      - **Kjut:** You have no money, effendi.
      - You can not afford that.
  - [Choice]: I'm looking for a drunken dwarf.
    - **Player:** I'm looking for a drunken dwarf.
    - (Condition: Before Forgettable Tale of a Drunken Dwarf:)
      - **Kjut:** Just go out in the streets, they can't be hard to find!
      - **Player:** No, a specific drunken dwarf, he keeps harassing me.
      - **Kjut:** That's still not very helpful. But let me see. There is one particularly drunken dwarf just to the northeast of here.
      - **Kjut:** He's scaring away my customers, he says there's something wrong with my kebabs. Quite annoying fellow.
      - **Player:** I don't think that's who I mean.
      - **Kjut:** Well, he does have a relative who wanders all over Gielinor. Comes back from time to time to buy more kebabs. Don't see him very often though.
      - **Player:** Do you think I'll have any chance of catching him here?
      - **Kjut:** I think he was back here quite recently, so I doubt he'll be back any time soon.
    - (Condition: After Forgettable Tale of a Drunken Dwarf:)
      - **Kjut:** I thought you would know plenty!
      - **Player:** How do you mean?
      - **Kjut:** Oh come on! You in the Laughing Miner? The whole city knows about you and your dwarven drinking buddies!
      - **Player:** Right, right.
  - [Choice]: Do you have any quests?
    - **Player:** Do you have any quests?
    - **Kjut:** Not really! I have a steady supply of meat coming in through the carts and I don't really need anything else!

#### Using Kjut's sink

- **Kjut:** Hey, do you mind? That's my property!

#### Using any item on Kjut's fireplace

- **Kjut:** Hey, what are you trying to put into my kebabs? Get away from there, get your own fireplace!
