## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Dig Site


#### Talk to option

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Katarina (Student):** Hi there. I'm studying for the Earth Sciences exam.
- **Player:** Interesting... This exam seems to be a popular one!

#### Pickpocket option

- I don't think I should try to steal from this poor student.

#### Using an animal skull on her

- Perhaps I should try talking to her first.
- Nothing interesting happens.

### After The Dig Site


#### Before unlocking research

- **Katarina (student)** Hi there! Thanks again. Hey, maybe I'll be asking you for help next time. It seems you are something of an expert now!

#### After unlocking research


##### Before joining research team

- **Player** How would you like to be part of my new research team?
- **Katarina (student)** Sounds great! Just one problem... I'm not an intern yet. Acting Guildmaster Reiniger wants me to make a notable discovery.
- **Katarina (student)** Ideally, something in my field of effigies, icons and simulacra. I've heard that Kharid-et has these training dummies modelled after chthonian demons...
- (Condition: Without a Zarosian training dummy:)
  - **Player** I've got you covered. You helped me out a lot with my exams, it's only fair.
  - **Katarina (student)** Tell you what - if you can bring me a restored Zarosian training dummy from Kharid-et, I'll head straight to research!
  - **Player** I'm on it!
- (Condition: With a Zarosian training dummy:)
  - **Player** I have a restored Zarosian training dummy from Kharid-et...
  - [Menu]: Give Zarosian training dummy to Katarina?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - (removes=Zarosian training dummy)
      - Katrina has joined your research team!
      - (pop-up)
        - {{Pop-up|Katrina has joined your research team!}}
      - **Katarina (student)**  Ooh, it's so cuddly! I mean, it's perfect, thanks, boss!
    - [Choice]: No.
      - **Player** Let me get back to you on this.
      - **Katarina (student)** Take your time, it's all good.

##### After joining research team

- **Katarina (student)** Ready for research whenever you need me, boss!

#### Pickpocket option

- (Condition: If the player has the Teddy quest item in their backpack or bank:)
  - I don't think I should try to steal from this poor student.
- (Condition: If the player fails to pickpocket her:)
  - **Katarina:** {{RSChat|Hey, What do you think you're doing?}}
  - You fail to pick the student's pocket.
- (Condition: If the player succeeds in pickpocketing her:)
  - (receives=Teddy)
  - You pick the student's pocket...

#### Using Teddy on her

- **Player:** I am not sure why I am giving this to the student...
- Nothing interesting happens.
