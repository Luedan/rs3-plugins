## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When attempting to speak to Khazard Guard (sleeping) in the Fight Arena Bar*
- **Player:** Hello?

- **Player:** I was wondering...

- **Khazard Guard:** *SNORE*
- **Player:** I see. Well, thanks anyway.
