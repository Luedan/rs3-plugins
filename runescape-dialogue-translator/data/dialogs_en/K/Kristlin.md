- Why are you here?
  - **Kristlin:** I travel with Menowin, and his group of friends. It allows me to talk to many different people, and to impart my knowledge as I go. In fact, meeting so many different people seems to increase my abilities. The more I travel the clearer my fortunes become.
  - **Player:** What abilities do you have?
  - **Kristlin:** I have only one ability, and that is to read the fortunes of people; to know what their destiny is. It's still only in the short-term at the moment, but as my powers increase so does the length of time I can see into the future for people.
    - How do you read fortunes?
      - **Kristlin:** I have a deck of fortune cards. All you need to do is choose the three cards that call to you the strongest after I spread them out on the table. You should go with your instinct. There are no right or wrong answers.
      - **Player:** Where did you get your cards from?
      - **Kristlin:** I created my deck of fortune cards myself. The names and images of the cards came to me in my dreams, over several months. I realized when I focused on those images my ability to read the future for people became stronger, and clearer. This was the best way I could think of to hone my focus. It seems to work quite well.
      - **Player:** Are you cards based on anything in-particular?
      - **Kristlin:** Just the flashes of images and concepts that come to me as I dream. They have been coming to me since I was a little girl. I believe one day I will understand what they truly are, but until that day I am a{{Sic}} happy to be a servant to their power.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - What is your fortune?
      - **Kristlin:** Alas, I dont now know that. I cannnot read my own fortune. I have tried many times, but the cards never give me a clear answer. I have come to accept this, though, and believe that my future is wrapped up in the fortunes of others, and through my work I am fulfilling my destiny.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - What does my fortune mean?
      - *(If a cloud of destiny isn't active:)*
        - **Kristlin:** Ah well, to understand your fortune you would first need to get a reading. Would you like me to read your fortune?
      - *(If a cloud of destiny is active:)*
        - **Kristlin:** Your fortune is your own, and can only truly be deciphered by you. All answers will come in time.
        - **Kristlin:** However, looking at your aura, you appear to be in a positive state, and I am sure that the fortune will become clear to you and will be a rewarding experience.
        - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - What does cloud of destiny mean?
      - **Kristlin:** Ah! Lucky people, when they receive a fortune, receive a 'cloud of destiny'. If they then do what the cloud of destiny portends, they receive a boon.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - More.
      - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - How did you meet Menowin?
    - **Kristlin:** He and his band of friends came through my village one day, looking for shelter as they traveled on to the next city. I was working in a bar the the time. Menowin was the last customer to leave that night, and as I was clearing up I offered to read his fortune. It was the strongest feeling I had ever got from a fortune and it was like fate was pushing me towards him. I believe he felt the same as he offered to let me join him on his travels.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if nothing has been asked before:)*
  - Do you have any cookies?
    - *(If the player does not have a cookie in their inventory or bank:)*
      - **Kristlin:** Would you like a cookie?
        - Yes.
          - **Player:** Is there a fortune inside the cookie?
          - **Kristlin:** What a strange idea. I might have to look into that for the future.
        - No.
          - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
    - *(If the player already has a cookie in their inventory or bank:)*
      - **Kristlin:** I'd offer you another, but it looks like you haven't eaten your last one.
- *(Only after asking something else:)*
  - Bye.
    - **Kristlin:** May your path be clear and guide you towards happiness.

### Cavern of Tainted Memories


#### Talking to

- **Kristlin:** Hello there my darling! I seem to have uncovered a special cavern in my quest to further my divining knowledge!
- **Player:** What makes this cavern so special?
- **Kristlin:** It appears to be home to a certain kind of rare wisp. They have been corrupted with the evil memories of the world.
- **Kristlin:** However it isn't all doom and gloom. It turns out these wisps can be harvested for a type of essence that can be cleansed and forged into some rather lovely weaponry.
- **Player:** That sounds pretty awesome!
- **Kristlin:** Quite. But I can't just be allowing this precious resource to be harvested unchecked. We don't know whether it is finite or not.
- **Kristlin:** I will allove you a short period of time in there in each day. If you bring me a cavern entry token, I'll allow you in again!
- When entering the cavern, you will have 2 minutes 30 seconds to collect as much tainted essence as you can before you are removed from the area. <span style="color:#008C2A">Stand in multiplier locations to recieve{{Sic}} increased XP and essence. Collect tainted chronicles from afar to gather a lump of essence.</span>
- **Kristlin:** If you don't want to receive Divination XP from the cavern, I could give you a bundle of other rewards instead.
- Would you like to receive Divination XP or mystery rewards?
  - Divination XP
    - **Kristlin:** Very well. You will now receive Divination XP whilst you are in the cavern.
  - Mystery rewards
    - **Kristlin:** Very well. You will now receive some mystery rewards when you leave the cavern.
    - <span style="color:red">Note :</span> You will still have to earn your rewards. The amount you receive will be based on the amount of XP you would have earned.

#### Changing prize

- **Kristlin:** If you don't want to receive Divination XP from the cavern, I could give you a bundle of other rewards instead.
- *(Continues as above.)*
