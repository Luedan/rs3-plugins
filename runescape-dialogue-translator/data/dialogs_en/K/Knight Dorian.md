    - **Knight Dorian:** It's up to you to decide how you wish to balance your squad based on their cost.
    - **Player:** What about if some of my troops fall in battle...how do I go about replacing them?

    - **Knight Dorian:** Troops defeated in combat will simply be removed from the Conquest and will be ready to fight again when you face a new opponent.
