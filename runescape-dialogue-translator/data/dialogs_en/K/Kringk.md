## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** What's going on here?

- **Player:** Well, excuse me!
- **Kringk:** No excuse for you - you in my way.

### After As a First Resort

- **Kringk:** I would offer haircut, but it hard to do style for puny human with puny head.

  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
