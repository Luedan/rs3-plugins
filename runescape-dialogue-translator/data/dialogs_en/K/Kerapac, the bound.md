## Dialogue Transcript


### Start of the fight

- **Kerapac:** No... Always you... Can't you just let it be, World Guardian?

### Shatter

- **Kerapac:** I'll tear right through you.

### Jumping Slam

*One of the following is picked at random*
- **Kerapac:** I will put you in your place.
- **Kerapac:** You will break beneath me.

### Attempting to use Devotion against Jumping Slam

- **Kerapac:** Your godly devotion won't save you here, Player.

### Lightning Strike

- **Kerapac:** Witness the raw power of the Staff.

### Phase 2 Start

- **Kerapac:** No. I control the threads of time.
- **Kerapac:** The time threads have split!

### Phase 3 Start

- **Kerapac:** I... can... contain... ALL. ITS. POWER.
- **Kerapac:** The time threads have split!

### Phase 4 Start (Normal Mode)

- **Kerapac:** If the needle won't heed my command. Then I'll use a greater weapon. The staff of Armadyl.

### Phase 4 Start (Hard Mode)

- **Kerapac:** Look at what you've done! You've fractured the threads of time!

### Echo of Kerapac defeated (Hard Mode only)

- **Player Echo:** We did it! He's dead!

### Ending Encounter

- **Kerapac:** NO! Let me die! JUST LET ME DIE!

### Killing a player

*One of the following is picked at random*
- **Kerapac:** Pitiful.
- **Kerapac:** Inevitable.
- **Kerapac:** We will all be free.
- **Kerapac:** Your timeline ends here.
