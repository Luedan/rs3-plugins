## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Biohazard

### After Biohazard, before Underground Pass

- **Player:** Hello King Lathas.
- **King Lathas:** Adventurer, thank Saradomin or your arrival!
- **Player:** Have your scouts found a way through the mountains?
- **King Lathas:** Not quite, we found a path to where we expected to find the Well of Voyage, an ancient portal to the western mainland. However during recent times a cluster of cultists have settled there, run by a madman named Iban.
- *(Quest acceptance screen)*

### After Underground Pass, before Regicide

*Before obtaining the Letter from the Messenger*
- **Player:** Hello King Lathas.
- **King Lathas:** Well hello there traveller. The magi are still resurrecting the Well of Voyage, but I'll have word sent to you as soon as it's ready.
- **Player:** Ok then, take care.
- **King Lathas:** You too.

### After Regicide, before Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Good day my lord.
- **King Lathas:** Hello Player. I've got nothing for you right now.

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Good day.
- **King Lathas:** What are you doing here? All Mourners should know that if they need to contact me, they need to use the proper channels!
- **Player:** Sorry, I'm a new recruit.
- **King Lathas:** Well do not let it happen again.
