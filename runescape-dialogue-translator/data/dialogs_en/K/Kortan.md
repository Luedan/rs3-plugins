## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Kortan:** Hello, you look like a bold adventurer. You've come to the right place for adventurers' equipment.
- Select an option
  - Oh, that sounds interesting.
    - (opens=Aemad's Adventuring Supplies)
  - How should I use your shop?
    - **Kortan:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - No, sorry, I've come to the wrong place.
    - **Kortan:** Hmph. Well, perhaps next time you'll need something from me?
