### After Plague City, before Underground Pass

- Koftik doesn't seem interested in talking.

### After Underground Pass, before Plague's End


#### In West Ardougne, outside of the entrance to the Underground Pass

- **Player:** Hello Koftik.
- **Koftik:** Hello Adventurer, how's things?
- **Player:** Not bad, yourself?
- **Koftik:** I'm good... just keeping an eye out.

#### In the Underground Pass, near the Well of Voyage


##### If you have not yet recently taken food from Koftik

- **Player:** Hello Koftik.
- **Koftik:** Hello Player. How's things?
- **Player:** I got pretty hurt getting here. These traps don't get any easier.
- **Koftik:** Nope, they don't. Here eat this, it should help.
- **Player:** Thanks Koftik.
- **Koftik:** No problem.

##### If you have recently taken food from Koftik


##### Conversation 1

- **Player:** Hello Koftik.
- **Koftik:** Hello Player. How's things?
- **Player:** These traps don't get any easier. But I'm alright, thanks.
- **Koftik:** Good to hear.

##### Conversation 2

- **Player:** Hello Koftik.
- **Koftik:** Hello Player. How's things?
- **Player:** I'm fine, thanks.
- **Koftik:** Good to hear.
