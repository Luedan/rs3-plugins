## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: cond=If the player has a dragon defender in backpack|Talk about advanced defenders.
    - **Kamfreena:** My eye sees you have yourself a dragon defender!
    - **Player:** Is that another cyclops pun?
    - **Kamfreena:** Sorry. Force of habit.
    - **Kamfreena:** I have heard that if you take the dragon defender with you to a certain challenge you can get even better defenders!
    - **Player:** Any hints on where?
    - **Kamfreena:** I heard of a group of brothers, east of Mort'ton. Rumors say they have Corruption sigil that is used in the construction of the defender!
    - **Player:** Any other information?
    - **Kamfreena:** It's also said that you can construct ranged and magic versions too, and all these defenders can get you even more powerful ones!
    - **Player:** Wow, that's incredible! How do you know about those?
    - **Kamfreena:** I like to keep my eye on things.
    - **Player:** Stop that.
    - **Kamfreena:** Sorry.
    - **Player:** Where are the others?
    - **Kamfreena:** Nex has an Ancient emblem that can be combined with a Chaotic splint can get you an advanced defender in your choice of combat style.
    - **Player:** Oooo, any more?
    - **Kamfreena:** Of course, the best defenders I've heard of are made from perfect chitin from the Kalphite King. Just like the other defenders, you'll need to bring along the previous defender or better for a chance to get the required pieces.
    - **Player:** Great! Thanks!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Talk about Kamfreena.
    - **Kamfreena:** Why hello there! I'm Kamfreena. Like the look of my pets? I think they're eyeing you up.
    - **Player:** That was a really bad pun.
    - **Kamfreena:** Sorry... I don't get to see the rest of the guild much, stuck up here. The Cyclopes don't talk much you see.
    - **Player:** Shouldn't that be cyclopses?
    - **Kamfreena:** Nope! Cyclopes is the plural of cyclops. One cyclops, many cyclopes.
    - **Player:** Oh, right, thanks.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Talk about the cyclopses.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Where are they from?
        - **Player:** Where are they from?
        - **Kamfreena:** They're from the far-eastern lands. Indeed, the slayer masters have been in contact with us and are very interested in them. Vannaka and Chaeldar were especially interested.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: How did they get here?
        - **Player:** How did they get here?
        - **Kamfreena:** Ahh.. our guildmaster, Harrallak, went on an expedition there. He brought them back with him.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Why are they here?
        - **Player:** Why are they here?
        - **Kamfreena:** For the warriors to train on of course! They also drop a rather nice blade.
        - **Player:** Oh? What would that be?
        - **Kamfreena:** Defenders.
        - **Player:** Err what are they?
        - **Kamfreena:** It's a blade you can defend with using your shield hand, like I have.
        - **Player:** Wow!
        - **Kamfreena:** For every 50 tokens you collect around the guild, you can spend one minute with my pets. As you obtain defenders, you can show them to me and obtain even better ones, but remember: if you lose them, you'll have to start at bronze all over again. I'd advise keeping a spare in the bank.
        - **Player:** Okay!
        - **Kamfreena:** Oh, by the way, you'll need to earn 200 tokens of one type or 30 tokens of each type before I'll let you in!
        - **Player:** Right, I'd better go play some games then!
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Bye!
        - **Player:** Bye!
        - **Kamfreena:** See you back here soon I hope!

### Defender dialogue


#### Attempting to enter with insufficient points

- [You don't have enough Warrior Guild tokens to enter the cyclops enclosure yet. Collect at least 200 of one type of token, or 30 of each type, then come back.]

#### First time entering the door, with sufficient points

- **Kamfreena:** Well, since you haven't shown me a defender to prove your prowess as a warrior,
- **Kamfreena:** I'll release some cyclopes which might drop bronze defenders for you to start off with, unless you show me another. Have fun in there.

#### Subsequent times entering the door


##### If the player has not earned or shown a defender

- **Kamfreena:** I'll release some cyclopes which might drop bronze defenders for you to start off with, unless you show me another. Have fun in there.

##### Entering with a bronze iron/steel/black/mithril/adamant/rune defender

- **Kamfreena:** Ah, I see that you have one of the defenders already! Well done.
- **Kamfreena:** I'll release some cyclopes that might drop the next defender for you. Have fun in there.
- **Kamfreena:** Oh, and be careful: the cyclopes will occasionally summon a cyclossus. They are rather mean, and can only be hurt with a rune or better defender.

##### Entering with a dragon defender

- **Kamfreena:** Ah, I see that you have an excellent defender already! Well done.
- **Kamfreena:** I'll release some cyclopes that drop dragon defenders! Have fun in there.
- **Kamfreena:** Oh, and be careful: the cyclopes will occasionally summon a cyclossus. They are rather mean, and can only be hurt with a rune or dragon defender.

##### Entering having left the defender in the bank

- **Kamfreena:** I remember you....
- **Kamfreena:** You don't have the defender you earned last time.
- **Player:** I have one in the bank, I'll go get it!
- **Kamfreena:** Ok!

##### Entering having earned a defender but subsequently lost it

- **Kamfreena:** I remember you....
- **Kamfreena:** You don't have the defender you earned last time.
- **Player:** Err.. no, don't have it on me. I kind of... lost it.
- **Kamfreena:** Sorry, but as you've lost any that you earned before I can only let you in to kill the cyclopes that drop the bronze defenders. Go on in when you're ready.

##### Using a defender on Kamfreena

- **Player:** I have a [metal] defender, please let me in to kill some more cyclopes!
- (Condition: If using a bronze defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Bronze eh? The next cyclopes are ready, toddle in when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using an iron defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Wow, iron eh? The next cyclopes are ready, toddle in when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using a steel defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Strong as steel eh? The next cyclopes are ready, toddle in when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using a black defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Black, hmmm, about half way to Rune then! Keep going! Cyclopes have been released, enter when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using a mithril defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Mithril? Getting good at this aren't you? Ok, I've released those cyclopes that sometimes drop adamant defenders, enter when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using an adamant defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** An adamant defender, eh? Trying for the Rune defender I'm betting. Ok, I've released those cyclopes, enter when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using a rune defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Well done, you truly are a brilliant warrior! I've released some cyclopes that may well drop a dragon defender, enter when you're ready.
- (Condition: If using a dragon defender on Kamfreena:)
  - **Kamfreena:** Well done! You truly are a warrior of the highest order! I've released the cyclopes; enter when you're ready.

##### When the player has less than 30 seconds left while inside

- **Kamfreena:** Your time is up. Please make your way to the exit.

##### Upon running out of Warrior Guild points while inside

- {{qact|The player is teleported out of the room to Kamfreena.}}
- **Kamfreena:** Next time, please leave as soon as your time is up.
