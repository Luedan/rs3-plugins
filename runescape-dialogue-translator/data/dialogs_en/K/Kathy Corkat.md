## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Swan Song


#### At Eagles' Peak

- **Kathy Corkat:** Oh dear, oh dear, oh dear...
- **Player:** Oh dear?
- **Kathy Corkat:** It's terrible!
- **Player:** What's terrible?
- **Kathy Corkat:** Oh, never mind, dearie. <br>What can I do for you?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Can you take me downstream in your boat?
    - **Player:** Can you take me downstream in your boat?
    - **Kathy Corkat:** Ooh, it's dangerous up there at the moment!
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: I'm not afraid - let's go!
        - **Player:** I'm not afraid - let's go!
        - **Kathy Corkat:** It's dangerous up there for me too, dearie.
        - **Player:** Would it help if I gave you some money?
        - **Kathy Corkat:** Ooh, that's better! I'll take you up<br> north for 50 shiny coins.
        - (Condition: Without 50 coins:)
          - **Player:** I haven't got that much with me.
          - **Kathy Corkat:** Ooh, and you almost had me persuaded!
        - (Condition: With 50 coins:)
          - [Menu]: Select an Option
            - [Choice]: Alright, I'll pay.
              - **Player:** Alright, I'll pay.
              - (removes=50 coins)
              - [Kathy Corkat rows you down the river to the sea...]
            - [Choice]: Not a chance!
              - **Player:** Not a chance!
              - **Kathy Corkat:** Then you can find your own way north, dearie!
            - [Choice]: cond=With a ring of charos (a) equipped|[Charm] A lady this beautiful shouldn't be so afraid!
              - **Player:** A lady this beautiful shouldn't be so afraid!
              - **Kathy Corkat:** Oooh, you shouldn't tease a poor girl so! But I'll take you downstream if you like!
              - [Kathy Corkat rows you down the river to the sea...]
      - [Choice]: Dangerous? Nah, forget it!
        - **Player:** Dangerous? Nah, forget it!
  - [Choice]: I don't think I want anything, thanks.
    - **Player:** I don't think I want anything, thanks.
    - **Kathy Corkat:** Everybody wants something, dearie!
    - **Player:** Huh? Oh, forget it!
