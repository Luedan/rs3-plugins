## Dialogue Transcript


### When first spoken to

- **Kent:** Hey, Dave! You know you're not allowed to use the exchange.
- **Player:** What? Why?
- **Kent:** You know why!
- **Player:** Let's say for the sake of argument that I don't. Could you explain?
- **Kent:** Because last year you bought all that shadow dye! You don't even own anything tough enough to withstand it. You dissolved three of your poor mum's dresses!
- **Player:** Oh, uh, I'm very sorry about that. But look, I really need some runes.
- **Kent:** What for?
- **Player:** I need to stop a friend from making a terrible mistake I'll get blamed for.
- **Kent:** Okay Dave, seeing as it's you. What kind of runes are you after?
  - Law Runes.
    - **Kent:** Do I look like I'm made of money?
    - **Player:** Actually, yes.
    - **Kent:** Well, yes, I suppose I sort of do. But I won't be able to afford any more nice suits if I just hand out law runes left and right.
    - *(Shows other options)*
  - Earth Runes.
    - **Kent:** Oh, I don't have any earth runes I'm afraid. You might try speaking to one of the bankers though. I think I heard something about a competition.
    - *(Shows other options)*
  - Air Runes.
    - **Kent:** Yes, I have a few air runes I might be able to spare for you. I want something in return though.
    - **Player:** What?
    - **Kent:** I really need something to drink. Be a mate and go fetch us one, would you?

### After first speaking to

- **Kent:** Do you have anything for me to drink?
  - *If you do not have the Emergency flask of stew on you, or if the flask is empty:*
    - *(Dialogue ends)*
  - *If you have the Emergency flask of stew on you:*
    - **Kent:** What's this?
    - **Player:** I brought you something to drink. Yum yum!
    - **Kent:** Is this... one of your stews?
    - **Player:** I can only assume so!
    - **Kent:** No thank you. They're too spicy for my blood. Maybe you could find some water? Isn't there a fountain around here somewhere?
  - *If you have the Flask of stew-water on you:*
    - **Kent:** What's this?
    - **Player:** Some water. Mostly.
    - *The thirsty clerk gulps down the water, lumps and all.*
    - **Kent:** Was that a hint of orange?
    - **Player:** Possibly!
    - **Kent:** Delicious. Here's your air runes. Say hi to Doris for me.
    - *The clerk drops three air runes into your hand.*
    - **Player:** Brilliant! I have all the runes I need to teleport. Let's give teleporting a go! Tropelet, revoekam egam!
