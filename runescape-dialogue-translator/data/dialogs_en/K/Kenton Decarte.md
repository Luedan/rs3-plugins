#### Talk to

- (Condition: If the player has completed the Shattered Worlds tutorial:)
  - [Menu]: Select an option|anchor=initial-options
    - [Choice]: Who are you?
      - **Kenton Decarte:** I am the commander of the Abyssal Knights: we are elite soldiers forged from an alliance between the Void Knights and the Temple Knights.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Much as the Void Knights defend Gielinor from deadly creatures originating from the void, the Abyssal Knights stem the tide of deadly creatures that pour forth from the abyss.
      - (initial|initial-options)
    - [Choice]: Tell me about these 'shattered worlds'.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Think of them as 'abyssal tumours'. Shattered worlds are a disease of our own making.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Teleportation magic momentarily pierces the abyss, allowing anima from different worlds to leak in. Over time, this anima has coalesced into twisted amalgams of worlds travellers have visited.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** These 'shattered worlds' are inhabited by abyssal creatures, transformed and sustained by consuming otherworldly anima. They are highly dangerous and aggressive to all but their own kind.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** If the inhabitants of the shattered worlds were to infiltrate our own, the result could be catastrophic.
      - (initial|initial-options)
    - [Choice]: Can you explain how shattered worlds work?
      - **Kenton Decarte:** The worlds contain unpredictable horrors!
      - **Kenton Decarte:** To access the standard shattered worlds use the blue portal to my south.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** My knights will need you to complete a specific objective on each world to help them gain control of it. Once you complete the objective, find the exit portal to the next world.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Occasionally, you will find extra objectives in a world. You are not required to do these, but you will be rewarded if you do.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** As you climb through the worlds the creatures they contain will get exponentially more powerful. You may find you can progress no further without increasing your power.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** If my knights notice you moving through worlds quickly enough, they will allow you to skip some to put your skills to use where they are most needed.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Venturing to a new world can trigger a mutator that applies strange effects to the environment or inhabitants of that world and subsequent worlds.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** When this happens you have a choice between three mutators. Some will be beneficial to you, some will inhibit your progress and some will do a little of both - choose wisely!
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Any monster affected by mutators will be marked by our squires with a skull symbol.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** After every five worlds you will reach the respite of a chest world which will award you some bonus anima. This also acts as a checkpoint and resets your mutators.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** Once a week, there is a chance of powerful challenge worlds spawning. You can attempt these using the red portal. You can claim large sums of anima by completing these.
      - **Kenton Decarte:** The quartermaster can reward you for any anima you collect on your journeys through the worlds.
      - (initial|initial-options)
    - [Choice]: [More...]
      - [Menu]: Select an option
        - [Choice]: What is this place?
          - **Kenton Decarte:** Long ago, this temple was constructed to guard a portal to the abyss, and prevent it from opening.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** Alas, the anima witch has broken the seals binding these portals, and opened the way to the shattered worlds.
          - **Player:** Tell me about this 'anima witch'.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** She is an exiled Void Knight...a powerful magic user who discovered the shattered worlds. She has increased her power and reversed her aging process by siphoning corrupted anima.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** She is also my estranged wife. She blinded me when I caught up to her.
          - **Player:** What a shocking twist!
          - **Kenton Decarte:** As I am out of action, the Abyssal Knights need your help to travel through world after world, and defeat the monsters therein.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** Retrieve as much anima as possible, to help close the portals she has opened, and restore Gielinor's leaking supply.
          - (initial|initial-options)
        - [Choice]: What rewards can I get here?
          - **Kenton Decarte:** Ah, of course.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** What adventurer would aid us out of a sense of duty, honour; dare I say 'heroism'? Not you, it seems.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** If you care not for the abyssal threat, and act only in your own self-interest, then yes, rest assured there is compensation to be found for your 'valuable time'.
          - **Kenton Decarte:** You shall not find remuneration from me, however. Should that be your motivation, seek out the quartermaster in this camp.
