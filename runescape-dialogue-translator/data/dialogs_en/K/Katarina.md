## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet


#### First time

- You pet Katarina, and take a copy of their pawprint.
- You have found Katarina.
- [You have discovered a new cat!]
- [You quickly note it down in your logbook.<br><br>'Katarina']

#### Afterwards

- You pet Katarina, and take a copy of their pawprint.
- **Katarina:** {{RSChat|Meow!}}

### Talk to


#### Without catspeak

- **Katarina:** Meow, meow meow.

#### With catspeak

- **Katarina:** I will get it! I will smash its tiny brains in.
- **Katarina:** Stupid rat think Katarina is - how you say - a walk in the cake?
- **Katarina:** Katarina will show how wrong rat is. Even if rat is all weird and glowy, like a mouldy candle.
