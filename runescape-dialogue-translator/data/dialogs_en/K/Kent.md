# Kent

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Kent](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AKent)
- **Letter**: `K`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Witchaven


#### After Sea Slug

- **Kent:** Hello there, Player!
- **Player:** Hello again Kent.
- **Kent:** I never did get the chance to thank you properly for saving Kennith and myself.
- **Player:** Oh, don't be silly. It was nothing really.
- **Kent:** Play it down if you will. It was a truly brave thing you did.
- **Player:** I only did what anyone would have done in my position. I have to go now, take care.
- **Kent:** You too, Player. Goodbye.

#### After The Slug Menace

- **Player:** Hello, Kent!
- **Kent:** Oh, hello, Player. It's great to see you again. You know, Kennith is always talking about you since you rescued him from the sea slugs. I'm sure he'd love to talk to you - he's just downstairs.
- **Player:** Sure, I might go and say 'Hello'.

#### After Kennith's Concerns

- **Player:** Hello, Kent. How are things in Witchaven with Kennith gone and
- **Kent:** Hi, Player. We miss him terribly, but there can be no denying that things are a lot more peaceful for us.
- **Player:** How is he doing out on the Fishing Platform?
- **Kent:** Bailey's looking after him well and Kennith seems less, erm, troubled.
- **Player:** Maybe Kennith has learnt his lesson.
- **Kent:** Oh, yes, he's grown up, that's for sure. I don't think we'll be seeing him enslaving whole populations of people over a lost toy again.

#### After Salt in the Wound

- **Player:** Hello, Kent. Have you seen Kennith lately?
- **Kent:** He dropped by just before he left. Handsome lad, eh? He certainly takes after his father.
- **Player:** Haha, sure.
