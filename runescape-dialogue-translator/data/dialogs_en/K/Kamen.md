## Dialogue Transcript


### After Underground Pass

- He looks a little drunk.
- **Kamen:** {{RSChat|Hic!}}
- **Player:** Hi there, you okay?
- **Kamen:** Ooooh, my head ...I'm fried.
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Kamen:** Too much of this home brew my friend. We make it from plant roots, but it blows your head off. You don't wanna put it near any naked flames. Want some?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Okay then.
    - **Player:** Okay then.
    - **Kamen:** Here you go... Hic!
    - **Player:** {{RSChat|Aaarrgghh!}}
    - {{Qact|The player takes 50 points of damage}}
    - The dwarf kindly gives you some food to help you recover from the ghastly drink.
    - (receives=stew, bread and meat pie)
    - **Kamen:** Ha ha! I warned you - it's strong stuff. Have a good meal; it may sober you up a bit.
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Kamen:** Your losh... Hic! Well maybe a good meal? I could part with some food for a few coins.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Okay then.
        - **Player:** Okay then.
        - **Kamen:** 75 coins pleashe.
        - (Condition: If the player has enough money:)
          - (removes=75 coins)
          - **Player:** Here you go.
          - (receives=stew, bread and meat pie)
          - **Kamen:** Thanks... Eat well.
        - (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
          - **Player:** Oh dear, I haven't enough money...
          - You can not afford that.
          - **Kamen:** Sorry, I can't just give it away to anyone that walks past.
      - [Choice]: No thanks.
        - **Player:** No thanks.
        - **Kamen:** Well come back any time.
