### In New Varrock


#### Before Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Katrine:** It's a private business.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What sort of business?
        - **Katrine:** We're undertakers. Ask more questions and I guarantee that your loved ones will be in the ground with a minimum of fuss.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: I'm looking for riches and fame.
        - **Katrine:**  Ask any more questions and you'll get stitches and maimed.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Farewell.
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: What happened to your arm?
    - **Katrine:**  It ran off and joined the circus. <br>Stop bothering me with ridiculous questions.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - **Katrine:**  It's been a pleasure. <br>Try not to get stabbed on the way out.

#### After Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: The Shield of Arrav is back in the Museum!
    - **Katrine:**Thank heavens. <br>I've been SO worried.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Stay back. She's armed!
    - **Katrine:** You think that laughing at a missing arm is funny?
    - **Player:** I'm only having a bit of 'armless fun.
    - **Katrine:** We'll see if you're a stand up comedian when you have no legs.
    - **Katrine:** My arm came off when I died, if you must know.
    - **Katrine:** It was Zemouregal. When the Big Z invaded, he came to us first. Wanted half of some shield that we had in our coffers.
    - **Katrine:** I stood my ground. Wasn't going to let him take anything from us.
    - **Player:** I guess that didn't go down too well?
    - **Katrine:** You think? He ripped off my arm and beat me to death with it. <br>But I died fighting, not quivering in a basement like that yellow-belly Straven.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (above)
