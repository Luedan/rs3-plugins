# Kilron

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Kilron](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AKilron)
- **Letter**: `K`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Biohazard

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Kilron:** Hello.
- **Player:** How are you?
- **Kilron:** Busy.

### After Biohazard and before Plague's End

- **Player:** Hello Kilron.
- **Kilron:** Hello traveller. Do you need to go back over?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Not yet Kilron.
    - **Player:** Not yet Kilron.
    - **Kilron:** Okay, just give me the word.
  - [Choice]: Yes I do.
    - **Player:** Yes I do.
    - **Kilron:** Okay, quickly now!

### After Plague's End

- **Kilron:** Looks like you won't be needing the rope ladder any more, adventurer. I heard it was you who started the revolution and freed West Ardougne!
