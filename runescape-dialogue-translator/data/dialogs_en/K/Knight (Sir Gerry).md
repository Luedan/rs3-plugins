# Knight (Sir Gerry)

## Dialogue Transcript


### Climb down hole before talking

- **Knight:** Cough... Hey, over here.

### Talk-to


#### First-time dialogue

- **Player:** Who are you? What are you doing here in the snow?
- **Knight:** My name is...Sir Gerry. I am...a member of a secret...society of knights. My time is short and I need...your help.
- (Condition: If the player has not completed Recruitment Drive:)
  - **Player:** A secret society of knights? What a surprise! Is there an old charter or decree that says if you're a knight you have to belong to a secret order?
  - **Knight:** I'm sorry, my friend... I do not understand your meaning. Please, time is short... Take this scroll to Sir Tiffy. You will find him in Falador park... You should not...read it... It contains information for his eyes only.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player has completed Recruitment Drive:)
  - **Player:** A secret society of knights? You don't mean the Temple Knights, do you?
  - **Knight:** Yes! Praise Saradomin! You...have been sent in...my hour of need. Please, take...this scroll to Sir Tiffy in Falador park... You should not...read it.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Without one free backpack space:)
  - **Player:** I'm afraid I'm already carrying everything that I possibly can.
  - **Knight:** I beg you ... drop something you don't need. This is of utmost importance... I will conserve what little strength I have and await your return... Please hurry!
- (Condition: With one free backpack space:)
  - [The knight hands you a scroll.]
  - (receives=knight's notes)

#### Subsequent dialogue

- He's still alive, but in no condition to talk. He appears to be almost unconscious.

### Search


#### Without the knight's notes


##### Without one free backpack space

- You find some handwritten notes, but your hands are too full to pick them up.

##### With one free backpack space

- [You find some handwritten notes on the knight.]
- (receives=knight's notes)

#### With the knight's notes

- You find nothing of value on the knight.
