## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When talking to the two Khazard Guards carrying beers in the Fight Arena Bar.*
*The Khazard Guard at the bar will always have the following dialogue:*
- This guard is singing.
- Badly.
- **Khazard guard:** Shoooow mee the waaay to go home!
- Very badly.
- **Khazard guard:** I'm tired and I wanna go to bed.
- **Player:** I think that would be a good idea, you need to sleep. And have a bath.
*The Khazard Guard at the table will always have the following dialogue:*
- **Player:** Hello. What are you doing here?
- **Khazard guard:** I want the finest wines available to humanity. I want them here, and I want them now.
- **Player:** Hmmm...
