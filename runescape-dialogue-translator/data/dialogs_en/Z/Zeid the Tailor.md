## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Zeid the Tailor:** I'm a little busy at the moment. What is it?
- Select an option
  - What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** Oh, it has wonderful weather.
    - **Player:** Wonderful? It's hot and dry all the time!
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** Not quite as hot as back home.
    - **Player:** Where's home, then?
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** Oh, it's a town to the south of the pass, called Pollnivneach.
  - So, what do you do here?
    - **Player:** So, what do you do here?
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** If I had cloth, patterns and customers, I'd be a tailor. As it is, I'm a tailor with nothing to do.
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** The silk merchant won't even sell me any silks, because he doesn't trust me!
    - **Player:** Anything I can do to help?
    - **Zeid the Tailor:** No, no, it's all being dealt with.
  - *If the player has asked any of the Al Kharid merchants about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Player:** I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** Of course, he's the one who's going to obtain cloth and clothes patterns so I can set up shop here.
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** And in such a good location, too. Not out of the way like back home.
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** The customers will soon pour in!
      - **Player:** Maybe I should talk to him...
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** Why not? He's always happy to talk to potential business partners.
  - *If the player has asked Ellis, Shop assistant, or Shopkeeper (possibly other Al Kharid merchants as well) about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Player:** I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** Me, threaten people?
      - **Player:** One of the shopkeepers did say they were threatened by a man with large scissors...
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** Oh, them. Don't mind them. I think they're worried about what effect our shops will have.
      - **Zeid the Tailor:** For all they know, when we open our shops their wares will look cheap and shabby by comparison!
