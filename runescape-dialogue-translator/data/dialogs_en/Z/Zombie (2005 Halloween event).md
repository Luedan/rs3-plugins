- **Letter**: `Z`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### When using the right-click call-Over option

- **Zombie:** Durrrr...
- **Player:** Hey! Come over here!
- **Zombie:** Who? Me?
- (The player performs the beckon emote.)
- **Player:** Yes, you!
- **Zombie:** Give me a moment...I can't move that fast.
- **Player:** Why not?
- **Zombie:** I used to be a magician's assistant until the accident...
- **Player:** What accident?
- **Zombie:** Looook...
- (The zombie breaks in half at the waist, leaving behind an upper and a lower half.)
- (end)

#### When using the right-click insult option

- **Zombie:** Braaaaains...
- **Player:** Zombie, it has come to my attention that you are a fool, a nitwit if you will.
- (The player performs the laugh emote.)
- **Player:** I propose that your head, instead of being filled with the brains you so crave, is instead filled with a mixture of crushed lemons and kelp!
- **Zombie:** Oh noooo..I'd best take it off then...
- **Player:** What?
- (The zombie's head rolls off, leaving them headless.)
- (end)

#### When using the right-click scare option

- **Zombie:** Traaa laaa laaa...
- (The player performs the cheer emote.)
- **Player:** Oogaboogabooga!
- **Zombie:** Aaaaaargh!
- (The zombie splits in two, then splices the one half and the other half back together.)
- {{tmissing|This scares the player}}
- (end)
