## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Player:** Hello. That hat doesn't look like standard Zamorakian attire.
- **Zamorak mage:** Shh! They haven't noticed me here yet.
- **Player:** Why are you here? If they catch you here, they will kill you!
- **Zamorak mage:** Well, the runecrafting wizards have got themselves into a bit of a state over a thesis published by a student.
- **Zamorak mage:** We're also researching a new branch of Runecrafting at the moment: weapon imbuing.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What did this paper say?
    - **Player:** What did this paper say?
    - **Zamorak mage:** Well, in short, it states that one day the altars like this one could run out of runic energy!
    - **Player:** That's terrible! How will humans perform magic if we cannot make runes?
    - **Zamorak mage:** Well, it is only a theory. Hardly any of the Runecrafting Guild wizards believe it to be true.
    - **Zamorak mage:** Still, we have not proved Wizard Finix's theories to be incorrect yet, and - if they're true - this is very worrying. Wizards have been dispatched to all the known altars to investigate.
    - **Player:** So, what can we do if Wizard Finix's theories are true?
    - **Zamorak mage:** Well, there is a new method being explored, which was detailed in Wizard Finix's thesis. He is the best person to explain it to you; he is currently in the Wizards' Tower, if you want to speak to him.
    - [Menu]: Travel to the Wizards' Tower?
      - [Choice]: Yes
        - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the Wizards' Tower.}}
      - [Choice]: No
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: What is weapon imbuing?
    - **Player:** What is weapon imbuing?
    - **Zamorak mage:** Well, if you bring an inert magic weapon to an altar, you can use rune essence to imbue the weapon instead of binding runes.
    - **Zamorak mage:** This process saturates the weapon with runic energy and thus increases its power. This way, even regular wood can be made into menacing magical armaments!
    - **Zamorak mage:** In theory, that is. During our studies, we only ever managed to imbue eternal magic wood, perhaps due to the strong inherent magical properties that it possesses.
    - **Zamorak mage:** Our fellow wizards aren't proficient enough to properly imbue the wood either. None were able to get more than trace amounts of runic energy to settle in it.
    - **Zamorak mage:** This altar here also doesn't seem to work for imbuing at all.
    - **Zamorak mage:** I saw some Zamorakians try it and it looks like they only had any luck with fully imbued weapons, which dissipated into runic energy whenever they tried to imbue them any further.
    - **Zamorak mage:** I have a theory that certain objects of abyssal origin may yield the same results.
    - **Zamorak mage:** Maybe you should try it for yourself and see if you can get any use out of that effect.
    - **Zamorak mage:** I'm too scared to attempt it myself.
