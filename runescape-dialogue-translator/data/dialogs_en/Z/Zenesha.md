## Dialogue Transcript


### In East Ardougne

- **Zenesha:** Hello there! I sell plate armour. Are you interested?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Sorry, I'm not interested.
    - (end)
  - [Choice]: I may be interested.
    - (opens=Zenesha's Platebody Shop)

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Zenesha:** I have a puzzle for you!
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Zenesha has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Zenesha:** Nope, that puzzle is all messed up. Try again!

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Zenesha has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Zenesha:** Great, that is just what I wanted!
