## Dialogue Transcript


### At the entrance to the Grand Exchange


#### Talking to her

- **Zaida:** Good day! How can I help you?
- (Condition: Only on the first time:)
  - **Player:** Nice yak you have there.
  - **Zaida:** Thank you kindly! As you can see we've been quite busy; we're just on our way to the Grand Exchange to start shifting all this treasure we found.
  - **Player:** You found all of this?
  - **Zaida:** That I did! Let me take a moment to tell you the wonders of treasure trails.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Talk about treasure trails.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Tell me more about treasure trails.
        - **Zaida:** Across the world there is treasure buried that can be recovered... if you know where to look!
        - **Zaida:** If you're lucky in your adventures you may come across something called a clue scroll. You may find some monsters drop them when they die, or may discover one while you are training your skills.
        - **Zaida:** When you read the clue scroll, it will point you to somewhere else on Gielinor. The challenge here is deciphering where the clue scroll wants you to go.
        - **Zaida:** But if you can figure it out, head to that location and do what the clue asks of you.
        - **Zaida:** Treasure trails can be multiple steps, so don't be surprised if it leads you to another clue scroll.
        - **Zaida:** Keep going and eventually you will find the treasure you seek!
        - **Zaida:** If you happen to find a clue scroll that you're unable to complete, I'll take it off your hands in return for an easier one.
        - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
      - [Choice]: Where can I get my own clue scrolls from?
        - **Zaida:** What type of clue scroll would you like to know more about?
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: Easy clues.
            - **Zaida:** Some of the ways you can get easy clue scrolls include killing various low-level monsters across Gielinor, by pickpocketing members of H.A.M. (they have a hideout in Lumbridge, don't tell them I let you know!), and as an uncommon drop when defeating evil trees.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: Medium clues.
            - **Zaida:** Some of the ways you can get medium clue scrolls include killing various mid-level monsters across Gielinor and purchasing one from Stanley Limelight with thaler.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: Hard clues.
            - **Zaida:** Some of the ways you can get hard clue scrolls include killing various high-level monsters across Gielinor, by pickpocketing dwarf traders and elven clan workers, and purchasing one from Stanley Limelight with thaler.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: Elite clues.
            - **Zaida:** Some of the ways you can get elite clue scrolls include killing various high-level monsters across Gielinor, by pickpocketing Waiko islanders and elven clan workers, using crystal triskelion keys, and purchasing one from Stanley Limelight with thaler.
            - **Zaida:** You can also get a guaranteed elite clue scroll once a week from treasure chests in your aquarium and by defeating the Skeletal Horror.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: Master clues.
            - **Zaida:** Ah, the master clues! The rarest of the rare. The best way to get your hands on these is by completing treasure trails as you'll occasionally find them within the reward caskets.
            - **Zaida:** There's also a small chance that you will get a master clue from anything that normally gives you an elite clue - it's quite rare though so good luck!
            - (previous2)
      - [Choice]: Where did clue scrolls originate?
        - **Zaida:** Ha! Well that's a tricky question even for me. Listen, I don't rightly know but there are some curiosities to the whole business.
        - **Zaida:** As far as I can tell, all of this is setup by a fella by the name of Uri. I've been hunting these clue scrolls for years and his supply of treasures seems pretty limitless.
        - **Zaida:** Of course, if that wasn't strange enough - there's also someone who looks identical to Uri, who seems set on sabotaging the whole thing.
        - **Zaida:** Then there are the wizards that pop up from time to time who may try to attack you.
        - **Zaida:** And even more bizarre than that? All the people that these clue scrolls point to - if you ask them about it afterwards, they have no idea what you're talking about! Like it never happened!
        - **Zaida:** I know what you're thinking, this all sounds like the ramblings of a mad woman. But I swear, every word I say is true!
        - **Zaida:** Basically, even after all these years I still have no idea. If you ever figure it out, I'd love to know. Just to put my mind at ease if anything else.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Who are you?
        - **Zaida:** The name's Zaida, and this brute here is Nigel.
        - **Zaida:** Do you want some advice? Scrap that, I'm going to give it to you anyway. You don't get anywhere in life without putting in hard work.
        - **Zaida:** I'm a treasure hunter, have been my whole life. Turns out there is treasure buried across the whole of Gielinor and if you spend the time looking there is more wealth than you can imagine just waiting for someone to discover it.
        - **Zaida:** I can't tell you what to do with your life but I'd give up that adventuring lark and focus on finding these trasures.
        - **Zaida:** After all, we all know that money buys happiness.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Goodbye.
        - **Zaida:** May good fortune come your way!
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if the free clue scroll hasn't been claimed:|Claim a free clue scroll.
    - ((With enough space in backpack:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you a clue scroll, but it seems you don't have any room to carry one at the moment.
    - ((If the player is eligible for the elite clue scroll:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this elite clue scroll. May it bring you good fortune!
      - [Zaida hands you a sealed clue scroll.]
    - ((If the player is eligible for the elite clue scroll but has too many:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this elite clue scroll but it seems you already have some. Talk to me again when you have completed one of them.
    - ((If the player is eligible for the hard clue scroll:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this hard clue scroll. May it bring you good fortune!
      - [Zaida hands you a sealed clue scroll.]
    - ((If the player is eligible for the hard clue scroll but has too many:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this hard clue scroll but it seems you already have some. Talk to me again when you have completed one of them.
    - ((If the player is eligible for the medium clue scroll:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this medium clue scroll. May it bring you good fortune!
      - [Zaida hands you a sealed clue scroll.]
    - ((If the player is eligible for the medium clue scroll but has too many:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this medium clue scroll but it seems you already have some. Talk to me again when you have completed one of them.
    - ((Otherwise:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this easy clue scroll. May it bring you good fortune!
      - [Zaida hands you a sealed clue scroll.]
    - ((If the player has too many easy clue scrolls:))
      - **Zaida:** I'd like to offer you this easy clue scroll but it seems you already have some. Talk to me again when you have completed one of them.
  - [Choice]: Downgrade a clue scroll.
    - **Zaida:** I can exchange any clue scroll you have for one of a lower tier. Just give me the clue you want to exchange.
  - [Choice]: Open the shop.
    - **Player:** What do you have for sale?
    - **Zaida:** I have titles, a pet, clue scrolls and more! Let's not forget the Globetrotter outfit that will help you on your trails.
    - ((Treasure Trails Reward Shop opens.))
  - [Choice]: cond=With globetrotter outfit pieces unlocked:|Reclaim the Globetrotter outfit
    - **Player:** Hi there. I would like to reclaim my Globetrotter outfit please.
    - ((If pieces are not owned:))
      - **Zaida:** I'm sorry, you don't own any pieces of the outfit. Would you like to see the store?
      - [Menu]: Open the Treasure Trails Reward Shop?
        - [Choice]: Yes.
          - ((Treasure Trails Reward Shop opens.))
        - [Choice]: No.
          - **Zaida:** Goodbye!
    - ((If pieces are owned:))
      - ((Without pieces to reclaim:))
        - **Zaida:** You already have all of your owned pieces of the outfit!
      - ((With pieces to reclaim:))
        - [Each outfit piece has a return fee of 500,000, due to it having combat stats.<br>To return all owned outfit pieces this will cost [number] coins.]
        - [Menu]: Pay the return fee?
          - [Choice]: Yes.
            - ((Without enough space in backpack:))
              - **Zaida:** You do not have enough space in your backpack, come back later!
            - ((With enough space in backpack:))
              - (removes=coins)
              - (receives=missing globetrotter outfit pieces)
              - ((Repeated for each returned item, then continues below:))
                - Zaida returns your [item] to your backpack.
              - **Zaida:** That's everything, goodbye!
              - **Player:** Thank you, goodbye!
          - [Choice]: No.
            - (end)
  - [Choice]: cond=With Charos' clue carrier unlocked:|Reclaim the Charos Clue Carrier
    - ((Without enough space in backpack:))
      - **Zaida:** You do not have enough space in your backpack, come back later!
    - ((With enough space in backpack:))
      - ((Without having lost Charos' clue carrier:))
        - **Zaida:** You already have that item.
      - ((Having lost Charos' clue carrier:))
