# Zaff

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- ''(Before What Lies Below, or on free-to-play worlds:)
  - **Zaff:** Would you like to buy or sell some staves?
  - (continue)
- ''(After What Lies Below:)
  - **Zaff:** Would you like to buy or sell some staves or is there something else you need?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - (opens=Zaff's Superior Spellcasting)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
    - **Zaff:** Well, 'stick' your head in again if you change your mind.
    - **Player:** Huh, terrible pun. You just can't get the 'staff' these days!
    - **Zaff:** Actually, I have an assistant now, but he's a bit thin. You might even call him a bit of a beanpole!
    - **Player:** *groans* Okay, I'm leaving now!
  - [Choice]: Do you have any battlestaves?
    - **Player:** Do you have any battlestaves?
    - **Zaff:** I'm not sure. My assistant Naff is in charge of those now.
  - [Choice]: *(After What Lies Below)* Can I have another ring?
    - **Player:** Can I have another ring?
    - ''(If the player does not have the ring:)
      - **Zaff:** Yes, of course, Player. Here you are. Please bear in mind that this ring has no charges left with which to summon me, however.
      - (receives=beacon ring)
    - ''(If the player already has the ring in inventory:)
      - **Zaff:** There's still one in your inventory, Player!
    - ''(If the player already has the ring in their bank:)
      - **Zaff:** Go and get the one that's in your bank, Player!

### In New Varrock

- **Zaff:** Would you like to buy or sell some magic equipment?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - (opens=Zaff's Superior Spellcasting (New Varrock))
  - [Choice]: Where's Naff gone?
    - **Zaff:** Ah, he was a top lad. He could have sold ketchup to a vampyre, that one.
    - **Zaff:** He died during the zombie attack. Tried to climb down into Varrock sewer to escape.
    - **Zaff:** That was before we knew that monsters from Tolna's Rift had taken it over.
    - **Zaff:** I'll never replace him. No 'staff' would compare.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Zaff:** Well, 'stick' your head in again if you change your mind.
