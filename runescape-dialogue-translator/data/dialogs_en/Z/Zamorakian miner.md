# Zamorakian miner

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Zamorakian miner](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AZamorakian%20miner)
- **Letter**: `Z`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Give ore

- (removes=strange ore)
- You hand in: [number] x Strange ore.

### Talk to

- The miner is muttering to themselves under their mask. You hear something about 'unusual rocks' and 'ore' and something extremely rude about lazy smiths.<br>He seems to want you to gather ore for him.

### 30 seconds before the event ends

- {{Qact|The unnatural outcrops disappear.}}
- **Zamorakian miner:** {{RSChat|Give me the resources now!}}
- {{Colour: Give me the resources now!}}

### Completing the event successfully

- For completing this event you receive: 1 x Sack of wild rewards.
