## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid


#### Talking to him

- **Zeke:** A thousand greetings, [sir/madam].
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you want to trade?
    - **Player:** Do you want to trade?
    - **Zeke:** Yes, certainly. I deal in scimitars.
    - (opens=Zeke's Superior Scimitars)
  - [Choice]: Nice cloak.
    - **Player:** Nice cloak.
    - **Zeke:** Thank you.
  - [Choice]: Could you sell me a dragon scimitar?
    - **Player:** Could you sell me a dragon scimitar?
    - **Zeke:** A dragon scimitar? A DRAGON scimitar?
    - **Zeke:** No way, [man/lady]!
    - **Zeke:** The banana-brained nitwits who make them would never dream of selling any to me.
    - **Zeke:** Seriously, you'll be a monkey's uncle before you'll ever hold a dragon scimitar.
    - (Condition: If the player has not completed Monkey Madness:)
      - **Player:** Oh well, thanks anyway.
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player has completed Monkey Madness:)
      - **Player:** Hmmm, funny you should say that...
      - (continue)
    - **Zeke:** Perhaps you'd like to take a look at my stock?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please, Zeke.
        - (opens=Zeke's Superior Scimitars)
      - [Choice]: Not today, thank you.
        - **Player:** Not today, thank you.
  - [Choice]: What do you think of Ali Morrisane?
    - **Player:** What do you think of Ali Morrisane?
    - **Zeke:** He is a dangerous man.
    - **Zeke:** Although he does not appear to be dangerous, he has brought several men to this town who have threatened me and several others.
    - **Zeke:** One man even threatened me with a hammer, saying that when he set up his smithy, my shoddy workmanship would be revealed!
    - **Player:** What will you do about these threats?
    - **Zeke:** Oh, I am quite confident in the quality of my work... as will you be if you take a look at my wares.
    - **Zeke:** Would you like to see them?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please, Zeke.
        - (opens=Zeke's Superior Scimitars)
      - [Choice]: Not today, thank you.
        - **Player:** Not today, thank you.

#### Handing him an Al Kharid flyer

- **Player:** Hi. I have this money off voucher.
- **Zeke:** So I see. Unfortunately, it seems to have expiredyesterday. Never mind.
- **Player:** But I only just got it!
- **Zeke:** I'm sorry. There's nothing I can do. Goodbye.
