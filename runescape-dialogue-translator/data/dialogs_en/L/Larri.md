# Larri

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Larri](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALarri)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *(If not selected last:)*
  - Who are you?
    - **Larri:** Hello there adventurer. My name is Larri. I'm the chief stall master for this here fayre, and happy to meet your acquaintance.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(If not selected last:)*
  - How does the test of strength machine work?
    - **Larri:** You just have to step up and take your best swing at the machine. There's not many that can make the bell ring, though. Do you think you're up to the challenge?
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - You will only get charged tokens for the times that you ring the bell.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - You will gain Strength XP from this attraction.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(If not selected last:)*
  - How does the pickaxe wanging work?
    - **Larri:** It works exactly as you expect. You get a total of ten wangs with a pickaxe each turn.
    - **Larri:** All you need to do is line yourself up with one of the targets, and throw to your heart's content.
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - You will only get charged tokens for the times that you hit with the pickaxe.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - You will gain Mining XP from this attraction.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(If not selected last:)*
  - How does the claw-dia stall work?
    - **Larri:** Ah, now this attraction was made for this location specifically. We've heard the rumours and stories of the magnificent creature called Clawdia, and we wanted to pay homage.
    - **Larri:** This is a much less dangerous way of wrangling with her though.
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - You will be charged 50 tokens when you use the claw-dia machine. A prize box will be released by the machine which can be opened to get different prizes.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - *(Only in 2018:)*
      - You can use the machine once per day. A prize box will be released by the machine which can be opened to get different prizes.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(If not selected last:)*
  - How are things going?
    - **Larri:** How very kind of you to ask. I'm having a splendid time here. I think this crater is such a lovely setting for our fayre.
    - **Larri:** I imagine we'll be coming back here again in the future.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
