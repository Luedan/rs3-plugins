## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Solstice Showdown

- **Lunar Knight:** Where's that Solar Knight at!?
- **Player:** Nice cloud, where can I get one?
- **Lunar Knight:** It's available from the Solstice Showdown!
- **Lunar Knight:** Now, if you'll excuse me, I have a duel to get back to.
- ((If not an Ironman Mode player and only on the first time:))
  - You have been awarded 1 Treasure Hunter key.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - You have been awarded 1 Treasure Hunter key.
