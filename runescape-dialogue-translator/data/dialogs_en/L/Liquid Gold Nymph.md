# Liquid Gold Nymph

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Liquid Gold Nymph](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALiquid%20Gold%20Nymph)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing golden mining suit

- (Condition: If not wearing any golden mining outfit pieces:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, darling, that outfit you're wearing is so DREARY. Wouldn't you rather wear something STYLISH and GOLD?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If wearing one piece of the golden mining outfit:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** You're wearing a LITTLE gold...I suppose that's a START. Would you like another piece of the outfit?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If wearing two pieces of the golden mining outfit:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** You need more gold, darling! More GOLD! I have another piece of the outfit for you here.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If wearing three pieces of the golden mining outfit:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** You're starting to look good, darling! You should add more pieces to the gold outfit! Would you like another?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If wearing four pieces of the golden mining outfit:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, you're looking VERY good INDEED. There's just one more thing missing, though, I think. Would you like the final piece?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please!
    - **Player** Yes, please!
    - (Condition: If giving the player the golden gloves:)
      - [The Liquid Gold Nymph gives you a pair of golden gloves.]
      - (receives=golden mining gloves)
    - (Condition: If giving the player the golden boots:)
      - [The Liquid Gold Nymph gives you a pair of golden boots.]
      - (receives=golden mining boots)
    - (Condition: If giving the player the golden trousers:)
      - [The Liquid Gold Nymph gives you a set of golden trousers.]
      - (receives=golden mining trousers)
    - (Condition: If giving the player other golden outfit items:)
      - The Liquid Gold Nymph gives you a golden [item name].
      - (receives=golden mining item)
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, you have NO taste, my dear, but each to their own.
  - [Choice]: Can I use the Varrock armour with the gold outfit?
    - **Player** Can I use the Varrock armour with the gold outfit?
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Yes! The gold armour looks SO much better when you wear the complete set, so I've made a special arrangement...
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** If you've earned your Varrock armour, and you're wearing all five pieces of my gold outfit, you'll get the Varrock armour effect when mining.
    - (Condition: If you have all pieces of the golden mining outfit excluding the Golden mining top:)
      - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** You COULD get the same effect by wearing the Varrock armour and the other four pieces of my set...but that won't look nearly as good.
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player** Who are you?
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, you might say I'm an aesthete, my love. Just a humble appreciator of beauty.

### After completing golden mining suit

- (Condition: If the complete golden mining suit is equipped:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, you look magnificent! Oh! I have NO more costume items to give you. You have quite EXHAUSTED my supply. But if you were to give me your pickaxe, perhaps I could ENHANCE it...
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the complete golden mining suit is not equipped:)
  - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** I've given you a LOVELY golden outfit, but you're not wearing it! Never mind, never mind. Would you like me to add gold to your pickaxe?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Okay.
    - **Player:** Okay.
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: Ask the Nymph to coat the pickaxe in your toolbelt.
        - (Condition: If you have a pickaxe that can be gilded in your toolbelt:)
          - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, doesn't it look FABULOUS? SO much better than your plain old one. Anyway, I have to go. See you around!
        - (Condition: If you have a crystal pickaxe in your toobelt:)
          - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** That pickaxe is coated with crystal. I'm so sorry. I don't understand elven magic enough to add gold to crystal.
        - (Condition: If you have any other pickaxe in your toolbelt:)
          - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** I'm so sorry. I can't add gold to that pickaxe.
      - [Choice]: Give the Nymph a different pickaxe.
        - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Just give me the pickaxe you'd like to be gold-plated.
        - Use a pickaxe on the Liquid Gold Nymph to have her coat it with gold.
  - [Choice]: Don't you have anything apart from gold?
    - **Player:** Don't you have anything apart from gold?
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Well...I suppose I could give you some advice on Mining.
    - The Liquid Gold Nymph gives you some advice on Mining.
    - (receives=2,500 Mining experience)
  - [Choice]: Can I use the Varrock armour with the gold outfit?
    - **Player** Can I use the Varrock armour with the gold outfit?
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Yes! The gold armour looks SO much better when you wear the complete set, so I've made a special arrangement... If you've earned your Varrock armour, and you're wearing all five pieces of my gold outfit, you'll get the Varrock armour effect when mining. You COULD get the same effect by wearing the Varrock armour and the other four pieces of my set...but that won't look nearly as good.
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player** Who are you?
    - **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, you might say I'm an aesthete, my love. Just a humble appreciator of beauty.

#### Using a pickaxe that can be gilded on the Liquid Gold Nymph

- [The Liquid Gold Nymph coats your pickaxe in gold!]
- **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, doesn't it look FABULOUS? SO much better than your plain old one. Anyway, I have to go. See you around!

#### Using a pickaxe that cannot be gilded on the Liquid Gold Nymph

- **Liquid Gold Nymph:** I'm SO sorry, but I can't add gold to that pickaxe.

#### Using other items on the Liquid Gold Nymph

- **Liquid Gold Nymph:** Oh, no my dear. I only deal with pickaxes.
