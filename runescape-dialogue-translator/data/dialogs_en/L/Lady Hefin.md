## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Lady Hefin:** Praise Seren for sending you, human.<br> You are the saviour of our city.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about Lady Hefin...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about yourself?
    - **Lady Hefin:** I am merely another traveller on the journey to enlightenment. It is a road few dare walk, but the light of Seren shall show us the way.
    - **Lady Hefin:** Who I am is not important. Nor indeed my origin. To focus on what we were inhibits our potential to become something more.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Clan Hefin...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about Clan Hefin?
    - **Lady Hefin:** Where other clans build or fight or destroy, clan{{Template:Sic}} Hefin looks inward.
    - **Lady Hefin:** We contemplate the physical and spiritual world, and meditate upon the wisdom of Seren.
    - **Lady Hefin:** We strengthen our bodies and minds, that we may be enlightened by her grace and the beauty of her spirit.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Hefin skills...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about Clan Hefin's skills?
    - **Lady Hefin:** We seek to cleanse our minds in prayer, and hone our bodies through agility exercises.
    - **Lady Hefin:** We welcome all who crave enlightenment, and provide for their physical and spiritual needs.
    - **Lady Hefin:** Why not leap atop a serenity post, and meditate in unity upon the wisdom of Seren? I think you will find the experience quite edifying.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - (end)
