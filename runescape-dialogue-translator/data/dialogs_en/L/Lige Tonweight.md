# Lige Tonweight

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Lige Tonweight](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALige%20Tonweight)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Random encounter after smithing an item in the Artisans' Workshop

- {{Qact|Lige appears next to the player.}}
- **Lige Tonweight:** {{RSChat|I have an order for a ceremonial sword Player!}}
- Lige has some ceremonial sword plans for you!

#### Talking to Lige Tonweight

- (Condition: If the player has backpack space:)
  - (receives=a ceremonial sword design)
  - **Lige Tonweight:** I've got some ceremonial sword plans here for you.
  - {{Qact|Lige disappears.}}
- (Condition: If the player has no backpack space:)
  - **Lige Tonweight:** I'm holding onto some plans for you, but you don't have any space in your backpack. Get some room and I'll give them to you.

### Upon completion of a ceremonial sword

- {{Qact|Lige appears next to the player.}}
- ((If the completed sword exactly matches the plan:))
  - This sword is now perfect and requires no more work.
  - For producing a perfect sword, you are awarded 120% of the normal experience. Excellent work!
- **Lige Tonweight:** Would you like to have that sword you made?
- **Select an Option**
  - Yes please.
    - (receives=the ceremonial sword)
    - **Lige Tonweight:** There you go, treat it well.
  - Can I have an off-hand version instead?
    - (receives=the off-hand ceremonial sword)
    - **Lige Tonweight:** There you go, treat it well.
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Lige Tonweight:** Very well, I guess I'll give this to my son.
- {{Qact|Lige disappears.}}
