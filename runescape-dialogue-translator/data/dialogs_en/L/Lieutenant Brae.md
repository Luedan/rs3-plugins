## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Black Guard HQ in western Keldagrim


#### Before Forgiveness of a Chaos Dwarf

- **Lieutenant Brae:** Hello, visitor. You should speak to Commander Veldaban if you need something.

#### After Forgiveness of a Chaos Dwarf and before Birthright of the Dwarves

- **Lieutenant Brae:** Hello, visitor. I'm sorry, I don't think I have much time to talk.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Have you taken over Veldaban's job?
    - **Player:** Have you taken over Veldaban's job?
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** Yes, I'm the new head of the Keldagrim Black Guard.
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** That's why I'm so busy. There are so many things to organize.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Where is Veldaban?
    - **Player:** Where is Veldaban?
    - (Condition: Before King of the Dwarves:)
      - **Lieutenant Brae:** He left the Black Guard. He's living somewhere in Keldagrim East now. You could try looking for him in the Laughing Miner pub.
      - (other)
    - (Condition: After King of the Dwarves:)
      - **Lieutenant Brae:** King Veldaban, you mean? I imagine he's in the palace, on the top floor.
      - (other)
  - [Choice]: Do you need any help?
    - **Player:** Do you need any help?
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** Supreme Commander Bisi doesn't want...
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** I mean, er, thank you for the offer, but there's nothing you can help with at the moment.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: I should go now.
    - **Player:** I should go now.
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** Enjoy your stay in Keldagrim, visitor.

#### After Birthright of the Dwarves

- **Lieutenant Brae:** What can I do for you, Player?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: So you're still head of the Keldagrim Black Guard?
    - **Player:** So you're still head of the Keldagrim Black Guard?
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** I am. I was having trouble with the responsibility at first, but the Supreme Commander says I've grown into the job.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: I want to talk about the Red Axe spies.
    - **Player:** I want to talk about the Red Axe spies.
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** Thanks for your help with that, Player. At first I was annoyed that Veldaban would send a human to judge dwarves, but perhaps only an outsider could have judged them fairly.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do you think of my choices?
        - **Player:** What do you think of my choices?
        - **Lieutenant Brae:** What do you want me to say? Is your conscience troubling you? Do you want me to tell you that you made the right choice?
        - **Lieutenant Brae:** As the king's agent you were empowered to judge the prisoners however you saw fit. From a legal standpoint, whatever you did was correct.
        - **Lieutenant Brae:** Decisions like that come with the territory, I'm afraid. No one said they were going to be easy.
        - (other)
      - [Choice]: Where do you send dwarves when they're exiled?
        - **Player:** Where do you send dwarves when they're exiled?
        - **Lieutenant Brae:** They're forbidden to enter lands controlled by Keldagrim. A lot of them seem to end up to the south of Yanille.
        - (other)
      - [Choice]: How do the executions take place?
        - **Player:** How do the executions take place?
        - **Lieutenant Brae:** A dwarf sentenced to death is taken to the mines outside the city and a Black Guard cuts off their head with an axe. It may not be a perfect system but at least it's quick.
        - (Condition: If Vigr, Klass, Brendt and Grundt were all spared or exiled:)
          - **Lieutenant Brae:** Luckily for the prisoners, you spared them all that fate.
          - (other)
        - (Condition: Otherwise:)
          - (other)
      - [Choice]: I should go.
        - (below)
  - [Choice]: I should go.
    - **Player:** I should go.
    - **Lieutenant Brae:** It was good to see you again, Player.
