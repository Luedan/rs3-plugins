## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Lunet:** Greetings, [mister/miss]. What can I do for you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you have any battlestaves?
    - (Condition: If the player has not yet completed the Hard Tirannwn tasks:)
      - **Lunet:** Unfortunately not. At least, not unless you help us elves out a bit first.
      - You need to claim the rewards for the Hard Tasks in the Tirannwn Task Set.
    - (Condition: If the player has completed the Hard Tirannwn tasks:)
      - **Lunet:** Ahh, you're one of those adventurers, aren't you? I've seen you around helping out. I've been told to always keep a few battlestaves in stock for those such as yourself.
      - (opens=Lunet's Leftover Staves)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, bye!
    - **Player:** Nothing, bye!
