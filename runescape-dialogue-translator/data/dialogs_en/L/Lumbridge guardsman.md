## Dialogue Transcript


### At the bridge east of Lumbridge Castle


#### If the player didn't participate in the Battle of Lumbridge

- {{Trandom}}
  - **Lumbridge guardsman:** Adventurer, feel free to help us clear Lumbridge of these goblins!
  - **Lumbridge guardsman:** Ah, goblins... Why won't they just go away?
  - **Lumbridge guardsman:** Watch out, these goblins are dangerous!
  - **Lumbridge guardsman:** Sorry, I'd love to stop for a chat but I have to secure the area.

#### If the player participated in the Battle of Lumbridge

- **Lumbridge guardsman:** Greetings, adventurer. Duke Horacio has recently provided us guards with advanced training, as well as much improved swords! I feel much more confident in our ability to defend Lumbridge now that we actually have proper equipment and training!
