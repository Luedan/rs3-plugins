## Dialogue Transcript


### Without catspeak

- **Lucifurr:** Meow, meow meow.

### With catspeak

- **Lucifurr:** So. Come to seek my wisdom? Or perhaps you wish to make a deal? I am so good at deals. I can get you anything.
- **Lucifurr:** You want power? Pfft, power is the game of kittens, I can make you a pharaoh, an emperor, even a god! It would be trivial. Power flows so easily, if you know where to look.
- **Lucifurr:** Ahh, but perhaps you seek power of the arcane variety. Knowledge, the greatest force anyone can wield. I can give you secrets. I can teach you forgotten knowledge.
- **Lucifurr:** I who was apprenticed to the High Magister Oreb, master of souls.
- **Lucifurr:** I who have read all the books in the Grand Library of Menaphos.
- **Lucifurr:** Promise me your soul. Surrender it to me and I will teach you all of these things. I will give you power beyond your wildest dreams.
- **Player:** Yeah, I’ll get back to you.
- **Lucifurr:** It’s only your soul, you’ll barely miss it.
