## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Standard dialogue

- **Lord Amlodd:** Behold, the great Lord Amlodd! Returned, from the plane of sp-!
- **Lord Amlodd:** Oh, I've done that part already. Anything I can help you with, Player?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Ask about Lord Amlodd...
    - **Player:** Could you tell me a little more of your experiences in the Spirit Plane?
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Ho ho, gladly! It's such a wonderful place. Hmm, now, what can I remember...
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Oh, yes - food! Food is an interesting thing in that place. You see, familiars don't need to eat.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** They're sustained by a strange connection between our worlds - possibly between all worlds. Truly, they feed off of the memories of others.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** The Amlodd believe that familiars are nothing more than manifest concepts - ideas brought to life through the power of anima.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** A spirit wolf comes into being due to those of us who THINK about wolves and the idea of wolfiness.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Wait, 'wolfiness'? That's not a word. Hmm, pretend that it is; you get the idea.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Though, I have also heard claims that the Spirit Plane is nothing more than an underworld for beings of limited consciousness.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Whatever is the case, they don't NEED to eat, and yet there I saw them, every day, doing through the motions of eating.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Predator and prey still going through the motions, and tiny spirit creatures eating spirit forms of plant life. It was fascinating.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** And yet, for all my time there, I don't recall ever eating myself.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** ...
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Ho, now I'M hungry. Hurry up with your questions. I'd like to get some dinner.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Clan Amlodd...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about Clan Amlodd?
    - **Lord Amlodd:** The Amlodd are the greatest of all elf clans!
    - **Lord Amlodd:** We divine the will of Seren, and call forth creatures from the Spirit Plane to help hold back the shadows!
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Most elves have an affinity with nature, but the Amlodd are the closest to it.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** I don't mean the simple stuff like the soil and the trees - that's Clan Crwys.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** No, I mean the Anima Mundi - the connection between all forms of life and the spirit of the world that flows through us all.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** And specifically, about the light energy that flows within it. We stay clear of dark energies wherever we might find them.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** That is except for the shadows we now summon here in order to cleanse them of corruption.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Amlodd skills...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about Clan Amlodd's skills?
    - **Lord Amlodd:** The Amlodd is comprised of highly skilled summoners and experienced diviners of Seren's will.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** We seek the aid of protectors from the Spirit Plane, and use their light to banish shadows.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Although the Dark Lord has been dealt with, it has cast a long shadow over the elven lands.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** For each shadow defeated, and each core cleansed in the Right of Light below, we are one step closer to returning Seren to us all!
    - **Player:** So, are light creatures associated with Seren in some way?
    - **Lord Amlodd:** Oh ho ho! No, my friend. Not exactly. They are their own beings, but they are intrinsically linked with Lady Seren.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** My belief is that they are attracted to certain beings who share their affinity to light.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** And, though this is just my theory, I believe when our lady shattered, they were drawn to the largest of her remnants.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** We all know how that worked out - all that Dark Lord nonsense. Yet we see it as our duty to cleanse these creatures of light as much as it is to cleanse Seren.
    - **Lord Amlodd:** In turn, these light creatures help us to locate parts of Seren - some of which we turn over to the other clans - so that they may perform their own duties.
    - (Condition: If the player has not yet completed "I'm Forever Washing Shadows":)
      - **Lord Amlodd:** In fact, if you would see it as your duty to cleanse these poor light creatures, then I would reward your efforts.
      - **Lord Amlodd:** To fully cleanse Seren, we must purify her mind, body and soul. The shadows reflect corruption of each of these things.
      - **Lord Amlodd:** So, in turn, we must cleanse the truthful, blissful and manifest shadow creatures. Please collect cores from each type - fifteen of each should suffice.
      - (continue)
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - (end)

- (receives=Divination XP lamp)
- Congratulations, you have completed 'I'm Forever Washing Shadows' - Aid Lord Amlodd in cleansing shadow cores.
- (continuea)
