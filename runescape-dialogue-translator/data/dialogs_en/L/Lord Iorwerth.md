## Dialogue Transcript


### After Regicide and before Plague's End


#### Without wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Good day, Lord Iorwerth.
- **Lord Iorwerth:** Ahh Player. Good day, sorry I cannot stop to talk. So much to do, so little time.
- **Player:** Very well, good bye.

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Lord Iorwerth:** What are you doing here?! You will ruin everything... Get back to Ardougne now!

#### While wearing full Mourner gear along with select non-Mourner items

- **Lord Iorwerth:** What are you doing here?! You will ruin everything... Get back to Ardougne now! And do not let me see you with that non-regulation gear again.

### Treasure Trails


#### Without space in backpack

- You don't have enough inventory space to take the puzzle box.

#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Lord Iorwerth:** Before I will help you, human, you must complete this for me!
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Lord Iorwerth has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Lord Iorwerth:** Do not waste my time, human!

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Lord Iorwerth has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Lord Iorwerth:** You have done well, human!
