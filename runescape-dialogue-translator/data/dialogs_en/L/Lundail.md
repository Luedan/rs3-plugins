## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Lundail in the Mage Arena bank.*
- **Lundail:** Hello [Sir/Miss]. How can I help you, brave adventurer?
- Select an option
  - What are you selling?
    - **Player:** What are you selling?
    - **Lundail:** I sell rune stones. I've got some good stuff, some really powerful little rocks. Take a look.
      - (opens=Lundail's arena-side rune shop)
  - What's that big old building above us?
    - **Player:** What's that big old building above us?
    - **Lundail:** That, my friend, is the mage battle arena. Top mages come from all over Gielinor to compete in the arena.
    - **Player:** Wow.
    - **Lundail:** Few return, most get fried, hence the smell.
    - **Player:** Hmmm.. I did notice.
