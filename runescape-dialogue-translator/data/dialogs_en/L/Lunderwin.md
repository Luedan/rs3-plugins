## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Lunderwin at the Zanaris marketplace.*
- **Lunderwin:** Buying cabbage am I, not have such thing where I from. Will pay money much handsome for wondrous object, cabbage you called. Say I 100 gold coins each fair price to be giving yes?
- *If the player has no cabbages in their inventory:*
  - **Player:** Alas, I have no cabbages either...
  - **Lunderwin:** Pity be that, I want badly do.
- Select an option
  - Yes, I'll sell you all my cabbages.
    - **Player:** Yes, I'll sell you all my cabbages.
    - **Lunderwin:** Business good doing with you! Please, again come, buying always.
      - {{qact|Player receives 100 coins for each cabbage in their inventory, and the cabbages are removed.}}
  - No, I will keep my cabbbages.
    - **Player:** No, I will keep my cabbbages.
