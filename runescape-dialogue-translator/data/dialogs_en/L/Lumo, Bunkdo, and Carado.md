### Standard dialogue


#### Lumo


##### During Monkey Madness

- **Lumo:** So human, what can we do for you?
- **Player:** Well I suppose I ought to help you escape.
- **Bunkdo:** No it's alright [laddy/lass], we quite like it in here.
- **Lumo:** Do your best to ignore him...
- **Lumo:** To be fair human, you're not much help stuck in there...
- **Player:** Well how do I get out?
- **Lumo:** Well, you can try picking the lock on your door. Watch out for the guards though.
- **Lumo:** We've been trying to pick ours but we haven't been able to do so yet.

##### After Monkey Madness

- **Player:** How did you get back in there?
- **Lumo:** Simple: we got caught again.
- **Player:** Deliberately?
- **Lumo:** Yes. We are working undercover here. Isn't that right, guys?
- **Carado:** Whatever you say, boss.
- **Lumo:** See.

#### Bunkdo

- **Bunkdo:** What are you looking at then? Never seen a Gnome in a Monkey jail before?
- **Player:** Not before I came here, no...

#### Carado

- **Carado:** Well I'm not complaining - this jail cell is larger than my room in the Grand Tree! It's a liitle sparse however...
- **Player:** We'll get you out of there soon enough.
- (Condition: During Monkey Madness:)
  - **Carado:** No hurries...
- (Condition: After Monkey Madness:)
  - **Carado:** I'm meant to be here now. We're working undercover.
