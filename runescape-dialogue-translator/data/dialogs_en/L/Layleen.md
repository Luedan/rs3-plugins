## Dialogue Transcript


### In Tai Bwo Wannai, after Jungle Potion

- **Layleen:** Hello there...thanks for helping out Trufitus commune with the gods Bwana. We've been able to return to the village thanks to your help.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Layleen:** Oh, I just walk around a bit...I have to help my Mum and Dad a lot though because I'm still only young. I'd love to visit the mainland one day though and get out of this boring village.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Is there anything interesting to do here?
    - **Player:** Is there anything interesting to do here?
    - {{Trandom}}
      - (Dialogue 1:)
        - **Layleen:** Bwana, if anyone else asks me that question today, I'll explode! Go and ask Murcaily!
      - (Dialogue 2:)
        - **Layleen:** I'm just enjoying the sunshine, Bwana. Try asking Murcaily.
      - (Dialogue 3:)
        - **Layleen:** Well, I think that there's some work to be done...perhaps Murcaily can help you. He usually tends to the hardwood grove to the east of Trufitus' hut.
      - (Dialogue 4:)
        - **Layleen:** Sorry Bwana, I'm already busy, why not go and talk to Murcaily! He's around the village somewhere.
  - [Choice]: cond=With Tai Bwo Wannai Cleanup started|I've been doing some work around the village.
    - (Condition: With unclaimed Tai Bwo Wannai favour:)
      - **Layleen:** Hey, I've seen you working around the village. You've been doing a lot of good work around here for us. Let me give you something for your trouble.
      - (receives=trading sticks)
    - (Condition: Without unclaimed Tai Bwo Wannai favour:)
      - **Layleen:** I've already given you some trading sticks and I don't have any more on me at the moment. Why not go and talk to some of the other villagers and get some contributions?
  - [Choice]: Ok, thanks.
    - **Player:** Ok, thanks.
