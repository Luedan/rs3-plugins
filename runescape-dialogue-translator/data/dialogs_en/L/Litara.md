## Dialogue Transcript


### In Barbarian Village

- **Litara:** Hello there. You look lost - are you okay?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm looking for a stronghold, or something.
    - **Player:** I'm looking for a stronghold, or something.
    - **Litara:** Ahh, the Stronghold of Security. It's down there.
    - Litara points to the hole in the ground that looks like you could squeeze through.
    - **Player:** Looks kind of...deep and dark.
    - **Litara:** Yeah, tell that to my brother. He still hasn't come back.
    - **Player:** Your brother?
    - **Litara:** He's a explorer too. When the miner fell down that hole he'd made and came back babbling about treasure, my brother went to explore. No one has seen him since.
    - **Player:** Oh, that's not good.
    - **Litara:** Lots of people have been down there, but none of them have seen him. Let me know if you do, will you?
    - **Player:** I'll certainly keep my eyes open.
  - [Choice]: I'm fine, just passing through.
    - **Player:** I'm fine, just passing through.
