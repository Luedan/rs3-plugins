# Lunch

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Lunch](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALunch)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When using the magic flute on Lunch


#### First time

- **Lunch:** Weird flute. Why are you showing it to me?
- **Player:** I don't really know any more. It's just a thing I do.

#### Second time

- **Player:** So... do you want this?
- **Lunch:** Not especially, no.

#### Third time

- **Player:** I just wanted to check, you haven't changed your mind about this flute have you?
- **Lunch:** If I take it, will you stop showing objects to me?
- **Player:** Most likely.
- **Lunch:** Fine. Hand it here then.
- **Player:** What will you give me in return?
- **Lunch:** Absolutely nothing!
- **Player:** I think I'll see what other buyers I can line up for it then.

#### Fourth time

- **Lunch:** Fine! I will take the flute!
- **Player:** And you'll give me another item in exchange?
- (removes=magic flute)
- (receives=goebie disguise kit)
- Lunch takes the flute reluctantly from you and gives you a small chest in return.
- **Player:** Good trade. Enjoy your new flute.
- **Lunch:** I will treasure it forever.
