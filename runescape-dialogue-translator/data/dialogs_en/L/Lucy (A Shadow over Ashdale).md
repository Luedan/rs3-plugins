## Dialogue Transcript


### After A Shadow over Ashdale

- **Lucy:** Hello! Good to see you again.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What can I do around here?
    - **Lucy:** Well, since you’re an adventurous type, you might want to head down to the Ashdale caves.
    - **Lucy:** There’s a stone pathway at the north-west corner of the island that leads directly to them.
    - **Lucy:** There are still a lot of those crassian things down there, although apparently they’re not a direct threat to the town.
    - **Lucy:** Still, it wouldn’t hurt to keep their numbers down.
    - {{Qact|Screen fades out and in. Cutscene shows the location of the Ashdale caves entrance}}
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can you give me directions?
    - **Lucy:** There’s a campfire and a fishing spot to the west, so if you wanted to do some fishing or cooking that would be the way to go.
    - **Lucy:** The quarry is to the east. Just head through that archway over there and keep going straight. You can’t miss it.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: How are things here?
    - **Lucy:** Well, there hasn’t been a mass abduction recently, so I’d say things are pretty good.
    - **Lucy:** Although no-one’s seen Digory Popplewell since the crassian incident, and naturally everyone fears the worst.
    - **Lucy:** Poor Mr Popplewell.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Lucy:**  See you later!
