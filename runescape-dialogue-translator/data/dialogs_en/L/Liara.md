## Dialogue Transcript


### Outside a heist entrance


#### First time

- **Player:** Hey, who are you? What are you doing here? You don't look like you should be here!
- **Liara:** What's it to you, snitch? I'm on official Thieves Guild' business.
- (Condition: Before completing From Tiny Acorns (or one of the subsequent miniquests):)
  - **Player:** Ooh, an actual guild of thieves! Do you rob from the rich and give to the poor?
  - **Liara:** Well, someone's enthusiastic...and, yes, we've been known to provide some karmic redistribution of wealth.
  - (continue)
  - **Liara:** You know what, I could use a gopher on my latest job, if you're up for it...
- (Condition: After completing From Tiny Acorns (or one of the subsequent miniquests):)
  - **Player:** Oh, you're with the guild - so am I!
  - **Liara:** A likely story.
  - **Player:** One which Darren would be happy to tell you, I'm sure.
  - **Liara:** Oh, you know Darren... Well, alright, then maybe you can lend me a hand...
  - (continue)
- **Player:** Player!
- **Liara:** The name's Liara.
- (continue|Afterwards)

#### Afterwards

- **Player:** Hi, Liara. So, how can I assist?
- (Condition: Below 95 Thieving:)
  - **Liara:** On second {{Sic|thoughts}}, you could do with a bit more experience. I don't want you getting lost in shadow.
  - To join in on the Vault of Hereditas heist requires 95 Thieving.
  - (popup)
    - {{Popup|To join in on the Vault of Hereditas heist requires 95 Thieving.}}
  - (end)
- (Condition: At or above 95 Thieving:)
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are heists?
    - **Player:** What are heists exactly?
    - **Liara:** On the surface, very simply just a specific target ripe for burglary. I make sure to only target joints that are either long-abandoned or inhabited by the unscrupulous.
    - **Player:** That way, you aren't hurting anyone that doesn't deserve a bad day?
    - **Liara:** Precisely, the {{Sic|Thieves Guild'}} has a code of honour to uphold!
    - **Liara:** Then, I nick anything of value not bolted down - plus a couple of things that are - using every technique in the book!
    - **Player:** So, like pickpocketing, picking locks, coshing, safecracking and the like?
    - **Liara:** Exactly! But try not the get caught, else you'll lose out on some of the loot you've *ahem* appropriated so far.
    - **Liara:** Then, when I make my escape, I fence the loot in order to help fund the Guild's altruistic activities.
    - **Liara:** They're a tour de force of triumphant thievery, a rush of relishable robbery, a smorgasbord of satisfy-.
    - **Player:** Stealing is fun!
    - **Liara:** It certainly has its merits. Though, remember the golden rule: only ever from people that deserve it!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What is this heist?
    - (Condition: At Kharid-et Dig Site:)
      - **Liara:** Right, so, this here is the Vault of Hereditas. Or one of them, at least - there could be loads beneath this place.
      - **Liara:** From casing the joint, it's full of ancient and powerful magical relics, which'll be worth a pretty penny.
      - **Player:** Magical relics? Well, I am partial to the odd one here or there.
      - **Liara:** Never my taste, I always preferred gold. Now, anything else?
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: On Anachronia:)
      - **Liara:** From what I can discern, this is the entrance to something called the Asuran Arsenal.
      - **Liara:** Think of it as a dragonkin weapons development, testing and storage facility.
      - **Player:** Ooh, dragon weapons?
      - **Liara:** Just for starters! However, there's stuff in here enough to wage war with the gods themselves!
      - **Liara:** And also with anyone foolish to set foot inside, given all the automated defences still in operation.
      - **Player:** That's me, I'm foolish enough to step inside!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What can I expect inside?
    - (Condition: At Kharid-et Dig Site:)
      - **Liara:** Right inside, there's a chest with some really tasty loot in it, but it's shielded... can't get to it.
      - **Liara:** Looks to be one of those shadow pylon thingies blocking the way, so you'll have to go all the way in, find a way past t, then loot the chest before you get out.
      - **Player:** What's between us and it?
      - **Liara:** Well, the ghosts of this old fort still roam its halls, forever on patrol. They have a canny ability to sense intruders even sometimes through walls - watch that!
      - **Liara:** There are also more barriers, like across this site. I reckon you'll have to root through the pockets of the corpses of these guards to find a seal to let you pass through.
      - **Liara:** Finally, some of the stuff you can nick is hidden in the Shadow Realm, so you might need to use batteries to power a nearby shadow anchor in order to loot them.
      - **Liara:** But not the batteries from out here, they don't seem to work - only the batteries you loot inside the heist work for some reason.
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: On Anachronia:)
      - **Liara:** The good news is, there aren't any living guards to deal with. The bad news is that this facility is monitored by some sort of holographic entity named the Keeper! If that thing spots you, you're toast.
      - **Liara:** But also, the bad news about the good news is the reason there's nothing alive in here is thanks to all the automated defences.
      - **Liara:** There are Nodon auto-cannons and flamethrowers all over the place.
      - **Liara:** Also, it looked like there was a heavy stone tablet that needed to be carried through those defences to a pylon deeper in.
      - **Liara:** Honestly, you wouldn't catch me dead in there...because I ain't going in there. If I did go in, I would be dead.
      - **Liara:** But you'll be fine, promise. Just be sure to take some food on this heist...
      - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What is of value here?
    - (Condition: At Kharid-et Dig Site:)
      - **Liara:** So, my source tells me this place is filled is stuff the empire *ahem* 'confiscated' from all their enemies - powerful relics.
      - **Liara:** My source suggests that a lot of what they took were weapons, but they didn't have any specifics for me.
      - **Player:** I guess I'll just have to find out for myself!
      - **Liara:** You're welcome to keep anything you find like that. I just want stuff I can fence for the Guild. Weapons are too hard to move, draw too much attention.
      - **Player:** So, who is this source of yours anyway? Is it an archaeologist?
      - **Liara:** I cannot lie...so I'll just not tell you. I've gotta keep my sources safe; they prefer to 'stick to the shadows', as it were.
      - **Player:** Hmm, I wonder...
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: On Anachronia:)
      - **Liara:** Well, given it's a facility for weapons, I'd wager there's a weapon or two to find here.
      - **Liara:** When I scouted the entrance, that Keeper thing mentioned something about delivering 'Mercy'.
      - **Liara:** And, apparently, the dragonkin had the concept of karmic retribution down to a T - they made it instant and deadly.
      - **Liara:** So look out for something related to that. Honestly, I'm stumped.
      - (previous)
  - [Choice]: I'm ready to head in!
    - (Condition: At Kharid-et Dig Site:)
      - (Condition: With the entrance closed:)
        - {{Qact|The vault door changes to the Vault of Hereditas heist entrance.}}
        - **Liara:** I've unlocked the entrance for you!
        - (continue)
      - **Liara:** Have fun!
    - (Condition: On Anachronia:)
      - (Condition: With the entrance closed:)
        - {{Qact|The dungeon entrance changes into the Asuran Arsenal heist entrance.}}
        - **Liara:** The way is open for you!
        - (continue)
      - **Liara:** Good luck!

### In Heist Game or Tutorial


#### Standard dialogue

- **Liara:** Ah, Player, how nice to see you again. What brings you to my humble island?
- **WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO SAY?**
  - What's going on here?
    - **Player:** What's going on here?
    - *If the player is a robber:*
      - **Liara:** Ah, good - the new recruit. Just bring me the loot. And if anyone asks, I'm a fisherman.
    - *If the player is a guard:*
      - **Liara:** Just preparing to go to sea. Just an honest...fisherman. Hoping for a big haul today.
    - **ANYTHING ELSE?**
      - (other|tutorial-initial-options)
  - Can you offer me any advice?
    - **Player:** Can you offer me any advice?
    - *If the player is a robber:*
      - **Liara:** Yes, yes. Just one moment.
      - *If there is at least one guard nearby:*
        - **Liara:** You don't have enough money to buy these fish. Come back later.
        - Liara is unwilling to give advice when guards are nearby.
      - *If there are no guards nearby:*
        - **Liara:** Doesn't look like there are any guards nearby to listen in on us.
        - **Liara:** My advice for you is to get into cover: take a disguise as soon as you can and act like the saps on this island. Walk around, have a seat, blend in, and then strike when they least expect it. Then, just walk away casually and bring me the loot.
        - **Liara:** Nice and easy, right?
        - **Player:** You could say that.
        - **ANYTHING ELSE?**
          - (other|tutorial-initial-options)
    - *If the player is a guard:*
      - **Liara:** Of course! I just came from Port Sarim; saw some shifty goings-on. You should go check it out.
      - **Player:** I can't go there, my duty's on this island!
      - **Liara:** Well, I'm just trying to help.
      - **ANYTHING ELSE?**
        - (other|tutorial-initial-options)
  - I'd like to leave.
    - If you leave the game you will not be able to return and you will not receive any rewards. Are you sure you want to leave?
    - **SELECT AN OPTION**
      - Yes
        - *If the player is a robber:*
          - **Liara:** Shame, I thought you had potential. Back we go then.
        - *If the player is a guard:*
          - **Liara:** Of course! It would be my pleasure to aid in the avoidance of duty.
        - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the Heist lobby.}}
      - No
        - (end)
  - Nothing.
    - **Player:** I'd best be off now!
