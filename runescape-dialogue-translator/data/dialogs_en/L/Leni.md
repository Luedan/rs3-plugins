# Leni

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Leni](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALeni)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to

- **Leni:** Welcome to NovtumberFest! These portals are here to help you nip back and forth to the event!
- **Leni:** Keep an eye out for my announcement for when portals open up.

### Announcing the portals

- *(The applicable line is seen whenever a portal becomes active:)*
  - **Leni:** A game of Flish Flingers just opened up!
  - **Leni:** A Guthixian cache has just opened up!
  - **Leni:** A shooting star just crash landed!
  - **Leni:** A sinkhole and big chinchompa entrance have just spawned!
  - **Leni:** Looks like you're able to get through to the spirit plane now for some Familiarisation!
  - **Leni:** There's an evil tree causing total havoc!
