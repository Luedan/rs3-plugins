# Lukas

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Lukas](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALukas)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Lukas:** Welcome to NovtumberFest! Wouldn't you like to make a barrel?
- Select an option
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - Who are you?
      - **Player:** Who are you?
      - **Lukas:** I am Lukas and I make the barrels around here!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What do I have to do here?
      - **Player:** What do I have to do here?
      - **Lukas:** Are ye good with a hammer?
      - **Player:** Sure am!
      - **Lukas:** Excellent! Head on over to the workbench over there and get to it!
      - You will receive Smithing XP for making barrels.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - How do you feel about your job?
      - **Player:** How do you feel about your job?
      - **Lukas:** It's great! I've got so good at it that it's like shooting fish in...
      - **Lukas:** a small box.
      - **Player:** ... Is that normally how that phrase goes?
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Bye.
    - **Lukas:** Tally ho!
