## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Tai Bwo Wannai Trio

- **Lubufu:** Watch where you're going, young whippersnapper!
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: I wasn't going anywhere...
    - **Player:** I wasn't going anywhere...
    - **Lubufu:** Well then go away from here!
  - [Choice]: What's a whippersnapper?
    - **Player:** What's a whippersnapper?
    - **Lubufu:** It's a whip. Which snaps. Like me. Now leave!
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Lubufu:** I am Lubufu - the only fisherman who knows the secret of the Karambwan!
    - **Player:** What's a Karambwan?
    - **Lubufu:** What a foolish question! Now leave!

#### Speaking to him again

- **Lubufu:** I said go away!
- **Player:** Ok, I'm going...

### After Tai Bwo Wannai Trio

- **Lubufu:**You deceived me you confoundering dolt! Because of you, my entire business is ruined! Get out of my sight!
