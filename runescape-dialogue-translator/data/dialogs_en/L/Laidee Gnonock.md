## Dialogue Transcript


### At the entrance to the Warriors' Guild, after While Guthix Sleeps


#### Without requirements to enter the Warriors' Guild

- **Laidee Gnonock:** You can't come in. You're not enough of a warrior.
- **Player:** And a gnome called 'Laidee' is?
- **Laidee Gnonock:** Are you being heightist?
- **Player:** No, I'm laughing at your name!
- **Laidee Gnonock:** You really shouldn't do that. I'm what's stopping you from entering the Guild, and it's not really warrior-like.
- **Player:** What's to stop me just stepping over you?
- **Laidee Gnonock:** I promised Ghommal that anyone unworthy would not gain entrance to the Guild. I will stick to that. Plus...you'd regret it.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Try to pass Laidee.
    - [You step forward to try to pass Laidee.]
    - {{Qact|The player is knocked off their feet and takes 10 points of damage.}}
  - [Choice]: Okay, you win.
    - **Player:** Okay, you win.

#### With the requirements to enter the Warriors' Guild

- **Laidee Gnonock:** Hello, I’m Laidee. Welcome to the Warriors’ Guild.
- **Player:** Umm...thank you, I think.
