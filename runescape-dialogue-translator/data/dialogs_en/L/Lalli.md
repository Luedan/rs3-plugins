#### During The Fremennik Trials

- (see|The Fremennik Trials)

#### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Lalli:** Ha! Stupid human! You no get any golden apples! Even though me much like stone soup!
- **Player:** Can I please have some more golden wool?
- **Lalli:** Me will sell you some golden wool, but me not sell you any golden apples!
- **Player:** How much for the golden wool?
- **Lalli:** Me want 1000 coins! You pay or go!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Pay
    - (Condition: Without at least 1,000 coins:)
      - You can not afford that.
      - **Player:** I don't have that much cash on me...
      - **Lalli:** Well stupid bad trade human don't get no wool then! Or any golden apples!
    - (Condition: With at least 1,000 coins:)
      - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
        - **Lalli:** Stupid human not got room to carry apple anyway.
      - (Condition: With space in backpack:)
        - (removes=1,000 coins) (receives=golden wool)
        - **Player:** Is that all? Here you go!
        - **Lalli:** Ha! Stupid human think he buy golden apples, but actually get stupid wool! Hahaha!
  - [Choice]: Don't pay
    - **Lalli:** You no pay, then you go! You no get any golden apples!
