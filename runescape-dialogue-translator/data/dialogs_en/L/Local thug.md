# Local thug

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Local thug](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALocal%20thug)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

(When speaking to Local thug in Edgeville or East Ardougne, after While Guthix Sleeps and before Ritual of the Mahjarrat.:)
- **Player:**Who are you? Where is Lucien?
- **Local thug:**Hahaha! You seek the great Lord Lucien, but he needs help from none such as you! Now, begone, you vile whelp!
(When speaking to Local thug in Edgeville or East Ardougne, and after Ritual of the Mahjarrat.:)
- **Player:** Still waiting around here for your lord to return?
- **Local thug:** Hahaha! He'll make you pay when he returns from his mission in the north, mark my words!
- **Player:** Seeing as he's dead, I doubt that.
- **Local thug:** What?

### Dialogue about lost pendant

(When speaking to Local thug in Edgeville or East Ardougne without Pendant of Lucien in inventory or bank, after While Guthix Sleep<nowiki/>s and before Ritual of the Mahjarrat:)
- **Player:**Who are you? Where is Lucien?
- **Local thug:**Hahaha! If you seek the assistance of Lord Lucien, you have come to the right place!
- **Player:**I've lost the pendant Lucien gave me.
- **Local thug:**Have another, it will remind you of his power!
- This strange thug has given you another pendant.
- *Player receives Pendant of Lucien.*
- **Local thug:**Now, begone, you vile whelp!
(When speaking to Local thug in Edgeville or East Ardougne without Pendant of Lucien in inventory or bank, and after Ritual of the Mahjarrat:)
- **Player:** Still waiting around here for your lord to return?
- **Local thug:** Indeed so, can I help?
- **Player:** I've lost the pendant Lucien gave me.
- **Local thug:** Have another, it will remind you of his power!
- This strange thug has given you another pendant.
- (receives=Pendant of Lucien)
- **Player:** Somehow I think this has more power than Lucien now...
