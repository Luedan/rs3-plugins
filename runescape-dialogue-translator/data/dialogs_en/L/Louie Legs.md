## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid


#### Talking to him

- **Louie Legs:** Hey, wanna buy some armour?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What have you got?
    - **Player:** What have you got?
    - **Louie Legs:** I provide items to help you keep your legs!
    - (opens=Louie's Armoured Legs Bazaar)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
  - [Choice]: What do you think of Ali Morrisane?
    - **Player:**What do you think of Ali Morrisane?
    - **Louie Legs:** I don't like these men he's brought in from the southern desert. They're up to no good, I'm sure. One of them even came round here and threatened me with a hammer! He said that it'd be better for us if we worked for Ali Morrisane and that it was our fault if we didn't and... bad things started to happen.
    - **Player:** What are you doing to do about it?
    - **Louie Legs:** Keep selling armour, of course! Would you like to buy some?
    - (initial)

#### Handing him an Al Kharid flyer

- **Player:** Hi. I have this money-off voucher.
- **Louie Legs:** So I see. Unfortunately, it seems to have expired yesterday. Never mind.
- **Player:** But I only just got it!
- **Louie Legs:** I'm sorry. There's nothing I can do. Goodbye.
