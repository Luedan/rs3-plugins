## Dialogue Transcript


### Trade

- (Condition: Before having received the free gift:)
  - **Lupe:** Hey, I've put together a little rituals starter pack for you!
  - (Condition: Without two free spaces in backpack:)
    - You need at least 2 free spaces in your Backpack.
    - **Lupe:** You need some room to take them, though...
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: With two free spaces in backpack:)
    - 2 souls fly over to power the vessel.
    - (receives=four basic ritual candles and 12 inkwells of basic ghostly ink)
    - [Lupe gives you some ritual supplies.]
    - **Lupe:** I see great things in your future!
    - (continue)
- (opens=Soul Supplies)

#### While on Kili's tier 90 tank task and not having talked to Kili about ScapeRune


##### Only on the first time

- **Player:** Sorry to bother you, but I don't suppose you know where I can find some blueberries?
- **Lupe:** What an odd question.
- **Lupe:** That reminds me of another odd exchange I had the other day!
- **Lupe:** I met a fella who had recently died. We were exchanging the stories of our death, and he said he died at the paws of a cat!
- **Player:** A cat?
- **Lupe:** An evil cat, he said. It was a strange land, where things worked backwards!
- **Lupe:** Cats rule over humans, fish cooked backwards...
- (Condition: If you have not spoken to Kharen before:)
  - **Player:** An evil cat, in a backwards land? Maybe this could help me.
- (Condition: If you have spoken to Kharen before:)
  - **Player:** Hmm... A place of opposites, and a cat called Evil Bob. Maybe Kili can point me in the right direction?

##### Afterwards

- **Lupe:** Hope you find those blueberries!

### Collect free supplies


#### Before having collected free supplies for the week


##### Before having claimed Underworld Grimoire 1

- (Condition: Before having received the free gift:)
  - **Lupe:** Hey, I've put together a little rituals starter pack for you!
  - (Condition: Without two free spaces in backpack:)
    - You need at least 2 free spaces in your Backpack.
    - **Lupe:** You need some room to take them, though...
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: With two free spaces in backpack:)
    - 2 souls fly over to power the vessel.
    - (Condition: Repeated in a pop-up:)
      - 2 souls fly over to power the vessel.
      - (continue)
    - (receives=4 basic ritual candles and 12 inkwells of basic ghostly ink)
    - [Lupe gives you some ritual supplies.]
    - **Lupe:** I see great things in your future!
    - (continue)
- **Lupe:** I will start preparing free daily ritual supplies for you if you complete at least one of the Underworld area task sets.
- **Lupe:** Speak to Icthlarin at the ritual site to get started with those.

##### Having claimed only Underworld Grimoire 1


##### Without space in backpack

- **Lupe:** It looks like you don't have enough space for all of my free supplies. You need 1 backpack spaces in total.

##### With space in backpack

- (receives=60 noted bones)
- **Lupe:** There you go, use them wisely.

##### Having claimed Underworld Grimoire 2 but not Underworld Grimoire 3


##### Without four free spaces in backpack

- **Lupe:** It looks like you don't have enough space for all of my free supplies. You need 4 backpack spaces in total.

##### With four free spaces in backpack

- (receives=60 noted bones, 20 noted baby dragon bones, 20 regular ritual candles, and 20 inkwells of regular ghostly ink)
- **Lupe:** There you go, use them wisely.

##### Having claimed Underworld Grimoire 3 but not Underworld Grimoire 4


##### Without seven free spaces in backpack

- **Lupe:** It looks like you don't have enough space for all of my free supplies. You need 7 backpack spaces in total.

##### With seven free spaces in backpack

- (receives=60 noted bones, 20 noted baby dragon bones, 20 regular ritual candles, 20 inkwells of regular ghostly ink, 20 noted dragon bones, 12 greater ritual candles, and 12 inkwells of greater ghostly ink)
- **Lupe:** There you go, use them wisely.

##### Having claimed Underworld Grimoire 4


##### Without space for the items in bank and focus storage


##### Without space for the items in backpack

- **Lupe:** It looks like you don't have enough space for all of my free supplies. You need space in your bank and focus storage, or 8 backpack spaces in total.

##### With space for the items in backpack

- (receives=60 noted bones, 20 noted baby dragon bones, 20 noted dragon bones, 20 regular ritual candles, 20 inkwells of regular ghostly ink, 12 greater ritual candles, 12 inkwells of greater ghostly ink, and 8 inkwells of powerful ghostly ink)
- **Lupe:** There you go, use them wisely.

##### With space for the items in bank and focus storage

- (Player has 60 noted bones, 20 noted baby dragon bones, and 20noted dragon bones added to their focus storage, and 20 regular ritual candles, 20 inkwells of regular ghostly ink, 12 greater ritual candles, 12 inkwells of greater ghostly ink, and 8 inkwells of powerful ghostly ink to their bank.)
- **Lupe:** There you go, use them wisely.

#### After having already collected free supplies for the week

- **Lupe:** You've already collected your free supplies. Don't you get greedy now! Come back [in [number] days/tomorrow] for more supplies.
