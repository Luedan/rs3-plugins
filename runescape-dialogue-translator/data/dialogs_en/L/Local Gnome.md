# Local Gnome

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Local Gnome](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALocal%20Gnome)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Tree Gnome Village


#### Before Tree Gnome Village is completed

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Local Gnome:** Lardi dee, lardi da.
- **Player:** Are you alright?
- **Local Gnome:** Hee hee, lardi da, lardi dee.
- [The gnome appears to be singing.]

#### After Tree Gnome Village is completed

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Local Gnome:** You're the best!
- **Player:** Thanks.
- **Local Gnome:** Well I'm better. Hee hee.
