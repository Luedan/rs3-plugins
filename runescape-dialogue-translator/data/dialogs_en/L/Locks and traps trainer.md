### In the Thieves' Guild


#### First time dialogue

- **Player:** What can you teach me?
- **Locks and traps trainer:** I'm the locks and traps trainer. Need any advice?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: About those hankies...
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What are these handkerchiefs for?
        - **Player:** What are these handkerchiefs for?
        - **Locks and traps trainer:** Oh, we use them to keep track of our members' training regimen. Blue handkerchiefs will earn you one point, red ones are worth four. You can claim a reward from any trainer with your hanky points.
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: How often can I do this?
            - **Player:** How often can I do this?
            - **Locks and traps trainer:** The number of hanky points you can claim for depends on your dedication to the art. At the moment, you can claim <number> hanky points' worth of training per week.
            - [Menu]: Select an Option
              - [Choice]: What are these handkerchiefs for?
                - (above)
              - [Choice]: Which activities give which handkerchiefs?
                - (below)
              - [Choice]: Bye for now.
                - **Player:** Bye for now.
      - [Choice]: Which activities give which handkerchiefs?
        - **Player:** Which activities give which handkerchiefs?
        - (Condition: Having completed only Buyers and Cellars:)
          - **Locks and traps trainer:** Our pickpocketing trainers and chests all carry blue hankies at present. We're looking to expand when we get more staff.
          - (continue)
        - (Condition: After completing subsequent capers:)
          - **Locks and traps trainer:** Our pickpocketing trainers, safes and easy chests carry blue hankies; red hankies can be found on the coshing trainers and behind harder locks.
          - (continue)
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: What are these handkerchiefs for?
            - (above)
          - [Choice]: How often can I do this?
            - (above)
          - [Choice]: Bye for now.
            - **Player:** Bye for now.
  - [Choice]: That's alright, thanks.
    - **Player:** That's alright, thanks.
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** Feel free to practise on my coffers; the ones in the south are examples of more complex locks.
  - [Choice]: I'm always happy to learn.
    - **Player:** I'm always happy to learn.
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** You'll come across a fair few doors and chests around the world, and people tend to lock 'em.
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** With a bit of skill, though, and sometimes some specialised tools, you can crack them open and disarm the triggers on the traps they hold.
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** If something looks suspicious, examine it closely before you touch it.
    - **Player:** Specialised tools?
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** Some locks are more complex than others; trickier ones need a lockpick. You can buy one in the shop if you lose yours or it breaks.
    - **Player:** Another mystery unlocked. Thank you.
    - **Locks and traps trainer:** Feel free to practise on my coffers; the ones in the south are examples of more complex locks.
