## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]


#### Talking to Lumberjack Leif

- **Lumberjack Leif:** Hello.
  - [Menu]: What would you like to talk about?
    - [Choice]: Lumberjack Leif.
      - **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
      - **Lumberjack Leif:** I'm cutting down maple trees for their wood. It's a living.
      - **Player:** Good luck with that.
      - **Lumberjack Leif:** Thanks.
    - [Choice]: Your approval rating.
      - **Player:**  What do people think of me around here?
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is below 25%)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You've not been making many friends since you arrived here. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is between 25% and 49%)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You haven't really won the heart of the locals, if you see what I mean. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is between 50% and 74%)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You haven't been here for very long, but people are reasonably fond of you. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is at 75% or higher)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You seem remarkably popular for a newcomer to these lands. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population liked you.

#### Trying to chop a tree

- **Lumberjack Leif:** Hey! These are the King's trees. You'll need his approval to make use of them.
- (Condition: At 100% approval rating, during Throne of Miscellania:)
  - **Lumberjack Leif:** Don't trouble yourself further, [Sir/Madam], you've already done so much!

### After [[Throne of Miscellania]]


#### Talking to Lumberjack Leif

- **Lumberjack Leif:** Good day, Your Royal Highness
  - [Menu]: What would you like to talk about?
    - [Choice]: Lumberjack Leif.
      - **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
      - **Lumberjack Leif:** I'm cutting down maple trees for their wood. It's a living.
      - **Player:** Good luck with that.
      - **Lumberjack Leif:** Thank you, Your Royal Highness.
    - [Choice]: Your approval rating.
      - **Player:**  What do people think of me around here?
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is between 25% and 49%)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** Your people are growing dissatisfied with the regime, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population supported you.
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is between 50% and 74%)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You are well regarded, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population supported you.
      - (Condition: If your approval rating is at 75% or higher)
        - **Lumberjack Leif:** You are universally adored, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population supported you.

#### Attempting to chop down a maple tree

*At the tree which Lumberjack Leif is chopping*
- Lumberjack Leif is already chopping that down.
*At below 100% approval rating*
- **Lumberjack Leif:** {{RSChat|Thanks!}}
*At 100% approval rating*
- **Lumberjack Leif:** Don't trouble yourself further, Your Highness, you've already done so much!
