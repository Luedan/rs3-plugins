# Lofu

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Lofu](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALofu)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *Without a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet and not wearing a monkey greegree*
  - **Lofu:** Ook. Ook! Ook! Ah Ah! Ook Ah Uh! *(or a variation of this)*
  - (end)
- *Without a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet, wearing a monkey greegree*
  - **Lofu:** Ook. Ook! Ook! Ah Ah! Ook Ah Uh! *(or a variation of this)*
  - You cannot understand what he is saying.
  - (end)
- *With a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet and wearing a monkey greegree*
  - **Lofu:** Get out of my way, you fool! Can't you see I'm busy?
  - **Player:** I'm sorry...
  - **Lofu:** Monkeys these days... you'll be acting like one of those awful Gnomes next...
  - (end)
- *With a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet, not wearing a monkey greegree*
  - **Lofu:** You're one of those humans that everybody has been talking about! The Marimbo Guard will kill you on the spot!
  - (end)
