## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing Great Gielinor Run


#### Without an active challenge

- **Linford:** Hey! Want to take part in a challenge?
- **Player:** What is it?
- **Linford:** I call it the Great Gielinor Run! I'm challenging people to run from here to Castle Drakan without any funny business - no teleports, no transport, nothing. Just a straight run across the continent. What do you say... are you interested?
- **Take part in the Great Gielinor Run?**
  - Yes.
    - **Linford:** That's great news! Now, run! Don't think about cheating I've got a vyre over there keeping watch. You're probably wondering how someone like me is in touch with a vyre but let's leave that as a story for another day...
  - No.
    - (end)

#### With an active challenge

- **Linford:** What are you doing waiting around here? I said Run!

### After completing Great Gielinor Run

- **Linford:** I can't believe you did it! I thought everyone would give up before they got there, but not you! Way to show me!
