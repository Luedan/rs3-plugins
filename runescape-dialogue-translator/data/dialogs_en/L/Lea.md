## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Only on the first time

- **Player:** Hello there, I don't think I have seen you around here before.
- The stranger looks you up and down.
- **Lea:** No. You have not.
- **Player:** Well, what brings you to this part of the world?
- **Lea:** I am waiting for an individual of some talent of creation.
- **Player:** I have made some things in my time, maybe I could be of some help.
- (Condition: Without 22 Invention:)
  - **Lea:** No. No you could not.
  - **Lea:** Not yet at least.
  - [You need 22 Invention to help out.]
- **Lea:** Hmmm. From what I have seen of you around here I think you might be of some use.
- **Player:** Excellent, I always do like to help...
- **Player:** Wait. What do you mean that you've seen of me? I haven't seen you anywhere but here.
- **Lea:** Come now. Sight is not necessarily a symmetric relationship. Now back to the matter at hand.
- **Lea:** I know of your talents, honed in the cave behind me and I would be willing to commission some work from you.
- **Player:** Oh, that might be a bit problematic, as my inventions run off...
- **Lea:** Yes yes, burning the torn memories of a dead god for your own ends.
- **Lea:** How very resourceful and ethical of you.
- **Lea:** An event, which some would say, was your fault.
- **Player:** That's not what happened!
- **Player:** I tried to save them.
- **Player:** ...
- **Player:** I failed.
- **Lea:** It matters not, the result was the same.
- **Lea:** Regardless, my powering of such items is not your concern.
- **Player:** If you say so...
- **Player:** So what are you after?
- **Lea:** Well, I find that when I drop coins into my coin pouch it makes quite the noise.
- [The stranger shakes their coin pouch in example.]
- **Lea:** I would like it to be quieter.
- **Player:** I have some ideas for how to do that...
- **Player:** ...and what else that might be useful for...
- **Lea:** Excellent. I eagerly await your return with the requested item.

#### During pickpocketing bag task

- **Lea:** We meet again, do you have the bag for me?
- (Condition: Without a pickpocketing bag:)
  - **Player:** Well, no, not yet.
  - **Lea:** Then do not bother me until you have a new bag. Nothing special, a basic bag is all I request of you.
- (Condition: With a pickpocketing bag:)
  - **Player:** Indeed I do.
  - **Lea:** Excellent.
  - (Condition: If the pickpocketing bag has been worn:)
    - [You offer the bag.]
    - **Lea:** I am not interested in second hand items. I want one new bag - nothing more, nothing less.
    - **Player:** Fine. I will go and find a new one.
  - (removes=pickpocketing bag)
  - [You hand over the bag.]
  - **Lea:** ...
  - **Player:** ...
  - **Player:** ... ... ...
  - **Player:** Well?
  - **Lea:** Well what?
  - **Player:** You have your new coin pouch...
  - **Lea:** Yes.
  - **Player:** And you seem happy with it?
  - **Lea:** Very much so.
  - **Player:** So this is the part where you pay me.
  - **Lea:** Oh dear me. Regrettably you seem to have forgotten something.
  - **Player:** What?
  - **Lea:** To agree the terms of our arrangement before handing over the bag. I never agreed to give you anything for the bag.
  - **Lea:** I merely said I would be willing to commission you for the work, you seemingly took the task on as a charitable gesture. For which I am most grateful.
  - **Player:** That's... But I...
  - **Player:** Why you sneaky...
  - **Lea:** Oh come now,  not like you got nothing out of your labours. Consider me your muse. You now have the expertise to make more of those bags for your own coin collection.
  - **Player:** I suppose I did get something from all this...
  - **Lea:** Very good. Now that matter is settled I would like to propose a further request, if you would be willing.
  - **Player:** Suppose I was, what would you be after? And more importantly what would you give me in return.
  - (Condition: Without 82 Invention:)
    - **Lea:** I do not think you will be of any more help at this time. I require someone with more refined skills than those you can currently offer.
    - [You need 82 Invention to help further.]
  - **Lea:** Since you asked, I would be interested in something to channel magical energies.
  - **Player:** Like a wand? I'm sure I could make one quite easily.
  - **Lea:** No no. Not to channel magic energies outward, but inwards. To capture it and bind it.
  - **Player:** So for crafting runes? Some kind of reverse wand?
  - **Lea:** To put it simply. Yes.
  - (Condition: If the player hasn't researched the binding rod:)
    - **Player:** I have noted down some thoughts on how to tackle a similar problem for myself.
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: If the player has already researched the binding rod:)
    - **Player:** I already know how to make a tool like that, I call it a 'Binding rod'.
    - (continue)
  - **Lea:** I am aware of your advancements but I would be interested in something less...
  - **Lea:** ...basic.
  - **Player:** No, of course not. A brand new never before seen tool isn't good enough for you.
  - **Lea:** Precisely. It is not my fault I have standards.
  - **Player:** So tell me - what would make this less basic?
  - **Lea:** Are you familiar with the elves in the west?
  - (Condition: If the player hasn't completed Plague's End or Extinction:)
    - {{Transcript missing}}
  - (Condition: If the player has completed Plague's End but not The Light Within:)
    - **Player:** Yes actually, I have helped them a lot and have been granted access to their city of Prifddinas.
    - **Lea:** Quite the adventurer with friends in high places. So in answer to my question: yes.
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: If the player has competed The Light Within but not Extinction:)
    - **Player:** Yes actually, I have helped them a lot and have not only been granted access to their city of Prifddinas but also helped bring back their god Seren.
    - **Lea:** Quite the traveller. So in answer to my question: yes.
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: If the player has completed both The Light Within and Extinction:)
    - **Player:** Yes, very much!
    - **Player:** I found a way under the mountain, solved a bunch of their problems, gained access to their city of Prifddinas.
    - **Player:** Then helped bring back their god Seren. Visited their homeworld. Met Seren's mother and her...
    - **Player:** ...aunts and cousins, sort of... I suppose...
    - **Player:** Before leaving what was left of them all on a world that I tried to save.
    - **Player:** A dead world that I failed to save. But there was nothing more I could do - I tried...
    - **Lea:** Quite. And you have my thanks for those endeavours.
    - (continue)
  - **Lea:** They have access to some very versatile crystal, which can function as everything from bow limbs to shields.
  - **Lea:** And as wands.
  - **Player:** Interesting... I wonder if...
  - **Lea:** Precisely my thoughts, so if you would consider bringing me one made with this crystal I would be very appreciative.
  - **Player:** Hmmm... yes... I could...
  - **Player:** Wait a moment. Before I do that we should agree payment for this  tool.
  - **Lea:** Indeed. It is good to see you learnt something from last time. So what would you ask of me?
  - **Player:** Errm, I'm not sure. Usually people just kind of offer me random things they happen to have to hand like old lamps, books or key rings.
  - **Lea:** Odd things to do favours for. I am afraid I do not have any oil lamps on me. I think I have a book on me if that'd interest you.
  - **Player:** Yes, that'll do. One new crystal contraption in exchange for one book. Deal?
  - **Lea:** Deal. I eagerly await your return with the item.
