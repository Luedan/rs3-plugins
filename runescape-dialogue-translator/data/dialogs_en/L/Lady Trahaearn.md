## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Lady Trahaearn:** Ah, hello again young Player, how can I help you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about the Seren Stones...
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** What would you like to know?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What are the Seren Stones?
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** The Seren Stones are pieces of Seren's crystaline essence that have become corrupted.
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** The corruption has hardened around the outside of the stones, lika a sinister shell.
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** It is the sacred duty of the Trahaearn to chip away at this corruption, to allow the stones to be purified by the Hefin.
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** What would you like to know?
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: How do I use them?
        - (Condition: If the player does not have 89 Mining:)
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** You don't, human. I sense in you a lack of skill with a pickaxe.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** I do not trust you not to chip the crystal itself. If you prove yourself a skilled miner then I may reconsider, but for now you are not to touch the crystals.
          - You need 89 Mining to use the Seren Stones.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** What would you like to know?
          - (previous)
        - (Condition: If the player has 89 Mining:)
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** Hmmm, you do seem skilled enough, for a human, I suppose there's no harm.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** It's hard work, but you need to use a pickaxe to chip the corruption off the outside of the stones.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** Doing so will give you an understanding of precision mining and you may also find corrupted ore.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** Corrupted ore is a foul substance with no other purpose than to be smelted away into nothingness.
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** If you find any, I would be graceful if you would dispose of it in a furnace somewhere. Only such heat is strong enough to destroy it.
          - (Condition: If the player has not claimed the Smithing XP lamp:)
            - **Lady Trahaearn:** In fact, if you smelt 100 corrupted ore for me, I'll reward your efforts.
            - (continue)
          - **Lady Trahaearn:** What would you like to know?
          - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Where do you find them?
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** Hrm. Is human eyesight as bad as I've been told?
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** They're directly north of me - those crystals over there.
        - **Lady Trahaearn:** What would you like to know?
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Ask about something else.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Lady Trahaearn...
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about yourself?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Huh? What?
    - **Player:** Erm...can you tell me about yourself?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** What? Speak up! I can't hear you.
    - **Player:** Can - you - tell - me - ab...
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** AHAHAHAHAHAHAAA! Ahem, sorry, it was too easy to pass up. I can hear you fine.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** I am Lady Trahaearn, leader of Clan Trahaearn.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** And yes, I am the oldest living elf in the city...likely the world.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Traditionally when the lord of a clan dies, they are succeeded by another who then leads the clan.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** But look around you. Look at the youngsters these days. No sense of decorum, or of how things should be.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** How could I leave my beloved clan to these children?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** No no no, it will not do.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** So I built this device - my masterpiece! It extends my life through a combination of crystal vapours and precise enchantments.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** With it I can continue to govern my beloved Trahaearn until a more suitable successor arrives.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Until then, my automatons and I will watch over the clan.
    - **Player:** You would trust machines over your own clan?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** It's not a matter of trust, but a matter of efficiency.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** My automatons get things done. They are efficient and unquestioning.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** They do not destroy with 'improvements', nor add features that only serve to ruin the overall design.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** They do what is asked of them and they do it well.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Unlike some of these youngsters.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Clan Trahaearn...
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about Clan Trahaearn?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** We are an ancient and noble clan of workers. We reshape the earth itself according to our designs.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Oh but we are much the same as the earth we shape, strong and each shaped to serve their purpose.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Like an anvil we hold firm the ways of the elves.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Like a hammer, we force shape upon elvenkind, giving it purpose and meaning.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Seren herself saw in us the future of the elves and she honoured our malleability and durability.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** I like to think she would be proud of what we have achieved.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Trahaearn skills...
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about the Trahaearn skills?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** We focus on the skills that lead to true creation: mining and smithing.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** We draw our raw materials from the very depths of the earth itself and then we refine them in the heat of our forges.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Is there a greater art? We find new purpose for dirt and metal, beyond just something pretty to look at.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Oh, we are more than just artists. We are also the chosen ones, who cleanse the corruption from Seren's broken body.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** We are the ones who chip the corrupted ore away from Seren's stones.
    - **Player:** What are the Seren Stones?
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** After Seren, in her grief and love for the elves, shattered herself. Pieces of her grow in the elven lands.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** But there is a darkness that grows there as well - a corruption.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** We find these stones and we bring them here. Then we spend our time carefully chipping the corruption away, purifying the stones.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** Oh, Hefin will say that they are the ones doing the purification, but they merely handle the final stage.
    - **Lady Trahaearn:** It is through the sweat and toil of Trahaearn that the stones are purified.
    - **Player:** Can I help?
    - (Condition: If the player does not have 89 Mining:)
      - **Lady Trahaearn:** Forgive me, but you do not yet possess the skill required to delicately remove the corruption. Perhaps with more training, but not now.
      - You need 89 Mining to mine the Seren Stones.
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: If the player has 89 Mining:)
      - **Lady Trahaearn:** You are friend to elfkind. We would welcome your assistance.
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - (end)

- **Lady Trahaearn:** Oh, I see you've been busy smelting corrupted ore.
- (Condition: Without one free backpack space:)
  - **Lady Trahaearn:** If you clear some room I have a small token of thanks for you.
  - (continuea|standard)
- (Condition: With one free backpack space:)
  - (receives=Smithing XP lamp)
  - {{RSFontAchievement|Uncorrupted Ore}}
  - (continuea|standard)
