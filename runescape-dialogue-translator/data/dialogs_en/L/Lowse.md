## Dialogue Transcript


### At Armadyl's Tower

- **Lowse:** Greeting, I am Lowse.
- **Player:** Low-sah? Am I saying that correctly?
- **Lowse:** Well enough.
- **Player:** It's a pleasure to meet you.
- **Lowse:** You also, Player. Your reputation precedes you.
- **Select an option**
  - Who are you?
    - **Lowse:** I am a diviner under the tutelage of head diviner Kamiee.
    - **Lowse:** She has tasked me to retrieve certain items for Armadyl in the tower above.
    - **Player:** Did you take part in the fight between Armadyl and Bandos.
    - **Lowse:** Not really. I joined up right before it ended. I was more interested in learning of divination than in fighting.
    - (initial)
  - What are you collecting?
    - **Lowse:** The items that Armadyl is after are all from a place named the Stormguard Citadel. Would you like to see the list?
    - **What would you like to do?**
      - View collections.
        - ((The Sky Is Lowse's interface opens.))
      - Talk about collections.
        - **Lowse:** This place was once one of Armadyl's numerous flying strongholds, but like much from the Third Age, it was lost.
        - **Lowse:** Our golem master, Gee'ka, has been sent to scout out the site itself, but we need working archaeologists to retrieve these items for us.
        - **Lowse:** However, you may also wish to discuss this directly with Armadyl above. He seeks out a couple of very specific items in particular.
        - (initial)
  - What are your collection rewards?
    - **Lowse:** We are willing to reward you with chronotes for all that you bring us, of course. That is a given.
    - ((Before completing Armadylean I:))
      - **Lowse:** I know... You can have my moonshroom mushroom spores! They're supposed to bring good luck, but I've never got them to grow.
      - **Lowse:** Though, I have heard that the Fairy Queen on Zanaris would love to get her hands on some. I'm sure she'd trade you something of equal value.
      - **Lowse:** Apparently, they used to belong to King Oberon, the first king of the fairies, and they are very hard to come by now.
      - **Lowse:** Unfortunately, I do not know how to reach Zanaris, but I sense that you might be able to - if not now then soon...
      - (continue)
    - ((After completing Armadylean I:))
      - **Lowse:** Did you take King Oberon's moonshroom spores to the Fairy Queen on Zanaris yet?
      - ((Before obtaining Queen Mab's Moonstone:))
        - ((Having lost the moonshroom spores:))
          - **Player:** Nope. In fact, I lost them! I don't suppose you have any more?
          - ((Without space in backpack:))
            - **Lowse:** As it so happens, I do, but you don't have room to take them.
          - ((With space in backpack:))
            - **Lowse:** *sigh* Did you miss the part where I said these were hard to come by? Here...
            - Take moonshroom sportes from Lowse?
              - Yes.
                - (receives=King Oberon's moonshroom spores)
                - (continue)
              - No.
                - (end)
        - ((Without having lost the moonshroom spores:))
          - **Player:** Not yet.
          - (continue)
      - ((After obtaining Queen Mab's Moonstone:))
        - **Player:** I did just that. She gave me a moonstone relic in return.
        - (continue)
    - ((Before completing Armadylean II:))
      - **Lowse:** Ah, yes, well... The team at Stormguard Citadel have been stumbling across all of these blueprint fragments. None of us have any use for them, so they're yours if you'd like. I have five hundred of them.
    - ((After completing Armadylean II:))
      - **Lowse:** Do anything good with those blueprint fragments yet?
    - ((Before completing Armadylean III:))
      - **Lowse:** I have a strange device, some sort of contraption you can place on your head that makes you more intelligent.
      - **Lowse:** It seems to make one better at artisan skills, learn things faster as it were. It's called Howl's Thinking Cap.
    - ((After completing Armadylean III:))
      - **Lowse:** Putting Howl's Thinking Cap to good use, are you?
    - ((Before giving aviansie dreamcoat to Armadyl:))
      - **Lowse:** One final thing... From what I gather, Armadyl's husband is said to have occasionally visited Stormguard Citadel, to sell clothes he'd tailored.
      - **Lowse:** If you find and restore an aviansie dreamcoat, maybe consider delivering it to Armadyl. I'm certain he would be thankful for such a gift.
      - (continue)
    - **Lowse:** I hope that those items are suitably enticing.
    - (initial)
  - Bye.
    - **Lowse:** Until we meet again.
