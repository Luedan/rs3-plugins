## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Lunar Diplomacy


#### Talking to him

- **Player:** Hello there.
- ** Lokar Searunner:** Don't talk to me outerlander! I don't have time for the likes of you!
- **Player:** What makes you say that?
- (Condition: Before completing The Fremennik Trials:)
  - ** Lokar Searunner:** You've experience yet to gain. Especially in keeping with the Fremenniks and their trials!/You're not yet a worthy enough adventurer to talk to me! You have some questing to do.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: After completing The Fremennik Trials, but without the requirements to start Lunar Diplomacy:)
  - **Lokar Searunner:** Only people who've travelled on quests far from here can talk to me!
  - (continue)
- **Player:** Oh.

#### Travel-Pirates' Cove option

- ** Lokar Searunner:** Sorry I am not going to take you to Pirates' Cove.

### After Lunar Diplomacy


#### At Rellekka

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Hi Lokar, can you take me back to your ship?
    - **Player:** Hi Lokar, can you take me back to your ship?
    - ** Lokar Searunner:** Sheesh! Make your mind up, [pal/lady], I'm not a taxi service! Do you want to go or not?
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: Yes please.
        - **Player:** Yes please.
        - {{Qact|Screen fades in and out. The player gets transported to Pirates' Cove.}}
      - [Choice]: No thanks. I've changed my mind.
        - **Player:** No thanks. I've changed my mind. <br>Again.<br> I don't want to go.
        - **Lokar Searunner:** You are possibly the most indecisive person I have ever met...
        - **Player:** Well, 'bye then.
  - [Choice]: It seems you've been charming Pauline Polaris.
    - **Player:** It seems you've been charming Pauline Polaris.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** What did you say?
    - ** Player:** Sorry. I mean, she was, erm, telling me a little about your business deal with her farm.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Oh. Right.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** So...
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Did she say much about me?
    - **Player:** Not really.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Oh.
    - **Player:** She said you'd got the seeds from the Jade Vine and that she was making it into livid.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Yeah, it stands for Lokar's Infernal Vine of Incredible Death!
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Muahahaha!
    - **Player:** Why am I not surprised.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** I stole them from that nuts Papa Mambo. Wasn't sure what to do with the seeds at the time, they just seemed valuable. Then, when I was at Lunar Isle, I remembered Pauline and wondered if she might be able to turn it into something truly deadly. Not that I said that to her, obviously.
    - **Player:** Obviously.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** I told her they could be used to help protect the people of Rellekka.
    - **Player:** So, you lied?
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Well...at first. Afterwards, I was pleased to find that Brundt would actually pay a hefty fee to get hold of them. Gullible idiot.
    - **Player:** I think Pauline would be very disappointed to hear that.
    - **Lokar Searunner:** Hey, don't you go telling her!
    - **Player:** Okay, okay!
    - **Player:** It's probably for the best. She might shut the whole enterprise down.
    - **Player:** Exactly!
    - **Lokar Searunner:** And, erm, then I won't have my deal with Brundt.
    - **Player:** Of course that's the reason.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Well, Pauline is probably itching to see you again.
        - **Player:** Well, Pauline is probably itching to see you again.
        - **Player:** Go visit her soon.
        - **Lokar Searunner:** Okay. Maybe I will.
      - [Choice]: Maybe you should leave Pauline alone.
        - **Player:** Maybe you should leave Pauline alone.
        - **Lokar Searunner:** Why?
        - **Player:** She's not used to your ways. It will only spell trouble.
        - **Lokar Searunner:** Don't worry yourself. Luckily for me, you can't decide what I do.
