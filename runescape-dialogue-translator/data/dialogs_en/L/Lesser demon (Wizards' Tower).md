## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to the demon


#### Before [[Rune Mysteries]]

- **Lesser demon:** Small mortal. No, you are not as interesting as she was. I will not speak to you.
- **Wizard Grayzag:** You there! Stop that! Speaking to the demon is strictly forbidden!
- Select an option
  - Why do you have a demon?
    - *(See Transcript:Wizard Grayzag#demon-origin)*
  - Oh, sorry.
    - (end)

#### After [[Rune Mysteries]]


##### Before claiming the reward


##### Without having 50 Magic and Prayer

- **Lesser demon:** So, the tower still stands, and I may be trapped here for another hundred years. I can wait. Return when you have half-mastered Magic and Prayer and you may even interest me.
- **Wizard Grayzag:** You there! Stop that! Speaking to the demon is strictly forbidden!

##### While having 50 Magic and Prayer

- **Lesser demon:** So, you have half-mastered the arts of Magic and Prayer. You interest me.
- **Wizard Grayzag:** You there! Stop that! Speaking to the demon is strictly forbidden!
- **Lesser demon:** Silence, guardian. You will not hear or see this one until I wish it. I have a deal for you to consider, mortal. Give me a single drop of your blood, and I will tell you secrets of the magical arts. Or vow not to do so, if you are squeamish. Perhaps your gods will reward you!
- Select an option
  - I'll give you a drop of blood. (Magic XP)
    - You give the demon a drop of your blood, and feel your mind expand with magical knowledge.
    - (10,000 Magic XP is rewarded.)
  - No! I will never deal with you! (Prayer XP)
    - You refuse the demon's offer, and your faithfulness to the gods is rewarded.
    - (10,000 Prayer XP is rewarded.)
  - I'll decide later.
    - (end)

##### After claiming the reward

- **Lesser demon:** Silence, small mortal. You no longer interest me.
