# Leatherworker

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Leatherworker](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ALeatherworker)
- **Letter**: `L`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Leatherworker:** Can I help you?
- Select an option
  - What do you do here?
    - **Leatherworker:** Well, I can cure plain cowhides into pieces of leather ready for crafting. I work with ordinary, hard or dragonhide leather and also snakeskin. What would you like to know about?
    - Select an option
      - Ordinary leather.
        - **Leatherworker:** I can work one piece of cow hide into ordinary leather. Is there anything else I can help you with?
      - Hard leather.
        - **Leatherworker:** Hard leather is made by specially treating normal leather by wetting and drying several times. It's then treated with pine oils. It's harder wearing than ordinary leather. Is there anything else I can help you with?
      - Snakeskin.
        - **Leatherworker:** I can work one piece of snake hide into a snakeskin. It only costs 15 pieces of gold. Snakeskin armour is great for intermediate level rangers and provides more defence than hard and studded leather. Is there anything else I can help you with?
      - Dragonhide leather.
        - **Leatherworker:** Dragonhide leather is made from dragonhides. One piece of hide can be worked into one piece of dragon leather. It costs 20 coins  each piece. Dragon leather is hard-wearing and has excellent flexibility. You can make strong and supple armour with this. Is there anything else I can help you with?
      - I'm bored of leather already.
        - **Leatherworker:** You may be bored now, but an arrow in the backside will soon fix that!
  - No thanks.
    - **Leatherworker:** Suit yourself.
