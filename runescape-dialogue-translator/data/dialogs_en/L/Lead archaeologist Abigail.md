## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Dealing with Scabaras

- **Lead archaeologist Abigail:** Greetings, wanderer. Is there any particular reason you have come to visit our humble camp?
- **Player:** I'm just exploring, really.
- **Lead archaeologist Abigail:** Hmm, well, try not to damage any of the antiquities. They have worth beyond your comprehension.
- **Player:** I'll bear that in mind.

### After Dealing with Scabaras

- **Lead archaeologist Abigail:** Hello again. Have you any news of the underground areas?
- **Player:** I have penetrated deeply into the cave system and discovered a temple inhabited by the High Priest of Scabaras. I even persuaded him to interfere less with the ways of the world and keep to himself.
- **Lead archaeologist Abigail:** This could lock away some religious details I might have found fascinating, but I suppose it's for the best.
- *If you have finished the Scabarite notes*
  - **Lead archaeologist Abigail:** Aha. I see you have gathered a mighty tome of research. I'll take a copy of that and throw you some cash for goodwill.
  - You receive 10,000 gold for a copy of your research notes.
  - (end)
