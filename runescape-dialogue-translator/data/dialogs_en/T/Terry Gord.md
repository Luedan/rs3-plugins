## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Void Knight Outpost, before Quiet Before the Swarm

- **Terry Gord:** Hi, how can I help you?
- [Menu]: What do you want to say?
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Terry Gord:** I'm Terry.
    - **Player:** Are you a Void Knight?
    - **Terry Gord:** No. I want to be. My father was, but when he died fighting the pests, my mother forbade me from joining up.
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Terry Gord:** This is the Void Knight Outpost. The Void Knights keep us all safe from the pests that are trying to invade. You should talk to one of them if you want to know more.
  - [Choice]: I'm fine, thanks.
    - **Player:** I'm fine, thanks.

### After Quiet Before the Swarm

- **Terry Gord:** I heard about the landing party. All my friends...and the Commodore...
- **Player:** Does it make you think twice about wanting to become a Void Knight?
- **Terry Gord:** Yes and no. I want to become one to honour their memory and keep the fight going, but to lose so many...
