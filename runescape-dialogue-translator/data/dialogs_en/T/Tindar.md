## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Tindar:** Creeespy frogs' legs! Get your creeeespy frogs' legs! You want some crispy frogs' legs? Just 10gp.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
- [Choice]: Yes please.
  - **Player:** Yes please!
  - (Condition: Without at least 10 coins:)
    - **Player:** But I don't have enough money on me.
  - (Condition: Without at least one free backpack space:)
    - **Tindar:** Looks like your hands are full. You'll have to free up some inventory space before I sell you anything.
  - (Condition: With at least one free backpack space:)
    - (removes=10 coins)
    - (receives=coated frogs' legs)
    - **Tindar:** There you go.
- [Choice]: No thanks.
  - **Player:** No thanks.
