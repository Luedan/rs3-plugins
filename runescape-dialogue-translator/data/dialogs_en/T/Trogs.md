# Trogs

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Trogs](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATrogs)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to without a Catspeak amulet

- **Trogs:** Meow?

### When spoken to with a Catspeak amulet


#### Before Purple Cat (miniquest)

- **Player:** You're purple!
- **Trogs:** Yes. Well spotted.
- **Player:** Are you naturally, you know, that colour? It's a rather vibrant hue.
- **Trogs:** Oh, no, I used to be more of a tortoiseshell until Wendy there started playing around.
- **Player:** Playing around?
- **Trogs:** Yes. I'm sure she'll tell you about it if you ask.

#### During the 2008 Halloween event

- *(If not wearing any costume items:)*
  - **Trogs:** No costume, eh? Aren't you planning on trick-or-treating this year?
- *(If wearing one costume item:)*
  - **Trogs:** Oh, I see you're wearing one costume item! Aren't you planning on trick-or-treating this year?
- *(If wearing two or more costume items:)*
  - **Trogs:** Rather nice costume, that. Are you trick-or-treating this year, then?

#### After Purple Cat (miniquest)

- **Player:** So, how do you like being purple?
- **Trogs:** Well, I felt rather conspicuous at first, but now I've grown to rather like the colour.
- **Player:** That's lucky.
- **Trogs:** Quite. As far as I know, Wendy doesn't know how to reverse the process.
