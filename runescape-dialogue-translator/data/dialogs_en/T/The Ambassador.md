## Dialogue Transcript


### Summoning the Crassian Leviathan

*One of these monologues will be spoken randomly*
- **The Ambassador:** I wondered when you would arrive. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_I_Wondered_When_You_Would_Arrive.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** When you would make your futile attempt to stop me. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_When_You_Would_Make_Your_Futile_Attempt_To_Stop_Me.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** You should surrender now. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Should_Surrender_Now.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Let the many mouths of my leviathan consume you. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Let_The_Many_Mouths_of_My_Leviathan_Consume_You.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** The pain will be far less than what you will face further in. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_The_Pain_Will_Be_Far_Less_Than_What_You_Will_Face_Further_In.ogg}}
-----
- **The Ambassador:** You shall go no further, abomination. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Shall_Go_No_Further,_Abomination.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** You trespass on a sacred moment. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Trespass_On_A_Sacred_Moment.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** We are changing the world. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_We_Are_Changing_The_World.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Your existence shall be little more than a footnote. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Your_Existance_Shall_Be_Little_More_Than_A_Footnote.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Fall now and be forgotten. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Fall_Now,_And_Be_Forgotten!.ogg}}
-----
- **The Ambassador:** I have seen the dead walk, but never so brazenly. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_I_Have_Seen_The_Dead_Walk,_But_Never_So_Brazenly.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** You are rotting before my eyes and your presence here only hastens your decay. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Are_Rotting_Before_My_Eyes_And_Your_Presence_Here_Only_Hastens_Your_Decay.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** It is for mercy's sake that my pet kills you now. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_It_Is_For_Mercys_Sake_That_My_Pet_Kills_You_Now.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** You would not like the world I am creating. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Would_Not_Like_The_World_I_Am_Creating.ogg}}
-----
- **The Ambassador:** So, this is who comes to kill me? Disappointing. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_So,_This_Is_Who_Comes_To_Kill_Me,_Dissapointing.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** I had expected something more, something special. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_I_Had_Expected_Something_More,_Something_Special.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** You are not worthy of my attention. My pet shall deal with you instead. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Are_Not_Worthy_of_My_Attention.ogg}} {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_My_Pet_Shall_Deal_With_You_Instead.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Die knowing that you are fit for nothing more, than as food for my pets. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Die,_Knowing_That_You_Are_Fit_For_Nothing_More_Than_As_Food_For_My_Pets.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** And perhaps, Not even that. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_And_Perhaps_Not_Even_That.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** *Laughs* {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Laugh.ogg}}

### The Fight


#### Phase 1

- **The Ambassador:** So you come to witness this great moment. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_So_You_Come_To_Witness_This_Great_Moment.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** To be present as his gaze is turned upon this world. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_To_Be_Present_When_His_Gaze_Is_Turned_Upon_This_World.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** To be one of the first, Blessed by his black radiance. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_To_Be_One_of_The_First_Blessed_By_His_Black_Radiance.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** I can understand such dreams. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_I_Can_Understand_Such_Dreams.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Come then, shed your mortal form and rejoice. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Come_Then,_Shed_Your_Mortal_Form_And_Rejoice.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Castil kreath fia Roake Kal. Skek Lac Ortius! {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Castil_Kreath_Fia_Roake_Kal_Skek_Lac_Ortius!.ogg}}

#### Fragment Summoning

*One is spoken randomly*
- **The Ambassador:** Your soul is but a candle in the darkness. I shall snuff it out! {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Your_Soul_Is_But_a_Candle_In_The_Darkness,_I_Shall_Snuff_It_Out!.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** BURN like the ants you are! {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_BURN_Like_The_Ants_You_Are!.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Have a taste...of his power. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Have_A_Taste_of_His_Power!.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** BEHOLD HIS GLORY! {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Behold_His_Glory!.ogg}}

#### Phase 2

- **The Ambassador:** You should surrender...now. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Should_Surrender_Now.ogg}}

#### Phase 3

*Seiryu appears from the darkness!*
- **The Ambassador:** You! Pathetic worm, you could have been magnificent. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Pathetic_Worm.ogg}} {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Could_Have_Been_Magnificent.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Must you fight so hard to reduce all the world to mediocrity? {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Must_You_Fight_So_Hard_To_Reduce_All_The_World_To_Mediocrity.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** Are you that pinioned by your weakness? {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Are_You_That_Pinioned_By_Your_Weakness.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** So be it. If you will not rise, then you shall FALL. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_So_Be_It.ogg}} {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_If_You_Will_Not_Rise,_Then_You_Shall_Fall!.ogg}}

#### Black energy barrage

- **The Ambassador:** [Player name]. Fall now, and be forgotten. (picks a random target from the group) {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Fall_Now,_And_Be_Forgotten!.ogg}}

#### Death

*One is spoken randomly*
- The Ambassador:*Cough* You think this is a victory? *cough* {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Cough!_You_Think_This_Is_A_Victory_Cough!.ogg}}
- The Ambassador:You live only because he has not noticed you. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_You_Live_Only_Because_He_Has_Not_Noticed_You.ogg}}
- The Ambassador:But soon his gaze will fall upon this world. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_But_Soon_His_Gaze_Will_Fall_Upon_This_World.ogg}}
- The Ambassador:And you will not be able to save them... {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_And_You_Will_Not_Be_Able_To_Save_Them.ogg}}
-----
- **The Ambassador:** *Cough* You think this is a victory? *cough* {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_Cough!_You_Think_This_Is_A_Victory_Cough!.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** All you have done is bought yourself a few moments of respite. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_All_You_Have_Done_Is_Bought_Yourself_A_Few_Moments_of_Respite.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** His arrival is inevitable. {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_His_Arrival_Is_Inevitable.ogg}}
- **The Ambassador:** And you will not be able to save them... {{Audio button|Ambassador_-_And_You_Will_Not_Be_Able_To_Save_Them.ogg}}

#### Using Greater Flurry, Fury or Barge

- **The Ambassador:** I see you've learned combat techniques from our Laboratory in Ullergrax. Vor...

#### Using Dragon Breath

- **The Ambassador:** Dragon breath? How quaint.

#### Using Blood/Shadow Tendrils

- **The Ambassador:**I am acquainted with the magicks of shadow and blood. His tendrils reach further.

#### Using Rapid Fire

- **The Ambassador:**Rapid Fire? A predictable rotation.
