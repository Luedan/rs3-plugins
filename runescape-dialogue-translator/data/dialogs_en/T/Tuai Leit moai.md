## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Tuai Leit moai:** Oh! Hello, little bug. I don't often get visitors. What brings you here?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Tuai Leit moai:** My name's Tuai Leit, but you can call me Sparkle.
    - **Player:** I can, but I'm not going to.
    - (previous|moai-options)
  - [Choice]: Are you related to the moai on Waiko?
    - **Tuai Leit moai:** Oh, Waiko. I love the sound of his voice. He's so dreamy.
    - **Tuai Leit moai:** No, we're not related. And he's married, the poor fool...to Aminishi, that ugly crone.
    - **Player:** So you don't know where I could find any of their children?
    - **Tuai Leit moai:** Sorry, sweetie, can't help you there. They've not returned my calls lately. I hope Waiko is alright.
    - **Tuai Leit moai:** I bet Aminishi told him not to talk to me anymore. Jealous old cougar.
    - (previous|moai-options)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
