## Dialogue Transcript


### In western Keldagrim

- **Player:** Say, aren't you a bit tall for a dwarf?
- **Tombar:** Was there anything in particular you wanted?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'd like a quest please.
    - **Player:** I'd like a quest please.
    - **Tombar:** I have nothing to do for you, I'm afraid. Ask around town, though, there are always people who need some work done around here.
  - [Choice]: No, I just like talking to strangers.
    - **Player:** No, I just like talking to strangers.
    - **Tombar:** Well I don't.
