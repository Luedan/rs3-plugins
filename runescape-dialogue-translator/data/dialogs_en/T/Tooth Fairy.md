## Dialogue Transcript


### Normal dialogue

- **Player:** How are you today?
- **Tooth Fairy:** Great, thanks! If you bring me some teeth, I'll offer you a fair price for them.
  - Bring you some teeth?
    - **Player:** Bring you some teeth?
    - **Tooth Fairy:** Yes, soldier, you heard me. The fairy economy is based on the appropriation of teeth - they're very valuable to us. If you bring me the right kind of teeth, I'll give you gold in return.
  - What sort of teeth and what price do you pay?
    - **Player:** What sort of teeth and what price do you pay?
    - **Tooth Fairy:** Specifically, we're looking for kebbit teeth, suqah teeth, 'old' teeth and, finally, monkey dentures.
    - **Player:** And prices?
    - **Tooth Fairy:** Oh, yes, prices... Kebbit teeth are quite common, so we only pay one gold for those; monkey dentures are not that in demand, so we only pay 50 gold; suqah and 'old' teeth will fetch you a hefty 2,000 gold each.
  - Where can I find the teeth you're after?
    - **Player:** Where can I find the teeth you're after?
    - **Tooth Fairy:** You can get kebbit teeth from hunting, I believe; suqah teeth you'll have to investigate yourself; old teeth you can gain from archaeological excavation areas; monkey dentures you'll also have to research yourself.
  - Okay, thanks.
    - **Player:** Okay, thanks.

### Situational dialogue

*If you don't have a tooth pet, and you have teeth in your inventory*
- **Tooth Fairy:** What can I do for you soldier? I might be able to offer you a tooth pet...for the right price. Or maybe you have some teeth you'd like to sell?
  - I'd like to claim a tooth pet.
    - **Player:** I'd like to claim a tooth pet.
    - **Tooth Fairy:** I'll need the following teeth in order to give you a tooth pet.
    - Kebbit tooth<br>Suqah tooth<br>Old tooth<br>...<br>Monkey dentures<br>Human child's tooth
    - *If you have some of the required teeth*
      - **Player:** I have some of those teeth.
      - **Tooth Fairy:** I'll have those, thanks.
      - You receive a tooth pet.
      - {{Qact|The 5 different teeth are removed from your inventory, and a tooth pet is placed in your inventory}}
  - I have some teeth to sell.
    - *(same as below)*
  - How are you today?
    - *(same as above)*
*If you have a tooth pet already and you have teeth in your inventory*
- **Tooth Fairy:** What can I do for you soldier? Maybe you have some teeth you'd like to sell?
  - I have some teeth to sell.
    - **Tooth Fairy:** Kebbit teeth: x :| Suqah teeth: x | Old teeth: x | Monkey dentures: x <br>Total coins: x
    - Accept x gold coins for all teeth?
      - Yes.
        - The Tooth Fairy give you some coins for the teeth.
      - No.
  - How are you today?
    - *(same as above)*
