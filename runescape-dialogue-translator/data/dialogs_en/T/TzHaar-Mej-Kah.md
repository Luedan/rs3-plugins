## Dialogue Transcript


### Normal dialogue

- **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** You want help, [Title]-[Caste]-Player?
- Select an Option
  - What is this place?
    - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** This is the Fight Pit. TzHaar-Xil made it for their sport but many JalYt come here to fight, too. If you are wanting to fight then enter the cage. You will be summoned when next round is ready to begin.
    - Are there any rules?
      - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** No rules... you use whatever you want. Last person standing wins and is declared champion. They stay in the pit for the next fight.
      - Do I win anything?
        - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** You ask a lot of questions. Champion gets TokKul as reward, more fights the more TokKul they get.
        - *(If the player has completed Brink of Extinction quest:)*
          - **Player:** You're still handing out TokKul as a reward? TzHaar-Mej-Jeh said it was going to be melted down in the sacred lava, to release your dead from their torment.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** TzHaar adjust slowly to change. You should know. You are TzHaar now.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** TokKul donated to TzHaar-Mej is returned to the sacred lava. Until new token is found, TzHaar still honour JalYt with TokKul.
        - *(Otherwise:)*
          - **Player:** ...
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Before you ask, TokKul is like your coins.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Gold is like you JalYt: soft. We use hard obsidian, forged in fire like TzHaar!
      - Sounds good.
        - *(Back to options)*
    - Thanks.
      - *(Dialogue ends.)*
  - Who's the current champion?
    - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Ah, that would be [Title]-[Caste]-[Player name of current Fight Pits champion]!
  - What did you call me?
    - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Are you not [Title]-[Caste]?
    - Select an Option
      - Why do you call me '[Title]-[Caste]'?
        - *(If the player is of the Ket caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** That what you are...you tough and strong, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Xil caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** That what you are...you agile and quick, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Mej caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** That what you are...you user of mystic powers, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Hur caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** That what you are...you skilled at making things, no?
        - *(If the player has completed The Brink of Extintion:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** And you are JalYt no longer. You are TzHaar now.
        - **Player:** Well, yes, I suppose I am...
        - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Then you [Title]-[Caste]!
        - Select an Option
          - What are you then?
            - *(If the player has not completed The Elder Kiln:)*
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Foolish JalYt. I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics of this city.
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
            - *(Otherwise:)*
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics for the city. The TzHaar-Mej guide the TzHaar when change is necessary, and tend to TzHaar eggs to ensure they are hot and healthy. TzHaar-Mej are keeps of knowledge and magic.
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
          - Thanks for explaining it.
            - (end)
      - *(If the player has not completed The Brink of Extinction:)*
        - I guess so...
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Well, then. There is no problem.
        - No, I'm not!
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Whatever you say, JalYt!
      - *(Otherwise:)*
        - Yes, I certainly am.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Then it is an honour to speak with you, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. You great and powerful TzHaar, and defender of our city.
        - You must have me confused with another TzHaar.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** I heard you are modest, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. No need. Revel in our praise. You deserve all honour.
  - No, I'm fine. Thanks
    - *(Dialogue ends.)*

### Fight Pit

- *(At the start of a round:)*
  - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Wait for my signal before fighting.
  - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Fight!
- *(If the player wins the round:)*
  - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Well done, you were the last person in the pit and won that fight! The next round will start soon, wait for my signal before fighting.
  - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Fight!
- *(When the player exits the Fight Pit after winning:)*
  - **TzHaar-Mej-Kah:** Well done in the pit, here take TokKul as reward.
