## Dialogue Transcript


### In eastern Keldagrim


#### Standard dialogue

- **Tati:** What'you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you have any pickaxes?
    - **Player:** Do you-
    - **Tati:** What? Speak up, I can't hear you!
    - **Player:** Uhm... I'm just looking for some pickaxes! Do you have any?
    - **Tati:** Do I have any pickaxes? Do I? Of course I do, this is a pickaxe shop, isn't it!
    - (opens=Pickaxe-Is-Mine)
  - [Choice]: Do you have any quests?
    - **Player:** Do you-
    - **Tati:** What? Stop mumbling!
    - **Player:** I want a quest!
    - **Tati:** I don't have any lousy quests... I've got someone who's helping me already! Well, I say helping... he takes his merry time to do his chores, my son does.
    - **Player:** Then perhaps I can help in some way?
    - **Tati:** Pfft, I doubt it... maybe when my son fouls up again, but not now.
  - [Choice]: Nothing really.
    - **Player:** Nothing really.
    - **Tati:** Then clear off!

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Tati:** My no good son must be down the pub again, drinking away all our money instead of getting one simple item from the market for me. Now, I have another errand. Perhaps you will be more useful?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's it about?
    - **Player:** What's it about?
    - **Tati:** I'm in need of some raw supplies. Get me [item] and be quick about it! I need them within the next [number] minutes!
    - **Player:** Hmm, [item] in [number] minutes, that sounds about alright.
  - [Choice]: I don't work for rude people.
    - **Player:** I don't work for rude people.
    - **Tati:** You're just as bad as my son, or worse!

##### Talking to Tati without the item

- **Tati:** Where're my [item]? My son could've gotten them faster!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I've had enough of this wild goose chase.
    - **Player:** I've had enough of this wild goose chase.
    - **Tati:** Why you useless little human! I should thrown you in the Kelda! With a lump of granite attached to your feet!
    - **Player:** Ha, who are you calling little here?
    - **Tati:** Get out!
  - [Choice]: I'm still trying to collect them.
    - **Player:** I'm still trying to collect them.
    - **Tati:** Try a little harder! I don't have all day you know!

##### Talking to Tati with the item

- **Player:** I've collected all [item] for you!
- **Tati:** Give 'em to me quick before I change my mind about rewarding you! Do you want me to pay you in gold or would you prefer me to teach you a bit about how you can mine more efficiently?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Show me the gold!
    - **Player:** Show me the gold!
    - (receives=coins)
  - [Choice]: Enlighten me.
    - **Player:** Enlighten me.
    - {{Qact|Screen fades in and out}}
    - (receives=experience)
    - You feel more experienced.
