### Standard dialogue

- **Talsar:** Hello there. This place...it amazes and terrifies me, it really does. To think such places can even exist in the first place... May the gods be merciful.
- {{anchor|initial-menu}}[Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Talsar:** I go by the name of Talsar. I'm a seer from Rellekka. When I got word of what my people found here, I had a vision. I knew that there was something here for me.
    - **Talsar:** So I travelled all the way here and on arriving I felt a connection with this place. I can feel its power twisting, bending, wrapping around me - even out here.
    - **Talsar:** And that's when I realised I could sense other places that felt the same - other dungeons exist that are like here. I get them in flashes but I think they may be places that adventurers, like you, are interested in.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are you talking about?{{anchor|what}}
    - **Talsar:** When I heard about this place, I travelled here from Rellekka. At first, we thought this place was a one of a kind, but there are other dungeons around the world similar to this.
    - **Talsar:** Most of the time they are hidden below the surface of the world but they occasionally break through. I have a feeling they've been hidden for a long time but that the magic around them has weakened, allowing us to find them.
    - **Talsar:** A few of the Fremennik warriors travelled to one of them and found a dungeon full of treasures but they couldn't break through the door to get to it.
    - **Talsar:** They say someone else was there - a creature like the ones inside Daemonheim - but when Thok threatened to punch him he disappeared and we made it no farther.
    - **Talsar:** That's where you come in. I think if we could get that creature to talk, we could get into that treasure room and take whatever is in there.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Where will the next sinkhole appear?
    - **Player:** Where will the next sinkhole appear?
      - (If on a members world:)
        - **Talsar:** Let's try to see...
        - {{qact|Talsar bangs his stick and a blue light glows beneath him}}
        - (Condition: If it is near Barbarian Outpost:)
          - **Talsar:** The next sinkhole will appear close to where barbarians train for agility in about [number] minutes.
        - (Condition: If it is near the Barrows:)
          - **Talsar:** The next sinkhole will appear near where several famous brothers lie in about [number] minutes.
        - (Condition: Otherwise:)
          - **Talsar:** The next sinkhole will appear [location hint] in about [number] minutes.
      - (If on a free-to-play world:)
        - You need to be on a members server to raid sinkholes.
  - [Choice]: More...
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do you think of this place?
        - **Talsar:** There's something terrifying, yet soothing, about this place. I know, I know, it makes no sense does it?
        - **Talsar:** The power I can feel here, it's intoxicating, but I finally feel like I have found a purpose here. I can use my gift for my people and keep them safe within Daemonheim.
        - **Talsar:** Of course, that does mean I have to put up with certain people that can drive you mad.
        - **Player:** What do you mean?
        - **Talsar:** I'm sure you've had the pleasure of meeting that mad man, Skaldrun, ranting about living for centuries and telling those tall tales.
        - **Talsar:** Honestly, I've never met a man so infuriating.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: About the Task System...{{anchor|task-system}}
        - (Condition: On a free-to-play world:)
          - **Talsar:** I'm afraid you'll need to be a member to claim any rewards for the task set.
        - (Condition: If the player has completed the hard Daemonheim achievements and the rewards have not been claimed:)
          - **Player:** I've completed all of the Hard tasks in the Daemonheim set.
          - **Talsar:** So you have.
          - [Talsar hands you an aura, 38,000 Dungeoneering tokens, and a lamp.]
          - **Talsar:** There you go. You can now pass through the magical barrier in the Dwarven Mine resource dungeon.
          - **Player:** Thanks! What else can I do?
          - **Talsar:** Because you completed the Hard Task set, you will have gained 2 more bind slots in your bind pool. I've also had a word with the Gorajo Hoardstalker and you should get more rewards for completing Sinkhole games.
          - **Talsar:** Marmaros has agreed to give you a little discount when you recharge your items as well.
          - **Thok of Daemonheim:** Hey! Talsar think tiny person head thick enough to learn from Thok?
          - **Talsar:** Erm...I...think so?
          - **Thok of Daemonheim:** Then Thok teach.
          - Thok has taught you a pale shadow of his formidable head-butt attack. <br>You may use it instead of the Backhand ability. You can activate this from the Wardrobe.
          - (continue)
        - [Menu]: Choose an option:
          - [Choice]: Tell me about the Achievement System.
            - **Talsar:** Very well: the Achievement System is a collection of deeds you may wish to complete while adventuring around the world.
            - **Talsar:** You can earn special rewards for completing certain achievements; at the very least, each is worth a cash bounty from Explorer Jack in Lumbridge.
            - **Talsar:** Some also give items that will help complete other achievements, and many count as progress towards the set for the area they're in.
            - [Menu]: Choose an option:
              - [Choice]: Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
                - **Player:** Tell me about the set reward for this area.
                - **Talsar:** For completing the Daemonheim set, you are presented with an aura.
                - **Talsar:** This aura will become increasingly useful as you complete greater difficulties of task sets.
                - **Talsar:** When you are presented with your rewards, you will be told of its uses.
                - You may also see what rewards a set item provides by hovering over the Rewards icon for its Exploration Achievement set in the Achievements interface.
                - (previous2|task-system)
              - [Choice]: How do I claim these rewards?
                - **Player:** How do I claim the rewards?
                - **Talsar:** You need to complete all tasks within a particular set, then you can claim your reward.
                - **Talsar:** Some of the Daemonheim set's tasks are simple, some require certain skill levels, and others require quests to be started or completed.
                - **Talsar:** To claim your Daemonheim set rewards, speak to Drangund the Dungeoneering Tutor, Marmaros the Rewards trader, Thok or myself.
                - (previous2|task-system)
          - [Choice]: Sorry, I was just leaving.
            - (end)

### When a sinkhole is active

- (Condition: If it is about one minute before the sinkhole appears:)
  - **Talsar:** I sense it! A sinkhole is about to appear on the surface in 1 minute! I can teleport you there, if you wish.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the sinkhole appears:)
  - **Talsar:** I sense it! A sinkhole... There's another one on the surface! I can teleport you there, if you wish.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the sinkhole entrance.}}
  - [Choice]: No.
    - **Talsar:** That's your decision I guess, but I think whatever these places are is worth investigating.
  - [Choice]: What are you talking about?
    - (above|what)
  - [Choice]: About the Task System...
    - (above|task-system)

### Teleport option

- (Condition: If the sinkhole is about to appear:)
  - (Condition: If the sinkhole is about to appear within several minutes:)
    - **Talsar:** The sinkhole won't appear for another [number] minutes. Are you sure you want to teleport there now?
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: If the sinkhole is about to appear within one minute:)
    - **Talsar:** The sinkhole won't appear for another minute. Are you sure you want to teleport there now?
    - (continue)
  - [Menu]: Teleport to sinkhole?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the sinkhole entrance.}}
    - [Choice]: No.
      - (end)
- (Condition: If the sinkhole is not about to appear:)
  - **Talsar:** Alas, there are no sinkholes on the surface at the moment. Was there anything else you wanted?
  - (above|initial-menu)
