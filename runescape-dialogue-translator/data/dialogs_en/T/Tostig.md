## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to


#### Normally

- **Tostig:** Welcome to the Pick and Lute! What can I get you?
- **Player:** Hi, what ales are you serving?
- **Tostig:** Well sir, our speciality is Asgarian Ale, we also serve Wizard's Mind Bomb and Dwarven Stout.
- **Tostig:** Would you like to buy a drink?
- Select an option
  - Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - (opens=The Pick and Lute)
  - No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Tostig:** Ah um... so um... does the grey squirrel sing in the grove?
    - Select an option
      - Huh?
        - **Player:** Huh?
        - **Tostig:** Er... never mind.
      - Yes, and the black cat dances in the sink.
        - **Player:** Yes, and the black cat dances in the sink.
        - **Tostig:** Ah you'll be wanting the trapdoor behind the bar.
      - No, squirrels can't sing.
        - **Player:** No, squirrels can't sing.
        - **Tostig:** No... of course they can't...

#### With a keg of mead before making a delivery to The Pick and Lute during The Need for Mead event

- **Tostig:** Welcome to the Pick and Lute! How can I help you?
- **Player:** I've come from the brewer, Leslie. They were wondering if you could try their latest batch of mead?
- **Tostig:** Leslie, you say? Not heard from them in a while.
- **Tostig:** Still meddling with all things bee, are they?
- **Player:** They sure are. I'm sure their mead is delicious too.
- **Tostig:** Well, I can see the seal on the keg isn't broken, so hand it over.
- (removes=keg of mead) (receives=50 nectar)
- (see|nectar)
- ((Shown in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|You find 50 x Nectar as you hand over the keg of mead.|colour=#00FF00}}
  - (continue)
- **Tostig:** Oh nice. Yes, that's great. Could use some improvements in the overall flavour though.
- **Tostig:** How much water was added during the brewing stage?
- **Player:** No idea...
- **Tostig:** Well, pass this on to Leslie. There's too much water and it's diluting the flavour.
- **Tostig:** The biggest problem here though is the intensity. I think Leslie needs to change up the food they feed to their bees.
- **Tostig:** Richer food will bring out richer flavours.
- **Player:** I'll pass that on.
