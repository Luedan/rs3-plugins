## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Welcome to the TzHaar library, [Race]-[Caste]-Player. Can TzHaar-Mej-Lor, Lord of TzHaar Library, help you?
  - Select an Option
    - A TzHaar library, eh?
      - **Player:** A TzHaar library, eh? I didn't know the TzHaar had libraries. Or knew how to read for that matter.
      - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** TzHaar have a very good language,[Race]-[Caste]-Player. We always have known how to carve and read but we have not always had library. It was idea we got when JalYt started to visit TzHaar city. You seem very lost and very misplaced in our culture. Yes, you crowd the Fight Pit which is good entertainment to watch, but you know nothing. So to make city easier for JalYt to understand, we set up library and got books for you. But you do not seem to want to learn. Almost only TzHaar visit library now. The few JalYt who do come here always ask about ashes and never about books. You seem to be obsessed with ashes.
    - What's with the ashes?
      - **Player:** How come the bookcases in the back are full of ashes?
      - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Yes, TzHaar call them ashes. We did not bring them here for TzHaar. They are for JalYt who visit the city, so you can read and learn about TzHaar.
      - **Player:** How are you supposed to read ashes?
      - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** They are JalYt books, you should know. I am TzHaar, we read on stone.
      - **Player:** Alright... well, Im off now. Bye.
    - What did you call me?
      - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Are you not [Title]-[Caste]?
      - Select an Option
        - Why do you call me '[Title]-[Caste]'?
          - *(If the player is of the Ket caste:)*
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** That what you are...you tough and strong, no?
          - *(If the player is of the Xil caste:)*
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** That what you are...you agile and quick, no?
          - *(If the player is of the Mej caste:)*
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** That what you are...you user of mystic powers, no?
          - *(If the player is of the Hur caste:)*
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** That what you are...you skilled at making things, no?
          - *(If the player has completed The Brink of Extintion:)*
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** And you are JalYt no longer. You are TzHaar now.
          - **Player:** Well, yes, I suppose I am...
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Then you [Title]-[Caste]!
          - Select an Option
            - What are you then?
              - *(If the player has not completed The Elder Kiln:)*
                - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Foolish JalYt. I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics of this city.
                - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
              - *(Otherwise:)*
                - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics for the city. The TzHaar-Mej guide the TzHaar when change is necessary, and tend to TzHaar eggs to ensure they are hot and healthy. TzHaar-Mej are keeps of knowledge and magic.
                - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
            - Thanks for explaining it.
              - (end)
        - *(If the player has not completed The Brink of Extinction:)*
          - I guess so...
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Well, then. There is no problem.
          - No, I'm not!
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Whatever you say, JalYt!
        - *(Otherwise:)*
          - Yes, I certainly am.
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** Then it is an honour to speak with you, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. You great and powerful TzHaar, and defender of our city.
          - You must have me confused with another TzHaar.
            - **TzHaar-Mej-Lor:** I heard you are modest, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. No need. Revel in our praise. You deserve all honour.
    - No, thanks
      - **Player:** No, thanks.
