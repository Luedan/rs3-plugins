## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet


#### First time

- You pet The Postcat, and take a copy of their pawprint.
- You have found The Postcat.
- [You have discovered a new cat!]
- [You quickly note it down in your logbook.<br><br>'The Postcat']

#### Afterwards

- You pet The Postcat, and take a copy of their pawprint.
- **The Postcat:** {{RSChat|Meow!}}

### Talk to


#### Without catspeak

- **The Postcat:** Meow, meow meow.

#### With catspeak

- **The Postcat:** Hello there, and what's your name?
- **Player:** Err...Player?
- **The Postcat:** Sorry, I don't have any post for you here.
- **The Postcat:** Say, you couldn't do an old cat a favour? I seem to have misplaced my pet human somewhere along my route today.
- **Player:** Oh, really?
- **The Postcat:** Yes, she's wearing black and white and responds to the name Jess. Let her know to meet me in Pollnivneach this evening if you see her, thanks!
