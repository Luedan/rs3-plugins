## Dialogue Transcript


### In Marim, on Ape Atoll


#### While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **The Monkey's Uncle:** Ook! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ook! Ah!
- You cannot understand what he is saying.

#### While wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **The Monkey's Uncle:** What are you doing in my house?
- **Player:** I'm just passing through!
- **The Monkey's Uncle:** Well I don't give a monkey's uncle!
