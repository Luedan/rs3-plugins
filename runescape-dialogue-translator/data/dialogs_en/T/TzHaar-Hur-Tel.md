          - *Ends dialogue*
        - **Player:** Thanks for explaining it.
        - *Ends dialogue*
    - **Player:** Yes, I certainly am.
      - **Tzhaar-Hur-Tel:** Then it is an honour to speak with you, TzHaar-Mej-*Player*. You great and powerful TzHaar and defender of our city.
      - *Ends dialogue*
    - **Player:** You must have me confused with another TzHaar.
