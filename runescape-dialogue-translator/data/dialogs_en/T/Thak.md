## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Forgiveness of a Chaos Dwarf


#### Talk-to option

- **Thak:** Sorry, human. This is a private mining area.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who owns this mining area?
    - **Player:** Who owns this mining area?
    - (Condition: Before completing Between a Rock...:)
      - **Thak:** It's jointly owned by the mining companies of the Consortium.
    - (Condition: After completing Between a Rock...:)
      - **Thak:** Dondakan bought it after he struck big with his cannon in the south mines. He's keeping it exclusive, though.
  - [Choice]: How much would it cost you for you to look the other way?
    - **Player:** How much would it cost you for you to look the other way?
    - **Thak:** It would end up costing me my job. Move along now.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
