## Dialogue Transcript


### In Marim, on Ape Atoll


#### While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **The Monkey's Aunt:** Ook! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ook! Ah!
- You cannot understand what she is saying.

#### While wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **The Monkey's Aunt:** Hello! What's a monkey like you doing here?
- **Player:** I'm just passing through actually.
- **The Monkey's Aunt:** Fair enough then.
