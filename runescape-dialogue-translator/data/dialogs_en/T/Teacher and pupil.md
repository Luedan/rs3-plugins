# Teacher and pupil

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Teacher and pupil](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATeacher%20and%20pupil)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Ground floor

- **Teacher:** Stop pulling, we've plenty of time to see everything.
- **Pupil:** Aww, but miss, it's sooo exciting.

### Second floor

- **Pupil:** That man over there talks funny, miss.
- **Teacher:** That's because he's an art critic, dear. They have some very funny ideas.

### Basement, male student with male teacher

- **Pupil:** Teacher! Sir! I need the toilet!
- **Teacher:** I told you to go before we got here.
- **Pupil:** But sir, I didn't need to go then!
- **Teacher:** Alright, come on then.

### Basement, female student with female teacher

- **Teacher:** What do you want to see now, dear?
- **Pupil:** The penguins, the penguins!
