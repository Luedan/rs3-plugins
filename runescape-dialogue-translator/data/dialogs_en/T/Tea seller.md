## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Tea seller:** Greetings! Are you in need of refreshment?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - (opens=Ye Olde Tea Shoppe)
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Tea seller:** Well, if you're sure. You know where to come if you do.
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - **Player:** What are you selling?
    - **Tea seller:** Only the most delicious infusion of the leaves of the tea plant. Grown in the exotic regions of this world. Buy yourself a cup.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from the tea stall

- **Tea seller:** {{RSChat|Hey! Get your hands off there!}}

#### Talking to him after stealing from the tea stall

- **Tea seller:** {{RSChat|You're the one who stole something from me!}}
- **Tea seller:** {{RSChat|Guards, guards!}}

#### Attempting to take the cup of tea on the table

- **Tea seller:** Hey! Put that back! Those are for display only!
