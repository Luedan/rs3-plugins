## Dialogue Transcript


### At Astram Farm in Taverley

''Note: Dialogue only plays for players with level 1 Crafting and no access to hard leather tanning

#### Using the Tan hide option with nothing suitable in backpack

- **Tanya Hyde:** You don't have any hides. Maybe you should kill some cows?

#### Using the Tan hide option with leather in backpack

- **Tanya Hyde:** You don't have any hides, but you do have some leather. Did I already tan it for you? I don't remember.

#### Using the Tan hide option with cowhide in backpack

- (removes=all cowhides in inventory) (receives=one leather for each cowhide)
- [Tanya tans your cowhides into leather. You can now craft the leather in your backpack into ranged armour.]
