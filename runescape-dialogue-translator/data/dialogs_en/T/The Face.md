# The Face

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:The Face](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AThe%20Face)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### On free-to-play worlds

- **The Face:** I have no time for you.

### Before Ratcatchers

- **Player:** Hello.
- She looks through you as if you don't exist.

### After Ratcatchers

- **Player:** Hello.
- **The Face:** Oh it's you again. *She glances at you with barely disguised disdain.*
- **Player:** *Aside* I wonder what her problem is?
- **The Face:** I must congratulate you on your performance, I have only ever seen one other display of musicality to match, as you should know.
- **Player:** Thank you, and don't worry, I won't tell Felkrash that you helped me.
