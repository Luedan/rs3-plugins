## Dialogue Transcript


#### Start of fight

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|You have made a grave mistake.}}

#### Tendril attack

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|Your anima will return to the source!}}

#### Uppercut

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|Gielinor, give me strength!}}

#### Stun

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|Hold still, invader.}}

#### So Much Power

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|SO. MUCH. POWER!}}

#### Weak anima bomb

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|Let the anima consume you!}}

#### Instant kill bomb (phase 4)

**Note:** This dialogue only occurs if the player creates a shield from the font Telos is draining at within 4 seconds after Telos begins draining it.
- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|You dare to defy me?}}

#### Activating a font (phase 5)

- **Telos, the Warden:** {{RSChat|You dare to use my power against me?}}
