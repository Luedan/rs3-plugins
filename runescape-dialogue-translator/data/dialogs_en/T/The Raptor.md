### Assignment advice


#### Aberrant spectres

- Aberrant spectres are fetid, vile ghosts. The very smell of them will paralyze and harm you. I would advise using a nose peg to avoid their stink. You would do well using a ranged weapon against them.

#### Abyssal demons

- **The Raptor:** The last Abyssal demon I killed I found slashing attacks took them down effectively.
- Abyssal demons are nasty creatures to deal with. They teleport around to confuse and trap their prey. They can be found within the Canifis Slayer Tower or inhabiting the Senntisten Asylum.

#### Acheron mammoths

- **The Raptor:** The last Acheron mammoth I killed I found stabbing attacks took them down effectively.
- Acheron mammoths are honourable and proud beasts from the frozen north that know the true worth of battle. When they charge you, ensure you get out of the way. They can be found on an iceberg that is accessible from a dock east of Rellekka.

#### Ankou

- **The Raptor:** The last Ankou I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- Ankou are undead beings that can be described as neither ghost nor skeleton, but a combination of both. Not much is known about these creatures, however, they make their home in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Armoured phantoms

- **The Raptor:** The last Armoured phantom I killed I found crushing attacks took them down effectively.
- You can find armoured phantoms swarming inside the crypts north of Fort Forinthry. Their hateful spirits seek only violence. You'll find crush weapons effective at breaking down their ability to move.

#### Aviansies

- Aviansies can be found within the God Wars Dungeon north of Trollheim. As they fly, you'll find it difficult to reach them with melee weapons, so try using ranged or magic weapons instead.

#### Black demons

- **The Raptor:** The last Black demon I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down effectively.
- Black demons are among the strongest of the Avernic demons. They can be found within the Brimhaven and Taverley dungeons. Using crossbows would be advantageous.

#### Black dragons

- **Raptor:** The last Black dragon I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down effectively.
- Black dragons are the strongest of the chromatic dragons. They can be found resting within Taverley Dungeon. Using crossbows would be advantageous.

#### Bound skeletons

- **The Raptor:** The last Bound skeleton I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- Animated by Zemouregal, you can find bound skeletons swarming inside the crypts north of Fort Forinthry. You'll find earth magic effective at breaking down their ancient bones.

#### Camel warriors

- **The Raptor:** The last Camel Warrior I killed I found crushing attacks took them down effectively.
- Camel Warriors use ancient magics to attack you. They can use cowardly disorientation magic to confuse you. You can find them in the deep desert, teach them a lesson in humility.

#### Dagannoth

- **The Raptor:** The last Dagannoth I killed I found air spells took them down effectively.
- Dagannoth are large, sea-dwelling creatures that are very aggressive. You'll often find them in caves near seawater.

#### Dark beasts

- **The Raptor:** The last Dark beast I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down effectively.
- Dark beasts are large, dog-like predators with massively muscled bodies that protect them from crushing weapons. They can be found within the Mourner tunnels.

#### Demons

- **The Raptor:** The last Abyssal demon I killed I found slashing attacks took them down effectively.
- You can find many types of demons around Gielinor, notably the abyssal demons infesting the Canifis Slayer Tower.

#### Dragons

- **The Raptor:** The last Green dragon I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down efficiently.
- You can find dragons through Gielinor. Many types of them can be found within Brimhaven Dungeon.

#### Fetid zombies

- **The Raptor:** The last Fetid zombie I killed I found air spells took them down effectively.
- These pitiful creatures have no mind of their own and seek only the flesh of their enemies. You can find fetid zombies inside the crypts north of Fort Forinthry. You'll find using magic effective against them.

#### Fire giants

- **The Raptor:** The last Fire giant I killed I found water spells took them down effectively.
- Like other giants, fire giants often wield large weapons. Learn to recognise what kind of weapon it is and act accordingly. They can be found in Brimhaven Dungeon. Using water spells would be advantageous.

#### Ghosts

- **The Raptor:** The last Risen ghost I killed I found a bow and arrows took them down effectively.
- You can find many types of ghosts around Gielinor, notably those flooding the crypts north of Fort Forinthry.

#### Greater demons

- **The Raptor:** The last Greater demon I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down effectively.
- Greater demons are relatively weak, however, you should still be careful as they can be dangerous. They can be found in Brimhaven Dungeon. Using crossbows would be advantageous.

#### Green dragons

- Green dragons are the weakest of the chromatic dragons and can be found west of the Dark Warriors Fortress. Using crossbows would be advantageous.

#### Grotworms

- **The Raptor:** The last Young grotworm I killed I found a crossbow with bolts took them down effectively.
- Grotworms have been spotted in a cave near Rimmington. They will bite and attack with some sort of acid spit.

#### Hellhounds

- Hellhounds are Shakroth class Avernic demons, the lowest class of demons from Infernus. You can find them in Taverley Dungeon and will find slash weapons effective against them.

#### Kalphites

- **The Raptor:** The last Kalphite Soldier I killed I found water spells took them down effectively.
- Kalphites are large insectoids that live in hives under the Kharidian desert. You can find them southwest of Shantay Pass.

#### Lesser demons

- Lesser demons are some of the weakest of all Avernic demons. They can be found inhabiting the Karamja Dungeon. Using crossbows would be advantageous.

#### Living wyverns

- **The Raptor:** The last Wyvern I killed I found fire spells took them down effectively.
- Living wyverns breathe a poison that will rapidly kill you unless you can freeze your blood using the glacial temperature of their home. But watch out; the colder you are, the harder their regular attacks will hit you. They can be found within the Asgarnia Ice Dungeon.

#### Ripper demons

- **The Raptor:** The last Ripper Demon I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- Ripper demons enjoy the bloodlust of battle, a sentiment I can agree with. They will expose your weaknesses, hitting you harder the worse your injuries are. They can be found in a fissure east of Al Kharid.

#### Risen ghosts

- These tortured spirits have been risen from the dead by Zemouregal and are bound to his will. You can find risen ghosts inhabiting the depths within the crypts north of Fort Forinthry, waiting for Zemouregal's command. You'll find bows effective against this target.

#### Skeletons

- **The Raptor:** The last Bound skeleton I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- You can find many types of skeletons around Gielinor, notably those flooding the crypts north of Fort Forinthry.

#### Undead

- **The Raptor:** The last Bound skeleton I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- You can find many types of undead around Gielinor, notably those festering inside the crypts north of Fort Forinthry.

#### Zemouregal's undead

- **The Raptor:** The last Bound skeleton I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- Undead raised by the necromancer Zemouregal. They lurk in the crypt north of Fort Forinthry.

#### Zombies

- **The Raptor:** The last Fetid zombie I killed I found air spells took them down effectively.
- You can find many types of zombies around Gielinor, notably those flooding the crypts north of Fort Forinthry.
