## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### On a league world

- **The Sage:** Hello, mortal. What do you seek?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me what is going on?
    - (Condition: During Catalyst League:)
      - **The Sage:** Some time ago, an ancient and powerful artefact was destroyed. The Stone of Jas.
      - **The Sage:** Though formless, its energies and magic endured, scattered and unseen.
      - **The Sage:** Over time, that power coalesced once more into something new. Relics.
      - **The Sage:** These Relics hold mere fragments of the Stone's former power.
      - **The Sage:** Yet even a fragment grants powers unlike anything you've encountered before.
      - **The Sage:** Is there anything else you need from me?
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: During Equilibrium League:)
      - **The Sage:** You have arrived in a world thrown out of balance.
      - **The Sage:** The life force of Gielinor, its anima mundi, is being torn apart by the battling forces of order and chaos.
      - **The Sage:** Guthix has dreamt of the terrible consequences of this conflict. His decree is this: If we wish to avoid disaster, heroes like yourself must work to heal the anima mundi, and restore the balance.
      - **The Sage:** As it is restored from its fragmented state, the anima mundi will transmute into Relics. A concentrated source of formidable power. Power that will be gifted to you.
      - **The Sage:** How you seek to use that power, for order or chaos, for good or ill, will be up to you.
      - **The Sage:** Is there anything else you need from me?
      - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me about tasks?
    - **The Sage:** In every League, there are a number of tasks for you to complete. Each task has a level of difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard, Elite and Master.
    - **The Sage:** Completing tasks earns you League Points. The number of points earned is independent on the difficulty of the task.
    - **Player:** What do these points do?
    - **The Sage:** Once you have achieved a certain amount of League Points you can unlock a new Relic of mine. These are powerful artefacts to help you in this world.
    - **The Sage:** League Points can also be used to buy rewards from the Leagues Reward Shop once the League ends.
    - (Condition: During Catalyst League:)
      - [Currently you are playing on your Leagues profile. The Leagues Reward Shop can only be opened on your main profile once the League ends.]
      - [You can access your main profile at any time by changing to a regular world.]
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: During Equilibrium League:)
      - [Currently you are playing on your Leagues profile. The Leagues Reward Shop can only be previewed during Leagues, and can be opened on your main profile once the League ends.]
      - [You can access your main profile at any time by changing to a regular world.]
      - (continue)
    - **Player:** So how many tasks are there?
    - **The Sage:** More than you'll be able to complete, most likely. It's up to you which ones you attempt.
    - **Player:** Thanks!
    - **The Sage:** Now, would you like to take a look at the tasks for this League?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please.
        - ((The Tasks interface opens.))
      - [Choice]: Not right now.
        - **The Sage:** Is there anything else you need from me?
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: cond=During Catalyst League:|Can you tell me about relics?
    - **The Sage:** Relics are powerful artefacts that grant unique abilities for the entire League.
    - **The Sage:** You may even recognise a few from an older RuneScape.
    - **The Sage:** Relics are separated into tiers, where you can claim only one from each tier.
    - **The Sage:** The first Relic tier is available to everyone. The others must be unlocked with League Points.
    - **The Sage:** League Points are earned by completing tasks.
    - **The Sage:** Once you have selected a Relic, you choice can't be changed for the rest of the League. Make your choices carefully.
    - **The Sage:** Now, would you like to take a look at the relics for this League?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please.
        - ((The Relics interface opens.))
      - [Choice]: Not right now.
        - **The Sage:** Is there anything else you need from me?
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: cond=During Equilibrium League:|Can you tell me about relics and blessings?
    - **The Sage:** Relics are powerful artefacts that grant unique abilities for the entire League. You may even recognise a few from an older RuneScape.
    - **The Sage:** Relics are separated into tiers, where you may claim only one from each tier.
    - **The Sage:** The first Relic tier is available to everyone. The others must be unlocked with League Points, which are earned by completing tasks.
    - **The Sage:** Blessings are powers and abilities unlocked by completing special combat tasks.
    - **The Sage:** Each blessing is aligned with Order, Balance or Chaos. As you unlock higher tier Blessings, the gods will cast their judgement on your choices, and new God Relics will be revealed to you.
    - **The Sage:** Once you have selected a Relic, your choice can't be changed for the rest of the League. Make your choices carefully.
    - **The Sage:** You will have limited  to reset your choice of Blessings, should you wish to change your mind.
    - **The Sage:** Now, would you like to take a look at the relics and blessings for this League?
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: Show me the Relics.
        - ((The Relics interface opens.))
      - [Choice]: Show me the Blessings.
        - ((The Blessings interface opens.))
      - [Choice]: Not right now.
        - **The Sage:** Is there anything else you need from me?
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do I have any items to reclaim?
    - (below|Item reclaim)
  - [Choice]: That's all for now.
    - **The Sage:** Very well. I'll be here if you ever need to know anything more.
