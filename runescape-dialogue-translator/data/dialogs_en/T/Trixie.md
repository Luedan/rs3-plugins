- *(If talking for the first time:)*
  - **Trixie:** Shh! Not so loud. People might hear us talking.
  - **Player:** Are you worried about that?
  - **Trixie:** Aren't you? Who knows who might be listening in. There are spies everywhere!
  - **Player:** Spies? Whose spies?
  - **Trixie:** Does it matter?
  - **Player:** I... don't know?
  - **Trixie:** See! It's about keeping your business you business, and that is definitely how I wish to keep mine. Private.

      - **Trixie:** There's a buyer for everything. One man's trash is another man's treasure.
