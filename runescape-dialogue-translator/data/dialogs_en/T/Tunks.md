## Dialogue Transcript


### At desert goebie camp after Call of the Ancestors

- **Tunks:** Hey, Player!
- **Player:** Hi Tunks, what are you doing here?
- **Tunks:** I'm on an adventure! I heard about these goebies boldly going where no goebies have gone before and knew I had to join them.
- *Tunks and the player say one of the following at random:*
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Player:** What's that on your head?
    - **Tunks:** Oh this? It's my new headdress. I met a little man called Gudrik who told me new adventurers all go to Ashdale, so that was the first place I went!
    - **Tunks:** The island was in the clutches of Morwenna the Cruel, but she was no match for the power of out ancestors.
    - **Player:** Wow! So what are you planning to do next?
    - **Tunks:** Well, I heard there are adventures to be had in a place called Silvarea to the north. That's where I'll head.
    - **Player:** Good luck on your travels!
    - **Tunks:** Thank you, Player!
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Tunks:** I met a short guy who tried to explain to me something about cogs I think?
    - **Tunks:** I didn't really understand what he was saying to be honest.
    - **Tunks:** But I did meet my new friend here!
    - **Player:** I can't believe how unfair that is...
