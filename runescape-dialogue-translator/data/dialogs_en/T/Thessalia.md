#### Change-clothes option{{anchor|change}}

- (Condition: If the player has weapons or armour equipped:)
  - **Thessalia:** You can't try them on while wearing armour. Take it off and speak to me again.
- (Condition: If the player has overrides equipped:)
  - You must deactivate any outfit overrides you have active before you can change your clothes.
- (Condition: Otherwise:)
  - ''(Clothing Selection inferface opens.)
