## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to

- *(If the Zoltan pet is following you:)*
  - I remember when I used to be like your pet; happy and carefree.
  - Actually, wait, no I don't.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **Player:** What does this machine do?
- Do not address The Mighty Zoltan as a machine!
- **Player:** What should I call you then?
- The Mighty Zoltan works well, I find.
- **Player:** Ok, Mighty Zoltan, what do you do?
- I can tell your fortune!
- *(One of the following occurs, then continues below:)*
  - **Player:** What, so I can find out if I'm destined to be happy?
  - **Player:** What, so I can find out if I'm destined to be powerful?
  - **Player:** What, so I can find out if I'm destined to be rich?
  - **Player:** What, so I can find out if I'm destined to be the ruler of all?
  - **Player:** What, so I can find out if I'm destined to find love?
- Not really.
- **Player:** But that's a fortune, right?
- Well, yes, I did used to give out fortunes like that but something happened.
- **Player:** Oh?
- They all come out quite sad now. I'm not sure what caused it. The longer I am here, the worse they seem to get.
- But the people who get the fortunes seem to be happy with the Divination reward that goes with it, so they don't seem to mind too much.
- **Player:** Doesn't it bring you down, though?
- It's hard to get any more down than I am.
- Would you like a mis-fortune?
- Would you like a mis-fortune?
  - Yes.
    - *(If confirmation prompt isn't toggled off (and in 2017 enough tokens are owned):)*
      - This activity gives Divination XP and costs ten tokens. Are you sure you want to continue?
        - Get a mis-fortune?
          - Yes.
            - *(Continues below.)*
          - No.
            - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
          - Yes, and don't ask again.
            - *(Continues below.)*
    - *(If the confirmation prompt is toggled off (and in 2017 enough tokens are owned):)*
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - *(Only in 2017:)*
      - *(If not enough tokens are owned:)*
        - You do not have enough fayre tokens to play on this machine. You need 10 tokens.
        - You can get more fayre tokens and tickets from the stall by the Bucking Baroo.
  - No.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
- The machine whirrs...
- The machine wobbles a bit...
- A strange strangled noise comes from the machine...
- More whirring sounds come from the machine.
- A single, lonely, horn blares in fanfare.
- A fortune is spat out of the machine.

### Getting a fortune

- *(If confirmation prompt hasn't been toggled off (and in 2017 enough tokens are owned):)*
  - This activity gives Divination XP and costs ten tokens. Are you sure you want to continue?
    - Yes.
      - *(Continues below.)*
    - No.
      - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
    - Yes, and don't ask again.
      - *(Continues below.)*
- *(Only in 2017:)*
  - *(If not enough tokens are owned:)*
    - You do not have enough fayre tokens to play on this machine. You need 10 tokens.
    - You can get more fayre tokens and tickets from the stall by the Bucking Baroo.
- The machine whirrs...
- The machine wobbles a bit...
- A strange strangled noise comes from the machine...
- More whirring sounds come from the machine.
- A single, lonely, horn blares in fanfare.
- A fortune is spat out of the machine.

### Walking away during operation

- Your inattentiveness to The Mighty Zoltan has disappointed him further.
