# Tenglong

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Tenglong](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATenglong)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without proper dragonhide amount

- **Tenglong:** Oh you're here about the Dragon Hides, let's see what you have.
- **Tenglong:** Sorry, you don't have all the things I need yet.
- **Tenglong:** Bring me 8 Green Dragonhides, 8 Blue Dragonhides and 8 Red Dragonhides.

### With proper dragonhide amount

- **Tenglong:** Oh you're here about the Dragon Hides, let's see what you have.
- **Tenglong:** Great! You brought me all the dragonhides!

### After bringing him dragonhides

- **Tenglong:** You have already completed this task.
