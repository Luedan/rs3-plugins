## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard

- **The Exile:** How may I serve you, naabe?/Good day, challem.
  - **Player:** How are things going?
    - *After Meet the Exile:*
      - **The Exile:** I have no more to tell you at this time, Naabe.
    - *After Emotional Baggage:*
      - **The Exile:** I am still considering your words to me, Portmaster. I am a worldbearer, but I am also not one. I must figure this out myself.
    - *After The Burden of Guilt:*
      - **The Exile:** Please, do not pressure me. I am not ready to share my burdens with you.
    - *After Unburdening the Soul:*
      - **The Exile:** The rest of my story is painful for me to think of. I will tell you it soon, I promise.
    - *After Blood from a Stone:*
      - **The Exile:** Every day is a struggle, and I am lost... but I am trying, Portmaster.
    - *After Weight on, Weight Off:*
      - **The Exile:** Good day, challem. I continue to practise the exercises the Gu masters taught me.
    - *After Lament of the Sea:*
      - **The Exile:** My mind goes cold when I ponder what foul intent Kakashi might have.
    - *After Gu: Masters of the Magic Mountain:*
      - **The Exile:** Worry not, challem. Things may seem dire, but you are wise. We will form a plan to stop Kakashi!
    - *After Guardians of the World:*
      - **The Exile:** Hello, challem. Fear not for Kami-shima - we shall protect it.
  - **Player:** About your voyages...
    - **The Exile:** I am ready to carry any burden you would hand me.
  - **Player:** Do you prefer to work alone?
    - *Without the Whaler unlocked:*
      - **The Exile: :** I'd work with someone who enjoys fishing. That was one of my responsbilities in the tribe.
      - Unlock the Whaler (90 Fishing required) to unlock additional joint voyages.
    - *With the Whaler unlocked and not present:*
      - **The Exile:** There is a man who is a whaler - I would work with him. Fishing is something I have an affinity with, and fondness of.
      - If both The Exile and Whaler are in port at the same time, additional joint voyages will be made available.
    - *With the Whaler unlocked and in the bar:*
      - **The Exile: :** I would work with your whaler friend. Back in Daemonheim, it was often my burden to fish and cook food for my tribe - heim crabs were my specialty.
      - *Voyage screen opens*
  - **Player:** How are your burdens?
    - **The Exile:** They hang heavy on my shoulders as always, but my shoulders are strong, as they should be, for I am a worldbearer.
