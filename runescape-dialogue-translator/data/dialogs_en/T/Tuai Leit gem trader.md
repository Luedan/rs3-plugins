# Tuai Leit gem trader

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Tuai Leit gem trader](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATuai%20Leit%20gem%20trader)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before purchasing the Tuai Leit sparkle


#### Without five bamboo

- **Tuai Leit gem trader:** You're looking for precious gems, what kind?
- **Player:** Tuai Leit sparkle
- **Tuai Leit gem trader:** You're in luck, I do have one of those in my collection. I'll trade it to you for 5 Bamboo

#### With five bamboo

- **Tuai Leit gem trader:** You're looking for precious gems, what kind?
- **Player:** Tuai Leit sparkle
- **Tuai Leit gem trader:** You're in luck, I do have one of those in my collection. I'll trade it to you for 5 Bamboo
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes
    - (Condition: Without any space in backpack:)
      - **Tuai Leit gem trader:** Sorry, you don't have enough space!
    - (Condition: With space in backpack:)
      - **Tuai Leit gem trader:** Wonderful! here you go, take care of it!
      - (removes= five bamboo) (receives=Tuai Leit sparkle)
      - **Tuai Leit gem trader:** It was a pleasure doing business with you.
  - [Choice]: No
    - **Player:** No, thanks.

### After purchasing the Tuai Leit sparkle

- **Tuai Leit gem trader:** It was a pleasure doing business with you.
