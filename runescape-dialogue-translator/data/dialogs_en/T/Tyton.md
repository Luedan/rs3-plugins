## Dialogue Transcript


### After Housing of Parliament

- **Tyton:** Hello, Player!
- **Player:** Hi Tyton.
- **Tyton:** Is there anything I can do for you?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Ask about the owls.
    - **Player:** What's the history between the owls and the City of Um?
    - **Tyton:** You'll have to be more specific than that.
    - **Player:** Well, did Death create you? How long have you been around?
    - **Tyton:** As far as I can remember, my siblings and I have been around since the before the inception of Um. Thus I would presume we were not creations of Harold.
    - **Player:** How were you created, then? What is your purpose?
    - **Tyton:** That is a much larger existential question that I am unable to answer.
    - **Tyton:** My personal theory is that we were appointed here upon the creation of life, to oversee the many events of the Underworld. But  knows.
    - **Player:** That's understandable. Do you ever get bored of the Underworld?
    - **Tyton:** Speaking for myself, no. The Underworld is vast, with a never ending flow of new life passing through.
    - **Player:** It's definitely not the worst place to be stuck in for eternity, I suppose.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Talk about Deryn.
    - **Player:** What do you think of Deryn?
    - **Tyton:** Hmm... Yes, Deryn is a rather kind soul to spend her time in Um looking after my siblings and I.
    - **Tyton:** Before her, we were left to fend for ourselves. Which wasn't an awful way of living by any means - there's food and water aplenty in the Underworld.
    - **Tyton:** I do enjoy feeling pampered, though - gourmet food with a fresh bath, right on my doorstep.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Nevermind.
    - **Player:** Nevermind.
    - **Tyton:** Farewell.
