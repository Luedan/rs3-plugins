        - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** Then you TzHaar-Mej!

          - [Choice]: What are you then?
            - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** I am TzHaar-Hur, one of the crafters for this city. We craft tools and sculptures, and carve homes for the TzHaar.
            - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** There are also the wise TzHaar-Mej who guide us, the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, and the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt for our food.
          - [Choice]: Thanks for explaining it.
            - (end)
      - [Choice]: Yes, I certainly am.
        - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** Then it is an honour to speak with you, TzHaar-Mej-Player. <br>You great and powerful TzHaar, and defender of our city.
      - [Choice]: You must have me confused with another TzHaar.
        - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** I heard you are modest, TzHaar-Mej-Player.
        - **Tzhaar-Hur-Zuh:** No need. Revel in our praise. You deserve all honour.
