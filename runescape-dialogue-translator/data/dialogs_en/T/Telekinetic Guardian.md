# Telekinetic Guardian

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Telekinetic Guardian](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATelekinetic%20Guardian)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hi.
- **Telekinetic Guardian:** Greetings young one. What is it you wish to know?
- Select an option
  - What do I have to do in this room?
    - **Player:** What do I have to do in this room?
    - **Telekinetic Guardian:** In this room you will see a maze within which one of my fellow Guardians has been turned to stone for the purposes of this exercise. You must move the statue using your telekinetic grab spell to the exit square at the edge of the maze to bring the Guardian back to life. Simply stand on the side that you wish for the statue to travel towards and cast the spell on the statue. Once you have solved the maze, the statue will change back into the Guardian and he will award you with Telekinetic Pizazz Points and teleport you to the next maze. You can switch to a better view of the maze by selecting the 'Observe' option on the statue and return your view to normal by selecting the same option again. There is also a 'Reset' option on the statue just in case things aren't going too well.
    - (previous)
  - What are the rewards?
    - **Player:** What are the rewards?
    - **Telekinetic Guardian:** As well as the experience in casting magic, you will get Telekinetic Pizazz Points for each maze successfully solved and bonus points for completing five mazes in a row without returning to the entrance.
    - (previous)
  - Got any tips that may help me?
    - **Player:** Got any tips that may help me?
    - **Telekinetic Guardian:** Have a good look at the maze before you try to solve it because this can save you time and runes required to navigate the maze. Although you will still be getting magic experience for moving the statue incorrectly, you won't be progressing towards collecting Telekinetic Pizazz Points. Lastly, all the mazes can be solved in ten moves or less.
    - (previous)
  - Thanks, bye!
    - **Player:**Thanks, bye!
    - **Telekinetic Guardian:** Use what you've learned, young one.
