## Dialogue Transcript


### After [[The Fremennik Isles]]


#### Talking to Thakkrad Sigmundson

- **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** Thank you for leading the Burgher's militia against the Troll King. Now that the trolls are leaderless I have repaired the bridge to the central isle for you as best I can.
- **Player:** Thanks Thakkrad. Does that mean I have access to the drakolith ores on that island?
- **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** Yes, you should be able to mine drakolith there if you wish.

#### Asking Thakkrad Sigmundson to craft goods

- What can I help you with?
  - Cure my yak-hide, please.
    - **Player:** Cure my yak-hide, please.
    - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** I will cure yak-hide for a fee of 5 gp per hide.
      - *If you do not have sufficient money with you*
        - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** You don't have enough gold to pay me!
      - *If you have sufficient money with you*
        - *If you do not have any yak-hides to be cured with you*
          - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** You have no yak-hide to cure.
        - *If you have any yak-hides to be cured with you*
          - Cure all my hides.
            - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** There you go.
          - Cure one hide.
            - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** There you go.
          - Cure no hide.
            - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** Bye.
          - Can you cure any other types of leather?
            - **Player:** Can you cure any other types of leather?
            - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** Other types of leather? Why would you need any other type of leather?
            - **Player:** I'll take that as a no then.
  - Nothing, thanks.
    - **Player:** Nothing, thanks.
    - **Thakkrad Sigmundson:** See you later. You won't find anyone else who can cure yak-hide.
