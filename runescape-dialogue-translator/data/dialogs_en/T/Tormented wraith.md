# Tormented wraith

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Tormented wraith](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATormented%20wraith)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Random dialogue without ghostspeak

- Woo wooo woo.

### Random dialogue with ghostspeak


#### Outside combat

- ((One of the following is occasionally seen:))
  - Bring me the dark warriors or suffer my displeasure.
  - I can feel them closing in.
  - I must have my vengeance.
  - I will not rest until every black knight is dead.
  - The stench of Kinshra still lingers in my wounds.

#### In combat

- ((One of the following is occasionally seen:))
  - ((Only with Protect from Melee or Deflect Melee active:))
    - Accept your punishment, weakling!
  - Bid farewell to your breath, black knight!
  - ((Only with Protect from Melee or Deflect Melee active:))
    - Coward! Hiding behind your false gods!
  - Prepare to suffer as I have!
  - Spill your life for me!
  - Suffer the pain of my torment, wretch!
  - You shall die for what you did to me!
  - ((Only with Protect from Melee or Deflect Melee active:))
    - Your dark prayers will not keep you safe forever!
  - ((Only with Protect from Melee or Deflect Melee active:))
    - Zamorak cannot save you from my wrath!

### Attempting to attack without wearing required equipment

- You find yourself unable to attack the wraith.

### Attempting to remove a piece of equipment in combat

- You can't change equipment in the presence of the angry wraith.

### Attempting to wear a piece of equipment in combat

- You can't get yourself to change equipment in the presence of the angry wraith.
