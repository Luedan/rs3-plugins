## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Love Story


#### Before speaking to the other bank guard

- **Player:** Hello?
- **Guard in tree:** Ssshhh! What do you want?
- **Player:** Well, it's not every day you see a man up a tree.
- **Guard in tree:** I'm trying to observe a suspect. Leave me alone!
- **Player:** What's this all about?
- **Guard in tree:** Haven't you heard? you'd better go and talk to the other guard, the one by the bank door. He'll tell you all about it.
- **Guard in tree:** Go on, ask him what's wrong with the bank wall. He loves when people ask him that.
- **Player:** Okay, if you say so.

#### After speaking to the other bank guard


##### Without stew in backpack

- **Player:** Hello?
- **Guard in tree:** Ssshhh! What do you want?
- **Player:** Well, it's not every day you see a man up a tree.
- **Guard in tree:** I'm trying to observe a suspect. Leave me alone!
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: This is about the bank robbery, right?
    - **Player:** This is about the bank robbery, right?
    - **Guard in tree:** Yes, that's right. We're keeping the suspect under tight observation for the moment.
    - **Player:** Can't you just... I dunno.... arrest him?
    - **Guard in tree:** I'm not meant to discuss the case. You know what confidentiality rules are like.
    - **Player:** Fair enough.
  - [Choice]: You're not being very subtle up there.
    - **Player:** You're not being very subtle up there.
    - **Guard in tree:** I'd be doing a lot better if nits like you didn't come crowding around me all day.
    - **Player:** But your legs are hanging down!
    - **Guard in tree:** Go away!
    - **Player:** Please yourself!
  - [Choice]: Can I do anything to help?
    - **Player:** Can I do anything to help?
    - **Guard in tree:** That's very kind of you. I'd rather like a nice bowl of stew, if you could fetch me one. I don't get many meal breaks.
    - **Player:** You want a bowl of stew?
    - **Guard in tree:** If you wouldn't mind...
    - **Player:** I'll think about it!

##### With stew in backpack

- **Player:** Hello?
- **Guard in tree:** Yes, what do you do... I say, is that stew for me?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Okay, take the stew.
    - **Player:** Okay, take the stew.
    - **Guard in tree:** Gosh, that's very kind of you! Here, have a few coins for your trouble.
    - (removes=stew) (receives=30 coins)
  - [Choice]: No, I want to keep this stew.
    - **Player:** No, I want to keep this stew!
    - **Guard in tree:** Fair enough. But if you change your mind, I'll probably still be here.

##### After giving him stew

- **Player:** I think I should leave him in peace to finish his stew!

##### Afterwards

- The guard drops the empty bowl out of the tree!
