## Dialogue Transcript


### At any Agility course


#### Calling out to you

*If it's the first time the scout called you:*
- **Talent scout:** Hey, Player! Come over here!
- Looks like the nearby scout is trying to catch your attention.
*If it's the second time the scout called you:*
- **Talent scout:** Hey, Player! I have a real opportunity for you!
- The nearby scout looks eager to talk to you.
*If ignored after the second time the scout called you:*
- **Talent scout:** Oh well, can't say I didn't try.
- Seems like the nearby scout has given up for now.

#### Talking to the Talent scout


##### If not called

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Talent scout:** Huh? Oh, hello. Can't you see I'm busy?
- **Player: **You look like you're just standing around. Why do you keep watching people like that?
- **Talent scout:** None of your beeswax! Run a few laps and maybe I'll let you in on it. Now, get lost!

##### Upon being called

- **Talent scout:** Hey, Player. Glad you could come to chat.
  - *If you have a follower out:*
    - **Talent scout:** First, you'll have to dismiss your follower. This is between you and me, chum.
    - You should dismiss your follower and talk to the scout again before he loses interest.
  - *If your hands are not free: *
    - **Talent scout:** You'll need to free up your hands before we talk, pal. It'll make things...easier.
    - You need to empty your hands and talk to the scout again before he loses interest.
  - *If you don't have a follower out and your hands are free: *
    - **Talent scout:** I'm here to offer you the chance of a lifetime. I've seen you running around and I think you've got what it takes.
    - **Player:** Wow, really? That's great. What do I have to do?
    - **Talent scout:** Well...
    - {{Qact|Talent scout points away and you turn to look in the direction he points; he knocks you out with a fist punch, and you are taken to the Pit.}}

##### Using a daily D&D token

- **Talent scout:** Hey, Player. Glad you could come to chat.
- **Talent scout:** I see you've recently won an all expenses paid trip!
- **Player:** Have I?... yes, yes I have! Erm, where to?
- **Talent scout:** Well...
- {{Qact|Talent scout points away and you turn to look in the direction he points; he knocks you out with a fist punch, and you are taken to the Pit.}}

#### Upon receiving your experience reward

- {{Qact|Talent scout appears.}}
- **Talent scout:** Surprise!
- **Player:** Oh no!
- {{Qact|Talent scout points away and you turn to look in the direction he points.}}
  - *Possibility 1:*
    - **Talent scout:** Wow, a double rainbow!
    - **Player:** Indoors?
  - *Possibility 2:*
    - **Talent scout:** What's that over there?
    - **Player:** Huh?
- {{Qact|Talent scout knocks you out with a fist punch, and you are taken back to an agility course.}}
- You are awarded X Agility experience for your participation in The Pit.
- **Player:** Ow, my head. What just happened?
- **Talent scout:** Heh heh heh.
  - *If a Nimble outfit piece was awarded:*
    - As you recover you notice a piece of the Nimble outfit has been placed in your inventory.
