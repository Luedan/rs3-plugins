## Dialogue Transcript


### Arriving

- A treasure turtle has appeared at the hub!

### Misty's messages

- (One of the following is seen occasionally, and is repeated in the dialogue box in format *Misty: {{Colour*:}}
  - **Misty:** {{RSChat|Check him out, don't miss your chance!}}
  - **Misty:** {{RSChat|I bet that chest is full of treasure.}}
  - **Misty:** {{RSChat|Lovely, lovely treasure...}}
  - **Misty:** {{RSChat|That's one pretty turtle!}}
  - **Misty:** {{RSChat|These treasure turtles are awfully rare I'll have you know.}}

### Check


#### If the player was on the same world and at the hub on arrival

- The treasure turtle is happy to share its treasure!
- ((The reward interface opens.))

#### If the player was on another world or outside the hub on arrival

- The treasure turtle isn't interested in sharing their treasure with you.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The treasure turtle isn't interested in sharing their treasure with you.
  - (continue)
- You must be in the area when the event starts to be eligible for the full rewards.

### Departing

- The treasure turtle has departed...
