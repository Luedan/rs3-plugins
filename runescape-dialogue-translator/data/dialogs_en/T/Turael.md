### Assignment advice


#### Birds

**Turael:** Birds aren't the most intelligent of creatures, but watch out for their sharp, stabbing beaks. Even the chickens in Lumbridge are after your blood, so get them before they get you.

#### Cows

**Turael:** Cows are bigger than you, and they'll often hit hard but react fairly slowly. You can slaughter the ones in Lumbridge - I bet they are up to no good.

#### Crawling hands

**Turael:** Crawling hands are undead severed hands; fast and dexterous, they claw their victims. They can be found in the Slayer Tower, north-east of the entrance to Morytania.

#### Gelatinous abominations

**Turael:** These mindless creatures attack relentlessly once provoked. There is a core floating in their chest, and they can only be beaten by bursting this core wearing spiked gauntlets, once you have damaged them enough.

#### Trolls

**Turael:** There are plenty of trolls not far from here. Head up to the cave between the Wall and Burthorpe castle if you want to find them. There are three sub-caves in there, one with shamans, another with warriors and another with rangers. Take your pick of how to take them down!

#### Wolves

**Turael:** Wolves are pack animals, so you'll always find them in groups. Watch out for their bite, it can be nasty. There are plenty to kill on White Wolf Mountain.
