### On RuneFest Treasure Island

- *(Appears randomly at the start:)*
  - **The Kendal:** That guy is so creepy...
  - **Player:** You mean Pete?
  - **The Kendal:** He hasn't stopped dancing since I got here, day or night. It's enough to ruin my appetite.
  - **The Kendal:** Anyway...
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **The Kendal:** I wonder, can you guess the name of this bear?
- *(Guess the Name interface opens, and once a name is selected, continues below.)*
- **Player:** Hmmm, is it... [Name]?
- *(If a wrong name was selected:)*
  - **The Kendal:** [Name]? Don't be ridiculous!
- *(If the correct name was selected:)*
  - *(On first time:)*
    - **The Kendal:** Yes! Well done.
    - colour=#00ff00{{Colour|#ffffff|.}}}}
  - *(On subsequent times:)*
    - **The Kendal:** Amazing, you've guessed it again!
