## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Burthorpe mine

- **Tobias Bronzearms:** Burthorpe needs weapons, and we miners provide the ore used to make them. You want to help out?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me about Mining.
    - **Tobias Bronzearms:** Mining is the source of all the metal in the world. Without mining, there'd be no tools, no weapons, no industry at all!
    - **Tobias Bronzearms:** Get your pick and swing it at some rocks! The more you swing, the better you'll get.
    - **Tobias Bronzearms:** The better you are at mining, the better the ore you can mine. Better ores are much more useful and much more valuable.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about mining.
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the glowing rocks.
        - **Tobias Bronzearms:** When mining, a nearby mining rock will occasionally glow. Mine that rock for a swing of extra damage, and therefore faster progress.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about geodes.
        - **Tobias Bronzearms:** Occasionally when mining, you'll find an untradeable geode: sedimentary, igneous, or metamorphic. Crack open the geode to find a reward inside.
        - You can find sedimentary geodes from free-to-play core rocks: copper and tin up to runite.
        - You can get igneous geodes from members' core rocks.
        - They also have a rare chance to yield a valuable metamorphic geode.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about ore boxes.
        - You can smith a bronze ore box. It stores about 100 tin and copper ore, and the backpack only has 28 spaces. So it's a good idea to bring an ore box whenever you're mining.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about pickaxes.
        - You can swap the pickaxe on your tool belt with a superior pickaxe. You should use the best pickaxe your Mining level allows. You can smith (and even upgrade, with a high enough Smithing level) or buy higher-quality pickaxes.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: (back)
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the skills menu.
    - {{Qact|Skills infobox opens.}}
    - You have many skills you can train. The more you practice a skill, the better it gets. Higher skill levels unlock new activities: equipment to craft, locations to explore, quests to complete, and much more.
    - Perform actions associated with a skill to earn experience points (XP) in that skill. Earn enough XP and you'll gain a level in that skill.
    - Most skills start at level 1, and you unlock all their benefits by leveling the skill to 99. (Some skills have unlocks up to level 120.)
    - The bottom number next to a skill icon is your base level in that skill (un-modified by buffs or debuffs). It is used for most requirement checks.
    - The top number is your modified skill level (temporarily boosted or drained by skill buffs and debuffs - such as potions or spells). It is used for most skill checks.
    - Achieve 99 in all your skills to earn the coveted Max Cape!
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
