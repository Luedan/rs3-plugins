# Taxidermist

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Taxidermist](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ATaxidermist)
- **Letter**: `T`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to

- **Taxidermist:** Oh, hello. Have you got something you want preserving?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - **Taxidermist:** Give it to me to look at then.
  - [Choice]: Not right now
    - **Player:** Not right now.
    - **Taxidermist:** Well, you go kill things so I can stuff them, eh?
  - [Choice]: What?
    - **Player:** What?
    - **Taxidermist:** If you bring me a monster head or a very big fish, I can preserve it for you so you can mount it in your house.
    - **Taxidermist:** I hear there are all sorts of exotic creatures in the Slayer Tower -- I'd like a chance to stuff one of them!

### Using eligible remains on her


#### Big bass

- **Taxidermist:** That's a mighty fine sea bass you've caught there.
- **Taxidermist:** I can preserve that for you for 1000 coins.
- {{anchor|preserve}}[Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
      - You can not afford that.
      - **Player:** But I don't have enough money on me.
      - **Taxidermist:** Don't waste my time, then!
    - (Condition: If the player has enough money:)
      - (removes=coins and remains) (receives=stuffed remains)
      - **Taxidermist:** There you go!
  - [Choice]: No thanks
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Taxidermist:** All right, come back if you change your mind, eh?

#### Big swordfish

- **Taxidermist:** Don't point that thing at me!
- **Taxidermist:** I can preserve that for you for 2500 coins.
- (continuea|preserve)

- **Taxidermist:** I can preserve that for you for 5000 coins.
- (continuea|preserve)

#### Basilisk head

#### King black dragon head

#### General Graardor's head or Kree'arra's head

### Using ineligible items on her


#### Raw fish

- **Taxidermist:** That's a pretty ordinary fish, isn't it? Not really worth preserving.

#### Raw chicken

- **Taxidermist:** Killing a chicken is hardly worth boasting about!

#### Any other item

- **Taxidermist:** Don't be silly, I can't preserve that!
