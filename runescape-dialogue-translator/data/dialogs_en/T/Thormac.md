## Dialogue Transcript


### After Scorpion Catcher

- **Thormac:** Thank you for rescuing my scorpions.
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - That's okay.
    - **Player:** That's okay.
  - You said you'd enchant my battlestaff for me.
    - **Player:** You said you'd enchant my battlestaff for me.
    - ((If you have't yet completed the Seers' Village Hard Tasks:))
      - **Thormac:** Yes, although it'll cost you 40,000 coins for the materials. What kind of staff did you want enchanting?
      - (continue)
    - ((If you have completed the Seers' Village Hard Tasks:))
      - **Thormac:** Yes. Usually, it would cost you 40,000 coins, but, since you made the effort to get to know the Seers' area, it will be 27,000 coins. What kind of staff did you want enchanting?
      - (continue)
    - ((Thormac's battlestaff interface opens.))
    - ((After making a selection:))
      - ((If you don't have the selected battlestaff with you:))
        - You don't have the correct battlestaff.
      - ((If you have the selected battlestaff with you:))
        - ((If you don't have sufficient money with you:))
          - You need [27,000/40,000] coins to pay Thormac.
        - ((If you have sufficient money with you:))
          - ((If money to be paid is in your money pouch:))
            - [27,000/40,000] coins have been removed from your money pouch.
            - (continue)
          - (removes=coins and the battlestaff) (receives=corresponding mystic battlestaff)
          - Thormac enchants your battlestaves.
  - ((Only if you have an Armadyl battlestaff with you:))
    - Could you enchant my Armadyl battlestaff?
      - **Player:** Could you enchant my Armadyl battlestaff?
      - **Thormac:** I don't know where you got this, but it already has plenty of mystic power flowing through it. I don't think I can make it any more mystical.
