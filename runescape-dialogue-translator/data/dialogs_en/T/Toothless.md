## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to with crocspeak


#### Before completing Tooth Fairy

- **Toothless:** *gaarrrble* *raaaarb*
- **Player:** ...Hello?
- **Toothless:** *GAAAARRRBBLE* *raaarb*
- You tap your crocspeak amulet.
- **Player:** Is this thing on?
- **Toothless:** *RRRRAAABBLE* *GAARRRRBB*
- **Player:** ...

#### After completing Tooth Fairy

- **Toothless:** Thanks again for returning my tooth!

#### Pointy tooth


##### Before completing Tooth Fairy

- The crocodile clicks their tooth back into place.
- **Toothless:** FINALLY! I can talk again.
- **Player:** But wasn't it just one of your many, many teeth?
- **Toothless:** Yes, but it was my front tooth! The most important of them all. Thank you, anyway, I'll make sure to take better care of them in the future.
- **Player:** No problem.
- (removes=pointy tooth)
- Congratulations! You have completed: 'Tooth Fairy' - Help find Toothless's tooth.

##### After completing Tooth Fairy

- **Toothless:** Thanks! I'll add it to my collection. If I get any more, I might start making tooth jewellery.
- (removes=pointy tooth)
- **Player:** Maybe I won't give you any more then.
