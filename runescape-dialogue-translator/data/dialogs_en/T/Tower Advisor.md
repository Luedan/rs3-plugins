## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there, what do you do here?
- **Tower Advisor:** Hi. We are in charge of this practice area.
- **Player:** This is a practice area?
- **Tower Advisor:** Surrounding us are four towers. Each tower contains trained archers of a different level. You'll notice it's quite a distance, so you'll need a longbow.
- (Condition: If the player's Ranged level is 50 or lower:)
  - **Tower Advisor:** As you're not very skilled, so I advise you to practise on the north tower. That'll provide the best challenge for you.
- (Condition: If the player's Ranged level is between 51 and 60:)
  - **Tower Advisor:** You seem to have some skill, so I advise you to practice on the east tower. That'll provide the best challenge for you.
- (Condition: If the player's Ranged level is between 61 and 70:)
  - **Tower Advisor:** You appear to be fairly skilled with a bow, so I advise you to practice on the south tower. That'll provide the best challenge for you.
- (Condition: If the player's Ranged level is 71 or higher:)
  - **Tower Advisor:** Looks like you're very skilled, so I advise you to practice on the west tower. That'll provide the best challenge for you.
