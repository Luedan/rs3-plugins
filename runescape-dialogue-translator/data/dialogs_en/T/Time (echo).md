## Dialogue Transcript

- **Guthix:** It cannot be true - this is not how it will end. I am a god; I am not subject to the mortal cycle like those I loved.
- **Guthix:** What [he/she] told me cannot be true. How are they even who they claim to be? This is not something that should be!
- **Guthix:** How can they come here and tell me things I cannot know? It must be a lie! And where did they get this key, that unlocks such a journey?
- **Guthix:** It cannot be the truth of this world! I made this place what it is - my time cannot be done!
- **Guthix:** Why me? Why now? I have given so much of myself to this place and this is how I am repaid?
- **Guthix:** This is the sacrifice that is asked of me? How can this happen to me; why would this be my fate?
- **Guthix:** I who have slept rather than bring suffering to people, who has not demanded worship, but avoided furthering such practices...and now my time is to come and I will be no more?
- **Guthix:** This cannot be right. This cannot be just. Where is the balance in this? Where is my own freedom? Why should I pay this cost?
- **Guthix:** What if I made a different offer to them? Is there a negotiation to this, a path to a truer harmony?
- **Guthix:** Perhaps there is a different path from the one I have tried to follow, one which leaves me with a different fate?
- **Guthix:** What if I were to find this person again, before my time has come? What would it take for them to change their part in what is going to happen?
- **Guthix:** No more of this eternal life? A death to come, unsought when it comes.
- **Guthix:** Why could it not have come so long ago, before all of this, before the eons that have passed since last I saw her, my beautiful Aagi? How can it be now it happens?
- **Guthix:** And, oh my daughter, how I have missed you! An empty heart for you still - I had almost forgotten in my rage how long I have lost you, how much I wish I was with you.
- **Guthix:** Your smile! I have no hope now to ever see it again - I will be as gone as you both are. I am lost.
- **Guthix:** So it is to come, I can see that now. They have the Amserdrwys, and so it must be true; [he/she] will be my friend.
- **Guthix:** It is not a fight I can win or one I wish to. Instead, I must accept what is to come.
- **Guthix:** The time has come to prepare for my own end...but I cannot let this world be exposed to the horrors others would unleash upon it. The Guardians must be in place.
- **Guthix:** [He/She] who told me of what is to come will be the one who completes them - they will serve me as I have served their people.
- **Guthix:** And they, in turn, will come to see me again and tell me of what will pass.
- **Guthix:** They will always remember their purpose, as I will always remember mine.
