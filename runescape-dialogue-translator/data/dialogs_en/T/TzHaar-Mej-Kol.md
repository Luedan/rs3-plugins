      - What did you call me earlier?

      - That was it.
        - **Player:** That was it.
          - *(End of dialogue)*
  - What did you call me?
    - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** Are you not [Title]-[Caste]?
    - Select an Option
      - Why do you call me '[Title]-[Caste]'?

          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** That what you are...you tough and strong, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Xil caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** That what you are...you agile and quick, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Mej caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** That what you are...you user of mystic powers, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Hur caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** That what you are...you skilled at making things, no?

              - **TzHaar-Mej-Kol:** I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics for the city. The TzHaar-Mej guide the TzHaar when change is necessary, and tend to TzHaar eggs to ensure they are hot and healthy. TzHaar-Mej are keeps of knowledge and magic.
