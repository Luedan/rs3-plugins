## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue, after TokTz-Ket-Dill

- **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** You need help, [Title]-[Caste]-Player?
- Select an Option
  - What did you call me?
    - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Are you not [Title]-[Caste]?
    - Select an Option
      - Why do you call me '[Title]-[Caste]'?
        - *(If the player is of the Ket caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** That what you are...you tough and strong, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Xil caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** That what you are...you agile and quick, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Mej caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** That what you are...you user of mystic powers, no?
        - *(If the player is of the Hur caste:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** That what you are...you skilled at making things, no?
        - *(If the player has completed The Brink of Extintion:)*
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** And you are JalYt no longer. You are TzHaar now.
        - **Player:** Well, yes, I suppose I am...
        - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Then you [Title]-[Caste]!
        - Select an Option
          - What are you then?
            - *(If the player has not completed The Elder Kiln:)*
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Foolish JalYt. I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics of this city.
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
            - *(Otherwise:)*
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** I am TzHaar-Mej, one of the mystics for the city. The TzHaar-Mej guide the TzHaar when change is necessary, and tend to TzHaar eggs to ensure they are hot and healthy. TzHaar-Mej are keeps of knowledge and magic.
              - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** There are also the mighty TzHaar-Ket who guard us, the swift TzHaar-Xil who hunt our food, and the skilled TzHaar-Hur who craft our homes and tools.
          - Thanks for explaining it.
            - (end)
      - *(If the player has not completed The Brink of Extinction:)*
        - I guess so...
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Well, then. There is no problem.
        - No, I'm not!
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Whatever you say, JalYt!
      - *(Otherwise:)*
        - Yes, I certainly am.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** Then it is an honour to speak with you, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. You great and powerful TzHaar, and defender of our city.
        - You must have me confused with another TzHaar.
          - **TzHaar-Mej-Malk:** I heard you are modest, TzHaar-[Caste]-Player. No need. Revel in our praise. You deserve all honour.
  - No, I'm fine. Thanks
    - *(Dialogue ends.)*

### Other dialogue
