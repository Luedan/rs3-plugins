## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock


#### Standard dialogue

- **Tramp:** Got any spare change, mate?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Yes, I can spare a little money.
    - **Player:** Yes, I can spare a little money.
    - (removes=one coin)
    - **Tramp:** Thanks, mate!
  - [Choice]: Sorry, you'll have to earn it yourself.
    - **Player:** Sorry, you'll have to earn it yourself, just like I did.
    - **Tramp:** Please yourself.

#### Subsequent dialogue, if the player refused to give money

- **Tramp:** I know I've asked you before, but have you any spare change this time?
- (continuea)
