## Dialogue Transcript


### Before agreeing to aid Teelee for the first time


#### Talk to

- **Player:** Greetings! These are some interesting looking mushrooms.
- **Teelee:** Oh, hello!<br>Why, yes, yes they are!
- **Teelee:** I haven't seen you around here before.<br>My name's Teelee. It's very nice to meet you.
- **Player:** That's a very...unique name.<br>I'm Player.
- **Player:** So, these mushrooms...<br>Are they just magical mushrooms? Or are they like the mushrooms found in fairyrings?
- **Teelee:** Ha! Oh, no no no. While these are magical mushrooms, they serve a far greater purpose than that of transportation.
- **Player:** Oh? And what might that be?
- **Teelee:** ...Oh, my dear. This grove is rife with magical energy. Be sure to explore the grove in its entirety. I'm sure you'll uncover a lot of hidden secrets!
- **Player:** Hmm, alright. So, back to these mushrooms. What makes them so special?
- **Teelee:** The mushrooms are entwined with the tree spirits of the grove. Have you seen the great tree? If you haven't yet ventured to the north, head that way and you'll see the magnificent tree.
- **Teelee:** The wisps that dwell here aid our tree with nutrients, allowing it to grow. I bet you've never seen a tree of this size in all your travels!
- **Player:** Perhaps not.<br>You mentioned the mushrooms are entwined with the grove. What did you mean by that?
- **Teelee:** That's the beauty of our grove! Many forms of magic harbour here. When our great tree requires additional nutrients, it channels its needs through these mushrooms. The colour of each mushroom informs us which essence we must gather from the wisps.
- **Player:** So you mean you're sacrificing the wisps?
- **Teelee:** Oh, gosh no! Don't be silly! The wisps have excess energy. We create a mixture from a small amount of energy, and offer it to the great tree. It's a ritual we perform each week.
- **Teelee:** So, would you be willing to help me gather from the wisps?
- **Player:** I guess I could. I don't think I'd benefit from it, though, and you don't seem to be that busy.
- **Teelee:** Actually, I'm guarding the mushrooms. Usually Bub or I will collect the energy while the other watches over the mushrooms, but she went exploring and still hasn't returned. She always wanders off when she's due to guard!
- **Teelee:** I could offer you something in return if you could help me out.<br>What do you say?
- Wisps of the Grove is a weekly Distraction and Diversion that awards Farming and Hunter XP upon completion. Once started, you'll receive a jar from Teelee. Take note of the mushrooms around the stones, and collect wisps to match.
- Once you have collected all the wisps you need, you may return to Teelee to receive an offering elixir. Take the elixir to the tree centre to receive your reward.
- [Menu]: Would you like to aid Teelee this week?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Player:** Hmm. Unknown rewards from a complete stranger seem right up my alley. Count me in. What exactly do I need to do?
    - **Teelee:** Excellent! Thank you so much! Right, so you need to take this jar and collect the wisps of the corresponding mushroom colours shown here. Once you've done that, come back and see me. You'll find the wisps spread out across the grove. If the mushrooms are the same colours you'll only need to find one wisp of that colour! Best of luck!
    - (below|Wisps of the Grove 2)
  - [Choice]: No.
    - **Player:** Sorry, maybe another time. I first want to take a look around this grove.
    - **Teelee:** Very well. If you change your mind, I'll be right here!

#### Wisps of the Grove
