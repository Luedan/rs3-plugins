## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Fremennik Trials


#### Talking to her

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Thora the Barkeep:** Hello yourself, outerlander. A little out of the way up here, aren't you?
- **Thora the Barkeep:** I would love to stop and chat with you, but unfortunately we have a custom that no Fremennik may speak with the outerlanders without the permission of our chieftain.
- **Thora the Barkeep:** Don't take it personally. The Chieftain is stood just over there, his name is Brundt. I suggest you go and introduce yourself.
- **Thora the Barkeep:** You wouldn't want to go making any enemies because you weren't aware of our customs now, would you?
- **Player:** No, I guess not. Thanks anyway.
- **Thora the Barkeep:** Not a problem, outerlander. It's always nice to see a new face in the long hall; it happens so rarely.

#### Trade option

- Only Fremenniks may buy drinks here.

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Thora the Barkeep:** Hello again, [Fremennik name]. I suppose you want a drink? Or are you going to try another scam with that terrible Askeladden again?

- **Thora the Barkeep:** Yes. Yes I do. I should never have trusted him not to come in here, even with that written promise. Anyway, do you want a drink or not?
