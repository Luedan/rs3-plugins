### Before The Dig Site

- **Player:** Hello. Who are you?
- **Terry Balando:** Good day to you. My name is Terry Balando, I am an expert archaeologist.
- **Terry Balando:** I am employed by Varrock Museum to oversee all finds at this site. Anything you find must be reported to me.
- **Player:** Oh, okay. If I find anything of interest I will bring it here.
- **Terry Balando:** Can I help you at all?

  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Terry Balando:** Good, let me know if you find anything unusual.
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me anything about the site?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me anything about the site?
    - **Terry Balando:** Yes, indeed! I am studying the lives of the settlers. During the end of the Third Age, there used to be a great city at the site.
    - **Terry Balando:** Its inhabitants were humans, supporters of the god Saradomin. It's not recorded what happened to the community here.
    - **Terry Balando:** I suspect nobody has lived here for over a millennium!
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about the tools an archaeologist uses?
        - (below)
      - [Choice]: Thank you!
        - **Player:** Thank you!
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about the tools an archaeologist uses?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me more about the tools an archaeologist uses?
    - **Terry Balando:** Of course! Let's see now...
    - **Terry Balando:** Trowels are vital for fine digging work, so you can be careful to not damage or disturb any artefacts.
    - **Terry Balando:** Rock picks are for splitting rocks or scraping away soil.
    - **Player:** What about specimen jars and brushes?
    - **Terry Balando:** Those are essential for carefully cleaning and storing smaller samples.
    - **Player:** Where can I get any of these things?
    - **Terry Balando:** Well, we've come into a bit more funding of late, so there should be a stock of each of them in the Exam Centre's tools cupboard.
    - **Terry Balando:** We also hand out relevant tools as students complete each level of their Earth Sciences exams.
    - **Player:** Ah, okay, thanks.

### Using items on him


#### Empty panning tray

- **Terry Balando:** I have no need for panning trays!

#### Full panning tray

- **Terry Balando:** Have you searched this tray yet?
- **Player:** Not that I remember...
- **Terry Balando:** It may contain something, but I don't want to get my hands dirty.
- The expert hands the tray back to you.

#### Special cup

- **Player:** Have a look at this.
- The expert cleans off the mud.
- **Terry Balando:** Looks like an award cup for some small find. Perhaps it belongs to one of the students?

#### Belt buckle

- **Player:** Have a look at this unusual item...
- **Terry Balando:** Let me see... This is a belt buckle. Not so unusual - I should imagine it came from a guard.

#### Bones

- **Player:** Have a look at these bones.
- **Terry Balando:** Ah, yes, a fine bone example! No noticeable fractures, in good condition. These are common cow bones, however; they have no archaeological value.

#### Broken armour

- **Player:** I found some armour.
- **Terry Balando:** It looks like the wearer of this fought a mighty battle.

#### Broken arrow

- **Player:** Have a look at this arrow.
- **Terry Balando:** No doubt this was shot by a strong warrior - it's split in half! It is not a valuable object, though.

#### Broken staff

- **Player:** Have a look at this staff.
- **Terry Balando:** Look at this... Interesting... This appears to belong to a cleric of some kind, though certainly not a follower of Saradomin.
- **Terry Balando:** I wonder if there was another civilization here before the Saradominists.

#### Buttons

- **Player:** I found these buttons.
- **Terry Balando:** Let's have a look. Ah, I think these are from the nobility, perhaps a royal servant.

#### Ceramic remains

- **Player:** I found some pottery pieces.
- **Terry Balando:** Yes, many parts were discovered. The inhabitants of these parts were great potters.
- **Player:** You mean they were good at using potions?
- **Terry Balando:** No, no, silly. They were known for their skill with clay.

#### Needle

- **Player:** I found a needle.
- **Terry Balando:** Hmm, yes. I wondered why this race were so well dressed! It looks like they had mastery of needlework.

#### Old tooth

- **Player:** Hey, look at this.
- **Terry Balando:** Oh, an old tooth. It looks like it has come from a mighty being. Pity there are no tooth fairies around here!

#### Other

- **Terry Balando:** I don't think that has any archaeological significance
