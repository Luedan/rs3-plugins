- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What's your role here?

    - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** I am tasked with nullifying this attack. It is doable, but takes much of my attention.
    - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** Still, a simple enough task, if not for the constant annoyance of Enakhra's presence.
  - ((Only with the Pillars of Ice mechanic toggled off:))
    - Take a break. I can handle this.
      - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** Beware. Those beams can prove troublesome, even to the strongest warriors. You must not let them hit you.

          - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** A bold move. Had you lived during Empire's ascendancy, you would have risen far. For a mortal.

        - Actually, wait!
          - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** Very well.
  - ((Only with the Pillars of Ice mechanic toggled on:))
    - Could you start blocking the icy beams again?
      - **Akthanakos (Pillars of Ice):** Of course.
      - The 'Pillars of Ice' mechanic has been disabled.
