## Dialogue Transcript


### Description in Collector Information interface

- I'll take anything Guthixian!

### Talk to

- **Artiefax:** Welcome, adventurer. Have you come to hear a tale, or to share one?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Artiefax:** My name is Artiefax. I am a teacher and storyteller who keeps the written and oral histories of our people, as my father did before me.
    - **Artiefax:** I've recently become interested in collections of artefacts from ages past.
    - **Artiefax:** These are not false idols or treasures to be coveted. They are the bearers of tales, and lessons, long forgotten.
    - **Player:** That's a beautiful way of looking at it.
    - **Artiefax:** Occasionally the past is beautiful. More often it is sordid, violent or tragic. Many stories the past offers are far from pleasant.
    - **Artiefax:** But they have much to teach us nonetheless.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What are you collecting?
    - **Artiefax:** Do you want to view my collections or discuss them?
    - [Menu]: What would you like to do?
      - [Choice]: View collections.
        - (opens=Lessons of the Past)
      - [Choice]: Talk about collections.
        - **Player:** Tell me more about your collections.
        - **Artiefax:** Word travels of a land seldom visited. Havenhythe.
        - **Artiefax:** Within it lie the remnants of a people who venerated Guthix, as we do, in an age long passed.
        - **Artiefax:** From the little I've gleamed they fell astray, debasing the beliefs and values they once held dear.
        - **Artiefax:** I would see the artefacts left by these people and learn their tale, so that it may be shared with my people. Thus, we can avoid repeating their mistakes.
        - **Artiefax:** After this, these pieces of history shall be returned to the earth, as it is meant to be.
        - **Player:** A noble aim. I'll help you if I can.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are your collection rewards?
    - (Condition: Before receiving guardian's tear and after receiving the tear of Inanna:)
      - **Player:** What rewards are you offering?
      - **Artiefax:** I have little in the way of material goods I can offer in exchange, but I am happy to give you chronotes. They hold value to archaeologists such as yourself.
      - **Artiefax:** I also have a collection of tetracompass pieces I can trade.
      - (Condition: Before receiving the tear of Inanna:)
        - **Artiefax:** There is also an item of great significance in my collection. A tear said to have been shed by a guardian of Guthix many ages ago.
        - **Artiefax:** I am willing to part with it, should you assist me.
        - **Artiefax:** If you combine it with expensive spices, you may be able to create a powerful relic.
        - **Player:** That does sound useful.
        - (previous)
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: After receiving guardian's tear but before receiving the tear of Inanna:)
      - (Condition: Without guardian's tear:)
        - **Player:** I lost the guardian's tear you gave me.
        - **Artiefax:** Fortunately, a kind stranger returned it to me.
        - (Condition: Without free backpack space:)
          - **Artiefax:** Alas, you have no room to take it.
        - (Condition: With free backpack space:)
          - **Artiefax:** Please, be more careful with it this time!
          - [Menu]: Take the guardian's tear from Artiefax?
            - [Choice]: Yes.
              - (receives=guardian's tear)
            - [Choice]: No.
              - (end)
      - (Condition: With guardian's tear:)
        - **Artiefax:** Have you used the guardian's tear I gave you yet?
        - **Player:** Not yet, no.
        - **Artiefax:** If you combine it with expensive spices, you may be able to create a powerful relic.
  - [Choice]: Bye.
    - **Artiefax:** Farewell.
