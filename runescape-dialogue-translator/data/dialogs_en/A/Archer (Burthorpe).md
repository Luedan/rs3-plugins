### Conversation 2 (Burthorpe Castle)

- **Player:**  Hello there! How is it going?
- **Archer:**  Uh... we're not supposed to talk while we're on duty.
- **Player:**  Oh, sorry, I didn't realise...
- **Archer:**  You know what; It's not your fault. I might as well have a chat.
- **Archer:**  I mean, this is supposed to be a secure military installation, you know? That's why they told us not to talk.
- **Archer:**  And then they went and wedged the front doors open and put a ladder of all things up along the wall!
- **Archer:**  Do you have any idea how many people I've almost shot when they peek over the wall there?
- **Archer:**  There is a war on! We could be knee-deep in trolls any second now! What are they thinking?
- **Player:**  It does seem a little odd...
- **Archer:**  Tell me about it. Next thing you know they'll build one of those house portals up here.
- **Archer:**  Then we'll have adventurers and householders popping out across the ramparts for a stroll, kids playing with the cannons...
- **Archer:**  I can see it now... 'Oh Mr Archer, Could you and your friends be dears and move? We have a dinner party at seven and you're ruining the ambiance.'
- **Archer:**  'Do you mind if we put up some hanging baskets? Only this parapet is frightfully grim'.
- **Player:**  Well, so long as things don't get that bad, you'll be all right, right?
- **Archer:**  Yeah, well, we can but hope.
- **Archer:**  Whoops, looks like I've been spotted. See you about, ok?
- **Player:**  Yeah, good luck!

### Conversation 3 (ambush point)

- **Player:**  How are things going?
- **Archer:**  Eh, I can't complain.
- **Player:**  Are you having any trouble with the trolls?
- **Archer:**  It's not the trolls that bother me out here. It's the lack of a decent bedroll.
- **Archer:**  That and having to come to work every day through that jungle-gym downstairs.
- **Archer:**  Still, it could be worse. I once spent a week trapped in the store room before they got rid of that bloody combination lock!

### Conversation 4 (ambush point)

- **Player:**  How are things going?
- **Archer:**  G'day mate. Things are goin' great.
- **Archer:**  Snipin' at trolls is great work. Out in the open air...
- **Player:**  So you're enjoying yourself?
- **Archer:**  Beats bein' stuck in a castle all day, mate!
