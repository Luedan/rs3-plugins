## Dialogue Transcript

- **Angry woman:** Those pesky sirens and their whining. I've had one of their silly melodies stuck in my head for days now.
- **Player:** I've heard they sometimes use their sweet songs to lure fishermen into the open water.
- **Angry woman:** They probably jump voluntarily because they can't stand the singing anymore...
