## Dialogue Transcript


### Attempting to mine anything in the mine before talking to Azur

- **Azur:** Oi! Come talk to me before you start messing with that.

### Before a certain progress of Heralds of Crimson


#### Talk to

- **Azur:** What?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Azur:** What's it to you?
    - **Player:** I was only asking.
    - **Azur:** Then ask someone with the time to indulge you.
    - **Azur:** Now be gone.
  - [Choice]: Can I mine here?
    - **Player:** Can I mine here?
    - (Condition: Unknown condition:)
      - **Azur:** I've enough to manage already without having to look after you as well.
      - **Player:** I can look after myself.
      - **Azur:** I doubt it.
      - **Azur:** Move.
    - (Condition: Unknown condition:)
      - **Azur:** Why would you even want to?
      - **Player:** No particular reason, it just looks interesting.
      - **Azur:** I've enough to manage already without having to look after you as well.
      - **Player:** I can look after myself.
      - **Azur:** I doubt it.
      - **Azur:** Unless you have a particular need to mine here, move along.
