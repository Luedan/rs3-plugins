    - {{anchor|first time}} **Agility assistant:** This is a very simple performance. When you mount the rope, you will have access to some new moves. You're just more likely to perform the trick right if you have the recommended Agility level is all. The final move shown is the juggling move. To juggle, select the object you want to juggle. The juggling equipment is also rated in the same way as the moves, however, so choose your object carefully!

    - **Agility assistant:** Fabulous! Just a few more details: you can only perform 10 moves, whether juggling or normal. At the end of 10 moves you have finished your performance and must dismount. You can perform emotes in between moves for extra points! No need to rush through the performance; it's not timed. Finally, you can only perform on the tightrope once a week. If you decide you don't feel like completing your performance, you can leave any time by using the ladder, but that will be the end of your performance.
