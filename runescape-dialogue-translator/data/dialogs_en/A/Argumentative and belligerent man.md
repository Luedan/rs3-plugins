## Dialogue Transcript

{{dialogue|npc|linkb=Argumentative man|link=Belligerent man}}
- **Player:** What are you arguing about?
- **Belligerent man:** We're arguing about the battle in Lumbridge! {{Audio button|Belligerent man - We're arguing about the battle in Lumbridge!.ogg}}
- **Argumentative man:** Indeed. We are going to join in... {{Audio button|Argumentative man - Indeed. We are going to join in....ogg}}
- **Belligerent man:** My fool of a brother thinks we should join with Saradomin! {{Audio button|Belligerent man - My fool of a brother thinks we should join with Saradomin!.ogg}}
- **Argumentative man:** That's because this idiot here does not understand what dealing with Zamorak will cost us! {{Audio button|Argumentative man - That's because this idiot here does not understand what dealing with Zamorak will cost us!.ogg}}
  - **Player:** What is this about a battle?
    - **Argumentative man:** Have you not heard? {{Audio button|Argumentative man - Have you not heard?.ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** Yes! The great god Zamorak has returned to Gielinor and is battling with Saradomin! {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Yes! The great god Zamorak has returned to Gielinor and is battling with Saradomin!.ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** You mean the great god Saradomin has returned to vanquish the evil Zamorak. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - You mean the great god Saradomin has returned to vanquish the evil Zamorak..ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** Zamorak will easily defeat the weak followers of Saradomin. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Zamorak will easily defeat the weak followers of Saradomin..ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** Can't you see that my brother? We should join the side of power before we are swept aside. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Can't you see that my brother? We should join the side of power before we are swept aside..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** It's you that needs to see that Zamorak will just bring chaos and death. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - It's you that needs to see that Zamorak will just bring chaos and death..ogg}}
      - **Player:** Can I join in?
        - **Belligerent man:** Yes, you can! Zamorak requests that all people help, and not just warriors. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Yes, you can! Zamorak requests that all people help, and not just warriors..ogg}}
        - **Argumentative man:** No, Saradomin needs skilled people to gather divine tears on the battlefield and defeat the minions of Zamorak. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - No, Saradomin needs skilled people to gather divine tears on the battlefield and defeat the minions of Zamorak..ogg}}
        - **Belligerent man:** Saradomin only needs the help because he is too weak to win the battle by himself. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Saradomin only needs the help because he is too weak to win the battle by himself..ogg}}
        - **Argumentative man:** Clearly the so-called 'all powerful Zamorak' is not all he claims to be, if he heeds help too. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - Clearly the so-called 'all powerful Zamorak' is not all he claims to be, if he heeds help too..ogg}}
        - **Belligerent man:** Zamorak only needs the help because people like you support Saradomin! {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Zamorak only needs the help because people like you support Saradomin! .ogg}}
          - **Player:** Where should I go to join in?
            - **Argumentative man:** You can join Saradomin's army to the west of Lumbridge castle. Talk to the officer in the camp to the north of the battlefield. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - You can join Saradomin's army to the west of Lumbridge castle. Talk to the officer in the camp to the north of the battlefield..ogg}}
            - **Belligerent man:** Don't waste your time with that. You should talk to the officer in the south camp and join the mighty Zamorak. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Don't waste your time with that. You should talk to the officer in the south camp and join the mighty Zamorak..ogg}}
              - **Player:** I hope you sort out your problems.
  - **Player:** Why should you join Saradomin?
    - **Argumentative man:** We should support Saradomin since he represents the good in the world. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - We should support Saradomin since he represents the good in the world..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** He has been protecting us from the evils of Zamorak for centuries, and it's time we helped him back. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - He has been protecting us from the evils of Zamorak for centuries, and it's time we helped him back..ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** It's all lies! Saradomin never cared about the people. At least Zamorak is honest in his pursuit of power and glory. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - It's all lies! Saradomin never cared about the people. At least Zamorak is honest in his pursuit of power and glory..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** The world would be a worse place with the chaos that Zamorak would unleash! {{Audio button|Argumentative man - The world would be a worse place with the chaos that Zamorak would unleash!.ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** Saradomin neither cares, nor has the power to deal with your petty problems, with Zamorak will reward the faithful. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Saradomin neither cares, nor has the power to deal with your petty problems, with Zamorak will reward the faithful..ogg}}
  - **Player:** Why should you join Zamorak?
    - **Belligerent man:** Zamorak is the most powerful god. When we defeat Saradomin, the rewards for the faithful will be great. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Zamorak is the most powerful god. When we defeat Saradomin, the rewards for the faithful will be great..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** But those rewards would come at a price - chaos would reign in the world! {{Audio button|Argumentative man - But those rewards would come at a price - chaos would reign in the world!.ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** Saradomin stands no chance of winning. We should be joining the side that will win; the side of power.<!--Lol.---> {{Audio button|Belligerent man - Saradomin stands no chance of winning. We should be joining the side that will win, the side of power..ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** You damn us to a slow death, brother. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - You damn us to a slow death, brother..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** At least I do not damn our very souls! {{Audio button|Argumentative man - At least I do not damn our very souls!.ogg}}
  - **Player:** Why don't you join opposite sides?
    - **Belligerent man:** I could never fight against my brother. Not even for Zamorak himself. {{Audio button|Belligerent man - I could never fight against my brother. Not even for Zamorak himself..ogg}}
    - **Argumentative man:** Agreed. We must be brothers in arms as well as by blood. {{Audio button|Argumentative man - Agreed. We must be brothers in arms as well as by blood..ogg}}
  - **Player:** I don't want to get involved!
    - **Argumentative man:** That's because you are not brave enough! {{Audio button|Argumentative man - That's because you are not brave enough!.ogg}}
    - **Belligerent man:** At least we agree on that! {{Audio button|Belligerent man - At least we agree on that!.ogg}}
