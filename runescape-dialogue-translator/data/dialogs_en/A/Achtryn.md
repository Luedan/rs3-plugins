### Assignment advice


#### Aberrant spectres

**Achtryn:** Aberrant spectres are fetid, vile ghosts. The very smell of them will paralyse and harm you. A nosepeg will help you ignore their stink and make you fight at full capacity. Sniff them out in the Slayer Tower in Morytania.

#### Ankous

**Achtryn:** Ankous are neither ghost nor skeleton, but a combination of both. Not much is known of these creatures. Perform some research in the depths of the Stronghold of Security.

#### Banshees

**Achtryn:** Banshees use a piercing scream to shock their enemies; you'll need some earmuffs to protect yourself from them. Their cries echo throughout the Slayer Tower in Morytania.

#### Basilisks

**Achtryn:** Basilisks, like cockatrices, have a gaze which will paralyse and harm their prey. You'll need a mirror shield to protect you. Basilisks live in the Rellekka Slayer Caves, along with their cockatrice relatives.

#### Bloodvelds

**Achtryn:** Bloodveld are strange, demonic creatures that use their long, rasping tongue to feed on just about anything they can find. If you wish to slay some, they live in the Slayer Tower to the north-west of Canifis.

#### Brine rats

**Achtryn:** Brine rats can be found in caves near to the sea. They are hairless, bad-tempered and generally unfriendly. If you have found Olaf's pirate treasure, you'll know where to find these targets.

#### Cave crawlers

**Achtryn:** Cave crawlers are small and fast, often seen creeping around in the Rellekka Slayer Dungeon. Avoid their barbed tongue or you'll get poisoned.

#### Cockatrices

**Achtryn:** Cockatrices, like basilisks, have a gaze which will paralyse and harm their prey. You'll need a mirror shield to protect you. If you are brave enough, head to Slayer Caves east of Rellekka.

#### Crocodiles

**Achtryn:** Crocodiles are large reptiles that live in the Kharidian Desert near water. River crocodiles are a bigger challenge than the ones found next to the ocean in the south east.

#### Cyclopes

**Achtryn:** Cyclopes are tricky giants, originally from the far-eastern lands. The brave warriors in the guild near Burthorpe have a collection of them; however, they are immune to anything other than melee attacks. Other specimens aren't so tough, and can be found in the God Wars Dungeon.

#### Desert lizards

**Achtryn:** Desert lizards are large reptiles with tough skin. They're cold-blooded, so dousing them with freezing water will finish them off after a tough battle. You can find them to the east of Sophanem.

#### Dogs

**Achtryn:** Dogs are much like wolves; they are pack creatures that will hunt in groups. I've heard there are fearsome wild dogs beneath Karamja, or near Ardougne if you are less adventurous.

#### Earth warriors

**Achtryn:** Earth warriors are an elemental warrior pieced together by rocks. Water washes them away easily so use spells of that type. They form from the rocks beneath Edgeville.

#### Ghouls

**Achtryn:** Ghouls aren't undead, but they are stronger and tougher than they look. They're also very cowardly and will run if they're losing a fight. They wait to ambush those entering the graveyard to the  North of Canifis.

#### Green dragons

**Achtryn:** Green dragons are the weakest dragons but they are still very powerful. Watch out for their fiery breath. These brutes live out in the Wilderness.

#### Grotworms

**Achtryn:** Grotworms have recently been spotted in a cave near Rimmington. The younger specimens haven't fully developed their mandibles yet, and will attack with some sort of acid spit.

#### Ice giants

**Achtryn:** Like other giants, ice giants often wield large weapons. Try using fire spells to melt them before they can hit you. Ice giants can be found underground to the south of Port Sarim.

#### Ice warriors

**Achtryn:** Ice warriors are a kind of ice elemental. Thurgo the dwarf recently spotted some in the caves south of Port Sarim.

#### Infernal mages

**Achtryn:** Infernal mages are dangerous spell users; beware of their Magic spells and go properly prepared. They have taken over an area upstairs in the Slayer Tower, plotting your doom.

#### Jellies

**Achtryn:** Jellies are nasty, cube-like gelatinous creatures that absorb everything they come across. Their acidic touch attacks in the same fashion as many magics, so prepare your armour beforehand. They keep parts of Rellekka Slayer Caves clean.

#### Jungle horrors

**Achtryn:** Jungle horrors can be found all over Mos Le'Harmless. They are strong, aggressive and fight in packs, so watch out!

#### Killerwatts

**Achtryn:** Killerwatts store huge amounts of energy in their bodies, which is released if they are touched. You'll need to wear heavily insulated boots to counter this shocking effect, plus they can attack speedily with both magic and missiles. To access their cloudy plane, you'll need to explore Draynor Manor.

#### Lesser demons

**Achtryn:** Lesser demons are magic creatures, so they are weak to ranged attacks. Although they're relatively weak, they are still dangerous. Taverley Dungeon is home to these foul creatures.

#### Molanisks

**Achtryn:** Molanisks are subterranean creatures. You can find them in caves deep below the ground. I heard that the goblins have recently had trouble with some, but they use a bell to lure them from the cave wall.

#### Moss giants

**Achtryn:** Like other giants, moss giants often wield large weapons. Use fire spells to burn them to cinders before they can damage you. Strangely for giants, some have been seen at the end of the Varrock sewers.

#### Ogres

**Achtryn:** Ogres are brutal creatures, favouring large blunt maces and clubs. They often attack without warning. South of Yanille is an area ruled by these man-devourers.

#### Otherworldly beings

**Achtryn:** Otherworldly beings are ethereal creatures, making them weak to ranged attacks. They can be found haunting Zanaris.

#### Pyrefiends

**Achtryn:** Pyrefiends are magical beings of fire and molten rock. They're quick and agile so try attacking them with a crossbow and bolts. You can find some fiends in the Rellekka Slayer Caves.

#### Rock slugs

**Achtryn:** Rock slugs are strange, stoney slugs, many of which have been seen in Rellekka Slayer Cave. You'll need to fight them until they are close to death, before finishing them off with salt.

#### Sea snakes

**Achtryn:** Sea snakes are long and slithery, with a venomous bite. The larger ones are more dangerous, so keep an eye on your health. They live on isles far to the north-west, or, rather, in the sea caves of those isles.

#### Shades

**Achtryn:** Shades are undead - The town of Mort'ton in Morytania is plagued by these creatures, so help if you can. There are some shades in the Stronghold of Security too, but you won't learn much from fighting those; stick to Mort'ton.

#### Shadow warriors

**Achtryn:** Shadow warriors are dark and mysterious. They hide in the shadows, so be wary of ambushes. The basement of the Legends' Guild harbours these menaces.

#### Turoths

**Achtryn:** Turoths are large, vicious creatures with thick hides. You'll need a leaf-bladed weapon, broad arrows or a magic dart to harm them. You can find a nest of them in the Rellekka Slayer Caves.

#### Vampyres

**Achtryn:** Vampyres are extremely powerful beings. They feed on the blood of the living, so watch that you don't get bitten. Look in the woods of Morytania.

#### Werewolves

**Achtryn:** Werewolves are feral creatures; strong and tough with sharp claws and teeth. The village of Canifis is home to many of them.
