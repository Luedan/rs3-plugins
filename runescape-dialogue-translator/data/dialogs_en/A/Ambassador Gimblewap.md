### Standard dialogue

- **Ambassador Gimblewap:** I can't believe that I've been made to wait so long.
- **Player:** What are you waiting for?
- **Ambassador Gimblewap:** I have been waiting for an audience with the King for days!
- **Player:** Well, he's clearly a busy man.
- **Ambassador Gimblewap:** Bah! I think he's just trying to insult the gnome nation, by making me wait. The cheek of these humans. Just because they're vertically challenged they think they can ignore us.
- **Player:** I'm not ignoring you.
- **Ambassador Gimblewap:** Hmm. I should be thankful for small mercies, I suppose.

### Gnome Restaurant delivery

- **Ambassador Gimblewap:** Many thanks. This will help wile away the time whilst I'm waiting for an audience.
