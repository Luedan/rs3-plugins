## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### First time

- **Aurriel:** Hello, young one.
- {{tselect|Select an Option}}
  - {{topt|Who are you?}}
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Aurriel:** I am Aurriel. I tend this place.
    - **Aurriel:** My line has watched over and tended to the colossi here for generations beyond count.
    - **Aurriel:** We tend the old stones, and honour the dead so what remains of them may pass back into the land in peace.
    - **Player:** Have you done this all your life?
    - **Aurriel:** Near enough.
    - **Aurriel:** Once I walked the roads myself. These days my memory serves me better than my legs.
    - (previous)
  - {{topt|What is this place?}}
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Aurriel:** A resting place, of a sort.
    - **Aurriel:** The stone here has taken in traces of other colossi; memories and energy settle here when they ought to have passed on.
    - **Aurriel:** We tend it, and we honour the dead by helping that burden pass on.
    - **Aurriel:** What gathers here is carried out to the old murals, each one attuned to a different colossus, so it may return to the land in the proper way.
    - **Player:** How do you do that?
    - **Aurriel:** With care, and with these memory stones.
    - **Player:** Memory stones?
    - **Aurriel:** That is what my master called them.
    - **Aurriel:** I could not tell you who first shaped them, only that they answer to what the colossi leave behind.
    - **Aurriel:** Theyhold the gathered energy well enough to bear it, and that is all I ahve ever needed of them.
    - **Aurriel:** You have listened kindly, so I will speak plainly.
    - **Aurriel:** I can no longer make the journeys I once did.
    - **Aurriel:** Would you help me honour the dead, and carry these remnants back to the colossi murals?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: How would I do that?
        - **Player:** How would I do that?
        - **Aurriel:** Take one of these memory stoens. Fill it with the gathered memories and energy held here.
        - **Aurriel:** Then carry it to the old murals raised in the colossi's memory, and offer it there.
        - **Aurriel:** In that way, what has been gathered here may return to the land.
        - **Player:** And you want me to do that for you?
        - **Aurriel:** I would ask it, yes.
        - (receives=a memory stone)
        - [pic=Memory stone detail.png]
        - **Aurriel:** Go carefully.
        - **Aurriel:** These are not trinkets, but remnants of those far older than you, or even I.
        - (end)
      - [Choice]: No, I would rather not.
        - {{tmissing}}
  - [Choice]: That is all for now.
    - {{tmissing}}

#### Subsequent times

- **Aurriel:** Yes?
- (Condition: If the player has lost their stonevein amulet:)
  - [Menu]: Would you like to reclaim your amulet?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - **Player:** I've lost the amulet you gave me.
      - **Aurriel:** Foolish. Luckily it has a way of making its way back here.
      - **Aurriel:** Take more care of it this time.
      - {{tact|receives=a stonevein amulet}}
      - {{tbox|pic=Stonevein amulet detail.png|Aurriel hands you an old stone amulet.}}
      - {{tact|continue}}
    - [Choice]: No.
    - (continue)
- {{tselect|Select an Option}}
  - {{topt|Who are you?}}
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Aurriel:** I am Aurriel.
    - **Aurriel:** I was brought here young, and taught by the last tender to keep watch over the dead colossi.
    - **Aurriel:** He taught me the stones, the murals, and the old ways of returning what gathers here.
    - **Player:** And now it rests with you?
    - **Aurriel:** I feel for just a little while longer, yes.
    - {{tact|previous}}
  - {{topt|What is this place?}}
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Aurriel:** A resting place, and an old one.
    - **Aurriel:** The colossus beside us fell long before my time. We know not how this one died, only that it did not leave cleanly.
    - **Aurriel:** Its stone still draws traces of other colossi. Memories and energy settle here when they ought to have passed on.
    - **Aurriel:** That is why we tend this place.
    - **Player:** So it cannot be left here?
    - **Aurriel:** It should not be.
    - **Aurriel:** What gathers here must be carried out, and given back where it belongs.
    - (previous)
  - {{topt|What do I do again?}}
    - **Player:** What do you want me to do?
    - **Aurriel:** Take a memory stone, and fill it here with what has gathered in the colossus.
    - **Aurriel:** When it is full, carry it to the mural it answers to, and offer it there.
    - **Aurriel:** The steon will guide you well enough, if you let it.
    - **Player:** So I am carrying these remnants back to where they belong?
    - **Aurriel:** Yes.
    - **Aurriel:** It is simple work to carry out, though not without meaning.
    - (previous)
  - {{topt|What are the stones?}}
    - **Player:** What are these memory stones?
    - **Aurriel:** Old things, shaped long before my time.
    - **Aurriel:** Whether they are sacred tools or simply clever craft, I cannot say.
    - **Aurriel:** I only know they will hold what the colossi leave behind: memory, energy, and something of the two together.
    - **Aurriel:** That is enough for our purposes.
    - (previous)
  - {{topt|Could I have another stone?}}
    - **Player:** Could I have another stone?
    - (Condition: With a memory stone already:)
      - **Aurriel:** You already carry one.
      - **Aurriel:** Use that first. I would rather not have them scattered about carelessly.
      - (end)
    - (Condition: Without a memory stone:)
      - **Aurriel:** Of course.
      - **Aurriel:** See that this one reaches a mural in good time.
      - (receives=a memory stone)
      - [pic=Memory stone detail.png]
      - (end)

### Interrupting the player after they've restored 10 murals

- **Aurriel:** Before you continue, speak with me, young one.

### When progress has been made in Colossi Divination

- **Aurriel:** Wait, young one.
- **Player:** Is something the matter?
- **Aurriel:** No. Quite the opposite.
- **Aurriel:** You have done a great justice by the colossi here.
- **Aurriel:** You have honoured their memory, and in doing so, honoured those who came before.
- **Aurriel:** This amulet belonged to my master, and to his master before him.
- **Aurriel:** It should serve you better.
- **Aurriel:** Take it. Let it guide you back to this place.
- {{tact|receives=a stonevein amulet}}
- {{tbox|pic=Stonevein amulet detail.png|Aurriel hands you an old stone amulet.}}
