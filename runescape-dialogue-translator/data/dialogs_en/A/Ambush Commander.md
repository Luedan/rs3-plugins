## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to the Ambush Commander

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Ambush Commander:** Good day. Come to see how things are going?
- *If the player has supplies in their inventory:*
  - **Player:** Actually, I've come to drop off these supplies!
  - (removes=supplies) (receives=three reward lamps)
  - **Ambush Commander:** Thanks. These supplies will come in handy. Here, have your reward.
  - *If the player has not completed five supply runs:*
    - **Ambush Commander:** We still need some more supplies though. Please speak to Denulth if you have a chance.
- *If the player has lost or destroyed one or more earned reward lamps:*
  - **Player:** Can I have those reward lamps that you owe me?
  - (receives=any missing reward lamps)
  - **Ambush Command:** Of course. Thanks again for the supplies.
  - **Player:** You're welcome.
- **Player:** Has there been any activity from the trolls?
- **Ambush Commander:** Not a lot. I think they are up to something, but we'll be ready for them.
- *If the player has not completed five supply runs:*
  - **Ambush Commander:** We still need some more supplies though. Please speak to Denulth if you have a chance.
