## Dialogue Transcript

{{dialogue|npc}}
- **Player:** Here is the delivery.
- **A strange courier:** Thank you very much. There are a lot of important things in here.
- **Player:** How do elves celebrate Easter anyway?
- **A strange courier:** To us, this is a season of rebirth, of communing with nature and of understanding the spirit of the world. We celebrate by having crystal eggs grown. These shine with a beautiful light, reminding us of where we came from and of our ties to the world. It is one of our most important celebrations.
- **Player:** I have completed all of my deliveries, now. (varies)
- **A strange courier:** Well done. I think you should return to the Easter Bunny now.
