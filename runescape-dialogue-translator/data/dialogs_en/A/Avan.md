## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Family Crest


#### Talk-to or Change-gauntlets options

- **Avan:** What? Can't you see I'm busy?
- **Player:** Well, sooooorry...

### After Family Crest


#### With the unimbued family gauntlets

- **Avan:** I have received word from my father of your assistance to our family in this matter. You have my thanks for restoring our honour.
- **Player:** Your father mentioned that you could improve these gauntlets in some way for me...
- **Avan:** Indeed I can. In my long time searching for the 'perfect' gold I learned a lot about smelting metals. I can improve these gauntlets so that they send bars to the metal bank as you smelt them. I can easily modify these for you.
- **Select an option**
  - That sounds good, improve them for me.
    - **Player:** That sounds good. Improve them for me!
    - ''Avan takes your gauntlets, takes out a small hammer, and pounds them into a slightly new shape, then hands them back to you.
    - {{tact|removes=family gauntlets}} {{tact|receives=smelting gauntlets}}
  - I think I'll check my other options with your brothers.
    - **Player:** I think I'll check my other options with your brothers before committing myself.
    - **Avan:** If you feel you really must consort with the likes of them. I await your decision.

#### With the imbued family gauntlets or without any of the gauntlets


##### Talk-to option

- **Avan:** I have received word from my father of your assistance to our family in this matter. You have my thanks for restoring our honour.
- **Player:** Not a problem.
- **Avan:** Yes well... Is there anything I can help you with?
- Choose an option:
  - Can you change my gauntlets for me?
    - **Player:{{anchor|change-gauntlets}}** Can you change my gauntlets for me?
    - *(If the player has the gauntlets:)*
      - **Avan:** Yes certainly, though it will cost you 25,000 coins?
      - Select an option
        - Great thanks!
          - *(If the player has at least 25,000 coins:)*
            - {{tact|removes=family gauntlets and 25,000 coins}} {{tact|receives=smelting gauntlets}}
            - ''Avan takes your gauntlets, takes out a small hammer, and pounds them into a slightly new shape, then hands them back to you.
          - *(If the player has less than 25,000 coins:)*
            - **Avan:** Unfortunately you don't have enough coins on you, come back with 25,000 and I'll change those gauntlets for you.
        - No that's ok thanks.
          - (end)
    - *(If the player already has smelting gauntlets in inventory:)*
      - **Avan:** Your gauntlets have already been forged.
    - *(If the player doesn't have the gauntlets:)*
      - **Avan:** You don't have any gauntlets I can imbue.
  - *(After having the family gauntlets enchanted for the first time:)*
    - Does your family own any more gauntlets?
      - **Player:** Does your family own any more gauntlets? I really appreciate the skill and effort you and your brothers have put into those gauntlets, and to prove it, I would like to own one of each pair.
      - **Avan:** I do have my own pair, as was handed to me by my father before I set out. They are a family heirloom and very precious to me. I wouldn't just part with them like that.
      - **Player:** How much would you want for them?
      - **Avan:** They have a lot of sentimental value and I do use them every day. It's hard to put a price on that.
      - **Player:** But if you had to?
      - **Avan:** 1 million?
      - Select an option
        - I would like to buy them for 1 million.
          - **Avan:** Are you sure? That's a lot of money!
          - Select an option
            - **Player:**Yes!
              - {{tact|removes=1,000,000 coins}} {{tact|receives=smelting gauntlets}}
            - **Player:**I suppose not.
              - (end)
          - **Player:** I suppose you had better keep them.
          - **Avan:** Thank you!
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
