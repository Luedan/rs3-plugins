### After Animal Magnetism


#### Talk to

- **Ava:** Hello again; I'm busy with my newest research, so can't gossip too much. Are you after information, an upgrade, another device or would you like to see my goods for sale?
- Select an option
  - I'd like information, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like information, please.
    - **Ava:** Just a few bits of information before you run away to persecute rock crabs or cows.
    - **Ava:** Firstly, your new device won't work if you are wearing certain metallic chest armours.
    - **Ava:** The magnetic attraction required for operation would cause feedback, so the device does not allow such incompatible item equippage.
    - **Ava:** Secondly, although the device is calibrated to attract only arrowheads, there is a chance that other magnetically active items will be attracted.
    - **Ava:** The arrow recovery function of the devices is slightly different and relies upon a harmonic bond between your arrows and the chicken's magnet. You'll thus only recover arrows which you have fired rather than those fired by nearby folks.
    - **Ava:** You'll also need to move about if you want to collect arrows since the gathering of long-lost arrowheads can't work otherwise.
    - **Ava:** Finally, the device will only work when worn. It automatically deactivates in other circumstances.
    - **Ava:** I was worried about what would happen if you were to place it in a bank in its active state, so I've made sure it only works when it's on your back.
  - I seem to need a new device.
    - **Player:** I need another arrow-attracting device, please.
    - **Ava:** Well, luckily for you, they have a habit of returning to me when people lose them, so I have some spares. They are homing chickens, it seems.
    - **Ava:** I'll need 999 gp, however, for the expenses involved in restoring the poor creature to health, or unhealth, or whatever.
    - **Player:** Why 999?
    - **Ava:** Well, it just sounds less expensive than 1000. Do you want that replacement or not.
    - Select an option
      - Sounds good to me.
        - **Player:** Sounds good to me.
        - *If the player has more than 999 gp*
          - **Ava:** Here's your device; take good care of your chicken.
        - *If the player has less than 999 gp*
          - **Ava:** You seem not to have enough cash; you could always sell some of your gear, though.
      - I'd prefer not to, actually.
        - **Player:** I'd prefer not to, actually.
        - **Ava:** I've better things to do than be irritated by you.
  - I'd like to upgrade my device, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like to upgrade my device, please.
    - *If the player has no upgradeable device.*
      - **Ava:** You don't have a device in your bags that I can upgrade, I'm afraid.
    - *If the player has Ava's attractor and 50 ranged, but not 75 steel arrows*
      - **Ava:** I need 75 steel arrows for the upgrade process, I'm afraid.
    - *If the player has Ava's attractor and 50 ranged, and 75 steel arrows*
      - **Ava:** You are ready to upgrade. I'll take 75 steel arrows and the old device, if that's all fine with you?
      - Select an option
        - Sounds good to me.
          - **Player:** Sounds good to me.
          - **Ava:** Here's your upgraded device; take good care of it.
        - I'd prefer not to, actually.
          - **Player:** I'd prefer not to, actually.
          - **Ava:** I've better things to do than be irritated by you.
  - I'd like to see your stuff for sale, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like to see your stuff for sale, please.
    - **Ava:** Here's my shop.
    - *(opens Ava's odds and ends store)*
  - I'll just head off, I think.
    - **Player:** I'll just head off, I think.
    - **Ava:** Thanks for the help with my bed; see you again.
