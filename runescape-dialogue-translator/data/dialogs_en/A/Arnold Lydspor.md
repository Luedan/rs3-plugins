### Standard dialogue after Swan Song

- **Arnold Lydspor:** Ah, you come back! What you want from Arnold, heh?
- {{tcond|If the player has items ready for collection in the Grand Exchange:}}
  - **Arnold Lydspor:** Oh, but first Arnold must tell you that items for you have arrived from Grand Exchange.
  - {{tact|continue}}
- {{tselect|What would you like to say?}}
  - {{topt|Can you open my bank account, please?}}
    - '' (Bank account opens.)
  - {{topt|I'd like to check my Bank PIN settings.}}
    - ''(PIN settings open.)
  - {{topt|Would you like to trade?}}
    - **Player:** Would you like to trade?
    - **Arnold Lydspor:** Ja, I have wide range of stock...
    - {{tact|opens=Arnold's Eclectic Supplies}}
  - {{topt|I'd like to see my Collection box.}}
    - ''(Collection box opens.)
  - {{topt|Nothing, I just came to chat.}}
    - **Player:** Nothing, I just came to chat.
    - **Arnold Lydspor:** Ah, that is nice - always I like to chat, but Herr Caranos tell me to get back to work! Here, you been nice, so have a present:
    - {{tact|receives=cabbage}}
    - **Player:** A cabbage?
    - **Arnold Lydspor:** Ja, cabbage is good for you!
    - **Player:** Um... thanks!
