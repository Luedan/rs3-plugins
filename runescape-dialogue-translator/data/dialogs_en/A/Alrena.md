# Alrena

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Alrena](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AAlrena)
- **Letter**: `A`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Plague City

- **Player:** Hello Madam.
- **Alrena:** Oh, hello there.
- **Player:** Are you ok?
- **Alrena:** Not too bad... I've just got some troubles on my mind...

### After Plague City

- **Alrena:** Thank you for rescuing my daughter! Elena has told me of your bravery in entering a house that could have been plague infected. I can't thank you enough!

### After Plague's End

- **Alrena:** Thank you for rescuing my daughter! Elena told me what you did... You're very brave. You not only entered a house that could have been plague infected, but even uncovered the truth of it, and led a revolution! I can't thank you enough!
