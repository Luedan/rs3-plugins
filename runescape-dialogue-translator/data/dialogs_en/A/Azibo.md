## Dialogue Transcript


#### Talking to Azibo

- **Azibo:** Hi there!
- **Player:** Hi, Azibo.
  - *If you have not yet collected and opened a Golden cracker*
    - **Azibo:** The makers of the world have one an award! Have a cracker, with our compliments!
      - Would you like a golden cracker?
        - Yes.
          - Azibo gives you a golden cracker.
          - **Player:** Thanks, Azibo!
          - **Azibo:** Pull it with another person and you'll get a special surprise! Now, what can I do for you?
            - *(Continues below)*
        - No.
          - **Azibo:** Feel free to collect it later! Now, what can I do for you?
            - *(Continues below)*
- **Azibo:** Want to see my spinning plates or kites? Want to check out my range of party items? Or did you want an item back?
  - Spinning plates?
    - **Player:** Spinning plates?
    - **Azibo:** There was a mix-up some years back with a man who lives in Draynor - something about 'dragon plates' being confused for armour.
      - *(Azibo's Toy Store opens)*
  - Wow, a kite!
    - **Player:** Wow, a kite!
    - **Azibo:** You're not the first to say that...
    - **Player:** Can I have one, please?
    - **Azibo:** Well, I suppose I did order more than I need... It's yours for the bargain price of 100 coins.
      - That's a bargain! I'll take one.
        - **Player:** That's a bargain! I'll take one.
          - *(Azibo's Toy Store opens)*
      - No, thanks, I don't want one.
        - **Player:** No, thanks, I don't want one.
  - Party items, you say?
    - **Player:** Party items, you say?
    - **Azibo:** Yes, I have all sorts of stuff - confetti, bubble makers, fireworks, firecrackers and some mugs! Take a look!
      - *(Azibo's Toy Store opens)*
  - I'd like to check my items, please.
    - **Azibo:** Sure thing, let me just see what you're missing.
      - *(Diango's Item Retrieval opens)*
  - Can you check my bank for items?
    - **Azibo:** Sure thing, let me see what I can take off your hands.
    - **Azibo:** I'll look through items that I will hold onto, items that could go into a costume room in your house and junk items.
      - *(Item Cleanup opens)*
