# Apothecary (ilujanka)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Apothecary (ilujanka)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AApothecary%20%28ilujanka%29)
- **Letter**: `A`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Apothecary:** Welcome, fellow dreamer.
- ((Before having built the apothecary:))
  - **Apothecary:**  If you could provide me some resources from your base camp, I could create an apothecary for you to train at within this dream, and teach you something of Ilujankan herblore techniques.
- ((After having built the apothecary but before having made progress:))
  - **Apothecary:** Thank you for the leaves. If you keep supplying me with more, I can teach you much about Ilujankan herblore techniques.
- ((After a small amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Apothecary:** You are learning much of our ways. Keep on as you are, and I will share a story with you.
- ((After a great amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Apothecary:** You have learned much. I shall soon share my tale with Pastkeeper Sharrigan.
- ((After having unlocked the story:))
  - **Apothecary:** I have recounted my tale to Pastkeeper Sharrigan. Thank you for all of your efforts. Perhaps you should train at one of the other stations for a time.
