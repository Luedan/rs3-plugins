## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Gower Quest, at the Gower farm

- **Andrew Gower:** Who are you?
- **Player:** I'm Player. I was wondering if you had any quests for me?
- **Paul Gower:** I have a quest, but I need a more experienced adventurer to help with it.
- You need 34 Quest Points to start Gower Quest.

### After Gower Quest, at the Life Altar


#### Normally

- **Andrew Gower:** All's well that ends well.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Can I use the life altar?
    - **Andrew Gower:** No, I'm afraid not. It's very dangerous.
    - **Player:** Oh, that's a shame. I was assuming it would unlock as a quest reward.
    - **Andrew Gower:** You don't know any spells that need life runes, do you?
    - **Player:** No.
    - **Andrew Gower:** No need to worry then!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can you return me to the surface?
    - **Andrew Gower:** Of course. I can send you back to our old farm if you like.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes please.
        - {{Qact|The player is teleported to Gower Farm.}}
      - [Choice]: No thanks.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

#### Having lost the cabbagespeak amulet

- (Condition: If the player has no space in backpack:)
  - **Player:** Oh. I don't have room for it in my inventory.
  - **Andrew Gower:** Oh, I'm sorry. I hate it when that happens.
  - **Player:** Not to worry, I'll clear a space and get back to you!
  - (continuea|main)
- (Condition: If the player has space in backpack:)
  - (receives=cabbagespeak amulet)

  - (continuea|main)
