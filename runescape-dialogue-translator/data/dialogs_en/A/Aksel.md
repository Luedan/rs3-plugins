### At the Artisans' Workshop


#### Normally

- **Aksel:** Welcome. What brings you to the home of the artisans?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's an artisan?
    - **Player:** What's an artisan?
    - **Aksel:** Artisans are a collective of skilled smiths who work towards upholding quality as their principle ethos.
    - **Aksel:**  Smithing can be seen as a coarse, unloving undertaking...bashing out platebodies as quickly as you can. It's not like that here.
    - **Aksel:** Recently, we've opened our doors to humans. Some of our stauncher members aren't happy about this, but if you work hard I'm sure you'll earn our respect.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about yourself.
    - **Player:** Tell me more about yourself.
    - **Aksel:** I built the furnace that rather dominates this room. Took me four and a half years; carved the rock myself. If you need any pointers on how to use it, talk to Egil.
    - **Aksel:** I don't do much actual smithing any more. I mainly potter around the workshop, helping where I can.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: I've got to go.
    - **Player:** I've got to go.
    - **Aksel:** Ah, take care.
