# Assistant Kara

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Assistant Kara](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AAssistant%20Kara)
- **Letter**: `A`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to

- **Assistant Kara:** Hey there, can I help you?
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Assistant Kara:** I'm a runecrafting scholar in training, and currently an assistant to Cat.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Assistant Kara:** These dunes have started to appear across Gielinor. I've been tasked with finding and removing them from the world.
    - **Assistant Kara:** I thought it would be a simple task, however every time we clear one, another pops up somewhere else!
    - **Assistant Kara:** Sometimes I wish I had super powers, at least that way I could have these removed in no time at all.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - That's all, thanks.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*

### After Small Dune has been cleared

- **Assistant Kara:** Awesome, that's all cleared up now!
