## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before constructing the water filtration system

- **Player:** Are you... fishing? There doesn't look to be any fish.
- **Anahita:** Oh, no, my dear! I am sifting through the oasis water for anything of value.
- **Player:** Sounds fun! Can I join in?
- **Anahita:** Yes! There's a pile of materials just over there that I'm sure could be made into something useful.
- **Player:** Thanks!

#### After constructing the water filtration system

- **Player:** So what exactly does this water filtration system do?
- **Anahita:** I'm glad you asked! This system filters through the water from the oasis, cleaning it in the process - anything too large that gets caught in it you can claim. If you come back every so often, you might find some valuable items in there! Of course, you might just find it's full of sandstone and seaweed instead.
- **Player:** Cool! I'll make sure to check it out.
