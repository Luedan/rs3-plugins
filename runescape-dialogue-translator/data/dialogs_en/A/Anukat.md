### Talk to


#### Without catspeak

- **Anukat:** Meow, meow meow.

#### With catspeak

- **Anukat:** Ah, finally. I trust you are here with an appropriate selection of entrees?
- **Player:** Excuse me?
- **Anukat:** Absolutely not. Why, the service here has been absolutely dreadful. I have been here for several minutes and not so much as a bowl of milk.
- **Anukat:** Honestly, quite how you still have your job, I do not know. Now go and be a good servant and bring me a fresh bowl of milk.
- **Player:** I'm not a servant.
- **Anukat:** After this terrible service, I shall absolutely make certain that you are not. Now go, go be useful or get out of my sight!
