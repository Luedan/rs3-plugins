## Dialogue Transcript


#### Talk to


##### For the first time before talking about Oak

- **Player:** Hello Aster.
- **Aster:** Greetings, your grace, it's good to see you. Do you have a moment to spare to discuss an expansion proposal?
- **Player:** Oh certainly Aster. What expansion did you have in mind?
- **Aster:** Actually, your grace. I've invited an old acquaintance of mine, who is much more familiar with the subject, to discuss it with you.
- **Aster:** They arrived earlier today, you should be able to find them outside the chapel.
- **Player:** Huh, well now I am intrigued. I'll go find out what this proposal is all about. Thanks Aster.

##### Afterwards

- ((Before the town hall is built:))
  - **Aster:** This would be an excellent spot to build a town hall, your grace.
  - (continue)
- ((After the town hall is built:))
  - **Aster:** Greetings, your grace.
  - (continue)
- **Choose an option:**
  - What does a town hall do?
    - **Aster:** The finest sleep you will ever experience, your grace.
    - **Player:** Sleep? Is that all?
    - **Aster:** Your grace, a good night's sleep is the best thing you can do to improve every aspect of your life.
    - **Aster:** I daresay the effects can be felt far beyond the walls of this place.
    - You currently have [number]/[0/500/1000/1500] rested XP.
    - While logged out (even outside the fort) you will accrue rested experience, which Aster can convert into bonus experience. 1 rested XP is given every 15 minutes.
    - Every 100 rested XP spent gives as much bonus XP as a small fallen star.
    - You will also gain a small amount of rested XP while training other skills in the fort.
    - If you upgrade the town hall, you will accumulate rested XP more quickly while training and increase the cap of rested XP you can hold.
    - At tier 1, you gain 1 rested XP every 3 minutes while training in the fort, and your cap is 500.
    - At tier 2, you gain 2 rested XP every 3 minutes while training in the fort, and your cap is 1000.
    - At tier 3, you gain 3 rested XP every 3 minutes while training in the fort, and your cap is 1500.
    - (above|start)
  - ((If you have built the town hall:))
    - I want to spend my rested experience.
      - ((If you don't have enough rested experience:))
        - **Aster:** I am sorry, your grace, but you simply haven't had enough rest recently.
        - You accumulate rested experience while logged out, or while gaining regular experience in the fort.
      - ((If you have enough rested experience:))
        - ((Rested experience interface opens.))
  - I want to change my title.
    - **Aster:** Of course, your grace. How would you like to be referred?
    - **Choose your title**
      - Duke.
        - **Aster:** Very well, your grace, I shall make it known that you wish to be addressed as Duke.
      - Duchess.
        - **Aster:** Very well, your grace, I shall make it known that you wish to be addressed as Duchess.
      - Dux.
        - **Aster:** Very well, your grace, I shall make it known that you wish to be addressed as Dux.
  - Goodbye.
    - (end)
