### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Artisan at the Clan Citadel.*
- **Player:** Hello.
- **Artisan:** Oh, hello there. How can I help you?
- Select an option
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Artisan:** I'm Hercule Netis. I make all forms of beautiful art to display around this wonderful space.
    - **Player:** Netis... I've heard that name before.
    - **Artisan:** Probably my brother, Jacques. He thinks himself the best art critic in the world. Last I heard he was 'working' in Varrock's Museum - if you can call standing around work.
    - **Player:** So you're a bit of a sculptor, then?
    - **Artisan:** Among many other things, yes. I practise all of the creative arts, but I enjoy the carving of statues most of all.
    - **Player:** What other skills do you have?
    - **Artisan:** I weave tapestries; sew banners and flags; have a modicum of carpentry and stonework; can grow topiary and other plants; install water features and other pieces of art as well as carving stone statues. Oh, and I tame dragons in my spare time.
  - Tell me about customising my clan citadel.
    - **Player:** Tell me about customising my clan citadel.
    - **Artisan:** Ah! Yes, the customisation of the citadel. You will like this. I was very excited to be given the opportunity to create for you a wealth of beautiful works of art and to turn my hand to architecture as well!
    - **Player:** So what can I do?
    - **Artisan:** There are certain areas around the citadel where you can ask me to build specific things. For instance, you may wish a fountain or an expertly crafted topiary in a particular place.
    - **Player:** Just one?
    - **Artisan:** In each place for these large things, yes. As your clan contributes more resources and your citadel becomes larger, the number of places I can do this increases, and the complexity of what I can do with the materials also gets better.
    - **Player:** What about things like those pot plants?
    - **Artisan:** Ah yes, those. I spent many months growing them. Some things that you see in many places around the citadel and keep can also be changed. When you choose for me to change these things, all of them will be changed as one. This will affect things such as flags, chairs, windows and the plants you mention.
    - **Player:** Anything else I should know?
    - **Artisan:** Should the day come when you tire of a particular customisation and wish to go back to having a more... basic... choice, I will of course have to demolish the piece and start anew, so you will still need to provide me with the resources to build the item you require.
    - **Player:** How many can I build at once?
    - **Artisan:** Unlike many artists, I can multi-task on 3 projects at once. Keep resources coming and, when a project is complete, you can choose another to take its place.
    - **Player:** So how do I get the ball rolling?
    - **Artisan:** Just fill out the customisation orders. Would you like to do this now?
    - Select an option
      - Yes
        - {{tact|Opens the Clan Citadel customization menu (requires Bank PIN)}}
        - *If the player is visiting another clan's citadel:*
          - **Artisan:** You'll need to visit your own clan citadel and have the appropriate permissions to do that.
      - No, not yet thanks.
        - **Player:** No, not yet thanks.
          - {{tact|initial}}
  - Bye
    - **Player:** Bye
