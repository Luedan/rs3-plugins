##### During a Christmas event

- **Player:** Hi, Aoife! Can I help you out here with anything?
- **Aoife:** You sure can! I'm in charge of making sure the hot chocolate doesn't go bad.
- **Player:** Does it go bad that easily?
- **Aoife:** Easier than you'd think! There's a certain art to it.
- **Aoife:** Every so often, I'll call out which ingredient needs adding to the cauldron. That might be sugar, chocolate, milk, spices, or even firewood to keep the pot hot. You can find everything nearby in the kitchen.
- **Aoife:** Otherwise just make sure to keep stirring!
- **Player:** Sounds easy enough. I'll get started!

##### Outside a Christmas event

- **Aoife:** Thanks for your help this year, Player!
