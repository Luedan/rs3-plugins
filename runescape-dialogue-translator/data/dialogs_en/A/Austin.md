## Dialogue Transcript


### Before giving him a ghost impling jar

- **Austin:** The name's Austin. How d'ya do?
- ((Without a ghost impling jar in the backpack:))
  - **Austin:** I'd be doin' better if I hads one of them thar ghost impling varmints in a jar. Thank'ee kindly.
- ((With a ghost impling jar in the backpack:))
  - **Austin:** Say there, is that ghost impling for me?
  - **Player:** The one in the jar?
  - **Austin:** Yessir. The jar keeps 'em fresher for longer.
  - **Player:** What?
  - **Austin:** What?
  - **Give ghost impling in jar to Austin?**
    - Yes.
      - (removes=one ghost impling jar)
      - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
      - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
        - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
        - (continue)
      - **Austin:** Thank'ee, pardner. You got yourself a friend for...well, for death?
    - No.
      - **Austin:** Aww. I'd take good care of the little mite.

    - (continue)

    - **Austin:** Gots to have someone to talk wit'.
    - (continue)
