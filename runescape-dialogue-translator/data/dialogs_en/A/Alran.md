## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Fist of Guthix waiting area


#### Before talking to Fiara

- **Alran:** Welcome to this grand cave of Guthix, traveller.
- **Player:** So, what do I do here?
- **Alran:** Ah, you should probably talk to the organ grinder not the monkey.
- **Player:** What?
- **Alran:** Talk to Fiara, she's in charge here.
- **Player:** Which is she? None of you look female to me.
- **Alran:** No, no - Fiara is the Guardian of this place. Created by Guthix to keep a watchful eye over this holy site.
- **Player:** Guardian? You mean that weird looking giant earwig at the back of the cave?
- **Alran:** Don't refer to a holy Guardian of Guthix like that. You can call her Lady Fiara.
- **Player:** But she's not a lady, she's a giant earwig.
- **Alran:** Don't argue, just go and talk to her. And be polite.
