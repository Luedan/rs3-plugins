# Angelica

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Angelica](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AAngelica)
- **Letter**: `A`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### After [[Violet is Blue]], in Yeti Town

- **Angelica:** I'm bored!
- **Player:** Why don't you go have some fun?
- **Angelica:** NO! Tell my parents to take me home!
  - [Respond responsibly]
    - **Player:** I don't think you'd want that. This is a time to be with your family and friends. Go on, have some fun, you won't regret it.
    - **Angelica:** I regret talking to you.
  - [Respond angrily]
    - **Player:** Listen here. Your parents went through a lot of effort to get you here. The least you could do is try to look like you're having fun!
    - **Angelica:** I don't want to!
  - [Respond childishly]
    - **Player:** Ooh look at me! I'm the yeti that's too cool for the festival! I'm so awesome and cool and everyone else sucks.
    - **Angelica:** ...why are you being so mean? You...you bully! Mummy! They're picking on me!
    - **Player:** I'm not sure that was my best moment...
  - [Walk away calmly]
    - **Angelica:** I didn't like you anyway!
