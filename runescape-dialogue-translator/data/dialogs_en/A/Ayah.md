## Dialogue Transcript


### 2022

- **Ayah:** Have you seen him?
- ((Before 21 April 2022:))
  - [Menu]: Choose an option:
    - [Choice]: Who are you looking for?
      - **Player:** Who are you looking for?
      - **Ayah:** The Easter Bunny! It's that time of year and I expected him to be here.
      - **Player:** Oh no, I haven't seen him for a few years. This is a celebration of Guthix instead.
      - **Ayah:** Driven into hiding, eh? I guess that's a good thing!
      - **Player:** What happened between you two? Most people like the Easter Bunny - he brings chocolate eggs and-
      - **Ayah:** Not just any eggs. MILK CHOCOLATE EGGS!
      - **Player:** Sorry, am I missing a problem here?
      - **Ayah:** What about all the people of the world who are lactose intolerant? What about the pain and suffering he bought upon those who suffer from this ailment?
      - **Player:** That's what this is about? You're hunting the Easter Bunny because you're lactose intolerant.
      - **Ayah:** Yes! And so far so good. If you do see him though, let me know immediately. For now let me keep watch and everyone will remain safe!
    - [Choice]: What is that outfit you're wearing?
      - **Ayah:** This? Why, this is my Egg Smasher outfit. Perfect attire for hunting the Easter Bunny!
      - ((Before 14 April 2022:))
        - [You can unlock the Egg Smasher outfit by opening bonus eggs in the 'Egg Hunt' Treasure Hunter promotion, beginning on the 14th April 2022.]
      - ((After the beginning of 14 April 2022:))
        - [You can unlock the Egg Smasher outfit by opening bonus eggs in the 'Egg Hunt' Treasure Hunter promotion.]
- ((After the beginning of 21 April 2022:))
  - (above|who)

### 2026


#### Before completing Corporate Egg-spionage

- **Player:** Oh, hey Ayah-
- **Ayah:** Sssh! You're going to get me caught!
- **Ayah:** It looks like something is about to go down here soon.
- **Ayah:** Why don't you go and see if your best pal the Easter Bunny needs any help, while I keep an eye out here..
- [The Intel Egg-straction activity requires completion of the 'Corporate Egg-spionage' quest.]

#### After completing quest

- **Player:** Oh, hey Ayah-
- **Ayah:** Sssh! You're going to get me caught when I'm so close!
- **Ayah:** These spies have got to go!
- **Ayah:** Sabotaging the rabbits in amazing disguise is my thing!
- **Ayah:** Once they're gone. I'll take over the Easter Bunny's kingdom, dispose of this dairy contraband, and begin my lactose-free empire!
- **Player:** Are you still not over that?
- **Ayah:** For now my hatred of dairy is merely an undercurrent of internal rage. Until these posers are dealt with...
- **Ayah:** So go on then. Pick their pockets and get rid of any information they're getting on these 'sweet little bunnies.'
- **Ayah:** Take any Spring Tokens you find as payment.
