## Dialogue Transcript


### Before the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Player:** Hi!
- **A'abla:** Hi. How can I help?
- {{tselect|Select an option}}
  - {{topt|Can you heal me?}}
    - **Player:** Can you heal me?
    - **A'abla:** Of course!
    - {{tcond|If at full health:}}
      - **A'abla:** You look healthy to me!
      - (end)
    - {{tcond|If not at full health:}}
      - You feel a little better.
  - {{topt|Do you see a lot of injured fighters?}}
    - **Player:** Do you see a lot of injured fighters?
    - **A'abla:** Yes, I do. Thankfully, we can cope with almost anything Jaraah really is a wonderful surgeon; his methods are a little unorthodox but he gets the job done.
    - **A'abla:** I shouldn't tell you this, but his nickname is 'The Butcher'.
    - **Player:** That's reassuring.
  - {{topt|Do you come here often?}}
    - **Player:** Do you come here often?
    - **A'abla:** I work here, so yes.
    - **A'abla:** You're silly!

### After the destruction of Duel Arena

- **A'abla:** Thankfully not many were wounded in the earthquake. But I'm doing my best to look after those that were.
