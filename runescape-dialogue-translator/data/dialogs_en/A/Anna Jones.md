##### When trying to dig the statue of Saradomin next to her

- **Anna Jones:** Excuse me. I am working on that statue at the moment. Please don't touch it.
- **Player:** You are? But you're just sitting there.
- **Anna Jones:** Yes. I'm on a break.
- **Player:** Oh, I see. When does your break finish?
- **Anna Jones:** When I decide to start work again. Right now, I'm enjoying sitting on this bench.

#### After starting The Hunt for Surok (miniquest)

- **Anna Jones:** I saw what happened there.
- **Player:** What do you mean?
- **Anna Jones:** I saw Surok cast that spell at you like that.
- **Player:** What? Aren't you going to help me then?
- **Anna Jones:** Oh heavens, no! Surok has a frightful temper at times - and you probably did something to deserve it anyway.
- **Player:** Surok is a traitor to Varrock and is wanted by the Varrock Palace Secret Guard!
- **Anna Jones:** Well I don't know about any of that, but I do know that I haven't been paid yet and until I get paid, I'm doing nothing further.
- **Player:** I don't think he's coming back, you know.
- **Anna Jones:** He'd better, or I'll be contacting the palace to make a formal complaint!
- **Player:** I...I see. Well, good luck then.
- **Anna Jones:** Thank you. Goodbye!
