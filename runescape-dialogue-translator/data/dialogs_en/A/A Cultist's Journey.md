### First task


#### Talking to the Curator about A Cultist's Journey


##### Before agreeing to help

- ((If City of Senntisten has not been completed:))
  - Warning: Completion of the City of Senntisten quest is recommended to fully understand this content.
  - **DO YOU WISH TO CONTINUE?**
    - Yes
      - (continue|start-cultist-journey)
    - No
      - (end)
- **The Curator:** Hello there, World Guardian. I'm very pleased to see you! I could really do with your help.
- **Player:** What do you need help with?
- **The Curator:** It has been brought to my attention that cults devoted to the Elder Gods have been springing up all over Gielinor. Obviously, this is bizarre. Why anyone would worship such openly hostile entities is a mystery to me. I am worried that these cults may pose a serious threat. Surely no one in their right mind would dedicate their lives to the Elder Gods. It is of the utmost importance that I find out more. The problem is, my old legs aren't what they used to be, so I'm not able to go traipsing around Gielinor investigating. Please could you help me?
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - Yes.
    - **Player:** Of course, I'll help.
    - **The Curator:** Excellent! My biggest concern right now is H.A.M, a cult known for its hatred of 'monsters' - although their definition of that word seems to be somewhat loose.
    - **Player:** Yeah, we've met.
    - **The Curator:** Then I needn't tell you how reprehensible they are. It appears that their leader, Johanhus Ulsbrecht, has become obsessed with the Elder Gods. A cult dedicated to the Elder Gods could be nothing, or it could be something truly dangerous. Distasteful as it may be to speak to Ulsbrecht, he may be our only lead. I need you to go and talk to him. Last I heard, he was somewhere around the Archaeology guild shouting his nonsense from the rafters to anyone who'll listen. Humour him as long as you can stomach, and with any luck you'll find out more.
    - **Player:** I'll head there now. Wish me luck...
  - No.
    - **Player:** I'm too busy right now, sorry.
    - **The Curator:** That is very unfortunate. Please reconsider World Guardian, I will be here when you change your mind.

##### After agreeing to help

- **The Curator:** Have you spoken to Johanhus yet?
- **Player:** Not yet.
- **The Curator:** Last I heard he was hanging around the Archaeology Guild. Could you go and speak to him?
- **Player:** Sure, I'll do that.

#### Talking to Johanhus Ulsbrecht at the Exam Centre


##### First time

- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** It is all starting to make sense to me now!
- **Player:** What is?
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Oh hello World Guardian. It's about time someone around here listened to me. Recently I uncovered an enthralling mystery. It seems that there are caches of First-Age armour buried across Gielinor. I am now very close to discovering their location and solving this mystery.
- **Player:** What has drawn you to this mystery?
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** The armour was crafted in honour of the Elder Gods, the first and most powerful divine entities. Their power is unrivalled by all the other weak gods you see around here these days. Myself and my fellow H.A.M members believe that they are the answer to our problems. They will sweep clear the land of all impurities.
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - Stay quiet.
    - **Player:** I see.
    - (continue)
  - Challenge him.
    - **Player:** This is absolutely ridiculous! The Elder Gods are most definitely not the answer to your 'problems'.
    - **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Of course they are.
    - (continue)
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Since becoming aware of them, we have been working tirelessly to learn more. Ultimately, we wish to gain their favour.
- **Player:** Why would you want to do that? They're trying to destroy us all.
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Of course they aren't. I am sure they will spare their loyal followers.
- **Player:** That's doubtful... How are you planning to get their attention anyway?
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Surely discovering and returning this lost armour to its former glory would do just that. Also, my robes are looking rather tired. This is an excellent opportunity to refresh my look.
- **Player:** Hmm... I'm really not sure that's a great-
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Anyway, I must get back to work.

##### Afterwards

- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** What am I missing? If I could just figure this out...

#### Returning to the Curator and asking about A Cultist's Journey

- **The Curator:** Have you spoken to Johanhus yet?
- **Player:** Yes, I have. Johanhus believes that he has uncovered a mystery. He believes that there are caches of First-Age armour dedicated to the Elder Gods buried somewhere in the world. He thinks he is close to finding out where and intends to go and recover the armour.
- **The Curator:** Incredible. There is no evidence of humans worshipping the Elder Gods throughout recorded history. If he's correct, he may have blundered upon a find of actual historical significance.
- **Player:** I'm not sure he cares about that much. He believes that if he finds the armour then the Elder Gods will recognise him and bestow him with their favour.
- **The Curator:** Why would he want that?
- **Player:** He believes that they are going to solve all of H.A.M's problems...
- **The Curator:** The fool. The Elder Gods are vast, primordial beings whose only concern is the pursuit of perfection in creation. Why would such beings care about being worshipped, and why would they see humans as special? Clearly, we are going to need to keep a very close eye on him. Not that it should be too difficult, he seems to leave a trail of evidence wherever he goes, which I am sure to hear about from somewhere. I'm sure I'll be needing your help again soon.

### Talking to the Curator about A Cultist's Journey between A Cultist's Journey tasks

- **The Curator:** Thank you for your help so far. I am sure I will require your assistance again soon.

### Second task


#### Talking to the Curator about A Cultist's Journey


##### First time

##### Afterwards

#### Talking to Johanhus Ulsbrecht at Feldip Hills


##### First time

- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Behold! At last, I have discovered the armour.
- **Player:** I can see that...
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Isn't it incredible? Now I will surely be noticed by the Elder Gods!
- **Player:** Um... I'm not sure the Elder Gods are interested in you. Or anyone, for that matter.
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** If they believe I am showing them my devotion, they will begin to take notice and respect me. Soon they will realise what a great ally I'll be to their cause. The monsters that occupy this land will be no more.
- **Player:** Hold on, didn't you say this armour was from the First Age? How is it so pristine?
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Armour devoted to such powerful entities must be built to last. Nothing but a few scratches here.
- **Player:** Right...
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Anyway, I do not have time to stand around and chat to you, World Guardian, I have so much to do.
- **Player:** Like what, exactly?
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** I don't have time to explain. The dreams were... amazing. Such vivid images. I'm still trying to make sense of them.
- **Player:** Um...
- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** Farewell, World Guardian!

##### Afterwards

- **Johanhus Ulsbrecht:** What is it you want? I have so much to do.

#### Returning to the Curator and asking about A Cultist's Journey

- **The Curator:** Have you found Johanhus yet?
- **Player:** Yes. Johanhus has found a cache of First-Age armour in the Feldip Hills. Strangely it's in surprisingly good condition...
- **The Curator:** How interesting. Perhaps this suggests that it is not simply armour, but a product of the raw, elemental magic left behind by the Elder Gods' acts of creation.
- **Player:** He was also rambling about dreams{{Sic|..}}
- **The Curator:** Fascinating. I wonder whether these items were created by early mages, inspired by visions caused by the magical fallout that saturated the world during the First Age. If some of that magic lingers in them, it could explain why Johanhus is having some colourful dreams. We will never truly know of course. But Johanhus is making a bold, and rather ignorant assumption by claiming that the armour was created for humans who worshipped the Elder Gods. Of course the elemental nature of the armour could well mean that it is associated with the Elder Gods. But it would be delusional to assume that they would want - or even acknowledge - worship. Especially from humans. After all, to the Elder Gods we're little more than a glitch in creation.
- **Player:** Johanhus seems to believe that the Elder Gods will think he is special... He has decided that it is a good idea to wear the armour!
- **The Curator:** The man is a fool. Did you learn anything else?
- **Player:** He claimed that he was too busy to talk to me further.
- **The Curator:** Very interesting... I wonder what he is planning next. I'll be seeing you again soon, I'm sure.
