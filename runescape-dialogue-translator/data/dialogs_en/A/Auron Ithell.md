## Dialogue Transcript


### Without the skill requirements

- **Auron Ithell:** Hey, I could use some help here. Oh, actually, never mind. Sorry. Maybe once you've trained a few more skills you can help.
- To start helping to build the statue of Rhiannon, you require the following skill levels:<br> 75 [skill]

### First time dialogue, or when golden rocks are toggled off

- **Auron Ithell:** Hey, you seem resourceful! Would you like to help me with a little project?
- **Auron Ithell:** I need someone to help me track down all the parts of the statue that's supposed to sit on this plinth.
- **Auron Ithell:** When the city was reverted to seed, the statue must have been compromised. The parts could be anywhere by now!
- [Menu]: Start receiving golden rocks?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Great! From now on, while you're training any of the elf clan skills, you might find a golden rock.
    - **Auron Ithell:** If you find two rocks from the same skill, bring them here and add them to the statue plinth.
    - **Auron Ithell:** You don't have to find them all in Prifddinas. They could be anywhere, and found doing almost anything related to the clan skills.
    - **Auron Ithell:** And if you ever want to stop finding golden rocks, talk to me again and I'll do that for you.
  - [Choice]: No.
    - (end)

### Standard dialogue, when golden rocks are toggled on

- **Auron Ithell:** How's the statue going? Got any questions?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me about yourself.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Me? You want to know about me? Oh, I'm no one special.
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Auron Ithell:** Oh! I'm tending this golden statue here! It got damaged in the city's reversion and regrowing.
    - **Auron Ithell:** I'm only an apprentice, still learning.
    - **Player:** So you're just curating this piece?
    - **Auron Ithell:** Oh! Don't get me wrong, it's an honour! We all have to start somewhere. Once the statue is fully repaired, I'll get to apprentice in the Harmonium instead!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the statue.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Ah! Now that I'm happy to talk about! This is - or will be, anyway - a statue of Rhiannon! She's a very prominent figure in elven legend.
    - **Auron Ithell:** The statue is wrought out of pure gold. It's an amazing feat of sculpture - gold is so hard to work with!
    - **Auron Ithell:** Though the legend is that Rhiannon herself was actually turned to gold, and this isn't really a statue.
    - **Player:** Can you tell me the legend?
    - **Auron Ithell:** Okay, but...don't tell anyone I told you. If my master caught me 'spouting such drivel' - his words - I'd lose my apprenticeship.
    - **Auron Ithell:** The legend goes that Rhiannon was one of King Baxtorian's daughters, born out east. While growing up, she befriended a human boy who was gifted at magic.
    - **Auron Ithell:** They'd spend their time together inventing spells, using runes from a nearby cache. Over time, they became close friends.
    - **Auron Ithell:** But as they both grew older, the runes in the cache started to run low, and then all they did was argue.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Rhiannon felt the runes should be used to create spells that would benefit those around them, but the human wanted to use them to benefit themselves.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Their friendship soured, and they ended up fighting each other. Although neither enjoyed conflict, they'd had to learn to defend themselves in that turbulent age. Their battle was long and fierce.
    - **Auron Ithell:** But here's where things get hazy... Sometimes the tale ends with Rhiannon gaining the upper hand and turning the boy to stone, cursed for all time. Other times, the boy wins the duel by casting his newest spell, turning her to gold.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Honestly, though, I think it's nonsense - I just like the story! It gives the statue some character!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: About golden rocks...
    - **Auron Ithell:** Of course! You should be able to find golden rocks while training any of the skills associated with the eight main elf clans.
    - **Auron Ithell:** That's melee and slayer, magic and ranged, mining and smithing, farming and woodcutting...
    - **Auron Ithell:** ...crafting and construction, divination and summoning, dungeoneering and herblore, and agility and prayer!
    - **Auron Ithell:** Most things you can do in those skills should work.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Oh! The Voice of Seren might also help you to find some of them a little more easily!
    - **Player:** What about if I want to stop finding golden rocks?
    - **Auron Ithell:** Do you want to stop finding them? I can do that if you'd like.
    - [Menu]: Stop receiving golden rocks?
      - [Choice]: Yes.
        - **Auron Ithell:** Okay, no more golden rocks for you. If you change your mind, you know where to find me!
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: No.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do you have something to put rocks in?
    - **Player:** Do you have anything in which I could store any golden rocks I find?
    - (Condition: If the player does not have the statue collection bag:)
      - **Auron Ithell:** Of course! This statue bag should do the trick!
      - (receives=statue collection bag)
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player already has the statue collection bag:)
      - **Auron Ithell:** Oh! You've already got a statue bag - you can store golden rocks in that!
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player also does not have the Statue of Rhiannon list:)
      - [Menu]: Would you also like a list of the statue pieces?
        - [Choice]: Yes.
          - (receives=Statue of Rhiannon list)
          - [Auron gives you the Statue of Rhiannon list.]
          - (initial)
        - [Choice]: No.
          - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Auron Ithell:** Okay, bye!

### Toggle golden rocks option


#### Turning them off

- **Auron Ithell:** Aww, you don't want to help me anymore? Okay, no more golden rocks for you. If you change your mind, you know where to find me!

#### Turning them on

- **Auron Ithell:** There you go! You should start finding golden rocks while you train now!
