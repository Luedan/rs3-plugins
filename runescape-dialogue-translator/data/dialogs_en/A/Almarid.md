## Dialogue Transcript

- **Almarid:** Hi, I'm Almarid, what brings you here?
- **Player:** I'm trying to find out what's happening. Have you got a moment?
- What would you like to ask?
  - Open Countdown to Menaphos Calendar
    - *(Countdown to Menaphos interface opens)*
  - What's all this increased activity in the desert about?
    - **Player:** What's all this increased activity in the desert about?
    - **Almarid:** Rumour has spread that the great city of Menaphos will be opening it's{{Sic}} gates.
    - **Player:** How do you know this?
    - **Almarid:** We were once citizens but were exiled a long time ago. Mutterings have recently spread through some of our contacts that something's happening soon.
    - **Player:** Are you going to try and go back inside then?
    - **Almarid:** We're hoping that if we repent and change our ways, it will be viewed favourably.
    - **Almarid:** If you speak to myself or my friends each day, we'll give you some of our erm... antiquities, so we can repent our thieving ways of old.
    - **Player:** Anything else I should know?
    - **Almarid:** Well we're not sure how much we're going to give away yet but the closer we are to the city opening, the more I'll give you, just incase it helps our case.
    - **Player:** Okay, got it.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Who are you and what are you doing here?
    - **Player:** Who are you and what are you doing here?
    - **Almarid:** My friends and I were once citizens of this great city but were exiled a long time ago.
    - **Player:** Why have you returned now?
    - *(Before the opening of Menaphos.)*
      - **Almarid:** It appears the gates of Menaphos will open in *[number of days until 5 June 2017]* days!
    - *(After the opening of Menaphos.)*
      - **Almarid:** It appears the gates of Menaphos have been opened!
    - **Almarid:** We're attempting to seek forgiveness and gain re-entry to this great city.
    - **Player:** Thanks for the information.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
