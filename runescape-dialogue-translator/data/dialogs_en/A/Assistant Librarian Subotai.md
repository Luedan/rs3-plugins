## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Merchant district of Menaphos, after The Jack of Spades


#### First time claiming the journal

- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Hello there, outsider, come talk; not too loud, though!
- **Player:** What is it?
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** I was wondering if you could help me. I work at the Grand Library, and we have lost some of our records recently.
- **Player:** Lost them? How did that happen?
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Shhh! Not so loud. Well, you see, the authorities have been clamping down on almost everyone recently. They confiscated a lot of the collections in the library.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Our most important collections lost were...
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Our prized collection of Kharidian insects, both old and new.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** The old preserved in amber from the days when these lands were filled with forests; the new that irritate us on a daily basis.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** The priceless Jewels of the Elid collection, comprising of numerous precious mineral samples, and some completely unique gems which we managed to smuggle out of the library.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** In our haste we managed to lose track of some of them.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Some can be replaced with fresh samples, but the unique gemstones will need to have their current holders tracked down.
- **Player:** OK.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** And the register of honored cats of the Feline Order of Menaphos. We will need to regain the signatures of each of the cats on this list.
- **Player:** ...
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** What? Why are you looking at me like that?
- **Player:** Nothing, how can I help?
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Here, take this journal. I've put some suggestions for locating all the things in there.
- (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
  - **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Oh, please come back when you have more space to carry it.
- (Condition: With space in backpack:)
  - (receives=Menaphos journal)
  - **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Here you go, one journal!

#### Before completing the journal


##### Without having lost the journal

- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Hello, friend, have you found everything in my journal?

##### Having lost the journal

- **Player:** I've lost my collection journal; could I get a replacement?
- ((With space in backpack:))
  - (receives=Menaphos journal)
  - **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Here you go, one journal!
- ((Without space in backpack:))
  - **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Oh, please come back when you have more space to carry it.

#### After completing the journal

- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** By helping to preserve this knowledge you have done a great service to the history of Menaphos.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:** Hopefully, this new Pharaoh will be a little less hateful of knowledge, and we will be able to properly restore the Grand Library.
- **Assistant Librarian Subotai:**  But I don't know, he seems pretty suspicious.
