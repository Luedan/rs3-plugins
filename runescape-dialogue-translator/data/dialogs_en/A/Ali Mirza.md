### In Al Kharid Palace


#### After Diamond in the Rough

- **Emir Ali Mirza:** Welcome! It is good to see you again. How can I be of service, friend of Al Kharid?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about Ozan.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** Though I met Ozan only briefly, I could tell that he has a good heart.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  He has a sharp tongue too...but he is not lacking in responsibility or honour.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  But I find upon my return that he took the Kharid-ib against my father's wishes, and to rescind my father's last order would be to dishonour him.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  So, as things stand, Al Kharid must view Ozan as an traitor to be arrested upon sight.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  Personally, I hope it does not come to that. In fact, if he were ever to return the Kharid-ib, I would welcome him home as a brother.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about the Kharid-ib.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** My father did not tell me all of the Kharid-ib's secrets, but he did tell me enough to know its true value to Al Kharid.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  It may seem harsh to you that he would value it above even his own son's life, but I understand his reasons.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  Of greater concern to me is that the Menaphites, and Amascut, not only have the diamond, but also know something of it.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:**  We cannot rely on Ozan alone to return it. Osman is also doing what he can and, when the time comes, I hope we can rely upon you again too.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Menaphos.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** As a prisoner, I was privy to much that my kidnappers spoke of. I am convinced this is all part of some wider Menaphite plot.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** And I now believe that, even during the war, their primary goal has always been to obtain the Kharid-ib.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** Now that they have it, the gods only know what their next step will be.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** I shall attempt to maintain the peace as long as possible...but I have assigned Osman to look into this matter further. We will have answers.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Ask about Lady Keli/Amascut.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** Yes, it is the most troubling aspect of all of this. That the Devourer herself would directly intervene in such a way terrifies me.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** Since the end of the God Wars, the history of the desert is rife with reports of her destructive tendencies.
    - **Emir Ali Mirza:** And now she is behind my kidnap and the theft of the Kharid-ib. I only hope that whatever she is up to can be stopped in time, because whatever it is, it won't be good.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: That's all.
    - (end)
