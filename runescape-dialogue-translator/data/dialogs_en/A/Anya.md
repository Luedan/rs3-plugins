#### After Hearts of Sanguine

- **Anya:** Lovely to see you again, dear. To what do I owe the pleasure?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about your homeland?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me more about your homeland?
    - **Anya:** It is a place of breathtaking beauty and unbridled savagery. A twisted shadow of the glory it once held.
    - **Anya:** Still, it is my home, and I refuse to give up on it. I would give anything to restore what I lost.
    - (previous|standard)
  - [Choice]: How much do you know about Morytania?

    - **Anya:** I have not seen it first-hand, but I have heard much.
    - **Anya:** Apparently it is a forsaken land, where your kind live in squalor, serving as little more than cattle for House Drakan and their sycophants.
    - **Anya:** How far the great House Drakan must have fallen. Fear and misery spoils blood.
    - **Anya:** That giving willingly is the sweetest of all.
    - (previous|standard)
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about yourself?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me more about yourself?
    - **Anya:** Patience, darling. Our adventures are far from over. We will have plenty of time to enjoy each other's company.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** See you later.
    - **Anya:** Good fortune with your adventures. Please, check in when you can, won't you?
