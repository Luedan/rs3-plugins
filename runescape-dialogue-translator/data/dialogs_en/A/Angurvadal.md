## Dialogue Transcript


### Confront

- **Player:** What are you doing down here? Stop hurting the people of Um!
- Angurvadal taunts you, somehow, given that it has no face or hands.
- **Player:** You want a rematch? I'll beat you next time!
- Angurvadal calls you out, which is an impressive feat for an animated sword.
- **Player:** Yeah, well, I will be ready. Just you wait!
- If Angurvadal were to speak, you sense that it just said, 'I'm so scared' in a mocking tone.
- **Player:** *grr*
- Angurvadal turns its...hilt(?) on you and goes back to attacking the citizens of Um.
