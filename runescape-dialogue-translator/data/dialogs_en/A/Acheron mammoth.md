## Dialogue Transcript


### Challenging to a duel


#### Player's statement

*When initiating a fight with an Acheron mammoth, the player automatically says one of the following lines:*
- **Player:** {{RSChat|CHAAAAARGE!}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|En garde!}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Good sir, I challenge you to a duel.}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|I challenge thee to a duel.}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|I mock your ancestors!}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|I'd throw down my gloves, but they're rather pricey.}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Let's do this!}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Mammoth! I challenge you!}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Manners maketh man}}
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Round one: FIGHT!}}

#### Mammoth's response

*The mammoth will then respond with one of their lines respective to what the player said:*
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|That's my job!}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|Ahaa as you wish.}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|I accept your challenge!}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|I'm sorry you feel the need to throw your life away.}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|Yours are not even worthy of that.}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|You're going to need them to defeat me!}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|I trust you have a good note about how your remains will be handled?}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|Prepare to join your ancestors!}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|Are we going to stand here talking, or are we going to fight?}}
- **Acheron mammoth:** {{RSChat|Erm... yes?}}

### Other mechanics

*When the dishonourable combat debuff is triggered.*
- Your dishonourable conduct during the duel has angered the gods, who refuse to listen to your prayers any longer.
- (All prayers are disabled.)
