## Dialogue Transcript


### At the farm by Port Phasmatys


#### Before Animal Magnetism


##### Without wearing a ghostspeak amulet

- **Alice's husband:** Woo woo!

##### While wearing a ghostspeak amulet

- **Player:** Your animals don't look too healthy.
- **Alice's husband:** It's that fountain thingy in the temple to the east. It's turned them all into zombies.
- **Player:** What use are zombie animals?
- **Alice's husband:** None at all, mate, except that those worshippers at that temple keep comin' and killin' 'em all for their bones. Don't ask me why.
- **Player:** But you're a ghost - surely you know something about it.
- **Alice's husband:** I don't know nuthin' about nuthin'. Oim a simple ghost with simple needs. All I know is, years ago, that temple started glowing green and, a few months later, I woke up dead. That's all there is to it.

#### After Animal Magnetism

- **Alice's husband:** Hello, how can I help you? I'm sellin' if you have ecto-tokens.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Could I buy those chickens now?
    - **Player:** Could I buy those chickens now?
    - **Alice's husband:** I can hand over a chicken if you give me 10 of them ecto-token thingies per bird.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Could I buy 1 chicken now?
        - **Player:** Could I buy 1 chicken now?
        - (Condition: If the player has enough ecto-tokens:)
          - **Alice's husband:** Ta, some day soon I'll be havin' a nice undead bed to kip in.
          - (removes=10 ecto-tokens) (receives=undead chicken)
        - (Condition: If the player does not have enough ecto-tokens:)
          - **Alice's husband:** I'm not a charity here, ya know. Bad enough all you cow-killing folks are a'slaughterin' me beasts. Come back when ya have enough tokens.
        - (Condition: If the player has enough ecto-tokens but no inventory space:)
          - **Alice's husband:** Turnip-head, come back when you have space.
      - [Choice]: Could I buy 2 chickens?
        - **Player:** Could I buy 2 chickens?
        - (Condition: If the player has enough ecto-tokens:)
          - **Alice's husband:** Great! I'm laying away me tokens for some killer cows. That'll learn them bone rustlers.
          - (removes=20 ecto-tokens) (receives=two undead chickens)
        - (Condition: If the player does not have enough ecto-tokens:)
          - (above)
        - (Condition: If the player has enough ecto-tokens but no inventory space:)
          - **Alice's husband:** Yer sack's too small. Throw away some of that junk yer carting about.
      - {{topt|Your animals don't look too healthy, I'll buy elsewhere.}}
        - **Player:** Your animals don't look too healthy, I'll buy elsewhere.
        - **Alice's husband:** Har har, how many ghost chicky farmers d'you know, then?
  - [Choice]: Your animals don't look too healthy.
    - (above|standard)
  - [Choice]: I'm okay, thank you.
    - **Player:** I'm okay, thank you.
  - [Choice]: Where could I get more of these ecto-tokens?
    - **Player:** Where could I get more of these ecto-tokens?
    - **Alice's husband:** The ghosts I talk to say that the tokens have something to do with the tower just east of here. If you need to collect some I'd try there.

##### Using an undead chicken on him

- **Alice's husband:** I sells but I dunt be a-buyin'.

##### Using any other item on him

- **Alice's husband:** Wot use is that there stuff to a ghosty like meself?
