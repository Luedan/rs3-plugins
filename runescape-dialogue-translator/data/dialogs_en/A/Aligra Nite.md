## Dialogue Transcript

{{dialogue|npc}}

### Before [[Desert achievements|Elite Desert tasks]]

- **Aligra Nite:** Alas effendi, I fear I can not trust one such as you until you have proved your worth to the people.
- You need to claim the Elite reward for the Desert Task Set.

### Standard dialogue after [[Desert achievements|Elite Desert tasks]]

*When speaking to Aligra Nite at the Desert Quarry.*
- **Aligra Nite:** Greetings, effendi. You have granite to sell me?
- **Player:** No...why? How much would you pay?
- *If the player has granite in their inventory:*
  - **Player:** I have granite, yes... Why should I sell it to you?
  - **Aligra Nite:** Ah, excellent! Of course you want to sell it to me! That way, you do not have to ship it all the way to the markets of the north. My trusty camel and I will do that - after buying it from you, of course.
  - **Player:** How much?
  - {{tact|continue}}
- **Aligra Nite:** Ahh, only the best prices. Of course, I have my overheads and travel costs to deduct...but I can offer you a price of 1726 for medium, 818 for small and 269 for tiny. I'm sure you'll agree that's very reasonable, given my expenses.
- **Player:** That's extortion! You could sell those for five times the price!
- **Aligra Nite:** Yes, yes you could - if you were willing to ship it all there. I, however, will take it from you quick-sharp, no hassle.

### Direct selling options

*When using right-click options to "Sell all" or "Sell type" with Aligra Nite at the Desert Quarry.*
- *If using "Sell all" with Aligra Nite:*
  - Sell all granite?
    - Yes
      - {{tact|receives=coins}} {{tact|removes=all granite}}
      - **Aligra Nite:** I am glad we could do business, effendi.
    - No
      - {{tact|end}}
  - *If player has no granite in inventory:*
    - **Aligra Nite:** Alas effendi, you have no granite to sell.
- *If using "Sell type" with Aligra Nite:*
- Choose an option *(Only options for the types of granite player has in inventory will be shown, if the player only has one type, no options will be shown, and granite will be automatically sold)*
  - Sell all medium granite
    - {{tact|receives=coins}} {{tact|removes=all 5kg granite}}
    - **Aligra Nite:** I am glad we could do business, effendi.
  - Sell all small granite
    - {{tact|receives=coins}} {{tact|removes=all 2kg granite}}
    - **Aligra Nite:** I am glad we could do business, effendi.
  - Sell all tiny granite
    - {{tact|receives=coins}} {{tact|removes=all 500g granite}}
    - **Aligra Nite:** I am glad we could do business, effendi.
