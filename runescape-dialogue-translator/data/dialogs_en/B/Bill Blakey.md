## Dialogue Transcript


### Southwest of Nature Grotto

- **Bill Blakey:** How sweet I roamed from fen to fen, <br>And tasted all the Myre's pride, <br>'Till I the queen of love did ken, <br>Who in the spirit beams did glide!
- **Bill Blakey:** She shew'd me lilies in her hair, <br>And blushing roses for her brow; <br>She led me through her gardens fair, <br>Where all her silver blooms do grow.
- **Bill Blakey:** With sweet Myre dews my wings were wet, <br>And Phoebus' kiss did slowly fade; <br>She'd caught me in her silken net, <br>And trap'd me as this silver shade.
- **Bill Blakey:** She loves to sit and hear me sing, <br>Then laughing, sports and plays with me; <br>Then stretches out her silver wing, <br>And mocks my loss of liberty.
