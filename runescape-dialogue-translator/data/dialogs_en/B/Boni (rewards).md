## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Flag Fall (miniquest)

- **Boni (rewards):** Hello there, stranger from the west. What rewards can I offer you today?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: How do I claim an uncharted isle?
    - (see|Flag Fall (miniquest))
  - [Choice]: Show me your reward shop.
    - (opens=Waiko Reward Shop)
  - [Choice]: Show me the Ports reward shop.
    - (opens=Ports Reward Shop)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)

### After Flag Fall (miniquest)

- **Boni (rewards):** Welcome back. I have such wonders to show you. Stay a while and peruse my treasures.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: How do I claim an uncharted isle?
    - **Boni (rewards):** It couldn't be easier. Simply plant a flag on an uncharted isle to claim the island. If you need another flag, you can purchase one from my shop.
  - [Choice]: Show me your reward shop.
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Show me the Ports reward shop.
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
