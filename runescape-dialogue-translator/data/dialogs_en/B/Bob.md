## Dialogue Transcript


### In Lumbridge

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: cond=With a construction contract for Bob's Axes|I want to talk about my contract.
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: What do you need repairing?
      - **Player:** What do you need repairing?
      - **Bob:** Let me see, right now I need you to repair the following pieces of furniture.
      - **Bob:** [Furniture 1], [Furniture 2], [Furniture 3], [Furniture 4], [Furniture 5]
      - (previous)
    - [Choice]: What materials do I need?
      - **Player:** What materials do I need?
      - **Bob:** You need: Planks x [number], Steel nails x [number], [Additional material] x [number]
      - (previous)
    - [Choice]: Leave
      - (end)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to trade.
    - **Player:** I'd like to trade.
    - **Bob:** Great! I buy and sell pickaxes and hatchet. There are plenty to choose from, and I've some free samples too. Take your pick...or hatchet.
    - (opens=Bob's Brilliant Axes)
  - [Choice]: Can you repair my items for me?
    - **Player:** Can you repair my items for me?
    - **Bob:** Of course I can, though the materials may cost you. Just hand me the item and I'll have a look.
  - [Choice]: About the Task System…
    - (Condition: If the player has completed the Easy tasks in the Lumbridge and Draynor set and the rewards not claimed:)
      - **Player:** I've completed all the easy tasks in the Lumbridge set.
      - **Bob:** Ah, a worthy , to say the least.
      - **Player:** Thanks. Was there a reward?
      - **Bob:** A reward? Yes indeed. I can give you an improved Explorer's Ring.
      - [Bob hands you a silver ring.]
      - **Bob:** There. I think you will find this ring more suited to an adventurer of your talents.
      - **Bob:** It possesses all of your previous ring's functions, with the added bonus of an extra run energy charge as well as 30 charges of the Low Alchemy spell!
      - **Bob:** The spell will allow you to transform certain items in your inventory into coins.
      - **Bob:** You can't say fairer than free money now, eh?
      - **Bob:** Not only that, but if you decide to do some Runecrafting then wearing this ring will give you a chance of getting extra runes when crafting fire, earth, air or water runes.
      - **Bob:** I tell you, adventurers these days are spoilt rotten. Why, in my day...(mumble)...dragons...(mumble)...bare hands.
      - **Player:** Um.
      - **Bob:** Oh, sorry! I got a little distracted there. Now, as an extra reward, I have also given you a magical lamp to help with your skills.
      - **Player:** Thanks very much! What happens if I lose the new ring?
      - **Bob:** Well, I would hope that you put a bit more pride in your possessions than that. If you do lose it, come back to me and I will give you another. Now, anything else?
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player has lost their Explorer's ring 2:)
      - **Bob:** Don't tell me you lost the ring I gave you?
      - **Player:** Yes, sorry about that. I don't know what happened to it.
      - (receives=Explorer's ring 2)
      - **Bob:** Huh! Adventurers these days. No respect for valuable there anything else?
      - (continue)
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the Achievement System.
        - **Bob:** Very well: the Achievement System is a collection of deeds you may wish to complete while adventuring around the world.
        - **Bob:** You can earn special rewards for completing certain achievements; at the very least, each is worth a cash bounty from Explorer Jack in Lumbridge.
        - **Bob:** Some also give items that will help complete other achievements, and many count as progress towards the set for the area they're in.
        - [Menu]: Choose an option:
          - [Choice]: Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
            - **Bob:** For completing the Lumbridge and Draynor set, you are presented with an explorer's ring.
            - **Bob:** This ring will become increasingly useful with each difficulty level of the set that you complete.
            - **Bob:** When you are presented with your rewards, you will be told of their uses.
            - You may also see what rewards a set item provides by hovering over the Rewards icon for its Exploration Achievement set in the Achievements interface.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: How do I claim these rewards?
            - **Bob:** You need to complete all of the Tasks in a set of a particular difficulty, then you can claim your reward. Most of the Lumbridge set's Tasks are straightforward, although you might find some require quests to be started, if not finished.
            - **Bob:** To claim the Lumbridge set reward, speak to Ned in Draynor Village, Explorer Jack in Lumbridge, or myself.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: Sorry, I was just leaving.
            - (end)
      - [Choice]: Could I buy a spare of the reward?
      - [Menu]: Choose an option:
        - [Choice]: Explorer's ring 1
          - **Bob:** Explorer's ring 1? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 500 coins.
          - [Menu]: Purchase another for 500 coins?
            - [Choice]: Yes
              - [You're handed another Explorer's ring 1.]
              - (receives=Explorer's ring 1) (removes= 500 coins)
            - [Choice]: No
              - **Player:** No thanks.
        - [Choice]: Explorer's ring 2
          - **Bob:** Explorer's ring 2? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 1,000 coins.
          - [Menu]: Purchase another for 1,000 coins?
            - [Choice]: Yes
              - [You're handed another Explorer's ring 2.]
              - (receives=Explorer's ring 2) (removes= 1,000 coins)
            - [Choice]: No
              - **Player:** No thanks.
        - [Choice]: Explorer's ring 3
          - **Bob:** Explorer's ring 3? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 2,000 coins.
          - [Menu]: Purchase another for 2,000 coins?
            - [Choice]: Yes
              - [You're handed another Explorer's ring 3.]
              - (receives=Explorer's ring 3) (removes= 2,000 coins)
            - [Choice]: No
              - **Player:** No thanks.
        - [Choice]: Explorer's ring 4
          - **Bob:** Explorer's ring 4? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 10,000 coins.
          - [Menu]: Purchase another for 10,000 coins?
            - [Choice]: Yes
              - [You're handed another Explorer's ring 4.]
              - (receives=Explorer's ring 4) (removes= 10,000 coins)
