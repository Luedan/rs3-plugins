# Bellemorde

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Bellemorde](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABellemorde)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to without a Catspeak amulet

- **Player:** Hello puss.
- **Bellemorde:** Hiss!

### When spoken to with a Catspeak amulet

- **Player:** Hello puss.
- **Bellemorde:** Hello human.
- **Player:** Would you like a fish?
- **Bellemorde:** I don't want your fish. I hunt and eat what I need by myself.
