## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Fishing Platform


#### After Sea Slug

- **Player:** Hello Bailey.
- **Bailey:** Well hello again Player. What brings you back out here?
- **Player:** Just looking around.
- **Bailey:** Well don't go touching any of those blasted sea slugs.

#### After The Slug Menace

- **Player:** Hello Bailey.
- **Bailey:** Well hello again Player. What brings you back out here?
- **Player:** Just looking around.
- **Bailey:** Well, be very careful out there! Those dratted slugs have been joined by giant lobsters!

#### After Kennith's Concerns

- **Player:** Hello, Bailey, how are you doing? Is Kennith causing you any problems?
- **Bailey:** Hello again, Player. Don't worry about us; we're doing fine. I've been living over here with these sea slugs for a long time - I'm not going to have any problems with one little child.
- **Player:** Are you sure? You know he can be quite a handful.
- **Bailey:** You worry too much! Kennith and I are getting on fine. In fact, he's helping me control the sea slugs. Together we might even be able to drive the dratted things off this platform.
- **Player:** I'm glad to hear it! Anything that can help defeat the sea slugs is worthwhile.
- **Bailey:** We can but hope.

#### After Salt in the Wound

- **Player:** Hello Bailey, how are you doing?
- **Bailey:** Not so good I'm afraid. I seem to have cut my arm quite badly, and I feel like I've been punched repeatedly.
- **Player:** Uh... that's strange.
- **Bailey:** Yea, but on the upside the sea slugs seem to have gone away.
- **Bailey:** ... Maybe I had something to do with it, but the stress of my heroism made me forget...
- **Player:** Hmm... sure...
