## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Bjorn:** Hey! scheck it out! Itsh an outerlandub! Yer shud go shpeak to the chieftain!
- **Player:** The who?
- **Bjorn:** That guy over there by that stuff! (hic) Yeh, abshoultely! He's da bosh!

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Bjorn:** Hey! Itsh you again! Whatshyerfashe!
- **Player:** Player?
- **Bjorn:** Nah nah nah, not them, the other one, whatshyerfashe!
- **Player:** [Fremennik name]?
- **Bjorn:** Thatsh what I said diddle I?
- **Player:** Um.... okay. I'll leave you to your drinking.
- **Bjorn:** Thanksh pal! You're alright!
