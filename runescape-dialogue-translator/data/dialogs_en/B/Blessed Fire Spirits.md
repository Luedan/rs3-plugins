# Blessed Fire Spirits

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Blessed Fire Spirits](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABlessed%20Fire%20Spirits)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawning

- A blessed fire spirit emerges from the bonfire.

### Collecting reward

- The fire spirit will reward you with extra Firemaking XP for the next [number] logs.
- (Condition: Without sufficient space for the reward in the backpack:)
  - You capture the blessed fire spirit and it gets taken to your bank.
- (Condition: Otherwise:)
  - (receives=a fire spirit)
  - You capture the blessed fire spirit and place it in your backpack.
