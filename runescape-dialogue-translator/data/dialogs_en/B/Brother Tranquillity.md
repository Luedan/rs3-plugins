# Brother Tranquillity

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Brother Tranquillity](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABrother%20Tranquillity)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without the requirements to start The Great Brain Robbery

- **Brother Tranquillity:** Excuse me...
- **Player:** Yes? Can I help you?
- **Brother Tranquillity:** Sorry to have bothered you; I'm looking for a more experienced adventurer.

### After The Great Brain Robbery


#### On Mos Le'Harmless

- **Player:** How's your head feeling?
- **Brother Tranquillity:** A lot better, thanks. Can I help you with anything?
- (Condition: If the player has lost their Barrelchest anchor:)
  - **Brother Tranquillity:** This washed up on the beach earlier. I thought you could get it fixed by Smithing Smith in Mos Le'Harmless. It could be valuable.
  - (receives=barrelchest anchor)
  - **Player:** Thanks.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Otherwise:)
  - **Player:** Not really. I was just popping by to see how you were all getting on and possibly help to cull some of the zombies.
  - **Brother Tranquillity:** Your concern is most gratifying. Saradomin be with you.
  - (continue)
- **Brother Tranquillity:** Would you like me to take you to Harmony?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - {{qact|The player is teleported to Harmony Island.}}
  - [Choice]: Not at the moment, thanks.
    - **Player:** Not at the moment, thanks.
