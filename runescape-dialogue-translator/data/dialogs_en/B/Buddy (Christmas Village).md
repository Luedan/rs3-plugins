## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- (Condition: If the player is transmogrified:)
  - {{Qact|The player returns to themselves.}}
  - (continue)
- **Buddy:** So, ye wanna look like me do ya?
- **Player:** No? I never said that.
- **Buddy:** I 'erd you did.
- **Player:** No, you di-
- {{Qact|Buddy transmogrifies the player into a random variant of snow imp.}}
- **Buddy:** Beauty!

### Transmog


#### When not transmogrified

- {{Qact|Buddy transmogrifies the player into a random variant of snow imp.}}

#### When transmogrified

- {{Qact|Buddy transmogrifies the player back into themselves.}}
