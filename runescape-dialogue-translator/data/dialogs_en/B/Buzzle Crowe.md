## Dialogue Transcript


### Spot


#### During the event


##### First time

- (receives=100 nectar)
- (see|Nectar)

  - [You spot a bee and find 100 Nectar!]
  - (continue|wonder)
- (Condition: If other bees have been previously spotted:)
  - [You spot another bee and collect 100 Nectar!]
  - (continue|wonder)
- (Condition: If Leslie has not been asked about spotting bees yet:)

##### Afterwards


##### With more bees to spot

- **Player:** I've already spotted this {{Sic|Bee}}!

##### Without more bees to spot

- **Player:** I've already found all of the bees!

#### After the event
