## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to

- (Condition: If the player's Fremennik name is Bardur:)
  - (Condition: If the player is male:)
    - **Bardur:** Ah, Bardur my brother-in-name! It is good fortune that we meet here like this!
  - (Condition: If the player is female:)
    - **Bardur:** Ah, Bardur my sister-in-name! It is good fortune that we meet here like this!
- **Player:** Hello there Bardur. Are you having fun?
- **Bardur:** I have been here all the long day, slaughtering the daggermouth spawns so that none may pass!
- **Bardur:** My sword arm grows weary, and the blood of my foes hangs heavy upon my clothing!
- **Bardur:** OF COURSE I AM HAVING FUN!!!
- **Player:** Can you tell me anything about the caves down here?
- **Bardur:** Aye, that I can [Fremennik name]! Yonder lies the lair of the Daggermouth Kings, the three beasts of legend!
- **Bardur:** I train my skills here upon its foul brood, to prepare myself for the fight ahead!
- **Player:** Okay, thanks Bardur. Do you have any food with you?
- **Bardur:** Ah, you did not come prepared [Fremennik name]?
- **Bardur:** I have nothing to spare, but my equipment grows weaker under the onslaught of the daggermouth spawns!
- **Bardur:** I will trade you a finely cooked shark if you have a replacement for my helm, my shield or my blade...
- (Condition: If the player does not have a Fremennik blade, Fremennik shield, or Fremennik helm:)
  - **Player:** Sorry, I don't have anything you'd be after...
- (Condition: If the player has a Fremennik blade, Fremennik shield, or Fremennik helm:)
  - **Bardur:** Give me any equipment you wish to trade, and I will pay you a shark for it.

### Using a Fremennik blade, Fremennik shield, or Fremennik helm on Bardur

- **Bardur:** Ah, just what I was looking for! You wish to trade me that for a cooked shark?
- [Menu]: Trade with Bardur?
  - [Choice]: YES
    - (receives=one shark)
    - **Bardur:** Ah, this will serve me well as a backup! My thanks to you [Fremennik name], I trust we will one day sing songs together of glorious battles in the Rellekka long hall!
  - [Choice]: NO
    - **Bardur:** As you wish, [Fremennik name] My weapons have lasted me this long, I will keep my trust in them yet.
