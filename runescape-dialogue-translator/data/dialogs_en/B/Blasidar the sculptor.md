# Blasidar the sculptor

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Blasidar the sculptor](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABlasidar%20the%20sculptor)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Giant Dwarf

- **Blasidar the sculptor:** Hello!
- **Player:** Ho there! Who are you?
- **Blasidar the sculptor:** Only the most famous sculptor in all of Keldagrim.
- **Player:** Does Keldagrim have a lot of sculptors then?
- **Blasidar the sculptor:** Well... I'm the best outside of Keldagrim too, I tell you! Especially here in the north.
- (Condition: If the player has also completed Mountain Daughter:)
  - **Player:** Yeah, maybe you should visit the mountain people topside of this mountain some time... all they have is a big lump of stone!
  - **Blasidar the sculptor:** Ah, the philistines!
  - **Blasidar the sculptor:** But I'd rather not go up there, they don't like dwarves much.
  - **Player:** Oh yes, good point.
- (Condition: Otherwise:)
  - **Player:** Sure, sure, of course.

### After The Giant Dwarf

- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Could you change my gravestone, please?
    - **Player:** Could you change my gravestone, please?
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** Certainly, if you can afford to pay for the materials! I'm sure there'll be something in that catalogue to suit your taste.
    - ((Gravestones interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: The weather's lovely, don't you think?
    - **Player:** The weather's lovely, don't you think?
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** Um... I really wouldn't know. The weather doesn't really interest us much down here in Keldagrim.
    - **Player:** I suppose not. I was just trying to make conversation.
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** Every human I meet eventually tries asking me about the weather! Surely you could find something interesting and relevant to say.
    - **Player:** What had you in mind?
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** Um...
    - **Player:** Er...
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** The new statue looks beautiful, don't you agree?
    - **Player:** Oh yes, quite.
    - **Blasidar the sculptor:** My finest piece of work, without a doubt.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
