## Dialogue Transcript


### In Rimmington


#### Standard dialogue

- **Brian:** Would you like to buy some archery equipment?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: No thanks, I've got all the archery equipment I need.
    - **Player:** No thanks, I've got all the archery equipment I need.
    - **Brian:** Okay. Fare well on your travels.
  - [Choice]: Let's see what you've got then.
    - (opens=Brian's Archery Supplies)

#### Yak to the Shadows

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Life of Brian
    - **Player:** I've brought you five nature runes.
    - ((If the player doesn't have the runes:))
      - **Brian:** I don't see any.
      - **Player:** I'll go find some.
    - ((If the player has the runes:))
      - (removes=five nature runes)
      - **Brian:** Err... thanks.
      - **Brian:** I don't really understand why you people keep giving me these.
      - **Player:** At least you're getting free stuff!
      - **Brian:** That's true. Always look on the bright side of life, I suppose.
  - [Choice]: Talk about something else
    - (above|Standard dialogue)
