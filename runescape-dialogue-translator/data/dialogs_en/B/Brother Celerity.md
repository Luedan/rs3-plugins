## Dialogue Transcript


### First conversation

- **Brother Celerity:** Oh, woe is me! Lamentations! Woe!
- **Player:** Are you alright, brother?
- **Brother Celerity:** I don't think so. I don't think I will ever be alright again...
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Brother Celerity:** I have been sent a vision. A most terrible vision indeed!
- **Brother Celerity:** Demons are beginning to push through into our world in unprecedented number.
- **Brother Celerity:** I have seen fields of bones...rivers of blood and brimstone!
- (Condition: If you have slain a Demon in Demon Flash Mob)
  - (receives=Malleus daemoniorum)
  - **Brother Celerity:** I sense that you have slain a demon general. This is your reward!
  - (continue|questions)
- (continue|questions)

### Additional conversations

- **Player:** Good day, brother. How are the visions?
- **Brother Celerity:** Still calamitous in the extreme! But what can I do for you?
- ((If the player has lost the Malleus daemoniorum:))
  - ((Without space in backpack:))
    - **Brother Celerity:** While I can sense that you have lost the book I gave you, you lack the space to carry a replacement.
    - **Brother Celerity:** If you make some space in your pack, then I will give it to you.
    - (continue|questions)
  - ((With space in backpack:))
    - (receives=Malleus daemoniorum)
    - **Brother Celerity:** Where is the book I gave you? Here, take another, quickly!
    - (continue|questions)
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - I need to ask you about these demons.
    - **Brother Celerity:** Of course. Ask away.
    - **SELECT AN OPTION**
      - What do the demons want?
        - **Brother Celerity:** I have consulted some of the most ancient tomes of demon slaying and I believe I have the answer.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Zamorak and Zaros are testing the boundaries of the world and drawing power to themselves.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Both of them command demonic legions, and now they are mustering their power.
        - **Brother Celerity:** They are sending forth powerful dukes and princes; commanders and generals.{{Sic|&nbsp;&nbsp;}}These fell foes are weakened by the passage into our world.
        - **Player:** That shouldn't make them too difficult to deal with...
        - **Brother Celerity:** Would that were the case, young one, but worse is to come.
        - **Brother Celerity:** The generals come with heralds and lieutenants, which they drain for power and sustenance. Once revitalised, they can rampage at will!
        - **Brother Celerity:** Every death they inflict empowers their dread masters. Oh, woe and calamity!
        - (previous2|questions)
      - What can I do to help?
        - **Brother Celerity:** Alone you will not stand a chance, my child. Alone you would be but food for the hungry monsters!
        - **Brother Celerity:** But if you gathered enough strong and stout companions, you might stand a chance.
        - **Brother Celerity:** The demons are weak, but blood and suffering heal them. If you can dispatch them quickly - before they are recovered - then their lords will be diminished also.
        - **Brother Celerity:** I have also heard it said that in the ancient times, when Zaros and Zamorak moved the lands openly, Saradomin blessed the slayers of such demons with magical armour.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Not only did it make the wearer deadly to demonkind, it also helped them learn the inner mysteries of the combat arts.
        - (previous2|questions)
      - What kind of demons are they?
        - **Brother Celerity:** They are the most powerful of demonic generals. Each of them bears both a title and an honorific to show the dark favour of their masters.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Generals and executioners are more deadly in combat, while castellans are harder to kill. Worse of all are the deacons, whose fists can set flesh aflame!
        - **Brother Celerity:** Worst of all, these vile beings can stun a man with the force of their blows, and a single strike can shatter rock, bathe a man in hellfire or entomb him in ice.
        - **Brother Celerity:** If such disaster were not enough, the beasts will drain power from their companions to become ever stronger with every second!
        - **Brother Celerity:** Mark my words and remember: you must slay the companions to stop the general becoming too powerful, and you must defeat them all before he will stoop to bloodying his fists upon you!
        - (previous2|questions)
      - Where can I find the demons?
        - **Brother Celerity:** If you are strong enough, you will hear the call of Saradomin to go and defeat the monsters.
        - **Brother Celerity:** They tend to appear in the most barren and out-of-the-way areas; places of poison, rot and death.
        - **Brother Celerity:** They favor the Wilderness most highly as a result.
        - **Brother Celerity:** But be warned! They drink of death like fine wine, so do not allow the bloodlust of that place to overcome you!
        - **Brother Celerity:** Every death in their presence, be it from their own hands or those of marauders, will cause them nothing but joy!
        - (previous2|questions)
      - I should probably go.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Be wary of the demons, my child! Be wary!
  - Can I ask you something else?
    - **Brother Celerity:** Certainly. What can I do for you?
    - **SELECT AN OPTION**
      - Who are you?
        - **Brother Celerity:** I am but a humble monk, although I have occasionally been stricken with visions such as these in the past.
        - **Brother Celerity:** I believe I have been chosen as a conduit for Saradomin to warn others of impending disasters.
        - **Brother Celerity:** My brothers do not listen to me much, however, as I was once struck down with the most fearsome ergotism. They fear my mind has become addled as a result.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Thankfully, the clouds of shrieking bats that live under my bed assure my I am well again, and that the visions I have are true.
        - **Player:** Um...
        - **Brother Celerity:** I have also spent many long hours poring over arcane tomes held in the monastery. From this I have learned much of the demon slayers of Saradomin and the threat we face.
        - (previous|questions2)
      - Is there a reward for killing the demons?
        - **Brother Celerity:** Well, the obvious reward is that you will become a spiritual protector of the world.
        - **Player:** But what else can I get out of it?
        - **Brother Celerity:** The demons will come arrayed with a collection of minions which will drop various items on death.{{Sic|&nbsp;&nbsp;}}In addition, the demon general will no doubt have quite valuable items that can be looted.
        - **Brother Celerity:** In addition, it is said that in the 3rd Age, Saradomin blessed demon slayers with a suit of magical armour that made them more deadly to demons.
        - **Brother Celerity:** There is a chance that should you help to slay the beast then you may be rewarded with a piece of this legendary armour yourself!
        - (previous|questions2)
      - ((Only if you have already asked "Who are you?" or "Is there a reward for killing the demons?":))
        - Can you tell me about more about these slayers?
          - **Brother Celerity:** From my researches in the fabled {{Sic|Malleus Daemonum}}, the demon slayers were a group of elite warrior-monks dedicated to Saradomin.
          - **Brother Celerity:** Where they walked the unclean fled, for each victory made them stronger in faith, and their blows shattered demons like the most fragile of clay pots.
          - **Brother Celerity:** For every demonic general they vanquished, Saradomin would occasionally invest in them a new item of armour to gird themselves with.
          - **Brother Celerity:** In this way they became stronger for each foe they pulled down, until even the generals of the Infernal Dimensions trembled when they took to the field.
          - **Brother Celerity:** However, with the banishment of Zaros and the Edicts of Guthix, the demons were sent away and the order waned into obscurity.
          - (previous2|questions)
      - I should probably go.
        - **Brother Celerity:** Be wary of the demons, my child! Be wary!

### Using [[Ruined essence]] on him

- **Brother Celerity:** Oh dear. I have read about this day, but I thought that it would not come within my lifetime.
- Brother Celerity produces a tiny box, which he seems hesitant to open.
- **Brother Celerity:** You must take this. I must take that. It is how it was written.
- **Brother Celerity:** Please. You must.
- **Player:** Well, if I must.
- (removes=Ruined essence)
- (receives=Creepy doll)
- Brother Celerity hands you the box, from which you withdraw an odd doll.
