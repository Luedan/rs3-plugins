## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Banker:** Hi there, can I help you with anything?
- What would you like to say?{{anchor|init}}
  - Is this a bank?
    - **Player:** Is this a bank?
    - **Banker:** Why yes, what else would it be? Anything else?
    - What would you like to say?
      - Do you get many customers?
        - **Player:** Do you get many customers?
        - **Banker:** We're the only bank on these two islands, so business is pretty good. Anything else?
        - (initial|init)
      - I'd like to access my bank account, please.
        - (below)
      - I'd like to check my PIN settings.
        - (below)
      - I'd like to see my Collection box.
        - (below)
  - I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - **Player:** I'd like to access my account, please.
    - **Banker:** Certainly.
    - *(Bank account opens)*
  - I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(PIN settings open)*
  - I'd like to see my Collection box.
    - *(Collection box opens)*
