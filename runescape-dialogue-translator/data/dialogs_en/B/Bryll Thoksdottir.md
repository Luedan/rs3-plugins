## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley

- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** I seek heroes to assist us in the siege of Daemonheim. Will you give us your aid?
  - Yes, please.
    - *(Transports you to the Daemonheim peninsula)*
  - What is Daemonheim?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Far to the east lies a dungeon complex which is the source of a dark and strange power. We are helping bands of heroes explore this complex to unearth and deal with this force.
    - (previous)
  - What dungeoneering recommendations do you have for me?
    - *(Dungeoneering challenge interface opens)*
  - Can you help me with the dungeoneering outfits?
    - **Player:** Can you help me with the dungeoneering outfits?
      - *If the player can't receive Dungeoneering fragments and hasn't unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces:*
        - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** At level 70 you can start to collect fragments to make elite skilling outfits. When you get to that level you will automatically start collecting fragments. Then you can use them with a blueprint to make yourself any remaining elite outfit pieces for this skill.
      - *If the player can receive Dungeoneering fragments but hasn't unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces:*
        - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** You are set up to receive fragments. When you get to level 80 you will be able to create outfit pieces.
      - *If the player can receive Dungeoneering fragments and has unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces:*
        - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** You are set up to receive fragments. When you have 3600 fragments you can use them to create an elite outfit piece. Simply go to an Invention workbench to create the pieces.
      - *If a full set of the Gorajan trailblazer outfit is owned:*
        - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** You have already created all of the possible outfit pieces. I will make sure you do not receive any further fragments. Remember you can combine the base outfit pieces to make the high tier versions, to get the additional benefits.
  - Not right now, thanks.
    - (end)

### At Daemonheim

- Tell me about yourself.
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** I'm Bryll, you might know my family, Thok, Thok and Marmaros.
  - **Player:** Thok...and Thok?
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Yes, both of them.
  - **Player:** Which one is Thok?
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** He's my brother of course.
  - **Player:** What about Thok?
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** That's my dad.
  - **Player:** Umm....
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Dear old Daddy Thok, I have missed him greatly since he passed.
  - **Player:** Wait... Thok's dead?
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** No not Thok you numbskull. Thok!
  - **Player:** Oh, so there are two Thoks.
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** You are pretty slow, aren't you?
  - **Player:** I just thought that your brother and father were... ...Never mind.
  - *(Shows initial options)*
- Goodbye.
  - **Player:** Bye, Bryll.
  - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Goodbye, Player.

### On Aminishi

- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Hello there, did you come to help me with the temple?
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Have we met before? My name is Bryll Thoksdottir, I'm a Fremennik explorer.
    - (previous)
  - Why are you here?
    - **Player:** Why are you here?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** The Fremennik are following the example of our greatest hero, to seek out the seeds of evil and destroy them and to aid the oppressed. While the main bulk of our band is trying their hardest to uproot the horrors of Daemonheim I'm travelling to investigate the rumours of places that might require a heroic intervention.
    - Bryll wipes her brow.
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** It's so hot here. I'm not used to this.
    - (previous)
  - What help do you need?
    - **Player:** What help do you need?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Since the Wushanko Isles became accessible again after the number of sea monsters dropped I've poked my nose around the Arc. The Acolytes of Seiryu stationed in the temple on the top of the hill worship a guardian beast of the sea and work to better themselves through a strict training regimen. While that's admirable there's a rumour that those at the top have been acting strangely and no-one's been able to see the guardian beast for a long time. Perhaps you and a couple of friends could investigate for me?
    - (previous)
  - What's in it for me?
    - **Player:** What's in it for me?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** You've already aided us in exploring Daemonheim and by helping me you can work on training your Dungeoneering skill here too. I'll award you tokens for your efforts. You can trade your tokens back at Daemonheim with my brother, Marmaros. He's got a lot of stock that can benefit you on your adventures. If you're not confident in your combat abilities and still want to investigate you could probably find a way to traverse the temple safely, but I can't reward you for that.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Farewell. I'm not going anywhere, should you want to want to come back later to help.

### At the Dragonkin Laboratory


#### Opening the Reward Shop for the first time

- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Player, I have something for you.
- *(Shows the lucky charms.)*
- These will give you a chance at rare loot in the elite dungeon 'Dragonkin Laboratory'.
- *(Shows the shifting tombs reward enhancers.)*
- These will give you bonus rewards if you take them to the 'Shifting Tombs' minigame in Menaphos.
- *(Shows the vital spark drop enhancers.)*
- These will give you bonus vital spark drop whenever you receive one.

#### When talked to without having completed Temple of Aminishi

- **Player:** Hello again.
- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Oh, hello, Player. You've caught me at a bad time, I'm struggling to find a way deeper into these ruins. It might take some time. Did you ever solve the mystery of that temple on Aminishi?
- **Player:** Not yet. Perhaps I'll come back later.

#### When talked to after having completed Temple of Aminishi

- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Oh, hello again, Player. I heard you freed the guardian spirit of Aminishi from a terrifying curse. Very heroic, you did a great thing!
- **Player:** What are you doing all the way out here, Bryll?
- **Bryll Thoksdottir:** I've heard rumors of more misdeeds carried out in this area. Some horrible, torturous experiments. So I was scouting the place out. I just managed to fix the lift mechanism that goes down into the ruins. Maybe you could go explore for me? Perhaps with a group of others.
- Bryll Thoksdottir
  - What's down there?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Lots of dragons. I wasn't down there for long, so I just grabbed what I could. I haven't been able to go very far in but I could see a vast cavern filled with ruined buildings and volcanic currents. The place is abandoned, apart from the wild creatures. I can't tell when they left, though. Magmatic activity is unpredictable. It might have been inhabited recently or be totally ancient.
    - **Player:** Did you spot anything significant?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Hmm, in the distance there was something that looked like a huge magic shield. I could only see it from certain angles. Perhaps it's protecting something valuable? Do be careful if you get close.
    - (previous)
  - Have you explored yet?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** To be honest I don't think I could handle it alone. I was hoping you'd offer to travel deeper inside.
    - (previous)
  - Isn't it dangerous around here?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** I was quite cautious, made sure I wasn't followed. Even brought a camouflage potion. We should be safe around here, but be careful if you head back west. Some people are likely to try and kill you out there.
    - (previous)
  - What can I get for exploring here?
    - **Bryll Thoksdottir:** Well you'll be able to train your Dungeoneering skill some more by defeating monsters down there. I'll award you some reward tokens for taking out the powerful ones too. You can spend them on some of Marmaros's stock back at Daemonheim. I've heard a couple rumors about some powerful combat techniques too. Perhaps there's some record of them here.
    - (previous)
  - I have to go.
    - (end)
