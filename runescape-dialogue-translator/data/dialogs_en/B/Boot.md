## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Dwarven Mine


#### Talking to him

- **Boot:** Hello traveller.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Hello there, what's going on back here
    - **Player:** Hello there, what's going on back here?
    - **Boot:** Just taking a break. This section of the mine was stripped of ore long ago.
    - **Player:** I'll leave you to it then, farewell.
  - [Choice]: Why are you called
    - **Player:** Why are you called Boot?
    - **Boot:** I'm called Boot, because when I was very young, I used to sleep, in a large boot.
    - **Player:** Huh, was that comfortable?
    - **Boot:** I don't remember much from back then, but apparently I didn't complain.
