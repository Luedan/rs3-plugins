## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Tower of Life

- **Player:** Hi there.
- **Bonafido:** A'up, how's it gowin'?
- **Player:** Not bad, thank you.
- **Player:** What are you building here?
- **Bonafido:** Nowt at moment. We're on strike!
- **Player:** You're on strike? Whatever for?
- **Bonafido:** Those strange alchemists are up ta somefin', best go ta one ov 'em.
- **Player:** Oh, okay.

### After Tower of Life

- **Player:** You still hangin' around the tower?
- **Bonafido:** Yeah, we need another job. But til then, we figured we may as well wait ta see what else would turns up.
- **Player:** Fair enough.
