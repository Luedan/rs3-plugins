## Dialogue Transcript

- **Bane:** Hey, welcome to Heathervein. You wanna buy some ores?
- [Menu]: Wanna buy some ores?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - *(Bane's Vein Exchange interface opens)*
  - [Choice]: Wasting no time on the sales pitch.
    - **Player:** Wasting no time on the sales pitch, huh?
    - **Bane:** Hey, you came into my shop. Why waste time?
    - **Player:** What sort of stock do you carry?
    - **Bane:** Well, most of the others mine that millipede all day, which has let me carve out a niche of dealing in...
    - **Bane:** Your bog-standard, plain, old, regular ores.
    - **Bane:** I've even got a bit of trade set up with a fella named Gidon over in Wendlewick...or rather, did before all this madness.
    - **Player:** Oh, I know Gidon!
    - **Bane:** Oh, well, in that case...just don't tell his sister about our little deal, okay?
