## Dialogue Transcript


### On board the ''Lady Zay''

- **Player:** What are you cooking?
- ** 'Beefy' Burns:** My speciality! What else could I be cooking?
- **Player:** Ok, and your speciality is...?
- ** 'Beefy' Burns:** Boiled shark guts with a hint of rosemary and a dash of squid ink.
- **Player:** I think I'll stick to making my own food.
- ** 'Beefy' Burns:** Your loss!
