# Bones (kitten)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Bones (kitten)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABones%20%28kitten%29)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to without a Catspeak amulet

- **Player:** Who's a cute little kitty?
- **Bones:** Miaow!

### When spoken to with a Catspeak amulet

- **Player:** Who's a cute little kitty?
- **Bones:** Jimmy doesn't like me talking to strangers.
