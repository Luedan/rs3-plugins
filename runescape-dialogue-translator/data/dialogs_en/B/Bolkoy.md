# Bolkoy

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Bolkoy](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABolkoy)
- **Letter**: `B`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Tree Gnome Village

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Bolkoy:** Hello stranger, new to these parts? I'm Bolkoy by the way. I'm the village shopkeeper. Would you like to buy something?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: What have you got?
    - **Player:** What have you got?
    - **Bolkoy:** Take a look.
    - (opens=Bolkoy's Village Shop)
  - [Choice]: No thank you.
    - **Player:** No thank you.
    - **Bolkoy:** Ok, maybe later.

### After Tree Gnome Village

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Bolkoy:** Welcome, welcome. It's good to see you again. The village is much safer now you have returned the orbs.
- **Bolkoy:** By the way, I'm the village shop keeper. Would you like to buy something?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: What have you got?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: No thank you.
    - (above)

### Treasure Trails

- **Bolkoy:** Hello! I take it you're here about the trail?
- **Player:** Yes. Do you have a challenge for me?
- **Bolkoy:** Yes, right here!
- (receives=challenge scroll)
- [Bolkoy has given you a challenge scroll!]

##### When talked to again

- **Bolkoy:** Please enter the answer to the question.
- (Condition: When given the wrong answer:)
  - **Bolkoy:** What's the matter with you? Can't you count? Try harder!
- (Condition: When given the correct answer:)
  - **Bolkoy:** Lucky for you, that's the right answer!
  - (continue)
- (removes=the challenge scroll and the old clue scroll (medium))
- ((If the trail continues:))
  - (receives=a new clue scroll (medium))
  - [Bolkoy has given you another clue scroll!]
- ((If the trail ends:))
  - (receives=reward casket (medium))
  - [You've found a casket!]
