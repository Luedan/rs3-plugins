# 'Black-eye'

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:'Black-eye'](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3A%27Black-eye%27)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Tower of Life

- **Player:** What's going on?
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Ah, we be building this 'ere tower. Lookin' good ain't it?
- **Player:** It does look pretty impressive, but what are you doing hanging around?
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Tea break, naturally.
- **Player:** Naturally. Not sure why I asked.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Fancy some?
- **Player:** Tea? Sure.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** You'll find a kettle in the box to the-
- **Player:** Erm...yeah...no...sorry...gotta go.

### After Tower of Life


#### If the player has the hard hat in their player owned house

- **Player:** Say, that's a nice helmet. I've got one just like it in my house.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Well I am so very pleased!

#### If the player has the hard hat in their bank

- **Player:** Say, that's a nice helmet.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Thank you - just as nice as the one in your bank!

#### If the player has the hard hat in their backpack

- **Player:** Say, that's a nice helmet.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Thank you - just as nice as the one you're carrying I'd say.

#### If the player has the hard hat equipped

- **Player:** Say, that's a nice helmet.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Why, thanks. Some would say as nice as the one you're wearing!

#### If the player has lost their hard hat

- **Player:** Say, that's a nice helmet.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Why, thanks.
- **Player:** I was just wondering if you might be able to help me out with one?
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Always glad to help a budding builder.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':**  But first, prove your Construction knowledge by answering some questions.
- **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** How many nails does it take to make a rocking chair?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: One
    - **Player:** One?
    - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** One? How do you suppose to use a single nail?
  - [Choice]: Five
    - **Player:** Five?
    - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Nope. Keep guessing!
  - [Choice]: Three
    - **Player:** Three?
    - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Bingo! Okay, now what takes 3 planks, 3 cloths and 3 nails to make, and helps remove light from a room?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Torn curtains
        - **Player:** Torn curtains?
        - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Nice one. Last question: I like fish and I want to put some in my garden, but I need a special water feature. What materials would I require?
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: 8 clay pieces and 3 limestone bricks
            - **Player:** 8 clay pieces and 3 limestone bricks?
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Oh, and you were so close!
          - [Choice]: 10 clay pieces
            - **Player:** 10 clay pieces?
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** That's it!
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** You seem to know your stuff. I got a spare helmet from a builder that died on... I mean, err, had to leave the job for greener pastures.
            - **Player:** Greener pastures?
            - **Player:** Fair enough.
            - (receives=hard hat)
            - Try the beckon emote while wearing an item of builders' clothing!
            - **Player:** Wait a minute! Is there blood on this helmet?
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Erm, no, that's just, erm, paint. Yes, paint! That's what it is!
            - **Player:** Oh, okay. Thanks. Bye.
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Ha, gullible fool.
            - **Player:** What?
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Nothing.
          - [Choice]: 10 limestone bricks
            - **Player:** 10 limestone bricks?
            - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Guess again!
      - [Choice]: A brown rug
        - **Player:** A brown rug?
        - **<nowiki/>'Black-eye':** Of course, because that's what everyone needs - a rug up at their window.
      - [Choice]: Opulent curtains
        - **Player:** Opulent curtains?
