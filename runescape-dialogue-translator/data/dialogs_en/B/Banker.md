## Dialogue Transcript

{{External|os=Banker/Dialogue|rsc=Banker/Dialogue}}

### Normal dialogue

- **Banker:** Good day. How may I help you?
- (Condition: If the player has items waiting for them in the Grand Exchange:)
  - **Banker:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - (opens=Bank PIN)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see my collection box.
    - (opens=Grand Exchange collection box)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Banker:** This is a branch of the Bank of Gielinor. We have branches in many towns.
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: And what do you do?
        - **Player:** And what do you do?
        - **Banker:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - [Choice]: Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Player:** Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Banker:** Yes we did, but people kept on coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.

### If talking to a Banker in Lletya or Prifddinas

- **Banker:** Good day. How may I help you?
  - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - *(Bank account opens)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(PIN settings open)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to see my collection box.
    - *(Collection box opens)*
  - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Banker:** This is an elven bank. The magic in this place allows you to access your account here in the western lands.

### In Lumbridge with the CS Week clue scroll

- **Banker:** Well solved! Don't forget to talk to me about a bank PIN to secure your stuff{{Sic}}
- You're handed a [clue scroll/pair of caskets].

### In Menaphos before unlocking the bank

- **Banker:** I require Ehsan's blessing to allow you to use the bank here.

### Banker on [[Etceteria]]

- **Banker:** Hi there, can I help you with anything?
  - **Player:** Is this a bank?
    - **Banker:** Why yes, what else would it be? Anything else?
      - **Player:** Do you get many customers?
        - **Banker:** We're the only bank on these two islands, so business is pretty good. Anything else?
        - *(Previous options)*
      - *(Previous options)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - *(Bank account opens)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(PIN settings open)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to see my collection box.
    - *(Collection box opens)*
