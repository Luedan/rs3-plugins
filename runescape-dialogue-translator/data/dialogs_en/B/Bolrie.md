## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Tree Gnome Village, after The Prisoner of Glouphrie

- **Bolrie:** Greetings, Player. I was...rude to you in the dungeons of Arposandra, and never gave you the thanks you deserved. What can I say - they were an old man's prejudices. Thank you.
- **Bolrie:** A warning, though: this isn't over. I'm going to find another way into Arposandra to have my revenge, if my legs will carry me. Blast it, I'll learn to glide there if I have to! When that day comes, I may ask for your help.
- **Player:** I'll help, you can count on that. You're not the only one who wants to get to the bottom of this.
