## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When talking to the Bow and Arrow salesman in the Ranging Guild.*
- **Player:** Hello.
- **Bow and arrow salesman:** A fair day, traveller. Would you like to see my wares?
- Select an option
  - Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
      - (opens=Dargaud's Bows and Arrows)
  - I'd like to ask you about magic crossbows.
    - **Player:** I'd like to ask you about magic crossbows.
    - **Bow and arrow salesman:** Ahh crossbows. Not exactly what I'd call skilled ranging, but I guess dwarven engineering is good, what did you want to know?
    - **Player:** The dwarves don't work with magic logs, I'm wondering if you know how to make a crossbow out of them?
    - **Bow and arrow salesman:** I don't... however I will look into it when I get spare time, for now you'll have to stick with the dwarven way of doing things.
    - **Player:** Ok, perhaps one day in the future I'll get to make magic crossbows!
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Bow and arrow salesman:** Okay good day to you.
