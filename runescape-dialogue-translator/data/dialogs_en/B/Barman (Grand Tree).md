## Dialogue Transcript

- **Barman:** Good day to you. What can I get you to drink?
- Select an option
  - What do you have?
    - **Player:** What do you have.
    - **Barman:** Here, take a look at our menu.
    - (opens=Blurberry's Bar)
  - Nothing thanks.
    - **Player:** Nothing thanks.
    - **Barman:** Okay, take it easy.
  - Can I buy some ingredients?
    - **Player:** I was just wanting to buy a cocktail ingredient actually.
    - **Barman:** Sure thing, what did you want?
    - Select an option
      - A lemon
        - **Player:** A lemon.
        - **Barman:** 20 coins please.
        - (removes=20 coins) (receives=lemon)
      - An orange.
        - **Player:** An orange.
        - **Barman:** 20 coins please.
        - (removes=20 coins) (receives=orange)
      - A cocktail shaker.
        - **Player:** A cocktail shaker.
        - **Barman:** 20 coins please.
        - (removes=20 coins) (receives=cocktail shaker)
      - Nothing thanks.
        - **Player:** Actually nothing thanks.
