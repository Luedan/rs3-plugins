## Dialogue Transcript

- **Bart:** What can I get you?
- **Player:** Do you have any spirits?
- **Bart:** I've got spirits, cocktails, ales... Do you want something?
- **Open shop?**
  - Yes please.
    - (opens=The Last Call)
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Bart:** I'll be here all night.
  - ((Only if Vessel of the Harbringer has been completed:))
    - Do you sell nettle tea?
      - **Player:** Do you sell nettle tea?
      - **Bart:** Nettle tea? I bet Netty over there has been talking your ear off over it.
      - **Player:** Just a bit.
      - **Bart:** To answer your question though, I'm afraid all of the nettle teas are reserved for Netty only.
      - **Player:** Oh. Okay.
