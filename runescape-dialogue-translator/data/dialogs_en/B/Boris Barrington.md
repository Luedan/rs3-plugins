## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Boris Barrington:** What can I do for you?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Can I rest here?
    - **Player:** Can I rest here?
    - **Boris Barrington:** I suppose.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Boris Barrington:** Oh... I lived in Meiyerditch. The Vyrewatch forced me to play music here. They did not enjoy it, of course, but they liked to see me suffer. Even in death, I am forced to remain.
    - **Player:** That's very sad.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: That's all, thanks.
    - **Player:** That's all, thanks.
