### Fort Forinthry


#### Talk to


##### Not building the Grove


##### For the first time before talking about Oak

- **Player:** Hello Bill, how is everything?
- **Bill:** Good, good. Those new outer fortifications have been holding up well and nothing new has come up since we got those in place.
- **Bill:** Although actually...
- **Player:** Yes Bill, is something wrong?
- **Bill:** A visitor arrived earlier today to speak with you.
- **Player:** I see, well if the guards allowed them in, then that doesn't seem too concerning?
- **Bill:** Yes, your grace, but since they arrived they've been very fixated on the tree outside the chapel.
- **Player:** Hmm, I see. Thanks Bill, I'll go see what they're so interested in.

##### Afterwards

- **Select an Option**
  - Where did the lumber yard go?
    - **Bill:** I miss my lumber yard.
    - **Player:** What happened to it?
    - **Bill:** Land got reclaimed.
    - **Bill:** Bank of Varrock couldn't wait to get their hands on the land. All to restore a bit of green to the King's Road and to pave a way for construction supplies to be bought here.
    - **Bill:** Rigged if you ask me...
    - **Player:** Seemed to have found yourself back on your feet though. You're the head of this workshop!
    - **Bill:** Suppose it isn't all bad.
    - (previous)
  - How do I build here?
    - **Bill:** You need to start off by bringing all the materials listed on any of the blueprints on this table here.
    - **Bill:** Once you've selected a blueprint to start, we'll put up the structure you'll need to build at, but you'll be the one to do all the construction.
    - **Bill:** Each building has a set of hotspots that line the walls. One of these hotspots will always be the optimal hotspot to build on and doing so will retain your 'Perfect build' multiplier as well as faster build progression.
    - **Bill:** Once you've finished a building, you can then either reinforce that same tier to train for Construction experience, or you can tier up your building for increased benefits and bonuses.
    - (previous)
  - What does building the workshop do for me?
    - **Bill:** The workshop holds all the equipment you need to work on the fort. You can process planks and stone here.
    - **Bill:** As you upgrade the workshop you will find building around the fort easier and more rewarding. With tier 1, any experience from Construction blueprints will be increased by 5%.
    - **Bill:** At tier 2, we should have the resources to setup remote access to the invention guild's machines from the workshop. You'll also earn 10% increased experience from Construction blueprints.
    - **Bill:** At tier 3, we will overcharge the remote invention machine access to enhance the power capacity of your machines by 25%. You'll also earn 15% increased experience from Construction blueprints.
    - (previous)
  - I don't really feel like talking anymore.
    - **Bill:** Well, am always here if you need to talk.
  - Do you still have construction supplies?
    - (below|Planks)

##### Building the Grove


##### First time

- **Player:** Hello Bill, I'm in need of some new plans being drawn up if you have the time.
- **Bill:** Oh, what are we looking to put together this time?
- **Player:** Nothing too exciting to begin with I'm afraid. We need to reinforce the border with the wilderness, to the east of the fort.
- **Bill:** Right okay, that should be simple enough. I can adapt one of the existing wall plans right now, this won't take long.
- **Player:** Fantastic, thanks Bill.
- **Bill:** ...and done! That should keep anything unwelcome from sneaking around from the east.
- **Player:** Perfect, great work Bill, I'll work on getting this constructed shortly.

##### Creating the blueprint


##### Before making the blueprint

- **Bill:** The plans you needed for that new wall segment are ready to go, just take a look at the table when you're ready to begin construction.
- **Player:** Thanks Bill, much appreciated.

##### After making the blueprint and before constructing the eastern border wall

- **Bill:** The materials are all in place by the eastern wilderness border outside, ready for construction when you have time.
- **Player:** Thanks Bill, much appreciated.

##### After speaking to Oak about completing the eastern border wall

- **Bill:** Hello, how are the new fortifications looking?
- **Player:** They're looking great, good job with getting those plans together so quickly.
- **Bill:** Thanks, adapting one of the existing wall plans wasn't too difficult.
- **Player:** Now that the area is secure, {{Sic|The}} plan is to use it to provide the fort with its own supply of lumber.
- **Bill:** Its own supply of lumber... ?
- **Player:** That's right, with the land to the-
- **Bill:** Like a lumber yard... ?
- **Player:** Oh no, this won't be a ya-
- **Bill:** If only we knew someone who owned a lumber yard.
- **Player:** Ha, I know how it might seem bu-
- **Bill:** A lumber yard just down the road even, which could provide all the lumber the fort requires.
- **Player:** Okay Bill, I know how it might look.
- **Player:** It's not actually a lumber yard we're building though.
- **Bill:** Oh... it's not?
- **Player:** No, not at all, this is about providing a space to grow trees.
- **Bill:** Oh, I see. Sorry about that then, it seems I may have got slightly carried away there.
- **Player:** Not a problem Bill, always good to find some humour where we can.
- **Bill:** How can I help then, your grace.
- **Player:** We're going to need plans for a small cabin. Nothing too grand, just a comfortable space for our grove tender Oak to reside.
- **Bill:** Certainly I'll get some plans drawn up now. We should be able to start construction when ever you're ready.
- **Player:** Thanks, Bill.
