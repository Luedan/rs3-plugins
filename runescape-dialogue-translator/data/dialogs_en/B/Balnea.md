## Dialogue Transcript


### Before As a First Resort

- **Player:** Hi there!
- **Balnea:** I'm ever so busy at the moment; please come back after the grand opening.
- **Player:** What grand opening?
- **Balnea:** I'm sorry, I really can't spare the time to talk to you.
- **Player:** Uh, sure.

### After As a First Resort

- **Balnea:** Hi, Player. What can I do for you today?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: So, how's business?
    - **Player:** So, how's business?
    - **Balnea:** Word seems to be spreading and, now that we've launched, we're beginning to drill down and strategize about long-term bench-marking and sure-fire value-adding propositions.
    - **Balnea:** Would you like to be involved in some horizon scanning? We could use someone with your talent for joined-up thinking and can-do ideation as we upsize this venture to the next level.
    - **Player:** Ah, yes. Now I remember why I was never going to talk to you again.
  - [Choice]: What did you say about hunting baby platypodes?
    - **Player:** What did you say about hunting baby platypodes?
    - **Balnea:** If you have the skill to care for it, you can catch a baby platypus and raise it as a pet.
    - **Balnea:** They need special care, so you can't have more than one at a time, and you can't catch one if you are unable to care for it.
    - **Balnea:** If you do manage to catch one, try feeding it raw fish. They seem to love that.
    - **Player:** Thanks for the info!
