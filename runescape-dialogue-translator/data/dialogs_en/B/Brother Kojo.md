## Dialogue Transcript


### After [[Observatory Quest]]

*After talking to the Observatory professor about Treasure Trails*—*with a coordinate clue in inventory*
- **Player:** Hello.
- **Brother Kojo:** Hello, traveller. How can I help?
- **Player:** I'm trying to learn how to be a navigator.
- **Brother Kojo:** I don't know if I can help you there.
- **Player:** The professor from the Observatory says that I need a watch.
- **Brother Kojo:** Ah, that I can help you with. I've been tinkering with the new idea of a watch and made a few. The problem is, the villagers don't see the point as they have the Clock Tower!
- **Player:** Can I have one?
- **Brother Kojo:** You can have this one! It's the display model.
- Brother Kojo has given you a watch.
