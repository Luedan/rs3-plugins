## Dialogue Transcript


### When using a limestone brick on him

- **Bricks:** Oh thank you, human! But it's not enough, I'll never have enough bricks.

### When a Trinket is received

- **Bricks:** Wait! Take this, it washed up on shore. It's not a brick so I don't want it, but maybe it's of some use to you.
- *Bricks gives you a delicate looking trinket.*
- **Player:** Errm, thanks.
