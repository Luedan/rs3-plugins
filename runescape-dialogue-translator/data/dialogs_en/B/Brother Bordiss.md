### At the Power Station, after Perils of Ice Mountain

- **Brother Bordiss:** Hello again!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I still don't understand why Ice Mountain was heating up.
    - **Player:** I still don't understand why Ice Mountain was heating up.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** No, I suppose I never did get a chance to explain it. It's something I learned from the druids of Guthix. It's about the balance of the atmosphere.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** Sunlight causes the world to heat up but, normally, excess heat radiates upwards into the sky and away from RuneScape. So, the land remains at a constant temperature.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** Different gases trap heat more or less well. The smoke from the dragon power station trapped heat very well, more so than normal air.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** You've had a taste of what would happen if the gases increased. Plants would die - except it wouldn't just be Monastery roses, it would be crops people needed to eat.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** Creatures would lose their habitat and go extinct - not just vulnerable icefiends, but all sorts of creatures.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** Heat would make water expand and ice melt, so sea levels would rise, and that would mean coastal cities flooding, as well as drowning underground cities like Keldagrim.
    - **Brother Bordiss:** If it were to go unchecked, it would mean the end of dwarven civilisation. Human civilisation too. Thank Saradomin you stopped it in time.
  - [Choice]: How is the windmill doing?
    - **Player:** How is the windmill doing?
    - **Brother Bordiss:** Quite well, actually. It looks like my estimates were all correct and the wind here is quite enough to power Nurmof's machine.
