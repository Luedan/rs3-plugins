## Dialogue Transcript


### As the crew of Sarim's Revenge


#### Port Sarim

- **Bosun Higgs:** Why are we just sitting here? Come on, let's get sailing.

#### Waiko

- **Bosun Higgs:** Captain, while we're docked, I'll see about getting some repairs done.
- **Player:** Anything you need from the markets, Evie?
- **Bosun Higgs:** How about some information on my brother's whereabouts? This isn't a holiday.
- **Player:** You're all about business, aren't you?
- **Bosun Higgs:** I'm Kinshra. Discipline is why we're a better fighting force than those stodgy White Knights.
- **Player:** I'm surprised, then, that the Black Knights let you leave to search for your brother.
- **Bosun Higgs:** Er, yeah, about that...
- **Bosun Higgs:** Actually, it's nothing. Forget I said anything.
- **Player:** Evie?
- **Bosun Higgs:** I said forget it.

#### Whale's Maw

- **Bosun Higgs:** Well, this place is a dump.
- **Player:** Why, what's wrong with it?
- **Bosun Higgs:** There's nothing here {{nbsp}}it's just the world's saddest beach resort, scattered with a few shipwrecks, a few stupid turtles and some lecherous fishermen.
- **Navigator Jemi:** Yeah, they make me uncomfortable too.
- **Quartermaster Gully:** Urgh, tell me about it.
- **Bosun Higgs:** It's taking everything I've got not to stab their eyes out.
- (Condition: If the Head of the Family miniquest has been completed:)
  - **Lookout Ekahi:** Eye, me too. It's not nice to stare.
- (Condition: If Flippers McGraw has been recruited:)
  - **Flippers McGraw:** Well, I like it! It makes me feel sexy.

#### Aminishi

- **Bosun Higgs:** You gotta respect these dragon monks. They are crazy good fighters. Shame they're all a bunch of utter lunatics.
- **Bosun Higgs:** If Zamorak found this lot, he could turn them into an effective fighting force.
- **Player:** Yeah, that's not why we're here, Evie.
- **Bosun Higgs:** I know, just saying. Still, the crazy part of them makes them more likely to follow Saradomin, don't you think?
- **Player:** Let's not bring politics into this, shall we? Leave these people to their own issues. The gods either can't come here or don't want to.

#### Cyclosis

- **Bosun Higgs:** Now, I'm just pitching an idea here, cap, but... Armoured cyclopes with mounted ballista turrets. Think it over. We'd rule the world.

#### Tuai Leit

- **Bosun Higgs:** This looks like a nice place to work on my tan. Watch out for thoose gnomes, though. They look a cheerful sort, but they'll stab you in the back when it's turned.
- **Player:** Sounds like you have a story to tell.
- **Bosun Higgs:** Not untill I get to know you a whole lot better. That's a tale I prefer to keep to myself.

#### The Islands That Once Were Turtles

- **Bosun Higgs:** So, I heard some turtles died here, or whatever. Can we go now?

#### Goshima

- (Condition: Before completing the Final Destination miniquest:)
  - **Bosun Higgs:** Stay sharp, cap. I hear something moaning out there, and not the good kind of moaning. There's something lurking around the gates of Goshima, and it doesn't look friendly.
- (Condition: Afterwards:)
  - **Bosun Higgs:** Ding dong, the khan is dead. Zip-a-dee-doo-dah. Can we go look for Jacob now?

### Menaphos

- **Bosun Higgs:** If the Black Knights had leaders like her, those White Knight fools would fall over themselves trying to be first to surrender.
