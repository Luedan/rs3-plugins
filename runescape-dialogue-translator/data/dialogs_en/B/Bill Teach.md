### After Cabin Fever

*First time speaking after Cabin Fever*
- **Player:**  Can I have that gold now?
- **Bill Teach:**  Sure ye can. Here.
*If the player has lost their book of piracy*
- **Player:**  I lost the book that Bill gave me.
- **Bill Teach:**  He warned me ye be a squid-fingered jellyfish of a pirate. If I gets ye another copy will ye keep a better eye on it?
- **Player:**  Yarp?
- **Bill Teach:**  Then here, have this other copy I dug up.
**1. Speak about Rocking Out.**
- **Bill Teach:**  That cage turned up in my pack last night, Player. It scared me silly!
- **Player:**  Wow, it must be magical!
- **Bill Teach:**  Take it back, lad. I fear it might be somethin' fer an adventurin' soul like you.
- *(Bill Teach hands you the magical cage)*
- *(The next dialogue takes place when you own the magical cage)*
- **Player:**  Now that I know what this is all about, what should I do?
- **Bill Teach:**  I don't know. I suppose we wait te see what he does next.
- **Player:**  I can't accept that. He could cause some real trouble next time.
- **Bill Teach:**  Well, I'm sure ye'll come up with somethin'.
**2. Speak about A Clockwork Syringe.**
- **Bill Teach:**  There be a storm brewin', Player. I hope I can count on ye when we come te weather it.
**3. Talk about something else.**
- **Player:**  Cap'n, I lost that book you gave me.
- **Bill Teach:**  Ye'll notice I'm not fallin' to the floor in shock right now.
- **Bill Teach:**  Look, if I gives ye another copy will ye keep a better eye on it?
- **Player:**  Aye Cap'n!
- **Bill Teach:**  Then here, have this other copy I dug up.
*(If you already have the book o' piracy, only the next line will show up)*
- **Bill Teach:**  If yer after a lift go to the ship and I'll take ye where ye want to go.
*(If you are in the basement below the bar in Mos'Le Harmless, only the next line will show up)*
- **Bill Teach:**  There be a storm brewin', Player. I hope I can count on ye when we come te weather it.
