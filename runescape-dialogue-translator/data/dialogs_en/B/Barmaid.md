## Dialogue Transcript

- **Barmaid:** Welcome to the Laughing Miner pub, human traveller.
- Choose an option:
  - Who is that man walking around outside?
    - **Player:** Who is that man walking around outside?
    - **Barmaid:** What man?
    - **Player:** I mean the dwarf, the one with the sign.
    - **Barmaid:** Oh, him. Yes, we employ him to advertise our pub, he's the cheapest labour we could find. We don't have a lot of money to spare you know, we pay him in beer.
    - **Player:** But what's wrong with him?
    - **Barmaid:** Well, he's drunk isn't he? I told you, he's cheap. He couldn't get any other work since the Red Axe fired him. Been drinking ever since.
    - **Player:** How did that happen?
    - **Barmaid:** I'm not quite sure, I think he kept wearing the wrong coloured cap all the time. They don't much like it if you don't wear your red uniform into work.
    - **Player:** They fired him for that??
    - **Barmaid:** The Red Axe will fire you for just about anything if they want to.
  - I'd like a beer please.
    - **Player:** I'd like a beer please.
    - **Barmaid:** That'll be 2 gold coins.
    - Select an option
      - Pay.
        - ''If the player has two coins:
          - (removes=2 coins) (receives=beer)
          - **Barmaid:** Thanks for your custom.
        - ''If the player doesn't have two coins:
          - You can not afford that.
      - Don't pay.
        - **Player:** Sorry, I changed my mind.
  - I'd like some food please.
    - **Player:** I'd like some food please.
    - **Barmaid:** I can make you a stew for 20 gold coins.
    - Select an option
      - Pay.
        - ''If the player has 20 coins:
          - (removes=20 coins) (receives=stew)
          - **Barmaid:** Thanks for your custom.
        - ''If the player doesn't have 20 coins:
          - You can not afford that.
      - Don't pay.
        - **Player:** Sorry, I changed my mind.
  - I have to go.
    - **Player:** I have to go.
