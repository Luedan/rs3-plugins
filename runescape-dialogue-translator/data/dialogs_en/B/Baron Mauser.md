#### With the amulet in backpack

- [To talk to cats, you need to wear your catspeak amulet.]

### With a catspeak amulet equipped


#### Before completing Cat's Bane, without the curdled milk in backpack

- **Player:** I don't know how you even got up here, but you can't stay.
- **Baron Mauser:** You do not know whom you address, peasant! This is MY town, and I go where I please. At least I would if it wasn't so tiring. Leave me!
- **Player:** But I need to, um, clear you out...sir, if you see fit.
- **Baron Mauser:** Hmm, I suppose a deal could be struck. Bring me milk! A bucketload should suffice. Do this and I shall venture forth.
- **Player:** A bucket of milk, is that all? <br>Where can I get some?
- **Baron Mauser:** A dairy cow resides within the cow pen beside the east bank, but cats fear that place. Now go, get my milk!

#### Milking the undead dairy cow with a bucket

- ((Shown in a pop-up:))
  - You milk the cow.

#### Before completing Cat's Bane, with the curdled milk in backpack

- **Baron Mauser:** And about time too, peasant! Ah, lovely curdled milk - just what I require.
- Before you can say anything, the cat grabs the milk and gracelessly waddles off in the direction of New Varrock's graveyard...
- (Condition: If the player still has cats to assist:)
  - You have made progress in the 'A Cat's Bane' Task. ([number]/4)
- (Condition: If the player has assisted all four cats:)
  - {{RSFontAchievement|Cat's Bane}}
  - **Player:** I wonder why they all headed off towards the graveyard...
