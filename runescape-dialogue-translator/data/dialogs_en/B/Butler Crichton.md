## Dialogue Transcript


### After Carnillean Rising

- **Butler Crichton:**  'Ello again, mate.
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: What happened to the old butler?
    - (Condition: If the player sided with the Carnilleans in Hazeel Cult:)
      - **Butler Crichton:**  You know more than me. Butler Jones got arrested after you exposed him as a spy.
      - **Butler Crichton:**  Still locked up somewhere, I suppose, but we needn't worry about him.
      - **Butler Crichton:**  Ceril might have tried other butlers after 'im; I don't really know. Still, he seems happy enough with me.
      - (other)
    - (Condition: If the player sided with the cultists in Hazeel Cult:)
      - **Butler Crichton:**   Butler Jones? He quit his job and left. Said his 'work here was complete', whatever that means. Weird chap.
      - (other)
  - [Choice]: What do you know about the Carnilleans?
    - **Butler Crichton:**  The Carnilleans are one of Ardougne's richest families. They're snooty, but there's no harm in them.
    - **Butler Crichton:**  They're bound to have a book on their history somewhere in the house, if you want to know more.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: You don't talk like a butler.
    - **Butler Crichton:**  Oh, I save all the formal stuff for Sir Ceril and the family.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Actually, never mind.
    - (end)
