## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley, after Wolf Whistle

- **Player:** Hello again, Bowloftrix. How are you doing?
- **Bowloftrix:** Well I can’t really complain.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me all about yourself.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Wow...uh...that’s kind of put me on the spot.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Why do you want to know all about me? I mean, you saved me and all, but I’m hardly very interesting.
    - **Player:**: Please, just humour me.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Fine...I’m a druid. I, you know, worship Guthix and nature.
    - **Bowloftrix:** I like long walks in the forst and eating vegan food. I mean, what else do you need to hear?
    - **Player:** Well I was hoping you might have an interesting story to tell me, or a quest for me to do or something.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Well, do many people have those?
    - **Player:** You would be surprised.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What do you think of Taverley?
    - **Bowloftrix:** I think it is ok. I’m a druid. My family has lived here for generations. In the end I’ve never known anything different.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Well, except that time I went on a fishing trip to Rimmington.
    - **Player:** Well, what did you think of it?
    - **Bowloftrix:** Meh. Not enough trees.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: How do you like being Pikkupstix’ assistant?
    - **Bowloftrix:** Guthix preserve me, this is the most harrowing thing that I have ever done!
    - **Player:**: More harrowing than being kidnapped by trolls?
    - **Bowloftrix:** By miles!
    - **Player:** That’s scarily impressive.
    - **Bowloftrix:** I mean, you hear stories, right? About how such-and-such fell down the stairs, or accidentally nailed their hand to a table.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Then you get there, and things don’t seem so bad. Before you know it, the guy you’re being trained by gets blasted to ashes by a backfire summon!
    - **Player:** Gosh!
    - **Bowloftrix:** He’s a walking disaster magnet, but it’s never him! He never gets a single scratch, or is anywhere near things when they go south!
    - **Player:** That’s terrible!
    - **Bowloftrix:** Uh, you wouldn’t mind taking this position for a while, would you? Just while I get myself healed up?
    - **Player:** Not a chance.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Well, time to go.
    - **Bowloftrix:** Be well.
