# Brigand

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Brigand](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABrigand)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Feud

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Brigand:** What do you want?
- **Player:** Well nothing really, I'm new to town and wanted to get to know the locals.
- **Brigand:** I'm too busy for chitchat. Come back if you have some business to discuss with us.

### After The Feud

- **Brigand:** You dog! You killed our champion and for that you will die.
- {{qact|The brigand attacks the player.}}

### Pickpocket

- (Condition: If the player is caught)
  - **Brigand:** {{RSChat|What do you think you're doing?}}
  - You fail to pick the bandit's pocket.
  - You've been stunned.

### Lure

- **Player:** Psst.  here, I want to show you something.
- (Condition: If the brigand is fooled:)
  - **Brigand:** What is it?
  - **Player:** It's a surprise! Follow me.
- (Condition: If the brigand is not fooled:)
  - **Brigand:** Go away! I'm busy.

### Knock-Out


#### If there is another brigand or bandit around

- Perhaps I shouldn't do this just yet. I think another bandit will see me.

#### If the player does not have a blackjack equipped

- **Player:** I need a different weapon to do this. <br/> I want to knock them out, not kill them.

#### If the player has a blackjack equipped

- {{Qact|The player hits the brigand over the head with the blackjack. The brigand falls to the ground.}}
- You smack the bandit over the head and render them unconscious.
- **Brigand:** {{RSChat|Zzzzzz}}
- {{Qact|The brigand gets up.}}
- **Brigand:** {{RSChat|Arghh my head.}}
