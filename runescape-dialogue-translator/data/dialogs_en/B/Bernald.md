## Dialogue Transcript

- **Bernald:** Do you know anything about grapevine diseases?

- **Bernald:** Oh, that's a shame. I hope I find someone soon, otherwise I could lose all of this year's crop.

### After Garden of Tranquility

- **Player:** How are your grapes coming along?
