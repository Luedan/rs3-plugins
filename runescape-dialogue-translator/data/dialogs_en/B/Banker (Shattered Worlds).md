# Banker (Shattered Worlds)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Banker (Shattered Worlds)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABanker%20%28Shattered%20Worlds%29)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Player:** Hello. I'd like to access my bank.
- **Banker:** Of course.
- (opens=Bank)

### On the free-to-play worlds

- **Banker:** I'm sorry, you're not permitted to do that.
- You can only access this bank from a members world.
- (end)
