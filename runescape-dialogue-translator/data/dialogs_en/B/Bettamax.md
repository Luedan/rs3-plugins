## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley

- **Bettamax:** Hello there.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Hello. Who are you?
    - **Player:** Hello. Who are you?
    - **Bettamax:** My name is Bettamax, I’m a botanist in training.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What’s a botanist?
        - **Player:** What’s a botanist?
        - **Bettamax:** Someone that studies and identifies different plants and their properties.
        - **Player:** What are you studying?
        - **Bettamax:** Currently nothing. I’m waiting for Astlayrix, my mentor, to come back from the herblore habitat.
        - **Player:** Habitat?
        - **Bettamax:** Oh, it’s fascinating. There are all these new creatures and plants and, well, I’m probably not the best person to explain it…
        - **Bettamax:** If you want to go see for yourself, I have a small stock of these weird teleport-bag things. They’ll take you straight there.
        - [Menu]: Would you like to buy one?
          - [Choice]: Yes.
            - (opens=Bettamax's Shop)
          - [Choice]: No.
            - **Player:** Maybe another time.
      - [Choice]: What are you studying?
        - (above|studying)
      - [Choice]: Can I see your shop?
        - (opens=Bettamax's Shop)
  - [Choice]: What’s that walking next to you?
    - **Player:** What’s that walking next to you?
    - **Bettamax:** Oh, that’s Wilbur. He’s a jadinko.
    - **Player:** What’s a jadinko?
    - **Bettamax:** I have no idea.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Can I see your shop?
