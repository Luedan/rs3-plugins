# Blanchy

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Blanchy](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ABlanchy)
- **Letter**: `B`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without catspeak

- **Blanchy:** Meow, meow meow.

### With catspeak

- **Player:** Oh, hey, friend.
- **Blanchy:** I'm not your friend, buddy.
- **Player:** I'm not your buddy, pal.
- **Blanchy:** I'm not your pal, mate.
- **Player:** I'm not your mate, chum.
- **Blanchy:** I'm not your chum, guy.
- **Player:** I'm not your guy, friend.
- *(Returns to the second line)*
