## Dialogue Transcript


### Benedict's World Tour


#### Talking to Benedict


##### First time

*While not using the NXT client*
- **Benedict:**<span style="line-height:20px;"> I'm afraid my wispy eyes can't see well enough to see if you have a trustworthy face.</span>
- <span style="line-height:20px;">This content is only available through the NXT client.</span>
*While using the NXT client*
- **Benedict:{{anchor|intro}}** Greetings, Madam/Sir Player. My name is Benedict Encumberyak, and I am in search of a helpful adventurer. I have served my master since I was a mere pup. However, since I have grown old and wearisome, I have found myself in a bit of a pickle. I appear to have mislaid several of my master's possessions. I need to reclaim these before my master decides that I am a bother! What say you, Madam/Sir Player - will you help an old pack yak with this little favour?
  - Yes, I'd be happy to help
    - **Benedict:** Fabulous! I made a scrapbook of my sketches from my travels, I wonder if I dropped the items nearby. Please be thorough when searching the area!
    - *Benedict Encumberyak hands you his scrapbook.*
    - *(returns to previous options)*
  - What's in this for me?
    - **Benedict:** Well, Madam/Sir Player, my master has a tendency to find all manner of junk that he likes to keep around. He likes to make me carry around this junk, so I'd need to offload them onto you so I have space to take his missing possessions back. I hope you don't mind Madam/Sir Player, they're just these old dusty lamps. I'm sure they won't be missed!
    - **Player:** Useless dusty lamps, you say? I'd be happy to take those off of your hands!
      - *(Returns to previous options)*
  - Who is your master?
    - **Benedict:** Well, Madam/Sir Player, I have served my master since I was a young pup, and we pack yaks live for a long time. That's all I can say...
      - *(Returns to previous options)*
  - No, I don't have time for this!
    - **Benedict:** That is a shame, Madam/Sir Player. Please return to me if you do wish to help.
  - *After agreeing to help*
    - <span style="font-weight:normal;">Good bye</span>
      - **Benedict:** See you soon, Madam/Sir Player.

#### After finding eight items (variant 1)

#### After finding the first eight items

- **Benedict:** Oh Madam/Sir Player, you've found the first 8 items on the list! Please, take this lamp in exchange for your hard work.
- Benedict Encumberyak hands you an old lamp.
  - *On a free-to-play world*
    - **Benedict:** Madam/Sir Player, I'm not sure you'll be able to find the remaining locations...
    - The remaining lost items can only be found on a members' world.

#### After finding twelve items

- **Benedict:** Madam/Sir Player, we're almost there, you've now found 12 of my master's possessions! I have more useless old lamps you can have in return - my master will never run out of these.
- Benedict Encumberyak hands you an old lamp.

#### After finding all items

- **Benedict:** Oh, Madam/Sir Player, you've done it! Please, take more of these useless lamps!
- Benedict Encumberyak hands you some old lamps
- **Benedict:** You've done it! Thank you Madam/Sir Player for finding my master's most valuable possessions! Let's keep this little mishap between us, hmm?

#### After completing the search

- **Benedict:** You've done it! Thank you Madam/Sir Player for finding my master's most valuable possessions! Let's keep this little mishap between us, hmm?
