## Dialogue Transcript


### Keldagrim

- **Bentamir:** Do you mind? You're in my home.
- **Player:** I'm sorry, should I have knocked?
- **Bentamir:** Very funny, human.
- **Bentamir:** We can't all live in plush houses, you know. But that doesn't mean us mining dwarves don't work hard.
- **Player:** Where do you do your mining?
- **Bentamir:** Normally the coal mine to the north. We need a lot of coal to keep our steam engines going, you know.
- **Player:** Can I do a bit of mining there as well?
- **Bentamir:** I'm not sure, but as long as no one notices I don't think anyone is going to care.
