## Dialogue Transcript


#### Talk to

- ((Before the Chapel has been built:))
  - **Father Flint:** This faithless place needs a chapel. Here will do.
  - (continue)
- ((After the Chapel has been built:))
  - **Father Flint:** Come in, come in.
  - (continue)
- **Select an Option**
  - What does a chapel do?
    - ((Before the Chapel has been built:))
      - **Father Flint:** We need an altar, of course, and burners for incense.
      - (continue)
    - ((After the Chapel has been built:))
      - **Father Flint:** We have an altar, of course, and burners for incense.
      - (continue)
    - **Father Flint:** You can always just pray at altars of course, but personally I like to pile up bones on them as offerings. Very pious.
    - **Father Flint:** If you burn herbs in the burners, the offerings are even more efficient! Everyone loves efficient offerings.
    - You can burn any type of herb at the chapel. Doing so grants extra experience while training prayer by offering bones or ashes, and occasionally saves offered materials.
    - All players on the same world contribute to a shared duration of burning herbs.
    - Upgrading the chapel improves your experience, the duration of herbs, and saves even more materials.
    - At tier 1, offerings are worth 250% experience. You have a 3% chance to save offerings. Each herb contributed by a player adds 15 seconds of benefit.
    - At tier 2, offerings are worth 300% experience. You have a 6% chance to save offerings. Each herb contributed by a player adds 18 seconds of benefit.
    - At tier 3, offerings are worth 350% experience. You have a 10% chance to save offerings. Each herb contributed by a player adds 21 seconds of benefit.
    - At tier 3, you can switch prayer books here.
  - Which god do you worship, anyway?
    - **Father Flint:** That would be... an ecumenical matter.
  - Goodbye.
    - (end)
