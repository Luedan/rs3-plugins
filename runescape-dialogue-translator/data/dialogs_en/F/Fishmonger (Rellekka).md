## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to him

- **Fur trader:** I don't sell to outerlanders.

#### Trade option

- Only Fremenniks may purchase fish here.

#### Attempting to steal from the fur stall

- The fishmonger is staring at you suspiciously. You cannot steal from his stall while he distrusts you.

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Fishmonger:** Hello there, [Fremennik name]. Looking for fresh fish?
