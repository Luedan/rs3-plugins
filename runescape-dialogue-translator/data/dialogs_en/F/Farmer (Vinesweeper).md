# Farmer (Vinesweeper)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Farmer (Vinesweeper)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFarmer%20%28Vinesweeper%29)
- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Inspecting a planted flag

- **Farmer:** {{RSChat|Ah, another flag to clear. Let's see what's there.}}
  - (If the flag was planted correctly)
    - **Farmer:** {{RSChat|Ah! A seed. Points for everyone near me!}}
  - (If the flag was planted incorrectly)
    - **Farmer:** {{RSChat|Hmm, no seeds planted here, I'm afraid.}}
    - **Farmer:** {{RSChat|I'll have to keep this 'ere flag. Sorry.}}
    - (Planted flag is removed.)

### Inspecting a dead plant

- **Farmer:** {{RSChat|Hmm. Looks like there's a plant here.}}
- **Farmer:** {{RSChat|Gracious me! This one's dead.}}
- (Dead plant is removed.)
