## Dialogue Transcript


### Catching fish

- **Fisherwoman:** What a catch!

### Random event


#### Jumping in

- **Fisherwoman:** {{RSChat|Woohoo!}}

#### Talk-to


##### First time during the incident

- You check to see if they're okay in the water.
- ((One of the following is seen, then continues below:))
  - **Player:** {{RSChat|Be careful!}}
  - **Player:** {{RSChat|That's awfully risky!}}
- ((Only seen occasionally:))
  - **Fisherwoman:** {{RSChat|Don't worry about me, the water feels great!}}

##### Afterwards

- You've already checked on them.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You've already checked on them.

#### Getting out after talked to

- You are rewarded for checking on the diver - You receive 1,940 Fishing XP.
