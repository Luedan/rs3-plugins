## Dialogue Transcript


### On Miscellania


#### Before Throne of Miscellania

- **Fishmonger:** Greetings, Sir. Get your fresh fish here!
- **Fishmonger:** I've heard that the Etceterian fish is stored in a cow shed.
- (opens=Island Fishmonger)

- **Fishmonger:** Greetings, Your Highness. Have some fresh fish!
- **Fishmonger:** I've heard that the Etceterian fish is stored in a cow shed.
- (opens=Island Fishmonger)
