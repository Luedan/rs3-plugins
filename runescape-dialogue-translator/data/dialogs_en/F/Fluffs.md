## Dialogue Transcript


### At Gertrude's House


#### Without a cat

- **Player:** Hello Fluffs.
- **Fluffs:** Purrrrr. So, you can talk to cats now?
- **Player:** Yes, I got this amulet from a Sphinx.
- **Fluffs:** Well, I should thank you for finding my kittens that time. I have a terrible time keeping track of them and I never know how many Gertrude has managed to sell.
- **Player:** They're cute little creatures, aren't they?
- **Fluffs:** Purrrrr!
- Fluffs looks proud.
- **Fluffs:** My children have always been popular with Adventurers. They have inherited their father's adventurous spirit.
- **Fluffs:** He's always travelling the world - when he can find some kind person to help him through doors.
- **Player:** Look after yourself, Fluffs.
- **Fluffs:** You too, Player.

#### With a cat in your inventory or following you

- **Player:** Hello Fluffs.
- **Fluffs:** Purrrrr. So, you can talk to cats now?
- **Player:** Yes, I got this amulet from a Sphinx.
- **Fluffs:** Well, I should thank you for finding my kittens that time. I have a terrible time keeping track of them and I never know how many Gertrude has managed to sell.
- **Cat:** Hi, mommy!
- **Fluffs:** Hello, dear!
- **Player:** Aww, that's sweet.
- **Fluffs:** Is Player looking after you well?
- **Cat:** No complaints, mom.
- **Fluffs:** Good, good. That's what I like to hear.

#### With a cat in the bank

- **Player:** Hello Fluffs.
- **Fluffs:** Purrrrr. So, you can talk to cats now?
- **Player:** Yes, I got this amulet from a Sphinx.
- **Fluffs:** Well, I should thank you for finding my kittens that time. I have a terrible time keeping track of them and I never know how many Gertrude has managed to sell.
- **Player:** They're cute little creatures, aren't they? I've got one in my bank.
- **Fluffs:** You've put one of my children in your bank?
- **Player:** It's fine in there, honest!
- **Fluffs:** Hisss!
{{Qact|Fluffs attacks you and deals 30 damage}}
- **Player:** Ouch!

### In the Varrock Rat Pits

- **Player:** Hey, aren't you Gertrude's cat?
- **Gertrude's cat:** What's it to you?
- **Player:** Well, seeing as I've had to go off and fetch you for Gertrude already...
- **Gertrude's cat:** Oh would you ever quit worrying, I come down here to watch the fights all the time.
- **Player:** Really?
- **Gertrude's cat:** Oh yes, it's a great place for catching a bit of tail.
- **Player:** Ummmm, ok. I'll leave you to it then.
