## Dialogue Transcript


### In East Ardougne market

- **Fur trader:** Would you like to trade in fur?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - (opens=Ardougne Fur Stall)
  - [Choice]: No.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from his stall

- **Fur trader:** {{RSChat|Hey! Get your hands off there!}}

- **Fur trader:**{{RSChat|shadow=yes|colour=yellow|You're the one who stole something from me!}}
