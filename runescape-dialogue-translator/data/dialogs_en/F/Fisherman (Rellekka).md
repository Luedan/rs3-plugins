## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Fisherman:** Don't talk to me outerlander. Anything you have to say to me, you can say to the Chieftain. Goodbye.

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Fisherman:** Welcome back, [brother/sister] [Fremennik name]. It has been too long since last we spoke! The fish are really biting today!

### After Hero's Welcome

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Fisherman:** Welcome back, [brother/sister] [Fremennik name]. It has been too long since last we spoke! The fish are really biting today!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can I ask you about V?
    - **Fisherman:** We'll keep his stories alive, at least his legend will live forever.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)
