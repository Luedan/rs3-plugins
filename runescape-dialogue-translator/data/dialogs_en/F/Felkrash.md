# Felkrash

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Felkrash](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFelkrash)
- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Ratcatchers

- **Player:** What was it I heard about some pits around here?
- {{anchor|tears}}- Felkrash breaks down into tears -
- **Felkrash:** It is... It used to be my rat pits. You could bring your cats here to fight. It was going to be glorious! It was open for years. Not a single person visited! How could so many be so foolish as to ignore such brilliance?
- **Player:** I guess brilliance is one word for it.
- - Felkrash glares at you angrily, you walk away while you can. -

### After Ratcatchers

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you train my cat for me?
    - **Player:** Can you show me how to train my cat into a wily cat?
    - (Condition: If the player does not have an overgrown cat with them:)
      - **Felkrash:** I can only train overgrown cats into wilycats.
    - (Condition: If the player already has a wily cat with them:)
      - **Felkrash:** You already have a mature cat, look after that first.
    - (Condition: If the player has an overgrown cat with them:)
      - Your overgrown cat has grown into a lean mean hunting cat.
      - **Felkrash:** You should really name your cat.
      - ((Name your cat interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: Can you rename my cat?
    - **Player:** Can you rename my cat?
    - (Condition: If the player does not have a wily or lazy cat with them:)
      - **Felkrash:** I'll only help you name wily or lazy cats. Bring me one and I'll help you out.
    - (Condition: If the player has a wily or lazy cat with them:)
      - ((Name your cat interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: What was it I heard about some pits around here?
    - (above|tears)

#### After naming the wily cat


##### With a catspeak amulet equipped

- **[Cat's name]:** Hey Player, you finally got around to naming me.
- **Player:** Sorry I took so long, [cat's name], it took a while to come up with a name for you.

##### Without a catspeak amulet equipped

- **Player:** So, cat, what do you think of the name?
- **[Cat's name]:** Miaaow!
- **Player:** Can you tell me more about Wily cats?

##### If the player was training their overgrown cat to a wily cat{{anchor|tellmemore}}

- **Player:** Can you tell me more about Wily cats?
- **Felkrash:** Of course, of course, they're the highest form of cat that I know of.
- **Felkrash:** They're tougher, stronger and faster than other variations of cat, they can catch butterflies and kalphite larva and are far superior to any other type of cat at hunting.
- **Player:** So is there any drawbacks to owning them?
- **Felkrash:** Wily cats need looking after or else they get fat and lazy.
- **Player:** So how do I look after them then?
- **Felkrash:** The key is to keep it active, bring it hunting regularly.
- **Felkrash:** Try keep it happy too, they can be a little nasty when they're cranky.
- **Player:** What happens if I neglect my cat, not that I would or anything?
- **Felkrash:** They would get fat and lazy, be much worse at hunting and would show you up as a really poor cat trainer.
- **Player:** Can I retrain my lazy cat?
- **Felkrash:** Yes you can get your lazy cat back in shape with a lot of rat catching, but you really shouldn't let it get into that state in the first place.
