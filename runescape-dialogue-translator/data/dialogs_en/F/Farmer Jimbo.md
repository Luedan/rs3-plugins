## Dialogue Transcript


### Dialogue

- **Player:** Hi there.
- **Farmer Jimbo:** Hey.
- Who are you?
  - **Farmer Jimbo:** Why, I'm an esteemed farmer and tree surgeon, Farmer Jimbo! People from far and wide request my help in healing and protecting their farming endeavours. I've even had requests from kings and queens before!
  - *If not at protected tree in Varrock*
    - **Farmer Jimbo:** Oh wait, that reminds me. I'm currently on a journey... King Roald! I was meant to be healing his tree!
    - **Player:** Right...
  - <i>dialogue returns to menu</i>
- Why are you here?
  - *If at the protected tree in Varrock*
    - **Farmer Jimbo:** King Roald asked me to look after this tree to ensure nothing unfortunate befalls it. There were rumors that this tree had died. But as you can see, it's in perfect health! It's so vibrant and full of life, it'll be fully grown before long. They grow up so fast...
  - *If at any other place*
    - **Farmer Jimbo:** I've been investigating certain trees while on my travels. Such as here.
    - *If in Yanille near the Timbo tree*
      - **Farmer Jimbo:** Such a shame. If only I could have reached this poor thing in time.
      - <i>Conversation terminates</i>
    - *If near the tree patches (see locations)*
      - *If tree is fully grown*
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** What a lovely tree. The person who grew it must have been a true professional.
        - *continues below*
      - *If tree is chopped down*
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** Stop! What are you doing? How dare you nurture a tree from a sapling to just take a hatchet to it!
        - *continues below*
      - *If tree patch is empty*
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** So much potential here, but not being utilised... How sad.
        - *continues below*
      - *If a sapling is planted in patch*
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** Aww, a little sapling. Young one, You'll soon grow up to be big and strong!
        - *continues below*
      - *If tree is healthy and growing*<!-- obtained from a pineapple tree in stage 3 -->
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** It makes me happy to see a young healthy tree!
        - *continues below*
      - *If tree is dead* <!-- obtained from a willow tree dead at stage 2 -->
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** Another loss, how sad.
        - *continues below*
      - **Farmer Jimbo:** Oh wait, that reminds me, my destination... King Roald! I was meant to be healing his tree!
      - *If at Varrock tree patch*
        - **Farmer Jimbo:** I should probably move out of his courtyard before he notices...
      - **Player:** Right...
      - <i>Conversation terminates</i>
- *If not at the protected tree in Varrock*
  - Ask about the protected tree in Varrock
    - **Farmer Jimbo:** A protected tree in Varrock?
    - **Player:** Yes, King Roald planted a tree and...
    - **Farmer Jimbo:** Yes! I was meant to be healing that tree! Oh dear, I should probably get moving.
    - <i>Conversation terminates</i>
- **Player:** Goodbye
  - <i>Conversation terminates</i><!-- Still needs: Diseased, Dead (in multiple stages diseased/dead?) and other grow stages. -->
