## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before completing Hearts of Sanguine

- **Farmer Rachel:** Another sheep's wandered off over night. If this continues, I'll've none left by winter.
- **Farmer Rachel:** All the noises in the night...the voices...
- Farmer Rachel stares into the fire and drifts away into quiet whispers that you can't quite make out.

#### After completing Hearts of Sanguine

- **Player:** I uncovered what became of your sheep. I'm afraid they were lured to a cave north of here and killed.
- **Player:** The entity responsible has been dealt with. Your flock should be safe.
- **Farmer Rachel:** That is sad news, but thank you for taking the time to help me, traveller. The whispers have been quiet of late as well.
- **Farmer Rachel:** Oh, but now I think about it, someone should check in on Silverquill!
- **Player:** Silverquill?
- **Farmer Rachel:** She's a friend of Inanna's - a gentle giant of a hedgehog who has her nest in that cave!
- **Farmer Rachel:** I've been so worried about my sheep, I didn't think about her. I hope she's alright too...
- **Player:** I'll check in on her as soon as I can.
- **Farmer Rachel:** Thank you. Take care, y'hear?

### Pickpocket


#### On a free-to-play world, or below level 10 Thieving

- You need to be a level 10 thief to steal from this target.
- (popup)
  - {{Popup|You need Thieving level 10.|warning=yes}}

#### With level 10 Thieving


##### Successful pickpocket

- You pick the target's pocket.
  - (Condition: If seeds are received:)
    - You steal [number] [name] seeds.
  - (Condition: If coins are received:)
    - [number] coins have been added to your money pouch.

##### Unsuccessful pickpocket

- **Farmer Rachel:** {{RSChat|What do you think you're doing?}}
- {{Qact|The player is hit for a few points of damage.}}
- You fail to steal from the target.
- You've been stunned.

##### Without space in backpack

- You cannot keep pickpocketing as you have a full inventory.
