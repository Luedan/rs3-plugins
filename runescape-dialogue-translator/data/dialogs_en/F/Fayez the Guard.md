# Fayez the Guard

- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Fayez the Guard:** I'm working. What do you have to say that's so urgent?
- Select an option
  - What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Fayez the Guard:** There's a lot of space here. More open space than back home.
    - **Player:** So where is back home?
    - **Fayez the Guard:** Pollnivneach. It's a town to the south of the Shantay Pass.
  - So, what do you do here?
    - **Player:** So, what do you do here?
    - **Fayez the Guard:** I'm on guard duty. Making sure nobody tries to steal anything from the house and tents in the middle of town.
    - **Player:** Why are you only guarding those buildings?
    - **Fayez the Guard:** That's all I've been hired to guard.
  - *If the player has asked any of the Al Kharid merchants about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Player:** I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Fayez the Guard:** Yeah, he hired me. He owns this house and these two tents, too.
      - **Player:** Is the work good?
      - **Fayez the Guard:** It pays better than back home.
      - **Player:** Why, what did you do back home?
      - **Fayez the Guard:** Never you mind.
      - **Fayez the Guard:** But Ali Morrisane pays us well, at least.
      - **Player:** Maybe I should talk to him...
      - **Fayez the Guard:** Why not? He always likes to meet potential business partners.
  - *If the player has asked Karim, Shop assistant, or Shopkeeper about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Player:** I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Fayez the Guard:** So? They talk too much.
      - **Player:** You're not going to deny it?
      - **Fayez the Guard:** Why bother? None of them can fight back, after all.
