#### When talked to

- **Foreman George:** Welcome to my new rebuildathon!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do I need to do here?
    - **Foreman George:** Simple really. Choose a sandcastle you want to help build and start working on it.
    - **Foreman George:** I've got some visiting dignitaries mind so it's worth trying to impress them while they're here to get a better reward.
    - **Foreman George:** It's hot work mind, so if you get too hot you might want to find yourself an ice cream to cool off.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What do I get for helping?
    - **Foreman George:** Straight down to business, eh?
    - **Foreman George:** Help me build these sandcastles and you'll get some Construction XP. If you take note of the visiting dignitary then you'll get a greater reward for impressing them with your work.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Foreman George:** My name's George, and I'm the foreman of this project.
    - **Foreman George:** Bit of a change from my previous work - helping to rebuild Lumbridge after the battle - but this looks to be just as challenging.
    - **Player:** Really? They just look like sandcastles - how hard can they be to build?
    - **Foreman George:** Are you questioning my building abilities?
    - **Foreman George:** Let's just say they're more advanced than your regular sandcastle - I've got the blueprints, you just focus on the building.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: We'll speak later.
    - (end)
