## Dialogue Transcript


### Before doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Farid Morrisane (ores):{{anchor|new-trader}}** Hmmm, a new trader. I suggest you speak with Brugsen Bursen or the Grand Exchange Tutor near the entrance for a lesson. Brugsen will give an interesting and complete lesson and the Tutor will give a smaller, plain lesson.

- (above2|new-trader)

### After doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** Hello, little boy.
- **Farid Morrisane (ores):** I would appreciate it if you didn't talk to me in such a manner. I'll have you know I'm an accomplished merchant.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Calm down, junior.
    - **Player:** Calm down, junior.
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** Don't tell me to calm down! And don't call me 'junior'.
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** I'll have you know I am Farid Morrisane, son of Ali Morrisane, the world's greatest merchant!
    - **Player:** Then why are you here and not him?
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** My dad has given me the responsibility of expanding our business here.
    - **Player:** And you're up to the task?
    - **Player:** What a grown up boy you are! Mummy and daddy must be very pleased!
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** Look mate - I may be young, I may be short, but I'm a respected merchant around here and don't have time to deal with simpletons like you.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can you help me out with the prices for ores?
    - **Player:** Can you help me out with the prices for ores?
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** That's easy. Been doing that since I was three.
    - ((Guide Prices: Ores opens up.))
  - [Choice]: I best go and speak with someone more my height.
    - **Player:** I best go and speak with someone more my height.
    - **Farid Morrisane (ores):** Then I shall not stop you, mister. I've got too much work to do.
