## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]

- **Flower Girl:** Hello.
- **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
- **Flower Girl:** I'm selling flowers, 15gp for three. Would you like some?
- Select an option
  - Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - **Flower Girl:** Thank you! Here you go.
    - (removes=15 coins) (receives=flowers (mixed))
  - No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.

### After Throne of Miscellania

- **Flower Girl:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.
- **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
- **Flower Girl:** I'm selling flowers, 15gp for three. Would you like some, Your Highness?
  - Yes, please.
    - **Flower Girl:** Thank you! Here you go.
  - No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
