### Standard dialogue

- Can you explain what I need to do?
  - **Faiza:** We need to go back to the start. All the monsters you have fought against and killed so far have developed your combat skills against them but that is not enough. Now it is time to understand them. Every creature has a soul - that spark within them that is the basis for everything that they are. As part of your training to become a Slayer Master, I request your assistance. The Order is in ruins covered in magic that has locked away the secrets of our past. You must capture these souls and deliver them back here. Each soul gathered will weaken the spell that we placed over the Order all those years ago. And in return, I will offer you further training.
  - **Player:** I have to capture the soul of every slayer creature?
  - **Faiza:** I never said the journey was going to be easy. These ushtabi are vessels that can contain the soul of a Slayer monster. Simple kill a monster with one of these with you and it has a chance of capturing the creature's soul. Bring it back here and you can deliver the soul in return for greater understanding and take another step on your way to becoming a Slayer Master.
  - You can purchase more ushtabi from Slayer Masters around the world.
  - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- What is the best way to catch a soul?
  - **Faiza:** As your understanding of Slayer increases, the chance of catching a soul significantly increases. Additionally, being on a Slayer task increases your chance of capturing the soul.
  - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- What else can I do with these souls?
  - **Faiza:** If understanding is not enough you can also capture these souls and bring them here. Within these walls, the soul remains in stasis unable to move to the afterlife. You may wish to release a soul in one of the rooms ahead. There they will stay, under your command, allowing you to kill them over and over again. You may find this useful if you are on a Slayer task. There are others that have also found this place and they too wish to learn the secrets of the Order. If you meet one of those people, perhaps they would allow you to witness the spirits they have captured.
  - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- What can you tell me about the Order?
  - **Faiza:** That can wait. First capture some souls and begin your journey of understanding.
  - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
- I have to go now.
  - **Faiza:** Farewell.
  - (end)
