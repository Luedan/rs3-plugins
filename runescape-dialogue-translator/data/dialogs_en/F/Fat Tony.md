## Dialogue Transcript


### Regular dialogue

**Fat Tony:** Go away, I'm very busy.
- Sorry to disturb you.
  - **Player:** Sorry to disturb you.
- What are you busy doing?
  - **Player:** What are you busy doing?
  - **Fat Tony:** I'm cooking pizzas for the people in this camp.
  - **Fat Tony:** Not that these louts appreciate my gourmet cooking!
    - So what is a gourmet chef doing cooking for bandits?
      - **Player:** So what is a gourmet chef doing cooking for bandits?
      - **Fat Tony:** Well, I'm an outlaw. I was accused of giving the king food poisoning! The thought of it! I think he just drank too much wine that night.
      - **Fat Tony:** I had to flee the kingdom of Misthalin. The bandits give me refuge here as long as I cook for them.
      - ''Shows previous options
    - Can I have some pizza too?
      - **Player:** Can I have some pizza too?
      - **Fat Tony:** Well, this pizza is really meant to be for the bandits. I guess I could sell you some pizza bases though.
        - Yes, okay.
          - **Player:** Yes, okay.
          - ''Redirected to Tony's Pizza Bases
        - Oh, if I have to pay I don't want any.
          - **Player:** Oh, if I have to pay I don't want any.
    - Okay, I'll leave you to it.
      - **Player:** Okay, I'll leave you to it.
- Have you anything to sell?
  - **Player:** Have you anything to sell?
  - **Fat Tony:** Well, I guess I can sell you some half-made pizzas.
  - *Redirected to Tony's Pizza Bases*
- Goodbye.
  - (end)

### When giving him an anchovy pizza
