# Fadli

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Fadli](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFadli)
- **Letter**: `F`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Fadli:** Good day. How may I help you?
  - I'd Like to access my bank account, please.
  - I'd like to check my PIN settings.
  - I'd like to see my collection box.
  - What is this place?
    - **Fadli:** This is a branch of the Bank of RuneScape. We have branches in many towns.
      - And what do you do?
        - **Fadli:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Fadli:** Yes we did, but people kept on coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.

### After the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Fadli:** What?
- ((If the player has items to collect in Grand Exchange:))
  - **Fadli:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- **WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO SAY?**
  - What do you do?
    - **Player:** What do you do?
    - **Fadli:** You can store your stuff here if you want. You can dump anything you don't want to carry while you're relaxing in the oasis and pick it up again on the way out.
    - **Fadli:** To be honest, I'm wasted here. But ever since the Duel Arena collapsed, I don't know where my fighting skills would be best placed.
    - **Player:** You could always try the Al Kharid security force!
    - **Fadli:** No thanks.
  - Do you visit the oasis?
    - **Player:** Do you ever visit the oasis?
    - **Fadli:** When I can. As much as I miss the arena, the oasis is a nice change of scenery. And the crocodiles seem to enjoy the rotten fruit I throw to them. Wanna buy some?
    - **WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO SAY?**
      - Yes, please.
        - **Player:** Yes, please.
        - (opens=Shop of Distaste)
      - No, thanks.
        - **Player:** No, thanks.
  - I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - I'd like to see my Collection box.
    - (opens=Grand Exchange collection box)
  - Would you like to trade?
    - **Player:** Would you like to trade?
    - **Fadli:** I suppose so.
    - (opens=Shop of Distaste)
