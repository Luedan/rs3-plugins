## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue after Swan Song

- **Franklin Caranos:** Hello again, Player.
- **Player:** Hello. How's the repair work going?
- **Franklin Caranos:** I'm working on it. I can always do with more iron sheets, so if you've got any more, I'll give you 20 gp per sheet.
- **Player:** Thanks, I'll remember that.

### Iron sheet dialogue

- (Condition: If the player has only one iron sheet:)
  - **Franklin Caranos:** Hello again, Player. Were you wanting to sell me that iron sheet? I'd give you 20 gp for it.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player has multiple iron sheets:)
  - **Franklin Caranos:** Hello again, Player. Were you wanting to sell me those iron sheets? I'd give you [amount] gp.
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Yes, that'll do nicely.
    - **Player:** Yes, that'll do nicely.
    - (receives=20 coins per iron sheet) (removes=all iron sheets in inventory)
  - [Choice]: No, I'd rather not.
    - **Player:** No, I'd rather not.
    - **Franklin Caranos:** That's ok.
