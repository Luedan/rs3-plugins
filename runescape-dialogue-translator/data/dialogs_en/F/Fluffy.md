## Dialogue Transcript


### Interact


#### Conversation 1

- **Player:** So, what do you do?
- **Fluffy:** I count eggs for the Easter Bunny.
- **Player:** How many have you counted: a thousand?
- **Fluffy:** I lost my count. I LOST MY COUNT!
- **Player:** I'm sorry. I guess you'll have to start from 1 again.
- **Fluffy:** No, wait! I was ON 1. I remembered.

#### Conversation 2

- **Player:** You seem a little distracted.
- **Fluffy:** I was thinking what red would smell like.
- **Player:** Colours don't have a smell.
- **Fluffy:** But if they did, what would it smell like?
- **Player:** I don't know.
- **Fluffy:** Thoughts like these keep me up at night.

#### Conversation 3

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Fluffy:** Hello to you too. Isn't it a wonderful day?
- **Player:** It seems very nice, yes.
- **Fluffy:** Not nice! It is wonderful!
- **Player:** If you say so...
- **Fluffy:** NO! I'LL GNAW YOU GOOD! SAY THAT IT'S WONDERFUL! NOW!
- **Player:** Okay, okay! It's a wonderful day.
- **Fluffy:** Yah! Wonderful days are nice!

#### Conversation 4 (if interacting with another player's follower)

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Fluffy:** Hello. You smell nice.
- **Player:** How strange.
- **Fluffy:** Goodbye.
