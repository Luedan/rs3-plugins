## Dialogue Transcript


### At the farm north of Lumbridge

- **Fred the Farmer:** What are you doing on my land?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: I'm looking for something to kill.
    - **Fred the Farmer:** What, on my land? Leave my livestock alone!
  - [Choice]: I'm lost.
    - **Fred the Farmer:** How can you be lost? Just follow the road east and south. You'll end up in Lumbridge fairly quickly.
  - [Choice]: cond=Only before Sheep Shearer|I'm looking for work.
    - (see|Sheep Shearer (miniquest))
  - [Choice]: cond=If the player has interacted with the penguins in sheep clothing|Fred! Fred! I've seen 'The Thing'!
    - **Fred the Farmer:** You... you actually saw it? Run for the hills! Player, grab as many chickens as you can! We have to...
    - **Player:** Fred!
    - **Fred the Farmer:** ...flee! Oh, woe is me! The shape-shifter is coming! We're all...
    - **Player:** FRED!
    - **Fred the Farmer:** ...doomed. What?
    - **Player:** It's not a shape-shifter or any other kind of monster.
    - **Fred the Farmer:** Well, what is it, [boy/girl]?
    - **Player:** Well, it's just two penguins... disguised as a sheep.
    - **Fred the Farmer:** ...
    - **Fred the Farmer:** Have you been out in the sun too long?

#### Having not yet claimed the reward for the Sheep Shearer miniquest

- **Player:** Hello again.
- **Fred the Farmer:** What are you doing on my...
- **Fred the Farmer:** Ah, it's the sheep shearer! That wool you gave me sold exceptionally well. Please accept this as an extra thanks.
- (receives=1,940 coins)
- [The farmer hands over 1,940 coins.]
- **Player:** Thank you!
- {{RSFontAchievement|Fred's Shearings}}
