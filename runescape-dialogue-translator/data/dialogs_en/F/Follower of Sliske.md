# Follower of Sliske

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Follower of Sliske](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFollower%20of%20Sliske)
- **Letter**: `F`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Golem parts

- **Player:** Who are you?
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I am just a lowly follower of Sliske, here to play a game.
  - **Player:** Goodbye.
- **Player:** What do you want?
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I have some golem parts, that I wish to...dispose of.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** And to do so, in honour of Sliske, I have created a little game.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** The first faction to get 20 different people to ask me for the parts will get them all.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** Do you want the parts for your faction?
    - **Player:** Yes, I would like the parts.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** Thank you for playing... I have counted your vote.
    - **Player:** No, I'm not playing your game.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** Life is a game. Only by dying can you not play.
    - **Player:** What do you get out of this?
      - **Follower of Sliske:** For me? Why, I just want to help you continue your beautiful war.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** Do you want these golem parts for your faction?
- **Player:** What do you want? <sub>If already voted</sub>
  - **Follower of Sliske:** You have done all you need do, for now.

### Tricking the Godless

- **Player:** Who are you?
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I am just a lowly follower of Sliske, here to play a game.
  - **Player:** Goodbye.
- **Player:** What do you want?
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I want your help. I have a little game to play.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I have managed to convince a Godless agent that I am a reliable source.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I can deceive them and make them launch one attack on [enemy faction].
  - **Follower of Sliske:** But I need 20 followers of [player faction] to ask me before 20 followers of [enemy faction] do.
  - **Follower of Sliske:** Do you want me to persuade the Godless to attack [enemy faction]?
    - **Player:** Yes, attack my enemy.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** Thank you. I hope you enjoy my game as much as I do.
    - **Player:** No, I'm not playing your game.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** Life is a game. Only by dying can you not play.
    - **Player:** What do you get out of this?
      - **Follower of Sliske:** For me? Why, I just want to help you continue your beautiful war.
      - **Follower of Sliske:** So, do you want me to persuade the Godless to attack your enemy?
- **Player:** What do you want? <sub>If already voted</sub>
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I have heard your voice, and will bear in mind your actions.
- **Player:** What do you want? <sub>If 20 players from one faction have voted</sub>
  - **Follower of Sliske:** I have all the help I need. Perhaps next time we can... play.
