## Dialogue Transcript


### Without a catspeak amulet equipped


#### Without the amulet in backpack

- [To talk to cats, you need a catspeak amulet. <br>You can get one from Tiny Zemouregal in New Varrock.]

#### With the amulet in backpack

- [To talk to cats, you need to wear your catspeak amulet.]

### With a catspeak amulet equipped


#### Before completing Cat's Bane, without the Necrofelinomicon in backpack

- **Player:** Come on, kitty, time to move on.
- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** P-p-please don't shout at me. I'm s-s-scared of whatever's in that kennel.
- **Player:** Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't mean to frighten you.
- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** It's fine. I'm used to it. I once belonged to a w-w-witch. She didn't treat me too well.
- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** B-b-but I did learn a lot.
- **Player:** Well, if you did move on, you'd get away from that kennel.
- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** I th-th-think I need some courage. There's something that would h-h-help.
- **Player:** Name it, and I'll see if I can get it for you.
- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** There's a book - reading it was my only comfort, but it was s-s-stolen. It's hidden in the Zamorak temple, behind the black stone altar slab.

#### Picking up the Necrofelinomicon

- [As you look at the altar you notice a strange book hidden beneath it.]

#### Before completing Cat's Bane, with Necrofelinomicon in backpack

- **<nowiki>'</nowiki>Fraidy cat:** You've found it - the book of power! You fool! At last, it is mine!
- Before you can say anything, the cat snatches the book and confidently strides off in the direction of New Varrock's graveyard...
- (Condition: If the player still has cats to assist:)
  - You have made progress in the 'A Cat's Bane' Task. ([number]/4)
- (Condition: If the player has assisted all four cats:)
  - {{RSFontAchievement|Cat's Bane}}
  - **Player:** I wonder why they all headed off towards the graveyard...
