### Charging a [[Lyre]]


#### Offering a [[raw bass|Raw bass]]

- **Fossegrimen:** A raw bass? You should know that is not a worthy offering, outlander.
  - *If you are not wearing a Ring of charos (a)*
    - **Player:** Yes, you're right - I'll find something more worthy of you.

    - Yes, you're right - I'll find something more worthy of you.
      - *(Same as above)*
    - [Charm] That's not a bass, it's just a very small shark.
      - **Player:** That's not a bass, it's just a very small shark.
      - **Fossegrimen:** That is a very small shark indeed, outlander. Small, but worthy.
      - Your lyre is now enchanted. You can use it to teleport to Rellekka. When the enchantment wears off you will need to re-enchant it.
      - **Fossegrimen:** I offer you this enchantment for your worthy offering.
