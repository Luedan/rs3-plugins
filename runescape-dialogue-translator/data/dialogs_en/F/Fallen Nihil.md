## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to during Birth by Fire

- **Fallen Nihil:** Thank you for heeding my call, human.
- **Player:** It's hard to ignore a demon stepping out of a bonfire.
- **Fallen Nihil:** I have been sent as an emissary of the Queen of Ashes, and require any and all help.
- **Player:** What is it you need?
- **Fallen Nihil:** Many interesting embers have been turning up in this world, and my Queen wishes to analyse their properties. Collect as many burning embers as possible and bring them back to me.
- **Player:** Sounds simple enough.
  - What are you?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** A failed experiment. Considered unworthy. Incredibly angry. Redeemed by Fire.
    - **Player:** It sounds like you've got a pretty large grudge against someone.
    - **Fallen Nihil:** That's not even the half of it.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What do you plan to do with these Burning Embers?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** Some questions are best left unanswered, human.
    - **Player:** Like 'What's your relationship with Zaros'?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** Yes, like that one.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - How did you get here?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** My Queen noticed this world appear in the heavens as if it had been revealed from behind a curtain. Last year she felt an increase in fire energy in this location. She had her pyradins spend a year preparing to use that energy. With it, she could bring me through the planes, in the hopes that the energy would build up once more.
    - **Player:** You worked an entire year in the hope that the energy might return?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** I did not personally; my task is to act as scout and herald for my Queen; to seek worlds that are ready for her Enlightenment, and deliver messages between those who are already Enlightened.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - **Fallen Nihil:** Without a continuous source, all fire comes to an end. I can only last here for the amount of time my Queen's strength allows me. Be sure to collect my embers quickly!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only after asking one of the latter two questions:)*
    - Who is the Queen of Ashes?
      - **Fallen Nihil:** A redeemer, and life-giver. My queen accepted me for what I was, and gave me a second chance in life. Soon, Gielinor will feel the same redemption.
      - **Player:** How soon is 'soon'?
      - **Fallen Nihil:** The Queen of Ashes rushes nothing, small one. She is biding her time and will arrive when she feels this world is ready.
      - **Player:** So... soon?
      - **Fallen Nihil:** Yes, soon.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*

### When talked to after Birth by Fire

  - What are you?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - What do you plan to do with these Burning Embers?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - How did you get here?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - *(Only after asking something else:)*
    - Who is the Queen of Ashes?
      - *(As during the event.)*

### Handing in burning embers

- *(If a reward isn't unlocked:)*
  - **Fallen Nihil:** I require more offerings before I can reward you.
- *(If a reward is unlocked:)*
  - **Fallen Nihil:** There you are!

### Claiming rewards

- *(Without rewards to claim and none claimed:)*
  - **Fallen Nihil:** You'll need to help me some more before I can offer you any rewards.
- *(Without rewards to claim and some claimed:)*
  - **Fallen Nihil:** I require more offerings before I can reward you.
- *(Without all rewards claimed:)*
  - **Fallen Nihil:** You've already claimed all of the rewards I have to offer.
