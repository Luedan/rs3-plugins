## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Fort Guard:** What's up, boss?
- **Select an Option**
  - You were a Dark Warrior?
    - **Fort Guard:** DARK WARRIORS RULE!
    - **Player:** So I hear. Tell me more about them.
    - **Fort Guard:** We're the strongest gang in the Wilderness!
    - **Player:** Still?
    - **Fort Guard:** I guess not. We were though.
    - **Player:** Are there a lot of bandit gangs in the Wilderness?
    - **Fort Guard:** A few, yeah. You got your Orphans, Rogues, your Furies, the Destroyers and uh... Gladiators.
    - **Fort Guard:** Most of them are dead now, or working for the Zammies. Better dead than red!
    - **Player:** What happened to the Dark Warriors then?
    - **Fort Guard:** We weren't gonna fight in no demon war. Our leader Cyrus said no, so they killed him.
    - **Fort Guard:** We been trying to get out of the Wildy ever since.
    - **Player:** Why?
    - **Fort Guard:** Have you seen it out there? Demons, dragons, even mammoths have been moving down from the far north.
    - **Fort Guard:** Demons I can handle, but those things? They scare me.
    - **Player:** I hear you.
    - **Fort Guard:** I guess we're in the Duchy of Forinthry gang now, though. Back on the rise!
    - **Player:** That's right! Keep up the good work.
    - **Fort Guard:** Right, boss.
  - All clear on the walls?
    - **Fort Guard:** Looks it for now. If we see any Zammies on the move we'll come running.
    - **Player:** Good job.
  - As you were.
    - (end)
