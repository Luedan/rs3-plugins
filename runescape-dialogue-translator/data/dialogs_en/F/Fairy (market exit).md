## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to the fairy or attempting to use the nearby fairy ring

- **Fairy:** This fairy ring will take you out of Zanaris. It leads to the place known as Al Kharid in your realm. Once passed, you can not return this way. Before leaving, make sure that you have fully sampled the delights of our marketplace.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I think I'll stay here a bit longer.
    - **Player:** I think I'll stay down here a bit longer.
    - **Fairy:** As you wish.
  - [Choice]: Yes, I'm ready to leave.
    - **Player:** Yes, I'm ready to leave.
    - {{Qact|The player is teleported to just outside the western entrance to Al Kharid.}}
