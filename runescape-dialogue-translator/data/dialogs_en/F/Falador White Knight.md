## Dialogue Transcript

**Falador White Knight:** Greetings! Duke Horacio has called for aid, and Falador answers! While we'd love to help our fellow Saradominists battle the evil forces of Zamorak, our primary duty is to protect the citizens of Lumbridge. Fear not, we shall protect you from any Zamorakian scum that decides to wander out of the crater!
