## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Miscellania and Etceteria dungeon

- **Ferd:** Good day, sir.
- **Player:** What are you doing down here?
- **Ferd:** Shoring up the walls.
- **Player:** What does that do?
- **Ferd:** Stops them falling down.
- **Player:** Oh, I see.
- **Ferd:** Aye. If you want to chatter, you'd better talk to Thorodin over there. I'm working.
- **Player:** Okay then.
