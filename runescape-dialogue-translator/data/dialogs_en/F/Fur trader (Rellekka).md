# Fur trader (Rellekka)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Fur trader (Rellekka)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFur%20trader%20%28Rellekka%29)
- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to him

- **Fur trader:** I don't sell to outerlanders.

#### Trade option

- Only Fremenniks may purchase furs here.

#### Attempting to steal from the fur stall

- The fur trader is staring at you suspiciously. You cannot steal from his stall while he distrusts you.

### After [[Hero's Welcome]]

*When speaking to Fur trader in Rellekka.*
- **Fur trader:** Welcome back, [Fremennik name]! Have you seen the furs I have today?
- Select an option
  - Can I ask you about V?
    - *Fur trader may reply to you with any of the following at random:*
    - Reply 1:
      - **Fur trader:** He died saving you, [Fremennik name]. Make sure you use his final gift wisely and live a life that we can all be proud of.
    - Reply 2:
      - **Fur trader:** He was a great hero to our people. Why couldn't they have gone for one of the bad gods, like Zamorak or Saradomin?
    - Reply 3:
      - **Fur trader:** Those cowards. They didn't even have the decency to face him as a warrior.
    - Reply 4:
      - **Fur trader:** It's sad that he's gone, but he will live on as an inspiration to countless heroes across countless worlds.
    - Reply 5:
      - **Fur trader:** We'll keep his stories alive, at least his legend will live forever.
  - Let me see your wares.
    - (opens=Fremennik Fur Trader)
