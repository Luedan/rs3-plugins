## Dialogue Transcript


### Worrying

- **Player:** Why the long face? No good catch today?
- **Fisherman:** I'm worried about old Groggy Gregg up in the crow's nest. He was once a great captain but now he's only sitting in his little wooden cage; drowning himself slowly in grog.
- **Player:** Oh...

### Relaxing

- **Fisherman:** I love it out here! I came here to make money and catch big fish, but I stayed for the peace and the calm the ocean brings to my mind and the camaraderie amongst the people here.
- **Player:** Don't you ever get seasick on these platforms here?
- **Fisherman:** Never! To me, it feels as if I'm cradled soothingly by the ocean, night and day.
- **Player:** Not sure my stomach agrees...

### Roaming

- **Fisherman:** Oi! Watch where you're going! It sure is getting awfully crowded here these days.
- **Player:** Cheer up! Isn't it lovely to have some company while fishing?
- **Fisherman:** Why would that be a good thing?
- **Fisherman:** Suddenly the platforms are overrun with young whippersnappers, stealing catches that could have been mine! I wonder who told everyone about this place.
