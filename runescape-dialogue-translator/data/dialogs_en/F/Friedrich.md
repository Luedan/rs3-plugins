## Dialogue Transcript

- **Friedrich:** Welcome to NovtumberFest! Look at that fancy tent!
- Select an option
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - **Player:** Who are you?
      - **Friedrich:** Hello! I am Friedrich, I'm in charge of the archery range.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What do I have to do here?
      - **Player:** What do I have to do here?
      - **Friedrich:** First of all just line up with one of the targets behind me.
      - **Friedrich:** Then pull back your bowstring and let your arrows fly!
      - You will receive Ranged XP for taking part in the archery range.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - How's it going?
      - **Player:** How's it going?
      - **Friedrich:** I'm great and you know what? I believe that you're going to be great on the range!
      - **Friedrich:** All you have to do is...
      - **Friedrich:** BOW-LIEVE!
      - **Player:** ...
      - **Friedrich:** ...
      - **Player:** Right...
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Bye.
    - **Friedrich:** Keep your aim true.
