## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to Factory Manager

- **Factory Manager:** Don't bother me, can't you see I'm busy?
- **Player:** No, I can't... can I go into the factory?
- **Factory Manager:** You can go in. The Consortium, in their infinite wisdom, has decided to open up the factory to all and sundry.
- **Player:** How come?
- **Factory Manager:** We've built a new blast furnace and always have a shortage of people to work on it.
- **Player:** I'll have a look, thanks.
- **Factory Manager:** Just don't go causing any trouble, I still run this factory!
