### At any farming patch with level 15 Farming or below


#### Upon standing idly near a Farming patch for five minutes

- **Farming Patch Spirit:** In case you didn't know, you don't have to stand by your Farming patch. Your crops will grow even if you're not around.

#### After continuing to stand idly nearby for another five minutes

- **Farming Patch Spirit:** Did you know that if your Farming patch has fully grown, it will never catch disease or die? It will be perfectly safe until you choose to harvest it.
