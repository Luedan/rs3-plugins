## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Throne of Miscellania


#### Talking to him

- **Fisherman Frodi:** Hello.
- [Menu]: What would you like to talk about?
  - [Choice]: Fisherman Frodi.
    - **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
    - **Fisherman Frodi:** I'm painting my toenails... what's it look like I'm doing? Silly outsider...
    - **Player:** Erm, good luck with that.
    - **Fisherman Frodi:** Thanks.
  - [Choice]: Your approval rating.
    - **Player:** What do people think of me around here?
    - (Condition: If the player's approval rating is low:)
      - **Fisherman Frodi:** You haven't really won the hearts of the locals, if you see what I mean. At the last poll, only 25% of the population liked you.
    - (Condition: If the player's approval rating is high:)
      - **Fisherman Frodi:** {{Transcript missing}}

#### Attempting to use a fishing spot

- **Fisherman Frodi:** Hey! This is the king's fishing spot. You'll need his approval to make use of it.

### After Throne of Miscellania

  - [Choice]: Fisherman Frodi.

    - **Player:** Just teasing!
    - (end)
  - [Choice]: Your approval rating.
    - **Player:** What do people think of me around here?
    - (Condition: If the player's approval rating is low:)
      - **Fisherman Frodi:** {{Transcript missing}}
    - (Condition: If the player's approval rating is high:)
      - **Fisherman Frodi:** {{Transcript missing}}
    - (Condition: If the player's approval rating was high and then dimished:)
      - **Fisherman Frodi:** Your people are growing dissatisfied with the regime, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, only [*x*]% of the population supported you.
