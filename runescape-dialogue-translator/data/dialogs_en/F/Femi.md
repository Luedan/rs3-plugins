## Dialogue Transcript


### First time dialogue

- **Femi:** Hello there.
- **Player:** Hi!
- **Femi:** Could you help me lift these boxes? They are really heavy!
- Select an option
  - Sorry, I'm a bit busy.
    - **Player:** Sorry, I'm a bit busy.
    - **Femi:** Oh, OK, I'll do it myself.
  - OK then.
    - **Player:** OK then.
    - **Femi:** Thanks traveller!
    - {{Qact|The player walks up to the nearby boxes and picks them up.}}
    - **Femi:** Thanks again friend!

### Subsequent dialogue

- The little gnome is too busy to talk.

### Treasure Trails

- **Femi:** Well done!
- (removes=the old clue scroll (medium))
- ((If the trail continues:))
  - (receives=a new clue scroll (medium))
  - Femi has given you another clue scroll!
- ((If the trail ends:))
  - (receives=reward casket (medium))
  - You've found a casket!
