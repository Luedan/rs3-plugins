## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*One of the following dialogues plays at random:*{{anchor|serf}}
- *Dialogue 1*
  - **Player:** How's the King treating you then?
  - **Freygerd:** Like serfs.
  - **Player:** Serf?
  - **Freygerd:** Yes, you know - peons, plebs, the downtrodden. He treats us like his own personal possessions.
  - **Player:** You should leave this place.
  - **Freygerd:** I keep trying to save up enough to leave, but the King keeps taxing us! We have no money left.
  - **Player:** Oh dear.
- *Dialogue 2*
  - **Player:** It's a bit grey round here, isn't it?
  - **Freygerd:** It gets you down after a while, you know. There are 273 shades of grey, you know, and we have them all.
  - **Player:** That's grey-t.
  - **Freygerd:** That attempt at humour merely made me more depressed. Leave me alone.
- *Dialogue 3*
  - **Player:** How are you today?
  - **Freygerd:** **sigh**
  - **Player:** That good? Everyone around here seems a little depressed.
  - **Freygerd:** **sigh**
  - **Player:** And not particularly talkative.
  - **Freygerd:** **sigh**
  - **Player:** I'll leave you to your sighing. It looks like you have plenty to do.
- *Dialogue 4*
  - **Player:** Cheer up! It's not the end of the world.
  - **Freygerd:** I'd prefer that, if it meant I didn't have to talk to people as insanely happy as you.
  - **Player:** Whoa! I think you need to get out more.
