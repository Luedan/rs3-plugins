  - ((If the players has the Gators cosmetic override active:))
    - **Frito:** Hold on a second...

    - **Frito:** Aren't they just? Look at them!

    - **Player:** Well, thank you. I'm glad someone appreciates fashion around here.
    - **Frito:** Indeed. If you want to make a fashion statement you have to be bold, and that is exactly what you've done here.
    - **Frito:** Ignore what anyone else says. THOSE are the shoes of the next generation.
    - (continue)
  - (continue)
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - How are you?
    - **Player:** How are you?
    - **Frito:** Fabulous.
    - **Player:** That's it? Nothing about what a wonderful place this is and how much you're enjoying it?
    - **Frito:** Nope. Now, if you have nothing better to ask, you're blocking the sunlight.
    - (initial|options)
  - Why haven't you eaten me?
    - **Player:** Why haven't you eaten me?
    - **Frito:** Oh please. There are much better-tasting foods out there than human. Take fruit, for example. Fruit pies, salads, smoothies, and more! Juicy, healthy, and absolutely delicious.
    - **Player:** I guess not all crocodiles are uncivilised beasts.
    - **Frito:** Indeed, some of us have good taste. I think my kind are gradually opening up to the possibility of other diets.
    - (initial|options)
  - Do you know Chito and Dundee?

    - **Frito:** Yes, they invited me here. I was having a rather splendid time down by Menaphos, but Chito is my cousin, so how could I say no?

    - **Frito:** I don't see it.
    - **Player:** Really? If you guys stood side-by-side, I wouldn't be able to tell who's who!
    - **Frito:** You really must get your eyes tested, Player.
    - **Player:** ...
    - (initial|options)
  - You look dazzling! (Only appears if the golden crocodile cosmetic upgrade is applied)
    - **Player:** You look dazzling!
    - **Frito:** I do, don't I? Much better than my boring old green look.
    - **Player:** Dundee made it for you!
    - **Frito:** Oh? That's surprising.
    - **Player:** How so?
    - **Frito:** He's not exactly the most fashionable person around here.
    - **Player:** I can't say I disagree...

### Using items on her with crocspeak


#### Coconut (but not coconut (open))

- **Frito:** COCONUT! Fruit from Crondis herself!
- Frito eats the coconut whole, cracking the shell cleanly in one big munch.
- (removes=coconut)
- **Frito:** You know, I wasn't sure about you, Player. But that was a really kind gesture.
- **Player:** Ah, it was nothing really. Enjoy!
- ((If Fruit Glorious Fruit hasn't been completed:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Fruit Glorious Fruit' - Feed Frito her favourite fruit.

- **Frito:** Fruit! Delicious fruit!
- Frito gobbles the fruit down before you have a chance to react.
- (removes=fruit item)
