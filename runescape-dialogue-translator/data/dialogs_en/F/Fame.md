## Dialogue Transcript


### The Drop: 15th Anniversary Edition.

- Tell me about the Drop: 15th Anniversary Edition.
  - *If the player completed Deathcon II*
    - **Fame:** Hello again sugar, my clever friend who completed the puzzle book.
  - **Fame:** I'm back, by popular demand. For the 15th Aniversary celebrations they've asked me to host a special edition of The Drop. We've hand-picked questions all about the history of RuneScape. If you get them all right, you'll get a new title and a huge prismatic star! I'm sure you'll be a winner in no time, you can't keep talent down.
- Enter The Drop: 15th Anniversary Edition.
  - *If The Drop has not started*
    - **Fame:** The Drop: 15th Anniversary Edition starts in X minutes.
- What are the rewards?
  - **Fame:** If you get all the answers correct sweetie, you can get the limited edition title, 'the Historian' and a huge bonus XP star! You've got to get them all right in one go though to get them, so keep trying! Then you'll get a star for the star you are. Break a leg, sweetie!
- Farewell.

### Anniversary island

- **Fame:** Good to see you sweetie!
- **Player:** You're looking well as always, Fame.
- Select an option
  - Why did you move the statues?
    - **Fame:** I loved the garden that Pete and I made for the fifteenth anniversary celebrations, and I wanted to make sure that it was preserved. It was so gorgeous, and the world deserves to keep beauty like that. And, there was the lovely serendipity of the Arc, and all of these new islands, so I knew just what I had to do. I claimed one as my own and got the statues shipped over here. So they can spend the rest of time in this glorious atmosphere and so everyone can enjoy them. Don't they look lovely?
    - **Player:** Absolutely. It's quite the setting for them.
    - (initial|initial)
  - Why are you here?
    - **Fame:** Well, darling, why wouldn't I be? Don't you just love the atmosphere? Every star has to have their bolt-hole, their little piece of tranquillity where they can truly be themselves. This is that place for me. My little haven.
    - **Player:** So you're staying here?
    - **Fame:** Well, if my public calls for me, I will of course answer them, it's only right. But for now, this is my home.
    - (initial|initial)
  - What are you planning to do next?
    - **Fame:** It's tiring having so much talent, so I'm taking this time to recuperate so I can come back at full capacity. Feel free to visit me from time to time, sweetie, your company is always welcome. But, yes, I shall be here until I am completely reinvigorated.
    - (initial|initial)
  - Bye.
    - **Fame:** Break a leg, sweetie!
    - (end)
