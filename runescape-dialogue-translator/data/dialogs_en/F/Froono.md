# Froono

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Froono](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFroono)
- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Froono:** Pretty Trees!
- **Player:** Having fun?
- **Froono:** I'm just looking at the magic trees. I think they're cool.
- **Player:** Well, they are magical. Valuable as well...
- **Froono:** When I grow up I want to chop down trees and make magic bows. Then I'd be rich.
- **Player:** Well, that does seem to be a common career around here.
