## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Fisher:** Welcome, fellow dreamer.
- ((Before having built the fishery:))
  - **Fisher:** If you could provide me some resources from your base camp, I could create a fishery for you to train at within this dream, and teach you something of ilujankan fishing techniques.
- ((After having built the fishery but before having made progress:))
  - **Fisher:** Thank you for the clay. If you keep supplying me with more, I can teach you much about Ilujankan fishing techniques.
- ((After a small amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Fisher:** You are learning much of our ways. Keep on as you are, and I will share a story with you.
- ((After a great amount of progress has been made:))
  - **Fisher:** You have learned much. I shall soon share my tale with Paskeeper Sharrigan.

### Contribute resources


#### Without having built the fishery

  - You must build a station before you can contribute resources to it.

#### Having built the fishery


##### With space for more resources

    - Yes.
      - The fishery now has [number]/350,000 Fishing XP stored in it.
