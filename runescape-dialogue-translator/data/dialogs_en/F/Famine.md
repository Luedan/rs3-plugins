## Dialogue Transcript


### Strange glowing orb (vibrating)

- **Famine:** You've unlocked the next step.
- **Player:** Really?
- **Famine:** Yes.
- **Player:** Like really though? This is the next step?
- **Famine:** Why not? I know Mazcab lore.
- **Player:** But but...
- **Famine:** HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA. I'm joking. But I've been asked to give you a message from a hooded stranger... It reads... 'Mod Raven woz ere.'

### At Stormguard Citadel Dig Site


#### Talk to

- Famine does not acknowledge you. She releases a heavy sigh.

#### Using a primal feast

- Famine is not amused by your antics.
- ((If The Feast and the Famine hasn't been completed:))
