## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Fairy chef:** 'Ello, sugar. I'm afraid I can't gossip right now, I've got a cake in the oven.

### Taking a chocolate bar while next to the fairy chef

- **Fairy chef:** Oi you! Did you think I didn't see that? That's my chocolate bar, that is!
- **Player:** Oh, sorry. I didn't realise it belonged to anybody.
- **Fairy chef:** That's alright, honey. You look a bit thin anyway. Take it if you need it.
