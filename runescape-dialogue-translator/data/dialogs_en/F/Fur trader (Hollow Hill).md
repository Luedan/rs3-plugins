## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** These hides are very soft. Did you trap these yourself?
- **Fur trader:** Hunt... skin...
- **Fur trader:** Fur keep cold out... wind bite less.
- **Player:** They look heavy.
- **Fur trader:** Heavy... means warm.
- **Fur trader:** This fur... death in snow.
