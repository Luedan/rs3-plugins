# Farmer Brumty

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Farmer Brumty](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AFarmer%20Brumty)
- **Letter**: `F`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Sheep Herder

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Farmer Brumty:** I have all the bad luck. My sheep all run off somewhere, and then those mourners tell me they're infected!
- **Player:** Well, I hope things start to look up for you.

### After Sheep Herder

- **Player:** Hello there. Sorry about your sheep...
- **Farmer Brumty:** That's alright. It had to be done for the sake of the town. I just hope none of my other livestock get infected.

### After Plague's End

- **Player:** Hello there. Have you heard the truth about your sheep?
- **Farmer Brumty:**  Aye. Councillor Halgrive told me about the fake plague. My poor sheep suffered and died for a pack of liars...it's the innocent who pay the price for man's ambition. To tell you the truth...
- ''If you killed King Lathas
  - **Farmer Brumty:** I'm glad you killed King Lathas. We were all sheep in his eyes. You brought justice to these lands.
- ''If you exiled King Lathas
  - **Farmer Brumty:** I am glad you exiled King Lathas. Good riddance to him. Though I hear his cousin's not much better.
