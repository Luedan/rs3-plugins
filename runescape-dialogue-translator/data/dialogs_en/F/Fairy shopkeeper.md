## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When talking to the Fairy shopkeeper in Zanaris.*
- **Fairy shopkeeper:** Can I help you at all?
- Select an option
  - Yes, please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=Zanaris General Store)
  - How should I use your shop?
    - **Fairy shopkeeper:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - No, thanks.
    - (end)
