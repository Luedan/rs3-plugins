### Post-quest dialogue, before [[Heart of Stone]]


#### After [[A Tail of Two Cats]]

- **Xenia:** I hear you learned Bob the Cat's secret. You're part of an exclusive club.
- **Xenia:** I grew up hearing stories of Robert the Strong. To think that he was real, and he's living among us...
- **Xenia:** Hah. Maybe in a few thousand years you'll be a cat, or I will, and some future adventurer will learn our secrets.

#### After [[All Fired Up]]

- **Xenia:** A lot of people feel safer now that you've completed the beacon network. I know I do.
- **Xenia:** There have been so many incursions from the Wilderness over the last few decades - more than most people realise. We need a quick way of calling heroes into action when we're threatened.
- **Xenia:** You've set up a system, and I hope you'll be there to answer the call when the beacons are lit.

#### After [[The Blood Pact]]

- **Xenia:** I visited Ilona in the Wizards' Tower the other day. She's doing well.
- **Xenia:** The world needs heroes. I was a hero{{Sic}}, once, but I'm not getting any younger. I need to make sure the new generation has its own heroes.

#### After [[Demon Slayer]]

- **Xenia:** So, you're the hero who slew Delrith, eh?
- **Xenia:** Gypsy Aris has been predicting for years that Delrith would return, but a hero would defeat him. For a while, I thought it might be me, then I thought it might be one of my proteges, maybe Sir Owen.
- **Xenia:** I'm glad it turned out to be you.

#### After [[Dragon Slayer]]

- **Xenia:** My contacts in the Champion's Guild told me how you dealt with Elvarg the dragon. I'm glad someone has finally slain that beast. You're quite the hero.

#### After [[Heroes' Quest]]

- **Xenia:** Achiettes told me she let you into the Heroes' Guild. Congratulations.
- **Xenia:** I wish I could convince Achiettes to become an adventurer. She's set so many challenges, and she's well-travelled and experienced, but she's never taken the leap...
- **Xenia:** Some people just don't believe in themselves enough, even though they have the talent, and nothing I say can convince them.

#### After [[Legends' Quest]]

- **Xenia:** You've made it. You're in the Legends' Guild: you're a legend in your own lifetime. Congratulations.
- **Xenia:** When Radimus and I started that guild, we weren't sure enough adventurers would meet the requirements to justify it. You and adventurers like you have proved Radimus right and me wrong.

#### After [[The Restless Ghost]]

- **Xenia:** Father Aereck told me that it was you put that old ghost in the graveyard here to rest.
- **Xenia:** It wasn't until Father Uhrney discovered the ghostspeak amulet that I knew how evil necromancy was. Whenever a necromancer disturbs someone's grave, the spirit can't rest until the remains are restored.
- **Xenia:** I've encountered ghosts who've been trapped for hundreds of years because someone disturbed their bones. By the time I put them to rest, they had gone mad. I'm glad the poor soul in Lumbridge didn't have to suffer that.

#### After [[Rune Mysteries]]

- **Xenia:** Ariane told me you helped her get into the ruins of the old Wizards' Tower. It can't have been easy: I know how secretive the Tower Wizards can be.

#### After [[Ritual of the Mahjarrat]]

- **Xenia:** Finally, Lucien got his comeuppance, thanks to you. You do them proud - the memory of those who died at his hands.

#### After [[Swan Song]]

- **Xenia:** Dissy - Dionysius, I mean - told me you'd worked together.
- **Xenia:** He said that was his last adventure, his 'Swan Song'. I could tell you about all the times he's said that before!

#### After [[Vampyre Slayer]]

- **Xenia:** Old Doctor Harlow told me about you. I hear you've taken up his vampyre-slaying mantle and finally put old Count Draynor down.
- **Xenia:** Back in the day, I helped Harlow kill a few of the vampyres who'd crossed into Misthalin, but Count Draynor was holed up in his manor and we couldn't get at him.
- **Xenia:** Draynor was the last vampyre west of Salve. I'm glad you've finished him.

#### Receiving the [[Helmet of Trials]]

- **Xenia:** Glad you came by. I've got something for you. You're one of the most accomplished heroes I've ever seen. You've done things I can scarcely imagine, and I've lost count of the people who owe you their lives. If there is any justice when history is written, your name will be among the greatest heroes of old. With Arrav, with Camorra; with Robert the Strong. I've talked to some people at the Legends's and Heroes' guilds, and we've created a special prize for you. Think of it as a token of recognition from the whole adventuring community.
  - **Player:** Thank you, Xenia, This is an honour.
    - **Xenia:** The honour is mine. You truly are an outstanding hero. I know with you on the scene, the world is in safe hands. Farewell, adventurer.
  - **Player:** I don't feel like i deserve this.
    - **Xenia:** I know how you feel. I've felt the same way for a lot of my adventuring career. There's always so much more to do - challenges to face, injustices to right. A hero's work is never done. Don't let that blind you to what you've already achieved though. Thinking about what you've accomplished so far can be a source of strength. Remember that. Farewell, adventurer.
  - **Player:** About time I got some recognition
    - **Xenia:** Ha ha! I see you didn't get where you are today by being humble! Well, if anyone has a right to be proud of himself/herself then it's you. I can't wait to see what you do next. Farewell, adventurer.
  - **Player:** I don't want your stupid helmet.
    - **Xenia:** Oh. I just thought that... Oh, well, never mind, then.
