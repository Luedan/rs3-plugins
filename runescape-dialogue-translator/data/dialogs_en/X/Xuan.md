## Dialogue Transcript


### In New Varrock

- **Xuan:** Good day, my friend! Good day!
- **Xuan:** It is my privilege to offer you access to an exclusive stock of the finest and most... nutritious... wares.
- **Select an Option**
  - [Choice]: Show me what you have.
    - (opens=Xuan's Fresh Produce)
  - [Choice]: Can I ask about your stock?
    - **Xuan:** Of course. Nothing would bring me greater joy!
    - **Xuan:** Age-worn botanists in lands far beyond this grew these fantastic vegetables with such precision and skill that they can re-vitalise your life energy. Each is a treasure beyond value.
    - **Player:** How do I use them?
    - **Xuan:** Why, you eat them. When you consume one of my delicious vegetables, the nutritious energy will create a wondrous and most satiating glow.
    - **Xuan:** The botanists call this effect 'fullness'. It is a curious bloated feeling. You may feel quite tired when 'full'. I would advise that you do not operate heavy machinery.
    - **Player:** What powers do they have?
    - **Xuan:** Each is unique! Some aid you with combat, some with training. Some are simply yummy in a casserole. I even heard of a vegetable which turned cannonballs into squid!
    - **Xuan:** The power will not last forever. Some time after consuming one of my life-sustaining vegetables, you may find that the hunger rises again in you. How long depends on the size of the vegetable.
    - **Xuan:** I'm also offering a special guarantee: should you discover a rotten vegetable in my stock, return it to me and I shall give you a full refund!
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Xuan:** One hundred thousand apologies! I am Xuan. The land of my birth lies far from here, by my home now is New Varrock, for the ruler of this city does not allow me to leave.
  - [Choice]: That's all, thanks.
    - **Xuan:** You are most welcome. Please, return as often as you like.

### Historical


#### In Burthorpe and Varrock

- **Xuan:** Good day, my friend! Good day!
- ((If the player has an unclaimed reward from a jack of trades aura:))
  - **Xuan:** Wait! I have something here for you...
  - (receives=a normal/master/supreme/legendary reward book, depending on aura tier)
  - [Xuan hands you your reward.]
  - (continue)
- **Xuan:** It is my privilege to offer you access to an exclusive stock of the finest and most exotic wares.
- **Select an Option**
  - [Choice]: Show me what you have.
    - (opens=Loyalty Programme Shop)
  - [Choice]: Can I ask you about your stock?
    - **Xuan:** Of course. Nothing would bring me greater joy!
    - **Select an Option**
      - [Choice]: Tell me about auras.
        - **Xuan:** Age-worn mystics in lands far beyond this carved the bones of fantastical beasts with such precision and skill that they can channel the power of magic. Each is a treasure beyond value.
        - **Player:** How do I use them?
        - **Xuan:** Why, you wear them. When you activate one, the magical energy will create a wondrous and very visible glow.
        - **Player:** What powers do they have?
        - **Xuan:** Each is unique! Some aid you with combat, some with training. I even heard of one which turned cannonballs into squid!
        - **Xuan:** The power will not last forever. After using it for a time, you need to let it replenish itself. How long depends on the aura.
        - **Xuan:** I'm also offering a special guarantee; the auras are enchanted so that, if you lose one, just come back to me and I'll return it to you!
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the outfits.
        - **Xuan:** I have travelled in many lands and seen ten thousand wonders, and all those which could be worn, I have brought here for your perusal.
        - **Xuan:** As if that wasn't enough, each outfit comes in several colours. One purchase gives you every colour!
        - Outfits can be activated from the Customisation interface.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about recolouring items.
        - **Xuan:** I can recolour some of your items. Have a look around the shop and you'll see which ones.
        - Purchasing the ability to recolour a specific item allows you to recolour that item as often as you like.
        - You must own the specific item and have it equipped or in your inventory for Xuan to recolour it. If you lose or sell the item, you will need to recover the item in order to recolour it.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about emotes.
        - **Xuan:** The things I have seen! Firebreathers! Stormcallers! The transcendence of gravity! I can teach you such techniques, or at least a passable imitation.
        - Purchased emotes are permanently unlocked for your character.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Tell me about titles.
        - **Xuan:** In many lands, titles of worth can be purchased. I have documentation here to claim such a title for yourself. Once owned, you may present yourself as any of your titles.
        - Titles appear before your name. For example, you could be called [Lady/Sir] Player. You can switch between any titles you have purchased.
        - Titles can be activated from the Customisation interface.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: [Learn about Loyalty Points.]
    - Loyalty Points are earned with continued RuneScape membership. The number of points you earn each month increases each month you remain subscribed. You can use points to purchase things from this shop. To learn more, visit our website.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Xuan:** One hundred thousand apologies! I am Xuan. The land of my birth lies far from here, but my home now is the trade routes of this world.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: That's all, thanks.
    - **Xuan:** You are most welcome. Please, return as often as you like.

#### Summer Beach Party

- **Select an Option**
  - [Choice]: How does the Terrorbird Racing work?
    - **Xuan:** These races are an extra part of the beach festivities for this year. They're very exciting. You can race once an hour and the idea is to get round all of the 8 gates in the quickest time possible. The quicker you are the greater your reward will be at the end of the race. As you go through each gate you'll be told where the next gate can be found. Remember, you'll need to be wearing a Terrorbird mount to be able to take part. It adds to the fun!
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Where can I get a Terrorbird costume?
    - **Xuan:** You can wear your own Terrorbird mount costume, but if you haven't got one I have a crate full I can lend to you. Simply grab one out of the box and try it on. I'll return to the box tomorrow, but you can always get another.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: How do the hi-scores work?
    - **Xuan:** The top 10 quickest times are stored and can be seen by checking on any of the race gates. Hopefully you'll make the top 10!
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Bye for now.
    - **Xuan:** Happy racing!
