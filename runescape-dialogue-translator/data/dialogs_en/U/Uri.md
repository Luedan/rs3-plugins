# Uri

## Dialogue Transcript


### Mid clue

*When spoken to in the correct costume and after wearing the correct outfit, one of these will be said at random*
- **Uri:** Once, I was a poor man, but then I found a party hat.
- **Uri:** There were three goblins in a bar, which one left first?
- **Uri:** Would you like to buy a pewter spoon?
- **Uri:** In the end, only the three-legged survive.
- **Uri:** I heard that the tall man fears only strong winds.
- **Uri:** In Canifis the men are known for eating much spam.
- **Uri:** I am the egg man, are you one of the egg men?
- **Uri:** The sudden appearance of a deaf squirrel is most puzzling, comrade.
- **Uri:** I believe that it is very rainy in Varrock.
- **Uri:** The slowest of fishermen catch the swiftest of fish.
- **Uri:** It is quite easy being green.
- **Uri:** Don't forget to find the jade monkey.
- **Uri:** I am the milkman. My milk is delicious.
- **Uri:** The brown cow flies at midnight.
- **Uri:** I wear the cheese; it does not wear me.
- **Uri:** How much hide would a hidey-hole hide if a hidey-hole could hide hide?
*The player responds with*
- **Player:** What?
- You've found a scroll box!
- (Uri waves with his suitcase and teleports away)
*When not following the instructions*
- **Uri:** I do not believe we have any business, comrade.

### At the end of a clue

- You've found a casket!
- (Uri waves with his suitcase and teleports away)
