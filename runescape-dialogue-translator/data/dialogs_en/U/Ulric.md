## Dialogue Transcript


### Information during the Travelling Artisan event

- ((First time:))
  - **Player:** Hey, what's going on here?
  - **Ulric:** Hello. I'm Ulric, a Travelling Artisan, and this is my Mental Health Awareness event!
  - **Ulric:** During this event, there are plenty of unique rewards to be crafted.
  - **Ulric:** You gather the resources, and I'll do the crafting!
  - **Ulric:** With each recipe crafted, I'll gain experience.
  - **Ulric:** Experience allows me to level up, which allows me to craft better recipes for you!
  - **Ulric:** Everyone's a winner!
  - **Ulric:** Additionally, I've invited representatives from the CPSL Mind, Rise Above The Disorder, and the Prince's Trust charities to come along for its duration.
  - **Ulric:** Be sure to chat with them every 2 days, as they'll each have a new question to ask you.
  - **Ulric:** Each question answered correctly will bag you a Small prismatic lamp, and 100 Travelling Artisan Patches.
  - **Player:** Great, thanks for the information!
  - (continue)
- ((Afterwards:))
  - **Player:** Hey!
  - **Ulric:** Hello. How can I help you?
  - (continue)
