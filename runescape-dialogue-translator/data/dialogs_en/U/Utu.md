- **Utu:** ...

- **Utu:** ...
- **Utu:** Very well. It shall be your duty. Explore the history of the temple. Help me restore my memories.
- **Player:** I'll get started, then. Thank you, Utu.

    - (Condition: As a pup:)
      - **Utu:** I remember little of myself. Of my past. This temple is all I know.
      - **Player:** Have you ever been inside?
      - **Utu:** No. When I approach its door a voice echoes across my mind. A warning. A command. To never enter the temple's depths.
      - **Player:** A voice in your head? Whose voice?
      - **Utu:** I do not know. But it is familiar.
      - **Utu:** Perhaps it is my own.
      - (previous|MoonriseOptions)
    - (Condition: As an adult:)
      - **Utu:** I woke before the Lunar Wheel many cycles ago, knowing naught but my name and my duty: to watch over this temple.
      - **Utu:** In truth, it is more than duty that keeps me here. I am bound to this place. To its past. Its secrets. To the very stone into which it is carved.
      - **Player:** What do you mean? You can't leave?
      - **Utu:** Can the moon flee the sky, to walk with mortals upon the land?
      - **Utu:** From the moment I awoke, an immeasurable sorrow has raged within my soul. Shame. Regret. Like a parasite, slowly consuming me from within.
      - **Utu:** I must know its cause. Until my memories have been restored, I will never know peace.
      - **Player:** You may not like what you learn.
      - **Utu: ** So be it. No knowledge could cause such suffering as this.
      - (previous|MoonriseOptions)
  - [Choice]: What is this dig site about?
    - (Condition: As a pup:)
      - **Utu:** This place stirs many feelings. Hollowness. Shame. Disgrace.
      - **Player:** Why's that?
      - **Utu:** I do not know. Perhaps I did, once.
      - **Utu:** It is lonely, standing watch over this temple. Two humans visited before you. One your size. One nearer mine. I had only recently awoken. I dared not reveal myself to them.
      - **Utu:** I has been less lonely, since meeting you.
      - **Utu:** I'm glad to hear that.
      - (previous|MoonriseOptions)
    - (Condition: As an adult:)
      - **Utu:** This is the great temple of Akshur-Nakkir. Jewel of the Nakkirian people. Seat of the divine.
      - **Utu:** We were as gods to the people who lived here. They came to us with offerings and worship. We granted them our blessings in return.
      - **Player:** Those who live here now call this place 'Moonrise'.
      - **Utu:** An apt name, for we held the moon in high regard.
      - **Utu:** The Lunar Wheel was a gift from Guthix, the slumbering god, himself. Carved by his own hands when he shaped this world. I cannot recall the true extent of its power.
