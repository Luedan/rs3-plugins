## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock


#### Before From Tiny Acorns, or on a free-to-play world

- The dwarf is hard at work on some sort of highly delicate construction, and doesn't pay you any notice.

#### After From Tiny Acorns

- **Urist Loric:** Sorry laddie, I'm not taking commissions at the moment.
- **Urist Loric:** I've lost almost all my capital, so I'm just selling tools until I make enough to go back into the jewellery and clockwork business.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can I see what you have?
    - (opens=Urist's Crafting Supplies)
  - [Choice]: cond=If the player does not have the craftsman's monocle|Is that your talisman by your foot there?
    - **Player:** Is that your talisman by your foot there?
    - **Urist Loric:** Where?
    - **Player:** Yoink!
    - [As he looks away you take the monocle from his top pocket.]
    - (receives=Craftsman's monocle)
  - [Choice]: I'm sorry to hear that.
    - **Player:** I'm sorry to hear that.
