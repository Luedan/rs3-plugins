## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### If spoken to without wearing a camulet

- What would you like to do?
  - Ask the camel about its dung.
    - **Player:** I'm sorry to bother you, but could you spare me a little dung?
    - The camel didn't seem to appreciate that question.
  - Say something unpleasant.
    - *Player may say any one of the following at random:*
      - *Dialogue 1:*
        - **Player:** Mmm... Looks like that camel would make a nice kebab.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *Dialogue 2:*
        - **Player:** I wonder if that camel has fleas...
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *Dialogue 3:*
        - **Player:** If I go near that camel, it'll probably bite my hand off.
        - *(Continues below)*
          - *Ugthanki may do any of the following at random:*
            - *Response 1:*
              - The camel turns its head and glares at you.
            - *Response 2:*
              - The camel tries to stamp on your foot, but you pull it back quickly.
            - *Response 3:*
              - The camel spits at you, and you jump back hurriedly.
  - Neither - I'm a polite person.
    - You decide not to be rude.
