## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Zogre Flesh Eaters

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Hey, what's going on here?
    - **Player:** Hey, what's going on here?
    - **Uglug Nar:** Dem's dead ogre's come out of the ground...dey's makin' da rest of us into sick-ums ...and dead-uns.
    - **Player:** That doesn't sound good!
    - **Uglug Nar:** Grish want's da person go down der - see what's what!
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - **Player:** What are you selling?
    - **Uglug Nar:**  Me's not got no glug-glugs to sell, yous bring me da sickies glug-glug den me's open da stufsies for ya.
  - [Choice]: Ok, thanks.
    - **Player:** Ok, thanks.
