# Snow mage (2007 Christmas event)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Snow mage (2007 Christmas event)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASnow%20mage%20%282007%20Christmas%20event%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- (Condition: Out of combat)
  - **Snow mage:** Let the snow rangers and warriors step up to fight. I shall show them that Magic is the mightiest art.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a warrior)
  - **Snow mage:** Foolish warrior. Your helm will not protect you from my mighty spells.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a ranger)
  - **Snow mage:** Let us see whether your arrows of ice will win against my bolts of arcane power.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a mage)
  - **Snow mage:** En garde, fellow wizard. We shall see who of us is the stronger.
  - (end)
