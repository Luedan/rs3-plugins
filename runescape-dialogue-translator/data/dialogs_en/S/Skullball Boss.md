# Skullball Boss

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Skullball Boss](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASkullball%20Boss)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without the [[ring of Charos]]

- **Skullball Boss:** Grrr - you don't belong in here, human!
  - *(if wearing the werewolf outfit)*
    - **Player:** I'm not a human, I'm a lycanthrope, just look at my claws.
    - **Skullball Boss:** They're made from paper.
    - **Player:** Well I don't want to hurt anyone with them do I?
    - **Skullball Boss:** That would be rather the point.
    - **Player:** I would never do such a thing, I'm a pacifist werewolf.
    - **Skullball Boss:** Worst excuse ever.
    - **Player:** Yeah... Not my best.
    - **Skullball Boss:** Go away now.

### With the [[Ring of charos]] equipped

- I would like to do the skullball course.
  - **Player** I think I'll give the Skullball Course a go.
    - *starts the Werewolf Skullball activity*
      - What are the instructions for using the skullball course?
        - **Player** What are the instructions for using the skullball course?
        - **Skullball Boss** The skullball comes out of one of these four spawnholes. Just kick the ball through the middle of each goal, through the skeleton's feet.
        - **Skullball Boss** There are 10 goals, which you must complete in order, and one final goal.
        - **Skullball Boss** An arrow will point to your ball, just in case lots of people are using the course at the same time as yourself.
        - **Skullball Boss** The better your time, the more agillity XP you will be awarded. The timer starts when you score your first goal.
      - I seem to have lost my ball - can I have another one?
        - **Player** I seem to have lost my ball - can I have another one?
        - **Skullball Boss** No problem, here's another one. You'll have to start from the beginning again, but the timer will be restarted too.
          - *(Skullball activity is reset)*
      - I give up, I can't do it - take my ball away.
        - **Player** I give up, I can't do it - take my ball away.
        - **Skullball Boss** Oh dear, such a defeatist.
          - *(Ends activity)*
- What are the instructions for the skullball course?
  - **Player** What are the instructions for using the skullball course?
  - **Skullball Boss** The skullball comes out of one of these four spawnholes. Just kick the ball through the middle of each goal, through the skeleton's feet.
  - **Skullball Boss** There are 10 goals, which you must complete in order, and one final goal.
  - **Skullball Boss** An arrow will point to your ball, just in case lots of people are using the course at the same time as yourself.
  - **Skullball Boss** The better your time, the more agillity XP you will be awarded. The timer starts when you score your first goal.
    - *(Ends dialogoue)*
