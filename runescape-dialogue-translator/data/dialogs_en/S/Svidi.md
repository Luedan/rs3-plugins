- **Letter**: `S`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Mountain Daughter

- **Player:** Hello. Where are you going?
- **Svidi:** None of your business! Leave me alone!
- **Player:** It just seemed as if you were walking in circles.
- **Svidi:** No I'm not! Now leave me alone!
- **Player:** Okay, okay.

### After Mountain Daughter

- **Player:** Why are you still wandering around here?
- **Svidi:** I'm now the official liaison between Rellekka and our camp. It involves a fair bit of walking around, you know.
- **Player:** I can imagine.
