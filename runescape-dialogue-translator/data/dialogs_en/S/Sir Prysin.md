## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- **Sir Prysin:** Hello, who are you?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: I'm a mighty adventurer, who are you?
    - **Sir Prysin:** I'm Sir Prysin, a bold and famous knight of the realm.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I'm not sure, I was hoping you could tell me.
    - **Sir Prysin:** Well, I've never met you before.
    - (Condition: If the player is wielding Silverlight:)
      - **Sir Prysin:** Though I see that you wield Silverlight. It seems that my ancestor's sword has found a new champion.
      - **Sir Prysin:{{anchor|sought}}** I once sought to claim Silverlight. The spirits cared not for my inheritance, and judged my worth.
      - **Sir Prysin:** I was found wanting. Tell me. What trials did you face?
      - [Menu]: Select an option
        - [Choice]: (Tell the truth.)
          - **Player:** I defeated a few skeletons, took a walk of faith across a chasm and answered some trivia questions.
          - (continue|what)
        - [Choice]: (Exaggerate the trials.)
          - **Player:**  I fought a legion of undead...leapt a chasm of poisonous scorpions...and answered a series of devilish riddles and quadratic equations.
          - (continue|what)
        - [Choice]: (Mock the trials)
          - **Player:** I took a walk, had a chat and killed some things. I don't know how you could have failed.
          - **Sir Prysin:{{anchor|what}}** What? That's all? What of the nine-headed serpent I was made to battle? The journey into the abyss to claim the Candelabrum of Invocation?
          - **Sir Prysin:** And the less said about the medical examination, the better...
          - **Player:** Well, that was surprising.
          - (initial)
    - (Condition: If the player is wielding Darklight:)
      - **Sir Prysin:** I see that you wield Silverlight, though it has changed much since I last saw it. It seems my ancestor's sword has found a new champion.
      - (continuea|sought)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

### In New Varrock


#### Before Dimension of Disaster: Demon Slayer

- **Sir Prysin:** Whatever it is you're selling, I'm not interested.

#### After Dimension of Disaster: Demon Slayer

- **Sir Prysin:** I don't know how you've managed to get that sword so dirty after just one use. I know I'd take better care of it, but even though it's a family heirloom, I'm not worthy to wield it.
- **Player:** About that, what happened with you and the trials?
- **Sir Prysin:** You should know - you've been through them too! Didn't you find them difficult? What trials did you face?
- [Menu]: What will you tell Sir Prysin about the trials?
  - [Choice]: Tell the truth
    - **Player:** I sobered up a drunkard, aced a pop quiz and was pretty much just handed the third trial.
    - **Sir Prysin:** What? Is that all? What of the twelve-headed hydra cat I was made to battle? The journey into Zemouregal's sock drawer to claim the Nightcap of Terror?
    - **Sir Prysin:** And that whole thing with the coughing I found VERY uncomfortable. <br>I still have flashbacks about that.
  - [Choice]: Exaggerate the trials
    - **Player:** I performed an exorcism on a legendary vampyre hunter, after braving a dark cave that was home to a savage beast.
    - **Player:** Then an animated grimoire posed me a raft of near impossible questions, all of which I had to answer.
    - **Player:** Finally, I had to place my faith in another to guide me towards my future. I was entirely at their mercy.
    - (continuea|ehntor)
  - [Choice]: Mock the trials
    - **Player:** I hassled a bunch of spirits until they were so annoyed with me that they just gave me the sword to get some peace and quiet.
    - (continuea|ehntor)

#### Using the Nightcap of Terror on him

- [You glibly dangle the Nightcap of Terror in front of Sir Prysin's face.]
- **Sir Prysin:** Sweet Saradomin...you claimed the Nightcap of Terror, and live to tell the tale. How is this possible?
- **Player:** It wasn't difficult. I just had to purchase an extortionate zombie outfit, defeat undead warriors, collect the vapour from their necrotic flesh...
- **Player:** Endure a series of fetch quests to dye it orange, traffic in the criminal underworld to get a lockpick, and search through Zemouregal's junk in his study until I found it.
- **Sir Prysin:** His study?<br>So you didn't have to endure the...trial by underwear?
- **Player:** Sorry, what?
- **Sir Prysin:** Zemouregal's sock drawer. That's where they sent me. It was unspeakably vile. I dare not speak of it. You can't imagine the things I've seen.
- **Sir Prysin:** The nether garments...the black silk and frilly lace...<br>I'm grateful zombies don't need to sleep, for I dare never dream again.
