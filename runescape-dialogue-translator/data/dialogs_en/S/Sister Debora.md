# Sister Debora

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Sister Debora](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASister%20Debora)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before One Piercing Note

- The sister politely ignores you, as if she is not permitted to speak to you yet.

### After One Piercing Note

- **Sister Debora:** Hello again, adventurer.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: How are you doing?
    - **Player:** How are you doing?
    - **Sister Debora:**Oh, I'm fine. The clock is running smoothly.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Why didn't you tell Abbess Benita who the killer was?
    - **Player:** Why didn't you tell Abbess Benita who the killer was?
    - **Sister Debora:** You're the adventurer. You solved the mystery and defeated the killer. It was better that she should hear it from you.
    - *(If the player lied about The Killer's true identity:)*
      - **Player:** But I lied, and you didn't contradict me.
    - *(If the player told the truth about The Killer's true identity:)*
      - **Player:** But I could have lied.
    - **Sister Debora:** There's a saying: a stopped clock is right twice a day.
    - **Sister Debora:** Well, I don't like that saying. A clock ten minutes slow is never right, but it's a lot more useful than one that's stopped.
    - **Player:** What are you getting at?
    - **Sister Debora:** What I am getting at is, perhaps it's better for someone to have the wrong time, if that's what keeps their clock going. It's better than setting the clock right but having it stop.
    - **Player:** You think it was right for me to lie?
    - **Sister Debora:** I think that you were the mechanic, and it was your decision how best to fix the clock.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
