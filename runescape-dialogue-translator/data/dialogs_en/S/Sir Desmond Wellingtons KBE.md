## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- Select an option
  - Who are you?
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** Sir Desmond Wellingtons, Knight Commander of the Order of Bear Explorers, and Quartermaster of the Queen of Snow's Royal Guard at your service!
    - Sir Desmond straightens his neck, in what you assume would be a salute if his four paws were not being used for standing.
    - **Player:** And what brings a gentlebear of your stature to this place?
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** Important work as always. Here to bring cheer and goodwill on behalf of the Queen. We are in tough times at the moment, and Her Highness doesn't want to give Gielinor the cold shoulder. It might just finish everyone off, eh?
    - **Player:** A surprisingly warm sentiment from a queen of ice!
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** Well, in that vein, I've drawn up battle plans to give us all a chance to strike cheer into the hearts of our fellow bears. I have designated this Operation Secret Santa!
    - (initial)
  - Can you tell me about the Secret Santa?
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** Happy to, happy to! There are two phases to Operation Secret Santa. Phase one: Donate. Kindly individuals can donate their hard-earned items into my care. I will distribute them in phase two. Mind you, we don't want any tat, so there is a minimum donation value. Whatever you give must be worth at least 10,000 coins on the Grand Exchange. I've got special rewards too - for anyone going beyond the call of duty. Each day I can give you a medium prismatic lamp for donations totalling 100,000 coins in value. If you do this 15 times, I can bestow you with the title of Secret Santa! There are also special honours for anyone who donates 200,000,000 coins worth in one fell swoop: the title of Top Secret Santa! Phase two: Gift. Anyone can ask me for a present, and I will randomly select an item from my stores to give them. Don't get greedy though, eh? I'll only let you take a gift once every 2 hours. That's the plan. Should be a jolly good time.
    - (initial)
  - I'd like to donate to the Secret Santa.
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** Bally sporting of you, bally sporting indeed!
    - ((If the player isn't in Ironman Mode:))
      - ((Gift Donation interface opens.))
    - ((If the player is in Ironman Mode:))
      - As an Ironman you cannot partake in the greatest joy of all - gift giving. All donations from Ironmen will be destroyed in a controlled explosion in accordance with standard Ironman rules of engagement. Any remaining residue will be destroyed in a second, larger, explosion. Your wealth will not be transferred to other players, but you will be able to receive cosmetic titles for your donations, and progress for related yak track tasks.
      - Would you still like to donate items?
        - Yes.
          - ((Gift Donation interface opens.))
        - Yes and don't ask me again.
          - ((Gift Donation interface opens.))
        - No.
          - (end)
  - I'd like to claim a gift from the Secret Santa.
    - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** State your name and rank and I'll check if I have anything for you.
    - ((If the player has not claimed a gift in the last 2 hours:))
      - ((Gift Claim interface opens.))
      - ((If the player walks away or otherwise closes the gift claim interface without taking or banking the gift(s):))
        - {{Colour|#036|WARNING:|#fff9}} If you log out, items in the reward chest will be moved to your inventory or bank. If both of those places are full you will lose the items.
    - *(If the player has claimed a gift in the last 2 hours:)*
      - ((With over an hour of cooldown left:))
        - color=#AE0000
      - ((With between a minute and an hour of cooldown left:))
        - color=#AE0000
      - ((With less than a minute of cooldown left:))
        - color=#AE0000}}
  - Can you tell me about my daily reward progress?
    - ((Without having reached the daily 100,000 value:))
      - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** You need to donate items totalling at least a value of [number] coins to recieve a daily reward. You need to donate enough to claim a daily reward on [number] more days to receive the Secret Santa title.
      - (initial)
    - ((Having reached the daily 100,000 value:))
      - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** You have donated enough wealth to receive your daily reward today. If you haven't collected it from me you can claim it like you would claim a standard Secret Santa gift.
      - ((Without Secret Santa unlocked:))
        - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** You need to donate enough to claim a daily reward on [number] more days to receive the Secret Santa title.
        - (initial)
      - ((With Secret Santa unlocked:))
        - **Sir Desmond Wellingtons KBE:** You have donated items of high enough value on enough days to receive the Secret Santa title already.
        - (initial)
