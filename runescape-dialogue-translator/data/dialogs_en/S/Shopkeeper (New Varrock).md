## Dialogue Transcript


### In New Varrock

- **Shopkeeper:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=New Varrock General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Shopkeeper:** You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. <br>You can also sell most items to the shop.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Didn't you used to be a shop assistant?
    - **Shopkeeper:** Yes, the general store owner died last year when the demon Delrith rampaged through Varrock.
    - **Shopkeeper:** Delrith used him as a pillow to sit on.
    - **Shopkeeper:** It took me ages to work the stains out of the floorboards.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)
