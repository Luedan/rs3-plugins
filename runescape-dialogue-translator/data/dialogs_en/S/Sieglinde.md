## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue after Birthright of the Dwarves

- **Sieglinde:** Can I interest you in a warhammer? I now have dragon warhammers in stock!
- '''Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (opens=Sieglinde's Warhammers)
  - [Choice]: Dragon warhammers?
    - **Player:** Dragon warhammers?
    - **Sieglinde:** Yes! The Black Guard recovered schematics on how to make them from the Red Axe base. Supreme Commander Bisi has asked me to make them for the elite Black Guard berserkers, and I'm also selling them here.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: What happened to Vigr?
    - **Player:** What happened to Vigr?
    - *(If Vigr was exiled:)*
      - **Sieglinde:** He was exiled! The Black Guard marched him straight out of the city. I've heard a lot of exiles end up near Yanille.
    - *(If Vigr was executed:)*
      - **Sieglinde:** He was executed as a Red Axe spy! His remains will have been thrown into the Kelda. I suppose it's what he deserves if he was really working for the Red Axe.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - (end)

##### Using an item on her sink

- **Sieglinde:** Oi! Did I say you could use that?

##### Using an item on her fireplace

- **Sieglinde:** Hands off! That fireplace belongs to me! Not some puny arrogant human!
- **Player:** Didn't all this belong to the Red Axe? And weren't you a member?
- **Sieglinde:**  I don't know what you're talking about.
- **Sieglinde:**  Oi! Did I say you could use my fireplace?

##### Using an item on, or attempting to smelt in her furnace

- **Sieglinde:** Oi! Did I say you could use my furnace?
