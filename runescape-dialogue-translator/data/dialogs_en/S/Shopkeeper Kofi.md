## Dialogue Transcript


### Before A Void Dance

- **Shopkeeper Kofi:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - (opens=Karamja General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Shopkeeper Kofi:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)

### After A Void Dance

- **Shopkeeper Kofi:** Thanks for helping me out, friend. What can I do for you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - (opens=Karamja General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - **Player:** Nothing, thanks.
