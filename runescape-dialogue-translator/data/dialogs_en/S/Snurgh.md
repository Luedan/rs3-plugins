# Snurgh

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Snurgh](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASnurgh)
- **Letter**: `S`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before As a First Resort

- **Snurgh:** Outta de way, human. Dis place not open yet!
- **Player:** That's not very friendly.
- **Snurgh:** Me said OUTTA DE WAY! You no can sleep here!
- **Player:** Alright, alright! Keep your hat on! I never said I wanted to.

### After As a First Resort

- **Player:** How's business?
- **Snurgh:** Is okay. De sleepers like de fluffsie pillows. Dey good for small, soft heads like yours. You big help to Snurgh. If you ever wants to try dem out, you can stay free anytime.
- **Player:** Why, thank you. I'll keep that in mind.
