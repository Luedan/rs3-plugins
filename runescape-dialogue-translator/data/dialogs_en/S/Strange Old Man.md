### Standard dialogue


#### Without a spade in backpack

- **Strange Old Man:**  Dig, dig, DIG! You want to dig? You need a spade!
- **Player:**  Yes you're right, I probably do. Where can I get one?
- **Strange Old Man:**  Ohh... Spades have cost me so much hassle - always being pestered for them! I ended up giving in and just putting one at each mound for you forgetful adventurers to use.

*Note: All of these conversations can also start with the Strange Old Man saying "Dig, dig, dig." first.*

### Task System

- ((If the player has completed the hard Morytania achievements and the rewards have not been claimed:))
  - **Player:**  I've completed all of the Hard Tasks in the Morytania set.
  - **Strange Old Man:**  So you have. I have some special leggings kept aside for you.
  - [The Strange Old Man hands you a pair of leggings and a lamp.]
  - **Strange Old Man:** There you go. They be a bit strange, but they should help you out when you're getting rid of pesky shades once and for all.
  - **Player:**  Thanks! What else do they do?
  - **Strange Old Man:**  Well, ghosties will understand you, and I've had a word with Hiylik, so you'll get better rewards for helping unfortunates to the temple. Ahh...they also protect a little from the foul influence of the Barrows, and they seem to be linked with those...tablet things you use to get to the Altar of Blood.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player has lost their Morytania legs 3:)
  - **Strange Old Man:** Ah, the leggings-wearer! How are you finding them?
  - **Player:** I lost them.
  - **Strange Old Man:** Oh, no!
  - **Strange Old Man:** Never mind, I have another pair. Here...
  - (receives=Morytania legs 3)
  - [The Strange Old Man hands you a pair of leggings.]
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the Achievement System.
    - **Strange Old Man:** Very well: the Achievement System is a collection of deeds you may wish to complete while adventuring around the world.
    - **Strange Old Man:**You can earn special rewards for completing certain achievements; at the very least, each is worth a cash bounty from Explorer Jack in Lumbridge.
    - **Strange Old Man:** Some also give items that will help complete other achievements, and many count as progress towards the set for the area they're in.
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
        - **Player:** Tell me about the set reward for this area.
        - **Strange Old Man:** For completing the Morytania set, you are presented with some leggings.
        - **Strange Old Man:** These leggings will become increasingly useful as you complete greater difficulties of task sets.
        - **Strange Old Man:** When you are presented with your rewards, you will be told of their uses.
        - You may also see what rewards a set item provides by hovering over the Rewards icon for its Exploration Achievement set in the Achievements interface.
        - (previous2)
      - [Choice]: How do I claim these rewards?
        - **Player:** How do I claim the rewards?
        - **Strange Old Man:** You need to complete all Tasks within a particular difficulty set, then you can claim your reward.
        - **Strange Old Man:** Some of the Morytania set's Tasks are simple, some require certain skill levels, and others require quests to be started or completed.
        - **Strange Old Man:**  To claim your Morytania set rewards, speak to Hiylik Myna just east of Paterdomus, Robin in Port Phasmatys, Old Man Ral in Meiyerditch or myself.
        - (previous2)
      - [Choice]: Sorry, I was just leaving.
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: Could I buy a spare of the reward?
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Morytania legs 1
        - **Strange Old Man:** Morytania legs 1? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 1,500 coins.
        - [Menu]: Purchase another for 1,500 coins?
          - [Choice]: Yes
            - [You're handed another Morytania legs 1.]
            - (receives=Morytania legs 1) (removes= 1,500 coins)
          - [Choice]: No
            - **Player:** No thanks.
      - [Choice]: Morytania legs 2
        - **Strange Old Man:** Morytania legs 2? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 2,500 coins.
        - [Menu]: Purchase another for 2,500 coins?
          - [Choice]: Yes
            - [You're handed another Morytania legs 2.]
            - (receives=Morytania legs 2) (removes= 2,500 coins)
          - [Choice]: No
            - **Player:** No thanks.
      - [Choice]: Morytania legs 3
        - **Strange Old Man:** Morytania legs 3? Yes, but I'm afraid it would cost you 7,500 coins.
        - [Menu]: Purchase another for 7,500 coins?
          - [Choice]: Yes
            - [You're handed another Morytania legs 3.]
            - (receives=Morytania legs 3) (removes= 7,500 coins)
          - [Choice]: No
            - **Player:** No thanks.
      - [Choice]: Morytania legs 4
