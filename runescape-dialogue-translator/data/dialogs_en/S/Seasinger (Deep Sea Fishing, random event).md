## Dialogue Transcript


### Arriving

- **Seasinger:** {{RSChat|Hey [Player/sailors], listen to my song!}}

### Listening


#### Before listening successfully


##### Targeting the player

- You listen to the seasinger's song.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up message:))
  - You listen to the seasinger's song.

##### Targeting another player

- She's not interested in you.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up message:))
  - She's not interested in you.

#### After listening successfully

- You've already listened to her song.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up message:))
  - You've already listened to her song.
