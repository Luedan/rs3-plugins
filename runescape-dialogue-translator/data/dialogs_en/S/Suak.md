## Dialogue Transcript


### After [[Mining and Smithing rework]]

- **Player:**  Hey what's going on here?
- **Suak:** You make burial armour for fallen ancestors.
  - What is burial armour?
    - **Suak:** Dwarves die lots. Miners crushed by rocks. Warriors stabbed by goblins. Smiths get black lung. To honour our dead, we build them armour so they can keep on working in afterlife.
  - How can I make burial armour?
    - **Suak:** You smith full set of adamant armour (or better). Helm, body, legs, gauntlets and boots. Then you upgrade each piece until it is best. Then smith them into burial armour set at burial anvil. It is simple. But slow. Like wife of Suak! Or you are lazy. You buy fully upgraded armour. You smith burial armour set. Suak does not like the lazy smith.
  - Tell me more about yourself.
    - **Suak:** Suak not so smart with words, but good with hammer.
  - What's up with the suits of armour?
    - **Suak:** Dwarves not buried with armour will be unable to make way to afterlife. They come here. We give them burial armour. They go. Suak thinks they pass on.
  - What do I get from this?
    - **Suak:** To build armour for our beloved dead. An honour. Great experience for you. For helping our dead, you earn respect of Suak.
