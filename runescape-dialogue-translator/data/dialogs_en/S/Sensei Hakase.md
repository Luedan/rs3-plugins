### Before Tuai Leit's Own

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Sensei Hakase:** Greetings! Welcome to Tuai Leit.
- **Player:** Thanks. What is this place?
- **Sensei Hakase:** This is our humble centre of learning. All manner of beings come here to study, to better their knowledge of the natural world and the spiritual world.
- **Sensei Hakase:** Somewhere, something incredible is always waiting to be known.

### After Tuai Leit's Own

- **Player:** Thank you for your assistance in trying to cure my friend.
- **Sensei Hakase:** How are things going?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Explain the situation.
    - **Player:** We convinced the original necromancer to remove her curse not only from him, but many others who were affected by it. She did so by tearing a piece of paper that was stuck on a jiangshi's forehead.
    - **Sensei Hakase:** Poppycock! What drivel. I'm sure there must be some sort of physical or spiritual explanation behind all of this superstitious mystical twaddle.
    - **Sensei Hakase:** Next you'll be trying to convince me of the benefits of homeopathic medicines, as if tigers' dangly bits ever did anyone any good.
    - **Sensei Hakase:** Excuse my disbelief, though - I am most pleased that your friend has recovered.
  - [Choice]: Is there anything for me to learn?
    - **Player:** Is there anything left for me to learn?

      - **Sensei Hakase:** I've told you before that you have nothing left to learn. Why would you ask me again?
