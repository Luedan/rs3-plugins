## Dialogue Transcript


#### Standard dialogue

- **Speedy Keith:** To what do I owe the pleasure?
  - What's your story?
    - **Speedy Keith:** I could tell you, but I'd have to kill you.
    - **Player:** Given the circumstances I'm not sure if that's a joke...
    - **Speedy Keith:** Right now, I'm not sure either.
      - I'll take the risk.
        - **Speedy Keith:** Ha, you're bold indeed.
        - The surrounding bandits give you a worrying glare.
        - **Speedy Keith:** Alright, I'll bite. Used to make a pretty penny evicting a few poor souls from their bodies.
        - **Player:** An assassin, then.
        - **Speedy Keith:** You'd be surprised how often you'll find 'employment' in a 'civilised' place like Misthalin.
        - **Speedy Keith:** I hadn't thought that someone might eventually take offence. I'm known to the authorities now, a fugitive.
        - **Speedy Keith:** Speaking on the topic, got anyone around these parts you need taking care of? My rates are reasonable.
          - I'll keep your offer in mind.
            - **Speedy Keith:** At your service.
          - Ah. Erm. No thank you.
      - I'll, erm, just let you get back to your business.
  - You seem to be pretty welcoming now.
    - **Speedy Keith:** I'm getting a good feeling from you. You're marked by your intentions.
    - **Player:** You can tell, huh? It is a little thrilling, and a little scary.
    - **Speedy Keith:** Wonderful isn't it? Blood pumping, adrenaline flowing.
    - **Speedy Keith:** We have more trust in someone willing to put themselves at risk. The overly cautious don't belong in these lands.
  - How did you get your nickname?
    - **Speedy Keith:** Well, that's a long story.
    - **Player:** Go on.
    - **Speedy Keith:** You see...
    - **Speedy Keith:** my kills have been described as notably rapid.
    - **Speedy Keith:** And my real name?
    - **Speedy Keith:** ...
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Speedy Keith:** ...
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Speedy Keith:** ...
    - **Speedy Keith:** That's Keith.
    - **Player:** ...
  - Goodbye.
