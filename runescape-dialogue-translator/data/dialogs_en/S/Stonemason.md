- **Stonemason:** Do you want to buy any stone for building? Or gold leaf?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please
    - (opens= Keldagrim Stonemason)
  - [Choice]: No thanks

  - [Choice]: What kind of stone do you sell?
    - **Player:** What kind of stone do you sell?
    - **Stonemason:** A few kinds. Limestone, of course. Here in Keldagrim we're surrounded by the stuff so I can practically give it away! You'll be able to build some kinds of furniture out of that.
    - **Stonemason:** Marble is much better, of course, but it costs a lot for me to get hold of it. You can make some beautiful pieces of furniture out of that.
    - **Stonemason:** I also sell gold leaf, which can be used to decorate furniture made of marble or mahogany.
    - **Stonemason:** Then for the very special furniture I sell these magic stones. Only the most skilled builders can work them, but they can create a few amazing pieces of furniture with them.
    - **Stonemason:** So, do you want to buy anything?
