## Dialogue Transcript


### After Within the Light and before Plague's End

- **Sanchez:** What's up, mate?
- **Player:** You look new here, have I seen you before?
- **Sanchez:** Maybe, me and my bruv work all over the world - global cleaning business, global problems.
- **Player:** Global problems? What's that meant to mean?
- **Sanchez:** Oh, come on, mate, you must have travelled a bit? Surely you've seen the amount of stuff people just drop on the floor.
- **Player:** Hmm, I guess you have a point.
- **Sanchez:** Yup, so me and my bruv' Scotty formed S&S cleaners. Got picked up right away by a guy called Mandrith - payed us 76k an hour, if you'd believe that? Turns out he relocated, though, so we ended up here.
- **Player:** Good to hear it's working out for you.
- **Sanchez:** Oh, yeah, totally. I'd better get back to it, in fact.
