#### Taking 100 snowballs


##### With sufficient backpack space

- (receives=100 snowballs)
- You take some snowballs. Snowverload will now attack you if you have them equipped!
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You take some snowballs. Snowverload will now attack you if you have them equipped!}}

##### Without sufficient backpack space

- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You don't have enough space in your backpack to take any snowballs.|warning=yes}}

#### Mechanics


##### Attempting to pelt while transmogrified

- You can't attack while transmogrified into this form.
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You can't attack while transmogrified into this form.|warning=yes}}

##### Attempting to pelt without wielding snowballs

- You can only attack Snowverload with snowballs!
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You can only attack Snowverload with snowballs!|warning=yes}}

##### Bauble


##### Getting trapped

- Snowverload traps you in a bauble! Roll in front of them to be smashed free.
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|Snowverload traps you in a bauble! Roll in front of them to be smashed free.|warning=yes}}

##### Failing to smash the bauble

- You weren't in range for Snowverload to smash the bauble. Best you roll away!
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You weren't in range for Snowverload to smash the bauble. Best you roll away!|warning=yes}}

##### Shattering

- Snowverload shatters you free from the bauble!

##### Breaking free by escaping

- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You break free from the bauble!|colour=#00FF00}}

- You have already received rewards from killing Snowverload this hour so cannot claim any more.
- Try again when they next appear.
