### Shear


#### At Zanaris


##### If the sheep has not been sheared

- **Sheep:** Hello, I've just finished composing my latest poem. Would you like to hear it?
- Select an option
  - Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - **Sheep:** Excellent! The human's an aficionado! Make yourself comfortable and I'll begin...
    - *(Dialogue 1:)*
      - **Sheep:** How goes it, blade of grass?<br />I'm glad you're not any thinner<br />You should invite your friends around<br />So I can have my dinner!
    - *(Dialogue 2:)*
      - **Sheep:** I wish I were a cow<br />My life would be so simple<br />I could lie around just eating grass<br />And wonder if I were a pimple.
      - **Cow:** Hahaha, I don't think!
      - **Sheep:** Well I thought it was funny.
    - *(Dialogue 3:)*
      - **Sheep:** T'was brillig and the slithey toathes<br />Did gyre and gimbol in the wabe<br />All mimsy were the borrowgroves<br />And the mome raths outgrabe.
      - **Cow:** That's not even your poem, you idiotic, animated ball of wool!
      - **Sheep:** How now foolish cow? Do you read poetry, instead of pulling a plough?
      - **Cow:** I'm not talking to you any more, you're nasty.
      - **Sheep:** Oh no! Cow's not talking to me!
      - **Sheep:** Do I look like I care?
    - *(Dialogue 4:)*
      - **Sheep:** Twinkle, twinkle little egg<br />Wouldn't you like to grow some legs?<br />And run around, on grass so green<br />arguing with the milk and cream.
  - No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Sheep:** Huh, the cow's right. You humans have absolutely no understanding of art!

##### If the sheep has been sheared

- **Sheep:** You give me a hair-cut and then expect me to talk to you? I think not!
