    - **Sir Cadian:** Back when we were fighting the lackeys of the Elder Gods on the four fronts in Senntisten, the gods were stopping the Elder Gods' eggs from hatching.
    - **Sir Cadian:** That included Zamorak. We knew it'd only be a matter of time before he betrayed the other gods, and sure enough - he upped and left at the first opportunity.
    - **Sir Cadian:** Somehow, the world was saved - not totally clear on how that happened, but no complaints.
    - **Sir Cadian:** Now, though, Zamorak's back with an all-out offensive against our cities. My guess is he thinks he can establish a foothold while we're recovering from the war.

    - **Player:** Why are we attacking this part of Senntisten?
    - **Sir Cadian:** Simple answer is 'Orders, straight from Commander Zilyana herself'. That's enough for me.
    - **Sir Cadian:** As for why she'd send us to this demon-infested pit... Well, I can only speculate.
    - **Sir Cadian:** We know that Zamorak's holed up far to the north, summoning all manner of nasty things from the planes beyond.
    - **Sir Cadian:** He's hiding behind a giant magical shield, and from what I've heeard, no-one's getting past it. Not even the other gods.
    - **Sir Cadian:** As for this place... Intelligence is that it used to be a sort of slum where Avernic demons lived. Seems they've settled back in - just like old times.
    - **Sir Cadian:** Now, in my experience, demons'll use a fiery portal to go to the shops for some bread. So there's every chance they've got a shortcut here...straight back to Zamorak's HQ.
    - **Sir Cadian:** If you ask me - and you did - that's why we're here. The Commander's got every battle-ready White Knight checking for potential routes behind enemy lines.
    - **Sir Cadian:** She must be hoping that one of us'll get lucky, and find a back door to Big Red himself.

    - **Sir Cadian:** As for the humans... The few we've captured ranted about the {{Sic|'Order of Dis,'}}, or the 'Chaos Sisters'. Demons cults, no doubt.
    - **Sir Cadian:** See, demons I get. They're evil. It's kind of what they do.
