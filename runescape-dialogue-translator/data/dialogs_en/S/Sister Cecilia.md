## Dialogue Transcript


### Before One Piercing Note

- The sister politely ignores you, as if she is not permitted to speak to you yet.

### After One Piercing Note


#### After the quest and before giving any advice

- **Sister Cecilia:** Thank you for helping us, adventurer. I really thought I was going to die, but you saved us.
- **Sister Cecilia:** Since you're here, adventurer...could I ask for your advice?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Of course.
    - **Player:** Of course.
    - **Sister Cecilia:** The murders in the abbey made me think about what I want to do with my life. I've been here since I was a child, and I thought I'd spend my whole life here, but now I'm not sure I want to.
    - **Sister Cecilia:** I could leave and become a secular musician, travel the world...
    - **Sister Cecilia:** But on the other hand, I do feel the presence of Saradomin here, and if I leave, I can never come back.
    - **Sister Cecilia:** What do you think I should do, adventurer?
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: You should leave the abbey.
        - **Player:** You should leave the abbey.
        - **Sister Cecilia:** Thank you, adventurer.
        - {{Qact|Screen fades in and out. Cecilia disappears and reappears outside as a guitarist for Valerio.}}
        - {{RSFontAchievement|Cecilia, I'm Begging You, Please...}}
      - [Choice]: You should stay.
        - **Player:** You should stay.
        - **Sister Cecilia:** Thank you, adventurer.
        - {{RSFontAchievement|Cecilia, I'm Begging You, Please...}}
      - [Choice]: I can't decide for you.
        - **Player:** I can't decide for you.
        - **Sister Cecilia:** Of course. I suppose I'll stay here and think about it.
  - [Choice]: Not right now.
    - **Player:** Not right now.

#### If persuaded to stay

- **Sister Cecilia:** I'm glad I stayed in the abbey. Wanting to leave was just a phase. There's so much I can do here to praise Saradomin. I think I might even be called to be abbess after Sister Benita retires.
- **Sister Cecilia:** I'll always wonder what life might have been like if I'd left, though.

#### If persuaded to leave

- **Cecilia:** I'm glad I left the abbey. There's so much more I can do with music now that I can focus on it for its own sake.
- **Cecilia:** I'll always wonder what life might have been like if I'd stayed, though.
