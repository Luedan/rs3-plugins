## Dialogue Transcript


### Chat box

;When cleaning an item
- EXTERMINATE!<ref>A reference to Daleks from *{{Wp|Doctor Who}}*</ref>
- Everybody's dead [Player]<ref>A reference to *{{Wp|Red Dwarf}}*</ref>
- You know, its times like these that I realise what a superhero I am.<ref>A reference to *{{Wp|Iron Man 3}}*</ref>
- Spring Cleaner: Omnomnom.<ref>A reference to Heavy from *{{Wp|Team Fortress 2}}*</ref>
- You know, I've always wondered what that thing was made of, now I know.
- Cake and grief counselling will be available at the conclusion of your next slayer task<ref>A reference to GLaDOS from *{{Wp|Portal (video game)|Portal}}*</ref>
- Does not compute.<ref>A catchphrase from  * My Living Doll* and *Lost in Space*</ref>
- Ah well, you didn't need it anyway.
- I'm sorry [Player], I'm afraid I can't do that.<ref>A reference to HAL 9000 from *{{Wp|2001: A Space Odyssey (film)|2001: A Space Odyssey}}*</ref>
;When disassembling an item
- **Only If the item produced only junk:**  Seems to be a lot of things made of pure junk in this world.
- YES DISASSEMBLE!
;When adding to pocket
- Where are we going? If it was bad you'd tell me, right?
- Would you like to play a game?<ref>Possibly an allusion to Joshua, in *Wargames*</ref>
;When changing modes
- **Dismantle mode:** No disassemble Spring Cleaner!<ref>A reference to Number 5 from *{{Wp|Short Circuit (1986 film)|Short Circuit}}*</ref>
- **Research mode:** I feel smarter already.<ref>Possible reference to Postal Dude from *{{Wp|Postal 2}}*</ref>
;When unchecking the mute option
- I knew you couldn't resist hearing me talk!
