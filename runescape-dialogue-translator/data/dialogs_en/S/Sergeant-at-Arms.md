# Sergeant-at-Arms

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Sergeant-at-Arms](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASergeant-at-Arms)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Sergeant-at-Arms:** Want something?
- Select an option
  - Who are you?
    - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** I'm the Sergeant-at-Arms. I organise Rated Clan Wars matches.
    - **Player:** You look like you've seen combat.
    - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** I was here at the Siege of Falador. A lot of people died. I didn't.
    - (initial)
  - I'd like to know more about Rated Clan Wars.
    - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** Rated Clan Wars is a pitched battle between two clans. You need to be in a clan to take part, and you need a Rated Clan Wars badge.
    - *If the player has Rcw rules in the inventory:*
      - (initial)
    - *If the player does not have Rcw rules in the inventory:*
      - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** There's a rulebook if you want to know more.
      - Would you like a copy of the rulebook?
        - Yes.
          - The sergeant gives you a copy of the rulebook.
        - No.
          - (initial)
  - I need a Rated Clan Wars badge.
    - *If the player is not in a clan:*
      - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** You need to be in a clan to get a badge. I can't just give them out to just anyone. Talk to the clan scribe over there. He can help you set up a clan.
    - *If the player is in a clan:*
      - *If the player does not have an Rcw badge in the inventory:*
        - The sergeant hands you an RCW badge.
        - (initial)
      - *If the player has an Rcw badge in the inventory:*
        - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** You've already got a badge. Don't waste my time.
        - (initial)
  - Goodbye.
    - **Sergeant-at-Arms:** See you around.
