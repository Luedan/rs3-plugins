## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Sergeant Damien in Balthazar's Big Top Bonanza:*
- *Sergeant Damien may say any one of the following at random:*
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Sergeant Damien:** Someone should whip these recruits into shape.
    - **Player:** Yes, sir!
    - (continue)
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Sergeant Damien:** Are you eyeballin' me, [boy/girl]?
    - **Player:** No, sir!
    - (continue)
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - **Sergeant Damien:** What a snivelling pile of maggots!
    - **Player:** Yes, sir!
    - (continue)
  - *Dialogue 4:*
    - **Sergeant Damien:** What is your major malfunction?
    - **Player:** I wasn't aware I had one, sir!
    - (continue)
  - *Dialogue 5:*
    - **Sergeant Damien:** It's like a circus in here!
    - **Player:** Yes, sir!
    - (continue)
- **Sergeant Damien:** At ease, soldier; I'm not on duty.
- **Player:** Then what are you doing here, Sergeant Damien, sir, if I might ask?
- **Sergeant Damien:** Balthazar's a buddy of mine, from my old unit. After the war - you know, THE war - old BB retired to civilian life; set up this little carnival of his.
- **Sergeant Damien:** Now my drill sergeant days are behind me, he offered me a job here as a roustie.
- **Player:** A roustie? What's that?
- **Sergeant Damien:** A roustabout, boy; a roughneck; a handyman!
- **Sergeant Damien:** I keep all the equipment in good repair, do all the manual labour, and - best of all - I get to keep the audience in line! There's no better work for an old soldier like me.
