## Dialogue Transcript


### Without catspeak

- **Stacey:** Meow!

### With catspeak


#### Before the beginning of 12 January

- **Stacey:** Can't talk. Hunting!

#### After the beginning of 12 January


##### At the Needle

- **Player:** Hello cat. Would you like to come stay in a great big oasis, out in the nice, warm sun?
- **Stacey:** How dare you speak to me, weakling? Do you not know who I am? I am the mightiest feline warrior in these lands!
- **Player:** Well, I am the World Guardian, you know.
- **Stacey:** Pfft. Sounds pretentious.
- **Stacey:** You want me to consider your proposal? In that case, I want you to prove you're a skilled warrior!
- **Stacey:** I have heard rumours of a great beast made of ice that has assembled an army of frozen warriors in an ancient city buried beneath the ground.
- **Stacey:** Defeat this beast and I shall consider your proposal.
- ((If the player has an Arch-Glacor kill:))
  - **Player:** The Arch-Glacor? Oh I've already done that.
  - **Stacey:** WHAT? I mean... Hrm... Perhaps you are a worthy warrior after all.
  - **Stacey:** Very well, I shall visit this oasis of yours. Perhaps there is a chance for glory there.
  - You have completed: 'Impress the hunter' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
  - {{Qact|Stacey disappears from the scene.}}
  - **Stacey:** Farewell warrior!
- ((If the player has no Arch-Glacor kills:))
  - **Player:** I shall seek out this monster.
  - **Stacey:** Good luck, you'll need it.

##### At Het's Oasis

- **Stacey:** Curse them! Those lizards think they can mock me from their watery sanctuaries?
- **Stacey:** They'll be sorry as soon as I learn how to swim!
