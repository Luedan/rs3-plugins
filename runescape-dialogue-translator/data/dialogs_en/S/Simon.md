# Simon

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to

- **Simon:** Hello there! Are you interested in buying one of my special team capes?
  - **Player:** What do team capes do?
    - **Simon:** If you and your friends all wear the same team cape, you'll be less likely to attack your friends by accident, and you'll find it easier to attack everyone else.
    - **Simon:** They're very useful in the Wilderness and other player-vs-player combat areas where you might come across friends you don't want to harm.
    - **Simon:** So would you like to buy one?
    - *Shows previous options*
  - **Player:** Yes please!
    - *Opens Simon's Wilderness Cape Shop*
  - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank please.
    - *Opens Bank of Gielinor*
  - **Player:** No thanks.
    - *Closes dialogue*

### During Zodi-yak Track

- **Player:** I brought you some astral runes.
  - **Simon:** Thank you so much!
  - **Player:** You know, you're really brave being out here in the wilderness all by yourself.
  - **Simon:** I used to be afraid, but I overcame all my fears.
  - **Simon:** Someone once told me 'Don't believe in the you that believes in me and don't believe in the me that believes in you. Believe in the you that believes in yourself.'
  - **Player:** Wow, those are strong words to live by!
  - **Simon:** They sure are.
