## Dialogue Transcript

- **Snowman:** Merry Christmas! Welcome to the Land of Snow!
  - [Choice]: Merry Christmas!
    - **Player:** Merry Christmas!
    - **Snowman:** And a Happy New Year!
  - [Choice]: What is the Land of Snow?
    - **Player:** What is the Land of Snow?
    - **Snowman:** The Land of Snow is where all the magic of winter weather comes from. Not nasty weather like blizzards, but clear, bright stars above and crips snow underfoot.
  - [Choice]: I want to go home.
    - **Player:** I want to go home.
    - **Snowman:** Very well! Go forth and have a happy Christmas!
    - {{Qact|You get teleported out of Land of Snow.}}
