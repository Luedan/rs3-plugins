## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Seradoomin:** What puny human do near Trollgrav. Seradoomin smoosh like tiny bug!
- **Player:** Woah, woah. I just wanted to talk!
- **Seradoomin:** Sorry, Seradoomin on edge lately. Not used to being a spooky.
- **Player:** That's okay.
- **What would you like to say?**
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Seradoomin:** Me helpin' train puny spookies.
    - **Seradoomin:** Need to make sure weaklins get into Trollgrav.
    - **Player:** Ah, okay.
    - **Player:** Can I get into Trollgrav?
    - **Seradoomin:** Silly human, Trollgrav only for trolls. You go to... Humangrav. Yeah Humangrav me guess.
    - **Seradoomin:** You want fight some troll spookies for me? Might help you get into Humangrav?
    - **Seradoomin:** Seradoomin tired of training dem.
    - **Player:** Uh... I'll think about it.
    - **Seradoomin:** Okay den.
    - (previous)
  - Why are you called Saradomin?
    - **Player:** I was just wondering, why are you called Saradomin.
    - **Seradoomin:** No it's Seradoomin not 'sah-rah-doh-min', stoopid human.
    - **Player:** Okay, well, why are you called 'Seradoomin'.
    - **Seradoomin:** Me fought humans supposed to be smart. All trolls named after first thing dey eat.
    - **Seradoomin:** When Seradoomin just small troll pebble, me eat silly human dat came too close to home.
    - **Seradoomin:** Dey already came wrapped up in big brown wrapper an every-fing like dey want to be eaten!
    - **Player:** Uh...
    - **Seradoomin:** Anyway when me munchin on dem, they keep saying 'Seradoomin! Seradoomin! help! help!'.
    - **Seradoomin:** We fink dat musta been mans name, so dat's why Seradoomin's mumma, 'Gufix' name him 'Seradoomin'.
    - **Player:** Right... What a... Lovely? Story.
    - **Seradoomin:** Yep, It my faverut.
    - (previous)
  - Goodbye
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Seradoomin:** Byebye puny.

### Attempting to enter Trollgrav

- **Seradoomin:** Where you fink you goin.
- **Player:** I was just seeing what's through this tunnel.
- **Seradoomin:** Dat Trollgrav, strictly trolls only.
- **Seradoomin:** Now go away, me very busy.
