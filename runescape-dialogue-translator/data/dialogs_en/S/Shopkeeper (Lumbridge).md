### In Lumbridge


#### With a construction contract

- **Shopkeeper:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ask about contracts
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do you need repairing?
        - **Shopkeeper:** Let me see, right now I need you to repair the following pieces of furniture.
        - ((Only the unrepaired out of the following are listed:))
          - **Shopkeeper:** [Furniture 1], [Furniture 2], [Furniture 3], [Furniture 4], [Furniture 5]
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: What materials do I need?
        - ((Only the materials still required for repairs are listed in the following:))
          - **Shopkeeper:** You need: Planks x [number], Steel nails x [number], [Additional material] x [number]
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: [Leave]
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: Ask about something else
    - (below|standard)

#### Standard dialogue

- **Shopkeeper:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=Lumbridge General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Shopkeeper:** I'm glad you ask! The shop has two sections to it: 'Main stock' and 'Free sample items'.
    - **Shopkeeper:** From 'Main Stock' you can buy as many of the stocked items as you wish. I also offer free samples to help get you started and to keep you coming back. Once you take a free sample, I won't give you another for about a half an hour. I'm not made of money, you know!
    - **Shopkeeper:** You can also sell most items to the shop.
    - **Player:** Thank you.
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)
