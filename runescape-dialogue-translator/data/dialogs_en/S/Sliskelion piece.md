## Dialogue Transcript


### During the [[God Scoreboard]] event

*Talking to a Sliskelion piece for the first time*
- **Sliskelion piece:** Congratulations <name> you are now the proud owner of a fabulous Sliskelion piece! These fabulous items are brought to you by the Great Sliske, gamemaster extraordinaire in honour of his great game. Sliske's great game is making waves across Gielinor and beyond, with whole religions screaming that question: Who's dying next? Well right now you can see who's winning by visiting the fabulous scoreboard in the centre of the Grand Exchange.
  - What is the point of these?
    - **Sliskelion piece:** Aside from being a fabulous decoration for any living room, these Sliskelion pieces can be traded in for amazing prizes. Just take 50 of these to the scoreboard in the Grand Exchange and you will be rewarded with power and knowledge. But don't worry, you won't have to walk all that way. When you have 50 Sliskelion pieces, they can teleport you straight there. Convenience and chic in one fantastically handsome package. You can find Sliskelion pieces by simply going about your daily activities.
  - What is Sliske's game?
    - **Sliskelion piece:** Sliske's game is a revolution made fun. All those stuffy old gods, now get to duke it out for the promise of power and prestige. The contestant with the most points gets to win the most fabulous of prizes, the Stone of Jas itself.
    - **Player:** How do contestants get points?
    - **Sliskelion piece:** Originally the game was to kill as many gods as possible. Sadly though we've been forced to amend those rules, due to a general lack of carnage and a surprising surge of reason. So now players collect wonderful Sliske points by defeating other gods or proving themselves worthy. See the scoreboard in the Grand Exchange to see how the contestants are faring.
  - What's the scoreboard?
    - **Sliskelion piece:** The name is confusing I know. But basically the score board is a big board that shows the current scores. It's in the centre of the Grand Exchange in Varrock, you really can't miss it.
  - Ok I get the point.
*Talking to a Sliskelion piece subsequently*
- **Sliskelion piece:** Everyone's asking: Who's dying next? Find out who's winning on the scoreboard in the Grand Exchange.

### Teleporting with or using on the Scoreboard

- **Sliskelion piece:**  Congratulations! You are now the proud owner of a fabulous Sliskelion XP lamp!

### Teleporting with a full inventory

- **Sliskelion piece:** Oh, you don't appear to have enough inventory space.

### Receiving a lamp after teleporting with a full inventory

- **Sliskelion piece:** I see you didn't receive your lamp from the last time you came here! So you are now the proud owner of a fabulous Sliskelion XP lamp!
