# Skillchipx

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Skillchipx](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASkillchipx)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When teleported to

- Speak to Skillchipx for more information.

### When talked to


#### Before obtaining the task list

- **Skillchipx:** Hello, have you come to help?
- **Player:** Maybe? What do you need?
- **Skillchipx:** Great, I could do with some help with a few jobs I've been given.
- **Player:** How many are there?
- **Skillchipx:** There are eight tasks, and if you help me with those, I'll find a special reward for you.
- **Player:** What do I need to do?
  - *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
    - **Skillchipx:** I've got a list here, but you don't have any space for it.
  - *(If there is space in the player's inventory:)*
    - **Skillchipx:** Take this, it details all the tasks required.
    - {{Qact|The player receives the Runefest 2017 task list.}}
    - **Player:** Ok, thanks!

#### After obtaining the task list

- *(If there are unclaimed reward lamps:)*
  - *(If there is no space in inventory:)*
    - You have earned a small prismatic lamp, but you don't have any space for it. Talk to Skillchipx when you have space to claim it.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - Your small prismatic lamp for completing [location] chip task has been added to your inventory.
    - *(Continues below.)*
- **Skillchipx:** Hello, have you come to help?
- **Player:** Maybe? What do you need?
  - RuneFest Tasks
    - **Skillchipx:** Great, I could do with some help with a few jobs I've been given.
    - **Player:** How many are there?
    - **Skillchipx:** There are eight tasks, and if you help me with those, I'll find a special reward for you.
    - **Player:** What do I need to do?
      - *(If the list has been lost:)*
        - *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
          - **Skillchipx:** I've got a list here, but you don't have any space for it.
        - *(If there is space in the player's inventory:)*
          - **Skillchipx:** Take this, it details all the tasks required.
          - {{Qact|The player receives the Runefest 2017 task list.}}
          - **Player:** Ok, thanks!
          - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
      - *(If the list is still owned:)*
        - **Skillchipx:** I've given you a list with all the tasks details on it already.
        - **Player:** Ok, thanks!
        - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Special Flour
    - **Skillchipx:** Well I was trying to make some special flour in the nearby windmill for RuneFest but the controls seemed to have got jammed?
    - **Player:** Want me to take a look?
    - **Skillchipx:** That'd be great. Perhaps some combination with the levers might do the trick.
    - **Skillchipx:** Try moving them up and down a couple of times, then left and right a couple of times.
    - **Player:** Is that it?
    - **Skillchipx:** It might be, who can say. Let me know what happens.

#### With special flour

- **Skillchipx:** Hello, have you come to help?
- **Player:** Maybe? What do you need?
- **Skillchipx:** I see you've got some of the Special Flour I asked for...
- {{Qact|The player gives the flour to Skillchipx.}}
- **Player:** No problem.
- You've made the Special Flour and have unlocked a skill chip!
- *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
  - You have earned a small prismatic lamp, but you don't have any space for it. Talk to Skillchipx when you have space to claim it.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
  - You have earned a small prismatic lamp. It has been added to your inventory.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If the player has unlocked all the skill chips:)*
  - Congratulations! You've completed this year's RuneFest tasks for Skillchipx!
  - You have earned a medium prismatic lamp. It has been added to your inventory.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- RuneFest Tasks
  - *(As before.)*
- Special Flour
  - **Skillchipx:** Thanks for your help with the flour, that's one more off the list.
  - *(Returns to the previous options.)*

#### After unlocking all the skill chips

- **Skillchipx:** Hello, Player. Thanks for all your help with the tasks. Don't forget, if you've got a high enough construction level you can display your Skillchips in your house.
- **Player:** Thanks!
