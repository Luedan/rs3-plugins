## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Sir Rebrum:** Halt, adventurer! Go no further! Mortal, wormy peril lies within this cave!
- [Menu]: Choose an Option:
  - [Choice]: Wormy?
    - **Sir Rebrum:** Wormy indeed! The most unsightly, ghastly and smelly worms you could imagine have suddenly risen from a hidden network of underground caves!
    - **Sir Rebrum:** But fear not! We, the Falador White Knights, have been officially dispatched on a mission to deal with this slithering menace!
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Can you tell me what's going on inside this cave?
        - **Sir Rebrum:** Well, you see, my unit was dispatched to secure the area after locals issued an alert. Giant worm creatures appeared, you see.
        - **Sir Rebrum:** I promptly matched in, did what needed to be done, and now the people are safe. We have successfully contained the creatures.
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: Have you actually been inside these caves?
            - **Sir Rebrum:** Of course! We valiantly led a charge inside, followed by an immediate tactical retreat!
            - **Sir Rebrum:** Do not mistake this for running away, however. I simply called for a strategy meeting following slight losses.
            - **Sir Rebrum:** After all, losing 70% of one's fighting force makes one reconsider their situation!
            - (previous2|wormy-options)
          - [Choice]: Thank you for your service, Sir Rebrum. I have to go now.
            - **Sir Rebrum:** Farewell!
      - [Choice]: What can you tell me about the enemies inside?
        - **Sir Rebrum:** Worms! Lots of them. They spit some kind of corrosive acid. We can handle the ones near the entrance.
        - **Sir Rebrum:** However, if you go further down the caves, you'll start meeting bigger ones with mandibles. They are no joke!
        - **Sir Rebrum:** By the way, I never reached the end of the caves myself, so who knows what's at the bottom.
        - (previous|wormy-options)
      - [Choice]: I have to go now.
        - **Sir Rebrum:** Farewell!
  - [Choice]: Who are you again?
    - **Sir Rebrum:** I am the famous Sir Rebrum: hero of justice extraordinaire and destroyer of all things evil!
    - **Sir Rebrum:** I know you'd love to hear about all my adventures, but I'm on duty here.
    - (continuea|wormy-options)
  - [Choice]: Don't worry, I eat peril for breakfast.
    - **Sir Rebrum:** Most impressive! Carry on, then.
  - [Choice]: *(After Song from the Depths, if the player has destroyed their coral crossbow:)* Would you happen to have found a Coral Crossbow?
    - **Sir Rebrum:** Why, yes I have! The Raptor passed by earlier. He said you might need it.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have at least one free backpack space:)
      - **Sir Rebrum:** Come back when you're less encumbered and I'll give it to you.
    - (Condition: If the player has at least one free backpack space:)
      - (receives=coral crossbow)
      - [You receive the Coral Crossbow.]
  - [Choice]: *(After Song from the Depths, if the player has destroyed their Remora's necklace:)* I don't suppose you've seen a necklace lying about?
    - **Sir Rebrum:** Why, yes I have! The Raptor passed by earlier. He said you might need it.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have at least one free backpack space:)
      - (above|come-back)
    - (Condition: If the player has at least one free backpack space:)
      - (receives=Remora's necklace)
      - [You receive the Siren's necklace.]
