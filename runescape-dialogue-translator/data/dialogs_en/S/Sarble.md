## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- This gnome lady appears to be singing ... to her toads.
- **Sarble:** La la la, toadies in their holeys... Oh hello, I didn't notice you.
- **Player:** Hello, What do you do here?
- **Sarble:** I look after the toads and worms. They are very useful in gnome cooking, don't you know.
- **Player:** Does singing to the toads help them grow?
- **Sarble:** Don't be silly, I just sing to pass the time while looking after them!
- **Player:** Of course...
