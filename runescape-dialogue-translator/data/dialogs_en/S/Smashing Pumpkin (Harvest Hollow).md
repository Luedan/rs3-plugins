# Smashing Pumpkin (Harvest Hollow)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Smashing Pumpkin (Harvest Hollow)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASmashing%20Pumpkin%20%28Harvest%20Hollow%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Smash


#### First time

- (Condition: Without having received a rotten pumpkin mask during previous events:)
  - (receives=rotten pumpkin mask)
  - [The strange pumpkin gives you an old pumpkin head.]
  - (continue|not-upgraded)
- (Condition: Without having upgraded the rotten pumpkin mask during previous events:)
  - You sense that the energy emanating from the pumpkin would help to upgrade the rotten pumpkin mask.
- (Condition: Having already received and upgraded the rotten pumpkin mask during previous events:)
  - The pumpkin appears to quiver in fear. Although it seems indestructible, it wonders why you continue to stamp on it since you have already upgraded your pumpkin mask.
  - (continue|Afterwards)

#### Afterwards

- {{Qact|The player attempts to smash the pumpkin. It remains whole.}}

#### Upon smashing 250 pumpkins

- You've smashed 250 pumpkins! You can now change the style of your Rotten pumpkin mask.
- ((Shown in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|You've smashed 250 pumpkins! You can now change the style of your pumpkin mask.}}
