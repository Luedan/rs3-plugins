## Dialogue Transcript


### Before being asked to bring a spicy flower


#### Talk to

- **Spicy Flower:** Urrgh, another one! Go away man-thing you no spooky.
- **Player:** Correct, I was just wondering what you are doing in this cave.
- **Spicy Flower:** Spicy Flower love cave, why Spicy Flower be anywhere else?
- **Spicy Flower:** You one of them zomboes? Not got no brains, me never liked them.
- **Player:** No, I am  very much alive.
- **Spicy Flower:** Den what you doing here. You lost or somefing?
- **What would you like to say?**
  - I'm here to slay some trolls!
    - {{anchor|slay}}**Player:** I'm here to kill all these trolls!
    - **Spicy Flower:** Thanks, you help make new troll spookies big and strong like Spicy Flower.
    - **Player:** No I...
    - **Spicy Flower:** You good zomboe, me try not to eat you.
    - (previous)
  - What is this place?
    - {{anchor|place}}**Player:** What is this place?
    - **Spicy Flower:** This entrance to Trollgrav.
    - **Player:** What's Trollgrav?
    - **Spicy Flower:** How you not know Trollgrav. Everyone know Trollgrav.
    - **Player:** Well I don't. What is it?
    - **Spicy Flower:** It where all the strong troll spookies come.
    - **Player:** If all the strong trolls go there, why aren't you in there?
    - **Spicy Flower:** I here to help the new spookies get big and strong so they can go to Trollgrav.
    - **Spicy Flower:** That and I on the look out for zomboes. You not zomboe are you?
    - **Player:** No, once again I am not a zombie.
    - (previous)
  - Why are you called Spicy Flower?
    - **Player:** Why are you called Spicy Flower? That seems like an odd name.
    - **Spicy Flower:** First thing me eat was Spicy Flower.
    - **Spicy Flower:** Me only troll that like Spicy Flower, so I eat as much as I like.
    - **Spicy Flower:** Me miss spicy flowers, Spicy Flower has only stupid cave flowers here.
    - **Spicy Flower:** Tell you wot, you bring me Spicy Flower, maybe I not smush you like bug.
    - **What would you like to say?**
      - I'll get you some flowers.
        - **Player:** Okay, what exactly are 'spicy flowers'?
        - **Spicy Flower:** You should know, it human food.
        - **Player:** Well, humans eat a lot of different food. If it's human food, where did you get it from?
        - **Spicy Flower:** We trolls trade for it off stinky humans. They come along looking for trolls shinies. We trolls not have shinies, so we just cook and eat them, keep their fings.
        - **Player:** I thought you said you traded for them?
        - **Spicy Flower:** We did trade. I just said we took his fings and ate him.
        - **Player:** That's not trading. Trading is where you give someone something to get another thing in return.
        - **Spicy Flower:** Spicy Flower do that all the time, but stupid to do with human, rather just eat dem, much less to work out.
        - **Player:** We're getting side-tracked, I'll go find you a 'spicy flower'.
        - **Spicy Flower:** Spicy Flower so happy! You might change mind about zomboes.
        - **Player:** For Saradomin's sake! I'm not a zombie!
        - **Spicy Flower:** How you know Seradoomin? Me thought only trolls know him.
        - **Player:** Wait, how do you know Saradomin? It didn't seem like much of a troll topic.
        - **Spicy Flower:**  Wot you talking bout, he over at other end of cave helping train spookies.
        - **Player:** I don't think I can do this anymore.
      - Maybe later.
        - **Player:** Uhh... maybe later?
        - **Spicy Flower:** Stinky human, me too sad to smush now.
  - Goodbye
    - {{anchor|bye}}**Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Spicy Flower:** You come back soon Mr Zomboe. Bring tasties.
