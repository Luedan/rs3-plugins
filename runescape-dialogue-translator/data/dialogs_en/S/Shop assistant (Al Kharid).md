## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid

- **Shop assistant:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=Al Kharid General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Shop assistant:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - (end)
  - [Choice]: What do you think of Ali Morrisane?
    - **Shop assistant:** I don't trust him. Not after he's sent his men round to threaten us. Did you know, we've had four different thugs come round and threaten us if we don't join them? The owner has had hammers, rakes, swords and even scissors waved at him!
    - **Player:** What will you do about it?
    - **Shop assistant:** We'll carry on as normal with the store. Business as usual! Can I interest you in anything?
    - (initial)
