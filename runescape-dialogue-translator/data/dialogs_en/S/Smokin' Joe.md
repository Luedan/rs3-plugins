### After Ratcatchers

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me where I would find...
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Phingspet and Grimesquit
        - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find Phingspet and Grimesquit?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Ah the sisters as strange a pair as your ever likely to meet.
        - **Smokin' Joe:** You're most likely to find them in the sewers of Varrock.
      - [Choice]: Jimmy Dazzler
        - **Player:** Can you tell me where I would find Jimmy Dazzler?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Ah young Jimmy, he must be a made man by now.
        - **Smokin' Joe:** I remember when ...
        - **Player:** Do you know where I would find him or not?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Hrmph! Probably the Flying Horse in Ardougne.
        - **Smokin' Joe:** I doubt he would be too far away from there.
      - [Choice]: Hooknosed Jack
        - **Player:** Can you tell me where I could find Hooknosed Jack?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** That old brawler? Is he still knocking about?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Well your best bet would be somewhere on the rough side of Varrock, the east side if I remember correctly.
      - [Choice]: The face
        - **Player:** Can you tell me where I would find 'The Face'?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** What a tragedy, that girl.
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Look for her in Port Sarim.
      - [Choice]: Felkrash
        - **Player:** Can you tell me where I would find Felkrash?
        - **Smokin' Joe:** Ah queen muck, you'll probably find her in the Port Sarim pits.
  - [Choice]: That's all.
    - (end)
