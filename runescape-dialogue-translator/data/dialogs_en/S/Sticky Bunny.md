## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- (Condition: Initially:)
  - **Sticky Bunny:** I don't believe we've met before! I'm Sticky.
  - **Player:** Hi! I'm Player.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Afterwards:)
  - **Sticky Bunny:** Hiya, Player!
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=Sticky-Bunny-options
  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What brought you here?
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Mainly my children - Cocoa, Stewy, Gooey, and Chewy. They had nothing but positive things to say about this new Burrow that my brother has set up, so I had to see it for myself.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** It's been lovely getting to watch them grow into the Bunny business.
    - **Player:** That's sweet. Do you do any work for the Bunny business?
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Not yet! But I'm hoping to propose a new honeycomb-filled chocolate to my brother at some point.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Though I can't take the credit for the idea - that goes to my wife, Honey. Unfortunately she's rather allergic to bees, so I'm the only one who can see this dream through to reality.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Oh, but I hope you don't take me to just be an opportunist here! I really do love the bees too. They do so much for us, it's only fair we repay them with some love and care.
    - (previous|Sticky-Bunny-options)
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about beekeeping.
    - **Player:** Can I help to look after the bees?
    - **Sticky Bunny:** The more the merrier! For the most part you can just tend to the hives, but make sure to keep an eye on anything which could disturb the bees.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Wasps will agitate them, whilst wilted flowers will upset them.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** If they get too agitated, well, I wouldn't recommend continuing to harvest their hives without gloves. And if they're not happy, that's sure to affect your Farming training.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** I've got plenty of wasp traps and flower food in the area to deal with the disturbances, so feel free to take as many as you need.
    - **Player:** I also noticed a pile of metal doodads on the way in. What are they?
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Metal doodads... Oh! The smokers! Yes, occasionally you might just lose track of the bees. That's where the smokers come in - simply use a smoker on the hive to regain track of the little bumbles.
    - (previous|Sticky-Bunny-options)
  - [Choice]: Can I earn anything here?
    - **Player:** Can I earn anything here?
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Well, in addition to Farming experience, I can also pay you some spring tokens for helping out.
    - **Sticky Bunny:** If you find any honeycomb shards whilst tending to the hives you're also free to keep them! I believe you can combine them into various types of complete honeycomb.
    - (previous|Sticky-Bunny-options)
  - [Choice]: Bye!
    - **Player:** Bye!
    - **Sticky Bunny:** Goodbye, Player!
