## Dialogue Transcript


### After Icthlarin's Little Helper

- **Player:** Hi!
- **Slave:** What do you want?
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Slave:** Word has it that you very nearly spoiled our fun!
- **Player:** What, you're upset that I tried to put the high priest's soul to rest?
- **Slave:** So that we could get back to work again? What makes you believe that he even deserves such a burial?
- **Player:** Doesn't everyone?
- **Slave:** What, even those that gain through the blood, sweat, tears and misery of others?
- **Player:** I was just trying to help and, as it turns out, I've not achieved not a whole lot.

### After Contact!

- **Player:** Hi!
- ''The slave gives you a murderous look, but is too busy to respond to you.

### Using an acne potion on him

- **Slave:** What are you doing?
- **Player:** I have this acne potion that I thought might clear up your spots?
- **Slave:** What, so that I can get back to work?
- **Player:** Um, no, so that you wouldn't suffer needlessly?
- **Slave:** I know your type, trying to ruin the small man's fun! I won't have it!
- **Player:** Look, I'm sorry. I only had good intentions, I only wanted to help you.
