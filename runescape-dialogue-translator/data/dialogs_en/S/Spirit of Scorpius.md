## Dialogue Transcript


### After [[Observatory Quest]]

- (Condition: If you are not wearing a Ghostspeak amulet:)
  - [This powerful spirit seems capable of speaking to you even though you are not wearing an Amulet of Ghostspeak.]
- **Spirit of Scorpius:** Who treads upon my grave?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - (Condition: If you do not have an unholy mould (first time):'')
    - [Choice]: I seek your wisdom.
      - **Player:** I seek your wisdom.
      - **Spirit of Scorpius:** Indeed, I feel you have beheld the far places in the heavens.
      - (receives=unholy mould)
      - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Unholy Crafting' - Unlock the ability to craft unholy symbols.
      - **Spirit of Scorpius:** Here is a mould to make a token for our Lord; a mould for the unholy symbol of Zamorak. Return to me when you desire my blessing./My Lord instructs me to help you. I see you have a mould for the unholy symbol of our Lord. Come to me when you desire my blessing.
  - [Choice]: I have come to seek a blessing.
    - **Player:** I have come to seek a blessing.
      - (Condition: If you do not have an unpowered symbol:)
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** No blessings will be given to those who have no symbol of our Lord's love.
      - (Condition: If you have an unpowered symbol:)
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** I see you have the unholy symbol of our Lord. I will bless it for you.
        - [The ghost mutters in a strange voice.<br> The unholy symbol throbs with power.]
        - (removes=unpowered symbol)
        - (receives=unholy symbol)
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** The symbol of our Lord has been blessed with power!
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** My master calls.
      - (Condition: If you have a powered unholy symbol:)
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** I see you have the unholy symbol of our Lord. It is blessed with the Lord Zamorak's power.
        - **Spirit of Scorpius:** Come to me when your faith weakens.
  - (Condition: If you have previously obtained an unholy mould:)
    - [Choice]: I need another unholy symbol mould.
      - **Player:** I need another mould for the unholy symbol.
        - (Condition: If you don't have an unholy mould:)
          - The ghost hands you another mould.
          - **Spirit of Scorpius:** A lost object is easy to replace. The loss of the affections of our Lord is impossible to forgive.
        - (Condition: If you have an unholy mould:)
          - **Spirit of Scorpius:** One you already have, another is not needed. Leave me be.
  - [Choice]: I have come to kill you.
    - **Player:** I have come to kill you.
    - **Spirit of Scorpius:** The might of mortals, to me, is as the dust to the sea.
