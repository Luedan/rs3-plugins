### Clan Cup

- **Scribe:** Hello there! Do you wish to participate in a special Clan Cup for your clan?
- **Player:** What's this about?
- **Scribe:** This is a special competition where I'll track all Clans who wish to dedicate time to this competition!
- **Scribe:** For this particular Clan Cup you'll be required to skin as many dinosaurs in Anachronia as possible!
- **Player:** How do I take part?
- *(If the player doesn't have a clan vexillum:)*
  - **Scribe:** Easy enough! You need to acquire a Clan Vexillum from myself, would you like me to give you one?
  - Will you take a clan vexillum from the scribe?
    - Yes.
      - **Scribe:** It would be my pleasure, sir.
      - The scribe gives you a vexillum.
    - No.
      - **Scribe:** No problem, should you want a Clan Vexillum in the future you can acquire one from myself or a fellow clan member.
      - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If the player has a clan vexillum:)*
  - **Scribe:** Easy enough! If you interact with the Clan Vexillum you've got there you'll get an option to start a session.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **Scribe:** If you wish to take part without a Vexillum, feel free to select the 'Clan Cup Functions' option on the trophy in Clan Chat!
- **Scribe:** Now on to the competition...
- **Scribe:** You'll have 1 hour to set the highest score you can, the top ten members for your clan will constribute to a clan total which will be compared against the other clans!
- **Player:** What can we win?
- **Scribe:** If your clan finishes in the top ten for the entire competition, expect some goodies including a boost to that avatar of yours and more!
- **Scribe:** My fellow scribes will be in touch to arrange prizes, should you finish in the top 10!
- **Player:** Thank you!
