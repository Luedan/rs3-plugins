## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before having received the free shard


##### Without backpack space for the shard

- **Shadow Gem Shopkeeper:** I have a free Shadow Gem Shard for you here. But you don't have space for it!

##### With backpack space for the shard

- (receives=one Shadow Gem shard)
- **Shadow Gem Shopkeeper:** Hey there! Here's a free Shadow Gem Shard!
- (continue|Afterwards)

#### Afterwards

- **Shadow Gem Shopkeeper:** Can I interest you in something from the Shadow Gem Shop?
- You can get more Shadow Gem Shards from the Crystal Capsules Treasure Hunter Promotion.
- (opens=Shadow Gem Shop)

### Open Shadow Gem Shop

- (opens=Shadow Gem Shop)
