## Dialogue Transcript


### First time dialogue

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright. You look like you could help.
- **Player:** What with?
- **Sensei Seaworth:** I'm trying to perform a study on some wandering spirits, but I don't have the budget to travel much in search of them.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** You seem the inquisitive type, plus you have your own ship from what I can gather.
- **Player:** Okay, how would I help, and what's in it for me?
- **Sensei Seaworth:** I'm studying entities I call kami spirits - they're these floating spectral things that appear to be wearing masks.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** For each new specimen you bring me, I can impart some of my knowledge to you.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** And if you can help me collect one of each type, I'm sure we can work out something more rewarding.
- **Player:** How do I catch them?
- **Sensei Seaworth:** You'll need a jar of some sort, and probably a net too. Although they are spirits, their masks make them easy to catch.
- **Player:** Would an impling jar work?
- **Sensei Seaworth:** I don't know; what's an impling? Maybe just give that a try and you tell me.
- **Player:** Will do. How many types  I hunting for?
- **Sensei Seaworth:** Honestly, I couldn't tell you. Maybe you could keep track of them in a journal or something. What I do know is that there are two kinds.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** Some you will only find on populated islands - those are quite common and easy to catch. They're used to living creatures and less skittish.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** And then there are those only found in the uncharted islands - they'll be more difficult to catch. I call them orokami to differentiate them, but they're basically the same as bakami.
- **Sensei Seaworth:** Any questions?
- [Menu]: Any questions about kami?
  - [Choice]: What are kami spirits?
    - (below)
  - [Choice]: What does 'bakami' mean?
    - (below)
  - [Choice]: No questions here.
    - (end)

### Standard dialogue

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Any questions?
- [Menu]: Any questions about kami?
  - [Choice]: What are kami spirits?
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** That's what I'm trying to figure out. So far, I've determined that they are spirits of some kind.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** Considering their tendency to be animalistic, my leading theory is that they are the wandering spirits of deceased sea monsters.
    - **Player:** Are they all creatures of the sea, though?
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** From what I've witnessed so far, no. There are bird-like ones, and mammals, and then some really strange ones, and mammals, and then there are some really strange ones too.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** You know, though, that there are more 'monsters' in the Wushanko Isles than live in the sea. My theory is that they are all connected somehow.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** Though it's true, we do have a lot of water here, so perhaps that's why we predominantly find sea monsters.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What does 'bakami' mean?
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** Well, that's just my name for them. It's sort of a joke, but I discovered them, so I got to name them!
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** You might well already know the word 'kami' - it's sort of our word for...well, it can mean a lot of things - spirits, gods - but we mainly use it when referring to sea monsters.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** But the word 'baka', that again can have many meanings - idiot, silly - but I see it in this case being closer to 'mischievous'.
    - **Player:** So, 'bakami' means 'idiot gods'?
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** That's losing the nuance. It's more like 'mischievous spirits of sea monsters'...though they aren't all sea monsters, and some seem friendly, others dangerous. Mostly, they're quite benign, though.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** 'Orokami' means much the same thing, though there is a slight difference. Whatever bakami are, orokami are the same but 'more' in some way.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Can I toggle my kami spirit reward?
    - [Menu]: Which would you prefer from catching kami?
      - [Choice]: Triple experience.
        - **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright. From now on, any kami spirit you catch, however you catch 'em, will earn you triple the usual experience!
        - (previous2)
      - [Choice]: Double loot.
        - **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright. From now on, any kami spirit you catch barehanded will earn you twice the normal loot! It won't work for opening kami jars, though. That'd be cheating.
        - (previous2)
  - [Choice]: No questions here.
    - (end)

### For the first time after completing the kami spirit collection

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright! You've caught one of each type of kami spirit for me. Many thanks!
- **Sensei Seaworth:** So, I can share some of my study with you. Would you rather gain more experience when catching kami or more loot?
- [Menu]: Which would you prefer from catching kami?
  - [Choice]: Triple experience.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright. From now on, any kami spirit you catch, however you catch 'em, will earn you triple the usual experience!
    - (continue)
  - [Choice]: Double loot.
    - **Sensei Seaworth:** Alright. From now on, any kami spirit you catch barehanded will earn you twice the normal loot! It won't work for opening kami jars, though. That'd be cheating.
    - (continue)
- (receives=Hunter XP lamp (The Arc))
- **Sensei Seaworth:** If you ever change your mind about the triple experience, double loot thing, come talk to me.
- (above|Standard dialogue)

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the towers puzzle

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Can you solve this for me?
- (receives=Towers puzzle scroll (master))
- [Sensei Seaworth has given you a Towers puzzle scroll!]

#### When talked to again without solving the towers puzzle

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Nope, that's not completed yet.

#### When talked to with the towers puzzle solved

- **Sensei Seaworth:** Excellent, here is your reward!
- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (master) and Towers puzzle scroll (master)) (receives= the new clue scroll (master))
  - [You've found another clue!]
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (master) and Towers puzzle scroll (master)) (receives=reward casket (master))
  - [You've found a casket!]
