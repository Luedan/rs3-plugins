## Dialogue Transcript


### Dialogue


#### Before 31st October

- **Strange girl:** It's Hallowe'en soon and we're so excited!
- **Strange girl:** It's our favourite time of the year and we want everyone to get together wearing our favourite costume.
- **Strange girl:** You should come see me when it's time and I'll get you all prepared for the costume gathering.
- Come back on 31st October to take part in the costume gathering.

#### On 31st October

- **Strange girl:** You don't have a costume! Quick, put this on!
- Select an option
  - Try on the costume.
    - You start to feel a little spooky...
  - No thanks.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*

#### After 31st October

- **Strange girl:** The costume gathering was awesome! Hopefully we can do it again next year.
