# Swamp toad (creature)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Swamp toad (creature)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASwamp%20toad%20%28creature%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Randomly

*One of the following is said occasionally by swamp toads:*
- **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|Croak}}
- **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|Ribbit}}

### While boating toads

- *Occasionally, while bloating a toad*
  - **Player:** {{RSChat|Come hear toady!}}
- You manage to catch the toad and inflate it with the swamp gas.
- (receives=bloated toad)

#### Without any bellows to bloat the toads

- You need an Ogre Bellow to do this.

#### If the player already has three [[bloated toad]]s in their inventory

- **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|Ribbitt!}}
- One of your bloated toads manages to escape.
- (One of the bloated toads in the player's inventory is removed, and another one replaces it once it is bloated.)
- You manage to catch the toad and inflate it with the swamp gas.

### Manually releasing toads

- *If releasing all in inventory*
  - You release the toads and see them hop off into the distance.
- *If releasing only one*
  - You release the toad and see it hop off into the distance.
- *Occasionally, each released toad will say one of the following as they hop away:*
  - **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|Ribbet!}}{{Sic}}
  - **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|Croak!}}

### When a toad is eaten

- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Sqwark!}}
- **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|!!Croak!!}}
- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Gobble!}}
- (The swamp toad is eaten and removed from the ground)

### When a toad pops

- (The toad explodes)

### Outside of the area that Chompy birds can be hunted

- You won't attract a chompy bird this far away from the ogre area.
