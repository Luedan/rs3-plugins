### Assignment advice


#### Birds

- **Spria:** You can find chickens near the flax field on the west side of the river.

#### Wolves

- **Spria:** Wolves are pack animals, so you'll always find them in groups. Watch out for their bite, it can be nasty. There are plenty to kill on White Wolf Mountain.

#### Cows

- **Spria:** Cows are bigger than you, and they'll often hit hard but react fairly slowly. You can slaughter the ones in Lumbridge - I bet they are up to no good.

#### Crawling hands

- **Spria:** The crawling hands are found in the northern parts of the enchanted cave under the lake, to the south-east of here.
- **Spria:** The last Crawling hand I killed I found fire spells took them down effectively.

#### Gelatinous abominations

- **Spria:** The gelatinous abominations are found in the enchanted cave under the lake, to the south-east of here.
- **Spria:** They can only be beaten by bursting this core wearing spiked gauntlets, once you have damaged them enough.
- **Spria:** The last Gelatinous abomination I killed I found a bow and arrows took them down effectively.

#### Trolls

- **Spria:** There are a lot of trolls wandering this area. The cave to the north-west of the castle is crawling with them.

#### Wolves

- **Spria:** You  can find juvenile wolves if you go south-west to White Wolf Mountain.
- **Spria:** The last Wolf I killed I found water spells took them down effectively.
