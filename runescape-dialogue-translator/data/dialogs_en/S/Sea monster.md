# Sea monster

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Sea monster](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASea%20monster)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Arriving

- A sea monster has appeared!
- **Sea monster:** {{RSChat|Rawwwrrr!}}

### Feeding without crates of rotten fish with crates available

- The sea monster is only interested in crates of rotten fish.

### Feeding without raw fish without crates available

- You have no raw fish that the sea monster is interested in.

### Feeding while already satisfied

- The sea monster is no longer hungry.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The sea monster is no longer hungry.

### Quick-taking from fish barrel

- (receives=as many crates of rotten fish as possible)
- You take [number] crates of rotten fish.

### Leaving


#### Unsatisfied

- The sea monster departs feeling unsatisfied.
- Unfortunately the encounter was not completed in time. Completing encounters successfully rewards deep sea fishing buffs.
- The sea monster has departed...

#### Satisfied

- **Sea monster:** {{RSChat|Raaaaarrr!}}
- As the sea monster departs, water splashes the area.
- Boost active: A 10% chance to gain an additional catch (10 minutes)
- The sea monster has departed...
