## Dialogue Transcript


### 2023 Christmas event


#### Talk to


##### First time

- **Spencer:** Hello there!
- **Player:** What's going on here?
- **Spencer:** We've been tasked by Santa to bring a whole bunch of fun to Gielinor this season. I'm handing out flyers that will let you know everything we have planned.
- **Spencer:** Can I interest you in one?
- **Player:** Well, I guess there's snow time like the present!
- ((If the player already has the flyer:))
  - **Spencer:** It looks like one of my colleagues has already managed to give you one! Please come back if you lose the flyer.
- ((If the player does not have the flyer:))
  - (receives=Santa's activity flyer)
  - **Spencer:** Here you go.

##### Afterwards

- **Choose an option:**
  - Remind me why you're here?
    - **Spencer:** We've been tasked by Santa to bring a whole bunch of fun to Gielinor this season. I'm handing out flyers that will let you know everything we have planned.
    - **Spencer:** Of course, if you lose the flyer, you can always come talk to me to get a new one. Alternatively, I can let you know all the interesting events we have planned.
    - (previous)
  - What awaits me this festive season?
    - (opens=Santa's activity flyer)
  - ((If the player does not have Santa's activity flyer:))
    - Can I have another flyer?
      - ((If the player does not have at least one free backpack space:))
        - **Spencer:** Come back when you have some inventory space.
      - ((If the player has at least one free backpack space:))
        - (receives=Santa's activity flyer)
        - **Spencer:** Here you go.
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye Spencer! It was ice to meet you!
    - **Spencer:** Bye for now!

#### Open Santa's Flyer

- (opens=Santa's activity flyer)

### 2024 Christmas event


#### During the event


##### Overhead lines

- ((A random one of the following is seen every 30 seconds. The line is also repeated as standard overhead dialogue.))
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|It's time for festive celebrations folks! 'ead over to the Christmas Village now!}}}}
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Listen up, listen up! Come an' visit the Christmas Village for the festive season!}}}}
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Oi, you! Join us in the Christmas Village for activities, rewards, and more!}}}}

##### Talk to

- **Spencer:** 'ello 'ello!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's going on?
    - **Player:** What's going on here?
    - **Spencer:** Only da best time of the year!
    - **Spencer:** Diango and da pixies 'ave joined us in the Christmas Village. He's a slowpoke so probably needs your 'elp to set up 'is workshop.
    - **Spencer:** There's also skilling activities, weekly visits from Santa 'imself, and wrapping paper to make prezzies too!
    - **Spencer:** We've also set up an advent calendar, and weekly buffs over the festive season, which we're callin' 'Winter Weeks'. Trademarked, that.
    - **Spencer:** Jump into that portal over there to join in!
    - **Player:** Wow, thanks! It sounds like there's plenty for me to get stuck into.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What can I get?
    - **Player:** What can I get from the festivities?
    - **Spencer:** Ya seet those people sittin' over there wearin' the pj's and slippers? You can get those from Santa's Lodge!
    - **Spencer:** If ye wanna sit like them, you can if you 'elp out Diango and the pixies in 'is workshop.
    - **Spencer:** Holly has some new stuff in 'er store too, so check that out! We're also wrapping up a bunch more new rewards in da Christmas presents this year - Noel can tell you about 'em.
    - **Player:** Thanks! That's a lot of new rewards to check out.
    - **Spencer:** Dey don't call it the season of giving for nuthin'!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Bye!
    - **Player:** Bye for now!
    - **Spencer:** Bye!

##### Open Event Interface

- (opens=2024 Christmas event)

#### After the event


##### Talk to


##### Before the beginning of 13 January 2025

- **Spencer:** You're too late! The festivities are over pal, but you can still visit the Christmas Village if ya got any goodies left to claim!

##### After the beginning of 13 January 2025

- **Spencer:** You're too late, Christmas is over! Come back next year.

##### Open Event Interface

- [The Christmas festivities have now ended.]
