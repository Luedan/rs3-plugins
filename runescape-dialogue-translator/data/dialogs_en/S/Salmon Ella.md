# Salmon Ella

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Salmon Ella](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASalmon%20Ella)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- ((Only at first time:))
  - Salmon Ella gives you a taijitu in thanks for the rescue.
  - (continue)
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - I got your message.
    - **Player:** I got your message. I'm here to rescue you! Maybe.
    - **Salmon Ella:** That message was only for my husband's eyes. How dare you snoop at a private letter!
    - **Player:** Maybe you shouldn't have put it in a bottle and thrown it in the ocean where anyone could find it.
    - **Salmon Ella:** Do you see a mailbox here? We're a bit far from the Waiko post office.
    - (previous)
  - Do you know Salmon Max?
    - **Player:** Let me guess. Your message was intended for Salmon Max?
    - **Salmon Ella:** You're smarter than you look.
    - **Player:** I get that a lot.
    - (previous)
  - How did you get here?
    - **Player:** So how did you wind up marooned on this island?
    - **Salmon Ella:** Oh, I'm not marooned, dearie. I'm staying here to keep an eye on our claim. Max popped back to Waiko with our boat to get supplies. I'm sure he'll be back any minute.
    - (previous)
  - Why are you here?
    - **Player:** Why are you here?
    - **Salmon Ella:** Why are any of us here?
    - **Player:** Deep. Very deep. I mean why are you on this island?
    - **Salmon Ella:** We've found them! We've finally found them. The legendary invisible salmon! I'm just waiting for Max to come back with a net and some bait, and then we can make our fortune!
    - **Player:** Salmon are fresh water fish. You need a fly fishing rod and feathers.
    - ((If the player has the baitless fishing perk:))
      - **Player:** And if you have the baitless fishing perk like me, you don't even need bait.
      - (continue)
    - **Salmon Ella:** Oh! You'd think a salmonite like Max would know better.
    - **Player:** Salmonite?
    - **Salmon Ella:** Yes, that's what our society call ourselves. Salmon enthusiasts. Salmonites. The seekers of salmon. It's all just a bit of fun, really. We never imagined we'd actually find them!
    - (previous)
  - Smell you later.
    - (end)
