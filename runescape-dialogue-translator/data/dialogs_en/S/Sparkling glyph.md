## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawn

- A cloud of sparkles has taken over a glyph!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - A cloud of sparkles has taken over a glyph!

### Restore

- You expel the sparkles on the glyph.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You expel the sparkles on the glyph.
  - (continue)
- The following reward is added to the ritual chest: 1 x [Item]

### Check durability

- ((The standard durability check for the glyph it appears over is shown.))
