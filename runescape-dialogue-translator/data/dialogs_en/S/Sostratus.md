## Dialogue Transcript


### Shop


#### Wearing a bedsheed or ectoplasm-covered bedsheet

- (Condition: Only on the first time:)
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Poor Imitation' - Disgruntle an inhabitant of Um by wearing a bedsheet.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: One of the following will be chosen at random:)
  - **Sostratus:** This conversation can continue after you remove that ridiculous outfit.
  - **Sostratus:** How dare you make such a mockery!
  - **Sostratus:** Is this your idea of humour?

#### Not wearing a bedsheed or ectoplasm-covered bedsheet


##### Before accepting the necromancer robes

- **Sostratus:** Oh, you're here for those necromancy robes. I took these off the last necromancer I killed.
- (Condition: Without five free spaces in backpack:)
  - You need at least 5 free spaces in your Backpack.
  - **Sostratus:** You need some room to take them, though...
  - (opens=Buried Bargains)
- (Condition: With five free spaces in backpack:)
  - (receives=a full set of tier 10 deathwarden robes)
  - 2 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - [Sostratus gives you some necromancer robes.]
  - **Sostratus:** Sorry about the bloodstains.
  - (opens=Buried Bargains)

##### After accepting the necromancer robes

- (opens=Buried Bargains)
