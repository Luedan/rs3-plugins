### Task System

- (Condition: If the Hard Falador achievements have been completed and the rewards not claimed:)
  - **Player:** I've come for my reward.
  - **Squire Asrol:** So, you've finished the Hard Tasks, well done! I believe congratulations are in order.
  - **Squire Asrol:** Right you are.
  - [The squire hands you a shield.]
  - **Squire Asrol:** This is the third stage of the Falador shield: a tower shield. It grants you all the benefits that the buckler and kiteshield did, full Prayer restore, and access to some interesting new seeds that my friend Wyson has found.
  - **Squire Asrol:** As before, Prayer restore can only be used once per day, but this time it restores all of your Prayer points!
  - **Squire Asrol:** The new seeds I mentioned were discovered by Wyson, who can be found in Falador Park. He'll give you some new seeds in return for the skin of the giant mole beneath Falador Park.
  - **Squire Asrol:** As well as these features, the shield is pretty handy in combat, and gives you a big Prayer boost.
  - **Squire Asrol:** Oh, and I've sent word to the Master Crafter at the guild to the south, he'll now let you use his bank deposit box.
  - **Squire Asrol:** If you should lose your shield, come back and see me for another one.
  - **Player:** Wow, thanks!
  - **Squire Asrol:** I've even thrown in an old teapot I've found in the castle.

  - **Player:** This isn't a teapot, it's a magical lamp!
  - **Squire Asrol:** Ah. That would explain a lot...
