## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawning

- A storm of souls has taken over a ritual component!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - A storm of souls has taken over a ritual component!

### Dissipating the final form

- {{Qact|The soul storm disappears.}}
- You banish the storm of souls from the ritual component.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You banish the storm of souls from the ritual component.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Without space for the rewarded item in focus storage:)
  - {{Transcript missing}}
- (Condition: With space for the rewarded item in focus storage:)
  - The following reward is added to your focus storage: 1 x [Item]
  - (Condition: If an uncut moonstone is rewarded:)
