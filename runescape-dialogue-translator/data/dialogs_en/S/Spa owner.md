- (Condition: When talking to her for the first time:)
  - **Spa owner:** Just look at you!

  - **Spa owner:** My mother always used to say 'you can stop when you're dead'. Well, she wasn't wrong!
  - **Spa owner:** She was so busy trying to look after everyone else that she walked straight into a nest of basilisks and they chomped her right down.
  - **Spa owner:** That's when I said to myself 'Look here Nelly, that's not what we want for you. No future in dying is there? It's a messy old business, isn't it? You want a life of living, a life of rest and relaxation, you do'.<div id="story"></div>
  - **Spa owner:** Long story short. I went on a day trip to Varrock museum a few years ago. Did you know they have a cape which turns you into a squirrel? Funny old business that. I'm allergic to nuts so you could imagine my shock.
  - **Spa owner:** The effect didn't last long but it shook me up and I felt a bit queasy. I tried to find the ladies room and thought I'd found it but there I am face-to-face with a dragon. In glasses!
  - **Spa owner:** Can you believe it? I didn't and I promptly threw up all over his desk. What a mess! All those papers from the second age ruined. I tried to clean them but just made it worse and the ink came right off.
  - **Spa owner:** Well, I'm not sure if you've ever seen an angry dragon - oh, of course you have, you're an adventurer - but when I looked up that's exactly what I expected to see.

  - **Spa owner:** Turns out those pages held a curse that had remained intact for thousands of years. Well, Mr. Mordaut was trying to break the curse and he was stumped. Then in comes Nelly with my...accident and destroy the papers to save the day.
  - **Spa owner:** Not often you get chance to be a hero but I was that day. I even got a medal!
