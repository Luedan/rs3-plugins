# Snow ranger (2007 Christmas event)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Snow ranger (2007 Christmas event)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASnow%20ranger%20%282007%20Christmas%20event%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- (Condition: Out of combat)
  - **Snow ranger:** Ha ha! None are a match for my bow.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a warrior)
  - **Snow ranger:** Your sword lacks finesse. I shall win the day.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a ranger)
  - **Snow ranger:** Let us test our bows against one another.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a mage)
  - **Snow ranger:** Your enchantments are no match for my arrows, sorcerer.
  - (end)
