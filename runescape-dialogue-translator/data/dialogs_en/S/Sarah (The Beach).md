## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if not asked last:|What's going on here?
    - **Sarah:** We've set up a fun coconut shy game. Just pick up some balls and try to knock off the coconuts.
    - **Player:** It looks like some of the coconuts are stuck. You wouldn't know anything about that would you?
    - **Sarah:** What are you saying, deary? That you don't trust little old me? How dare you accuse me of such things!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if not asked last:|How does the coconut shy work?
    - **Sarah:** Simply pick up some balls and try to knock off the coconuts. You get {{Sic|10}} balls at a time to throw.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if neither "What's going on here?" or this question was asked last:|How does the hook a duck work?
    - **Sarah:** Simply pick up a rod and try to catch the ducks. You get {{Sic|10}} goes at a time.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: How are things going?
    - **Sarah:** I'm having a whale of a time. You can tell a lot about a person from the types of balls they throw, and I find that very interesting.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do normal balls mean?
        - **Sarah:** Ah, the choice of the balanced. For those who are happy to flow with the wind and be one with the world.
        - (continue)
      - [Choice]: What do cabbages mean?
        - **Sarah:** Cabbages. These people are strong-willed and spontaneous, loyal to the end and always fight for what they believe in.
        - (continue)
      - [Choice]: What do chinchompas mean?
        - **Sarah:** Chinchompas, the choice of the creative soul, the artist. Kind souls who are often misunderstood.
        - (continue)
    - **Sarah:** It's not an exact science though. I'm still ironing out the kinks.
    - (previous)
