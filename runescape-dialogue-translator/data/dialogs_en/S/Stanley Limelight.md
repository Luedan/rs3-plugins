## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### First time

- **Stanley Limelight:** Behold, another to bathe in the glory of the Spotlight!
- **Player:** Who, me?
- **Stanley Limelight:** Indeed!
- **Stanley Limelight:** I represent the League of Many, dedicated to the art of friendly competition, and I seek to reward those who play games together.
- **Stanley Limelight:** Whenever you play minigames I will reward you with thaler, the coin of our League.
- **Stanley Limelight:** Play the game on which our Spotlight falls and we will give you those thaler at five times the normal rate!
