## Dialogue Transcript


### After King of the Dwarves


#### If you use a pretty flower on Sparkly Crystal

- **Sparkly Crystal:** Look dad it you! Fritty Plower!
- **Pretty Flower:** Even own boy make fun of me. This sad day for trolls.

#### If you use a big rock on Sparkly Crystal

- **Sparkly Crystal:** Big rock!
- **Big Rock:** What?
- **Sparkly Crystal:** Hahaha, big rock, big rock!
- **Big Rock:** What you want?
- **Sparkly Crystal:** Nothing.
