# Seravel

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Seravel](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASeravel)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing Shilo Village

- **Seravel:** I'm getting out of here Bwana, the place is infested with zombies.

### After completing Shilo Village

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Seravel:** Hello Bwana. Are you interested in buying a ticket for the 'Lady of the Waves'?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, that sounds great!
    - **Player:** Yes, that sounds great!
    - (Condition: If the player has at least 25 coins:)
      - (removes=25 coins) (receives=ship ticket)
      - **Seravel:** Great, nice doing business with you.
    - (Condition: If the player doesn't have at least 25 coins:)
      - **Seravel:** Sorry Bwana, you don't have enough money. Come back when you have 25 Gold Pieces.
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Seravel:** Fair enough Bwana, let me know if you change your mind.
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about the ship.
    - **Seravel:** It's a ship that can take you to either Port Sarim or Port Khazard. The ship lies west of Shilo Village and south of Cairn Island. The tickets cost 25 Gold Pieces. Would you like to purchase a ticket Bwana?
    - (other)
