# Seren spirit

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Seren spirit](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASeren%20spirit)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Seren spirit appearing

- A Seren spirit appears, interested in your actions.
- (popup)
  - {{Popup|A Seren spirit appears, interested in your actions.|color=#00FFFF}}

#### With an active spirit attraction potion effect

- A Seren spirit appears. Your spirit attraction potion allows you to catch it as it spawns.

### Capturing a Seren spirit


#### With space in bank

- The Seren spirit gifts you: [quantity] x [Item]. The gift is sent to your bank.

#### Without space in bank, but with space in backpack

- The Seren spirit gifts you: [quantity] x [Item]. The gift is sent to your backpack.

#### Without space in bank nor backpack

- The Seren spirit gifts you: [quantity] x [Item]. The gift drops to the floor.

### When the spirit disappears without being interacted with

- The Seren spirit disappears before you could receive a gift from it.

  - {{Popup|The Seren spirit disappears before you could receive a gift from it.|warning=yes}}
