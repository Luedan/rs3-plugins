## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet

- **Player:** {{RSChat|Who's a good pup?}}
- **Stray Dog:** {{RSChat|Woof woof!}}

### Shoo-away

- **Player:** {{RSChat|Thbbbbt!}}
- **Stray Dog:** {{RSChat|Whine!}}

### Giving items


#### Bones

- **Stray Dog:** {{RSChat|Woof woof!}}
- (removes=the used bones)
- You give the dog some nice bones. It happily gnaws on them.
- *If the achievement 'Dog and Bone' hasn't been completed:*
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Dog and Bone' - Give a bone to one of Varrock's stray dogs (or to your pet stray dog if you have one).

#### Raw or cooked meat

- **Stray Dog:** {{RSChat|Woof woof!}}
- (removes=the used item)
- You give the dog a nice piece of meat. It gobbles it up.

#### Anything else

- **Stray Dog:** {{RSChat|Grrrr!}}
- The dog doesn't seem interested in that.
