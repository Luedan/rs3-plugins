# Shambling horror

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Shambling horror](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AShambling%20horror)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Initial appearance

- A shambling horror has spawned on the ritual site!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - A shambling horror has spawned on the ritual site!

### Sever link

- Find the component the shambling horror is targeting to sever the bond.
- {{Qact|A short burst of green smoke is emitted from the correct component.}}

### Interacting with the correct component

- {{Qact|The shambling horror disappears.}}
- You sever the bond to the shambling horror.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You sever the bond to the shambling horror.
  - (continue)
- The following reward is added to the ritual chest: [number] x [Item]
- (Condition: If an uncut moonstone is rewarded:)
  - The following reward is added to the ritual chest: 1 x Uncut moonstone

### Interacting with an incorrect component

- This wasn't the shambling horror's target.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - This wasn't the shambling horror's target.

### Failing to interact with the correct component in time

- {{Qact|The shambling horror disappears.}}
- The shambling horror manages to reach its target and collapses.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The shambling horror manages to reach its target and collapses.
