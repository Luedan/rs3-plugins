## Dialogue Transcript


### In Falador Castle

- **Sir Atcha:** Hi! Sir Atcha service.
- **Player:** I think you missed a couple of words there.
- **Sir Atcha:** You got my meaning. Gotta save time. Archaeology is a fast-paced business.
- **Player:** It really isn't...
- **Sir Atcha:** How can I be of service, good person?
- **Select An Option**
  - Who are you?
    - **Sir Atcha:** I'm Sir Atcha, a White Knight, of course. My role is something of a mix of a scholar, scribe, quartermaster and general dogsbody.
    - **Player:** What rank are you?
    - **Sir Atcha:** Oh, I'm just an initiate! I signed up to the White Knights after the Battle of Lumbridge, and I've worked hard to achieve my rank. Before this, I used to work as a clerk at Varrock Museum, which is why I've been given the honour of collecting Saradominist artefacts.
    - **Sir Atcha:** Saradomin himself has taken a personal interest in my work; can you believe it? I hope I don't mess up.
    - (initial)
  - What are you collecting?
    - **Sir Atcha:** Want to look at my collections or just talk about them?
    - **What would you like to do?**
      - View collections.
        - ((Everlight White Knight interface opens.))
      - Talk about collections.
        - **Sir Atcha:** I'm collecting anything Saradominist, of course! Right now, that means whatever you find at Everlight. Saradomin took the loss of Everlight harshly, so I'm told. It's not something he speaks about, though.
        - **Sir Atcha:** Padomenes - he's really cool - also has a vested interest in my finds. He used to live there, I think, or he had family that did? Maybe he's after something specific, I don't know.
        - **Player:** How do you feel knowing that the person managing Everlight is a vampyre? How does Saradomin feel about that?
        - **Sir Atcha:** I mean, it's weird, I know, but she seems cool. A little mean and curt at times, so I've heard, but she hasn't eaten anyone yet!
        - **Sir Atcha:** And besides, Queen Efaritay is at Everlight too. She just doesn't like staying at that site for personal reasons. I get that.
        - **Sir Atcha:** Saradomin hasn't really said anything about it. Not to me, anyway, but then, I am just a novice. If he had an issue with the Efaritay-Vanescula alliance, I'd probably have heard at least something...
        - (initial)
  - What are your collection rewards?
    - **Sir Atcha:** Oh, right, cool! I've been authorised to trade a few relics in return for completing my collections.
    - ((Before completing Sir Atcha: Saradominist I:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** I have...wait for it...a lock of hair! Wait, don't go! It's special, from a reliquary. It was taken from the wife of Dharok as a keepsake.
      - **Sir Atcha:** If you combine it with an amulet of the forsaken, I think the two things combined will give you some berserker-level strength.
      - (continue)
    - ((After completing Sir Atcha: Saradominist I:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** Well, I already traded you that lock of hair from Dharok's betrothed. If you combine it with an amulet of the forsaken, that should make for a powerful relic!
      - ((If the player has lost the lock of hair:))
        - **Player:** About that...
        - **Sir Atcha:** I gotcha, have another lock!
        - Take lock of hair from Sir Atcha?
          - Yes.
            - (receives=lock of hair)
            - (continue)
          - No.
            - (end)
      - ((If the player still has the lock of hair:))
        - **Player:** Right, yes, I should do that...
        - (continue)
      - ((After creating Dharok's Memento:))
        - **Player:** And it did just that! Thank you.
        - (continue)
    - ((Before completing Sir Atcha: Saradominist II and III:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** For my second and third collections, you can have...a couple of tetracompass pieces! I hope you find these things worthwhile! I'm sure they have value; you'll figure it out.
      - (continue)
    - ((Before completing Sir Atcha: Saradominist IV:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** Ooh, I have a hat!
      - **Player:** Okay...
      - **Sir Atcha:** It's a magic hat! It's called Petasos. Makes you gather stuff faster!
      - **Player:** You don't use it, do you? Because if so, you're not convincing me it works.
      - **Sir Atcha:** I don't get it...
      - **Player:** Not to worry. Learning gathering skills faster sounds pretty handy, thank you.
      - (continue)
    - ((After completing Sir Atcha: Saradominist IV:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** Is that Petasos hat speeding up your gathering, Player? I bet you go like a flash now!
      - (continue)
    - **Sir Atcha:** Ooh, plus there's always chronotes for all of my collections!
    - ((Before giving Reldo a Dominarian device:))
      - **Sir Atcha:** Oh, and one last tip! Reldo - Varrock Palace's resident librarian - has expressed interest in something called a Dominarian device. My guess is if you deliver a restored one to him, you'd get something of value in return...
      - (continue)
    - (initial)
  - Bye.
    - **Sir Atcha:** K'atcha later!
