## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When talking to the Squire in the Void Knight Outpost smithy.*
- **Player:** Can you repair my items for me?
- **Squire:** Of course I'll repair it, though the materials may cost you. Just hand me the item and I'll have a look.
*Using the right click option "Repair all" with the squire without having any items able to be repaired results in the following dialogue:*
- **Squire:** You do not have any items that can be repaired or recharged.
