## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Player:** Are you the Easter Bunny?
- **Sour Bunny:** No, no! The Easter Bunny is my uncle. He's tasked me with spreading the word to adventurers about the Easter celebrations we're hosting!
- **Player:** Is there anything I can take part in?
- **Sour Bunny:** Yes, yes! We have egg hunts galore, skilling activities, and plenty of rewards for you to earn.
- **Sour Bunny:** Just hop into this portal, which will take you to the Blooming Burrow.
- **Sour Bunny:** Once you've arrived, talk to the Easter Bunny outside his house to find out more!
