## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to him

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Sigmund The Merchant:** Hello outerlander. By the laws of our tribe, I am afraid I may not speak to you without the express permission of the chieftain.
- **Player:** Where would I find him?
- **Sigmund The Merchant:** In the longhall, outerlander.

#### Trade option

- Only Fremenniks may trade with this merchant.

### After [[The Fremennik Trials]]

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Sigmund The Merchant:** Greetings again [brother/sister] [Fremennik name]! What can I do for you this day?
- Choose an option:
  - Can I see your wares?
    - **Player:** Can I see your wares?
    - **Sigmund The Merchant:** Certainly, [Fremennik name].
    - (opens=Sigmund the Merchant (shop))
  - Nothing thanks.
    - **Player:** Nothing thanks.
    - **Sigmund The Merchant:** Well, feel free to stop by anytime that you wish [Fremennik name]. You are always welcome here!
