## Dialogue Transcript


### In western Keldagrim


#### Standard dialogue

- (Condition: Before Birthright of the Dwarves:)
  - **Santiri:** Welcome, human, to the Quality Weapons Shop! Can I interest you in a purchase?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: After Birthright of the Dwarves:)
  - **Santiri:** Welcome, human, to the Quality Weapons Shop! Since the defeat of the Red Axe, I now have dragon battlestaves in stock. Can I interest you in a purchase?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, I'm looking for some weapons.
    - **Player:** Yes, I'm looking for some weapons.
    - (opens= Quality Weapons Shop)
  - [Choice]: cond=After Birthright of the Dwarves|Dragon battlestaves?
    - **Player:** Dragon battlestaves?
    - **Santiri:** Yes! Colonel Grenda recovered Hreidmar's dragon battlestaff from his palace. I've examined it and learned how to create more.
    - **Santiri:** It's a remarkable weapon. Hugi the librarian claims it might date back to the very early days of Keldagrim, before even the Age of Kings!
    - **Santiri:** Unfortunately it requires a lot of expensive raw materials, so I've got to charge you for them, even if you did lead to its capture.
    - **Santiri:** Are you interested?
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Santiri:** Good day to you, human. I have a little task for you, are you interested?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What’s the task?
    - **Player:** What’s the task?
    - **Santiri:** Oh, it’s nothing too major. But I need [item] in the next [number] minutes.
    - **Player:** Sure, got it. That’s [item] in the next [number] minutes, I’ll get right on it.
  - [Choice]: Sorry, I’m a little busy.
    - **Player:** Sorry, I’m a little busy.
    - **Santiri:** No problem, I’ll find someone else to do it!

##### Talking to Santiri without the item

- **Santiri:** Did you get [item], perchance?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: No, I’ve given up on that.
    - **Player:** No, I’ve given up on that.
    - **Santiri:** That’s very unfortunate to hear. I suppose I will need to find someone else now.
  - [Choice]: No, I’m still looking for them.
    - **Player:** No, I’m still looking for them.
    - **Santiri:** Keep it up, I’m sure you’ll succeed.
