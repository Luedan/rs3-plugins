## Dialogue Transcript


### First time dialogue

- *If player doesn't own Yak Track Task List:*
  - (receives=Yak Track Task List)
- **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Hello.
- **Player:** Ahhh! It's a talking telescope!
- **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Excuse me, there is a talking yak underneath the telescope.
- **Player:** Ahhh! It's a talking yak!
- *If the player participated in the original Yak Track:*
  - **Player:** Wow, deja vu...
- **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Yes, yes, it is quite shocking to see a talking yak in this day and age. I am Sir Yaktrick Moore, savant of the stars.
- **Player:** Nice to meet you, I am Player.
- *If the player has any skills at level 99, they are listed in this order, with Invention not in the list:*
  - **Player:** I am a savant of : Attack, Strength, Ranged, Magic, Defence, Constitution, Prayer, Agility, Herblore, Thieving, Crafting, Runecrafting, Mining, Smithing, Fishing, Cooking, Firemaking, Woodcutting, Fletching, Slayer, Farming, Construction, Hunter, Summoning, Dungeoneering, Divination.
  - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Show off.
- (continue|initial-options)

### Standard dialogue

- *If player doesn't own Yak Track Task List:*
  - (receives=Yak Track Task List)
- **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Welcome back, how can I help you?
- Select an option{{anchor|initial-options}}
  - How does the Yak Track work?
    - **Player:** How does the Yak Track work?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Ah, the Yak Track! It's an opportunity to go on an adventure with your very own yak and earn many rewards along the way! Simply put, you can complete tasks to progress along the track. You will be given a choice between two tasks at a time, but you only need to complete one of them to progress. If you don't fancy doing either of them then you can use a task skip to move on. As you progress along the track you will earn rewards; Premier Club members and those who have purchased a Premier Pass will earn additional premium rewards. If you purchase a Premier Pass you will receive any premium rewards you would have been eligible for.
    - **Player:** Sounds great!
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - That's a really big telescope.
    - **Player:** That's a really big telescope.
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Yes.
    - **Player:** ... Can I touch it?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** No.
    - **Player:** Please?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Absolutely not.
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - Do you have a tragic backstory?
    - **Player:** Do you have a tragic backstory?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** No.
    - **Player:** Do you have a backstory?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Yes.
    - **Player:** Can I hear it?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Yes.
    - **Player:** Can I hear it now?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Fine. I am Sir Yaktrick Moore, savant of the stars. When I was but a young yak I travelled all over Gielinor with my master. We would spend our days on the road and our nights sleeping under the stars. How I value those good old days we used to spend together. Alas, he is no longer with us.
    - **Player:** Oh no. What happened to him?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** He settled down with his wife and had a beautiful family. I still see them on the regular, I'm pretty much a part of the family too.
    - **Player:** Wow, that really was a non tragic backstory. Are you sure nothing awful happened? No curses? No fatal mistakes?
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Nope. I'm just a yak that has a big telescope on his back.
    - **Player:** Man, that's disappointing.
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** What!?
    - **Player:** Oh, nothing!
    - (initial|initial-options)
  - Bye!
    - **Player:** Bye!
    - **Sir Yaktrick Moore:** Goodbye.
    - (end)
