# Swabbie Steve

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Swabbie Steve](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASwabbie%20Steve)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

*If the player is not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet*
- **Player:** Hey there, little monkey!
- **Swabbie Steve:** Ook!
*If the player is wearing a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet*
- **Player:** Hey there, little monkey!
- **Swabbie Steve:** I'm not a child, yer know! I'm a bloodthirsty pirate! I just happen to also be a monkey.
- **Player:** Oh, sorry. So, er, why are you here?
- **Swabbie Steve:** It was The Harbinger that sunk us. Fortunately, they're not big into taking my kind. Unfortunately, that didn't stop 'em attacking and killing a bunch of us and sinking our ship, The Barrel.
