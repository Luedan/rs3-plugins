## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to him

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Swensen the Navigator:** Hello outerlander. This is my home, I would be grateful if you would leave.
- **Player:** Oh. Okay.
- **Swensen the Navigator:** I am sorry outerlander, I will not offer you hospitality until my Chieftain has vouched for your honesty. This is our way.

#### Attempting to climb down the ladder

- **Swensen the Navigator:** Hey! Outerlander! Do you normally just barge into someones home and wander around as you please?
- **Player:** Erm.... Well, yes actually.
- **Swensen the Navigator:** Well not here you don't.

### After The Fremennik Trials

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Swensen the Navigator:** Greetings to you brother [Fremennik name]. How fare you today?
- **Player:** I am fine thanks Swensen. How are you doing?
- **Swensen the Navigator:** I am fine too!
