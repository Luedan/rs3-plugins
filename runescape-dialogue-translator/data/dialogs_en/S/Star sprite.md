# Star sprite

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Star sprite](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AStar%20sprite)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Without having mined the star

- **Star sprite:** I don't think I heard you mining this star.
- **Player:** Really? I... er... I've been here this whole time!
- **Star sprite:** My ears are very sensitive, and I'm quite sure I didn't hear you.
- **Player:** Worth a shot.

#### Having mined the star


##### Before claiming the current day's reward

- **Star sprite:** Thank you for helping me out of here.
- (receives=rewards and a Shooting Star status effect)
- **Star sprite:** I have rewarded you by making it so you can mine extra ore for the next [number] minutes. Also, have 152 cosmic runes, 52 astral runes, 70 gold stone spirits, and 50,002 coins.

##### After claiming the current day's reward

- **Star sprite:** Hello, strange creature.
- (Condition: On first time:)
  - **Player:** I'm not strange.
  - **Star sprite:** Hehe. If you say so.
  - **Player:** If anything, you're the strange, glowing, one.
  - **Star sprite:** Isn't that funny? One of the things I find odd about you is that you DON'T glow.
  - **Player:** So what are you? Where did you come from?
  - **Star sprite:** I'm a star sprite! I was in my star in the sky, when it lost control and crashed into the ground. With half my star sticking in the ground, I became stuck. Fortunately, I was mined out by the kind creatures of your race.
  - (continue|options)
- (Condition: After the first time:)
  - **Player:** Hello!
  - (continue|options)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What is a star sprite?
    - **Player:** What is a star sprite?
    - **Star sprite:** We're what makes the stars in the sky shine. I made this star shine when it was in the sky.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What happened to your star?
    - **Player:** What happened to your star exactly?
    - **Star sprite:** I was in my star in the sky, when it lost control and crashed into the ground. If it weren't for your kind, I would still be stuck in there!
    - **Player:** What are you going to do without your star?
    - **Star sprite:** Don't worry about me. I'm sure I'll find some good rocks around here and get back up into the sky in no time.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: I thought stars were huge balls of burning gas.
    - **Player:** I thought stars were huge balls of burning gas.
    - **Star sprite:** Most of them are, but a lot of shooting stars on this plane of the multiverse are rocks with star sprites in them.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Are you okay?
    - **Player:** Are you okay? That looked like a nasty crash.
    - **Star sprite:** Yes, these things happen all the time.
    - **Player:** Err... Well, I'm glad you're okay at least.
    - **Star sprite:** Thank you.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye!
    - **Player:** It's been lovely talking to you, but I've got to go.
    - **Star sprite:** Goodbye strange creature!

### Teleport


#### Before talking to Wizard Rosalina

- **Star sprite:** What are you trying to do?
- **Player:** I'm.... honestly not sure.
- [You must visit Wizard Rosalina on the second floor of the Wizards' Tower to be able to teleport from a fallen star.]

#### After talking to Wizard Rosalina

- [Menu]: Teleport to Wizard Rosalina?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Star sprite:** Happy travels!
    - {{Qact|Player teleports near Wizard Rosalina.}}
  - [Choice]: No.
    - (end)
