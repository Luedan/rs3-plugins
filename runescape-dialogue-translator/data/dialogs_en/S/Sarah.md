## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Falador Farm

- **Sarah:** Hello. How can I help you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - (opens=Sarah's Farming Shop)
  - [Choice]: Can you give me any farming advice?
    - **Player:** Can you give me any farming advice?
    - **Sarah:** Yes - ask a gardener.
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me how to use the loom?
    - **Player:** Can you tell me how to use the loom?
    - **Sarah:** Well, it's actually my loom but I don't mind you using it, if you like. You can use it to weave sacks and baskets in which you can put vegetables and fruit.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do I need to weave sacks?
        - **Player:** What do I need to weave sacks?
        - **Sarah:** Well, the best sacks are made from jute fibres. You can grow jute yourself in a hops patch. I'd say about four jute fibres should be enough to weave a sack.
        - **Player:** Thank you, that's very kind.
      - [Choice]: What do I need to weave baskets?
        - **Player:** What do I need to weave baskets?
        - **Sarah:** Well, the best baskets are made with young branches cut from a willow tree. You'll need a very young willow tree, otherwise the branches will have grown too thick to be able to weave. I suggest growing your own.
        - **Sarah:** You can cut the branches with a standard pair of secateurs. You will probably need about six willow branches to weave a complete basket.
        - **Player:** Thank you, that's very kind.
      - [Choice]: Thank you, that's very kind.
        - **Player:** Thank you, that's very kind.
  - [Choice]: I'm okay, thank you.
    - **Player:** I'm okay, thank you.
