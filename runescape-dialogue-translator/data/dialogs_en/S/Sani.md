## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Sani:** Hello! Welcome to my workshop. My name is Sani. Feel free to use any of my anvils, I can't use them all at once!
- **Player:** Thanks!

### In New Varrock

- **Sani:** Welcome to the most popular smithy in all Misthalin!<br>We make the latest armoured zombie fashions.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you sell ore?
    - **Sani:** I do now. Take a look at my wares!
    - (opens=Sani's Orefully Affordable Store)
  - [Choice]: Can I use the anvils?
    - **Sani:** Of course! Just tell all your friends (and any zombies of high social standing), that Sani is keeping to regulations and honouring our good Lord Zemouregal.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Where did the furnace come from?
    - **Sani:** It's a new addition, paid for by Lord Zemouregal. He even uses it some nights, to get rid of unwanted matter. Praise be to Zemouregal.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Why are no zombies trying to eat me?
    - **Sani:** Bah, we're all past that. Humans tend to run, scream and shoot you in the head with crossbow bolts. Zemouregal rations us two parcels of Zemo Bites a day.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
