## Dialogue Transcript


### In East Ardougne market

- **Sandwich lady:** Hello, dearie. Would you like some of my lovely baked goods?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Let's see what you have.
    - (opens=Sandwich Lady Bakery)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
  - [Choice]: I'd just like to chat.
    - **Sandwich lady:** How lovely - I do like a good natter!
    - **Player:** So, how's the sandwich business treating you?
    - **Sandwich lady:** Oh, I can't complain! Well, I could - this market stall is a bit of a step down from the tea shop I used to own.
    - **Player:** Oh, why? What happened?
    - **Sandwich lady:** I had to sell up to settle my court fees.
    - **Player:** Why would you need to pay court fees? You must be the friendliest person I've ever met!
    - **Sandwich lady:** That's kind of you to say, love...but as nice as I might seem, I do have a bit of a short fuse. All I did was to put one of my baguettes in that gentleman's breadbasket!
    - **Player:** That doesn't sound so bad.
    - **Sandwich lady:**Well, it doesn't...until you realise that by 'breadbasket' I mean his stomach...and by 'put in' I mean repeatedly bash with violent force and murderous intent! And all because he took a pie when I'd only offered him a sandwich!
    - **Player:** When you put it like that...
    - **Sandwich lady:** You'd at least think it would give me reputation enough to stop the thieving beggars around here pinching from my stall! But no - I'm still seen as that lovely old sandwich lady, wouldn't hurt a fly. So you'd best not steal from me or I'll knock your loaf off!
    - **Player:** No, ma'am!

#### During task 13 of Yak to the Shadows

- [Menu]: What would you like to talk about?
  - [Choice]: General Chat.
    - *If she hasn't seen the player steal recently:*
      - (continuea|In East Ardougne market)
    - *If she has seen the player steal recently:*
      - (continuea|Trading or speaking to her after stealing from her stall)
  - [Choice]: Bakers Dozen.
    - **Player:** I heard you are looking for thirteen chocolate cakes.
    - **Sandwich lady:** That's right.
    - *If the player doesn't have at least 13 chocolate cakes:*
      - **Player:** I'll come back when I have enough.
    - *If the player has at least 13 chocolate cakes:*
      - (removes=13 chocolate cakes)
      - **Player:** Here you go!
      - **Sandwich lady:** Thank you!
      - **Player:** What do you want all these cakes for anyway?
      - **Sandwich lady:** Chocolate cake sandwiches! I'm close to perfecting the recipe.
      - **Player:** I can't decide if that sounds delicious or disgusting.
