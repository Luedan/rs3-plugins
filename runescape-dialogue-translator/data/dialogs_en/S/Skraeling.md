# Skraeling

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Skraeling](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASkraeling)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Throne of Miscellania

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Skraeling:** Good day.

### After Throne of Miscellania

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Skraeling:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.

### Getting caught while attempting to steal from a stall

- **Skraeling:** {{RSChat|Hey! Get your hands off there!}}

### Occasionally upon being killed by the player

- **Skraeling:** {{RSChat|I'm a gonner!}}
