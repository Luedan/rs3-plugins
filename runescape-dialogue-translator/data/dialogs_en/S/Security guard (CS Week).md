## Dialogue Transcript


### Clue 1

{{Qact|Dig directly south of the Cradle of Life that is hidden within death.}}
- **Security guard:** Aha! Question time!<br>Should you log in anywhere other than the RuneScape website?
  - Yes!
    - **Security guard:** {{RSChat|Incorrect!}}
    - IMPORTANT: You should never log in in anywhere other than the RuneScape website.
    - {{Qact|The guard teleports away.}}
  - No!
    - **Security guard:** Good, you're smarter than you look.
    - The guard happily hands you a scroll box.
    - Well done, you received a [pair of caskets/scroll box]!

### Clue 2

{{Qact|In a place of pestilence, dig directly east of a goo-ridden escape.}}
- **Security guard:** Aha! Question time!<br>What do I do if my account is compromised?
  - Post on social media about it.
    - **Security guard:** {{RSChat|Wrong!}}
    - Wrong! Social media will often not solve the problem directly and will only succeed in directing you to the RuneScape website to regain access to your account.
    - {{Qact|The guard teleports away.}}
  - Secure my computer and reset my RuneScape password.
    - **Security guard:** Correct! Securing your systems with virus and malware scans is important before resetting your account password to ensure your details are not compromised again. Don't forget to secure your email account too!
    - The guard happily hands you a scroll box.
    - Well done, you received a [pair of caskets./scroll box!]

### Clue 3

{{Qact|Dig behind the poster in a jail cell filled with chess pieces and a rock hammer.}}
- **Security guard:** Aha! Question time!<br>You have been offered an opportunity to check out a free giveaway or double XP sign-up via social media or stream. What should you do?
  - Respond quickly so as not to miss the offer.
    - **Security guard:** {{RSChat|Wrong!}}
    - Wrong! Jagex will NEVER make offers like this - it is an attempt to obtain your account details. Please report the incident using whatever reporting methods offered by the site you are on.
    - *(The guard disappears.)*
  - Report the incident and do not click any links.
    - **Security guard:** Correct! This is an attempt to obtain your account details and should be reported to the relevant organisation via their own reporting systems.
    - The guard happily hands you a scroll box.
    - Well done, you received a [pair of caskets/scroll box]!

  - *(If players enters 7:)*
    - **Security Guard:** Correct! Well done!
    - The guard happily hands you a reward.
    - Well done, you received a [pair of caskets/scroll box].

### Clue 6

{{Qact|Use the Safety First emote on the bottom floor of the Stronghold of Security. Equip safety gloves and fancy boots.}}
- **Security guard:** Aha! Question time!<br>How do you set a bank PIN?
  - Talk to any banker in RuneScape.
    - **Security guard:** Correct! Simply talking to a banker will give you the option to set a bank PIN. Never use personal details for passwords or bank PINs!
    - The guard happily hands you a scroll box.
    - You're handed a [clue scroll/pair of caskets].
  - Use the Account Management section on the website.
    - **Security guard:** {{RSChat|Wrong!}}
    - Wrong! Your password can be changed from the Account Management section, but you must talk to a banker to set a bank PIN. Never use personal details for passwords or bank PINs!
    - {{Qact|The guard teleports away.}}

### Clue 7

  - By emailing RuneScape.
