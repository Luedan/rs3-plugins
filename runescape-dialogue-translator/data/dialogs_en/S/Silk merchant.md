# Silk merchant

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Silk merchant](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASilk%20merchant)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In East Ardougne market


#### Without silk in backpack

- **Silk merchant:** I buy silk. If you ever want to sell any silk, bring it here.

#### With silk in backpack

- **Player:** Hello. I have some fine silk from Al Kharid to sell to you.
- **Silk merchant:** Ah, I may be interested in that. What sort of price were you looking at per piece of silk?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: 20 coins.
    - **Player:** 20 coins.
    - **Silk merchant:** Okay, suits me.
    - (removes=silk) (receives=20 coins)
  - [Choice]: 80 coins.
    - **Player:** 80 coins.
    - **Silk merchant:** 80 coins? That's a bit steep! How about 40 coins?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Okay, 40 sounds good.
        - **Player:** Okay, 40 sounds good.
        - (removes=silk) (receives=40 coins)
      - [Choice]: 50, and that's my final price.
        - **Player:** 50, and that's my final price.
        - **Silk merchant:** Done.
        - (removes=silk) (receives=50 coins)
      - [Choice]: No, that is not enough.
        - **Player:** No, that is not enough.
  - [Choice]: 120 coins.
    - **Player:** 120 coins.
    - **Silk merchant:** You'll never get that much for it. I'll be generous and give you 50 for it.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Okay, I guess 50 will do.
        - **Player:** Okay, I guess 50 will do.
        - (removes=silk) (receives=50 coins)
      - [Choice]: I'll give it to you for 60.
        - **Player:** I'll give it to you for 60.
        - **Silk merchant:** You drive a hard bargain but I guess that will have to do.
        - (removes=silk) (receives=60 coins)
      - [Choice]: No, that is not enough.
        - **Player:** No, that is not enough.
  - [Choice]: 200 coins.
    - **Player:** 200 coins.
    - **Silk merchant:** Don't be ridiculous! That is far too much. You insult me with that price.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from his stall

- **Silk merchant:** {{RSChat|Hey! Get your hands off there!}}

#### Trading or speaking to him after stealing from his stall

- **Silk merchant:** {{RSChat|You're the one who stole something from me!}}
- **Silk merchant:** {{RSChat|Thief! Thief! Thief!}}
