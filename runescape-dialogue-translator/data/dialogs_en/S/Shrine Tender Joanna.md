#### After Visions of Havenhythe

- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** It's an honour to have you visit our shrine again, blessed of The Great Wolf.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** Is there anything I can help you with?
- **Player:** I was hoping you could tell me more about Inanna.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** Of course!
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** Inanna is benevolent, nurturing and patient. Stepping in to protect her children when the need arises while providing them the distance to thrive independently of her.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** She provided guidance to our ancestors when they first arrived here. Every Wendlewicker and their family has a unique history with and connection to her.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** Even my brother and I have differing philosophies, despite both tending her shrine.
- **Player:** What are your beliefs?
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** Inanna is more than a mere messenger of the gods. She is a deity in and of herself.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** She is the one who lives among our people and safeguards our lands, while asking nothing of us in return.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** The other gods crave worship, while The Great Wolf is selfless and humble. That is why she and she alone deserves my reverence.
- **Player:** I understand. Thank you for sharing that with me.
- **Shrine Tender Joanna:** You are very welcome. May Inanna watch over you, my friend.
