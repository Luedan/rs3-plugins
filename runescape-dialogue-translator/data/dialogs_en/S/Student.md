## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Student #1 (Girl with pigtails and ponytail)

- **Student:** *Sob!* I’m going to fail! I hate exams.
- **Player:** Cheer up! I’m sure you’ll do fine. Try to concentrate.
- **Professor Henry:** No talking in the exam, please.
- **Player:** Sorry!

#### Student #2 (Guy in brown shirt)

- **Student:** Bah! I hate maths.
- **Player:** I thought this was a Player Safety exam?
- **Student:** Yes, it is - but I just hate maths.
- **Player:** Ah, okay.
- **Professor Henry:** Please leave the students alone while they take their exams. We don’t want any cheating in here.
- **Player:** Oh! Sorry about that.

#### Student #3 (Guy in black shirt)

- **Student:** What’s 769 x 426?
- **Player:** What? Er… I don’t know.
- **Student:** Me neither.
- **Professor Henry:** No talking in the exam, please.
- **Player:** Sorry!

#### Student #4 (Girl with bun hairstyle)

- **Player:** Sorry!

#### Student #5 (Girl with spiked ponytail hairstyle)
