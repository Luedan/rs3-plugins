## Dialogue Transcript


### In the White Knights' Castle

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Sir Vyvin:** Greetings traveller.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Do you have anything to trade?
    - **Player:** Do you have anything to trade?
    - (Condition: Before completing the Wanted! quest, or on a free-to-play world:)
      - **Sir Vyvin:** No, I'm sorry.
    - (Condition: After completing the Wanted! quest, but the player is unranked:)
      - **Sir Vyvin:** Well, maybe I do, but you need to prove yourself worthy as a White Knight before I will sell you anything.
      - **Player:** And how can I do that?
      - **Sir Vyvin:** Kill Black Knights and make sure you don’t kill any White Knights!
      - **Player:** Well, okay then.
    - (Condition: After completing the Wanted! quest, and the player is ranked:)
      - **Sir Vyvin:** Well, of course! <br>I AM the White Knights armourer after all!
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Novice" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** You are currently at Novice level within the White Knights, so your access to equipment is limited.
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Peon" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** You are at Peon level within the White Knights, so not much to choose from I'm afraid.
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Page" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** You've reached Page level, that shows a bit of dedication you know! <br>Your available equipment will reflect that!
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Noble" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** And may I say what a pleasure it is to serve any Knight who has achieved Noble level!
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Adept" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** And it is so very rare that I see any White Knight who has reached Adept level!
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: If the player is at "Master" level:)
        - **Sir Vyvin:** And for a dedicated White Knight to have reached Master level like yourself, you get only the very best of all equipment available!
        - (continue)
      - (opens=White Knight Master Armoury)
  - [Choice]: Why are there so many knights in this city?
    - **Player:** Why are there so many knights in this city?
    - **Sir Vyvin:** We are the White Knights of Falador. We are the most powerful order of knights in the land. We are helping the king Vallance rule the kingdom as he is getting old and tired.
  - [Choice]: Can I just distract you for a minute?
    - **Player:** Can I just talk to you very slowly for a few minutes, while I distract you, so that my friend over there can do something while you're busy being distracted by me?
    - **Sir Vyvin:** ... ...what?
    - **Sir Vyvin:** I'm... not sure what you're asking me... you want to join the White Knights?
    - **Player:** Nope. I'm just trying to distract you.
    - **Sir Vyvin:** ... ...you are very odd.
    - **Player:** So can I distract you some more?
    - **Sir Vyvin:** ... ...I don't think I want to talk to you anymore.
    - **Player:** Ok. My work here is done. 'Bye!
