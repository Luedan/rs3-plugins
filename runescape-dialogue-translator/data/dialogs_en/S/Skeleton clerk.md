## Dialogue Transcript


### In New Varrock


#### West bank, ground floor

- **Skeleton clerk:** These are the private vaults of His Fleshiness, Zemouregal. <br>Go away.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: I want to access my bank.
    - **Skeleton clerk:** The bank of New Varrock is now the personal treasury of our glorious leader, Zemouregal. <br>We no longer provide a banking service to fleshy folk.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

#### East bank, upstairs

- **Skeleton clerk:** I have no idea why you are in the private vaults of Zemouregal. <br>Begone.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: I want to access my bank.
    - **Skeleton clerk:** This is the far-superior eastern vaults of Zemouregal. <br>We have protected the treasure of Magnificent Zemouregal at least fifty times better than the western vaults.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)
