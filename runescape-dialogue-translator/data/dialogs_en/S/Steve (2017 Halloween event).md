# Steve (2017 Halloween event)

- **Letter**: `S`

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to

- Select an option
  - Where were you on the full moon?
    - **Steve:** I decided to do battle with the giant mole. It was a tough fight, but I prevailed. I am the mole slayer, FEAR THE SWING OF MY BLADE!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Did you see anything suspicious?
    - **Steve:** I was too busy fighting a mole. A great big mole, with sharp, pointed teeth - none of these garden-variety moles.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Who do you suspect of being the werewolf?
    - **Steve:** Jamie is a bit creepy. He keeps laughing when he treads on spiders. It's weird.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - [Accuse Steve of being the werewolf.]
    - **Player:** Steve! Don't think you can hide your fangs from me! I see you for what you really are: A WEREWOLF!
    - *(Steve dies.)*
    - **Player:** Ah...oops. Guess I was wrong.
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Steve:** Goodbye.
