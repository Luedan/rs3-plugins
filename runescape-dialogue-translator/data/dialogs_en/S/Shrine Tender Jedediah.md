#### After Visions of Havenhythe

- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** May The Great Wolf watch over you, friend. You are always welcome at this shrine.
- **Player:** Can you tell me more about Inanna?
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** It would be my pleasure.
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** My beliefs are somewhat traditional. She is the guardian of our lands, a herald and messenger sent to us by the gods themselves in ages past.
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** In her we see Guthix's patience. Saradomin's wisdom. Zamorak's strength.
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** When we pray to Inanna, we are offering prayers to each of the gods she represents. She is a unifying embodiment of all we value and cherish.
- **Player:** That's definitely a unique approach to worship.
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** Most outlanders are confused by our beliefs. From what I understand there is a great deal of conflict between the followers of different gods on the mainland.
- **Player:** That's putting it lightly.
- **Player:** Thank you for sharing this with me.
- **Shrine Tender Jedediah:** It is nothing. Go in peace, friend.
