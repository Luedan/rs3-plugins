## Dialogue Transcript


### Without 70 Summoning

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Sparky:** Chatterchatterchatter chatterchatter chatter!
- **Player:** Hmm. If I was more skilled with animals, I would be able to figure out what he's saying.
