## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to her

- **Sassilik:** I cannot speak to you outerlander! Talk to Brundt, the Chieftain!

#### Pickpocketing her

- **Sassilik:** {{RSChat|You stay away from me outerlander!}}
- They are too suspicious of you for you to get close enough to steal from them.

### After [[Hero's Welcome]]

*When speaking to Sassilik in Rellekka.*
- **Player:** Hello.
- *Sassilik may greet you with any of the following at random:*
- Greeting 1:
  - **Sassilik:** How are you, [Fremennik name]? I hope all fares well with you this day!
  - **Player:** I'm fine, thanks, how are you?
  - **Sassilik:** All is well with me also!
- Greeting 2:
  - **Sassilik:** Hello again, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. What need you of me this day? You can always rely on my assistance!
- Greeting 3:
  - **Sassilik:** Hello. Did you want something, [Fremennik name]?
- Greeting 4:
  - **Sassilik:** [Fremennik name], it has been too long since last we met!
- Greeting 5:
  - **Sassilik:** Welcome back, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. It is always a pleasure to see you here!
- Greeting 6:
  - **Sassilik:** Hello to you, too!
- Greeting 7:
  - **Sassilik:** Always good to see you, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name].
  - Select an option (all greetings continue with dialogue below)
    - Can I ask you about V?
    - *Sassilik may reply to you with any of the following at random:*
    - Reply 1:
      - **Sassilik:** He died saving you, [Fremennik name]. Make sure you use his final gift wisely and live a life that we can all be proud of.
    - Reply 2:
      - **Sassilik:** He was a great hero to our people. Why couldn't they have gone for one of the bad gods, like Zamorak or Saradomin?
    - Reply 3:
      - **Sassilik:** Those cowards. They didn't even have the decency to face him as a warrior.
    - Reply 4:
      - **Sassilik:** It's sad that he's gone, but he will live on as an inspiration to countless heroes across countless worlds.
    - Goodbye.
      - (end)
