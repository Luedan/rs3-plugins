## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[King's Ransom]] quest

- **Squire:** I'm sorry, my lord, but you cannot enter this area.
- [You need to have completed the King's Ransom Quest to gain access to the Knight Waves.]

### Before the [[Knight Waves training ground]]


#### Before asking about the rules

- **Squire:** Greetings, brave knight!
- **Player:** Hello. What's through that door?
- **Squire:** That door leads to the training ground for the Knights of the Round Table.
- **Player:** Can I go in there?
- (Condition: If you have not yet completed the requirements for Knight Waves training ground:)
  - {{tmissing}}
- (Condition: If you have completed the requirements for Knight Waves training ground:)
  - **Squire:** Of course, my [lord/lady]. But I must tell you the rules first. Would you like to hear them now?
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Yes please.
      - **Player:** Yes please.
      - **Squire:** Stuns and binds will not work in the training grounds. Merlin has cast a spell over the grounds to prevent anyone using prayer, stuns or binds.
      - **Squire:** Upon entering the grounds, you will fight each of the Round Table Knights in turn. Each knight is more skilled than the last, but all have different attack styles that can be exploited. There are eight knights in all, and each will do damage not only to your life points, but also your combat stats. If you should die in the grounds, Merlin will teleport you back here. You will not lose any of your items.
      - **Squire:** If you feel you cannot complete the battle, you may exit the training grounds. However, if you exit or die, you will have to start over with the first knight the next time you enter.
      - **Squire:** If you are a great warrior and defeat Sir Lancelot, mightiest of all knights, you will receive great rewards. Four new prayers will become available to you and your respawn point will be set to Camelot, as well as 20,000 XP in to each of your combat stats.
      - **Squire:** If you do not want your respawn set to Camelot, Merlin can change it for you.
      - **Squire:** Would you like me to repeat any of that for you?
      - [Menu]: Select an Option {{anchor|rules}}
        - [Choice]: Tell me about the training ground.
          - **Player:** Tell me about the training ground.
          - **Squire:** Merlin will prevent the use of Prayer, Stuns and binds.
          - **Player:** Why?
          - **Square:** These grounds are for only the best knights of the land. It is not just testing your combat ability, but your honour and your chivalry. You must face your enemy head on, honourably and bravely. Be watchful of your stats, as the knights can do damage to your combat stats as well as your life points.
        - [Choice]: Tell me about dying or leaving the grounds.
          - **Player:** Tell me about dying or leaving the grounds.
          - **Squire:** As these are only training grounds, we keep our knights safe. If you should die, you will be teleported back to here and all of your items will be safe.
          - **Squire:** However, leaving the training grounds, in any way, will mean you will have to start from the beginning the next time you enter. These grounds do not favour cowards.
          - **Player:** I have to start again? What do you mean?
          - **Squire:** You fight each knight of the Round Table, starting with Sir Bedivere and finishing with Sir Lancelot. Leaving means you will have to begin fighting Sir Bedivere again and work your way back up.
          - **Player:** What if I log out during the fights?
          - **Squire:** If you log out during a fight, upon logging back in you will fight the knight you were fighting when you logged out.
        - [Choice]: Tell me about the rewards.
          - **Player:** Tell me about the rewards.
          - **Squire:** King Arthur believes in rewarding his knights for their chivalry. Having proven yourself in combat against his knights, and in your services rendered to Camelot, you will be rewarded with four new prayers, and a new respawn at Camelot, as well as 20,000 XP in to each of your combat stats.
          - **Player:** What do the new prayers do?
          - **Squire:** Ah, patience is a virtue in knights, my [lord/lady]. Fight in the training ground and earn your reward to find out!
          - **Player:** Then tell me about the respawn - how will it work?
          - **Squire:** Ah, Merlin has offered, as a reward for your help, to let you respawn in the safety of Camelot, should you befall misfortune elsewhere in the world. Should you wish to change your respawn back to Lumbridge, simply speak to Merlin.
        - [Choice]: No, thank you.
          - (below)
    - [Choice]: No, thank you.
      - **Player:** No, thank you.
