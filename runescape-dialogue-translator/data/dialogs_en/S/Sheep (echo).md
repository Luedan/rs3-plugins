# Sheep (echo)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Sheep (echo)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASheep%20%28echo%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Guthix:** I respect the sheep, bar none, for their purpose is essential, albeit secret.
- **Guthix:** Where there is wool, there is a way.
- **Guthix:** Some day, you too might discover the true power of the sheep, innocent in appearance but essential in bearing.
- **Guthix:** Sheep are under my protection. Harm them at your peril, or you shall pay for your transgression.
- **Guthix:** I shall not tolerate such sheer audacity.
- **Guthix:** Allow me to ram this point home: watch out for the sheep with webbed feet. They are mutton but trouble, and more cunning than they look.
- **Guthix:** Though if you cannot bleat them, join them.
