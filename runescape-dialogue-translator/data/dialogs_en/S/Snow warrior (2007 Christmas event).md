# Snow warrior (2007 Christmas event)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Snow warrior (2007 Christmas event)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASnow%20warrior%20%282007%20Christmas%20event%29)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- (Condition: Out of combat)
  - **Snow warrior:** Hail, noble visitor. We, who are about to melt, salute you.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a warrior)
  - **Snow warrior:** Ha ha! There's nothing like the clash of ice on ice.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a ranger)
  - **Snow warrior:** Ha ha! Your holly bow is no match for my ice sword.
  - (end)
- (Condition: If fighting a mage)
  - **Snow warrior:** I will triumph over your spells, yet.
  - (end)
