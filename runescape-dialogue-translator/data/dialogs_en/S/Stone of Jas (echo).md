## Dialogue Transcript

- **Guthix:** Ah, the Stone.<br>Known by many names, including, much to my discomfort, the Fist of Guthix.
- **Guthix:** Its ancient power has opened up boundless potential, and the lust for that power has irrevocably scarred this perfect world.
- **Guthix:** The races I brought to Gielinor have fought, died and longed for it. I set these events in motion, inciting millennia of conflict and death. I hold myself accountable for these atrocities.
- **Guthix:** I sought to share its power with mortals, that they might not be subjugated by the gods. They left their gods behind, attracted by the potential of magic.
- **Guthix:** I distilled the Stone's essence into runic magic, stones of power, but the discovery of magic spells I left in the hands of those that followed me to Gielinor.
- **Guthix:** The diversity of magical effects they achieved was inspiring...though many only sought mastery of only the basest elements, drawing on them for raw offensive power.
- **Guthix:** I should have seen this as a sign of things to come, but I hoped that in free reign they might see the harmful impact for themselves, and gain enlightenment from their destructive impulses.
- **Guthix:** Alas, the colonists returned to me, empty-handed and hungry for more power; their desire for rune stones almost an addiction.
- **Guthix:** Reluctantly, I sought to indulge them, but I sensed within the Stone a growing presence: anger, violent rage.
- **Guthix:** It grew as I drew power from the Stone, and I feared that this terrible ferocity might be unleashed.
- **Guthix:** I realised that I had abused the Stone's power too long, and the consequences if I persevered would be grave; so I hid the Stone, for our own protection.
- **Guthix:** My refusal was poorly received. Some turned to desperate worship, others to violence over the jealously hoarded rune stones, now a precious commodity stained in spilt blood.
- **Guthix:** In retrospect, I was motivated by cowardice and fatigue, but it was at this time that I withdrew into slumber.
- **Guthix:** I hoped that, should I awake, I might bear witness to a more civilised age.
- **Guthix:** That time did not come.
