# Shady ghost

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Shady ghost](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AShady%20ghost)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### While wearing a ghostspeak amulet

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Shady ghost:** Hello, stranger.
- *If the player doesn't have all the pieces of the First age outfit:*
  - **Shady ghost:** Got somethin' that might interest ya, stranger. He he he.
  - **Player:** What're you sellin'?
  - **Shady ghost:** Got some real old trinkets, accessories if you like. Holy. Might help you train your inner powers. Yours for 1,000 ecto-tokens.
  - *(If the player has at least 1,000 ecto-tokens:)*
    - Purchase a piece of the first-age outfit for 1,000 ecto-tokens?
      - I'll take one!
        - You purchase a piece of the first-age outfit from the ghost.
        - (receives=a piece of the First age outfit)
        - **Shady ghost:** All yours. Goodbye, stranger.
      - Erm, no thanks.
        - (end)
  - *(If the player has less than 1,000 ecto-tokens:)*
    - **Player:** It doesn't look like I have enough. Maybe another time.
- *(If the player already has all of the outfit pieces:)*
  - **Shady ghost:** I'd show you my wares, but it seems I'm out of stock at the moment.
  - **Player:** I'll leave you to it then. Goodbye.
  - **Shady ghost:** Goodbye, stranger.

### Without wearing a ghostspeak amulet

- **Shady ghost:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.
