# Shady lurker

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Shady lurker](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AShady%20lurker)
- **Letter**: `S`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to during Aiding the Exile

- *(If there are Menaphite coins to hand in:)*
  - *(Dialogue from handing in Menaphite coins occurs, then continues below.)*
- **Shady lurker:** Hey there traveller! I don't suppose you're allowed through those gates are you? If you are then I may just have a job for you.
- **Player:** What is it you need?
- **Shady lurker:** Menaphite coins! These beauties are all over the places beyond these walls. If you can bring 'em back to me, I can reward you!
- **Player:** That sounds like a good deal to me.
  - Can't you just go and collect them yourself?
    - **Player:** Can't you just go and collect them yourself?
    - **Shady lurker:** Ah, I should have guessed you would ask that. The short answer is no, they cast me out! I was once a citizen of Menaphos but I am no longer allowed within those walls. It's not something I really want to talk about.
    - **Player:** Sorry to hear that, from what I heard it's a beautiful city.
    - **Shady lurker:** Oh you don't need to tell me!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What do you do?
    - **Player:** What do you do?
    - **Shady lurker:** Nowadays I travel around in search of interesting items, and then I trade them with travellers like yourself. I've got some pretty interesting offerings that I know are of a specific interest to a few people within Menaphos.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - **Player:** How long will you be around for?
    - **Shady lurker:** Not too long as I'm a busy guy, I have things to do, people to trade y'know? I'll be around for a little while though as I need a good bunch of Menaphite coins before I'm ready to move on. One of my clients needs a large chunk of them for their own reasons that I will not go into.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*

### Handing in Menaphite coins


#### Without Menaphite coins

- You do not have anything to hand in.

#### With Menaphite coins

- Handing in event currency will increase your progress for this event unlocking unique rewards. This will remove currency up to the amount that is required to complete the event.
- Do you wish to continue?
  - No
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
  - Yes
    - You hand in [X] Menaphite coins. You need to hand in [Y] more to complete the event.
    - *(Dialogue from claiming rewards occurs.)*

#### With all rewards claimed

- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.

### Claiming rewards


#### Without rewards to claim and none claimed

- **Shady lurker:**  You'll need to help me some more before I can offer you any rewards.

#### With rewards to claim

- **Shady lurker:** There you go Player.
- *(If items are obtained:)*
  - *(If the inventory is full:)*
    - You do not have enough inventory space for some of the rewards. Please make some space and try again.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - Congratulations, you have been awarded [item(s)].
    - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If a customisation is unlocked:)*
  - Congratulations, you have unlocked [reward].
  - *(Continues below.)*
- You have unlocked [X]/9 of the rewards for the 'Aiding the Exile' event.

#### Without rewards to claim and some claimed

- **Shady lurker:** You need to give me some more Menaphite coins before I can reward you.
- You have unlocked [X]/9 of the rewards for the 'Aiding the Exile' event.

#### With all rewards claimed

- **Shady lurker:** You've already got everything I have to offer.
- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.

### When talked to after Aiding the Exile

- **Shady lurker:** Thanks for the help Player. I've got a basket full of these coins now, so make sure you collect your rewards as I'll be off shortly.
  - Can't you just go and collect them yourself?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - What do you do?
    - *(As during the event.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - *(As during the event.)*
