## Dialogue Transcript


### South of Death Plateau

- **Player:** Are you OK?
- **Soldier:** Urrrgggh... I'll be OK, the trolls only leave the plateau at nightfall. The guys are bringing a stretcher shortly.
- **Player:** As long as you're sure.
- **Soldier:** It's my own fault really, I was having a walk and wandered too far past the danger sign. The trolls throw rocks down at any one who goes up the path!
- **Soldier:** Don't go up there!
- **Player:** OK, thanks for the warning.
