## Dialogue Transcript


### In East Ardougne market

- **Silver merchant:** Silver! Silver! Best prices for buying and selling in all Kandarin!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - (opens=Ardougne Silver Stall)
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.
  - [Choice]: cond=After Making History:|Ask about the Outpost.
    - **Player:** Hello again.
    - **Silver merchant:** Hello, I hope Jorral was pleased with that journal.
    - **Player:** I'm sure it's been a valuable find.
    - (Condition: If the enchanted key has been lost:)
      - **Player:**What I came to ask was: I lost that key you gave me.
      - **Silver Merchant:**Oh dear. Can't help you there.
      - **Player:**Suppose I could have a chat with Jorral, maybe he has ideas for getting another.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from his stall

- **Silver merchant:** {{RSChat|Hey! Get your hands off there!}}

#### Trading or speaking to him after stealing from his stall

- **Silver merchant:**{{RSChat|shadow=yes|colour=yellow|You stole from me. Talk to me when you've considered your actions.}}
