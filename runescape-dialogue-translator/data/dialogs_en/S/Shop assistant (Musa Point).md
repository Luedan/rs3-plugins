## Dialogue Transcript


### At Musa Point

- (Condition: Before A Void Dance:)
  - **Shop assistant:** Can I help you at all?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: After A Void Dance:)
  - **Shop assistant:** It's a beautiful day today, no? Can I do anything for you?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you selling?
    - (opens=Karamja General Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Shop assistant:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)
