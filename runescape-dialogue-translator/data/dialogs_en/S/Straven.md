    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.

### In New Varrock


#### Before Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Straven:** This is a private business. <br>As in, ask too many questions and you get kicked in the privates.
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - **Straven:**   Yeah, it's been a highlight of the past few minutes.

#### After Dimension of Disaster: Shield of Arrav

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: The Shield of Arrav is back in the Museum!
    - **Straven:** I'm glad we could come to an arrangement that didn't involve running, screaming or disembowelment.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the Shield of Arrav.
    - **Straven:** That's a blast from the past! We stole that from the museum years ago!
    - **Straven:** Over thirty years ago, the Phoenix Gang were subject to infighting and the shield got broken in half.
    - **Straven:** Shortly after the fight, some gang members decided they didn't want to be part of our gang any more.
    - **Straven:** Katrine and her lackeys split off to form their own gang. The Black Arm Gang.
    - **Straven:** On their way out, they looted what they could from us - which included one of the halves of the shield. We've been rivals with the Black Arm ever since.
    - **Straven:** That was only the start of our troubles. When Zemouregal invaded Varrock, he came after both gangs. He wanted each half of the shield.
    - **Straven:** After he killed Katrine and I, he brought us back as zombies.
    - **Player:** What happened to the shield after that?
    - **Straven:** I don't really know. Zemouregal stored the two halves in separate vaults. Maybe only Arrav can make it work?
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (above)
