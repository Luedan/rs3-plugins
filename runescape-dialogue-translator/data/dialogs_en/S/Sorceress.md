## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid

- **Sorceress:** Who are you and what do you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: None of your business!
    - **Player:** None of your business! I go where I like and do what I like.
    - **Sorceress:** Not in my house. Be gone!
    - **Sorceress:**{{RSChat|Be gone, intruder!}}
    - {{Qact|The player is teleported outside the house}}
  - [Choice]: I'm here to kill you!
    - **Player:** I'm here to kill you!
    - **Sorceress:** I think not!
    - **Sorceress:**{{RSChat|Be gone, intruder!}}
    - {{Qact|The player is teleported outside the house}}
  - [Choice]: Can I have some sq'irks please?
    - **Player:** Can I have some sq'irks please?
    - **Sorceress:** What do you want them for?
    - **Player:** Someone asked me to bring them some.
    - **Sorceress:** Who?
    - **Player:** You find yourself compelled to answer truthfully: Osman. <!-- Yes, that's exactly how it appears in the chatbox -->
    - **Sorceress:** In that case I'm sorry, you can't. I have had a falling out with him recently and would rather not oblige him.
  - [Choice]: I'm just passing by.
    - **Player:** I'm just passing by.
