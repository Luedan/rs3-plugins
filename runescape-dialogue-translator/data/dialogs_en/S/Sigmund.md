## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Lost Tribe, in Lumbridge Castle

- **Sigmund:** Can I help you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Do you have any quests for me?
    - (see|The Lost Tribe#Basement Intruder)
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Sigmund:** I'm the Duke's advisor.
    - **Player:** Can you give me any advice then?
    - **Sigmund:** I only advise the Duke. But if you want to make yourself useful, there are evil goblins to slay on the other side of the river.
