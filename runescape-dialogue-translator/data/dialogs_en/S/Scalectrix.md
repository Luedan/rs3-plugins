## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley


#### Before Wolf Whistle

- **Scalectrix:** Oh dear… oh dear…
- **Player:** Are you alright?
- The young druidess seems intent on staring into the well and wringing her hands, and does not reply.
- **Player:** Uh… ok then! Have a nice day!

#### After Wolf Whistle

- **Scalectrix:** It looks like those troll leaders have fled back to the mountain.
- **Player:** So have all the trolls followed them?
- **Scalectrix:** No, it looks like they have been sneaking past in small groups and hiding in the well.
- **Player:** Well hopefully the numbers should be small enough for me and the other soldiers to deal with.
- **Scalectrix:** Guthix protect you, take care if you do go inside.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: So, tell me about yourself.
    - **Scalectrix:** Of course! I suppose introductions were sort of lost in all the kafuffle.
    - **Scalectrix:** I’m Scalectrix, although that is my druid name. I was originally from Burthorpe, but came to live here when the trolls invaded.
    - **Scalectrix:** My home was one of the ones destroyed. The druids took me in and let me live here among them, like the other refugees.
    - **Scalectrix:** Apparently I have a talent for summoning, so I came to work with Pikkupstix while I got settled and the trolls were repelled.
    - **Scalectrix:** One day I hope, while working for him, I will attain the level of master and wear a Summoning skillcape too!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What brought you to Taverley?
    - **Scalectrix:** The war, mostly. I lived in Burthorpe all my life, and then the major push from the trolls destroyed my house and displaced me down here.
    - **Scalectrix:** I’d always liked coming to Taverley when I had free time from my work in the games room, and I was lucky that the druids took me in.
    - **Scalectrix:** A lot of the refugees moved back up once the trolls were repelled.
    - **Scalectrix:** But I love it here! I never want to leave!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: How do you think the trolls got here?
    - **Scalectrix:** Do you mean in general, or here in this well?
    - **Player:** Here in this well.
    - **Scalectrix:** Well a mass of trolls would have been spotted by the troops, so I suppose they are scouts and stragglers.
    - **Scalectrix:** If one or two of them at a time slip past the guard through the wells and tunnels, or in the confusion of combat, then they could have found each other.
    - **Scalectrix:** Although I get the feeling those two leaders were scouts, who must have found this well and let the others know.
    - **Scalectrix:** That might be why there are more still sneaking in there; the leaders ran off and never told them to stop coming.
    - **Scalectrix:** Still, now the guards know to look for them, there are patrols of the Imperial Guard from Burthorpe looking around to sweep up any survivors.
    - **Player:** And me, of course.
    - **Scalectrix:** Of course! I’m sure you’ll help keep them at bay!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I’d best be off.
    - **Scalectrix:** Be well.
