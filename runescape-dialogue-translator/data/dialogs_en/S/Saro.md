## Dialogue Transcript


### In western Keldagrim


#### Standard dialogue

- **Saro:** Welcome to my store, human! Are you interested in buying anything?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Yes, I'm looking for some armour.
    - **Player:** Yes, I'm looking for some armour.
    - (opens=Quality Armour Shop)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Saro:** Say, would you be interested in doing a little task for me?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do you need me to do?
    - **Player:** What do you need me to do?
    - **Saro:** A special client has a little request for me. I will need [item] in the next [number] minutes.
    - **Player:** Right, I’ll get you [item] in the next [number] minutes. I can do that, yes.
  - [Choice]: I don’t have time for that, no.
    - **Player:** I don’t have time for that, no.
    - **Saro:** That’s really unfortunate, I hope I can find someone else.

##### Talking to Saro without the item

- **Saro:** How are you doing getting me my [item]?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I don’t want to look for them anymore.
    - **Player:** I don’t want to look for them anymore.
    - **Saro:** Next time, can you decide on that in advance? You’ve put me in a spot of trouble now. I suppose I’ll find a way out though.
  - [Choice]: I’m still looking for them.
    - **Player:** I’m still looking for them.
    - **Saro:** Good, good. Hurry back!

##### Talking to Saro with the item

- **Player:** I’ve brought you your [item]!
- **Saro:** Excellent, much appreciated! Do you want me to pay you in gold or do you want me to show you how to fight properly in these?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Show me the gold!
    - **Player:** Show me the gold!
    - (receives=coins)
  - [Choice]: Enlighten me.
    - **Player:** Enlighten me.
    - {{Qact|Screen fades in and out}}
    - (receives=experience)
    - You feel more experienced.
