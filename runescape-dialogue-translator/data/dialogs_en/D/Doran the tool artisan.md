## Dialogue Transcript


### Information during the Travelling Artisan event

- ((First time:))
  - **Player:** Hey, what's going on here?
  - **Doran the tool artisan:** Ah! Welcome! The name's Doran and I'm known as a travelling artisan! We're having a special event at the moment where we can craft a bunch of unique rewards especially for you!
  - **Player:** Oh that sounds great!
  - (continue)
- ((Afterwards:))
  - **Player:** Hey!
  - **Doran the tool artisan:** How can I help?
  - (continue)
- Select an option
  - How does this event work?
    - **Player:** How does this event work?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Here's how it works. I have a bunch of recipes for all sorts of bits and bobs. But what I don't have is the stuff to make 'em. So that's where you come in. If you see a recipe that tickles your fancy and you gather up all the materials required then your old pal Doran will make it for you!
    - **Player:** That sounds great! But what do you get out of it?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Ah, the more stuff I make, the more experience I get. And as I gain more experience I'll be able to make even better stuff for you.
    - **Player:** Excellent!
    - (previous|initial-options)
  - What kind of experience are you getting?
    - **Player:** What kind of experience are you getting for this?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** That'd be artisan tool crafting experience.
    - **Player:** Artisan tool crafting? I've never heard of that skill before.
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** It's a real thing, honest! Look, if I was going to make up a fake skill I'd probably go for something ridiculous. Like, I don't know, sailing or something.
    - **Player:** Yeah, I guess you're right.
    - (previous|initial-options)
  - What is your proudest creation?
    - **Player:** What's your proudest creation?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Alright you know how sometimes you'll have a lot of stuff to carry. And sometimes, your arms aren't going to cut it?
    - **Player:** I do.
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Well, sometimes you'll need some sort of cart which can be dragged around with all of your stuff in it.
    - **Player:** Wow, are you telling me that you invented the cart!?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** No, no, no, stop getting ahead of yourself. Now, you know sometimes you want to make sure that the cart doesn't roll away so you have those little legs on the back?
    - **Player:** Oh those are genius! Did you create those?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** No! Let me finish my story.
    - **Player:** Sorry.
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Now on the bottom of those legs, there's a little scrap of material that discretely sits between the legs and the ground. Keeps it in place and it doesn't scuff the floor.
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** ... That's it, that's my proudest creation.
    - **Player:** ... Really?
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Yes! Are you not amazed?
    - **Player:** Oh, yeah! No! Totally! A real game changer in the transport industry I'm sure!
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** *grumble*
    - (previous|initial-options)
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye!
    - **Doran the tool artisan:** Take care.
    - (end)
