## Dialogue Transcript


### South of Keldagrim


#### Before Between a Rock...

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** What?
- **Player:** I said hello!
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Hello!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Why, I'm Dondakan, of course!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Why are you firing a cannon at a wall?
    - **Player:** Why are you firing a cannon at a wall?
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** I can't tell you!
    - **Player:** Why not?
    - (Condition: If the player has not completed Fishing Contest - this takes priority over not completing Dwarf Cannon:)
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** You're a human! This is a dwarven thing!
      - **Player:** Isn't there anything I can do that will make you tell me?
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Perhaps if you were to help some of my fellow dwarves...
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** I hear there is a fishing competition that a few of my distant kinsmen are keen on winning, for instance.
      - **Player:** A fishing competition? What are dwarves doing in a fishing competition?
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** They're not in it! They can't fish if their lives depended on it!
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** That's where you come in! Run along now!
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: If the player has completed Fishing Contest but not Dwarf Cannon:)
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Well... you are the Fishing Champion... but I still can't really speak about this.
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** The cannon is a top-secret dwarven project, you know.
      - **Player:** Doesn't look very secret to me, standing out there in the open.
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Being cheeky eh?
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Maybe you should speak to Lawgof, the Commander of the Black Guard in Kandarin.
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: If the player has completed both Fishing Contest and Dwarf Cannon:)
      - (see|Between a Rock...#An Unbreakable Rock)
  - [Choice]: Bit noisy here, isn't it?
    - **Player:** Bit noisy here, isn't it?
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Cozy? No, not really, it's damp and cold!
    - **Player:** No! I said it's noisy!
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Then stop shouting!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye!
    - **Player:** Goodbye!

#### After Between a Rock...

- **Player:** How are you doing, Dondakan?
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Oh, just fine, just fine! This gold is certainly creating a steady flow of income for me! After the company takes some of the cut, of cause.
- [Menu]: Select an Option{{anchor|normal}}
  - [Choice]: Can you shoot me into the rock again?
    - **Player:** Can you shoot me into the rock again?
    - (Condition: With golden helmet equipped:)
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Of course, just a second, let me adjust my cannon again.
    - (Condition: Without golden helmet equipped:)
      - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** You need to wear that golden helmet... unless you want to crack open your skull on the rock.
  - [Choice]: Are you selling those boots of yours?
    - **Player:** Are you selling those boots of yours?
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** What, you mean these granite boots?
    - **Player:** Yes, I'd be happy to pay quite some money for them!
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Haha, I bet you would, I bet you would!
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** But I no longer need the money, this rock is generating plenty! Besides, I'm rather fond of them now... best boots I ever had!
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Oh please...
        - **Player:** Oh please...
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** No, I won't sell them.
      - [Choice]: Pretty please?
        - **Player:** Pretty please?
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** No!
        - **Player:** Oh come on!!!
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Well...
        - **Player:** Yes?
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** How much gold would you be willing to pay for it? Not that I'm selling my boots, mind, I'm just curious.
        - {{tinput|Enter amount:}}
          - (Condition: If 0-999 is entered:)
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** What, are you trying to scam old Dondakan? You want me to sell these boots for a measly [number] coins?
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** I'm not selling them anyway.
          - (Condition: If 1,000-74,999 is entered:)
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** If I were selling my granite boots, which I'm clearly not, I would certainly not accept a mere [number] coins for them.
          - (Condition: If 75,000 or more is entered:)
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Haha, nice try Player!
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Pity I'm not selling my boots, not for any price or any item. I just want to see how high you would go!
            - **Player:** Bah, thanks a lot.
          - (Condition: If a figure greater than the amount in money pouch is entered:)
            - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Hey, that's quite a bit of money. Which you don't have on you. Oh well, I wasn't selling my boots anyway.
      - [Choice]: Never mind, I don't want them anymore.
        - **Player:** Never mind, I don't want them anymore.
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Great, because I don't want to sell them.
        - **Player:** Okay.
        - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Right.
  - [Choice]: That's great, but I have to go now.
    - **Player:** That's great, but I have to go now.
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Come back any time!

#### After Birthright of the Dwarves


##### If Veldaban is king

- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** I suppose I should be proud of how incorruptible my son is...but would it kill him to use his royal powers to give his dad's business a helping hand?
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Oh well...I suppose this rock is still providing a steady stream of income for me. After the company takes its cut, of course.
- (continuea|normal)

##### If Veldaban abdicated

- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** So my son got to be king, and then stepped down! It's like I've always said - the current generation lacks ambition!
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** His royal powers could have made us both so rich!
- (continuea|normal)

##### If Veldaban is dead

- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** I wish I'd spent more time with Veldaban when he was alive, rather than being obsessed with this rock. My only son, and I ignored him because he became a Black Guard rather than a miner.
- **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Since you helped me mine the rock, I've had all the riches I could want...but all the gold in the world won't bring my son back.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm sorry for your loss.
    - **Player:** I'm sorry for your loss.
    - **Dondakan the Dwarf:** Thanks.
  - [Choice]: Can you shoot me into the rock again?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Are you selling those boots of yours?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: That's great, but I have to go.
    - (above)
