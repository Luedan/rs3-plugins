## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Dwarf:** Welcome to the Mining Guild. Can I help you with anything?
  - What have you got in the guild?
    - **Dwarf:** Ooh, it's WONDERFUL! There are orichalcite, runite and coal rocks, all exclusively for people with at least level 60 mining.
    - *(If the player has not achieved level 60 Mining:)*
      - **Player:** So you won't let me go in there?
      - **Dwarf:** Sorry, but rules are rules. Go do some more training first. Can I help you with anything else?
    - *(If the player has achieved level 60 Mining:)*
      - **Player:** It's a good thing I have level X Mining.
      - **Dwarf:** Yes, that's amazing! Did you want anything else?
        - (previous)
  - What do you dwarves do with the ore you mine?
    - **Dwarf:** What do you think? We smelt it into bars, smith the metal to make armour and weapons, then we exchange them for goods and services.
    - **Player:** I don't see many dwarves selling armour or weapons here.
    - **Dwarf:** No, this is only a mining outpost. We dwarves don't much like to settle in human cities. Most of the ore is carted off to Keldagrim, the great dwarven city. They've got a special blast furnace up there - it makes smelting the ore so much easier. There are plenty of dwarven traders working in Keldagrim. Anyway, can I help you with anything else?
      - (previous)
  - Can you help me with the gemstone golem outfits?
    - **Player:** Can you help me with the gemstone golem outfits?
      - *If the player can't receive gemstone golem fragments and hasn't unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces:*
        - **Dwarf:** At level 70 you can start to collect fragments to make elite skilling outfits. When you get to that level you will automatically start collecting fragments. Then you can use them with a blueprint to make yourself any remaining elite outfit pieces for this skill.
      - *If the player can receive gemstone golem fragments but hasn't unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces: *
        - **Dwarf:** You are set up to receive fragments. When you get to level 80 you will be able to create outfit pieces.
      - *If the player can receive gemstone golem fragments and has unlocked the blueprint required to make the outfit pieces: *
        - **Dwarf:** You are set up to receive fragments. When you have 3600 fragments you can use them to create an elite outfit piece. Simply go to an Invention workbench to create the pieces.
      - *If the player already owns the magic golem outfit, or all of the outfit pieces:*
        - **Dwarf:** You have already created all of the possible outfit pieces. I will make sure you do not receive any further fragments.
        - **Dwarf:** Remember you can combine the base outfit pieces to make the high tier versions, to get the additional benefits.
  - No thanks, I'm fine.
    - **Player:** No thanks, I'm fine.
