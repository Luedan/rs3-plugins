##### At Nodon Front


##### Without cup of tea

- **Diana:** And what do you want, human? Are you here to bring me gifts worthy of my station?
- **Player:** What station?
- **Diana:** I am a cat. There is no higher position of status in all the realms.
- **Player:** Right.
- **Diana:** Well if you have no gift for me, begone from my sight.
- **Player:** Wait! I was hoping you'd come stay at the oasis and guard it from the dead.
- **Diana:** For free? No, human. That simply will not do. I demand tribute! Bring me a beverage. Warm, and with an aroma worthy of my nostrils.

##### With cup of tea

- **Diana:** What is that smell? That aroma...both soothing and compelling.
- **Player:** This? It's a cup of tea.
- **Diana:** I crave it! I desire it! Let me consume it's deliciousness!
- **Player:** Will you come and stay in the oasis if I do?
- **Diana:** Yes. Anything. Just give me the tea!
