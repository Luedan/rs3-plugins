## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Without a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet

- **Dealga:** Ook. Ook! Ook! Ah Ah! Ook Ah Uh!
- You cannot understand what he is saying but the monkey clearly wants to show you his wares and opens up his shop.
- (opens=Dealga's Scimitar Emporium)

#### With a monkeyspeak amulet or cramulet

- **Dealga:** Oooh, still not used to dealing with you humans...
- **Dealga:** Would you like to buy or sell some scimitars?
