# Dig Site workman

- **Letter**: `D`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Dig Site


#### Talk to option

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Dig Site workman:** Good day; what can I do for you?
- Select an option
  - What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Dig Site workman:** I am involved in various stages of the dig, from the initial investigation to the installation of the mine shafts.
    - **Player:** Oh, okay, thanks.
  - I'm not sure...
    - **Player:** I'm not sure...
    - **Dig Site workman:** Well, let me know when you are.
  - Can I dig around here?
    - **Player:** Can I dig around here?
    - **Dig Site workman:** You can only use a site you have the appropriate exam level for.
    - Select an option
      - Appropriate exam level?
        - **Player:** Appropriate exam level?
        - **Dig Site workman:** Yes, only persons with the correct Earth Sciences certificate can dig here. A level 1 certificate will let you dig in a level 1 site and so on.
        - **Player:** Ah, yes; I understand.
      - I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Player:** I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Dig Site workman:** Well that's nice for you. You can't dig around here without a certificate though.

#### Attempting to dig at the training grid without a trowel

- **Dig Site workman:** Hey, you'll need a trowel to dig in that training dig spot.
- **Player:** Oh, where can I get one of those?
- **Dig Site workman:** Talk to the examiners at the Exam Centre south of here. They can help you out.

#### Attempting to dig at a level 1 grid

- **Dig Site workman:** Hey, you can't dig here. You need to pass the level 1 Earth Sciences exam before you are allowed.
- **Player:** Oh, where can I do that?
- **Dig Site workman:** Talk to the examiners at the Exam Centre south of here. They can help you out.

#### Attempting to dig at a level 2 grid

- **Dig Site workman:** Sorry, you haven't passed the level 2 Earth Sciences exam; I can't let you dig here.
- **Player:** Oh, where can I do that?
- **Dig Site workman:** Talk to the examiners at the Exam Centre south of here. They can help you out.

#### Attempting to dig at a level 3 grid

- **Dig Site workman:** Sorry, you haven't passed the level 3 Earth Sciences exam. I can't let you dig here.
- **Player:** Oh, where can I do that?
- **Dig Site workman:** Talk to the examiners at the Exam Centre south of here. They can help you out.

### After The Dig Site

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Dig Site workman:** Ah, it's the great archaeologist! Congratulations on your discovery.
