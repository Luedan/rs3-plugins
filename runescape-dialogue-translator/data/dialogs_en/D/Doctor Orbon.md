## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue before Sheep Herder

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Doctor Orbon:** How do you feel? No heavy flu, shivers, nausea or loss of appetite?
- **Player:** Uh... no... I feel fine...
- **Doctor Orbon:** How about nightmares then? Are you experiencing any problems with especially scary nightmares?
- **Player:** No... not since I was young.
- **Doctor Orbon:** Good, good, I had to make sure. This plague really has an incredibly fast contagion rate. It spreads faster than the common cold!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: The plague? Tell me more.
    - **Player:** The plague? Tell me more.
    - **Doctor Orbon:** It came from the west somewhere. It is extremely quick spreading, and utterly deadly.
    - **Player:** What are the symptoms?
    - **Doctor Orbon:** It varies slightly from case to case, but usually abnormal nightmares and flu-like symptoms, progressing as you succumb more. Eventually, it results in the presence of a thick black tar-like substance secreted from the nose and eyes.
  - [Choice]: Okay, I'll be careful.
    - **Player:** Okay, I'll be careful.
    - **Doctor Orbon:** You do that adventurer. You do not want to become infected by the plague.
