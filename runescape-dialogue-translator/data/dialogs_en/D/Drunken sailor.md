# Drunken sailor

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Drunken sailor](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADrunken%20sailor)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- You shake the snoring sailor by the shoulder. It doesn't look like he's going to wake up any time soon.

### Search

- *If the player does not have the sailor's hat:*
  - *If the player does not have at least one free inventory space:*
    - You find the sailor's hat in one of his pockets. You put it in your inventory.
    - (receives=sailor's hat)
  - *If the player has at least one free inventory space:*
    - You find the sailor's hat in one of his pockets. You don't have room in your inventory for it.
- *If the player has the sailor's hat:*
  - You search the sailor's pockets but find nothing.
