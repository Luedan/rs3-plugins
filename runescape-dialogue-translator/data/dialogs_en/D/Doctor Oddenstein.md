# Doctor Oddenstein

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Doctor Oddenstein](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADoctor%20Oddenstein)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- Select an option
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - How does the Terrorbird Racing work?
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** These races are a part of the beach festivities this year. They're very interesting.
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** You can race once an hour and the idea is to get round all of the 8 gates in the quickest time possible.
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** The quicker you are the greater your reward will be at the end of the race.
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** As you go through each gate you'll be told where the next gate can be found.
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** Remember, you'll need to be wearing a Terrorbird mount to be able to take part. It adds to the fun!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - Where can I get a Terrorbird costume?
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** You can wear your own Terrorbird mount costume but you can also get one for free from this crate.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - How do the hi-scores work?
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** The top 10 quickest times are stored and can be seen by checking on any of the race gates.
      - **Doctor Oddenstein:** Hopefully you'll make the top 10!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Bye for now.
    - **Doctor Oddenstein:** Happy racing!
