## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Dig Site


#### East

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Dig Site winch operator:** Why, hello there! What can I do you for?
- Select an option
  - What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** Well, my job involves digging for finds, cleaning them and transporting them for identification.
    - **Player:** Sounds interesting.
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** I find it very interesting and very rewarding. So glad to see you're taking an interest in the site. Hope to see you out here digging sometime!
  - I'm not sure...
    - **Player:** I'm not sure.
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** Well, let me know when you are and I'll do my very best to help you!
  - Can I dig around here?
    - **Player:** Can I dig around here?
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** You can only use a site you have the appropriate exam level for.
    - Select an option
      - Appropriate exam level?
        - **Player:** Appropriate exam level?
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** Yes, you need to have been trained in the various techniques before you can be allowed to dig for artefacts.
        - **Player:** Ah, yes. I understand.
      - I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Player:** I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** Really? What qualifications do you have?
        - *(During the Dig Site quest, if the player has acquired the Level 1 Certificate)*
          - {{Transcript missing}}
        - *(During the Dig Site quest, if the player has acquired the Level 2 Certificate)*
          - **Player:** I have passed my Earth Sciences Level 2 Exam!
          - **Dig Site winch operator:**Congratulations, remember though that you can only use a site you have the appropriate exam level for.
        - *(During, the Dig Site quest, if the player has acquired the Level 3 Certificate)*
          - **Player:** I have passed all three Earth Sciences Level exams!
          - **Dig Site winch operator:**Well done! That means you can dig anywhere on this site, but you need to impress the examiner to use the winches.
          - **Player:**Okay!
        - **Player:** Erm... Kinda.. Sort of... None.
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** Well, the examiner at the Exam Centre can help you.
        - **Player:** Thanks, I'll talk to her.

#### West

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Dig Site winch operator:** 'Ello. What can I do for you?
- Select an option
  - What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** Me? I'm a workman. I get paid to shift dirt, essentially. It's not a very good job, but it pays the bills.
    - **Player:** Oh, okay, thanks.
  - I'm not sure...
    - **Player:** I'm not sure.
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** Well, let me know when you are. I can't be standing around yakkin' all day, I got work to do!
  - Can I dig around here?
    - **Player:** Can I dig around here?
    - **Dig Site winch operator:** You can only use a site you have the appropriate exam level for.
    - Select an option
      - Appropriate exam level?
        - **Player:** Appropriate exam level?
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** Yea, only people who have passed level 1 certificate can dig in a level 1 site, and so on and so forth.
        - **Player:** Ah, yes. I understand.
      - I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Player:** I am already skilled in digging.
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** Well, that's nice for you, but regulations say you can't dig around here without a certificate.
        - **Player:** Oh, right.
        - **Dig Site winch operator:** The examiner at Exam Centre can help you.
        - **Player:** Thanks, I'll talk to her.

#### Attempting to operate either winch

- There is a sign on the winch saying: 'Private area - invitation only.'
- **Dig Site winch operator:** Sorry, this area is private. The only way you'll get to use these is by impressing Terry, the archaeological expert over at the Exam Centre.
- **Dig Site winch operator:** Find something worthwhile and he might let you use the winches. Until then, get lost!
