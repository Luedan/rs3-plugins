# Deathshead moth

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Deathshead moth](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADeathshead%20moth)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawn

- A deathshead moth is drawn to your ritual.

### Catch

- You absorb some necrotic energy from the moth and it flutters away.
- ((Only on the first time:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'On Death's Head Be It' - Catch a deathshead moth.
