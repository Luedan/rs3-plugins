## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Without the report from Within the Light

- **Dwarf:** While I was clearing the rubble, I found a dead elf with a document on him. Would you like it?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - [The dwarf hands you a document.]
    - (receives=Report)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - (end)

#### With the report

- **Dwarf:** This elven architecture ain't like anything a dwarf would make. Now that I've cleared the rubble, I'm going to take a look at it before I go back to Keldagrim.
