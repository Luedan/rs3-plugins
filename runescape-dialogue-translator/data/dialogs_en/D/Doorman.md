## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing [[Icthlarin's Little Helper]]


#### Talking to one

- **Doorman:** Nobody may enter or leave Sophanem for any reason, so says the new High Priest of Icthlarin.

#### Attempting to exit through the eastern entrance

- **Doorman:** I cannot permit you to leave. The city is under quarantine due to the outbreak of plagues.
- **Player:** I am not afflicted by any plagues, so let me pass.
- **Doorman:** No!

### After completing Icthlarin's Little Helper

- **Doorman:** Good day, adventurer. I must thank you for protecting our High Priest. Word has it that it was quite the battle.
- **Player:** It was nothing, really. I'm quite good at that type of thing.
- **Doorman:** Don't let us keep you. I'm sure you've got plenty of other important questing to get on with.
