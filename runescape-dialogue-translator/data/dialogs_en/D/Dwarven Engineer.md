# Dwarven Engineer

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Dwarven Engineer](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADwarven%20Engineer)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Dwarven Engineer:** I wish you'd just leave me alone.
- **Player:** Why, what's wrong?
- **Dwarven Engineer:** I was a great engineer once. Still am actually. They just don't let me do the work I do best anymore.
- **Player:** What do you do then?
- **Dwarven Engineer:** I'm an assistant in this shop. I get all the lousy jobs too.
- **Player:** Well, I'm sorry to hear that.
