# Dedicated miner

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Dedicated miner](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADedicated%20miner)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When a nearby player doesn't have a mining & smithing flyer

**Dedicated miner:** color=yellow

### Talking to the dedicated miner

- **Player:** Wow, what is that you are wearing?
- *(Miner with an orikalkum pickaxe:)*
  - **Dedicated miner:** I'm wearing the orikalkum pickaxe. You will be able to smith it yourself in the upcoming Mining and Smithing rework. I'm also wearing the ruby golem outfit to further boost my mining capabilities.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- *(Miner with a pickaxe of earth and song:)*
  - **Dedicated miner:** I'm wearing the pickaxe of earth ang song. A fusion of elven and dwarven technology. You will be able to smith it yourself in the upcoming Mining and Smithing rework.{{Sic}} I'm also wearing the golden mining suit from the Lava flow mine in Keldagrim to further boost my mining capabilities.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **Player:** Oh wow! Do you have more information about the rework?
- *(If you have at least one inventory space:)*
  - {{Qact|You receive a mining & smithing flyer.}}
  - You're handed a flyer{{Sic}}
- *(If your inventory is full:)*
  - **Dedicated miner:** If you've got a free space, I can give you a flyer{{Sic}}
