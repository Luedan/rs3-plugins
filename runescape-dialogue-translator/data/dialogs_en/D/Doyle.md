## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before Guys and Dolls

- **Doyle:** Argh! I'm too s-s-scared. Leave me alone.

#### During Guys and Dolls

- (see|Guys and Dolls)

#### After completing Guys and Dolls

- **Player:** Hey, Doyle. So, how does this pumpkin order thing work?
- (Condition: Without harvest list:)
  - (Condition: Without free backpack space:)
    - **Doyle:** If you're here to help out, please make some space and I'll give you Dolly's latest order list!
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: With free backpack space:)
    - (receives=harvest list)
    - **Doyle:** Hey, if you're here to help out, here's Dolly's latest order for you to fill!
    - (continue)
- **Doyle:** All you've gotta do is harvest pumpkins from the patches in front of me and deposit them in the harvest cart right here.
- **Doyle:** You can hand in parts of the order as you go or wait until you've harvested everything in the order.
- **Doyle:** You'll get a nice bit of experience when the order is completed, and a bonus if you do it all in one go!
- **Player:** Are those crows still hassling you?
- **Doyle:** They are - every time a pumpkin gets harvested, there they swoop in looking for seeds. Their eyes are bigger than their bellies!
- **Doyle:** You can shoo them away easy enough, but it's hard work one at a time.
- **Doyle:** I can scare them all at once, but every time I do I'll need a minute to recover.
- **Doyle:** However, once a crow has been scared away from a patch a new pumpkin will grow in it. So it's gotta be done.
- **Player:** Sounds simple enough - harvest pumpkins, shoo crows away, and fill Dolly's order by putting pumpkins in the harvest cart.
- **Player:** Anything else I should know?
- **Doyle:** Oh, yeah! There's a compost bin in the corner opposite the harvest cart. You can trash any pumpkins you don't want in there. Might help for making room for different ones, or just when you've finished for the day.
- **Doyle:** Also, you can get random Halloween stuff while harvesting, but you'll get the experience mostly from completing orders.
- (Condition: With "Talk to" as the default option:)
  - [You can also configure Doyle's left click option to make it easier to scare all crows off.]
