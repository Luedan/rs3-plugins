# Dromund's cat

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Dromund's cat](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADromund%27s%20cat)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to without a Catspeak amulet


#### Before The Giant Dwarf

- **Dromund's cat:** Miaow.

#### After The Giant Dwarf

- **Dromund's cat:** Meow!

### When spoken to with a Catspeak amulet


#### Before The Giant Dwarf

- **Dromund's cat:** What do you want?
- **Player:** I didn't know dwarves kept cats.
- **Dromund's cat:** Why wouldn't they? A friend of mine lives at the palace, actually. Well, I say friend - used to be a friend.
- **Player:** What happened?
- **Dromund's cat:** He got adopted by Hreidmar, the director of the Red Axe.
- **Player:** Oh?
- **Dromund's cat:** I don't like the Red Axe! They stomp around everywhere. You've got to be careful of your tail with those people around.

#### After The Giant Dwarf

- **Dromund's cat:** I know what you did, Player!
- **Player:** What did I do?
- **Dromund's cat:** You stole a boot from my master.
- **Player:** All for a greater good.
- **Dromund's cat:** Hiss.
