### Het's Oasis


#### Talk to

- ((Only on first time:))
  - *(Before Back to the Freezer:)*

    - **Player:** Y-yeah... It's just that - well... You're by far the tallest gnome I've ever met.
    - **Dundee:** Hah! Suppose I would be. Kinda refreshing to have someone just come out and say it. I wasn't always the strapping fella you see before you, but that's a story for another time.
    - (continue)
  - *(After Back to the Freezer:)*
    - **Player:** Hey! It's the unusually tall gnome who gave me the crocspeak amulet!
    - **Dundee:** Hey! It's the average-sized human with fanciful notions about penguins!
    - (continue)
- ((Only after the first time:))
  - **Dundee:** Hi, Player!
  - (continue)
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Dundee:** Well, I was hanging out with my pal Chito here near my camp down south, when we felt this huge earthquake!
    - **Dundee:** At least, I think it was an earthquake...
    - **Dundee:** So we came up here to check it out! Chito seems to like it here, with plenty of scarabs to feast on, and just the perfect size pool to have a swim in.
    - (previous)
  - What can I do here?
    - **Player:** So, what is there to do around here?
    - **Dundee:** Where to start... First off, you see those scarabs infesting the waters? Ol' Chito here can help you out with hunting them down. So can his cousin Frito on the other side of the oasis.
    - **Dundee:** Despite appearances, they're really just big softies. They'll be happy to catch them for you.
    - **Dundee:** You can also gather some unique flowers from various plants you may have seen dotted about the area.
    - **Dundee:** I've done some experiments with them myself, and they seem to each have certain properties that attract different scarabs...
    - **Dundee:** Oh, and there's also a bunch of damaged walls and rubble you can probably use to train your agility. Not my cup of tea, but if that takes your fancy...
    - **Dundee:** Maybe stay off those statues though, they look quite fragile and of historical importance...
    - **Player:** Thanks!
    - (previous)
  - Tell me about yourself.
    - **SELECT AN OPTION**
      - Where are you from?
        - **Player:** Where are you from?
        - **Dundee:** The Tree Gnome Stronghold! It's a lovely little city. I do miss it sometimes.
        - **Player:** Do you ever go back to visit?
        - **Dundee:** Not often, no. It's such a long trip from here, and I have to leave poor Chito by himself, so I rarely visit.
        - **Player:** Oh.
        - **Dundee:** I try to keep in touch with my family by mail. Fortunately, the local postcat delivers right to my tent doorstep!
        - **Dundee:** Sometimes they send me homemade food - by express delivery so they don't go bad, of course.
        - **Dundee:** My favourites are worm holes and spicy crunchies. Can't go wrong with them!
        - **Player:** Except maybe the worms...
        - **Dundee:** I'm sure you'd change your mind if you tried them fresh out the oven.
        - **Player:** Somehow, I'm not convinced.
        - **Dundee:** Suit yourself. More for me!
        - (previous)
      - How did you get to be so tall, anyway?
        - **Player:** If I may ask... How did you get to be so tall? I've never met a gnome anywhere near your height.
          - *(Before Back to the Freezer:)*
            - **Dundee:** Well, I've always eaten my veggies, and got plenty of exercise.
            - **Player:** And that made you twice the height of any gnome in recorded history?
            - **Dundee:** Alright, you've rumbled me. But it's not something I'll tell just anyone. You never know who's listening.
            - **Dundee:** Come back when the heat's off. Then we'll talk.
            - **Player:** O...kay?
            - (previous)
          - *(After Back to the Freezer:)*
            - **Dundee:** Can't lie - you did us all a favour, dealing with those suspicious penguins. I guess I can let you in on the truth.
            - **Dundee:** It was a dark stormy night...
            - **Dundee:** Just kidding. It was probably a mediocre sunny day, I don't really remember.
            - **Dundee:** Anyway, it all starts when I was but mere a gnome child.
            - **Dundee:** I was outside playing with the terrorbirds in their pen. Then suddenly, they start running around screaming! So, I look around to see what's causing it...
            - **Dundee:** And there it was. The most beautiful creature I'd ever seen in my 5 years of life. A scaly, slimy yet rough, muddy green, giant reptile.
            - **Dundee:** It was fascinating. I began to walk up to it, outstretching my tiny arms just to give it a pet...
            - **Dundee:** Unfortunately my enjoyment was cut short. My father hastily grabbed me and shooed away the poor croc.
            - **Player:** Why on earth would he do that? I'm sure a child is perfectly safe alone with a crocodile.
            - **Dundee:** Exactly!
            - **Dundee:** So, that's where my interest in crocodiles began.
            - **Dundee:** I had to learn more about these curious beasts. But, being sheltered away in the Tree Gnome Stronghold there isn't much opportunity to study them close up.
            - **Dundee:** I put it to the back of my mind for many years...
            - **Dundee:** Until one day, when I went with my grandparents to Ardougne Zoo.
            - **Dundee:** And there it was. Peeping its head out from the water. This glorious being.
            - **Dundee:** I knew, at this moment, that this is what I was born to do. I was born to study crocodiles.
            - **Player:** To each their own, I guess.
            - **Dundee:** Once I got back home, I packed my things and went on my way.
            - **Dundee:** There was just one problem. My size. One gulp and they could swallow me whole!
            - **Dundee:** As much as I trusted them, I didn't want to take that risk.
            - **Dundee:** I asked around for advice. Anyone who would listen - musicians, bartenders, bank tellers, you name it.
            - **Dundee:** Until one day, I heard about this gigantic mole. Apparently, this mole wasn't always this big - they only grew to this humongous size after eating a plant covered in this special potion!
            - **Dundee:** So of course, I had to find out more about this potion. Turns out it's owned by this gardener in Falador.
            - **Dundee:** To Falador I went!
            - **Dundee:** Prepared with all  my life savings, I went and asked him for just a mere drop. A drop! But he laughed in my face. Said it's priceless.
            - **Dundee:** I didn't need him anyway! I had a munch on one of his precious plants when he wasn't looking.
            - **Player:** I'm not sure that's entirely legal...
            - **Dundee:** Never underestimate a gnome with a dream, Player.
            - **Dundee:** Anyway, within a few hours, I was almost triple the size! Had to get a whole new wardrobe and everything.
            - **Dundee:** You pretty much know the rest. I travelled to the desert and set up camp right by the crocodiles. I'm so big that they don't seem to mind.
            - **Player:** Truly inspirational! You should write a book.
            - **Dundee:** Perhaps one day!
            - (previous)
