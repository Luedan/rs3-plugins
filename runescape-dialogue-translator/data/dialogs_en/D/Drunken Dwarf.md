## Dialogue Transcript


### Wandering

- (Condition: Before Forgettable Tale of a Drunken Dwarf:)
  - **Drunken Dwarf:** Nice to meet you, matey!<br>'Ere, have some a' the good stuff!
- (Condition: After Forgettable Tale of a Drunken Dwarf:)
  - **Drunken Dwarf:** Nice to see you again, matey!<br>I remember your kebab stories!<br>'Ere, have some a' the good stuff!
- (receives=kebab and beer)
- **Player:** Thanks!
- **Drunken Dwarf:** Have a nice day, matey!

### King Drunken Dwarf III

- **King Drunken Dwarf III:** Wahay, matey!
  - **Player:** *You're* the rightful king?
    - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** That's what whatsername says! Could have been anyone...*hic*...turns out it was me!
  - **Player:** You were crowned 'King Drunken Dwarf'?
    - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** Well whatsername used my name, I think, but I got totally sloshed at the post-coronation party and can't remember it. So I've asked everyone to call me 'King Drunken Dwarf'!
  - **Player:** I could restore your memory using the memory wand.
    - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** The memory what? Oh yes! The thingy you used on the ogre. Whatsername told me about it.
    - (Condition: If you do not presently have the wand:)
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** You'd have to go get it first, though, matey!
    - (Condition: If you do have the wand:)
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** Sure, why not!
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** ...
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** Well, have you used it yet? Any time you're ready.
      - **Player:** I did use it. Didn't anything happen?
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** No, mate! Not that I remember!
      - **King Drunken Dwarf III:** I guess this means no one magically tampered with my memories. If I can't remember things, it's just because I'm a bit drunk!
  - **Player:** Goodbye.
