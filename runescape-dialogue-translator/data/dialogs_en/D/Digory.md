## Dialogue Transcript


### Before A Shadow over Ashdale

- **Digory:** Hello there!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Digory:** Digory Popplewell, at your service! I must say you're looking very well, my friend - very well indeed!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: You seem very happy.
    - **Digory:** Yes - and why not? The sun is shining, the air is sweet, and the sea is as blue as a maiden's eyes. A fine time to be alive, wouldn't you say? Haha!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are you doing?
    - **Digory:** What am I doing? Not very much! Doing things is bad for your health, you know. That's why I make it a policy to do as little as possible!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Why is your friend so grumpy?
    - **Digory:** Oh, pay no attention to old Ned. I know he can be a bit prickly, but he's not a bad fellow, really - he just gets a little riled up sometimes.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Digory:** Goodbye, my friend!
