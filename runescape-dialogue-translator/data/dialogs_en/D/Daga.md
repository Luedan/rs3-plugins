## Dialogue Transcript


### In Marim, on Ape Atoll


#### While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Daga:** Ook! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ook! Ah!
- You cannot understand what he is saying.

#### While wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Daga:** Would you like to buy or sell some scimitars?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes, please.
    - (opens=Daga's Scimitar Smithy)
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
  - [Choice]: Do you have any Dragon Scimitars in stock?
    - **Player:** Do you have any Dragon Scimitars in stock?
    - (Condition: If the player has not yet completed Monkey Madness:)
      - **Daga:** Sorry but I'm all out of stock at the moment, though I'm expecting another delivery soon.
    - (Condition: If the player has completed Monkey Madness:)
      - **Daga:** It just so happens I recently got a fresh delivery, do you want to buy one?
      - [Menu]: Select an Option
        - [Choice]: Yes, please.
          - **Player:** Yes, please.
          - (opens=Daga's Scimitar Smithy)
        - [Choice]: No, thanks.
          - **Player:** No, thanks.
