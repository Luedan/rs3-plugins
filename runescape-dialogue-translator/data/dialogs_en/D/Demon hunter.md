## Dialogue Transcript


### Give butterflies


#### Without butterflies

- You don't have anything to hand in.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You don't have anything to hand in.

#### With butterflies

- (removes=butterflies)
- You hand in: [number] x Butterfly.

### Talk to

- **Demon hunter:** 'Get the butterfly wings', they said. 'No, you're not too short,' they said. Idiots! I can't reach them in the air!
- **Demon hunter:** You're bigger than me. Grab all the butterflies you can, before they fly away. Some bite, though, so watch your hands.

### 30 seconds before the event ends

- {{Qact|The butterflies disappear.}}
- **Demon hunter:** {{RSChat|Give me the resources now!}}
- {{Colour: Give me the resources now!}}

### Completing the event successfully

- For completing this event you receive: 1 x Sack of wild rewards.
