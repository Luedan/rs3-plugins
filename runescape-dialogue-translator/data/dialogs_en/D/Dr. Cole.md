## Dialogue Transcript


### Initial dialogue

- **Dr. Cole** Welcome to the Brassica digsite!
- **Player** This doesn't look much like a digsite.
- **Player** In fact, I'm fairly certain that this is exactly the same cabbage patch that has always been here.
- **Player** Except now, for some reason... there is a giant cabbage?
- **Player** Also, why am I a chef...?
- **Dr. Cole** Cole's Law!
- **Player** Yes please, unless it has too much cabbage in it.
- **Dr. Cole** No, you don't understand!
- **Dr. Cole** Cole's...
- **Dr. Cole** Law!
- **Player** Ohhhh.... It's just it sounds a lot like...
- **Player** Nevermind.
- **Player** So what exactly is Cole's Law?
- **Dr. Cole** Well, let me explain...
- ((Screen fades out and back in.))
- Several hours later...
- **Dr. Cole** That's it! Simple, isn't it?
- **Player** Sure...
- **Player** So you want me to bring you Cabbage bosons...? That for some reason I can get by killing a giant cabbage?
- **Dr. Cole** Spot on! Together we can prove the existance of the widely speculated quantum cabbage field.
- **Player** Yes.
- **Player** What's in it for me...?
- **Dr. Cole** Oh, yes! I almost forgot!
- **Dr. Cole** Each time you bring me a Cabbage boson, I'll offer you a reward.
- **Player** Great! I'll be right back with one of those bosons in no time.
