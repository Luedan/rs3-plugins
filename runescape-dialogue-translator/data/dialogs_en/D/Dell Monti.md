# Dell Monti

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Dell Monti](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADell%20Monti)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Dell Monti:** Avast ye, landlubber! Give us yer pineapples!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Dell Monti:** I be Dell. A pineappley pirate paragon without a parrot.
    - **Player:** That's just odd.
    - **Dell Monti:** Arr.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: *(If the player has completed the Easy Karamja achievements:)* Got any pineapples for me?
    - **Player:** Got any pineapples for me?
    - **Dell Monti:** Arrr, you've earned 'em.
    - (receives= forty pineapples)
    - *(If the player has already claimed for the day:)*
      - **Dell Monti:** Now yer just bein' greedy. Come back tomorra'.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: *(If the player has completed the Elite Karamja achievements)* Got any apples for me?
    - **Player:** Got any apples for me?
    - **Dell Monti:** Arrr, you've earned 'em.
    - (receives= forty cooking apples)
    - *(If the player has already claimed for the day:)*
      - **Dell Monti:** Now yer just bein' greedy. Come back tomorra'.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What does a pirate have to do with pineapples?
    - **Player:** What does a pirate have to do with pineapples?
    - **Dell Monti:** It all started aboard ship. We was leagues fr'm land and becalmed. Arr, then tha thirst came.
    - **Player:** For rum?
    - **Dell Monti:** Nae, not tha rum. We had plenty o' that.
    - **Player:** For water?
    - **Dell Monti:** Yeuch! Yer suggestin' I poisons meself?
    - **Player:** Well, what were yer...I mean, you, thirsty for?
    - **Dell Monti:** Pineapples!
    - **Player:** Pineapples?
    - **Dell Monti:** Pineapples! Lovely, juicy pineapples.
    - **Player:** Pineapples?
    - **Dell Monti:** Yer startin' ta sound like me parrot, mate.
    - **Player:** Sorry, it's just a bit odd.
    - **Dell Monti:** Arr, 'tis that. So much so tha' me parrot flew off and tha crew gave me tha nickname of Pineapple Pete.
    - **Player:** That's funny!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Where's your parrot?
    - **Player:** Where's your parrot?
    - **Dell Monti:** He flew off when the pineapple thirst hit. Haven't seen 'im since.
    - **Player:** That's sad.
    - **Dell Monti:** Arr, 'tis. But I have me pineapples ta keep me company, an' Parrot Pete only used ta say one thing.
    - **Player:** Oh? What was that?
    - **Dell Monti:** Was somethin' about...pickin' somethin up.
    - **Dell Monti:** Arrr, tha's it, he used ta parrot it all day: 'Pick up a penguin.' Thing is, he'd never seen a penguin, an' neither 'ave I.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Player:** Never mind.

### Claim option

- ''(If the player has not completed any of the Karamja achievements:)
  - **Dell Monti:** Arrr, I have nothing for ye. My fruit will only go wi' a proven ally o' Karamja.
- ''(If the player has completed the Easy Karamja achievements:)
  - [Dell hands you 140 pineapples.]
  - (receives= one hundred and forty pineapples)
  - (continue)
- ''(If the player has completed the Elite Karamja achievements:)
  - [Dell hands you 140 apples and 140 pineapples.]
  - (receives= one hundred and forty cooking apples and one hundred and forty pineapples)
  - (continue)
- **Player:** Thanks!

#### If you have already claimed

- **Dell Monti:** Arrr, don't be greedy. Ye've already had all yer fruit for the week. Come back in [X] days/tomorra'.
