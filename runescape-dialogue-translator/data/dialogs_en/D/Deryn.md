## Dialogue Transcript


### After Housing of Parliament

- **Deryn:** Hello, love!
- **Player:** Hi Deryn!
- **Deryn:** What can I help you with today?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Talk about Deryn.
    - **Player:** So, what made you want to take care of the owls here in Um?
    - **Deryn:** Well, back in my life, I was in charge of looking after the terrorbirds in the Tree Gnome Stronghold.
    - **Deryn:** When my time had come to part with the living world, I realised I wanted to have a bit of change in death.
    - **Player:** One lifetime of terrorbirds is enough for anyone, I'd imagine.
    - **Deryn:** Now, don't get me wrong, terrorbirds are lovely creatures!
    - **Deryn:** I truly enjoyed spending my life with them, but there was a whole variety of jobs out there that I was yet to try.
    - **Player:** So... you became a keeper for a different bird instead?
    - **Deryn:** It's not what I was expecting, either! But once I arrived in Um, and I found that the poor owls had no one to look after them, I couldn't say no!
    - **Player:** Do they need anyone to look after them? They've been surviving for quite some time before you came along.
    - **Deryn:** I'm sure they were surviving just fine, but, as with anyone, the dears needed a bit of love and attention. So I was more than happy to step up.
    - **Player:** I guess it was meant to be.
    - **Deryn:** I guess so!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Talk about owl tracking.
    - **Player:** Can you tell me about owl tracking?
    - (Condition: Without having tracked all 14 owls:)
      - **Deryn:** Of course, love. Now that we've fixed up the owlery, many of the owls will be returning back to Um.
      - **Deryn:** If you come across any, I would appreciate if you could just track them for me.
      - **Deryn:** I don't imagine they'll all be back at once, though, so make sure to keep checking in as you grow your necromancer skills!
      - **Player:** No problem. How many owls have I tracked so far?
      - (Condition: With no owls or 2–13 owls tracked:)
        - **Deryn:** You've tracked [number] owls for me at the moment. I don't think that's all of them, though, so keep on going!
        - (initial)
      - (Condition: With 1 owl tracked:)
        - **Deryn:** You've tracked 1 owl for me at the moment. I don't think that's all of them, though, so keep on going!
        - (initial)
    - (Condition: Having tracked all 14 owls:)
      - **Deryn:** You seem to have tracked them all down for me, dear! It's lovely to have them all back in Um once again.
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Bye for now.
    - **Deryn:** Farewell, dear!
