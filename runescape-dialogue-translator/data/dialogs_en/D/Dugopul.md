# Dugopul

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Dugopul](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ADugopul)
- **Letter**: `D`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *If the player is not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet:*
  - **Dugopul:** Ook! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ah Ook! Ook.
- *If the player is wearing a monkeyspeak amulet:*
  - **Dugopul:** Not many monkeys come down here...
  - **Player:** That's quite understandable really.
  - **Dugopul:** I don't really understand it at all. I've spent my life here.
