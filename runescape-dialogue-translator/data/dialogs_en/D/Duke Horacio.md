## Dialogue Transcript


### In Lumbridge Castle

- **Duke Horacio:** Greetings. Welcome to my castle.
- (Condition: If the player is speaking to him for the first time:)
  - {{RSFontAchievement|Hail to the Duke, Baby|Speak to Horacio, the Duke of Lumbridge.}}
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: cond=After Dragon Slayer:|I seek a shield that will protect me from dragonbreath.
    - (Condition: Without an anti-dragon shield in backpack or in bank:)
      - **Duke Horacio:** A knight going on a dragon quest, hmm? What dragon do you intend to slay?
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: With an anti-dragon shield in backpack or in bank:)
      - **Duke Horacio:** What, another one? You adventurers do like to stockpile, don't you... any particular dragon in mind?
      - (continue)
    - **Duke Horacio:** Of course. Now you've slain Elvarg, you've earned the right to it!
    - (Condition: Without at least one free backpack space:)
      - **Duke Horacio:** You don't appear to have enough free space to take the shield, at the moment.
    - (Condition: With at least one free backpack space:)
      - (receives=anti-dragon shield)
      - [The Duke hands you the shield.]
  - [Choice]: Have you any quests for me?
    - **Duke Horacio:** No, all is well.
  - [Choice]: Where can I find money?
    - **Duke Horacio:** I hear many of the local people earn money by learning a skill. Many people get by in life by becoming accomplished smiths, cooks, miners and woodcutters.
  - [Choice]: cond=After partial completion of You Are It:| What can you tell me about Uri?
    - **Duke Horacio:** Hm? Uri was the undertaker that helped organise the farewell of my dear Oriana.
    - **Duke Horacio:** He was very accommodating; I admit in my grief I asked a lot of him but he never complained.
    - **Duke Horacio:** Perhaps the kindest gesture was that he allowed me to organise a casket of Oriana's belongings to be placed with her.
    - **Duke Horacio:** One of those things being the letters we exchanged when we first met. Oh, Ori, I miss you so.
