## Dialogue Transcript


### Catching a Devotion Sprite


#### During the event


##### For the first time in a given day


##### Without space in backpack for a lovely gift box

- You could snatch a treat from the sprite, if you had space in your backpack.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You could snatch a treat from the sprite, if you had space in your backpack.

##### With space in backpack for a lovely gift box

- (receives=a lovely gift box)
- The sprite slips from your grasp, but you manage to grab a Lovely gift box! It has been added to your inventory.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The sprite slips from your grasp, but you manage to grab a Lovely gift box! It has been added to your inventory.

##### Subsequent attempts

- The sprite is too layered in chocolate to try and catch again today. Try again tomorrow.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The sprite is too layered in chocolate to try and catch again today. Try again tomorrow.

#### After the event

- The sprite no longer has treats available. They're just having a nice float around.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The sprite no longer has treats available. They're just having a nice float around.
