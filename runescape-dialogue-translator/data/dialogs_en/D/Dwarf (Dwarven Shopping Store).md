## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Dwarven Mine

- **Dwarf:** Can I help you at all?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=Dwarven Shopping Store)
  - [Choice]: How should I use your shop?
    - **Dwarf:** I'm glad you ask! You can buy as many of the items stocked as you wish. You can also sell most items to the shop.
  - [Choice]: No, thanks.
    - (end)
