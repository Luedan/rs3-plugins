### After the Bird and the Beast world event

- *Who are you?*
  - **Player:** Who are you?
  - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:** I Murknose, big strong goblin. But - even I not as strong as Big High War God.
  - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:** So I tell you about Big High War God. I am his emi... mish... I tell you all about him.
- *Can you tell me about the Big High War God?*
  - **Player:** Can you tell me about the Big High War God?
  - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  What do you want to know about Big High War God?
  - *Who is Big High War God?*
    - **Player:** Who is Big High War God?
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Bandos is Big High War God. Bandos command us to fight, and we fight! Bandos big, and Bandos strong. Very strong. Bandos is strongest god.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  But Bandos fought Armadyl - bird god - and Armadyl recruited many, many people to do fighting for him.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Army of Bandos fought well! Fought hard. Lost.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  So Bandos dead now. But he still right! Strongest always win. Armadyl's army was stronger. Proof what Bandos think right, even though he die of it.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  So join us and be strong! Be strongest and fiercest and best at fighting! Be top of the pile.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Everyone else get crushed.
  - *Why should I follow Big High War God?*
    - **Player:** Why should I follow Big High War God?
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Because strongest will always rule the world! Strongest commands and weaker take orders. If you strong, you follow path of Big High War God.
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Following Bandos make goblins strong. Following way of strength and bigness work for you too.
  - *What does the path of the Big High War God say?*
    - **Player:** What does the path of the Big High War God say?
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  Big High War God want war! Big High War God want to fight! The strongest must rule, and you can be strong too!
    - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  That what Bandos wanted. That what strongest want now. That maybe what you want too.
- *I want to declare my support for the Big High War God!*
  - **Murknose, Emissary of Bandos:**  You clever! Big High War God strongest god. You take banner, show you support strongest god. I give you Book of the Gods. You like reading, right? Weird. Anyway, you read stories about gods in that book. Ha, but book not complete. You complete some jobs, I give you more pages.
