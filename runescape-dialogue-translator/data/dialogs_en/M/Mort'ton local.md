## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Using Serum 207 on them

- **Mort'ton local:** Oh...thank you for using this serum on me. Here, have something for your trouble.
- *The local gives you some [item].*

#### Using Serum 208 on them

- **Mort'ton local:** Oh...thank you, you used the permanent serum on me. Here, have something for your trouble.
- *The local gives you some [item].*

#### Talking to them again

- **Mort'ton local:** Oh, I feel much better for that super serum. I really hate it when I'm afflicted!
