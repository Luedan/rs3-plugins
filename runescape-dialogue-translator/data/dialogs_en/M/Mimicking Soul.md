# Mimicking Soul

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mimicking Soul](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMimicking%20Soul)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawning

- A mimicking soul appears nearby. Corner them before they get away.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|A mimicking soul appears nearby. Corner them before they get away.|colour=#1EFF00}}

### Reaching the Mimicking Soul

- {{Qact|The Mimicking Soul changes to a variant that can be guided.}}
- As you corner the mimicking soul, they revert to their true form.

### Failing to reach in 30 seconds

- {{Qact|The Mimicking Soul disappears.}}
- The mimicking soul disappears before you can catch it.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|The mimicking soul disappears before you can catch it.}}

### Guide

- {{Qact|The Mimicking Soul disappears.}}
- As you guide the mimicking soul to the underworld, they thank you by sharing some of their [Skill] knowledge.
- You gain [number] [Skill] XP.

### Failing to guide in 30 seconds

- {{Qact|The Mimicking Soul disappears.}}
- The lost soul disappears before you could guide it onwards.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|The lost soul disappears before you could guide it onwards.}}
