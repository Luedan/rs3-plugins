## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to during Agents of Fury

- *(If there are Zamorakian emblems to hand in:)*
  - *(Dialogue from handing in Zamorakian emblems occurs, then continues below.)*
- **Miithrius:** Welcome, human.
- **Player:** I hear you're requesting assistance, demon.
- **Zymthara:** This is correct. We work for Nymora, one half of the deadly Twin Furies. And while she's currently... pre-ocuppied, we've been tasked with collecting emblems belonging to those who have crossed Zamorak.
- **Player:** So you're asking me to kill?
- **Miithrius:** Oh, we've never been the ripping, murdering type ourselves. We're sure you'll find plenty of the emblems we seek on your travels, once you start looking properly.
- **Zymthara:** Or, of course, kill to your heart's content. Whichever works for you.
- **Player:** Sounds simple enough.
- **Miithrius:** Is there anything else you'd like to ask?
  - Why can't Nymora do this herself?
    - **Player:** Why can't Nymora do this herself?
    - **Zymthara:** Battling the generals of several other gods must be time consuming.
    - **Player:** Can't she leave her sister to fight for a while?
    - **Miithrius:** Clearly you don't appreciate the pure force of the sisters when together...
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - I take it the Furies' Chosen is busy too?
    - **Player:** I take it the Furies' Chosen is busy too?
    - **Zymthara:** Ha, him? He's merely a representative! And a human at that!
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Zymthara:** I mean, of course, he is simply a human. YOU are clearly much, much more. [Beautiful/Handsome], too.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - **Player:** How long will you be around for?
    - **Miithrius:** Nymora requires us to do many things - we won't be around for long. Be sure to bring us the emblems of these traitorous demons quickly.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - GREGOROVIC!
    - **Player:** GREGOROVIC!
    - **Zymthara:** WHERE? DEATH TO THE FACELESS ONE!
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Player:** So much for being the calm type.
    - **Miithrius:** You're testing our patience, human.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - For a demon, why do you both appear so... human?
    - **Player:** For a demon, why do you both appear so... human?
    - **Miithrius:** Oh this? Well, since many of the demons from Infernus find the human look utterly hideous, it helps strike fear into the hearts of those we chase down, so chose to adorn ourselves in such a way.
    - **Player:** Demons think we're ugly?!
    - **Zymthara:** Oh no, not all of them. However, the ones like me do only use the good looking parts, now you mention it...
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*

### Handing in Zamorakian emblems


#### Without emblems

- You do not have anything to hand in.

#### With emblems

- Handing in event currency will increase your progress for this event unlocking unique rewards. This will remove currency up to the amount that is required to complete the event.
- Do you wish to continue?
  - No
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
  - Yes
    - You hand in [X] Zamorakian Emblems. You need to hand in [Y] more to complete the event.
    - *(Dialogue from claiming rewards occurs.)*

#### With all rewards claimed

- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.

### Claiming rewards


#### Without rewards to claim and none claimed

- **Miithrius:** You'll need to help me some more before I can offer you any rewards.

#### With rewards to claim

- **Miithrius:** There you are!
- *(If a customisation is unlocked:)*
  - Congratulations, you have unlocked the [reward].
  - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If mystery boxes are obtained:)*
  - *(If the inventory is full:)*
    - You do not have enough inventory space for some of the rewards. Please make some space and try again.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - Congratulations, you have been awarded two [event mystery boxes/big event mystery boxes].
    - *(Continues below.)*
- You have unlocked [X]/10 of the rewards for the 'Agents of Fury' event.

#### Without rewards to claim and some claimed

- **Miithrius:** I require more offerings before I can reward you.
- You have unlocked [X]/10 of the rewards for the 'Agents of Fury' event.

#### With all rewards claimed

- **Miithrius:** You've already claimed all of the rewards I have to offer.
- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.
