## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Magda:** Greetings, child. What brings you to my humble home?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Magda:** Magda is my name - Magda the mystic, as some call me. I see things others cannot.
    - **Magda:** And I see much potential in you, my child.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - **Magda:** This ruin has been my home for many years. <br>No doubt it seems basic to you, but it serves me well, and no-one seems to mind my living here.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about Ashdale?
    - **Magda:** Ashdale was built over 400 years ago, which is a small span compared with some of Gielinor's more ancient settlements.
    - **Magda:** So far it has played no part in the conflicts of mortals and gods, but I suspect that may be about to change.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about Gielinor?
        - **Magda:** Gielinor is a large place, my child, and I know little of what lies east of Morytania or west of Prifddinas, but I will tell you what I can.
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about eastern Gielinor?
            - **Magda:** The east of Gielinor is home to the desert lands, Morytania, and Daemonheim.
            - **Magda:** The desert lands contain the ruins of ancient civilisations and strange beasts found nowhere else in Gielinor.
            - **Magda:** Daemonheim is the site of an ancient fortress, beneath which lurk all manner of foul creatures.
            - **Magda:** As for Morytania, it is a dark and dangerous place, and many who venture there do not return.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about western Gielinor?
            - **Magda:** The west of Gielinor is probably the most diverse part of the mainland, home to elves, gnomes, dwarves, and barbarians, among others.
            - **Magda:** Unfortunately, these races do not always get along, and the west is often a place of conflict.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about central Gielinor?
            - **Magda:** Central Gielinor contains the great cities of Falador and Varrock, and a vast desolation known as the Wilderness.
            - **Magda:** The civilised lands are mostly safe, and contain many opportunities for adventure, but the Wilderness can be a dangerous place.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: I'd like to know more about the gods.
            - (below)
          - [Choice]: I'd like to ask something else.
            - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Can you tell me more about the gods?
        - **Magda:** There are a great many gods, but Saradomin, Zamorak and Guthix are perhaps the most notable.
        - [Menu]: Select an Option
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about Saradomin?
            - **Magda:** Saradomin represents order and wisdom. Most of those who live here worship him, and the church that stands to the east was built in his name.
            - **Magda:** When the way was opened for the gods to return to Gielinor, Saradomin was the first to do so.
            - **Magda:** His followers see him as a kind figure, but history has shown that it is unwise to anger him.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about Zamorak?
            - **Magda:** Zamorak is a chaos god, and is often perceived as evil, but like all gods he is a complex being with many sides to his personality.
            - **Magda:** He was one of the first gods to return to Gielinor, when it became possible for them to do so.
            - **Magda:** Of all the younger gods, it is Zamorak who most desires power, and that is why he seeks the Stone of Jas - the most powerful of all the Elder Artefacts.
            - (previous)
          - [Choice]: What can you tell me about Guthix?
            - *(If asked less than four times without exiting dialogue:)*
              - **Magda:** Guthix was the most powerful of the younger gods, the order of beings that includes Saradomin and Zamorak.
              - **Magda:** For thousands of years he protected the people of Gielinor from his fellow gods with a magical barrier.
              - **Magda:** But then Guthix was slain, and his divine protection fell away.
              - **Magda:** Now the gods are starting to return to Gielinor, and their arrival will change the world forever.
            - (previous)
            - *(If asked four or more times without exiting dialogue:)*
              - **Magda:** Guthix is the name of a cat I once had. Lovely little fellow. Black as midnight, he was, with a white patch over one eye.
              - **Magda:** Excellent mouser, too - he could sniff out a rodent from half a mile away. I do miss that little scamp.
              - (previous)
          - [Choice]: I'd like to know more about Gielinor.
            - (above)
          - [Choice]: I'd like to ask about something else.
            - (initial)
      - [Choice]: I'd like to ask about something else.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Goodbye{{Sic}}
        - **Magda:** Goodbye, my child.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Magda:** Goodbye, my child.
