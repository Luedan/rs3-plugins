## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Mack:** Hello adventurer. Looking to sell some fish? I'll give you a good deal.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Mack:** I'm Mack, a fish trader from Berylbrook.
    - **Player:** Oh, you're from Berylbrook? Shame, seems like it's seen better days.
    - **Mack:** You can say that again, but every cloud has a silver lining.
    - **Player:** What do you mean?
    - **Mack:** Since the village was destroyed, I've been the only one supplying fish.
    - **Mack:** I can charge whatever I want. I've been making a killing!
    - **Player:** That seems a little exploitative.
    - **Mack:** Is it? Young adventurers like you stop for a bit of fishing and I give them gold.
    - **Mack:** And the weres of Heathervein get a supply of fish.
    - **Mack:** Everybody wins!
    - **Player:** Hmm...
    - **Mack:** Now, looking to make a trade?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Trade.
        - (opens=Fish For Gold)
      - [Choice]: Goodbye.
        - **Player:** Goodbye.
        - **Mack:** Come back soon.
  - [Choice]: Okay.
    - (opens=Fish For Gold)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Mack:** Come back soon.
