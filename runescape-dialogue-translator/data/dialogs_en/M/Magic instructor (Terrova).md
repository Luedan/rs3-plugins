You must wield a magic weapon to cast combat spells. You'll begin your adventure in {{colour|blue|Lumbridge}}. {{colour|blue|Lumbridge}} is a quaint welcoming town in the kingdom of {{colour|blue|Misthalin}}. I suggest you complete the {{colour|blue|Blood Pact}} quest in {{colour|blue|Lumbridge}}. It will reward you with {{colour|blue|Melee}}, {{colour|blue|Ranged}} and {{colour|blue|Magic}} weapons. Speak to {{colour|blue|Xenia}} in the {{colour|blue|Lumbridge cemetery}} to begin. I'm going to deactivate the protective spells around this island so you can {{colour|blue|Home Teleport}} to {{colour|blue|Lumbridge}} using the {{colour|blue|Lodestone Network}}. If you ever get lost, you can freely teleport to a lodestone you have discovered. You'll have a mentor when you arrive in {{colour|blue|Lumbridge}} to help you on your first few adventures. He's a dwarf called {{colour|blue|Gudrik}} who lives in {{colour|blue|Taverley}}. Should you need guidance, you'll be able to {{colour|blue|contact your mentor}}.
