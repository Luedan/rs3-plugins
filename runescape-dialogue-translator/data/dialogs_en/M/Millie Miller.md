## Dialogue Transcript


### At the mill northwest of Lumbridge

- **Millie Miller:** Hello, adventurer. Welcome to Mill Lane Mill. Can I help you?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Millie Miller:**I'm Miss Millicent Miller, the Miller of Mill Lane Mill. Our family has been milling flour for generations.
    - **Player:**Don't you ever get fed up with flour?
    - **Millie Miller:**It's a good business to be in. People will always need flour.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Millie Miller:**This is Mill Lane Mill, source of the finest flour in Gielinor, and home to the Miller family for many generations.
    - **Millie Miller:** We take wheat from the field nearby and mill it into flour.
    - (other)
  - {{anchor|How do I mill flour}}[Choice]: How do I mill flour?
    - **Millie Miller:**Making flour is pretty easy. First of all, you need to get some wheat. You can pick some from wheat fields. There is one just outside the mill, but there are many others scattered across the world.
    - **Millie Miller:**Feel free to pick wheat from our field! There always seems to be plenty of wheat there.
    - **Player:**Then I bring my wheat here?
    - **Millie Miller:**Yes, or to one of the other mills in Gielinor. They all work the same way.
    - **Millie Miller:**Just take your wheat up two levels to the top floor of the mill and place some into the hopper.
    - **Millie Miller:**Then you need to start the grinding process by pulling the lever near the hopper. You can add more wheat, but each time you add wheat you'll have to pull the hopper lever again.
    - **Player:**So, where does the flour go then?
    - **Millie Miller:**The flour appears in this room here. You'll need an empty pot to put the flour into. One pot will hold the flour made by one load of wheat.
    - **Millie Miller:**You can buy empty pots from the general store in Lumbridge, south-east of here. You can also buy them from the Grand Exchange in Varrock or make them yourself.
    - **Millie Miller:**That's all there is to it and you'll have a pot of flour.
    - **Player:**Great! Thanks for your help.
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: What can I do with the flour I mill?|cond=After choosing "How do I mill flour?"
    - **Millie Miller:**Flour is ideal for making tasty cakes, pies or delicious bread. I'm not a cook so you'll have to ask a cook to find out how to bake things.
    - **Millie Miller:**You could also sell it on the Grand Exchange in Varrock for a profit! Likewise, if you need flour and do not have time to make it yourself, you can buy some there.
  - [Choice]: I'm fine, thanks.
    - (end)
