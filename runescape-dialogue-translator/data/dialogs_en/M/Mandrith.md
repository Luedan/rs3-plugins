### Assignment advice


#### Abyssal beasts

**Mandrith:** Abyssal beasts can be found east of the Lava Maze.

#### Abyssal demons

**Mandrith:** Abyssal demons are nasty creatures to deal with. They aren't really part of this realm and are able to move very quickly to trap their prey. They can be found south of the Lava Maze.

#### Abyssal lords

**Mandrith:** Abyssal lords can be found east of the Lava Maze.

#### Abyssal savages

**Mandrith:** Abyssal savages can be found south of the Scorpion Pit

#### Acheron mammoths

**Mandrith:** Acheron mammoths are honourable yet powerful beasts from the frozen north. When they charge you, get out of the way! They can be found in the snow to the west of here.

#### Black demons

**Mandrith:** Black demons are weak to ranged attacks. They're still the strongest demon and very dangerous, however. They can be found near the Demonic Ruins.

#### Dark beasts

**Mandrith:** Dark beasts are large, dog-like predators with massively muscled bodies that protect them from crushing weapons. They can be found south of the Lava Maze.

#### Gargoyles

**Mandrith:** Gargoyles are winged creatures of stone. You'll need to fight them until they are close to death before breaking them apart with a rock hammer. They can be found in the eastern ruins, north of the Graveyard of Shadows.

#### Glacors

**Mandrith:** Glacors were brought to this world by Lucien. They use magic, ranged and melee attacks. You can find in the snow west of here.

#### Greater demon berserkers and ash lords

**Mandrith:** You can find greater demon berserkers and ash lords west of the Lava Maze.

#### Hydrix dragons

**Mandrith:** You can find hydrix dragons north west of the Lava Maze.

#### Ice strykewyrms

**Mandrith:** The coldest of their kind, they are found on the Frozen Waste Plateau. As they are beings of ice, I've found that fire spells inflict far more damage against them.

#### Kal'gerion demons

**Mandrith:** Kal'gerion demons are powerful creatures that use both magic and melee. They reside near the Demonic Ruins. I would recommend you take cover when they smash the ground, or when they use magic to pull you towards them.

#### Lava strykewyrms

**Mandrith:** Lava strykewyrms are the most powerful of their kind. They attack with all combat styles and are weak to nothing. They can be found deep within the Wilderness to the south-west of the Lava Maze. I would watch your back while slaying them.

#### Living wyverns

**Mandrith:** Living wyverns breathe a poison that will kill you rapidly unless you are freezing. But watch out; the colder you are the harder their regular attacks will hit you.  They can be found in the snow west of here.

#### Onyx dragons

**Mandrith:** You can find onyx dragons south east of Red Dragon Isle.

#### Revenant ghosts

**Mandrith:** Revenant ghosts range from ghostly imps to dragons, they are all very powerful. They have the ability to heal themselves, so make sure you're prepared! These ghosts reside in the Forinthry Dungeon.

#### Ripper demons

**Mandrith:** Ripper Demons are driven crazy by the scent of blood. They will hit you harder the worse your injuries are. And watch out - they can sometimes drop on you from above, which is lethal.  They can be found north of Red Dragon Isle.

#### Soulgazers

**Mandrith:** You can find soulgazers north east of the Graveyard of Shadows.
