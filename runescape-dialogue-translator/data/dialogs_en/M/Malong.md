# Malong

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Malong](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMalong)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without all the jewellery

- **Malong:** Oh you're here about the Jewellery, let's see what you have.
- **Malong:** Sorry, you don't have all the things I need yet.
- **Malong:** Bring me a Diamond Ring, an Emerald Necklace and a Ruby Bracelet.

### With all the jewellery

- **Malong:** Oh you're here about the Jewellery, let's see what you have.
- **Malong:** Great! You brought me all that shiny jewellery!

### After bringing him the jewellery

- **Malong:** You have already completed this task.
