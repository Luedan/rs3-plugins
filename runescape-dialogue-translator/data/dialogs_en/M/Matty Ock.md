## Dialogue Transcript

- **Matty Ock:** I'm Matty Ock and welcome to my mattock shop!
- **Player:** Is that even a real name?
- **Matty Ock:** It's my brand! Plus, I invented mattocks, so obviously I named them after myself.
- **Player:** That sounds like the opposite is true.
- **Matty Ock:** Look, I sell mattocks up to rune - do you want one or not?
- (opens=Matty Ock's Mattock Shop)
