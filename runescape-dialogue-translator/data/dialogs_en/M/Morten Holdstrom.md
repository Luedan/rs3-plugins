## Dialogue Transcript

- **Morten Holdstrom:** Good day to you.
- **Player:** Hello. What are you up to?
- **Morten Holdstrom:** Ah, today is a surströmming day! The herring I buried six months ago is ready to be dug up.
- **Player:** Eughr! What are you going to do with it?
- **Morten Holdstrom:** Eat it, of course! It will be fermented just-right by now.
- **Player:** Fermented? You eat rotten fish?
- **Morten Holdstrom:** Hmmm, tasty. I'm guessing you don't want to come round and try it?
- **Player:** You guess correctly.
