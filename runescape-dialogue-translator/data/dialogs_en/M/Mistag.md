## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Dorgeshuun mines


#### Talking to him

- **Mistag:** Hello, friend?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can I sell you some ore?
    - **Player:** Can I sell you some ore?
    - **Mistag:** Certainly. I will buy any iron or silver ore you mine. Speak to me again when you have some.
  - [Choice]: Why do the Dorgeshuun live underground?
    - **Player:** Why do the Dorgeshuun live underground?
    - **Mistag:** Our ancient legends say that goblins were created by an evil god in order to fight in a huge way. This god sent the Dorgeshuun tribe to fight a battle where all would die.
    - **Mistag:** But our ancestors escaped by hiding in a deep hole in the ground where our god could not find us. Eventually an earthquake sealed the hole and they were forever safe.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: The war is now, so you can return to the surface.
        - **Player:** The war is over now, so you can return to the surface.
        - **Mistag:** Even if the gods are no longer at war, I find it hard to believe that a tribe of peaceful goblins would be safe above. I have heard of humans coming down into the caves to slaughter our people!
        - **Mistag:** But even if we could return, we would not want to. We are adapted to living underground, and we have built a home for ourselves that we do not want to leave.
      - [Choice]: What was the name of your god?
        - **Player:** What was the name of your god?
        - **Mistag:** Our ancestors did not speak his name, thinking that to do so would attract his attention. His name has been unspoken for so long that it is now entirely forgotten.
        - **Mistag:** That is for the best. We have survived perfectly well without the gods, and we do not want a return to the old ways.
  - [Choice]: What happened to your arm?
    - **Player:** What happened to your arm?
    - **Mistag:** I lost it in a mining accident a few years ago. This area is very unstable.
    - **Mistag:** That's why we put markers on the wall showing the safest path.
  - [Choice]: Can you show me the way out of the mines?
    - **Player:** Can you show me the way out of the mine?
    - **Mistag:** Certainly. Come back soon!
    - Mistag shows you the way through the tunnels.
