## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to Mod Raven atop the hourglass tower in the City of Um

- **Mod Raven:** It is strange to be here. They do know I'm not dead, right? Someone has told them?
- **Player:** I mean...this is the Underworld?
- **Mod Raven:** True, it is the perfect place for a snack. The souls here are delicious after all.
- **Mod Raven:** So rife with precious suffering, how I adore it.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I miss you, Raven.
    - **Player:** I miss you Raven.
    - **Mod Raven:** That's a lie and you'll burn for it.
    - **Mod Raven:** But I miss you too...
    - (continue)
  - [Choice]: I'm glad you're gone.
    - **Player:** I'm glad you're gone.
    - **Mod Raven:** Who are you again? Do I know you?
    - (continue)
- **Mod Raven:** Anyway, enough idle chatter. There are lost souls here and I have a shiny new spoon.
- (If the player has not yet received souls for this interaction:)
  - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
    - (continue)
- **Mod Raven:** Dinner time!
