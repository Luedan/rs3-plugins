## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Mudflippers:** Da hoggies ain't biting today.
- **Player:** 'Hoggies'?
- **Mudflippers:** Oh, sorry, da hog fishies. They sometimes swin down da waterfall from up top.
- **Player:** 'Up top' - Berylbrook - is overrun by sanguine werewolves. Maybe they are eating them all?
- **Mudflippers:** Could you tell them to stop? I want my fishies!
