# Muggh

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Muggh](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMuggh)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before As a First Resort

- **Muggh:** Hey, what you doing here? We not open yet.
- **Player:** Just having a nosey, really.
- **Muggh:** You bring dat nose back here when we open for business. I fix you up good.
- **Player:** Fix me up?
- **Muggh:** Yeah, me give you facial. Try to make your ugly face look bit nicer.
- **Player:** Charming.

### After As a First Resort

- **Muggh:** Hey, what you doing here? You want me give you facial?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Why, sure, since you make it sound so delightful.
    - **Player:** Why, sure, since you make it sound so delightful.
    - (Condition: If the player is wearing headgear:)
      - **Muggh:** Take dat thing off your head, human, else you no get facial.
    - (Condition: If the player already has mud on their face:)
      - **Muggh:** Silly human, you already gots face full of muds.
    - (Condition: Otherwise:)
      - Muggh smacks a fistful of mud on your face.
  - [Choice]: Um, maybe later.
    - **Player:** Um, maybe later.
