## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Mhistyll:** Good day, Player. Welcome to my shop. Have you come to help with the crystal urchins?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Mhistyll:** I am Mhistyll. I am the keeper of the cave that lies behind me.
    - **Mhistyll:** I also reward those who try and deal with the infestation of crystal urchins in the waterfall above.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are the crystal urchins?
    - **Player:** What are the crystal urchins?
    - **Mhistyll:** The rock pool at the top of the waterfall used to be filled with regular urchins.
    - **Mhistyll:** But a crystal within the caves below extended into the pool, and the urchins changed. Now they are living crystal. They breed rapidly and cannot be killed by their regular predators.
    - **Mhistyll:** If something is not done then the waterfall will become a cascade of spiny, crystal death!
    - **Player:** That seems a little melodramatic.
    - **Mhistyll:** Some of them are the size of beach balls. Can you imagine the damage they will cause when they start raining down onto the city?
    - **Player:** That's a good point.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What can I do to help?
    - **Player:** What can I do to help?
    - **Mhistyll:** To help fish out the urchins you will need to climb to the top of the waterfall.
    - **Mhistyll:** There is a route next to me leading up the side of the waterfall but you will need to bring a crossbow and grapple.
    - [You need at least 90 Ranged, 90 Agility and 90 Strength to use the grapple location, as well as a crossbow and grapple.]
    - **Mhistyll:** Once there you can fish small, medium and large urchins, if you are a sufficiently talented fisher.
    - **Mhistyll:** You can then grind them to dust, which can be used to make perfect juju fishing potions.
    - **Mhistyll:** Or if you don't want to do that then you can trade them to me.
    - **Mhistyll:** I have lamps that can help increase your fishing skill, as well as the song that can let you create a crystal fishing rod.
    - **Mhistyll:** Also, if you find a hermit crab in the pool then I have a collection of objects you can use to give them a home.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can I see what you have for sale?
    - **Player:** Can I see what you have for sale?
    - **Mhistyll:** Certainly.
    - (opens=Prifddinas Waterfall Fishing Shop)
