## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Without the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Mike:** Arr? Be ye wantin' te go on account with our gang o' fillibusters?
- **Mike:** The powder monkey be takin' a caulk after gettin' rowdy on bumboo, so there be plenty of room for ye.
- **Player:** Riiiiight...
- **Player:** I'll just be over here if you need me.

#### With the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Mike:** Wanna buy some clothes? Good quality, none of yer rags.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'll take a look.
    - **Player:** I'll take a look.
    - (opens=Dodgy Mike's Second-hand Clothing)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Mike:** Then what are you doin' in my shop? Move along.
  - [Choice]: Why does that shirt have a hole in it?
    - **Player:** Why does that shirt have a hole in it?
    - **Mike:** It's to put your head through.
    - **Player:** In the stomach? Isn't that an odd place for a neck hole?
    - **Mike:** Oh! It seems as if I must have slipped while cleaning it and put a bit of a hole in the front.
    - **Mike:** Silly me.
    - **Player:** What were you cleaning it with, a blood-covered scimitar?
    - **Mike:** ...
    - **Mike:** If I say yes will you buy it?
    - **Player:** No.
    - **Mike:** Then no, I was not cleaning it with a blood-covered scimitar.
    - **Player:** Where do you get these clothes?
    - **Mike:** Found em.
    - **Player:** You found a bunch of slashed up clothing, some of which has been shoddily cleaned and stitched together?
    - **Mike:** Yes.
    - **Mike:** Look, do you want in on this or not?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: I'll take a look.
        - (above)
      - [Choice]: No thanks.
        - (above)
  - [Choice]: Where did that pillar come from?
    - **Player:** Where did that pillar come from?
    - **Mike:** You wanna buy it?
    - **Player:** Is it for sale?
    - **Mike:** Would you buy it if it was?
    - **Player:** If it was could you sell it to me?
    - **Mike:** If I couldn't would you still give me the money?
    - **Player:** No.
    - **Player:** Where did this pillar come from?
    - **Mike:** Dunno. There are a load of islands out this way, and a lot of them have little bits and pieces like this scattered around.
    - **Mike:** The way I see it, there must have been some more people out here at one point, then they went away.
    - **Player:** Where?
    - **Mike:** Search me.
    - **Player:** I'd rather not.
