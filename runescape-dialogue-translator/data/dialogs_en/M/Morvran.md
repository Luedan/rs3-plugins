### Assignment advice


#### Abyssal demons

**Morvran:** Abyssal demons are nasty creatures to deal with. They aren't really part of this realm and are able to move very quickly to trap their prey. They can be found in Canifis Slayer Tower and in the Senntisten Asylum.

#### Acheron mammoths

**Morvran:** Acheron Mammoths are honourable yet powerful beasts from the frozen north. When they charge you, get out of the way! They can be found on an iceberg that is accessible from a dock near Rellekka.

#### Adamant dragons

**Morvran:** Adamant dragons are even more dangerous than mithril dragons. Look for them in Brimhaven dungeon, beneath a staircase north-west of the iron and steel dragons.

#### Airut

**Morvran:** The airut are aggressive creatures from another world. They have powerful arms and attack with great vigour. Certain abilities can counter and stun them, however.

#### Aquanites

**Morvran:** Aquanites are amphibious creatures that attract prey using the glowing lures on their heads. They attack stronger opponents from a distance with water magic, and dislike those who pray against it. They dwell in the Fremennik Slayer Dungeon, in a chamber past the kurasks.

#### Order of ascension members

**Morvran:** The Order of Ascension are magic users. It is strongly suggested you use ranged attacks. Their tactics can make them tough one-on-one, so bringing a friend along would be beneficial.

#### Aviansie

**Morvran:** Aviansie can be found in the God Wars Dungeon north of Trollheim. As they are flying, you'll find melee attacks are not going to cut it.

#### Black demons

**Morvran:** Black demons are weak to ranged attacks. They're still the strongest demon and very dangerous, however. Seek them out in the Edgeville or Brimhaven dungeons.

#### Black dragons

**Morvran:** Black dragons are among the strongest dragons and are very fierce. Watch out for their fiery breath. I am sure you will have seen them in your travels and have honed your techniques on lesser dragons.

#### Camel warriors

**Morvran:** Camel warriors can use ancient magics to attack you. They also have the blessings of Akthanakos and can create powerful, disorientating mirages.  They can be found in the deep desert.

#### Celestial dragons

**Morvran:** Celestial dragons are one of the strangest dragons I assign. They use melee but also have a powerful dragonfire attack, as well as a fascinating ability to momentarily stop time. You can find them in the depths of Dragontooth Island.

#### Chaos giants

**Morvran:** You can find Chaos giants out in the Barendir tunnels, beyond Keldagrim. Be careful, as they can launch their Dragon throwing axe at range or take a swipe at you if you get close. Perhaps you could recover some.

#### Corrupted creatures

**Morvran:** You can find corrupted creatures in the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon.

#### Creatures of the lost grove

**Morvran:** You can find the creatures of the lost grove within the lost grove, south of the poison waste.

#### Cres's automatons

**Morvran:** Without their master, Cres's automatons roam Guthix's chambers attacking any who enter. They are programmed to use magic, melee or ranged and can perform a violent area-of-effect attack. Guthix's chambers may be reached via his shrine, or the excavation west of the Legends' Guild.

#### Crystal shapeshifters

**Morvran:** Crystal shapeshifters are deranged remnants of the elves on Tarddiad. They are quite mad and, according to Angof, beyond redemption. Show them mercy by ending their suffering.

#### Dagannoth

**Morvran:** Dagannoth are large, sea-dwelling creatures that are very aggressive. You'll often find them in caves near sea water. There is no joy so great as to cleanse these from the face of Gielinor.

#### Dark beasts

**Morvran:** Dark beasts are large, dog-like predators with massively muscled bodies that protect them from crushing weapons. We found some while mining the Mourner tunnels, in search of the Temple of Light.

#### Desert strykewyrms

**Morvran:** You'll find these east of Al Kharid, atop the mountain. Watch out for their vicious ranged attacks!

#### Edimmu

**Morvran:** Edimmu are ghosts which failed to escape their body. They're ghosts and zombies, yet somehow different altogether. They use ranged attacks and are highly aggressive. You can find them in a resource dungeon, which the Meilyr clan in Prifddinas has access to.

#### Elves

**Morvran:** I'm not advocating the killing of my kind, you understand - we enjoy being tested in combat. You'll find melee fighters in Clan Iorwerth's segment of Prifddinas; mages and rangers in Clan Cadarn's segment.

#### Ganodermic creatures

**Morvran:** Ganodermic beasts and runts live in the depths of a cave north-east of the Mage Training Arena. The creatures of that dungeon are coated in toxic fungus, so you should fight them from a distance. The fungus helps them repair physical damage, so you might prefer using magical attacks.

#### Gargoyles

**Morvran:** Gargoyles are winged creatures of stone. You'll need to fight them until they are close to death before breaking them apart with a rock hammer. They can be found in Canifis Slayer Tower.

#### Gemstone dragons

**Morvran:** You can find gemstone dragons beneath the gem mine in Shilo Village.

#### Glacors

**Morvran:** Glacors were brought to this world by Lucien. They use magic, ranged and melee attacks. You can find them in an underground cavern on the Frozen Plateau.

#### Greater demons

**Morvran:** Greater demons are weak to ranged attacks. Though not the strongest of demons, they are still dangerous. There are some in Brimhaven Dungeon.

#### Grotworms

**Morvran:** Grotworms have recently been spotted in a cave near Rimmington. They will bite and attack with some sort of acid spit. Be careful - they are very aggressive.

#### Ice strykewyrms

**Morvran:** The coldest of their kind, they are found beneath the Fremennik mountains. As they are beings of ice, I've found that fire spells inflict far more damage against them.

#### Iron dragons

**Morvran:** Iron dragons are some of the weaker metallic dragons, with iron scales that are far thicker than normal iron armour. Their fiery breath and sharp claws can be negated by a number of means. Look for them in Brimhaven Dungeon.

#### Kal'gerion demons

**Morvran:** Kal'gerion demons are powerful creatures that use both magic and melee. They reside in a resource dungeon near Daemonheim. I would recommend you take cover when they smash the ground, or when they use magic to pull you towards them.

#### Kalphites

**Morvran:** Kalphites are large insects that live in hives under the desert sands. I am sure that, by now, you have seen them to the south-west of Shantay Pass.

#### Lava strykewyrms

**Morvran:** Lava strykewyrms are the most powerful of their kind. They attack with all combat styles and are weak to nothing. They can be found deep within the Wilderness so I would watch your back while slaying them.

#### Living wyverns

**Morvran:** Living wyverns breathe a poison that will kill you rapidly unless you are freezing. But watch out; the colder you are the harder their regular attacks will hit you.  They can be found in the Asgarnia Ice dungeon.

#### Mithril dragons

**Morvran:** Mithril dragons are very dangerous opponents. There's some in the Ancient Cavern, north of where you'll find Kuradal. You are a battle master - test your skills.

#### Muspah

**Morvran:** Muspah are creatures born of nightmares. Ancient magic is the most effective thing to use against them. You can find them by travelling to Freneskae. They'll be aggressive unless you wear your shard of Zaros.

#### Mutated jadinkos

**Morvran:** These warped jade vine creatures will not hesitate to use their environment to help them in combat if they cannot retaliate otherwise. The baby jadinkos are easiest to kill, but the males offer the greatest rewards. Ranged weapons made from vines are best against them.

#### Nechryael

**Morvran:** Nechryael are demons of decay that summon small winged beings to help them fight their victims. They can be found in the Chaos Tunnels or in Canifis Slayer Tower.

#### Nightmare creatures

**Morvran:** You can find nightmare creatures on Freneskae, which can be accessed through the world gate.

#### Nihil

**Morvran:** The surviving subjects of Zaros's experiments. They have powerful charge attacks and fight in packs of four. You can find them by travelling to Freneskae. They'll be aggressive unless you wear your shard of Zaros.

#### Nodon dragonkin

**Morvran:** Nodon dragonkin are deeply intelligent creatures, armed with advanced siege weaponry. However, they are weak to dragonbane weapons. You can find them fighting for Jas in Senntisten.

#### Ripper demons

**Morvran:** Ripper Demons are driven crazy by the scent of blood. They will hit you harder the worse your injuries are. And watch out; they can sometimes drop on you from above, which is lethal.  They can be found in a fissure near Al Kharid.

#### Rune dragons

**Morvran:** Rune dragons are even more dangerous than adamant dragons. I highly recommend you bring dragonbane ammunition to pierce their armour. To find them, you must travel to Kethsi via the World Gate in Arandar.

#### Shadow creatures

**Morvran:** Shadows are light creatures that have been corrupted by the Dark Lord. The Amlodd summon them to their section of the city in order to defeat them and then cleanse their cores.

#### Soul devourers

**Morvran:** You can find soul devourers in the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon.

#### Stalker creatures

**Morvran:** You can find stalker creatures within Zamorak's hideout.

#### Steel dragons

**Morvran:** Steel dragons are dangerous and metallic, with steel scales that are far thicker than normal steel armour. Look for them in Brimhaven Dungeon.

#### Tormented demons

**Morvran:** Tormented demons are powerful creatures that use magic, ranged and melee attacks. They also have the ability to shield themselves. You can find them below the Tears of Guthix cave.

#### Tzhaar

**Morvran:** The TzHaar are mighty warriors - prove yourself against them. You'll find them deep within the Karamja Volcano.

#### Vyrewatch

**Morvran:** These foul creatures can be found throughout Meiyerditch and Darkmeyer in Morytania. They have a telepathic ability which enables them to dodge incoming attacks, so you'll need a weapon specially created to harm them.

#### Waterfiends

**Morvran:** Waterfiends are elemental creatures found north of Kuradal in the Ancient Cavern. Those followed by familiars are keen to slay them.
