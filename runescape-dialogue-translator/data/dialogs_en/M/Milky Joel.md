# Milky Joel

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Milky Joel](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMilky%20Joel)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Milky Joel!
- The coconut barely even reacts to your overly excited call.
- **Player:** Why so cold? We're here to rescue you!
- The coconut gives a near imperceptible shrug, as if it couldn't care less.
- **Player:** Fine, be that way! Maybe I'll just leave you here.
- The coconut shakes a little in fear and pines in your general direction.
- **Player:** Oh, who am I kidding? I can't stay mad at you. Come here!
- As you embrace, Milky Joel whispers in your ear - just a few words that send a chill down your spine, though you're unsure why: 'The Harbinger'
