## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Miltog:** Do you want to buy a lamp, surface-dweller?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (opens=Miltog's Lamps)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Miltog:** Suit yourself.
  - [Choice]: Can you fill a lamp with oil, please?
    - **Player:** Can you fill a lamp with oil, please?
    - **Miltog:** You can do that yourself! Just put some swamp tar in the lamp oil still here and then use the lamp on the still.
  - [Choice]: How do the lamps around the city work?
    - **Player:** How do the lamps around the city work?
    - **Miltog:** They work by our very own Dorgeshuun magic!
    - **Miltog:** Sometimes they don't work very well, though. The light orbs blow and they have to be replaced.
    - **Miltog:** If you ever see a lamp that's gone out, you can fix it by replacing the light orb. You can make new light orbs out of glass, and use a piece of wire on them to add the filament.
    - **Miltog:** There's a machine in the south of the city that makes the wire you need.
