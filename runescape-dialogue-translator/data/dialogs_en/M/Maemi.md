# Maemi

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Maemi](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMaemi)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without an Amulet of Catspeak

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Talk to the cat.
    - **Player:** Here, kitty kitty!
    - **Maemi:** Hiss!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Pet the cat.
    - {{Qact|Maemi scratches the player, dealing 1 life point of damage.}}
    - **Player:** {{RSChat|Ouch!}}
  - [Choice]: Leave the cat alone.
    - (end)

##### Using a bucket of milk on her

- [The cat excitedly licks at the creamy milk on the surface.]
- **Maemi:** Meow!

##### Using a bucket of milk on her immediately afterwards

- [The cat doesn't show much interest in more milk. She's probably too full.]
- **Maemi:** Mew...

### With an Amulet of Catspeak

- **Maemi:** What do you want, human?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who's a cute little kitty then?
    - **Maemi:** I advise you to desist in addressing me in that impertinent tone, unless you enjoy claw marks in your shins.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Is Helda your owner?
    - **Maemi:** My owner?
    - **Maemi:** Pffft! What insolence.
    - **Maemi:** Such impertinence! <br>I own her, of course. The human does my bidding, and attends to my needs.
    - **Maemi:** As it should be.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Does Helda have any relatives?
    - **Maemi:** There are no other kittens in my pet's litter, as far as I am aware.
    - **Maemi:** Though to be honest, I have a hard time telling you humans apart.
    - **Player:** Perhaps it would be easier for you if we were fitted with identification tags.
    - **Maemi:** What a splendid idea! I shall bring it up at our next meeting of the council.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I've spoken with an animal enough for today.
    - **Maemi:** What a coincidence. <br>I was just thinking the same thing.

##### Using a bucket of milk on her

- [The cat excitedly licks at the creamy milk on the surface.]
- **Maemi:** Mmm, thank you, human. <br>You're more useful than I initially speculated.

##### Using a bucket of milk on her immediately afterwards

- **Maemi:** Oh, thank you human, but I'm quite full at the moment. I must watch my figure.

### Using any item (besides a bucket of milk) on her

- The cat sneers disdainfully at your offering.
