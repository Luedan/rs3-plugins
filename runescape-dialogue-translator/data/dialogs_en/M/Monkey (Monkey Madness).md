## Dialogue Transcript


### Dropping the monkey outside of Karamja, then talking to it

- **Player:** Hello there, monkey.
- **Monkey:** Are we there yet??
- **Player:** Not quite yet ... you should get back in my backpack now.
- (receives=Monkey (Monkey Madness))

- (Condition: Upon dropping the monkey:)

- (removes=Monkey (Monkey Madness))
