## Dialogue Transcript


### At Port Sarim

- **Monk of Entrana:** Do you seek passage to holy Entrana? If so, you must leave your weaponry and armour behind -- this is Saradomin's will.
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: Yes, okay, I'm ready to go.
    - **Player:** Yes, okay, I'm ready to go.
    - The monk quickly searches you. {{anchor|search}}
    - *(If a combat aura is equipped and there are no other prohibited items:)*
      - **Monk of Entrana:** I'm sorry, but we do not allow combat auras on to our holy island.
      - **Player:** Oh, sorry about that.
    - *(If holding an Explorer's ring and there are no other prohibited items:)*
      - **Monk of Entrana:**I'm sorry, but we do not allow the explorer's ring on our holy island.
      - **Player:**Really? Why not?
      - **Monk of Entrana:**Its magic is too strong and may cause disruption in some areas.
      - **Player:** That sounds very interesting. Can you tell me more?
      - **Monk of Entrana:** Curiosity killed the cat, my friend. Needless to say, you will need to place the ring in your bank first.
    - *(If holding one prohibited item:)*
      - **Monk of Entrana:** I see you are peace-loving at heart and I'm sure you didn't intend to deceive me, but you seem to have one objectionable item in your possession.
      - **Player:**I do apologise; what item would that be?
      - The following item is not allowed on Entrana: [item]
    - *(If holding two to four prohibited items:)*
      - **Monk of Entrana:** I don't think you've been honest with me. You appear to be trying to snuggle a number of banned items onto Entrana.
      - **Player:** Oh, really? That's shocking! I wonder how they got there. I assure you I have nothing but good intentions. Can you tell me what items you have issue with?
      - The following items are not allowed on Entrana: [items]
    - *(If holding five to ten prohibited items:)*
      - **Monk of Entrana:** My word! You're a walking armoury! There is no way that I am allowing you to set foot on Entrana until you remove them.
      - **Player:**You monks are all wimps! Ah, well, can you at least tell me what items you have issue with?
      - The following items are not allowed on Entrana: [items]
    - *(If holding prohibited ten or more prohibited items:)*
      - The following items are not allowed on Entrana: [items]
    - *(Otherwise, if not holding prohibited items:)*
      - After a quick search, the monk smiles at you and allows you to board.}} {{anchor
      - {{qact|Screen fades in and out}}
      - The ship arrives at Entrana.
  - [Choice]: No, not right now.
    - (end)
