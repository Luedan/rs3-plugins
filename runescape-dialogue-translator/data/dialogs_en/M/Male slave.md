# Male slave

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Male slave](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMale%20slave)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

While mining they will say certain phrases, complaining about being enslaved. These phrases do not appear in the chat box and are only visible on the main game window. In no particular order the phrases they can say are:
- **Male slave:** I'm sick of this place.
- **Male slave:** What I wouldn't give for a good nights rest.
- **Male slave:** I feel so weak I could faint.
