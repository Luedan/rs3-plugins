## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Master Farmer Lilah:** Let me know if you see a wolf heading this way. A few well-placed arrows will make quick work of any beast or man trying to get at my livestock.
- **Master Farmer Lilah:** Haven't lost a single animal in all my years of farming, and I intend to keep it that way.

### Pickpocket


#### Below 38 Thieving

- You need to be a level 38 thief to steal from this target.
- (popup)
  - {{Popup|You need Thieving level 38.|warning=yes}}

#### With 38 Thieving


##### Successful pickpocket

- You pick the target's pocket.
  - (Condition: If seeds are received:)
    - You steal [number] [name] seeds.
  - (Condition: If coins are received:)
    - [number] coins have been added to your money pouch.

##### Unsuccessful pickpocket

- **Master Farmer Lilah:** {{RSChat|Cor blimey, mate! What are ye doing in me pockets?}}
- {{Qact|The player is hit for a few points of damage.}}
- You fail to steal from the target.
- You've been stunned.

##### Without space in backpack

- You cannot keep pickpocketing as you have a full inventory.
