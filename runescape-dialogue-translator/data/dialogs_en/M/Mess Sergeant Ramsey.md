### In the Pick and Lute inn in Taverley

- ((Mess Sergeant Ramsey begins with one of the following dialogue at random:))
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Where is the library? What am I saying? I mean where is the cheese? Can I help you?
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Where is the grapefruit? Has anyone seen my grapefruit? Can I help you?
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** What happened to the meat I've been stacking? Who took it? Can I help you?
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** I had a tray of hors d'oevres here a moment ago! Where are they? Can I help you?
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Where are my trousers? Oh! I'm wearing them! Can I help you?
  - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Where is the mouse? This recipe requires a mouse! Wait, not a mouse, a moose! Wait, not a moose, some mousse! Can I help you?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I need cooking supplies.
    - (opens=Mess Sergeant Ramsey's Cooking Supplies Shop)
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about cooking.
    - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Cooking is how you turn all of your fine but inedible ingredients into something you can eat. Eating is important, especially when you've been wounded in battle.
    - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** You'll find a lot of food items in the world. Cook everything you find! Cook things that seem like no one would ever eat them.
    - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** The only way to get better at it is to practice. So cook all the fish you catch, all the potatoes you grow, and your skill will improve.
    - **Mess Sergeant Ramsey:** Better cooks make better food, but of course you need better ingredients. Fishing and farming can help you with that.
  - [Choice]: Tell me about the skill menu.
    - {{Qact|Skills infobox opens.}}
    - You have many skills you can train. The more you practice a skill, the better it gets. Higher skill levels unlock new activities: equipment to craft, locations to explore, quests to complete, and much more.
    - Perform actions associated with a skill to earn experience points (XP) in that skill. Earn enough XP and you'll gain a level in that skill.
    - Most skills start at level 1, and you unlock all their benefits by leveling the skill to 99. (Some skills have unlocks up to level 120.)
    - The bottom number next to a skill icon is your base level in that skill (un-modified by buffs or debuffs). It is used for most requirement checks.
    - The top number is your modified skill level (temporarily boosted or drained by skill buffs and debuffs - such as potions or spells). It is used for most skill checks.
    - Achieve 99 in all your skills to earn the coveted Max Cape!
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - (end)
