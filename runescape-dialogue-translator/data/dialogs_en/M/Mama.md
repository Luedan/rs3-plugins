## Dialogue Transcript


### On Mos Le'Harmless


#### Without the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Mama:** Arr? Be ye wantin' te go on account with our gang o' fillibusters?
- **Mama:** The powder monkey be takin' a caulk after gettin' rowdy on bumboo, so there be plenty of room for ye.
- **Player:** Riiiiight...
- **Player:** I'll just be over here if you need me.

#### With the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Mama:** Hello stranger, you come for a drink?
- **Player:** What sort of drinks do you have?
- **Mama:** We got some cold beers, some warm stews and some 'rum'.
- **Player:** Not, Captain Braindeath's 'rum'?
- **Mama:** Yes! There was some sorta problem at the brewery, but they got it all sorted out now.
- **Mama:** You wanna buy something?
- (opens=The Other Inn)
