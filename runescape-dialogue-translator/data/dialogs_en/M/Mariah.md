# Mariah

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mariah](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMariah)
- **Letter**: `M`

## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Void Knight Outpost, before Quiet Before the Swarm

- **Mariah:** Hi, how can I help you?
- [Menu]: What do you want to say?
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Mariah:** I'm a girlfriend of one of the Void Knights. I come here from Falador to visit him when I get time off work.
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Mariah:** This is the Void Knight Outpost. The Void Knights keep us all safe from the pests that are trying to invade. You should talk to one of them if you want to know more.
  - [Choice]: I'm fine, thanks.
    - **Player:** I'm fine, thanks.

### After Quiet Before the Swarm

- **Mariah:** He's dead! He's dead! My Mikhal is dead!
- **Player:** Maybe you should go back to the mainland, see some family?
- **Mariah:** And leave his grave? Never! I'm going to stay here, with him.
