# Minnowman

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Minnowman](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMinnowman)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Overhead

- ((One of the following is seen occasionally:))
  - **Minnowman:** {{RSChat|Any spare minnow going?}}
  - **Minnowman:** {{RSChat|I'm here to collect some minnow!}}
  - **Minnowman:** {{RSChat|Oi oi, give us your minnow!}}

### Talk-to

- **Minnowman:** Got any minnow for me? I need a bunch for deliveries.
- **Minnowman:** I need a stack of [number] to be precise.
- ((Without the requested minnow:))
  - The Minnowman requires [number] magnetic minnow.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - The Minnowman requires [number] magnetic minnow.
- ((With the requested minnow:))
  - ((The following is shown in an interface titled "MINNOWMAN":))
    - Do you wish to give the Minnowman [number] magnetic minnow?
      - CANCEL
        - (end)
      - OK
        - (continue|Hand-in)

### Hand-in


#### Without the requested minnow

- The Minnowman requires [number] magnetic minnow.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The Minnowman requires [number] magnetic minnow.

#### With the requested minnow


##### If not helped before in this encounter

- (removes=magnetic minnow)
- You help the Minnowman with his minnow situation.
- ((Repeated in pop-up:))
  - You help the Minnowman with his minnow situation.

##### If already helped before in this encounter

- You've already offered your help.
- ((Repeated in pop-up:))
  - You've already offered your help.

### Leaving

- ((One of the following is seen, then continues below:))
  - **Minnowman:** {{RSChat|I'll be off now, I've got minnow to deliver!}}
  - **Minnowman:** {{RSChat|Thanks for the minnow chaps}}
- ((If the player helped:))
  - You are rewarded for helping the minnowman - You receive 1,940 Fishing XP.
