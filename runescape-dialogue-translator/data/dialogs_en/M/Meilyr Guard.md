## Dialogue Transcript


### While on a task


#### Talking to the Guard

- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Can I pass that barrier?
    - **Meilyr guard:** Only those who have been instructed by a slayer master may enter. This area is too dangerous to let just anyone in.
    - (Condition: If assigned Edimmu:)
      - **Player:** I was sent by [Slayer Master] to kill Edimmu.
    - (Condition: If assigned Creatures of Daemonheim:)
      - **Player:** I was sent by Laniakea to slay creatures that have escaped the depths of Daemonheim.
    - **Meilyr guard:** Hmm, really? If they believe you can survive, you may pass. Just don't expect me to help you if you get in trouble. I'm already closer to those... things... than I'd like to be.
  - [Choice]: What are you guarding?
    - **Meilyr guard:** I'm guarding you from yourself. This area has claimed the lives of many Clan Meilyr's best explorers in the past. Enough for us to need to build this barrier. The more who enter, the more die, and ultimately become these creatures. It's a terrible cycle.

### While not on a task


#### Attempting to cross the barrier

- **Meilyr Guard:** I can not allow you to enter this place. Only those with permission from a Slayer master may enter.
