## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Spirits of the Elid

- **Malik the Carter:** Water, water get your fresh water here, 1000 coins a waterskin, a bargain in these difficult times.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Thats extortionate! Why so much?
    - **Player:** Thats extortionate! Why so much?
    - **Malik the Carter:** Well it's the cheapest water you'll find in this town.
  - [Choice]: Ok, I'll buy some!
    - (removes=1,000 coins) (receives=Waterskin (4))
    - [You buy a full waterskin for 1000 coins!]
  - [Choice]: I think I'll give it a miss.
    - **Player:** I think I'll give it a miss.

### After Spirits of the Elid

- **Player:** You look glum.
- **Malik the Carter:** Business here really seems to have dried up. I had a good thing going here and you spoiled it.
- **Player:** Well it serves you right really. Taking advantage of people like that.
- **Malik the Carter:** So want to buy some water?
- **Player:** How much?
- **Malik the Carter:** To you 1000 coins for a waterskin!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Ok, I feel sorry for you.
    - (removes=1,000 coins) (receives=Waterskin (4))
    - [You buy a full waterskin for 1000 coins!]
  - [Choice]: I think I'll give it a miss.
    - **Player:** I think I'll give it a miss.
