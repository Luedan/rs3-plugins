## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### First time speaking to her

- **Player:** Good day, old hag.
- **Maysa the Hag:** Old hag indeed! I have a name you know!
- **Player:** Let me guess it wouldn't be Ali would it?
- **Maysa the Hag:** That's a rather ignorant assumption! My name is Maysa. And no, you can't call me May!
- **Maysa the Hag:** Now what do you want from the Old hag of Pollnivneach? To hex someone? Power, beauty, eternal youth or something else drab like that?
- **Player:** Actually none of those, I'm new in town and I just wanted to get to know the locals.
- **Maysa the Hag:** I'm busy brewing potions, so if you disturb me again without reason, I will turn you into a frog!
- **Maysa the Hag:** Oh I'm sorry I won't let it happen again.

#### Subsequent times

- **Player:** Good day, Maysa.
- **Maysa the Hag:** Good day to you too adventurer.
- **Maysa the Hag:** Have you good reason to disturb me again?
- **Player:** Nope!
- **Maysa the Hag:** I warned you about needlessly bothering me. That's it I'm hexing you into a frog!
- **Player:** Riddup, ribbit!
- **Maysa the Hag:** Don't taunt me because I will, once I find time to get around to it!

### Using the broomstick from Swept Away on her

- **Player:** Could you enchant this broom for me?
- **Maysa the Hag:** Ah, this is Maggie's broom, isn't it? Yes, I can enchant it for you. Just one moment.
- [You receive 1,997 Magic experience.]
- **Player:** Excellent! Many thanks.

#### If already enchanted

- **Player:** Could you enchant this broom for me?
- **Maysa the Hag:** Eh? I've already enchanted that for you.
- **Player:** Oh, right.
