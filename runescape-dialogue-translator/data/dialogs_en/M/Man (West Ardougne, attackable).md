## Dialogue Transcript


### Between Plague City and Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Good day.
- **Man:** We don't have good days here anymore. Curse King Tyras.
- Select an option
  - Oh ok, bad day then.
    - **Player:** Oh ok, bad day then.
  - Why, what has he done?
    - **Player:** Why, what has he done?
    - **Man:** His army curses our city with this plague and then wanders off again, leaving us to clear up the pieces.

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Man:** I have nothing to say to you Mourner, go away!

### After Plague's End

- **Man:**  Good day! Ah, it's so good to be able to say good days again! Not like the bad days when the Mourners were in control.
