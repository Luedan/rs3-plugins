# Mazcab guide

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mazcab guide](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMazcab%20guide)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### At 0 to 749 reputation

- **Ranger:** I'm sorry human, the Airut are out in full force. It's too dangerous to leave shelter.

### At 750 to 1999 reputation

- **Ranger:** Good day, human! Where can I take you to?
- 1. Tuska portal (entrance to Mazcab; where Goebie scout stands)
- 2. Forest entrance (entrance to Nemi forest to which you are currently closest)
- 3. I'd like to stay here

### At 2000 and greater reputation

- **Ranger:** Good day, human! Where can I take you to?
- 1. Tuska portal (entrance to Mazcab; where Goebie scout stands)
- 2. Forest entrance (entrance to Nemi forest to which you are currently closest)
- 3. Kanatah (if you are currently on the southeast side of the Nemi forest)
- 3. Otot (if you are currently on the northern side of the Nemi forest)
- 4. I'd like to stay here
