## Dialogue Transcript


### At Mill Road Mill, north of Ardougne


#### Before The Chosen Commander

- **Milton the Miller:** Oh, hello. What do you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Milton the Miller:** I'm Milton the Miller of Mill Road Mill. I'll give you one guess what I do for a living.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: You're a miller?
        - **Player:** You're a miller?
        - **Milton the Miller:** And they say common sense is dying out.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: You're an undercover White Knight?
        - **Player:** You're an undercover White Knight?
        - **Milton the Miller:** Very funny.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: You're an evil culinaromancer?
        - **Player:** You're an evil culinaromancer?
        - **Milton the Miller:** Very funny.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: You're a knight who is also a culinaromancer?
        - **Player:** You're a knight who is also a culinaromancer?
        - **Milton the Miller:** Hah, yes. They call me the Knight of the Living Bread.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Milton the Miller:** It's a windmill, you idiot.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: How do I mill flour?
    - **Player:** How do I mill flour?
    - **Milton the Miller:** You go upstairs, put some wheat in the hopper and pull the lever, then the milled flour comes out here.
    - **Milton the Miller:** Then you keep doing that all day, every day, because it's the family business and you have to stay in the family business.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.

#### After The Chosen Commander

- **Milton the Miller:** Oh, hello. What do you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I want to get in to Sigmund's base.
    - **Player:** I want to get in to Sigmund's base.
    - **Milton the Miller:** Didn't you hear? The base was destroyed. There's no H.A.M. activity here any more.
    - **Milton the Miller:** To be honest, I think that's for the best. Sigmund was always a little more extreme than what I was comfortable with.
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: How do I mill flour?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (above)
