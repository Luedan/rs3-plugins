**Denial**.
- **Seren:** He is dead! {{Audio button|Seren - He is dead!.ogg}}
- **Seren:** How can he be dead? How is this possible? We are the divine offspring of the creators of this universe - how could it be possible for us to die at the hands of mortals? {{Audio button|Seren - How can he be dead? How is this possible? - **Seren:** Surely that means I cannot die, even though he could. And I must not, for if I do, then what will happen to those under my care? {{Audio button|Seren - Surely that means I cannot die, even though he could. And I must not, for if I do, then what will happen to those under my care?.ogg}}

Yet my sudden departure would soon see the elves follow me into oblivion. {{Audio button|Seren - Though Zaros is gone, his legacy - at least - will remain.
