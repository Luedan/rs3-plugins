# Monk (Lost City)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Monk (Lost City)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMonk%20%28Lost%20City%29)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### On free-to-play worlds

- **Player:** Top of the day to you!
- **Monk:** Yes, Saradomin has certainly blessed us today!

### On members' worlds, before Lost City

- **Player:** Why are all of you standing around here?
- **Monk:** None of your business. Get lost.

### On members' worlds, after Lost City

- **Monk:** I already told you. I'm not talking to you anymore.
