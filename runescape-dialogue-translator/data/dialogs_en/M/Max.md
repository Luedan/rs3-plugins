## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Running

- **Max:** Can't stop right now! Catch me up when I next stop?

#### Skilling

- ((If previously talked to while running:))
  - **Max:** Sorry about that. XP waste if I stopped back there, eh?
  - (continue)
- **Max:** How can I help you?
- **What would you like to say?**
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Max:** Oh, sorry - I'm Max. Some say I'm a bit obsessed with skilling.
    - **Player:** And I'm Player, nice to meet you.
    - **Max:** Indeed, so can I help you with anything?
    - (other)
  - ((Only if Max is wearing a max cape:))
    - Nice cape you have there...
      - **Player:** Nice cape you have there...
      - **Max:** This? Thanks! It's a symbol that I've trained all my skills to level 99.
      - ((If the player doesn't have level 99 in all skills:))
        - **Player:** Wow, that's quite impressive.
        - **Max:** Thanks. I have faith in you, Player - one day you'll be here and I'll sell you one for yourself!
        - **Player:** We'll see about that, thanks.
        - **Max:** Farewell and good luck.
      - ((If the player has level 99 in all skills:))
        - **Player:** I've maxed all my skills!
        - **Max:** Indeed so. Would you like a cape to show that fact off? 2,871,000 coins - 99,000 for each skill!
        - **Pay 2,871,000 for a max cape?**
          - I'll take one!
            - **Player:** I'll take one!
            - ((Without two free spaces in backpack:))
              - **Max:** That's good, but you've got no space in your inventory for the cape and hood.
            - ((With two free spaces in backpack:))
              - ((Without enough coins:))
                - You can not afford that.
                - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
                  - You can not afford that.
                  - (continue)
                - **Max:** That's good, but you've got not enough money for the cape.
              - ((With enough coins:))
                - (removes=2,871,000 coins) (receives=a max cape and max hood)
                - **Max:** Thanks. Enjoy!
          - No, thanks.
            - **Player:** No, thanks.
  - ((Only if Max isn't wearing a max cape:))
    - ((After the release of Invention:))
      - Don't you usually have a cape on?
        - **Player:** Don't you usually have a cape on?
        - **Max:** Yeah, usually...but I have to max invention before I can wear it again.
        - **Player:** Yeah, I have more training to do before I can wear that cape too.
        - **Max:** Well, good luck with it. Gotta go - need some inspiration.
    - ((After the release of Archaeology:))
      - Don't you usually have a cape on?
        - **Player:** Don't you usually have a cape on?
        - **Max:** Yeah, usually...but I need to max Archaeology before I can wear it again.
        - ((If the player doesn't have level 99 in all skills:))
          - **Player:** Yeah, I have more training to do before I can wear that cape too.
          - **Max:** Well, good luck with it. Gotta go - need to discover some artefacts.
        - ((If the player has level 99 in all skills:))
          - (above|I've maxed all my skills)
    - ((After the release of Necromancy:))
      - Don't you usually have a max cape on?
        - **Player:** Don't you usually have a max cape on?
        - **Max:** Yeah, usually...but I need to max Necromancy before I can wear it again.
        - ((If the player doesn't have level 99 in all skills:))
          - **Player:** Yeah, I have more training to do before I can wear that cape too.
          - **Max:** Well, good luck with it. Gotta go - need to {{Sic|discover some artefacts}}.
        - ((If the player has level 99 in all skills:))
          - (above|I've maxed all my skills)
  - Nothing, thanks.
    - **Player:** No, thanks.
    - **Max:** Farewell, and good luck with your skills.
