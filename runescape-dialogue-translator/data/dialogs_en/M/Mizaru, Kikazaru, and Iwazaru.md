# Mizaru, Kikazaru, and Iwazaru

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mizaru, Kikazaru, and Iwazaru](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMizaru%2C%20Kikazaru%2C%20and%20Iwazaru)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### On Ape Atoll


#### Before Recipe for Disaster

- These monkeys are too busy arguing with each other to notice you.

#### After Recipe for Disaster


##### Without an amulet of monkeyspeak

- **Player:** Hello?
- **Iwazaru:** Ah Ah! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ah Uh!
- **Mizaru:** Ook Ook! Ook Ook! Ook! Ook Ah Ook!
- **Mizaru:** Ook Ah Uh! Ook! Ah Uh Ah! Ah Uh Ah!
- You don't understand a word. Maybe you've forgotten to wear something.

##### With an amulet of monkeyspeak

- Mizaru is singing.
- **Mizaru:** Born from an egg on a mountain top,
- **Mizaru:** The punkiest monkey that ever popped-
- **Player:** What are you singing?
- **Mizaru:** It's an old monkey legend.
- **Player:** Sounds like a fun story.
- **Mizaru:** All monkey stories are fun. I'll tell it to you some time.

### At the Monkey colony, after Do No Evil


##### Without an amulet of monkeyspeak

- **Mizaru:** Ook. Ook Ah Uh! Ook Ook! Ook Ook!
- You cannot understand what he is saying.

##### With an amulet of monkeyspeak

- Mizaru and Kikazaru are singing.
- **Kikazaru:** Born from an egg on a mountain top,
- **Mizaru:** The punkiest monkey that ever popped-
- **Player:** Hey.
- **Kikazaru:** Bouncing 'round the desert in a bowler hat,
- **Mizaru:** A singer and a swinger, a wildcat!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What will you do now?
    - **Player:** What will you do now?
    - **Mizaru:** Expand our empire till it consumes the desert, then rain death and destruction upon your people.
    - **Player:** Er-
    - **Mizaru:** A thousand years of darkness awaits these lands.
    - **Player:** Gah-
    - **Mizaru:** Ha! Ha! Ha! Nah, we'll probably just catch a tan and slurp chimp ices.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I thought you said the king cursed your senses.
    - **Player:** I thought you said the king cursed your senses.
    - **Mizaru:** And you believed us?
    - **Kikazaru:** Is the human saying something stupid again?
    - **Iwazaru:** (Iwazaru nods.)
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Are you going to tell me what that monkey song is about?
    - **Player:** Are you going to tell me what that monkey song is about?
    - **Mizaru:** Maybe, some day.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Leave.
    - **Player:** Bye.
    - **Mizaru:** See ya later, crocodile.
    - **Kikazaru:** In a while, alligator.
