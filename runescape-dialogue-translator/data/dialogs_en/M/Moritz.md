- **Letter**: `M`

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Moritz:** Welcome to NovtumberFest!
- Select an option
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - **Player:** Who are you?
      - **Moritz:** I am Moritz the glassblower!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - What do I have to do here?
      - **Player:** What do I have to do here?
      - **Moritz:** It's very simple.
      - **Moritz:** Head over to the Glass Blowing Furnace and follow my lead!
      - You will receive Crafting XP for making steins.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not selected last:)*
    - How did you become a glass blower?
      - **Player:** How did you become a glass blower?
      - **Moritz:** I used to play the trumpet, but apparently I am tone deaf!
      - **Moritz:** So... I took what experience I had from that and put it to good use.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Bye.
    - **Moritz:** Keep yer wits about ye.
