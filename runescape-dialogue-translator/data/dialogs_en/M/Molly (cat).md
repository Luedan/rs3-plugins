## Dialogue Transcript


### Before 11 January

### After the beginning of 11 January


#### At Grand Tree


##### Without catspeak

- **Molly:** Meow!

##### With catspeak

- **Molly:** Hrm. What is it human? Can't you see I am busy?
- **Player:** Well, I just wondered whether you'd want to come and watch over the new oasis in Al Kharid.
- **Molly:** Tempting. I do enjoy lying around in the sun. However I couldn't possibly leave this village until I have completed my experiment.
- **Player:** Dare I ask?
- **Molly:** It's simple really. These small humanoids are fascinating and they consume fascinating things.
- **Molly:** I wish to know what it is to live like them. Since they are small and I am also small, it stands to reason that only one thing prevents me from truly understanding them.
- **Molly:** I wish to feast as they feast. In particular I crave a beverage I have heard them speak of with great relish.
- **Molly:** I wish to sample a Blurberry Special!
- **Molly:** Alas, they do not appear to understand my requests to consume one. There is too great a language barrier.
- **Molly:** But, if you were to procure one for me, I would be more inclined to visit this oasis of yours.
- **Molly:** I want one properly made by one of these gnomes as well. One made by Blurberry himself, no less!
- ((Without a premade Blurberry Special:))
  - **Player:** I'll see what I can do.
- ((With a premade Blurberry Special:))
  - **Player:** I have one right here!
  - **Molly:** Fantastic! Let me try it!
  - Give Molly the premade Blurberry Special?
    - Yes
      - (removes=premade Blurberry Special)
      - You have completed: 'Cocktails with Cats' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
      - {{Qact|Molly disappears from the scene.}}
      - **Molly:** Magnificent!
    - No
      - **Molly:** Such a cruel tease!
