# Meskhenet

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Meskhenet](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMeskhenet)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Spirits of the Elid

- **Player:** Good day to you.
- **Meskhenet:** Hello.
- **Player:** You don't look too happy.
- **Meshkenet:** True. We've not fallen on the best of times here.
- **Player:** Any way that I can help?
- **Meskehenet:** Possibly. I'd go talk to Awusah the Mayor of Nardah. He's in the big house on the east side of the town square.

### After Spirits of the Elid

- **Player:** How's life now the curse has been lifted?
- **Meskhenet:** Much better thanks to you. We're all very impressed.
