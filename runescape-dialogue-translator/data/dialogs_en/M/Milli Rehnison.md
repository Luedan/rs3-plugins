## Dialogue Transcript


### After Plague City

- **Milli Rehnison:** Have you found Elena yet?
- **Player:** Yes, she's safe at home.
- **Milli Rehnison:** I hope she comes and visits sometime.
- **Player:** Maybe.

#### While wearing full mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Milli Rehnison:** I know you Mourners are up to no good, go away!

### After Plague's End

- **Milli Rehnison:** It's so good that people can go in and out of the city now! Elena has come to visit often.
