## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to

- **Megan:** Hi! I'm Megan. Welcome to the Party Room!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: One beer please, Megan!
    - **Player:** One beer please, Megan!
    - **Megan:** Certainly [sir/ma'am]! Coming right up! That's two coins, please.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have at least two coins:)
      - You can not afford that.
      - ((Repeated in a popup:))
        - You can not afford that.
        - (continue)
      - **Megan:** I said 2 coins! You haven't got 2 coins!
      - **Player:** Sorry, I'll come back another day.
    - (Condition: If the player has at least two coins:)
      - 2 coins have been removed from your money pouch.
      - (receives=a beer)
      - **Player:** Thanks, Megan.
  - [Choice]: Can you dance, Megan?
    - **Player:** Can you dance, Megan?
    - **Megan:** Can I dance?!
    - **Megan:** CAN I dance?!!
    - **Player:** Dance with me Megan!
    - {{Qact|The player and Megan perform a series of synchronised emotes.}}
  - [Choice]: Do you have any news?
    - **Player:** Do you have any news?
    - **Megan:** Not at the moment. I've heard that the known world is expanding as new places are discovered.
    - **Megan:** These are exciting times indeed!
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Player:** Never mind.
