## Dialogue Transcript


### After The Fremennik Isles

- **Mawnis Burowgar:** It makes me proud to know that the helm of my ancestors will be worn in battle.
- **Mawnis Burowgar:** I thank you on behalf of all my kinsman, [Fremennik Name].
- ''(If the player has lost the Helm of Neitiznot:)
  - **Player:** Ah yes, about that beautiful helmet...
  - **Mawnis Burowgar:** You mean the priceless heirloom that I gave to you as a sign of my trust and gratitude?
  - **Player:** Err yes, that one. I may have mislaid it.
  - **Mawnis Burowgar:** It's a good job I have alert and loyal men who notice when something like this is left lying around and picks it up.
  - **Mawnis Burowgar:** I'm afraid I'm going to have to charge you a 50,000GP handling cost.
  - (receives=Helm of Neitiznot) (removes=50,000 coins)
  - **Mawnis Burowgar:** Please be more careful with it in future.
  - [The Burgher hands you his crown.]

### After Glorious Memories

- [Menu]: Ask about:
  - [Choice]: The Helm of Neitiznot.
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Chieftain Brundt's birthday feast.
    - **Player:** It was a marvellous feast, wasn't it?
    - **Mawnis Burowgar:** Very much so, [Fremennik Name]. Also, it's much easier to talk about what happened to Nial now that Freygerd is happy. Did you want to hear about that battle again?
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please.
        - **Player:** Yes, please.
        - (see|Glorious_Memories#Mawnis' Story)
      - [Choice]: No, thanks.
        - **Player:** No, thanks.
        - **Mawnis Burowgar:** Well, I'll tell it to you whenever you want.

### After Blood Runs Deep

- [Menu]: Ask about:
  - [Choice]: The Helm of Neitiznot.
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: The war against the daggermouths.
    - **Mawnis Burowgar:** Well done driving back those daggermouths, [Fremennik Name].
  - [Choice]: Chieftain Brundt's birthday feast.
    - (above)

### If the player is wearing the silly jester outfit

- **Mawnis Burowgar:** Ah, jester. I am no longer in need of jest.
- **Mawnis Burowgar:** If my Champion wishes to talk with me, he should dress as one.
- The Burgher gives you a wink.
