# Mod Nova (NPC)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mod Nova (NPC)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMod%20Nova%20%28NPC%29)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hi! Are you a Mod?
- **Mod Nova:** Sure am, I'm just here to check out the night sky, and this is one of the best places to view it form.
- *(If this was the player's first time talking to the JMod:)*
  - **Player:** Are you going to RuneFest?
  - **Mod Nova:** I am, it's going to be a star-studded event!
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **Player:** Awesome, have fun!
- *(If this was the player's first time talking to the JMod:)*
  - You've found one of the JMods going to RuneFest!
  - *(If the player has found all of the JMods:)*
    - You've found all of the JMods going to RuneFest and have unlocked a skill chip!
      - *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
        - You have earned a small prismatic lamp, but you don't have any space for it. Talk to Skillchipx when you have space to claim it.
        - *(Continues below.)*
      - *(If there isn't space in the player's inventory:)*
        - You have earned a small prismatic lamp. It has been added to your inventory.
        - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If the player has unlocked all the skill chips:)*
    - Congratulations! You've completed this year's RuneFest tasks for Skillchipx!
    - You have earned a medium prismatic lamp. It has been added to your inventory.
