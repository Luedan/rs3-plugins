## Dialogue Transcript

- **Merchant Vernon:** This is my patch! Find your own place to flog surface foods.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Merchant Vernon:** I'm selling 'exotic' food to these goblins: milk, eggs, bread, rice...everything from the surface fascinates them.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy something?
    - **Player:** Can I buy something?
    - **Merchant Vernon:** Oh, no. I'm only selling to the cave goblins. Besides, you're used to this food, so you wouldn't buy it for the prices I'm asking.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
