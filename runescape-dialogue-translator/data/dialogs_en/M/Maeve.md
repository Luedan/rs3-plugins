##### Without a marionette

- **Player:** How does crafting marionettes work exactly?
- **Maeve:** It's a multi-step process. You'll need to start with taking some dry fir wood from the crate in the corner.
- **Maeve:** Step one is to carefully carve the wood over at the carving bench. Once you've crafted its head, torso, arms, legs, and a handle, it'll be ready for painting.
- **Maeve:** Step two is to paint the marionette over at the painting bench. Then lastly, step three is to assemble your marionette handle and body over at the finishing bench.
- **Maeve:** Once you're done, hand any marionettes you've made over to me.
- **Player:** That's quite an involved process.
- **Maeve:** We're experts at our craft here, Player! Only the best marionettes are allowed to leave this workshop!
- **Player:** Yes, ma'am!
