## Dialogue Transcript


### Wearing a bedsheet or ectoplasm-covered bedsheet

- ((One of the following will be chosen at random:))
  - **Mick Casey:** How dare you make such a mockery!
  - **Mick Casey:** Is this your idea of humour?
  - **Mick Casey:** This conversation can continue after you remove that ridiculous outfit.

### Not wearing a bedsheet or ectoplasm-covered bedsheet

- ((If you haven't collected the 25 souls:))
  - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
    - (continue)
- **Mick Casey:** Sorry, kid, I'm in the middle of a case. Try me again some other time.
