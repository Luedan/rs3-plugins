# Mysterious ghost (Lennissa)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Mysterious ghost (Lennissa)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMysterious%20ghost%20%28Lennissa%29)
- **Letter**: `M`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Lennissa after Curse of the Empty Lord (miniquest) is completed.*
- **Player:** Hello Lennissa. Can you remind me where to find Dhalak?
- **Mysterious ghost:** Dhalak? I know not where, but he would try and make the most of his situation if he has been cursed, and find a place to lift his spirits!
- **Player:** Okay, well I'll try and find him for you then.

### Dialogue for Ghostly robes

*When speaking to Lennissa after Curse of the Empty Lord (miniquest) is completed but the player has lost their Ghostly robe bottom.*
- **Player:** Could I have that robe bottom again? I seem to have misplaced it somewhere...
- **Mysterious ghost:** Certainly, I am not sure how, but it returned to me by some magic or other.
