## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Feud

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Menaphite Thug:** What do you want?
- **Player:** Well nothing really, I'm new to town and wanted to get to know the locals.
- **Menaphite Thug:** I'm too busy for chitchat. Come back if you have some business to discuss with us.

### After The Feud

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Menaphite Thug:** Traitor foul spawn of Icthlarin. A curse on you. I should never have trusted you.
- **Player:** What can I say, you live and learn.
- **Menaphite Thug:** I will avenge my master!
- {{qact|The Menaphite attacks the player.}}

### Pickpocket

- (Condition: If the player is caught)
  - **Menaphite Thug:** {{RSChat|What do you think you're doing?}}
  - You fail to pick the Menaphite's pocket.
  - You've been stunned.

### Lure

- **Player:** Psst.  here, I want to show you something.
- (Condition: If the Menaphite is fooled:)
  - **Menaphite Thug:** What is it?
  - **Player:** It's a surprise! Follow me.
- (Condition: If the Menaphite is not fooled:)
  - **Menaphite Thug:** Go away! I'm busy.

### Knock-Out


#### If there is another Menaphite thug around

- Perhaps I shouldn't do this just yet. I think another Menaphite will see me.

#### If the player does not have a blackjack equipped

- **Player:** I need a different weapon to do this. <br/>I want to knock them out, not murder them.

#### If the player has a blackjack equipped
