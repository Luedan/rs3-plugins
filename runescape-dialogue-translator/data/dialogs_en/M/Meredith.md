# Meredith

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Meredith](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMeredith)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing the Easy Tirannwn achievements

- **Meredith:** Come back when you've completed the Easy Tirannwn Tasks - I'll be able to help you then.

### After completing the Easy Tirannwn achievements


#### Without limestone in inventory

- **Meredith:** When you have some, I'll gladly take any limestone off your hands. It will be at 50% less than you could get elsewhere though.

#### With limestone in inventory

- **Meredith:** I'll gladly take that limestone off your hands. It will be at 50% less than you could get elsewhere though.
- Would you like to sell at 50% below the GE price?
  - Yes
    - (Any unnoted limestone in the player's inventory is sold at 50% of the GE price.)
  - No
    - **Meredith:** As you wish. I'll be around if you change your mind.
