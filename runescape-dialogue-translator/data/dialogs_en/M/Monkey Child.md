### After Monkey Madness


#### First time talking to the monkey child after obtaining the talisman

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: How are you today?
    - **Player:** How are you today?
    - **Monkey Child:** I'm good, uncle! I'd quite like my toy back now...
  - [Choice]: I've lost that toy you gave me...
    - **Player:** I've lost that toy you gave me...
    - **Monkey Child:** You lost it!? What am I going to play with now?
    - The monkey begins to cry...

#### Attempting to talk to the monkey child immediately afterwards

- The monkey is too busy crying.

#### Talking to the monkey child after some time has passed, or after logging out and back in

- **Monkey Child:** I'm feeling a bit better now...
- **Player:** It's good to see you've cheered up then.
- **Monkey Child:** Yes - Aunty gave me a new toy!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: That's really sweet of her!
    - **Player:** That's really sweet of her!
  - [Choice]: Wow - can I borrow it?
    - **Player:** Wow - can I borrow it?
    - **Monkey Child:** Only if you promise to give it back...
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Ok, I promise!
        - **Player:** Ok, I promise!
        - (receives=monkey talisman)
        - [The monkey child gives you some kind of talisman.]
      - [Choice]: Actually, I don't really need it.
        - **Player:** Actually, I don't really need it.

#### Talking to the monkey child afterwards

- (above|First time talking to the monkey child after obtaining the talisman)

#### Returning a monkey talisman to him

- **Monkey Child:** Hello there, uncle!
- (removes=monkey talisman)
- **Monkey Child:** Thank you uncle - I love you!
- The monkey child gives you a hug!

#### Using an additional monkey talisman on him

- **Monkey Child:** Thank you uncle, but I don't want one!

#### Using any greegree on him

- **Monkey Child:** Hello there, uncle!
- **Monkey Child:** I don't want that - it looks really scary!

#### Using any other item on him

- **Monkey Child:** Hello there, uncle!
- **Monkey Child:** I have no use for that.
