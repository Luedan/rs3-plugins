## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]


#### Talking to Miner Magnus

- **Miner Magnus:** Hello.
  - What would you like to talk about?
    - Miner Magnus.
      - **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
      - **Miner Magnus:** I'm mining coal. It's pretty tough going.
      - **Player:** Good luck with that.
      - **Miner Magnus:** Thanks.
    - Your approval rating.
      - *If your approval rating is below 25%*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You've not been making many friends since you arrived here. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - *If your approval rating is between 25% and 49%*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You haven't really won the heart of the locals, if you see what I mean. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - *If your approval rating is between 50% and 74%*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You haven't been here for very long, but people are reasonably fond of you. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population liked you.
      - *If your approval rating is at 75% or higher*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You seem remarkably popular for a newcomer to these lands. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population liked you.

#### Trying to mine a rock

- **Miner Magnus:** Hey! These are the King's coal rocks. You'll need his approval to make use of them.

##### At 100% approval rating

- **Miner Magnus:** Don't trouble yourself further, Sir/Madam. You've already done so much!

### After [[Throne of Miscellania]]


#### Talking to Miner Magnus

- **Miner Magnus:** Good day, Your Royal Highness
  - What would you like to talk about?
    - Miner Magnus.
      - **Player:** Good day. What are you doing?
      - **Miner Magnus:** I'm mining coal. It's pretty tough going.
      - **Player:** Good luck with that.
      - **Miner Magnus:** Thank you, Your Royal Highness.
    - Your approval rating.
      - **Player:**  What do people think of me around here?
      - *If your approval rating is between 25% and 49%*
        - **Miner Magnus:** Your people are growing dissatisfied with the regime, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, only [approval rating]% of the population supported you.
      - *If your approval rating is between 50% and 74%*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You are well regarded, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population supported you.
      - *If your approval rating is at 75% or higher*
        - **Miner Magnus:** You are universally adored, Your Royal Highness. At the last poll, [approval rating]% of the population supported you.

#### Attempting to mine coal rocks


#### At below 100% approval rating

- **Miner Magnus:** Thanks!

#### At 100% approval rating

- **Miner Magnus:** Don't trouble yourself further, Your Highness, you've already done so much!

#### With full storerooms

- **Miner Magnus:** The storerooms are full, your Highness. You should talk to Advisor Ghrim to collect what we've gathered for you.
