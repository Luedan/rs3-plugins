## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Before completing the Trader Woes achievement

- **Player:** Hey there. I'm Player. I was wondering if you'd let me fish here.
- **Maya:** Good to meet you, Player. I'm sorry, but I'm busy at the moment.
- **Maya:** If you can get Nathan the banker to leave me alone, maybe I can show you around later.
- **Maya:** Lately, he's been begging me to let him use my ship for some trade venture.
- **Maya:** Not only that, but I also lost my small boat the other day.
- **Maya:** I'm so distracted that I can barely tend to the farm myself.
- **Maya:** You can find Nathan at the bank up in Wendlewick.

#### Before unlocking the fish farm

- (see|Maya Use Your Fish Farm?#Talking to Maya after (partial) completion of Trader Woes)

#### After unlocking the fish farm

- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Could you explain the fish farm again?
    - **Player:** Could you explain the fish farm again?
    - **Maya:** Certainly! First, you need to populate one or more pools with fish.
    - **Maya:** Then, fill the food hatch next to the pools with raw fish.
    - **Maya:** As a general rule, fish will eat any other fish less ferocious than themselves.
    - [The fish at the farm will eat most types of raw fish of a lower level.]
    - **Maya:** Once you push the central controls, the farming process will start.
    - **Maya:** The fish will start feeding and multiplying until they run out of space.
    - **Maya:** But that's not all! As your fish grow in number, so does their...waste.
    - **Maya:** It takes some time, but dirty pools eventually lead to algae growth and algae will kill your fish unless you remove it.
    - **Maya:** If you see some dirty water in a pool, just throw a clam at it. They are excellent water filters!
    - **Maya:** There's a crate of them at the other end of the farm. Take as many as you'd like.
    - **Maya:** If you don't manage to keep your pools clean, you can at least stuff any algae that you collect into a food hatch.
    - **Maya:** Once hte fish have had some time to feed and multiply, you can harvest the pool by fishing up all the fish that are inside.
    - **Maya:** The empty pool will be quite dirty, so just throw in another clam and you're good to rinse and repeat.
    - **Maya:** You can farm up to four pools at once, if you've repaired all of them.
    - **Maya:** If you farm all four pools at once, fish will grow even faster.
    - (end)
  - [Choice]: Which kinds of fish can you exchange?
    - **Player:** What kinds of fish can you exchange?
    - (Condition: With only tuna and lobsters unlocked:)
      - **Maya:** I can exchange tuna and lobster for bank notes and vice versa.
      - **Maya:** If you show me that you're serious about fish farming, I'll deal with some more beefy fish for you as well.
      - **Maya:** Once you catch [number] more swordfish here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange swordfish.
      - **Maya:** Once you catch [number] more sharks here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange sharks.
      - **Maya:** Once you catch [number] more giant crayfish here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange giant crayfish.
      - **Maya:** Since I also need to eat, I'll charge 10% of the value of any bank notes you bring me.
      - (end)
  - [Choice]: Could you exchange my fish?
  - [Choice]: Could you exchange my bank notes?
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Player:** Never mind.
    - (end)

### Exchange fish


#### Before completing the Trader Woes achievement

- **Maya:** I'm sorry, but I'm busy at the moment. Maybe I can help you with that later.

#### Before unlocking the fish farm

- **Maya:** I can help you with that once I've sufficiently introduced you to the art of fish farming.

#### Without suitable fish

- **Maya:** You don't seem to have any fish that I can exchange.
- **Maya:** Once you've caught 250 swordfish here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange swordfish.
- **Maya:** Once you've caught 250 sharks here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange sharks.
- **Maya:** Once you've caught 250 giant crayfish here at the fish farm, I'll let you exchange giant crayfish.
