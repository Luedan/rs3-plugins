# Menaphite guard (Sophanem Slayer Dungeon)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Menaphite guard (Sophanem Slayer Dungeon)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMenaphite%20guard%20%28Sophanem%20Slayer%20Dungeon%29)
- **Letter**: `M`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Entering the dungeon for the first time

- **Menaphite guard:** Halt there! Ahead of here lies only death.
- **Player:** What's in there?
- **Menaphite guard:** Unnatural creatures. There is a power in there that chills me just thinking about it. Just stood here, I can feel it creeping over me; it makes me just want to up and abandon my post and run back to my family.
- **Player:** It can't be that bad. Can I just take a look?
- (Condition: If players do not reach level 88 Slayer:)
  - **Menaphite guard:** You can if you wish, but from observing you, I do not feel you are sufficiently trained in the art of Slayer to survive down there.
  - [You need level 88 Slayer before you are able to harm any of the monsters in the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon.]
- **Menaphite guard:** Hmm, yes, you do look like you can handle yourself, but you can't go in there just yet. These creatures do not fall as normal beasts do, they exist on the boundary of life and death. So the priests say, anyway.
- **Menaphite guard:** There is an item, though - the feather of Ma'at. They may look like normal feathers, but they contain some magic...or perhaps a curse, I do not truly know. With a feather upon you, these creatures will fall to your blows.
- **Player:** Where can I get these feathers?
- **Menaphite guard:** There are two places. The first is from me - you didn't think they would leave me to guard without them, did you? I can sell them to you, of course, for a cost.
- **Menaphite guard:** However, if you would like to collect them yourself, I hear that the workers have been recovering them from the tombs below Menaphos. If you were able to gain access to Menaphos and its tombs, then I'm sure you will be able to find some down there.
- (continue|When talked to)

### Entering the dungeon after the first time without a feather of Ma'at

- **Menaphite guard:** Halt there! Ahead of here lies only death, especially if you don't have any feathers of Ma'at. I'll be happy to sell you some, of course, for a cost.
