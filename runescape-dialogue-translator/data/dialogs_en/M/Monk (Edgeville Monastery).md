## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Monk:** Greetings traveller.
- Select an option
  - Can you heal me? I'm injured.{{anchor|heal}}
    - **Player:** Can you heal me? I'm injured.
    - **Monk:** Ok.
    - (The monk casts a spell on the player and their life points are regenerated.)
    - You feel a little better.
  - Isn't this place built a bit out of the way?{{anchor|out-of-the-way}}
    - **Player:** Isn't this place built a bit out of the way?
    - **Monk:** We like it that way actually! We get disturbed less. We still get rather a large amount of travellers looking for sanctuary and healing here as it is!
  - How do I get further into the monastery?
    - **Player:** How do I get further into the monastery?
    - **Monk:** You'll need to talk to Abbot Langley about that. He's usually to be found walking the halls of the monastery.
