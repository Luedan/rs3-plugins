## Dialogue Transcript


### Before the hotfix of 17 January 2022

- **Medjed:** Meow.

### After the hotfix of 17 January 2022


#### At Croesus Front


##### Without catspeak

- **Medjed:** Meow.

##### With catspeak

- **Medjed:** Greetings mortal! Come to worship the great Medjed? Lord of the Underworld?
- **Player:** Who?
- **Medjed:** The insolence! You stand in the presence of he who smites! I am Medjed, Lord of the Dead!
- **Player:** Right. I don't suppose you want to come to the oasis, do you? I've got a task I need to complete.
- **Medjed:** Hrmm, the oasis. Once a place of great death and torment. I could do well there.
- **Medjed:** But I cannot leave this place, not without sufficient...compensation.
- **Player:** What do you want?
- **Medjed:** Souls! Bring me thirteen  I shall bless your oasis with my divine presence!
- ((If the player has 13 soul runes:))
  - **Player:** I have thirteen souls right here.
  - **GIVE MEDJED 13 SOUL RUNES?**
    - Yes.
      - **Medjed:** A most worthy offering.
      - (removes=13 soul runes)
      - You have completed: 'The Madness of Medjed' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
      - {{Qact|Medjed disappears.}}
      - **Medjed:** The oasis shall know fear!
    - No.
      - **Player:** Actually, I'll hold on to them for a bit.
      - **Medjed:** Do not tease me mortal! I will smite you with rays...from my eyes!
      - **Player:** Of course you will.

#### At Het's Oasis


##### Without catspeak

- **Medjed:** Meow!

##### With catspeak

- **Player:** Enjoying yourself, Medjed?
- **Medjed:** There are many mice here to smite. I shall grace this place with my divine presence a little while longer.
- **Player:** Good to hear.
