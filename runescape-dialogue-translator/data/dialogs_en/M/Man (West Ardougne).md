# Man (West Ardougne)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Man (West Ardougne)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AMan%20%28West%20Ardougne%29)
- **Letter**: `M`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Between Plague City and Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear


##### Man #1 (wearing beige shirt and brown pants)

- **Player:** Good day.
- **Man:** An outsider! Can you get me out of this hell hole?
- **Player:** Sorry, that's not what I'm here to do.

##### Man #2 (wearing rags)

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Man:** Go away. People from the outside shut us in like animals. I have nothing to say to you.

#### While wearing full Mourner gear


##### Man #1 (wearing beige shirt and brown pants)

- **Player:** Hey ya.
- **Man:** You better watch your back, Mourner!
- **Player:** Sorry, are you threatening me?
- **Man:** Just think of me next time you're in a dark alley! Because I'll be waiting for you.

##### Man #2 (wearing rags)

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Man:** Go away. You Mourners helped the people from the outside shut us in like animals. I have nothing to say to you.

### After Plague's End


#### Man #1 (wearing beige shirt and brown pants)

- **Man:** It'll be hard work rebuilding the city after the state the Mourners left it in.

#### Man #2 (wearing rags)

- **Man:** It's good to be able to go freely between East and West Ardougne again. I hadn't seen my family for years.
