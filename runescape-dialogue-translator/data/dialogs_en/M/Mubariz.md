### After the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Mubariz:** Oh, woe is me. My livelihood gone, in an instant. Literally swallowed up whole by the desert.
- **Player:** What do you mean?
- **Mubariz:** Once I was the overseer of the Duel Arena. I maintained a place where the greatest warriors in the land did battle.
- **Mubariz:** Oh, but it was glorious. The clash of steel against steel. Spell against spell. Fist against face.
- **Mubariz:** Such beautiful sounds.
- **Mubariz:** But then... but then....
- **Mubariz:** The ground just fell away and the arena plunged into the desert itself. Everything I had built there. Everything...just...gone.
- **Mubariz:** So now I stand here. Lamenting the greatest moments of my life and waiting to see what new opportunities arise.
