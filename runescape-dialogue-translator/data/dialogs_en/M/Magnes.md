## Dialogue Transcript

- **Magnes:** Welcome to the Last Leg! Make yourself comfortable.
- **Player:** The Last Leg - that's the name of your tavern here? What's the meaning?
- **Magnes:** Ha! It's named for our millipede friend outside. The miners come here after a hard day of work.
- **Magnes:** One of these days, they'll mine the last leg off that thing, but they'll come right here to celebrate.
- **Player:** Is that not an odd thing to celebrate.
- **Magnes:** It'll be a huge accomplishment. We might call it a millipede, but it's got more'n a thousand legs.
- **Magnes:** That thing's longer than what's on the surface, keep finding new sections every month.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Could I buy a drink, please?
    - **Player:** Could I buy a drink, please?
    - **Magnes:** Yes, may I interest you in a Sundown Stout? It's a local favourite.
    - **Player:** What's in it?
    - **Magnes:** I'm afraid the recipe's a secret, can't have you going and brewing your own.
    - *(Last Leg Tavern interface opens)*
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Magnes:** Careful out there, little lamb.
