## Dialogue Transcript


### Dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there.
- (Condition: Before Myreque quests:)
  - **Meiyerditch miner:** Oh, hello, you must be new here.
- (Condition: After Myreque quests:)
  - **Meiyerditch miner:** Oh, hello.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Where are we?
    - **Player:** Where are we?
    - **Meiyerditch miner:** What, you can't tell from your surroundings that you're in a mine? You must have been hit hard on the head to not notice that.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - (Condition: Before River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** We're miners, perhaps that might give you a clue! We're forced to mine for those stinking vyres. Once we've filled the cart, they sometimes let us go back to Meiyerditch, but then we get tithed. Don't know if I prefer it in here or out there!
    - (Condition: After River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** We're miners. We used to be forced to mine here for the vyres, but now we choose to. Turns out this stuff we're mining makes them less hungry. Who knew, right? So, anyway, we need a lot of this stuff.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Do you have a spare pick?|cond=If players have no pickaxe in the backpack or equipped
    - **Player:** Do you have a spare pick?
    - **Meiyerditch miner:** Sure, got one here you can use.
    - (receives=Bronze pickaxe)
    - [The miner hands you a rustic-looking pickaxe.]
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: How do you get out of here?|cond=If players have a pickaxe in the backpack or equipped
    - **Player:** How do you get out of here?
    - (Condition: Before River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** They can't keep you in here forever because they have to tithe you, so once you've filled the cart, they let you go. Takes about fifteen loads to get the cart filled.
    - (Condition: After River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** Here, I'll show you the way out...
      - {{Qact|The camera fades out and in. The player appears in the entrance to the mine.}}
  - [Choice]: What is it that you're mining?|cond=
    - **Player:** What is it that you're mining?
    - (Condition: Before River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** We're mining for daeyalt. Don't rightly know what it does - we don't ask too many questions - but I've been doing it since I was a kid. The carts keep getting filled, and they keep going along the tracks. Goes to some huge refinery place, it does - massive, like another city under the earth.
      - **Player:** What? Have you seen this place?
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** Not myself, like, but other people I know of have seen it and they wouldn't lie!
      - **Juvinate guard:** You two shut up and get back to work!
    - (Condition: After River of Blood:)
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** We're mining for daeyalt. The new leaders are saying it even turns vyres who used to be human back to normal. And for those that've always been vyres, it apparently makes them less hungry, so they don't need to feed on us anymore, or less often, or something.
      - **Meiyerditch miner:** So we're gladly mining cart-loads of this stuff now. It all helps, right?
  - [Choice]: Okay, thanks.
    - **Player:** Okay, thanks.

### Mining

Before River of Blood is complete, while mining they will say certain phrases, complaining about being enslaved. These phrases do not appear in the chat box and are only visible on the main game window. In alphabetical order the phrases they can say are:
- I can't think straight I'm so tired.
- I feel so weak I could faint.
- I hate those vyres.
- I'm rich in experience, poor in wealth.
- I'm sick of this place.
- I'm so tired I could sleep standing up.
- Ooh, my arms.
- Ooh, my back.
- What I wouldn't give for a good night's rest.
- Work, work, work, that's all I ever do.
