## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to

- **Player:** Hey there!
- **Misty:** Hey there traveller.
- [Menu]: SELECT AN OPTION
  - [Choice]: What are you doing?
    - **Player:** What are you doing?
    - **Misty:** What does it look like I'm doing? I love sitting here waiting for some of the greatest fish throughout the sea to come and visit us.
  - [Choice]: Has anything interesting happened here recently?
    - (below|Encounter Info)
  - [Choice]: What stock does the travelling merchant have today?
    - **Player:**  What stock does the travelling merchant have today?
    - **Misty:** I've heard rumours that this is the stock the travelling merchant has today.
    - (below|Travelling Merchant Stock)

### Encounter Info

- **Player:** Has anything interesting happened here recently?
- (Condition: Without an active encounter:)
  - **Misty:** No, I've been watching closely, and nothing has happened for [time].
- (Condition: With an active encounter:)
  - **Misty:** Take a look! This [Event] has been here for [time].
- (Condition: With an encounter just about to start:)
  - **Misty:** Look, something interesting looks like it is just starting to happen right now!
  - **Misty:** Take a look! This [Event] has been here for [time].
