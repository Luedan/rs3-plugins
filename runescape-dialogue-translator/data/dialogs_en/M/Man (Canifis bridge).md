## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Can I get past please?
- **Man:** Not just yet. The swamp has flooded the roads again, it's unsafe to pass.
- **Player:** So how long will the road be blocked for?
- **Man:** Who can tell? It's one of the drawbacks to living in a swamp. You get used to it after a while.
- (end)
