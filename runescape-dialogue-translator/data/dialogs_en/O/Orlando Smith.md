### At the Ancient Door


#### One week before the release of The World Wakes


##### Attempting to open the Ancient Door

- **Orlando Smith:** Hang on a minute!
- {{Qact|The player walks over to Orlando Smith.}}
- (continue|Talking to Orlando Smith)

##### Talking to Orlando Smith

- **Player:** What's going on here?
- **Orlando Smith:** Alright, mate? Exciting stuff, let me tell you!
- **Orlando Smith:** Well, actually, I can't tell you. Not yet, anyway.
- **Player:** Why not?
- **Orlando Smith:** Gotta be sure first, mate! Can't go making wild statements about world-changing discoveries without any basis.
- **Player:** Can't you even give me a hint?
- **Orlando Smith:** Hmm... Well, alright - but only because I'm so excited about it m'self.
- **Orlando Smith:** I believe, this could be...the ancient, original dwelling of Guthix. Where he used to actually live!
- **Orlando Smith:** Course, I can't be sure until I get in there and 'have a look.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can I help at all?
    - **Orlando Smith:** Tell ya what: come back in a while and I'm sure I could use an 'and.
    - **Player:** I look forward to it!
  - [Choice]: Who's Guthix?
    - **Orlando Smith:** Guthix is one of the gods! Some say the most powerful god of them all.
    - **Orlando Smith:** It's believed 'e was the first god to come to Gielinor, shapin' it in 'is vision. He brought us humans in, among others.
    - **Orlando Smith:** Belief 'as it that Guthix lives off balance; he thrives from us livin' in harmony. So, back in the Third Age, when the other gods came along and started fightin'...well...Guthix just up and banished 'em all!
    - **Orlando Smith:** Course, it could all be legend, but it's fascinating stuff for a historian.
    - **Player:** That sounds exciting! Can I help at all?
    - **Orlando Smith:** Tell ya what: come back in a while and I'm sure I could use an 'and.
    - **Player:** I look forward to it!
