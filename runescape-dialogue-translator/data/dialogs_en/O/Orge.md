- **Orge:** Me Orge. You Jayne?
- **ME ORGE. YOU JAYNE?**

      - **Orge:** Orge try again. You Jayne? Or you Jayne not? There be no 'could'.
      - (previous|initial-options)

      - **Orge:** STOP HURTING ORGE!
      - (previous|initial-options)
  - No, I'm Player.
    - **Orge:** Player dumb name. But it not your fault.
    - **Orge:** If Player see Jayne, Player tell Orge.
    - (previous|initial-options)
  - Did Jayne hug you and squeeze you and call you Orge?
    - **Orge:** Yeah! How you know?
    - (If the player has level 99 Divination:)
      - **Player:** My Divination level is: 99.
      - **Orge:** Weird flex, but okay.
    - (If the player does not have level 99 Divination:)
      - **Player:** Lucky guess.
    - **Orge:** Orge look for Jayne. Orge miss hugs.
