## Dialogue Transcript


### Before As a First Resort


#### Attempting to talk or bank through a banker or the bank booth

- **Ogress banker:** ...
- **Player:** Excuse me, can I get some service here, please?
- **Ogress banker:** GRAAAAAH! You go away, human! Me too busy with training to talk to puny thing like you.
- **Balnea:** I do apologise, sir. We're temporarily unable to meet your banking needs. We'll be open just as soon as we realise our customer experience goals and can guarantee the high standards of service that you expect from all branches of the Bank of Gielinor.
- **Player:** What did you just say to me?
- **Balnea:** We're closed until I can teach these wretched creatures some manners.
- **Player:** Ah, right. Good luck with that.

### After starting As a First Resort

- **Ogress banker:** Hi, human! You want to take or give your things?
- **Ogress banker:** Oooh! First me say you haves items ready for collection from da Grand Exchange.
- **Player:** What is this place?
- **Ogress banker:** Dis Oo'glog bank place! We friends with Bank of RuneScape. We no thumpy-thumpy customers for their stuffses.
- **Player:** That's...good to hear.
