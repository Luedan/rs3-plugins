## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When talking to Ordan at the Blast Furnace in Keldagrim.*
- **Player:** Can you un-note any of my items?
- **Ordan:** I can un-note tin, copper, iron, coal, mithril, adamantite, luminite and rune ores.

### Interaction with Dwarven miner

*Occasionally a dwarven miner will bring ores to Ordan, which will result in overhead dialogue.*
- *The Dwarven Miner will always engage the dialogue by acknowledging amount and type of ores brought, to which Ordan will always reply with the same dialogue.*
  - **Dwarven Miner:**{{RSChat|Here, [amount] ores of [type].|colour=yellow|shadow=yes}}
  - **Ordan:** {{RSChat|Thanks, I'll send them to storage.|colour=yellow|shadow=yes}}
