## Dialogue Transcript

### Before Dragon Slayer


#### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Good day to you.
- **Oziach:** Aye, 'tis a fair day my friend.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm not your friend.
    - **Player:** I'm not your friend.
    - **Oziach:** I'm surprised if you're anyone's friend with those kind of manners.
  - [Choice]: Yes, it's a very nice day.
    - **Player:** Yes, it's a very nice day.
    - **Oziach:** Aye, may the gods walk by yer side. Now leave me alone.

#### Attempting to trade with him

- **Oziach:** I ain't got nothing to sell ye, adventurer. Leave me be!

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Oziach:** The solving of this puzzle could be the key to your treasure.
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Oziach has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Oziach:** That isn't right, keep trying.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Oziach has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Oziach:** Well done, traveller.
