# Orange Flame secretary

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Orange Flame secretary](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AOrange%20Flame%20secretary)
- **Letter**: `O`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Birthright of the Dwarves

- **Player:** That's odd, isn't this the office of the Red Axe?
- **Orange Flame secretary:** No, quite clearly this is not. Can't you see the banner?
- **Player:** Hmm... it looks like some kind of orange flame.
- **Orange Flame secretary:** Very clever, yes. That's us, that is. The Orange Flame.
- **Player:** But what are you doing here?
- **Orange Flame secretary:**The Red Axe has committed treason. They have departed the city. We have clawed our way up from the rabble of the minor companies. We're now wealthy enough to trade on the Trade Octagon.
- **Player:** Need any help with that at all?
- **Orange Flame secretary:** That is a scandalous suggestion! Get out of here!

### After Birthright of the Dwarves

- **Orange Flame secretary:** I hear you finally defeated the Red Axe in their secret base! That's what a company gets for committing treason and leaving the city. The Orange Flame will make no such mistake!
