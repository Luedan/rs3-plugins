### First conversation

- **Odd Billy:** Skreeeee! An adventurer! Perfect! You must assist me!
- **Player:** What do you need?
- **Odd Billy:** I study all things with feathers. I've travelled the world to make a compendium of all bird life!
- **Odd Billy:** There's just one small problem...
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Odd Billy:** Skreeeee! Pirates! Pirates are what's wrong! I often stow away on boats to get around, but one day I lost all my notes on the Arc region in an...unwanted visit.
- **Player:** Sorry to hear that. How can I help?
- **Odd Billy:** Skreeeee! I only need you to observe any tropical birds you see and take some notes! Nothing too taxing!
- (Condition: If the player has not started watching birds:)
  - **Player:** That seems easy enough! Sure. Count me in.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player has already started watching birds:)
  - **Player:** Maybe you should take a look at this...
  - You show him your journal.
  - **Odd Billy:** Skreeeee! BRILLIANT! That's a great start!
  - **Player:** Count me in, though. I'll continue to look for more.
  - (continue)
- **Odd Billy:** Skreeeee! THIS IS THE BEST DAY EVER! Another adventurer on my list of lackeys!
- **Odd Billy:** I mean, thank you for your help! Skreeeee...
