## Dialogue Transcript


### When a nearby player does not have a mining & smithing flyer

**Orikalkum fanatic:** color=yellow

### Talking to the orikalkum fanatic

- **Player:** Wow, what is that you are wearing?
- **Orikalkum fanatic:** I'm wearing the fully upgraded orikalkum armour. You will be able to smith it yourself in the upcoming Mining and Smithing rework.
- **Orikalkum fanatic:** To smith it, first you'll need to mine some orichalcite ore, which requires 60 Mining and an appropriate pickaxe.
- **Orikalkum fanatic:** Then with 60 Smithing, you'll be able to smelt the ores into orikalkum bars, and smith orikalkum armour and weapons.
- **Player:** Oh wow! Do you have more information about the rework?
- *(If you have at least one inventory space:)*
  - {{Qact|You receive a mining & smithing flyer.}}
  - You're handed a flyer{{Sic}}
- *(If your inventory is full:)*
  - **Orikalkum fanatic:** If you've got a free space, I can give you a flyer{{Sic}}
