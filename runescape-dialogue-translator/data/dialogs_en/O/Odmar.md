# Odmar

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Odmar](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AOdmar)
- **Letter**: `O`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In Keldagrim marketplace

- **Odmar:** What to buy? What to buy? Oh, hello human.
- **Player:** Anything I can help you with?
- **Odmar:** Only if you can help me make a suggestion on what to buy.
- **Player:** I smell a quest!
- **Odmar:** No, not really. I just need some advice on what to buy, that's all.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: New clothes.
    - **Player:** You should buy some new clothes. You can't go around town wearing what you're wearing.
    - **Odmar:** You're right, this is a little drab. I shouldn't really go around Keldagrim-West wearing this, should I?
    - **Odmar:** Of course I still don't know what colour I should get. I don't want to accidentally offend the wrong company of course.
    - **Player:** Never mind.
  - [Choice]: A warhammer.
    - **Player:** You should get yourself a big mighty warhammer.
    - ''(Before The Giant Dwarf:)
      - **Odmar:** I don't think so. The warhammer shop is run by the Red Axe. I'd rather stay out of there.
      - **Player:** You don't like the Red Axe?
      - **Odmar:** They're all a lot of bullies if you ask me.
    - ''(After The Giant Dwarf and before Birthright of the Dwarves:)
      - **Odmar:** I don't think so. The warhammer shop is run by the Red Axe. I'd rather stay out of there.
      - **Player:** You don't like the Red Axe?
      - **Odmar:** They're all a lot of bullies if you ask me.
      - **Player:** But I thought they left the city?
      - **Odmar:** They may have, but I still think that shopkeeper is in league with them, banners or no.
    - ''(After Birthright of the Dwarves:)
      - **Odmar:** I don't think so. The warhammer shop used to be run by the Red Axe. I'd rather stay out of there.
  - [Choice]: A piece of rope.
    - **Player:** Get yourself a long piece of rope.
    - **Odmar:** What would I do with a rope?
    - **Player:** I don't know. I generally find a rope useful while adventuring.
    - **Odmar:** I don't do a lot of adventuring to be honest.
  - [Choice]: An oversized hat.
    - **Player:** An oversized hat. In a ridiculous colour.
    - **Odmar:** No one is selling hats here, that's not a very helpful suggestion at all.
  - [Choice]: Nothing.
    - **Player:** Nothing. You should buy absolutely nothing.
    - **Odmar:** Now that's the best idea I've heard all day.
    - **Player:** I thought so! Now why don't you give me some of the money I saved you?
    - **Odmar:** No, I don't think so. I think I'd rather buy something with my money after all.
