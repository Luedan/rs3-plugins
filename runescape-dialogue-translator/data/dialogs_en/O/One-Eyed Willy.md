# One-Eyed Willy

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:One-Eyed Willy](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AOne-Eyed%20Willy)
- **Letter**: `O`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** So that's why they call you One-Eyed Willy.
- **One-Eyed Willy:** What, just because I'm a cyclops?
- **One-Eyed Willy:** How incredibly rude of you to assume that. I've never been so insulted.
- **Player:** So, you're not One-Eyed Willy? If that's true, who is?
- **One-Eyed Willy:** The parrot.
- **Player:** You named your parrot One-Eyed Willy? Okay...
- **One-Eyed Willy:** I didn't name him - that's his name! And he's not MY parrot; I'm HIS cyclops! His seeing-eye cyclops!
- **Player:** But you're wearing an eyepatch, and the parrot isn't. I don't...
- The parrot seems to whisper-squawk something in the cyclops's ear.
- **One-Eyed Willy:** I know, right. Tourists!
- The parrot seems to whisper-squawk something foreboding in the cyclops's ear.
- **One-Eyed Willy:** No, I don't think we should tell them about The Harbinger.
- The parrot seems to whisper-squawk something rude into the cyclops's ear.
- **One-Eyed Willy:** He says you should probably go. He doesn't like you.
- **Player:** I'm sorry, I'll try not to rescue castaways in the future.
- **One-Eyed Willy:** I don't like you either.
