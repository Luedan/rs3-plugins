## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking with him

- **Ocellus:** What?
  - What's the Order of Ascension?
    - **Ocellus:** Disappointment. Failure. Worshippers of Guthix.
      - Why should I fight worshippers of Guthix?
        - **Ocellus:** Guthix refuse worship. Worship is slavery. Worse crime though. Ascended doing terrible thing. Terrible thing in Guthix's name.
      - What are they doing?
        - **Ocellus:** Death of Guthix shock Ascended. Create bad plan. Creating new god. New Guthix to worship. Body of crystal matrix. Spirit from humans. Dead humans. Still unfinished. Stop before they finish.
          - Isn't creating a new Guthix a good thing?
            - **Ocellus:** Ascended creation mindless monster. Not Guthix at all. Would have much power and no wisdom. Like god. Bring ruin.
          - (Back)
            - *(returns to previous options)*
      - Where did they come from?
        - **Ocellus:** Founded in this place. Long history. Secret. Mistake. Made to stand against worship. Now stands for worship.
      - How do I fight them?
        - **Ocellus:** Ascended use powerful magic. Taken from Guthix. Bring bows, crossbows. Pierce magic. Kill Ascended.
        - You need a ranged weapon and a Slayer level of 81 to fight Ascended.
      - (Back)
        - *(returns to previous options)*
  - Who are you?
    - **​Ocellus:** Ocellus. Virius. Guardian.
      - Guardian of what?
        - **Ocellus:** Guthix.
        - **Player:** Why weren't you with us at Cres's chamber?
        - **Ocellus:** Important work. Guthix need work. Not need bodyguards.
      - What does Virius mean?
        - **Ocellus:** My people. Virii. Not from this world.
      - Why are you here?
        - **Ocellus:** Ascended need stopping. Mock Guthix. Kill humans wastefully. Create aberration.
      - (Back)
        - *(returns to previous options)*
  - Can you give me anything to help?
    - **Ocellus:** Crossbow? Algarum thread?
      - Crossbow.
        - **Ocellus:** Legiones. Ascended leaders. Carry magical signets. Kill Legiones. Take signets. Kill lesser Ascended. Take shards. Many shards. Bring signets and shards. Bring powerful crossbow. Dragonkin-made. Fashion more powerful weapon. Very powerful.
        - Bring 1 dragon crossbow, 6 different Ascension signets and 100 Ascension shards to Ocellus and he will create an Ascension crossbow.
          - Do I have everything you need?
            - *If you have the items.*
              - **Ocellus:** Yes. Create?
                - Choose an option:
                  - Yes, create an Ascension crossbow using this dragon crossbow.
                    - **Ocellus:** Work complete.
                    - (end)
                  - I've changed my mind.
                    - (end)
            - *If you don't have the items.*
              - **Ocellus:** No.
              - (the message for creation appears)
          - (Back)
            - *(returns to previous options)*
          - That's all.
            - *(dialogue terminates)*
      - Algarum thread.
        - **Ocellus:** Algarum thread. Taken from Ascended. 500,000 coins to you.
          - What is it?
            - **Ocellus:** Found deep in ocean. Woven together. Strong thread. Traders found. Brought to surface. Ascended stole it. Making armour. Took it from them. Stopped them making. Combine with sirenic scale. Make very strong armour. Good to use with bow, crossbow.
          - I'll take it.
            - *Ocellus's Store opens if you have enough gold.*
            - *If you do not have enough gold.*
              - **Ocellus:** Not enough gold.
          - No thanks.
            - **Ocellus:** Okay then.
      - Neither.
  - That's all.
    - *(Dialogue terminates)*
