#### Before Watchtower


##### North-western gate


##### First time

- **Ogre guard:** Stop, creature! Only ogres and their friends allowed in this city. Show me a sign of companionship, like a lost relic or somefing, and you may pass. Until then, back to whence you came!

##### First time

- *If the player doesn't have a gold bar:*

  - **Ogre guard:** No gold, no passage. Get out of this city!
