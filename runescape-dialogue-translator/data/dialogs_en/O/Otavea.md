## Dialogue Transcript


### Before tier 41 of Journey to Iaia

- **Otavea** Hello Player, Lovely weather we're having today.

### During tier 41 of Journey to Iaia


#### First time

- **Otavea:** Greetings Player, what brings you here?
- **Player:** Hello Otavea. I've been sent to make sure you and your people are doing okay, under the circumstances.
- **Otavea:** That's much appreciated Player. There has been some trying times recently. But there is hope on the horizon for our people, with new land for us and new generations in the future.
- **Otavea:** Speaking of. I'm in the process of running supplies from the mainland to the island you refer to as Anachronia.
- **Otavea:** Some of my ilujanka have began integrating into life on the island. Apparently rearing some mighty creatures, and 'pruning' some of the local fungi!
- **Otavea:** I have Luke the Gurh here with me. Could you put together a nice dish for him to eat? He's been working hard and needs to keep his energy up.
- **Otavea:** Just mix this redcane together with some fresh vegetables, that should perk him up!
- **Player:** On it!
- ((Without space in backpack:))
  - {{Transcript missing}}
- ((With space in backpack:))
  - (receives=redcane)
  - **Otavea:** Thank you kindly Player. Here is some redcane!

#### Before feeding Luke

- **Otavea:** Hello Player. Have you had any success with my gurh?
- **Player:** I'm still working on it.
- ((If you have no redcane or redcane medley:))
  - ((Without space in backpack:))
    - **Otavea:** You'll be needing some redcane to make a nice medley. I'll give you some redcane once you have some space in your backpack.
  - ((With space in backpack:))
    - (receives=redcane)
    - **Otavea:** You'll be needing some redcane to make a nice medley. Here you go!
- ((If you have redcane or redcane medley:))
  - **Otavea:** Not to worry. Don't forget to use that redcane to create a tasty treat!

### After tier 41 of Journey to Iaia

- **Otavea** Thanks for the assistance Player. Luke the Gurh seems much happier now.
- **Otavea** I'll get these supplies shipped off shortly. Do pay a visit to my friends on the island if you get a chance. I hear they're creating some items of interest with a nice young lady.
