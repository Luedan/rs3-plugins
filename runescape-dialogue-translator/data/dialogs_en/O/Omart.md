## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Biohazard

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Omart:** Hello.
- **Player:** How are you?
- **Omart:** Fine thanks.

### After Biohazard and before Plague's End

- **Player:** Hello Omart.
- **Omart:**  Hello Adventurer. I'm afraid it's too risky to use the ladder again, but I believe that Edmond's working on another tunnel.

### After Plague's End

- **Omart:** Looks like you won't be needing the rope ladder any more, adventurer. I heard it was you who started the revolution and freed West Ardougne!
