# Old man

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Old man](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AOld%20man)
- **Letter**: `O`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Ghosts Ahoy

- **Player:** What are you doing on this shipwreck?
- **Old man:** Shipwreck?!? Not shipwreck, surely not! Just in port, that's all!
- **Player:** Don't be silly - half the ship's missing!
- **Old man:** No no no - the captain's just waiting for the wind to change, then we're off!
- **Player:** You mean the skeleton sitting here in this chair?
- **Old man:** You must show more respect to the Captain.

### After Ghosts Ahoy but before completing Legal Entitlement

- **Player:** How is it going?
- **Old man:** Wonderful, wonderful! Mother's coming to get me!
- ((If Vessel of the Harbinger has been completed:))
  - **Player:** I have some bad news for you...
  - **Old man:** Oh? Has she been held up?
  - **Player:** I'm sorry to say that your mother recently passed away.
  - **Old man:** ...
  - **Old man:** ...
  - **Old man:** Oh, mother. My dear old mother.
  - **Old man:** A long time may have passed since we have seen each other, but the love was still here. I missed her dearly.
  - **Player:** Her house is empty, if you wanted to move there. It's a bit nicer than this shipwreck.
  - **Old man:** I will take you up on your suggestion. The least I can do is take care of her pride and joy, now that she's gone. Thank you, Player!
  - {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. The old man has moved to Netty's old house.}}
  - Congratulations! You have compelted: 'Legal Entitlement' - Inform the Old man about the fate of his mother after 'Vessel of the Harbinger'.

### After Legal Entitlement

- **Player:** How are you?
- **Old man:** This house is much cosier than the shipwreck. It even has a skeleton I can talk to!
