## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### Normally

- **Gummy Bunny:** Oh *omnom* hi Player!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Open The Grand Eggs-change.
    - (opens=The Grand Eggs-change)
  - [Choice]: How can I get spring tokens?
    - **Player:** How can I get spring tokens?
    - **Gummy Bunny:** *omnom* Various activities around the Blooming *om* Burrow will offer spring tokens as a reward for helping out!
    - **Gummy Bunny:** You could work at the factory for a while *omnomnom*, or check out the chocolate mine, the foiling station, or the chocolate rabbit burrows.
    - **Gummy Bunny:** *omnom* Oh, and there's carrot boating and beekeeping too! *omnom*
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Bye!
    - **Player:** Bye, Gummy!
    - **Gummy Bunny:** Bye *omnom* bye!

#### With "the Egg" title before having unlocked its "Wrapped" variant

- You've unlocked the 'Wrapped' colour for the 'the Egg' title!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You've unlocked the 'Wrapped' colour for the 'the Egg' title!
  - (continue)
- **Gummy Bunny:** *omnomnom* Nice *nom* title! *omnom* I like *omnomnom* the ones we wrap *om* the treats in! *omnom*
