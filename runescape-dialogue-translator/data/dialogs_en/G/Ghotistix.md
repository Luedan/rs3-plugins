## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Ghotistix:** Greetings.
- ((Before having been asked to help with herbs by Polletix:))
  - **Ghotistix:** Before we go any further, if you're looking to help out here, you should probably go talk to Polletix over there.
  - **Player:** Great, thanks!
- ((After having been asked to help with herbs by Polletix:))
  - **SELECT AN OPTION**
    - What are you doing?
      - **Player:** What are you doing?
      - **Ghotistix:** To put it simply, I'm trying to make these pools inhabitable for fish.
      - **Ghotistix:** But for whatever reason, they're not taking to it well.
      - **Ghotistix:** They just bob lifelessly across the surface.
      - **Player:** Is there anything I can do to help?
      - **Ghotistix:** I appreciate the offer, but I'm not sure what you could do right now.
      - **Player:** Okay, bye!
    - Bye.
      - **Player:** Okay, bye!

#### Successfully
