## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Ghosts Ahoy]]


#### Without an amulet of ghostspeak

- The ghostly guards wail at you incomprehensibly, and though you cannot understand their exact words you understand their meaning - you may not pass the barriers of Port Phasmatys.

#### With an amulet of ghostspeak

- **Ghost guard:** All visitors to Port Phasmatys must pay a toll charge of 2 Ectotokens.
- Select an option
  - I would like to enter Port Phasmatys - here's 2 Ectotokens.
    - **Player:** I would like to enter Port Phasmatys - here's 2 Ectotokens.
    - (removes=2 ecto-tokens)
    - {{Qact|The player walks through the energy barrier into Port Phasmatys.}}
  - I'm not paying you Ectotokens just to go through a gate.
    - **Player:** I'm not paying you Ectotokens just to go through a gate.
    - **Ghost guard:** Sorry - it's Town Policy.
  - Where can I get Ectotokens?
    - **Player:** Where can I get Ectotokens?
    - **Ghost guard:** You need to go to the Temple and earn some. Talk to the disciples - they will tell you how.

### After [[Ghosts Ahoy]]

- **Ghost guard:** All visitors to Port Phasmatys must pay a toll charge of 2 Ectotokens. However, you have done the ghosts of our town a service that surpasses all value, so you may pass without charge.
