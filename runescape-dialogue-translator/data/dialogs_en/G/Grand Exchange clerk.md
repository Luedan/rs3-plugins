# Grand Exchange clerk

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Grand Exchange clerk](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGrand%20Exchange%20clerk)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Normal dialogue

- **Player:** Hi there.
- **Clerk:** Good day to you, [sir/madam]. How can I help?
- **Player:** I want to access the Grand Exchange please.
  - **Clerk:** Only too happy to help you, [sir/madam].
- **Player:** I want to collect my items,
  - **Clerk:** As you wish, [sir/madam].
- **Player:** Can I see a history of my offers?
  - **Clerk:** If that is your wish.
- **Player:** Can you help me with item sets?
  - **Clerk:** It would be my pleasure, [sir/madam].
- **Player:** I'm fine, actually.
  - **Clerk:** If you say so, [sir/madam].
  - *(Dialogue ends)*

### Before Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Grand Exchange clerk:** Excuse me, sir, but may I ask you to speak with Brugsen Bursen or the Gand Exchange Tutor near the entrance for a lesson. Brugsen will give an interesting, complete lesson on the Grand Exchange and the Tutor will give a smaller, plain lesson.

### In Menaphos


#### Before unlocking the Grand Exchange

- **Grand Exchange clerk:** Sorry, I require Ehsan's blessing to allow you to use the Grand Exchange here.

#### After unlocking the Grand Exchange

- **Grand Exchange clerk:** Greetings, traveller. Ehsan said that we were to allow you to use the Grand Exchange here. You must be some sort of hotshot.
- *Player has the normal dialogue options, plus an additional one:* **Player:** I want to visit the Varrock Exchange, please.
  - Yes
  - {{qact|The player is teleported to the Grand Exchange in Varrock}}
  - No
  - *(Dialogue ends)*
