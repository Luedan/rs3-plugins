# Goblin (Land of the Goblins)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Goblin (Land of the Goblins)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGoblin%20%28Land%20of%20the%20Goblins%29)
- **Letter**: `G`

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to

NPC may say any one of the following at random:
- *Dialogue 1*
  - **Goblin:** Shh! Not to talk in temple!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Sorry.
      - **Player:** Sorry.
      - **Goblin:** Shhh!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - **Player:** Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - **Goblin:** Me not know about Yu'biusk. You should ask high priest. He knows lots about stuff.
      - (end)
- *Dialogue 2*
  - **Goblin:** Praise Big High War God!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Praise Big High War God!
      - **Player:** Praise Big High War God!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
- *Dialogue 3*
  - **Goblin:** Me hear there ghost in temple crypt! No one see it, but sometimes they hear it. 'Wooo... wooo...' it say.
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Who you gonna call?
      - **Player:** Who you gonna call?
      - **Goblin:** Big High War God!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: I ain't afraid of no ghost!
      - **Player:** I ain't afraid of no ghost!
      - **Goblin:** Me not 'fraid of no ghost neither! Ha ha ha! Me strong warrior of Big High War God, not 'fraid of nothing!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
- *Dialogue 4*
  - **Goblin:** Me hear Dorgesh-tribe come back from under ground. Maybe now all tribes united, prophecy will come true! Chosen Commander come, lead all tribes to victory over world!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about the prophecy?
      - **Player:** Can you tell me about the prophecy?
      - **Goblin:** Me not know about prophecy. You should ask high priest. He knows lots about stuff.
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
- *Dialogue 5*
  - **Goblin:** Me glad me finally get to secret goblin temple! Me walk long way to get here, always in danger of humans.
  - **Goblin:** But now me here, me safe! There no humans here!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: No, of course not.
      - **Player:** No, of course not. No humans at all.
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
- *Dialogue 6*
  - **Goblin:** Me hate humans! When me leave temple, me go and kill many humans!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Why do you hate humans?
      - **Player:** Why do you hate humans?
      - **Goblin:** Humans kill my brother! We trying to attack humans, but they kill brother with swords!
      - **Player:** Why were you attacking them?
      - **Goblin:** Because they kill my uncle! Uncle go to raid human farm, but human kill him with spiky fork!
      - **Player:** Why was he raiding the farm?
      - **Goblin:** Because humans kill his friend. Me think. Me can't remember. But it must have been good reason!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
- *Dialogue 7*
  - **Goblin:** Me mighty warrior of Big High War God! Me find farmer working in field and me attack! He so scared he run off! Then me trample on crops! This deeds of great warrior of Big High War God!
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Well done.
      - **Player:** Well done.
      - **Goblin:** Big High War God smile on me!
      - (end)
    - [Choice]: Can you tell me about Yu'biusk?
      - (above)
