## Dialogue Transcript


### Dialogue

- **Gruva Patrull:** Ho! Outlander.
- **Player:** What's down the scary-looking staircase?
- **Gruva Patrull:** These are the stairs down to the mining caves. There are rich veins of many types down there, and miners too. Though be careful; some of the trolls occasionally sneak into the far end of the cave.
- **Player:** Thanks. I'll look out for them.
