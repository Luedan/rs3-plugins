## Dialogue Transcript


### In the King's Axe Inn in Keldagrim

- **Gauss:** I say, I say, a toast to this fine human!
- **Player:** What? I mean, err... why?
- **Gauss:** Why not!
- (Condition: With no suitable beverage in the backpack:)
  - **Player:** I don't have anything suitable to toast with.
  - **Gauss:** Then go get something from the bar!
- (Condition: With a beer or dwarven stout in the backpack:)
  - **Player:** Why not indeed! Cheers!
  - **Gauss:** Cheers!
  - {{Qact|The player and Gauss toast each other.}}
  - (removes=beer or dwarven stout)
  - (receives=beer glass)
- (Condition: With a jug of wine and no beer or dwarven stout in the backpack:)
  - **Player:** Well, I have this jug of wine...
  - **Gauss:** No no, get a pint of beer, jugs of wine aren't good to toast with! Wine stains are terribly hard to wash out of a beard.
