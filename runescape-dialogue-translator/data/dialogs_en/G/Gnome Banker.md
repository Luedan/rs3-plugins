## Dialogue Transcript

### Normal dialogue

- **Gnome Banker:** Good day. How may I help you?
  - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - *(Bank account opens)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(PIN settings open)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to see my collection box.
    - *(Collection box opens)*
  - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Gnome Banker:** This is a branch of the Bank of Gielinor. We have branches in many towns.
      - **Player:** And what do you do?
        - **Gnome Banker:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - **Player:** Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Gnome Banker:** Yes we did, but people kept on coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.

### If one has items waiting for them in the Grand Exchange

- **Gnome Banker:** Good day. How may I help you?
- **Gnome Banker:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
