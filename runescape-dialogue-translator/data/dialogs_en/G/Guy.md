### After completing Guys and Dolls

- **Player:** How're those coloured logs working out for ya?
- **Guy:** The variety of colours is doing wonders for my pale complexion!
- **Player:** I can see that...through you. So, what can I do here to help?
- **Guy:** Well, you were always free to bring and burn your own logs. You can get spooky tokens and other Halloween stuff while doing so, because I'm generous like that.
- **Guy:** But seeing how you helped me out, you are also welcome to make use of Dolly's coloured logs too, so you don't need to bring your own.
- **Guy:** I've placed baskets containing each of the five different log colours around the edges of the bonfire. Burn whichever you like!
- **Guy:** Though, if you move around to match the logs you're burning to the current colour of the bonfire, it'll be more rewarding.
- **Player:** Right, so when the bonfire changes colour, I move to burn the logs that match that colour. Well, that sounds simple enough. Thanks, Guy!
