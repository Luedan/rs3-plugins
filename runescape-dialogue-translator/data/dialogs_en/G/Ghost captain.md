# Ghost captain

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Ghost captain](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGhost%20captain)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### At Port Phasmatys


#### Without a ghostspeak amulet

- **Ghost captain:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.

#### With a ghostspeak amulet

- **Player:** Where do you sail to, in such a small boat?
- **Ghost captain:** I can take you to Dragontooth Island, which is a most pleasant island in the sea between Morytania and the lands of the east.
- **Player:** Pleasant? Around here? How much does that cost, then?
- **Ghost captain:** I charge no fee. I can't put a price on feeling the sea breeze in my hair.
- **Player:** But you're a... Never mind.
- Select an option
  - Please take me to Dragontooth Island.
    - {{Qact|Screen fade out/in. The player arrives at Dragontooth Island.}}
    - After a long boat trip you arrive at Dragontooth Island...
  - Maybe I'll go later.
    - (end)

### At Dragontooth Island

- Select an option
  - Can you take me back to Phasmatys, now?
    - **Player:** Can you take me back to Phasmatys, now?
    - *(If the player cannot understand ghosts:)*
      - The ghost sailor wails at you incomprehensibly, and points at the boat, nodding his ghostly head.
      - (continue)
    - *(If the player can understand ghosts:)*
      - **Ghost captain:** Yes, climb in.
      - (continue)
    - {{Qact|Screen fade out/in. The player arrives at Port Phasmatys.}}
    - After a long boat trip you return to Port Phasmatys...
  - There isn't much on this island, is there?
    - **Player:** There isn't much on this island, is there?
    - *(If the player cannot understand ghosts:)*
      - **Ghost captain:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
      - (continue)
    - *(If the player can understand ghosts:)*
      - **Ghost captain:** Beautiful, isn't it?
      - (continue)
    - **Player:** If you say so.
