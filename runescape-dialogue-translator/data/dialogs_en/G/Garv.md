# Garv

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Garv](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGarv)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Heroes' Quest

- **Garv:** Hello. What do you want?
- **Player:** Can I go in there?
- **Garv:** No. In there is private.

#### Attempting to enter through the door

- **Garv:** Oi! Where do you think you’re going, pal?
- **Player:** Can I go in there?
- **Garv:** No. In there is private.

### After Heroes' Quest

- **Garv:** Hello. What do you want?
- **Player:** I want for nothing!
- **Garv:** You're one of a very lucky few, then.
