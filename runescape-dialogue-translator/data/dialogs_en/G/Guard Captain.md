## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Talking to him or using a beer on him

- **Player:** Excuse me...
- **Guard Captain:** I don' need a hand drinkin me beer, go 'way!
- **Player:** But...
- **Guard Captain:** Talk to tha' hand coz thish face ain't lishtnin'!

#### Using a non-beer beverage on him

<!-- Such as dragon bitter or greenman's ale -->
- **Guard Captain:** Yeeeuuuch! I hatesh that shtuff, jusht bring ush a beer! Mmmmm beer!
