## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Hazeel Cult

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Guard:** Hello. If you've come to admire the Carnillean family home, I must warn you not to cause any trouble.
- **Guard:** Due to recent criminal activities against such a prominent member of Ardougnian politics, I have been sent here on special guard duty to ensure the Carnilleans' security.
- **Guard:** Anyone caught interfering with the person or possessions of said Carnilleans will be taken to jail so quickly that their feet won't touch the floor!
- **Player:** Well, I wasn't actually planning on doing anything like that...
- **Guard:** Glad to hear it. Let's keep it that way, hmmm?
