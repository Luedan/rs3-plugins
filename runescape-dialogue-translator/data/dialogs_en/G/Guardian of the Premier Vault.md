- *If not a Gold Premier Club 2018 member:*
  - **Guardian of the Vault:** I'm afraid you need to be a 2018 Gold Premier Club Member to make use of anything I find though.
- *If a Gold Premier Club 2018 member:*
  - **Guardian of the Vault:** As a 2018 Gold Premier Club Member I'm happy for you to make use of them.
