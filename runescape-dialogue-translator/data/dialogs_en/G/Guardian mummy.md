### Recharge Sceptre


#### Without a partially depleted sceptre


##### Without a fully charged sceptre

- **Guardian mummy:** You don't have any sceptres to recharge.

##### With a fully charged sceptre

- **Guardian mummy:** Your Sceptre is already fully charged, are you trying to waste my time?

#### With a partially depleted sceptre


##### Without enough of the selected artefact to recharge the sceptre or if the sceptre is too close to maximum charges to recharge with the selected artefact

- **Guardian mummy:** You do not have anything that I can use to recharge the Sceptre. You have displeased the king.

##### With enough of the selected artefact to recharge the sceptre and if the sceptre is not too close to maximum charges to recharge with the selected artefact

- **Guardian mummy:** I have added [X] charges to the Sceptre. It now has [X] charges. Now, begone.
