## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Geoff:** Geoff's busy.
- What do you do here?
  - **Player:** What do you do here?
  - **Geoff:** Fletch.
  - **Player:** Anything else?
  - **Geoff:** Sit.
  - **Player:** Uh-huh.
  - **Geoff:** Think.
  - **Player:** Yeah.
  - **Geoff:** Plot my revenge...
  - **Player:** What was that last one?
  - **Geoff:** Think.
  - **Player:** Oh, okay.
- Do you always talk about yourself in the third person?
  - **Player:** Do you always talk about yourself in the third person?
  - **Geoff:** Nope.
  - **Player:** Fair enough.
  - **Geoff:** So, what do you want with Geoff?
  - **Player:** You did it again!
  - **Geoff:** Just because Geoff is talking like this at the moment, doesn't mean he does it all the time.
  - **Player:** But you do it all the time!
  - **Geoff:** You're just inferring that from Geoff's predilection for a particular perspective.
  - **Player:** My brain hurts.
- What do you think of Sparky?
  - **Player:** What do you think of Sparky?
  - **Geoff:** Poor little fella.
  - **Player:** What happened?
  - **Geoff:** It was an accident at the Ranging Guild. Shot down. Shot down in the prime of his... ...life. Waaaaaaa!
  - **Player:** Steady on now-
  - **Geoff:** Waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!
  - **Player:** Stiff upper lip, old chap.
  - **Geoff:** Waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!
  - **Player:** Well...er...have a nice day.
- Why are you so mopey?
  - **Player:** Why are you so mopey?
  - **Geoff:** You think Geoff's mopey?
  - **Player:** Yeah, why so sad?
  - **Geoff:** Maybe Geoff wants to be mopey. Maybe Geoff wants to be sad.
  - **Player:** But why not be happy like me?
  - **Geoff:** Oh, you think you're happy, but are you really happy?
  - **Player:** Yup, pretty sure!
  - **Geoff:** Oh, you think you're sure, but are you really sure?
  - **Player:** Erm, I think I have to go now.
  - **Geoff:** Oh, you think you have to go, but do you really have to go?
  - **Player:** Yes!
