## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Without an amulet of ghostspeak

- **Ghost shopkeeper:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.

#### With an amulet of ghostspeak

- **Ghost shopkeeper:** Can I help you at all?
- Select an option
  - Yes please. What are you selling?
    - (opens=Port Phasmatys General Store)
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
