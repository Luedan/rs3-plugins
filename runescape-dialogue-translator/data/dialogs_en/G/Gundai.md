## Dialogue Transcript


### Next to Gundai

- **Player:** Hello, what are you doing out here?
- **Gundai:** I'm a banker, the only one around these dangerous parts.
- ((If the player has items to collect:))
  - **Gundai:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- **WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO SAY?**
  - I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - (opens=Bank PIN)
  - I'd like to see my Collection box.
    - (opens=Grand Exchange collection box)
  - Well, now I know.
    - **Player:** Well, now I know.
    - **Gundai:** Knowledge is power, my friend.

### Two paces away from Gundai

- **Gundai:** Good day. How may I help you?
- ((If the player has items to collect:))
  - **Gundai:** Before we go any further, I should inform you that you have items ready for collection from the Grand Exchange.
  - (continue)
- What would you like to say?
  - I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - (opens=Bank PIN)
  - I'd like to see my collection box.
    - (opens=Grand Exchange collection box)
  - What is this place?
    - **Gundai:** This is a branch of the Bank of RuneScape. We have branches in many towns.
    - What would you like to say?
      - And what do you do?
        - **Gundai:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Gundai:** Yes we did, but people kept on coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.
