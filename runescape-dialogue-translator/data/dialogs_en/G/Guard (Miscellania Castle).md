# Guard (Miscellania Castle)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Guard (Miscellania Castle)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGuard%20%28Miscellania%20Castle%29)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]


#### Before completing [[Heroes' Quest]] or first time after completing it

- **Guard:** Halt! Who goes there?
- **Player:** My name is [Fremennik name], will you not let me through?
- **Guard:** I'm afraid the King won't give an audience to anyone except a worthy hero.
- *(If the player has not completed Heroes' Quest:)*
  - **Guard:** If you become a member of the Heroes' Guild, he will definitely grant you an audience.
- *(If the player has completed Heroes' Quest:)*
  - **Player:** I am a member of the Heroes' Guild; does that not show my worth?
  - **Guard:** Then you may pass.

#### Subsequent times, after completing Heroes' Quest

- **Guard:** Welcome back, Sir.

### After [[Throne of Miscellania]]

- **Guard:** Welcome back, Your Royal Highness.
