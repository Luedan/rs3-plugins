# Gnoeals

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Gnoeals](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGnoeals)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

Occasionally when being attacked by a Gnoeals, the player will be given one of the following in the chatbox:
- Suddenly your boots feel tight.
- You feel a little more...hairy.
- Your palms are itching.
- You feel your hands getting bigger.
- It's hard to keep your back straight.
- Have your hands always been this big?
