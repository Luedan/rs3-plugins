# Goblin musician

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Goblin musician](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGoblin%20musician)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Goblin musician:** Me Thump-Thump. Me make thump-thumps with thump-thump drum. Other goblins listen.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Can I ask you some questions about resting?
    - [Menu]: Can I ask you some questions about resting?
      - [Choice]: How does resting work?
        - **Player:** So how does resting work?
        - **Goblin musician:** You stoopid. Goblin sit down, goblin rest, goblin feel better.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: What's special about resting by a musician?
        - **Player:** What's special about resting by a musician?
        - **Goblin musician:** Drumming good! Make you feel better, boom booms make you run longer!
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Can you summarise the effects for me?
        - **Player:** Can you summarise the effects for me?
        - **Goblin musician:** Wot? You sit down, you rest. Listen to Thump-Thump is better.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: That's all for now.
        - (below)
  - [Choice]: That's all for now.
    - **Player:** That's all for now.
    - **Goblin musician:** You listen to boom boom. Good!
