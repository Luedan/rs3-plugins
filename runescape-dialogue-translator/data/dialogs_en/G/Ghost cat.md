# Ghost cat

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Ghost cat](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGhost%20cat)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet


#### Without catspeak ability

- **Ghost cat:** {{RSChat|*meow*}}
- The cat seems interested, but confused. You don't understand this particular cat.

#### With catspeak ability

- **Ghost cat:** {{RSChat|*purr*}}
- (Condition: Only on first time:)
  - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - 25 souls fly over to power the vessel.
    - (continue)
  - The cat blinks at you, and you at it, as a wave of shared understanding and familiarity hits you both.<br>Jack, the ghost cat, is appeased and lends his support.

### Using a raw or cooked ghostly sole

- (removes=raw ghostly sole or ghostly sole)
- **Ghost cat:** {{RSChat|*purr*}}
- You feed the cat a ghostly sole. He seems to enjoy it.
- (Condition: Only on first time:)
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Sole to Soul' - Feed the ghost cat their favourite fish.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If Remy is unlocked:)
  - You have unlocked the pet: Remy.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - You have unlocked the pet: Remy.
    - (continue)
  - You can activate this via the 'Pets' tab in the 'Customisations' interface.
  - The ghostly sole seems to dislodge the cat's dinner from last night, as he choked up a ghostly rat.
