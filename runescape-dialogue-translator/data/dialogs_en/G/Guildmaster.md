# Guildmaster

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Guildmaster](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGuildmaster)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Entering the guild


#### Attempting to enter without 33 Quest Points

- **Guildmaster:** You have not proved yourself worthy to enter here yet.
- The door won't open - you need at least 33 Quest Points.

#### Entering with 33 Quest Points

- **Guildmaster:** Greetings bold adventurer. Welcome to the guild of Champions.

### In the guild


#### Before Dragon Slayer

- **Guildmaster:** Greetings!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Guildmaster:** This is the Champions' Guild. Only adventurers who have proved themselves worthy by gaining influence from quests are allowed in here.
  - [Choice]: Can I have a quest?
    - (see|Dragon Slayer)

#### After Dragon Slayer

- **Guildmaster:** Greetings!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: Can I have another key to Melzar's Maze?
    - **Player:**  Can I have another key to Melzar's Maze?
    - (Condition: If the player does not have the key:)
      - **Guildmaster:** Of course! Here you go.
      - (receives=Maze key)
    - (Condition: If the player has the key in their backpack or in bank:)
      - **Guildmaster:** I think you've already got a key. Maybe you should check your bank.
    - (Condition: If the player has the key in their steel key ring:)
      - **Guildmaster:** I think you've already got a key. Maybe you should check your key ring.
