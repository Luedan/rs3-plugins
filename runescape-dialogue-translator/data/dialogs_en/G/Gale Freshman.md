## Dialogue Transcript


### On a normal world

- **Gale Freshman:** Hello there, Player. Can I help you with anything?
- What do you want to talk about?
  - What's going on?
    - ((Before the noon of 26 September 2022:))
      - **Gale Freshman:** From September 26, 12:00 Gielinor time, adventurers will be able to venture into the Fresh Start Worlds, where everything is new again, starting from nothing!
      - **Gale Freshman:** If you would like to join them, you should create a new account once the event has begun, which will give you the choice to become a Fresh Start Worlds challenger.
      - (continue)
    - ((After the noon of 26 September 2022:))
      - **Gale Freshman:** Currently there are adventurers out there on Fresh Start Worlds, where everything is new again, starting from nothing!
      - **Gale Freshman:** Unfortunately for you, only new adventurers can go forth and experience it.
      - (continue)
    - **Gale Freshman:** On these worlds adventurers will experience the game at an accelerated rate, as these worlds will only exist for a couple of months.
    - **Gale Freshman:** They will also gain access to other tools to help them reach new heights as they progress! Like this armour I am wearing, which will improve as I gain experience.
    - **Gale Freshman:** As well as other things, such as increased harvests from resources, speedy access to auras, increased life and prayer points and many more.
    - **Gale Freshman:** There will also be new hiscores and world firsts to race for during the first eight weeks, with prizes for those who get there first!
    - **Gale Freshman:** Those who reach a world first or a top spot in the hiscores at the end of these eight weeks will receive a fresh title and an exclusive tradeable halo.
    - **Player:** That sounds great!
    - **Gale Freshman:** There's more!
    - **Gale Freshman:** It is also possible to unlock inverted skillcapes for any skill whose cape they unlock whilst on these Fresh Start Worlds.
    - *(Pictured: an inverted Archaeology master cape)* They reveal a different yet familiar skillcape.
    - **Player:** Interesting. Is there anything else?
    - **Gale Freshman:** There are some additional fresh-looking rewards there for adventurers to earn, including halo-wearing skilling pets and a halo which everyone on Fresh Start Worlds can earn to commemorate their achievements...
    - **Gale Freshman:** ...or sell for their efforts.
    - **Gale Freshman:** So there is plenty for them to be getting on with.
    - **Gale Freshman:** Can I help you with anything else?
    - (previous)
  - Nothing.
    - **Player:** Nothing, I was just leaving.

#### Talk to

- **Gale Freshman:** Hello there, Player. Can I help you with anything?
  - What's going on?
    - **Gale Freshman:** We are on a Fresh Start World. Here everything is new again, starting from nothing. The world is there for the taking by adventurers such as yourself!
    - **Gale Freshman:** On these worlds adventurers will experience the game at an accelerated rate, as these worlds will only exist for a couple of months.
    - **Gale Freshman:** They will also gain access to other tools to help them reach new heights as they progress! Like this armour I am wearing, which will improve as I gain experience.
    - **Gale Freshman:** As well as other things, such as increased harvests from resources, speedy access to auras, increased life and prayer points and many more.
    - **Gale Freshman:** There will also be new hiscores and world firsts to race for during the first eight weeks, with prizes for those who get there first!
    - **Gale Freshman:** Those who reach a world first or a top spot in the hiscores at the end of these eight weeks will receive a fresh title and an exclusive tradeable halo.
    - **Player:** That sounds great!
    - **Gale Freshman:** There's more!
    - **Gale Freshman:** It is also possible to unlock inverted skillcapes for any skill whose cape they unlock whilst on these Fresh Start Worlds.
    - They reveal a different yet familiar skillcape.
    - **Player:** Interesting. Is there anything else?
    - **Gale Freshman:** There are some additional fresh-looking rewards there for adventurers to earn, including halo-wearing skilling pets and a halo which everyone on Fresh Start Worlds can earn to commemorate their achievements...
    - **Gale Freshman:** ...or sell for their efforts.
    - **Gale Freshman:** So there is plenty for you to be getting on with. But you shouldn't dawdle - the worlds won't be around forever!
    - **Player:** Sounds like it! I should probably get to it!
    - **Gale Freshman:** Can I help you with anything else?
    - (previous)
  - Can I view the Fresh Start Worlds Reward Shop?
    - (The Fresh Start World Reward Shop opens)
  - Can I view the event information?
    - (The event overview interface opens)
  - I can help you leave these worlds. Are you sure? You won't be able to come back once you leave.
    - Yes. I want to leave.
    - No. I want to stay.
      - **Gale Freshman:** Excellent. Feel free to ask again if you change your mind.
      - **Gale Freshman:** Can I help you with anything else?
      - (previous)
  - Nothing.
    - **Player:** Nothing, I was just leaving.
    - (end)
