# Guard (Etceteria)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Guard (Etceteria)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGuard%20%28Etceteria%29)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Royal Trouble

- **Player:** What are you guarding here?
- **Guard:** The area under the palace is going to be excavated soon, and I'm guarding the entrance to it.
- **Player:** Oh, I see. Can I go down there?
- **Guard:** Of course not! There's not even a rope to let you down there. You'd break both your legs if you tried it.

### After Royal Trouble

- **Guard:** I'm surprised you managed to get out of there without breaking any bones.
- **Guard:** Still, the Queen says it should be safe, and I'm to let you go back down if you want.
