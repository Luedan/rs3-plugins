# Ghost banker

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Ghost banker](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGhost%20banker)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Ghost Banker:** Good day. How may I help you?
- Select an option
  - **Player:** I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - *(Will open your bank.)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - *(Will open you bank pin settings.)*
  - **Player:** I'd like to see my collection box.
    - *(Will open you collection box.)*
  - **Player:** What is this place?
    - **Ghost Banker:** This is a branch of the Bank of Runescape. We have branches in many towns.
    - Select an option
      - **Player:** And what do you do?
        - **Ghost Banker:** We will look after your items and money for you. Leave your valuables with us if you want to keep them safe.
      - **Player:** Didn't you used to be called the Bank of Varrock?
        - **Ghost Banker:** Yes we did, but people kept coming into our branches outside of Varrock and telling us that our signs were wrong. They acted as if we didn't know what town we were in or something.
