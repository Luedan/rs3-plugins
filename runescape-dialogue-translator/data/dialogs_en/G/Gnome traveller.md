# Gnome traveller

- **Letter**: `G`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Speaking to the male gnome traveller

- **Player:** Hello there! What're you doing in the city?
- **Gnome traveller (male):**  Why do you want to know?
- **Player:** I was just wondering. I thought this was a dwarven city, not a gnome city.
- **Gnome traveller (male):**  It's not a human city either, but you're here all the same.
- **Player:** Fair point.
- **Gnome traveller (male):**  Anyway, we're here to do some business with one of the companies of the Consortium. Getting supplies for our people back at, er, the Grand Tree.
- **Player:** What kind of special supplies do the dwarves have that you need then?
- **Gnome traveller (male):**  Oh, nothing special, nothing special I assure you... it's just that the dwarves have everything in large supplies.

### Speaking to the female gnome traveller

- **Player:** Hello there mister gnome!
- **Gnome traveller (female):**  Miss gnome to you, thank you.
- **Player:** Oh, sorry about that... So what are you doing in fair Keldagrim?
- **Gnome traveller (female):**  Fair? Hardly...
- **Gnome traveller (female):**  We are the companions of the leader of our expedition. We have come here to bargain for much needed supplies for our people.
- **Player:** Oh yes, where are you from?
- **Gnome traveller (female):**  Ehm... from Tree Gnome Village.
- **Gnome traveller (male):**  The Grand Tree!
- **Gnome traveller (female):** Oh yes, sorry, that's what I meant to say.
