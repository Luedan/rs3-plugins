# Gnome woman

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Gnome woman](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGnome%20woman)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Option 1

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome woman:** Hello adventurer. Here are some wise words:
- **Player:** OK.
- **Gnome woman:** Happiness is inward and not outward. So it does not depend on what we have but on what we are!

### Option 2

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome woman:** Hi. I've never seen so many humans in my life.
- **Player:** I've never seen so many gnomes!
- **Gnome woman:** So we're both learning.

### Option 3

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome woman:** Hello traveller. Are you eating properly? You look tired.
- **Player:** I think so.
- **Gnome woman:** Here, get this worm down you. It'll do you the world of good.
- The gnome gives you a worm.
- **Player:** Thanks!

### Option 4

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome woman:** Well good day to you kind sir. Are you new to these parts?
- **Player:** Kind of.
- **Gnome woman:** Well if you're looking for a good night out: Blurberry's cocktail bar is great!

### Option 5

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome woman:** Some people grumble because roses have thorns. I'm thankful that thorns have roses!
- **Player:** Good attitude!

### Option 6

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Player:** How are you?
- **Gnome woman:** Not bad, a little worn out.
- **Player:** Maybe you should have a lie down.
- **Gnome woman:** With three kids to feed I've no time for naps!
- **Player:** Sounds like hard work!
- **Gnome woman:** It is but they're worth it.
