# Grace (2017 Halloween event)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Grace (2017 Halloween event)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGrace%20%282017%20Halloween%20event%29)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to

- Select an option
  - Where were you on the full moon?
    - **Grace:** I was over at the Party Room. I had this amazing idea for an evening of song, dance and delicious spicy food.
    - **Grace:** I called it 'Curry-OK' I really think it'll catch on.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Did you see anything suspicious?
    - **Grace:** I heard a strange sound coming from the castle - a clanging noise followed by a high pitched shriek.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Who do you suspect of being the werewolf?
    - **Grace:** Jamie is super angry all the time. If that doesn't scream werewolf, I don't know what does!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - [Accuse Grace of being the werewolf.]
    - **Player:** Grace! You cannot hide your true nature from me. I know you're a werewolf!
    - *(Grace dies.)*
    - **Player:** Ah...oops. Guess I was wrong.
  - Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Goodbye.
    - **Grace:** Goodbye.

### When accused

- **Player:** Grace! You cannot hide your true nature from me. I know you're a werewolf!
  - *Grace dies*
    - **Player:** Ah...oops. Guess I was wrong.
