### Standard dialogue at Anachronia base camp

- [Menu]: Select An Option
  - [Choice]: Manage base camp.
    - (Base Camp Management opens.))
  - [Choice]: Archaeology collection.
    - **Player:** What are you collecting?
    - ((Before the collection is completed.))
      - **Giles:** I've heard that some dragonkin items have been found on the island, I'm looking for a ceremonial dragonkin device and tablet.
      - ((Archaeology collection interface opens.))
    - ((After the collection is completed:))
      - **Giles:** I'm not collecting anything else at the moment. You have already given me everything I was looking for.
      - ((Archaeology collection interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: Tell me about yourself.
    - **Giles:** Ah, there's really not much to tell...
    - **Giles:** Let's see. I am a senior accountant for the Varrock museum. While I trained and worked at the palace, I showed a keen interest in history and was transferred there to assist with the running of their operations.
    - **Giles:** Lots of big plans there, but not a lot of sense when it comes to money. Luckily, I helped them manage their resources a bit better.
    - **Player:** Is that why the boat that brought us here took so long to build?
    - **Giles:** Ah. Haha. Yes, that was a project started before I took over. I didn't see the opportunity in it so left a skeleton crew on it while the museum focused on different areas of interest.
    - **Giles:** Of course, then certain events took place which changed priorities. Suddenly building the boat was the most important thing and here we are.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Reclaim.
    - **Player:** Do you have anything for me to reclaim?
    - ((If there is nothing to reclaim or if Desperate Measures hasn't been completed:))
      - **Giles:** You have nothing to reclaim from me at the moment.
    - ((Before creating the cosmic accumulator:))
      - ((If the cosmic focus has been lost:))
        - **Giles:** I found this cosmic focus, I think it's yours.
        - ((Without space in backpack:))
          - **Giles:** You don't seem to have any inventory space for this at the moment.
        - ((With space in backpack:))
          - (receives=cosmic focus)
          - Giles kindly hands over the Cosmic focus.
      - ((If the cosmic pyramid has been lost:))
        - **Giles:** I see you have lost the cosmic pyramid I gave to you! Luckily I have another you can have.
        - ((Without space in backpack:))
          - **Giles:** You don't seem to have any inventory space for this at the moment.
        - ((With space in backpack:))
          - (receives=cosmic pyramid)
          - Giles kindly hands over the Cosmic pyramid.
    - ((After creating the cosmic accumulator:))
      - **Giles:** I found this item that looks like the cosmic pyramid I gave you, I assumed it was yours.
      - ((Without space in backpack:))
        - **Giles:** You don't seem to have any inventory space for this at the moment.
      - ((With space in backpack:))
        - (receives=cosmic accumulator)
        - Giles kindly hands over the Cosmic accumulator.
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Giles:** I will ensure the base camp perseveres while you are away.
