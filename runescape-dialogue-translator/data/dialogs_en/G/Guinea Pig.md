### After the beginning of 14 January


#### At Falador Park


##### Without catspeak

- **Guinea Pig:** Squeak!

##### With catspeak

- **Guinea Pig:** Squeak!
- **Player:** That's an odd noise for a cat to make. Shouldn't you be meowing?
- **Guinea Pig:** HA! Shows what you know! I'm not a cat. I'm a guinea pig!
- **Player:** How did you come to that conclusion?
- **Guinea Pig:** The facts are very clear. I am called Guinea Pig, therefore I must BE a guinea pig!
- **Guinea Pig:** It's really very simple. I'm surprised you're struggling with it. You must be quite slow.
- **Player:** How about this? If I can convince you you're a cat, will you come and look after the oasis for me?
- **Guinea Pig:** Haha! If you can prove the impossible, then sure!
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - Look at your tail!
    - **Player:** Look at your tail! It's so long and swishy. Guinea pig tails are short and static.
    - **Guinea Pig:** Ha! Nonsense. I was just born with a longer tail than most. That doesn't prove anything!
    - (continue)
  - Look at your whiskers!
    - **Player:** Look at your whiskers! They're much longer than any guinea pig's whiskers!
    - **Guinea Pig:** Thank you! I'm glad you noticed. I've taken extra care to groom these to be superb examples of what a guinea pig can strive for. With whiskers, that is.
    - **Guinea Pig:** But these hardly prove I'm not a guinea pig!
    - (continue)
  - You're too tall!
    - **Player:** You're too talk to be a guinea pig! Their legs are very short and stumpy, yours are long and lithe.
    - **Guinea Pig:** When I was a guinea piglet-
    - **Player:** You've even got that wrong, baby guinea pigs are called pups!
    - Guinea Pig ignores your logic.
    - **Guinea Pig:** When I was a guinea piglet, I slipped on some ice and skidded over a cliff edge. I held on with all my paws for dear life.
    - **Guinea Pig:** But my guinea pig body was so large that it dragged me over the ledge, slowly stretching my legs out.
    - **Player:** You know that makes no sense at all right?
    - **Guinea Pig:** Your rude disbelief of my childhood tragedy doesn't count as proof.
    - (continue)
- **Player:** Okay, if you're a guinea pig how come...
- **SELECT AN OPTION**
  - Predators aren't attacking you!
    - **Player:** Predators aren't attacking you! If you were a guinea pig, something would be trying to eat you right now, wouldn't it?
    - **Guinea Pig:** Of course not! Guinea pigs are the top of the food chain! Everyone knows that.
    - **Player:** How... How can you be so wrong?
    - (continue)
  - I can speak to you!
    - (continue)
  - Where are the other guinea pigs?
    - **Player:** Where are the other guinea pigs? Guinea pigs are very social creatures. You wouldn't be here on your own, would you?
    - **Guinea Pig:** Oh, they're here. We guinea pigs are masters of stealth. You just can't see them!
    - **Player:** That's oddly unnerving.
    - (continue)
- **Player:** Look, I am talking to you. Which I can only do because I can speak to cats!
- **Player:** Not rodents, not dogs, not pigs, not horses, cats!
- **Player:** If you were a guinea pig, I would only hear squeaking. But here we are, having a rather confusing conversation.
- **Guinea Pig:** I... Erm... I...
- **Guinea Pig:** My whole life is a lie.
- **Guinea Pig:** You're right. I...I am a cat. I shall head to the oasis...to consider this truth.
- You have completed: 'Confused Cats' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
- **Guinea Pig:** Squeak...
- {{Qact|Guinea Pig disappears and appears at the oasis.}}

#### At Het's Oasis


##### Without catspeak

- **Guinea Pig:** I particularly enjoy knocking things off tables. It really winds people up. I love it.
- **Player:** I've created a monster.
