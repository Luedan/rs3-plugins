### In Al Kharid, before The Jack of Spades


#### After Stolen Hearts

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Talk about quests.
    - **Grand Vizier Hassan:** I have no time for idle chat, I must prepare the emir's body for burial.
    - **Player:** Will he be mummified?
    - **Grand Vizier Hassan:** No, those rites are performed in the southern cities - Menaphos and Sophanem. We're a little less backwards here in Al Kharid.
    - **Grand Vizier Hassan:** After I have anointed the body, he will be carried out into the sun - the people will want to see their emir one last time. He'll then be entombed below the palace.
