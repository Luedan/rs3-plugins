## Dialogue Transcript


### In Burthorpe, before While Guthix Sleeps


#### Without requirements to enter the Warriors' Guild

- **Ghommal:** Welcome to Warriors' Guild!
- **Player:** Can I join?
- **Ghommal:** Maybe! You go and become warrior and we see!

- **Ghommal:** Then you go and become stronger warrior!

#### With requirements to enter the Warriors' Guild

- **Ghommal:** Ghommal welcome you to Warrior Guild!
- **Player:** Umm...thank you, I think.
