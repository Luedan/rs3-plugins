## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### If the player can understand ghosts

- **Player:** Hi there. Why do you still bother having ships here? I mean - you're dead, what use are they to you?
- **Ghost sailor:** We keep ships because we still need trade in Phasmatys. Every trader that comes to Phasmatys is made to worship the Ectofuntus, so that the Ectopower doesn't run out.
- **Player:** So, without traders to worship in the Temple you're history, right?
- **Ghost sailor:** Aye, matey.

#### If the player cannot understand ghosts

- **Ghost sailor:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.
