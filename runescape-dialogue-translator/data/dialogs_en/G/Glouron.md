#### After The Prisoner of Glouphrie

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Glouron:** I am Glouron, I am watching over this spirit tree.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: About this tree.
    - **Glouron:** This is one of the largest spirit trees I've seen. She's been growing here since the first city was founded and has been on her own for all those years.
    - **Glouron:** She was so tall that she could look down at that old elf camp, but not talk to anyone.
    - **Glouron:** Of course now that she's got people to talk to, she barely shuts up!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: About Arposandra.
    - **Glouron:** What about it?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: What do the elves have to do with Arposandra?
        - **Glouron:** What? Nothing. Arposandra is a gnome city, for gnomes capable of seeing the greater good!
        - **Player:** What do you mean by the 'greater good'?
        - **Glouron:** The ability to prevent the limitation of progress due to outdated concepts of morality.
        - **Glouron:** Arposandra is a city of the future! These elves are trapped in the past.
        - **Glouron:** They tried to pin that plague on us of course, well the Iorwerth did, but the Crwys lot have acknowledged that it wasn't our doing. Mostly because the plague was a lie.
        - **Glouron:** That's why I'm here, to help soothe relations between Arposandra and Prifddinas.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Can I see Arposandra?
        - **Glouron:** No. I doubt you possess the appropriate sense of vision for the glory that is Arposandra!
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: What are your plans?
        - **Glouron:** I don't know what you mean, we certainly don't have any plans in the works at all.
        - (previous)
      - [Choice]: I'd like to talk about something else.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: About the elves.
    - **Glouron:** After the Iorwerth attempted to blame us for that fake plague of theirs, I've been working with the Crwys elves to help repair diplomatic relations with Prifddinas.
    - **Glouron:** It's a slow process, but we're making good ground.
    - **Glouron:** Although Lord Crwys gives me the heebie-jeebies. He sometimes just stands there looking longingly towards the sun, very odd.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: That's all for now.
    - (end)
