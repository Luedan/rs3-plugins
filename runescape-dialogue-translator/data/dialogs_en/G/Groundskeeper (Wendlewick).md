## Dialogue Transcript


### In Wendlewick


#### On a free-to-play world

- **Groundskeeper:** Hello there, adventurer. Are you here to learn about the birds and the bees?
- **Player:** Excuse me?
- **Groundskeeper:** I can help you with bird trapping and beekeeping. Wasn't that clear?
- **Groundskeeper:** Birdhouse trapping and beekeeping is only available for members.

#### On a members' world

- **Groundskeeper:** Hello there, adventurer. Are you here to learn about the birds and the bees?
- **Player:** Excuse me?
- **Groundskeeper:** I can help you with bird trapping and beekeeping. Wasn't that clear?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me about bird trapping.
    - **Player:** Tell me about birds.
    - **Groundskeeper:** Birds are a nuisance. They eat the bees, dig up seeds, and they don't half make some noise with incessant *chirp chirp*.
    - **Groundskeeper:** That's where birdbox trapping comes in! It's how me effectively manage it.
    - **Groundskeeper:** I can teach you if you like? It'd save me having to trek out down to the shrine and beyond every day to reset the traps. You'd be welcome to keep anything you find too.
    - **Player:** Tell me more.
    - **Groundskeeper:** It's very easy to understand, you take a log of any type and a clockwork mechanism, craft it into a birdhouse trap, place it on a spot, then fill it with enough seed...
    - **Groundskeeper:** ...come back a while later and *poof*. You've trapped yourself some birds, and if you're lucky some bird nests too.
    - (Condition: Before completing When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird:)
      - (Condition: Before going through 'Do you have any beekeeping supplies?' dialogue:)
        - You are halfway to completing: When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird
      - (Condition: After going through 'Do you have any beekeeping supplies?' dialogue:)
        - {{RSFontAchievement|When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird}}
    - (Condition: First time:)
      - **Groundskeeper:** Seeing as you're so willing to help, let me get you started with the first.
      - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
        - **Groundskeeper:** But first you'll want to make space for 3 items in your backpack.
      - (Condition: With space in backpack:)
        - [pic2=Clockwork detail.png]
        - (receives=logs, clockwork and 10 potato seeds)
        - **Groundskeeper:** Craft that into a birdhouse and then put{{fact}} it on that plot just there by the fence.
        - (continue)
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Does the type of seed matter?
        - **Player:** Does the type of seed matter?
        - **Groundskeeper:** Not at all! For birds, all seeds are equal. Just a light sprinkling of say... one for your basic birdhouse.
      - [Choice]: Does the type of wood matter?
        - **Player:** Does the type of wood matter?
        - **Groundskeeper:** Only for experience gained! The better the wood, the better the house, the better the bird!
        - **Groundskeeper:** Higher quality houses will also require more seeds to fill, but also trap faster. You reap what you sow and all that!
      - [Choice]: Where do I get clockwork?
        - **Player:** Where do I get clockwork?
        - **Groundskeeper:** Craftable at a workbench in your house.
        - **Groundskeeper:** Alternatively, I'll sell you some, but at a premium.
        - [Menu]: Buy some supplies?
          - [Choice]: I'll buy some now.
            - (opens=The Birds and the Bees)
          - [Choice]: I've got more questions.
            - (previous)
      - [Choice]: Where can I find other birdhouse plots?
        - **Player:** Where can I find other birdhouse plots?
        - **Groundskeeper:** If you head east from here there are three dotted about Marigold Farm.
        - **Groundskeeper:** Then if you head south-east and you'll find another at Eastfold farm.
        - **Groundskeeper:** If you head south from there you can find one near the fairy ring and another at the shrine tender's house.
        - **Groundskeeper:** The final plot is to the west near Dalia's tree patches.
      - [Choice]: Sounds good!
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: Do you have any beekeeping supplies?
    - **Player:** Do you have any beekeeping supplies for sale?
    - **Groundskeeper:** I do indeed!
    - (opens=The Birds and the Bees)
    - (Condition: Before completing When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird)
      - (Condition: Before going through 'Tell me about bird trapping.' dialogue:)
        - You are halfway to completing: When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird
      - (Condition: After going through 'Tell me about bird trapping.' dialogue:)
        - {{RSFontAchievement|When a Mummy Bird Loves a Daddy Bird}}
  - [Choice]: I'm okay for now.
    - **Player:** I'm okay for now.
    - **Groundskeeper:** Ah, maybe another time.
