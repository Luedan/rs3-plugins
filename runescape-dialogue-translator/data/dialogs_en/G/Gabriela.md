## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Gabriela:** Hey it's the hero! Wow, you're amazing! How on earth did you manage to tackle those two juvinates and Gadderanks? And the town is lovely - you've done a great job!
- **Player:** You're more than welcome.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: What do you do here?
    - **Player:** What do you do here?
    - **Gabriela:** I'm Gabriela. I'm looking after my husband who isn't feeling too well!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: How do you like your town?
    - **Player:** How do you like your town?
    - **Gabriela:** It's just perfect, thanks so much for your efforts.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What's happening around here?
    - **Player:** What's happening around here?
    - **Gabriela:** Oh, not much really, we're all still hiding from the vampyres and eking out a living from this terrible place, hopefully soon we'll be strong enough to make the journey to the temple on the Salve.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Is there anything I can do to help out?
    - **Player:** Is there anything I can do to help out?
    - **Gabriela:** Not as far as I know, we have some basic amenities now which is so very useful, we're all incredibly grateful to you. I've never really seen or known a hero before, so I have nothing to compare one to, but I suspect in most people's judgement, you would be one! I for one am very pleased to have met you.
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Okay, thanks.
    - **Player:** Ok, thanks.
