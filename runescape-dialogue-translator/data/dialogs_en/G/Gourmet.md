## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to option

- **Gourmet:** Ooh, a surface-dweller! Have you got any exotic surface foods to sell?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Not at the moment.
    - **Player:** Not at the moment.
    - **Gourmet:** A pity. Dorgeshuun food seems so dull now I've tasted surface food.
  - [Choice]: Yes!
    - **Player:** Yes!
    - **Gourmet:** Splendid! Show me what you've got!
  - [Choice]: What kind of foods do you like?
    - **Player:** What kind of food do you like?
    - **Gourmet:** All kinds! I don't want to keep eating the same food, even if I like it. I want to try new tastes!
      - **Gourmet:** Why not try something on me and see if I like it?
