## Dialogue Transcript


### In Keldagrim marketplace


#### Standard dialogue

- **Gunslik:** What can I interest you in? We have something of everything here!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Oh good!
    - **Player:** Oh good!
    - (opens=Gunslik's Assorted Items)
  - [Choice]: Nothing, thanks.
    - **Player:** Nothing, thanks.

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Gunslik:** Hullo, Player. Interested in doing a little job for me?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Sounds interesting, what can I do for you?
    - **Player:** Sounds interesting, what can I do for you?
    - **Gunslik:** I’ve got a very specific task for you, for a very specific customer. Can you get me [item] in the next [number] minutes?
    - **Player:** Er, you want [item]?
    - **Gunslik:** For a very special customer, yes.
    - **Player:** Right, then… I’ll get you [item] in the next [number] minutes. I'm on it.
  - [Choice]: I’m not interested, no.
    - **Player:** I’m not interested, no.
    - **Gunslik:** I’m really sorry to hear that, I thought you were just the right person for the job.

##### Talking to Gunslik without the item

- **Gunslik:**Did you get me [item], Player? I don’t really have an awful lot of time anymore.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I don’t know and I don’t care. I quit!
    - **Player:** I don’t know and I don’t care. I quit!
    - **Gunslik:** No need to be rude about it! Especially since you promised me!
  - [Choice]: Just give me a little bit more time.
    - **Player:** Just give me a little bit more time.
    - **Gunslik:** Sure, but please hurry up!

#### Talking to Gunslik with the item

- **Player:** Am I still on time? I’ve got you [item]!
- **Gunslik:** You certainly are! Thank you very much!
- **Player:** Show me the gold!
- (receives=coins)
