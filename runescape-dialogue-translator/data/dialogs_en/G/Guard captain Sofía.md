## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Tell me about...**
  - This ranger's workroom.
    - **Guard captain Sofía:** We make arrows and bows here, of course!
    - **Guard captain Sofía:** Here you'll have access to all the facilities available to help you stock up on arrows and bolts.
    - At tier 1, you'll have a 2% chance for the workroom to supply materials while fletching various weapons and ammo, as well as a 1% chance to fletch an extra bundle of arrow shafts in the workroom.
    - At tier 2, you'll have a 1% chance to fletch an extra bundle of ranged ammo and headless arrows in the workroom, as well as a 2% chance to fletch fully strung bows when fletching unstrung bows in the workroom.
    - At tier 3, you'll have a 5% increase to earned Fletching XP along with access to 'Wisdom of Anima' perks while inside the ranger's workroom. With 'Wisdom of Anima' enabled, dinarrows are imbued with great efficiency but award little XP. Once unlocked, the perk can be toggled via the fletching workbench.
    - You can increase your wisdom of each resonant anima type by making the corresponding Elder God arrows at any building tier, however a tier 3 ranger's workroom is required to unlock the perks.
  - Being guard captain.
    - **Guard captain Sofía:** It's a big step up from what I've done in the past, however I am ready to help protect the fort.
    - **Player:** Brilliant. I feel a lot better knowing the fort is in capable hands.
  - ((Once Granny Rowan has been talked to at the botanist's bench but before getting access to the bench:))
    - Talk about Granny Rowan
      - ((Only on first time:))
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** What can I do for you, Your Grace?
        - **Player:** There's been an alarming lapse in security.
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** What is it?
        - **Player:** A rather enigmatic guest has appeared in the townhall. Rowan, she called herself. 'Granny' Rowan. Says she wants to help with the new botanist's bench.
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** Granny Rowan? I know of her. My old man used to tell me stories about her, as did his old man. She's as old as the wilderness herself, or so they say.
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** A tricky old bint, she is. No wonder she managed to slip her way in. We need to up our patrols.
        - **Player:** What can you tell me about her?
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** We dark warriors had dealings with her on occasion. Traded her food and other goods, in exchange for potions and medical supplies.
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** Not sure {{Sic|what's she's}} doing here, but she's harmless... I think.
        - **Player:** You think?
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** We could use her help, impending attack and all. Don't worry, I'll keep my good eye on her.
        - **Player:** I'd better give her the good news, then.
      - ((After first time, before talking with Granny Rowan:))
        - **Guard captain Sofía:** Did you have that chat with old Granny Rowan? I wouldn't turn down her help, odd as she is.
        - **Player:** Not yet.
