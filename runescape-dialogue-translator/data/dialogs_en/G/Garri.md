# Garri

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Garri](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGarri)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - Who are you?
    - **Garri:** Hey there! I'm Garri, the newest recruit to the fayre. It's my fun task to let people know how the helter skelter works.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - How does the helter skelter work?
    - **Garri:** Thanks to the grease that was made when we first built it, you will slide down it a treat.
    - **Garri:** Simply climb up the inside of the helter skelter and then, when you get up to the top, sit down and enjoy the smooth ride.
    - **Garri:** It's better if you lift your arms in the air as you go down.
    - You will receive Constitution XP for riding the attraction.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not asked last:)*
  - How are things going?
    - **Garri:** I'm having a blast! I barely even notice the smell of the grease any more.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
