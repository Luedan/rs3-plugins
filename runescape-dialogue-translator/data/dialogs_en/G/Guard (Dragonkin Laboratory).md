## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to the Guard

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Guard:** Can we help you with anything?
- *What would you like to say?*
  - Can I go through the gate?
    - **Player:** Can I go through the gate?
    - **Guard:** If you want, we will let you pass into the Wilderness. Be warned that other players might attack you there, and you won't be allowed through the gate while you're in combat.
    - *What would you like to say?*
      - I am not afraid.
        - **Player:** I am not afraid.
        - **Guard:** Your speech is brave. Travel south-west, and you should reach the city of Varrock, if you don't die first.
        - **Guard:** Now, can we help you with anything else?
        - *What would you like to say?*
          - What are you doing here?
            - (below)
          - Is there any other way out of here?
            - **Player:** Is there any other way out of here?
            - **Guard:** Not...really. You could try one of your teleport spells, or those lodestone things you adventurers seem to be keen on.
            - **Guard:** It's far safer than trying to walk through the Wilderness. Now, can we help you with anything else?
            - (previous)
          - No thanks.
            - **Player:** No thanks.
            - (end)
      - Forget it, I'm not going through there.
        - **Player:** Forget it, I'm not going through there.
        - **Guard:** That is wise. Now, can we help you with anything else?
        - (previous)
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Player:** What are you doing here?
    - **Guard:** We are guarding Bryll and the other adventurers against the vicious fighters who roam the Wilderness.
    - **Player:** Good luck with that.
    - **Guard:** Thank you. Now, can we help you with anything else?
    - *What would you like to say?*
      - Can I go through the gate?
        - (above)
      - Is there any other way out of here?
        - (above)
      - No thanks.
        - (below)
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - (end)

### Trying to pass through the gate into the Wilderness

- **Guard:** You can enter the Wilderness, if you like, but you can't return this way if you're in combat.
- *Proceed through the gate?*
  - Yes, I understand the restrictions.
    - (Wilderness warning interface opens)
  - No, I'll stay here.
    - **Player:** No, I'll stay here.
    - (end)
