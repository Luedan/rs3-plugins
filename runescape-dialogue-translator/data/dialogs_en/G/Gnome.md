# Gnome

- **Letter**: `G`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Encounter

*Gnomes have many distinct options for dialogue, which seem to be randomly chosen when spoken to.*

#### Option 1

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome:** How's life treating you?
- **Player:** Not bad, not bad at all.
- **Gnome:** It's good to see a human with a positive attitude.

#### Option 2

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome:** Can't stop sorry! Busy, busy, busy!
- The gnome is too busy to talk.

#### Option 3

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome:** ... wha?
- The gnome seems a little drunk.
- **Player:** Are you OK?
- **Gnome:** Youssh go geshh em.
- **Player:** Pardon?
- **Gnome:** Gessh ush a cosshtail.
- **Player:** A what?
- **Gnome:** Blurssh berry.
- Perhaps he's had enough!

#### Option 4

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome:** I don't think I can take much more!
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Gnome:** It's just the wife, she won't stop moaning!
- **Player:** Maybe you should give her less to moan about.
- **Gnome:** She'll always find something.
