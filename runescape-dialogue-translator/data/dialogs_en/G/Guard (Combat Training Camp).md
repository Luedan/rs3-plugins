## Dialogue Transcript


### Attempting to open the gate


#### Before Biohazard

- **Guard:** This is a restricted area, you can only enter under the authority of King Lathas.
- The gates are locked.

#### After Biohazard

- **Guard:** The king has granted you access to this training area. Make good use of it, soon all your strength will be needed.
- {{Qact|The player walks through the gate.}}

### Talking to one of the guards with shields

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Guard:** What do you want? Leave us be!

### Talking to one of the guards with crossbows


#### With short full facial hairstyle

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Guard:** Well hello brave warrior. These ogres have been terrorising the area, they've eaten four children this week alone.
- **Player:** Brutes!
- **Guard:** So we decided to use them for target practice. A fair punishment.
- **Player:** Indeed.

#### With short beard facial hairstyle

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Guard:** Hello soldier.
- **Player:** I'm more of an adventurer really.
- **Guard:** In this day and age we're all soldiers. No time to waste  Fight! Fight!
