## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- (Condition: If the player has recently been caught stealing from one of the market stalls:)
  - **Grocer Hannah:** {{RSChat|You're the one who stole something from me!}}
  - **Grocer Hannah:** {{RSChat|Help!}}
- **Grocer Hannah:** Hello, pet. Can I interest you in the freshest produce in Wendlewick? We're running a special offer for those interested!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do you have in stock?
    - **Player:** What do you have in stock?
    - **Grocer Hannah:** Anything and everything, dear. All locally sourced.
    - (opens=The Village Grocery)
  - [Choice]: Tell me more about this special offer.
    - **Player:** Tell me more about this special offer.
    - **Grocer Hannah:** Of course, pet. All you need to do is sign here-
    - [Hannah slides an incredibly long roll of parchment over the counter.]
    - **Player:** 'Available for a limited time only...terms and conditions may apply...the Village Grocery will not be held responsible for any stomach upset, burnt food or death caused by the use or misuse of our produce...'
    - **Grocer Hannah:** On second thought, let's just show you our fine wares, my dearest.
    - (opens=The Village Grocery)
  - [Choice]: Farewell.
    - **Player:** Farewell.
    - **Grocer Hannah:** Come back soon!
