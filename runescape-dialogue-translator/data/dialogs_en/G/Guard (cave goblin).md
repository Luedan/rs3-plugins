## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- {{trandom}}
  - (Condition: Dialogue 1:)
    - **Guard:** Don't you try anything, surface-dweller. I'm keeping an eye on you.
  - (Condition: Dialogue 2:)
    - **Guard:** Welcome, surface-dweller, to the hidden city of Dorgesh-Kaan!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 3:)
    - **Guard:** Surface-dweller!
    - **Player:** What?
    - **Guard:** Have a nice day!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 4:)
    - **Guard:** I don't want to be unfriendly, but I can't help noticing that since surface-dwellers started coming into the city there has been an increase in CRIME!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 5:)
    - **Guard:** Surface-dweller! I don't know how you got into the city, but you will never escape!
    - **Player:** But... I thought I was allowed into the city!
    - **Guard:** You are. I just wanted to say that. And now that you're allowed into the city, I'll never be able to!
