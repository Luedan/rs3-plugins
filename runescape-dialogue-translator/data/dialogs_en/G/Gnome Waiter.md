## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Gnome Waiter:** Hello madam, can I tempt you with any of the dishes on our new menu?
  - **Player:** I'll take a look.
    - **Player:** I'll take a look.
    - **Gnome Waiter:** I hope you like what you see. Although all these premade dishes are good to eat - they were made by the last human assistant chef. He wasn't up to Chef's exacting standards - and you may find that they will not be accepted by some people as the 'real' thing.
  - **Player:** Not really.
    - **Player:** Not really.
    - **Gnome Waiter:** That's your choice madam, enjoy your stay.
