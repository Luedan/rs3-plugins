## Dialogue Transcript


### Guard at entrance to Mountain Camp


#### Climbing over the rockslide


##### Entering before Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Guard:** You stay right there, outerlander.
- (Condition: If the player has done The Fremennik Trials:)
  - **Player:** Don't worry, I'm not an outerlander, I am [Fremennik name], a Fremennik!
  - **Guard:** I said stay there, outerlander! You are not welcome in our camp.
- (Condition: If the player has not done The Fremennik Trials:)
  - **Player:** Why, what's beyond here?
  - **Guard:**Our camp, outerlander, and we don't want any trouble there.

##### Entering during Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Guard:** I suppose I might as well let you pass now, since I can't seem to stop you anyway.
- **Guard:** Just remember that I'm watching you.

##### Exiting before or during Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Guard:** Go, outerlander, and do not hurry back.

##### Entering or exiting after Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Guard:** You may pass freely, outerlander.

#### Talking to Guard


##### Before Mountain Daughter quest, after climbing rope to the North

- **Guard:** Hey, how did you get here?

##### During Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Player:** Can you come and help me? The chieftain's daughter -
- **Guard:** No, outerlander, I'm guarding this mountain pass from trolls and inquisitive people like you.
- **Player:** Doesn't look like you were very successful in the last regard.
- **Guard:** If the chieftain would let me, I would correct that oversight.

##### After Mountain Daughter Quest

- **Guard:** You may pass freely.
- **Player:** Thanks... and try to lighten up a bit, alright?
- **Guard:** Don't press your luck, outerlander.

### Guard in the Mountain Camp

- **Guard:** Feel free to come in and see the highlight of our culture, outerlander.
