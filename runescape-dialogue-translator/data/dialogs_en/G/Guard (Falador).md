# Guard (Falador)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Guard (Falador)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGuard%20%28Falador%29)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** You're a long way out from the city.
- **Guard:** I know. We guards usually stay by banks and shops, but I got sent all the way out here to keep an eye on the brigands loitering just south of here.
- **Player:** It sounds more exciting than standing around guarding banks and shops.
- **Guard:** It's not too bad. At least I don't get attacked so often out here. Guards in the cities get killed all the time.
- **Player:** Honestly, people these days just don't know how to behave!
- (end)
