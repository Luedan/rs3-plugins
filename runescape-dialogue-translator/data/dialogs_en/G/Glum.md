## Dialogue Transcript


### Before As a First Resort

- **Player:** Do you know anything about this ship?
- **Glum:** No, I just waiting here for de humans who make it go to come back. I wanna ride when dey open for business.

### After As a First Resort

- **Glum:** Stupid humans. Dey won't let me have a ride in de boat unless I gives dem lots of shiny pretties.
- **Player:** That's so often the way, I'm afraid.
