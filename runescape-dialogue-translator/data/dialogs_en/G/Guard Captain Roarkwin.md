## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Port Sarim jail


#### Standard dialogue

- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** Blast that Jed! We've managed to make repairs to the prison, but we're short by a few 'guests'.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** Someone should do something about this! Trader Stan is upset about his ship being stolen too.

#### During the Port Sarim Invasion


##### First time talking to him

- **Player:** Hey, what's going on here? Can I help?
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** Certainly! We'll take all the help we can get, right now.
- ''If the initial 200 tokens haven't been claimed:
  - **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** Here, take these!
  - The guard captain hands you two-hundred Port Sarim tokens. These can be cashed in for supply caches, and also used to configure which skills you will earn experience in during this event.
- **Player:** What do you need help with?
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** We're under attack by a bunch of pirates called the Skulls, led by the notorous Jed - otherwise known as the Iron Link.
- **Player:** That guy? I have some unfinished business with him. He tried to kidnap a bunch of people not long ago, but Meg and I put a stop to it.
- **Player:** Well, I put a stop to it and Meg, er, was also involved.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** It seems that wasn't enough for him, and now he's back trying to abduct more locals.
- **Player:** Why attack the jail, though?
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** We have a fairly large collection of inmates here, and we're mostly trained to keep them from escaping, not fend off a full-scale pirate assault.
- **Player:** Still, it's risky, and not nearly as subtle as his previous attempts.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** My guess is his boss didn't take too kindly to his earlier failure, and now he's desperate. He could fill his hold with prisoners from here, for whatever dark purpose he has.
- **Player:** Right then, so, how can I help out?
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** There's a bunch of stuff you can help us out with.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** First and most obvious, you can take out the Skulls' landing parties. My guards are doing what they can to keep them outside of the jail, but they need support from more experienced fighters.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** Jed's ship is pounding the prison. Do what you can to repair the old lighthouse tower - if that thing falls it'll collapse the whole prison.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** We also have a cannon up on the roof. If you have any experience in making things explode at a distance, you might want to muck in with that.
- **Guard Captain Roarkwin:** And of course, that cannon needs feeding with ammunition. There are some portable furnaces and a mixing table on the jail roof, so if you can smith us some cannonballs or mix black powder, that'd be a big help.
- Each of these activities will generate tokens, up to a maximum of 2,000 per day. Tokens (200) can be cashed in for supply caches, which contain various rewards.
- Right-click on a token to 'configure' which skills you will gain experience in for each each  of the Invasion of Port Sarim activities.
