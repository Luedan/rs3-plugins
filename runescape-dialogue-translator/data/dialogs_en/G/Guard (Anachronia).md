## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock Dig Site

- **Guard:** Happy to be sailing with you Player, time to board the boat, eh?

### During the initial expedition on Anachronia


#### On arrival

- **Guard:** Look how pretty everything is!

#### After passing the bones

- **Guard:** Kind of scary, venturing into the unknown. If I die...
- **Guard:** Tell my family I love them.

### North of the base camp


#### Talk to


##### Before completing the course

- **Guard:** You're Player, right? Tell me, is there something else going on here?
- **Player:{{anchor|wdym}}** What do you mean?
- **Guard:** Look around us! This island, this place... it isn't normal. I've never seen anything like this.
- **Guard:** And before we left I heard the archaeologists talking about the island. According to them it should be covered in fossils!
- **Guard:** What we saw on the way here... they were anything BUT fossils!
- [Menu]: How would you like to respond.{{Sic}}
  - [Choice]: [Reassure]
    - **Player:** You're right; there is more to this island than meets the eye.
    - **Player:** However, I have confidence that with everyone here we can figure out what is going on and come out of it stronger than we began.
    - **Guard:** I really hope you know what you're talking about.
  - [Choice]: [Dismiss theory]
    - **Player:** People get things wrong all the time. The archaeologists were guessing from an ocean away, sometimes you have to visit a place to understand what is really going on.
    - **Player:** Focus on your mission and everything will be okay.
    - **Guard:** I really hope you know what you're talking about.

##### After completing the course

- **Guard:** You're Player, right? Tell me, is there something else going on here?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: What do you mean?
    - (continuea|wdym)
  - [Choice]: What are my Agility course records?
    - (below|After completing the course once)
  - [Choice]: Can I reset my lap progress?
    - (below|Reset lap progress)

#### Agility records


##### Before completing the course

- **Guard:** Did you know you can run around the island? The start of the course is here... or is it the end? I'm not really sure as you can run both ways round it.
- **Guard:** If you do give it a go, I'll keep count of your laps and let you know your fastest time.

##### After completing the course once

- **Guard:** So far you have completed 1 lap and your fastest lap was [number] minutes and [number] seconds.

##### After completing the course between 2 and 15 times

- **Guard:** Are you doing laps around the island? So far you have completed [number] laps and your fastest lap was [minutes]:[seconds].

##### After completing the course between 16 and 100 times

- **Guard:** You must be keeping yourself fit running around the island? So far you have completed [number] laps and your fastest lap was [minutes]:[seconds].

##### After completing the course more than 100 times

- **Guard:** You sure do love running round around, don't you? So far you have completed [number] laps and your fastest lap was [minutes]:[seconds].

#### Reset lap progress

- [Menu]: Are you sure you want to reset your lap progress?
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Guard:** All done! I've forgotten any progress you made on the lap.
  - [Choice]: No.
    - (end)

### North-eastern base camp

- **Guard:** Wow what a place! Have you smelled that air?
- [The guard takes a deep breath. You respond with a deep breath of your own.]
- **Player:** It smells...
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Amazing.
    - **Guard:** I know! What a treat!
  - [Choice]: Terrible.
    - **Guard:** Each to their own.
  - [Choice]: What is that?
    - **Guard:** Hmm...I'd say a mix of untouched earth, pungent plantlife and poo.
    - **Player:** It really is something else...

### West of the base camp


#### Northern guard

- **Guard:** Be careful out there, okay? We can protect the camp but there's not enough to secure the whole island.
- **Player:** Do you think we're safe here?
- **Guard:** Safe isn't the word I'd use. Secure, perhaps?
- **Guard:** Secure, yes that seems about right. We have the bridge covered and I feel confident we can defend this point.

#### Southern guard

- **Guard:** Exciting new opportunity! That's what they told me!
- **Player:** Well they weren't lying.
- **Guard:** I guess not. Do you think it's too late to ask for a transfer?
- **Player:** Probably.
