## Dialogue Transcript

- **Guthix:** Before I settled to my rest, I walked among my creatures and sought out each of those who were my Guardians.
- **Guthix:** When I found each, I asked but one question, for I know their service would be for lifetimes to come, aeons of time, and I should recognise their service to this world and myself.
- **Guthix:** So I asked: 'What gift can I leave you?'
- **Guthix:** I came to Juna last; faithful Juna. Whose egg I saved as last of her kind, so long ago; her world now a place of only death.
- **Guthix:** She who only knew Gielinor, and loved this world as much as I. And, as I had asked the others, I asked her too.
- **Guthix:** The others had asked for treasures, favours, powers or artefacts that they wanted or needed for what they thought they might face.
- **Guthix:** I knew that Juna would not be one of those. I was not wrong in this assessment; she lived up to everything I hoped of her.
- **Guthix:** For a moment, she caught my eye, before lowering her head. Her eyes welled up with tears while she pondered the answer we both knew was deep within her heart.
- **Guthix:** At last she whispered it, as much hissed as spoken... 'Stay with us'.
- **Guthix:** It was a pure expression of what she felt, and I knew it to be all she desired; that she knew she had no right to ask it of me, her god, and that I could never grant such a thing to her or the others.
- **Guthix:** It was the thing we all knew could not now be asked of me. Her eyes would not meet mine; she was clearly ashamed to ask it.
- **Guthix:** I reached out to her, placed my hand upon the top of her head, and she looked up at me as I smiled at her. For in that moment, I knew my gift to her, and it was the greatest of gifts.
- **Guthix:** 'This world is yours now, Juna. Yours forever. Free from the manipulation of gods. That is my first gift, but you deserve another.'
- **Guthix:** For in my own eyes, tears had formed too, and one rolled down my cheek.
- **Guthix:** I spoke again: 'I give you my tears, and in those tears you know that I am ever-living, that I am with you, and that I weep to be apart from you.'
- **Guthix:** And I lifted my hand at last, as the tear fell from my jaw and landed on the floor below Juna. But I could not stay, nor mourn, for the time has come for me to leave.
- **Guthix:** I knew she would think my greatest gift was the tears, but she would be wrong.
- **Guthix:** Freedom is the greatest gift, and one so easily forgotten.
