## Dialogue Transcript


### In Keldagrim marketplace

- **Gulldamar:** Finest silver in all of Keldagrim, come and see!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Right, what do you have?
    - **Player:** Right, what do you have?
    - **Gulldamar:** Silver, what else!
    - (opens=Silver Cog Silver Stall)
  - [Choice]: Not interested, thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.

#### Getting caught while attempting to steal from his stall

- **Gulldamar:** {{RSChat|That's my silver!}}
