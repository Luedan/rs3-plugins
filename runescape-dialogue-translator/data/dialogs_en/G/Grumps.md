### With a catspeak amulet equipped


#### Before completing Cat's Bane, without Dimies the sardine in backpack

- **Player:** You don't have to go home, but you can't stay here.
- **Grumps:** This is my home, peon. <br>I lead the Manx of Zamorak.
- **Player:** Well, Zemouregal wants you out.
- **Grumps:** That fool! My lord will one day return and oust him. I must be patient.
- **Grumps:** I will make you a deal, human. I shall move on from this place if you procure me a sardine. And it MUST be alive!
- **Player:** Where can I get one of those in New Varrock?
- **Grumps:** My spies inform me that the crazy fish lady, Gertrude, imprisons one as a 'pet'. Bring it to me!

#### Picking up Dimies the sardine

- [You take Gertrude's pet sardine.]

#### Before completing Cat's Bane, with Dimies the sardine in backpack

- **Grumps:** Yes! The live sacrifice! Well done, minion, my preparations are complete.
- Before you can say anything, the cat swipes the fishbowl and menacingly stalks off in the direction of New Varrock's graveyard...
