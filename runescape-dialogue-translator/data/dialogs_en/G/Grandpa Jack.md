# Grandpa Jack

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Grandpa Jack](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AGrandpa%20Jack)
- **Letter**: `G`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to

- **Grandpa Jack:** Hello young [man/woman]!<br/>Come to visit old Grandpa Jack? I can tell ye stories for sure. I used to be the best fisherman these parts have seen!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me a story then.
    - **Player:** Tell me a story then.
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Well, when I were a young man we used to take fishing trips over to Catherby; The fishing over there - now that was something!
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Anyway, we decided to do a bit of fishing with our nets, I wasn't having the best of days turning up nothing but old boots and bits of seaweed. Then my net suddenly got really heavy!
    - **Grandpa Jack:** I pulled it up...
    - **Grandpa Jack:** To my amazement I'd caught this little chest thing!
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Even more amazing was when I opened it it contained a diamond the size of a radish! That's the best catch I've ever had!
  - [Choice]: Sorry, I don't have time now.
    - **Player:** Sorry, I don't have time now.
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Sigh...
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Young people - always in such a rush.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy one of your fishing rods?
    - **Player:** Can I buy one of your fishing rods?
    - (Condition: If the player has a fishing rod in their backpack:)
      - **Grandpa Jack:** But young [man/woman], you have your own. I'll not sell you mine when you already have one!
    - (Condition: If the player does not have a fishing rod in their backpack:)
      - **Grandpa Jack:** Of course you can young [man/woman]. Let's see now...I think 5 gold is a fair price for a rod which has won the Fishing contest before eh?
      - [Menu]: Select an Option
        - [Choice]: Very fair, I'll buy that rod!
          - **Player:** Very fair, I'll buy that rod!
          - **Grandpa Jack:** Excellent choice!
          - (Condition: If the player does not have at least one free backpack space:)
            - **Player:** Only problem is, I don't have any space to carry one right now. Let me get back to you on that.
            - **Grandpa Jack:** Right you are. I'll be here.
          - (Condition: If the player has at least one free backpack space:)
            - (removes=5 coins) (receives=fishing rod)
            - [You hand over the money and receive a fishing rod.]
            - **Player:** Thanks!
            - **Grandpa Jack:** You're welcome young 'un.
        - [Choice]: That's too rich for me, I'll go to Catherby.
          - **Player:** That's too rich for me, I'll go to Catherby.
          - **Grandpa Jack:** If you're sure.... passing up an opportunity of a lifetime you are.
  - [Choice]: I've forgotten how to fish, can you remind me?
    - **Player:** I've forgotten how to fish, can you remind me?
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Of course! Let me see now...You'll need a rod and bait. You can fish with a net too, but not in the competition.
    - **Player:** Ok... I think I can get those at Catherby.
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Then simply find yourself a fishing spot, either in the competition near here, or wherever you can - I recommend net fishing in Catherby. Net or Lure the fish in the fishing spot by clicking on it and then be patient...
    - **Player:** It's that simple?
    - **Grandpa Jack:** Yep! Go get em tiger.
