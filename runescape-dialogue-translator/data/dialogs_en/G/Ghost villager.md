## Dialogue Transcript


### Without an amulet of ghostspeak

- **Ghost villager:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.

### With an amulet of ghostspeak

*(One of the following is said at random:)*
- **Ghost villager:** This cold wind blows right through you, doesn't it?
- **Ghost villager:** We do not talk to the warm-bloods.
- **Ghost villager:** What do you want, mortal?
- **Ghost villager:** Why did we have to listen to that maniacal priest?
- **Ghost villager:** Worship the Ectofuntus all you want, but don't bother us, human.
