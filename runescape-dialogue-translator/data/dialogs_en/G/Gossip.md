## Dialogue Transcript


### At the entrance to Sinclair's Mansion


#### Before Murder Mystery

- **Gossip:** There's some kind of commotion up at the Sinclair place I hear. Not surprising all things considered.

#### After Murder Mystery and before King's Ransom


##### Without meeting the quest requirements for King's Ransom

- **Gossip:** Hmm, too bad you're not very experienced. I hear there is some more trouble going on up at the Sinclair Mansion.
- ((Quest Overview interface for King's Ransom opens.))
  - [Choice]: Not Right Now
    - **Player:** Very odd coincidence, but I don't really have time to look into it now.
    - **Gossip:** Ah, too bad. I'll have to get news from my other sources.
  - [Choice]: Accept Quest
    - You do not meet the requirements to start : 'King's Ransom'.

- **Gossip:** Well, this has certainly been exciting! The Sinclairs under house arrest, Morgan Le Faye attempting yet again to interfere with King Arthur and Merlin, and even the Black Knights sticking their noses into it.
