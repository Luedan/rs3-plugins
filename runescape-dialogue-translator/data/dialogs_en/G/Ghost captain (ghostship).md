- **Letter**: `G`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### If the player can understand ghosts

- **Player:** Where do you sail to?
- **Ghost captain:** At the moment, nowhere. The mini-Ectofuntus that powers the Ghostship is in major need of repair, and until it gets it, this ship is going nowhere.

#### If the player cannot understand ghosts

- **Ghost captain:** Woooo wooo wooooo woooo
- You cannot understand the ghost.
