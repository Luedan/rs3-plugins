#### Before showing interest in a job

- **Gianne Jnr:** Hello, welcome to Aluft Aloft, the gnome food delivery service. We deliver gnome foods to customers all over Gielinor. Do you want a job? We are always looking for new staff.
- [Menu]: Are you interested in a job?
  - [Choice]: Yes, tell me more.
    - **Player:** Yes, tell me more.
    - **Gianne Jnr:** First of all you will need some basic Gnome Cooking and Cocktail making training. Go and talk to my Father, Aluft Gianne snr. and Blurberry, if you haven't already, and they'll tell you what you need to do.
    - **Gianne Jnr:** Once you have got recommendations from both of them I'd be happy to take you on at Aluft Aloft!
    - **Gianne Jnr:** Once you are working for us, I will give you a choice of either an easy or hard job. I will then ask you to make a piece of Gnome Cuisine, and tell you who ordered it, with some information about how to find him or her. Don't take too long, the order is only valid for a short period of time.
    - **Gianne Jnr:** If you complete the delivery successfully, you will be given a tip by the customer and credit towards your own 'free delivery token'.
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: What's the difference between an easy and a hard job?
        - **Player:** What's the difference between an easy and a hard job?
        - **Gianne Jnr:** The foods that you need to cook for the easy jobs are more straight forward than the hard ones, and the customers will be within the Gnome Stronghold, or one of the Gnome Glider Pilots on duty. You will get one credit for completing a delivery, and the tips you get will be a small amount of cash.
        - **Gianne Jnr:** The hard jobs could require you to cook any piece of gnome cuisine or cocktail. The customers could be anywhere in Gielinor. If you are going to complete the delivery in time, you'll probably have to use some form of transport. Gnome gliders are especially useful here.
        - **Gianne Jnr:** For the hard jobs you will be given more time to complete the delivery though. You get three credits for completing it, and the tips you get tend to be useful objects, and even a few unusual items, that you won't be able to get hold of anywhere else!

      - [Choice]: What are these free delivery tokens?
        - **Player:** What are these free delivery tokens?
        - **Gianne Jnr:** I'll give you a free delivery token when you earn 12 credits. You can then use the token to make a food order yourself! This is a great privilege, we normally only deliver to gnomes. You may only have one token at a time, and they are non-transferrable.
        - **Gianne Jnr:** If you gain more credits then for every 12 extra credits you earn, you will receive an extra item of food or drink per delivery, up to a maximum of ten items. You can continue to make deliveries after this, but you will not get any more credits - just the normal tip and cooking training.
        - **Gianne Jnr:** Once you call for a delivery your token will be used up, and you'll have to earn another one, if you want another delivery.

        - **Player:** Will this help me with my cooking?
        - **Gianne Jnr:** You will be able to develop your cooking skills during deliveries - you'll get the normal training for making the items, but also a bonus for successfully delivering the goods.
