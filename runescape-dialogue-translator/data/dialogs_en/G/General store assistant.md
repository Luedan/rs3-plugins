- **General store assistant:** Hey! Lovely day we're having here!
- **Player:** You seem awfully happy. Are you okay?
- **General store assistant:** Never been better. It's a lovely sunny day; there's a nice breeze in the air and I'm seeing the world. What could be wrong with that?
- **Player:** Well, when you put it like that. Yeah, you're right! It is a lovely day!
- **General store assistant:** Lovely day! Lovely day? Are you mad? Have you seen what's out there?
- **General store assistant:** Big creatures with sharp teeth, ready to rip us apart at any moment. What's stopping one crossing the bridge right now?
- **General store assistant:** When my boss said he had a unique opportunity for me, this was NOT what I was expecting.
- **General store assistant:** Please! Do what you need to do so we can get off this island AS SOON AS POSSIBLE!
- **Player:** You can count on me! Have a lovely day!
- **General store assistant:** AHHHHH!
