## Dialogue Transcript


### 2015

- **Guido Fawkes:** Remember remember, nope I've forgotten.
- Who are you?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** The name's Fawkes, Guido Fawkes, but you can call me Guy.
  - **Player:** Ok friend.
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Hold it, I'm not your 'friend', buddy.
  - **Player:** Well, I'm not your 'buddy', pal.
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Whoa, I'm not your 'pal' either, Guy!
  - **Player:** I'm not your 'guy' Guy!
- What's going on here?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** I'm celebrating, so I started a fire!
  - **Player:** What an odd way to celebrate... mind if I use it?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Knock yourself out. You'll get vastly increased firemaking experience when using the bonfire, you'll also save some logs on occasion and increased chances of a fire spirit appearing!
  - **Player:** Can I do anything else?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Emmet and Sera can teach you about poi dancing and fire breathing. They're on the East and West sides of the bonfire, respectively, or you can speak to Alice, she's got some supplies to help out with the celebration.
  - **Player:** Ok, thanks!
- Where did all the sand go?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Well, it took me a while to move it but I did it.
  - **Player:** Where did you put it?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** It's all in that bucket right there.
  - **Player:** What... seriously?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Only joking! Lets just say, that the desert in Al Kharid is a bit larger.
- What's that bucket doing here?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Safety first of course! I kept this small amount of sand just incase the fire gets out of control.
  - **Player:** We wouldn't want that to happen would we?
  - **Guido Fawkes:** Definitely not!
- Nevermind.

Chad and Evey can teach you about lantern building and spirit summoning. - What's that bucket doing here?
  - *(Same as 2015)*
