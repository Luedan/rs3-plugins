## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Robe Store owner:** Welcome to the Magic Guild Store. Would you like to buy some magic supplies?
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (opens=Magic Guild Store - Mystic Robes)
  - [Choice]: Can I buy a skillcape of Magic?
    - (Condition: If the player has a ring of charos (a) equipped:)
      - **Robe Store owner:** I don't do this for anyone, but I've got a special offer today! Normally I require 99,000 coins for this prestigious item, but for you it's half price. Only 92,000 coins!
      - [Menu]: Choose an option:
        - [Choice]: 92,000 coins? That's much too expensive.
          - **Player:** I know where to find you if I change my mind.
        - [Choice]: I think I have the money right here, actually.
          - (Condition: If the player has no backpack space:)
            - **Robe Store owner:** Unfortunately all skillcapes are only available with a free hood, it's part of a skill promotion deal; buy one get one free, you know. So, you'll need to free up some backpack space before I can sell you one.
          - (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
            - **Player:** But unfortunately, I was mistaken.
            - **Robe Store owner:** Well, come back and see me when you do.
          - (Condition: If the player has enough money:)
            - (receives=magic cape and magic hood) (removes=92,000 coins)
            - **Robe Store owner:** Excellent! Wear that cape with pride my friend.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have a ring of charos (a) equipped:)

          - **Robe Store owner:** You...really have mastered it. What an honour! It would be my pleasure to sell you a Magic master cape.
