## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### At the tannery, before starting Finding Tali

- **Reuben:** Oi! Haven't you heard of knocking?
- **Player:** Sorry, I thought your shop was open.
- **Reuben:** A new face? Don't see many of those round these parts.
- **Reuben:** I should be the one apologising. Shouldn't 'ave shouted at you like that. Just got a lot going on, you know?
- **Player:** What's the matter?
- **Reuben:** It's my kid, Tali.
- **Reuben:** She went wandering off yesterday. Never came home. I'm worried something fierce.
- **Reuben:** She's a good kid. Got her 'ead screwed on right, and she's nearly fully grown. Doesn't feel right reprimanding her for wanting some independence.
- **Reuben:** But she shouldn't be camping out in the Highweald forest on her own. It's not safe.
- **Player:** Why haven't you gone looking for her?
- **Reuben:** It's a difficult time for us. Have a feeling I'm the last person she'd want to see right now.
- **Reuben:** Last time she went hiking through the Highweald alone Adam practically chewed her ear off. Don't need 'im getting involved again either.
- **Reuben:** She said she was going to explore the ruined watchtower by the passage into the mountains. If you're 'eaded that way, would you mind keeping an eye out?
- **Player:** I'll let you know if I find her.
- **Reuben:** Thanks, mate. Appreciate it.

#### At the tannery, afterwards, before finding Tali

- **Reuben:** Have you found Tali? I can't rest easy knowing she's still out there.
- **Reuben:** She said she was going to explore the ruined watchtower by the passage into the mountains. If you're 'eaded that way, would you mind keeping an eye out?
- **Player:** I'll let you know if I find her.
- **Reuben:** Thanks, mate. Appreciate it.

#### Out of the tannery, with Tali

- **Reuben:** What were you doing out there? Are you hurt? Why didn't you come home?
- **Tali:** I'm-
- **Tali:** I'm sorry, papa. I only wanted to go somewhere I could remember the good times. Away from all of this...misery. But I lost my equipment, and...
- ((Tali sighs.))
- **Reuben:** I understand. I do. But it's not safe wandering around the forest on your own. Even Adam wouldn't-
- **Tali:** I know!
- **Tali:** I know...
- **Reuben:** ...
- **Reuben:** Listen. I can't force you to stay here. If you want to explore the ruins out east or wherever 'ave you, it's not my place to stop you.
- **Tali:** This is my home, and you're my family. I'd never want to leave for good.
- **Tali:** But it would be wonderful to see the archaeology guild again. To follow in father's footsteps and go on on proper expeditions!
- **Player:** You're welcome to join me some time, if you'd like.
- **Tali:** I'd love to!
- **Reuben:** Just take care of yourself, alright? I couldn't bear to lose you too.
- **Tali:** I will. I promise.
- **Reuben:** Thank you for all your help, friend. If you need any hides tanned, come see me, you 'ear? It's on the house.

#### At the tannery, after completing Finding Tali

- **Reuben:** Hey there, mate. If you need hides tanned, I'm your man.
- **Reuben:** I made Zeke's armour, you know. My finest work, if I do say so myself.
- **Player:** I'll think about it.

#### Below the watchtower with Tali

- **Tali:** Good to see you again [player].
- **Player:** You too. I'm not intruding, am I?
- **Reuben:** You're good. I was just about to share a tale my grandfather used to tell me about this 'ere tower. You can stay and listen, if you'd like.
- **Player:** I'm all ears.
- **Tali:** Your grandfather told you stories about this place?
- **Reuben:** He went on a journey across Havenhythe, when he came of age. Said our ancestors found this tower when they arrived and repurposed it for their needs.
- **Reuben:** It started as a watchtower but became a place of worship. Those who followed the God of Order gathered 'ere all hush hush.
- **Tali:** That explains all the stars of Saradomin.
- **Reuben:** People started gossiping about the rites they performed here. Suspicion let to fear. Followers of a rival god came in the dead of night and set the tower ablaze.
- **Player:** History doesn't repeat, but it often rhymes.
- **Reuben:** We've always been a small community. That sort of division would 'ave been the end of us.
- **Tali:** What happened?
- **Reuben:** They tore down every shrine, but one. A small altar on the outskirts of town. One where all gods and their followers would have equal standing.
- **Reuben:** Still stands today, at the shrine to Inanna.
- **Tali:** Did your grandfather tell you a lot of stories about the history of Havenhythe?
- **Reuben:** Oh, he had a tale for just about everything!
- **Reuben:** Not unlike your father.
- **Tali:** ...
- **Tali:** I miss him. I miss him so much.
- **Reuben:** Yeah, I do too.
- **Player:** Thank you for the story. Take care, both of you.
