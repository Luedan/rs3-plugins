## Dialogue Transcript


### Give ectoplasm


#### Without ectoplasm

- You don't have anything to hand in.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You don't have anything to hand in.

#### With ectoplasm

- (removes=ectoplasm)
- You hand in: [number] x Ectoplasm.

### Talk to

- **Reanimated skeleton:** About time someone showed up to help. You see those ghosts? Get me their ectoplasm. Quick, now, before my bones fall apart again.

### 30 seconds before the event ends

- {{Qact|The ghosts and Graveside Phantoms disappear.}}
- {{Colour: Give me the resources now!}}

### Completing the event successfully

- For completing this event you receive: 1 x Sack of wild rewards.
