## Dialogue Transcript


### In Behind the Scenes

- **Romeo:** 'A lovestruck Romeo sings a street suss serenade...'
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: That song sounds familiar.
    - **Romeo:** I've been down here since 2010 and I still haven't gotten used to it.
    - **Player:** Gotten used to what?
    - **Romeo:** All the references.
    - **Romeo:** Up in Gielinor there are loads of references all the time, but no one ever talks about it.
    - **Player:** Oh yeah, didn't they remove you from the game because you were too blatant a reference?
    - **Romeo:** Yes!
    - **Romeo:** Down here though it's all pop culture all the time.
    - **Player:** Why do you think there are so many references in the game?
    - ** Romeo:** There's two good reasons and one bad one, I reckon.
    - **Romeo:** Good reason one is to show respect for a source of inspiration.
    - **Romeo:** All art is imitative, and a well-placed reference acknowledges that fact without disrupting the flow of the work.
    - **Player:** Oh, like homages?
    - **Romeo:** Yes, exactly. Good reason two is to create a sense of camaraderie with the audience.
    - **Romeo:** Including a reference signals to the audience that the author shares their life experiences and loves all the same things.
    - **Player:** What's the bad reason?
    - **Romeo:** When it's used as a substitute for actual content. Using a lazy reference because you couldn't think of a line yourself is as bad as using a cliche.
    - **Romeo:** One other important thing is, the perfect reference is visible to those who get it, but invisible to those who don't.
    - **Player:** Hm?
    - **Romeo:** If it's obvious that it's a reference but you don't know what it's referencing, that makes you feel excluded from an in-joke.
    - **Player:** You must hate this quest then?
    - **Romeo:** Yeah...
    - **Player:** You've put a lot of thought into this.
    - **Romeo:** Surprising, isn't it? I'll be honest though, on any other subject I'm still the same randy old dog as always.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)
