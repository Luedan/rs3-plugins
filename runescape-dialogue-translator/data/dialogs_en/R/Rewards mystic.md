All the rewards are made from the same sacred clay that your team works so hard to harvest, which gives them some rather special properties.
    - What would you like to say?
      - Tell me more about tool rewards.
        - **Player:** Tell me more about tool rewards.
        - **Rewards Mystic:** These hallowed tools are made from sacred clay, and can be transformed into a pickaxe, woodcutting hatchet, harpoon, butterfly net, fletching knife, hammer or needle. The volatile tool is made of more powerful clay, but is more unstable, so although you get more bonus experience, you have no control over which tool it becomes. Eventually, either type of tool will run out of sacred power and stop working. You can always come back here and buy more charges for it with points from the primordial realm.

        - **Player:** Tell me more about equipment rewards.

When you operate a piece of the morphic armour and choose a new combat style, all currently equipped morphic armour and weapons will change to that style of combat. While you are using the equipment, you will gain extra experience in whichever combat skills you are training. Eventually, the armour will run out of sacred energy, but you can always come back and earn more charges for it.
