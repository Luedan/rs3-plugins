## Dialogue Transcript


### In Rimmington


#### Talking to him

- **Rommik:** Would you like to buy some Crafting equipment?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Let's see what you've got then.
    - (opens=Rommik's Crafty Supplies)
  - [Choice]: No thanks, I've got all the crafting equipment I need.
    - **Player:** No thanks, I've got all the crafting equipment I need.
    - **Rommik:** Okay. Fare well on your travels.

#### Treasure Trails

- (Condition: Only the first time:)
  - **Player:** I've come about this treasure trail.
  - **Rommik:** Fair enough. Answer my question and you can continue.
- (receives=challenge scroll (medium))
- [Rommik has given you a challenge scroll!]

##### When talked to again

- **Rommik:** Please enter the answer to the question.
- Enter amount:<br>[Input field]
- (Condition: When given the wrong answer:)
  - **Rommik:** Are you sure about that?
- (Condition: When given the correct answer:)
  - (removes=the clue scroll (medium) and challenge scroll (medium))}
  - ((If the trail continues:))
    - (receives=a new clue scroll (medium))
    - [Rommik has given you another clue scroll!]
  - ((If the trail ends:))
    - (receives=reward casket (medium))
    - [You've found a casket!]
  - **Rommik:** Bang on!
