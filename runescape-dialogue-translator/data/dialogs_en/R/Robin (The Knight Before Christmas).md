- **Letter**: `R`

## Dialogue Transcript

- *(If the player doesn't own a Yak Track Task List:)*
  - (receives=Yak Track Task List)
  - (continue)
- *(Only on first time:)*
  - **Robin:** Ho ho barooooooooooooooo.
  - **Player:** Ahhh! It's a talking Reindeer!
  - **Robin:** Actually, 'it' is a talking Yak. A Christmas Yak with a big red nose. The best kind of Yak.
  - **Player:** Ahhh!
  - **Robin:** Merry Christmas to you too, Player.
  - **Player:** How do you know my name!?
  - **Robin:** Santa isn't the only one with a naughty list you know.
  - **Player:** ...
  - **Player:** So what is a talking Reindeer doing here anyway?
  - **Robin:** Well, Player. I've been asked to deliver you all a Christmas themed Yak Track of course! To make things extra festive, you'll be tasked with Present Hunts throughout the track.
  - (continue|present-hunt)
- *(After the first time:)*
  - **Robin:** Hello again, Player. How can I help you?
  - (continue)
- Select an option{{anchor|init-opts}}
  - How does the Yak Track work?
    - **Player:** How does the Yak Track work?
    - **Robin:** You get to go on an adventure and earn rewards along the way. You can complete tasks to progress along the track.
    - **Robin:** You will be given a choice between two tasks at a time, but you only need to complete one of them to progress. If you don't feel like doing either of them then you can use a task skip to move on. As you progress along the track you will earn rewards; Premier Club members and those who have purchased a Premier Pass will earn additional premium rewards. If you purchase a Premier Pass you will receive any premium rewards you would have been eligible for.
    - **Player:** Sounds great!
    - **Robin:** Is there anything else I can help you with, Player?
    - (initial|init-opts)
  - How do Present Hunt tasks work?
    - **Robin:{{anchor|present-hunt}}** During Present Hunt tasks, you'll find presents scattered all over Gielinor. I've added an extra touch of Christmas magic, so you may find that presents relocate from time to time, just to make things a little different. Each present you collect will bag you a festive goodie, and will progress your active Present Hunt task.
    - **Player:** Sounds great! So how do I find these presents?
    - **Robin:** Why using a set of Magical jingle bells of course! The bells can be used to obtain location clues for each present. You can also ring the bells, causing them to glow. This will alert you of any nearby presents for the next minute.
    - *(If the player doesn't have magical jingle bells:)*
      - **Robin:** Here, Player, I actually have a set right here for you.
      - (receives=magical jingle bells)
      - Robin hands you a set of Magical jingle bells. You can use these to assist you with Present Hunt tasks.
      - **Player:** Thanks Robin!
      - (continue)
    - **Robin:** Is there anything else I can help you with, Player?
    - (initial|init-opts)
  - Bye!
    - **Player:** Bye!
    - **Robin:** Bye, Player. Merry baroooooooooooooooooooo.
