# Rowdy dwarf

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Rowdy dwarf](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARowdy%20dwarf)
- **Letter**: `R`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In eastern Keldagrim


#### Standard dialogue

- **Rowdy dwarf:** Get OUT of my WAY!

#### Minitask dialogue

- **Rowdy dwarf:** Hey you! Human person thing! I've got a lil' task for you!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What can I do for you then?
    - **Player:** What can I do for you then?
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** Oooo, I want ehm... What did I want again?
    - **Player:** You tell me.
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** I want... a [item]! What do you mean, what am I going to do with a [item]? That's my business it is!
    - **Player:** I didn't say anything...
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** Good, good... now get me a [item] within [number] minutes, and I'll have ehm... I don't know, I'll have something for you!
  - [Choice]: Stay away from me!
    - **Player:** Stay away from me!
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** Awww, you’re just a worthless human, stop wasting my time!

##### Talking to him without the item

- **Rowdy dwarf:** Got my ehm, ehm... got whatever I asked you to get yet, human?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: No, and I can't be bothered anymore.
    - **Player:** No, and I can't be bothered anymore.
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** Bah, you're just a no good lousy human.
  - [Choice]: No, not yet, what was it again?
    - **Player:** Sorry, no, not yet... What was it again anyway?
    - **Rowdy dwarf:** Lemme think... ehm... yeah, I want you to get a [item], no questions asked!

##### Talking to him with the item

- **Player:** Look, I've got what you asked me to get!
- **Rowdy dwarf:** Did you now, did you? Ehm.
- **Rowdy dwarf:** What did I ask you to get again?
- **Player:** You wanted me to get a [item], remember? Well, here you go!
- **Rowdy dwarf:** Ooo, that'sh great! Let me get your reward... here, some coins for you.
- (receives=coins)
