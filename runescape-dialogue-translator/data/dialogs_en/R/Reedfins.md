## Dialogue Transcript

- **Player:** Why aren't you cutting the trees with the others?
- **Reedfins:** Do you see da faces on da trees? They always watching us...
- **Player:** They're just trees. They don't have eyes.
- **Reedfins:** Maybe not now, but once maybe. I can feel them on me.
- **Reedfins:** That why I not chopping them anymore. Feels wrong.
- **Reedfins:** And how can I be a woodcutter if I not cut wood?
- **Reedfins:** So just rethinking whole life.
