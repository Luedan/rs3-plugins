## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Rhod:** Hello there! I've not seen anyone arriving here for ages. I'm Rhod.
- **Player:** Player. The {{Sic|rest of island}} is struggling with this Sanguine threat.
- **Rhod:** That what you're calling whatever has happened to the folks over in Berylbrook?
- **Player:** Yes - it's some kind of blood affliction.
- **Rhod:** Like we didn't already have one of those to deal with... You know, what with being werewolves.
- **Rhod:** Still, those 'sanguine werewolves', like you say - they don't cross over to us, so you're safe here.
- **Rhod:** Unless one of US chooses to eat you...
- **Player:** What?
- **Rhod:** Kidding. I use dark humour to hide my unease. Enjoy your visit!
