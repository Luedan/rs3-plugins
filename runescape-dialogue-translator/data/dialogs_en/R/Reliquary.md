## Dialogue Transcript


### Searching the Reliquary


#### Before claiming the Holy Cithara

- [Inside the reliquary are some ancient fragments of bone, and a jewelled cithara. You feel a sense of awe as you approach the instrument, as if a holy power watches over it.]
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Attempt to take the cithara.
    - (Condition: If wearing the incorrect ring:)
      - An invisible force gently but firmly moves your hand away from the cithara.
    - (Condition: If worthy to take the Cithara:)
      - **Spirit of Saint Elspeth:** O holy rapture! That mine eyes, long dead, should even now behold a living saint! Please, pilgrim, take my blessed instrument, and play and sing to Saradomin's praise.
      - (receives=the Holy Cithara)
      - [Saint Elspeth allows you to take the cithara.]
      - {{RSFontAchievement|Reliquary of Cithara}}
    - (Condition: If not worthy:)
      - **Spirit of Saint Elspeth:** You are so close to worthy, but fall short. You may return in time, to try again.
    - (Condition: If not capable of ghostspeak:)
      - **Spirit of Saint Elspeth:** Woooo... Woooooo...
      - The spirit prevents you from taking the cithara.
  - [Choice]: Leave the cithara where it is.
    - (end)

#### Afterwards

- Inside the reliquary is nothing but some ancient fragments of bone.
