## Dialogue Transcript


### In Taverley

- **Runestax:** Peace in balance, friend.

### At the tribute stone, north of Falador

- **Runestax:** Peace in balance, friend.
- **Player:** What do you do here?
- **Runestax:** Every year, we come back here on pilgrimage to this tribute stone. It is a stack of rune essences brought by all those who wanted to pay Guthix their respects after his passing.
- **Runestax:** You can use it to pray and commune, just like us.
- **Player:** Thanks!
