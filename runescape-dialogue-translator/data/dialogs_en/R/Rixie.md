## Dialogue Transcript

- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - Who are you?
    - **Rixie:** I'm Rixie, one of the vendors here at the fayre, so you can buy yourself a lovely souvenir to take home with you.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - How do the portals work?
    - **Rixie:** They're amazing, aren't they? We had them made by a wizard, a long time ago now. They're splendid things, never had a problem with them.
    - **Rixie:** We simply set them up in the new location for the fayre and they calibrate themselves. It's quite incredible to watch.
    - **Rixie:** They seem to know where the most useful places in the near vicinity are and set themselves up to point to there.
    - **Rixie:** It's just my job to work out how many we need. When they stop glowing, were{{Sic}} all done.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
- *(Only if not selected last:)*
  - How are things going?
    - **Rixie:** It's a noisy life, living and working at the fayre, but I wouldn't change it for the world.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
