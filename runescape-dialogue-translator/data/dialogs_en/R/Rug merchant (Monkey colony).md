# Rug merchant (Monkey colony)

- **Letter**: `R`

## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Desert Monkey Colony


#### While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Rug merchant:** Ook. Ah Uh Ah! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ah Ook!
- You cannot understand what he is saying.
- **Assistant:** Perhaps I can be of use?
- **Player:** Not unless you speak Monkey.
- *(One of the following conversations will play:)*
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Assistant:** Ook! Akk! Oookawwwaaa!
    - The monkey seems to understand him.
    - ((Magic carpet network interface opens.))
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Assistant:** Oook! Akkk! Awwwwakkaa!
    - The monkey gives you a banana.
    - (receives=banana)
    - **Assistant:** Ah, I am a little rusty, it seems. Ook! Akk! Oookawwwaaa!
    - The monkey seems to understand him.
    - ((Magic carpet network interface opens.))

#### While wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Rug merchant:** Welcome to Chimpish Airways: the premium monkey carpet station for a monkey in a hurry.
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: I'd like to travel.
    - **Player:** I'd like to travel.
    - **Rug merchant:** Of course, all travel is free for you from this hub. Where would you like to go?
    - ((Magic carpet network interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: What does Ali Morrisane think of this set up?
    - **Player:** What does Ali Morrisane think of this set up?
    - **Rug merchant:** He came here, with a monkeyspeak amulet, and accused us of stealing his carpets. He was pretty angry at first, but, with a bit of heavy-handed negotiation, we made a deal.
    - **Player:** A deal?
    - **Rug merchant:** He collects money on carpets flying to us, and we collect money on the carpets flying towards his stations. Win-win.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What's up with your assistant?
    - **Player:** What's up with your assistant?
    - **Rug merchant:** Oh yeah, he's a bit miserable, isn't he? Last time we ever hire someone by post. I thought humans were supposed to be cutesy and full of energy; if anything, he's driving away business.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see what's lost and found.
    - (Condition: If there is lost and found to be collected:)
      - (receives=Bag of lost items)
      - **Rug merchant:** Yes, I have found many things that have not been claimed. They fall from people's packs as the carpets fly up. Here, have one. Come back tomorrow for more!
    - (Condition: If the player has collected for the day, or is not eligible:)
      - **Rug merchant:** Sorry, I haven't found anything yet. Come back tomorrow.
  - [Choice]: I've got to go.
    - **Player:** I've got to go.
    - **Rug merchant:** On behalf of Chimpish Airways, we hope to see you soon.
