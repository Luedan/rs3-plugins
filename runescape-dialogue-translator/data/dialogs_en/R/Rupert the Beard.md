## Dialogue Transcript


### At the tower north of Falador


#### Before Grim Tales (58 Thieving required)

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Drain Pipe:** I say! Where on Gielinor did that voice come from?
- **Player:** Uh, hello there! Can you hear me?
- **Drain Pipe:** Why yes. Yes I can. My word. Is that...is that one of the gods? Guthix perhaps? Saradomin?
- **Player:** No, no. I'm not either of them.
- **Drain Pipe:** Egad! You're not...Zamorak...are you? I mean, uh...begging your pardon of course, my Lord. No offence intended!
- **Player:** Ha ha! No, I'm speaking to you through the pipe here! I'm at the bottom of your tower.
- **Drain Pipe:** Oh. Oh I see! Oh, yes. How clever! Remarkable idea! However did you think of that? Good show, old bean!
- **Player:** Who are you?
- **Drain Pipe:** Rupert the Beard. At your service, old fruit.
- **Player:** Rupert the Beard?
- **Drain Pipe:** Yes, old turnip. On account of my wonderfully long bearded chin, you see.
- **Player:** So what are you doing up there exactly? And how come you talk so strangely?
- **Drain Pipe:** What am I doing? Well, I guess I'm stuck in a matter of speaking. Dear me, yes! I'm stuck. And I speak in the King's speech, I'll have you know.
- **Player:** Really? Which king?
- **Drain Pipe:** Which K...? Why, THE King old spud! THE King! Only one worth mentioning in my opinion.
- **Player:** I see. Well is there anything I can do to help you up there?
- **Drain Pipe:** Hmmm. No, no, I can't think of anything at the moment. I'm a bit stuck. Let me know if you get any ideas though.
- **Player:** I'll do my best!

#### After Grim Tales

- **Player:** Hello, Rupert.
- **Rupert the Beard:** Hello again, old cheesecake! Wonderful to see you.
- **Player:** How are the investigations going?
- **Rupert the Beard:** Thanks to you freeing me, very well! I have much more to do of course, but now I am free, I can continue where I left off.
- **Player:** That's great news!
- (Condition: If the player has lost their dwarven helmet:)
  - **Player:** Ummm...
  - **Rupert the Beard:** What's the trouble, old bellybutton fluff?
  - **Player:** I lost that helmet you gave me.
  - **Rupert the Beard:** I see. Well you might be in luck. I have recently made another one to replace it. I guess you could have it for 60,000 gold. For the materials, you understand.
  - [Menu]: Select an Option
    - [Choice]: Ok, that sounds fair.
      - **Player:** Ok, that sounds fair.
      - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
        - **Rupert the Beard:** Hmm. It looks like you're going to need more space in your inventory first, old jumper. Come back and see me again when you've made some room.
        - **Player:** Ok.
      - (Condition: With less than 60,000 coins:)
        - You can not afford that.
        - **Rupert the Beard:** Hmm. It looks like you don't have enough on you, old porridge. Come back and see me again when you have a bit more gold on you.
        - **Player:** Ok.
      - (Condition: With 60,000 coins:)
        - (removes=60,000 coins) (receives=dwarven helmet)
        - **Rupert the Beard:** There you are! I hope this one serves you well enough, old cabbage.
        - **Player:**Thanks, Rupert!
    - [Choice]: I don't think I can afford that, sorry.
      - **Player:**I don't think I can afford that, sorry.
      - **Rupert the Beard:** No problem! Come back and see me if you change your mind, old garters.
