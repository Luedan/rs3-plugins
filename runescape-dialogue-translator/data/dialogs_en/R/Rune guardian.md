## Dialogue Transcript


#### Conversation 1

- **Rune guardian:** Have you ever thought that, when you examine an item, that item is also examining you?
- **Player:** I can't say it's ever occurred to me.

#### Conversation 2

- **Player:** How can I succeed in combat?
- **Rune guardian:** You must lash out with every limb, like the octopus who plays the bongos.
- **Player:** I thought it was the penguins that played the bongos?
- **Rune guardian:** Only during 'Happy Hour.'

#### Conversation 3

- **Player:** What do you want to do now?
- **Rune guardian:** Seeking wisdom is most fulfilling. It is sometimes disguised as a pebble in your shoe, or a baguette, but it is there.
- **Player:** I'll keep my eyes open.

#### Conversation 4

- **Player:** Hmm, I'm not sure I feel like training today.
- **Rune guardian:** He who questions training only trains himself at asking questions.
