## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hi!
- **Rufus:** Grrreetings frrriend! Welcome to my worrrld famous food emporrrium! All my meats are so frrresh you'd swear you killed them yourrrself!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Why do you only sell meats?
    - **Player:** Why do you only sell meats?
    - **Rufus:** What? Why, what else would you want to eat? What kind of lycanthrrrope are you anyway?
    - **Player:** ...A vegetarian one?
    - ** Rufus:** Vegetarrrian...?
    - **Player:** Never mind.
  - [Choice]: Do you sell cooked food?
    - **Player:** Do you sell cooked food?
    - **Rufus:** Cooked food? Who would want that? You lose all the flavourrr of the meat when you can't taste the blood!
  - [Choice]: Can I buy some food?
    - **Player:** Can I buy some food?
    - **Rufus:** Cerrrtainly!
    - (opens=Rufus's Meat Emporium)
