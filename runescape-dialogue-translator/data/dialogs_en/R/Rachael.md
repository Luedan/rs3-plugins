## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Burthorpe Games Room

- **Rachael:** Welcome to the Burthorpe Games Rooms!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: How do I play board games?
    - **Player:** How do I play board games?
    - **Rachael:** You can challenge someone to a game anywhere in the games rooms by using the right click option. Choose the type of game and then choose the options you want such as time per move. If you want to play a particular game
    - **Rachael:** there are challenge rooms dedicated to that game. In the challenge rooms you can see other players ranks by right clicking them, their skill will be displayed instead of their combat level. Once you have enough experience you will be
    - **Rachael:** able to use the challenge rooms on the first floor! If your opponent accepts the challenge you will be taken to one of the tables in the main room where you will play your game of choice. Once you have finished your game you will
    - **Rachael:** go back to the challenge room where you can challenge again!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: What games can I play?
    - **Player:** What games can I play?
    - **Rachael:** Currently we offer Draughts, Runelink, Runesquares, and Runeversi.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Draughts?
        - **Player:** Draughts?
        - **Rachael:** Draughts uses standard rules, apart from: a draw is declared if no piece has been taken or promoted for forty moves. To play Draughts go to the challenge room in the SW corner.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Runelink?
        - **Player:** Runelink?
        - **Rachael:** Yup, you have to get four runes in a row. The challenge room for Runelink is in the SE corner.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Runesquares?
        - **Player:** Runesquares?
        - **Rachael:** Yes, you take it in turns to add a line with the goal of making squares. Everytime you make a square you take another turn. The challenge room for Runesquares is in the SW corner.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Runeversi?
        - **Player:** Runeversi?
        - **Rachael:** Yep, the aim is to have more of your runes on the board than your opponent. You can take your opponents pieces by trapping them between your own. The challenge room for Runeversi is in the SE corner.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do you have any news?
    - **Player:** Do you have any news?
    - **Rachael:** There are some notice boards in the main entrance, check them from time to time for the latest news.
  - [Choice]: Can I buy a drink please?
    - **Player:** Can I buy a drink please?
    - **Rachael:** Certainly [sir/miss], our speciality is Asgarnian Ale, we also serve Wizard's Mind Bomb and Dwarven Stout.
    - (opens=The Toad and Chicken)
  - [Choice]: Thanks!
    - **Player:** Thanks!
