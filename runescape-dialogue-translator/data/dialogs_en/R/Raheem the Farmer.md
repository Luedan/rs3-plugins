## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Raheem the Farmer:** Oh, er, hello. What do you want?
- Select an option
  - What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about Al Kharid?
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** There's not much farming land around here. Only that little patch outside.
    - **Player:** Can you give me any advice on farming here in the desert?
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** Like I said, I only know about cactuses...
    - **Player:** Just tell me about cactuses, then.
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** First you have to weed the patch using a rake.
    - **Player:** Can you give me any other advice?
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** Not really. I've not done much farming recently.
    - **Player:** Well, can you at least sell me any gardening tools or seeds?
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** Sorry. They haven't been delivered yet.
  - So, what do you do here?
    - **Player:** So, what do you do here?
    - **Raheem the Farmer:** I'm going to set up a shop selling farming implements. That patch out there may be small, but it's all we've got.
    - (above|advice)
  - *If the player has asked any of the Al Kharid merchants about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Player:** I hear you work for Ali Morrisane...
      - **Raheem the Farmer:** Yes, he bought these tents and had them put up for us. He says he'll also get our goods in so we can start selling them soon.
      - **Raheem the Farmer:** I only know how to farm cactuses, so this spot is perfect.
      - **Player:** Maybe I should talk to him...
      - **Raheem the Farmer:** Of course. He's always happy to talk to possible business partners.
  - *If the player has asked Shop assistant or Shopkeeper about Ali Morrisane:*
    - I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Player:** I hear you've been threatening the other shopkeepers.
      - **Raheem the Farmer:** Now why would you think that?
      - **Player:** One of the shopkeepers told they were threatened by a man with a rake...
      - **Raheem the Farmer:** Those people just don't want us to succeed. Don't listen to them!
