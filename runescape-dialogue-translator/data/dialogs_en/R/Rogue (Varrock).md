## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Rogues' Castle

- **Rogue:** Have you come to bust me out of here?
- Select an Option
  - Why are you locked up here?
    - **Player:** Why are you locked up here?
    - **Rogue:** I used to be a part of the group of rogues here, until they accused me of having stolen the gold we had stolen from people who had previously stolen it from someone else.
    - **Player:** So they locked you up?
    - **Rogue:** Yeah, I kept telling them I didn't steal nothing, but they just refused to believe me.
    - **Player:** So, did you steal it?
    - **Rogue:** That's not the point! What's important is that I said I didn't.
    - **Player:** Eh...I see.
    - **Rogue:** So, have you come to release me or what?
    - Select an Option
      - Yes, I have.
        - (continue|Yes)
      - No, I haven't.
        - (continue|No)
  - Yes, I have.
    - **Player:** Yes, I have. The doors were locked but I used this magical ring to travel into a parallel realm where all recent changes are opposite to the other side. So by closing the doors there, I opened the doors he-
    - **Rogue:** Man, did you break the doors open using your head, or what? Regardless, I appreciate the help, man. If you ever need anything, come see me at my place in Varrock. I've got a business you might be interested in.
    - **Player:** Whereabouts in Varrock?
    - **Rogue:** Assuming it hasn't been occupied by any of the muggers, I've got a place in the not-so-nice parts of town, right behind the archery store. Come and see me there. Anyway, I've got to run now before my so-called companions realise what you and your thick head have done.
    - The prisoner sneaks out of the castle and heads south towards Varrock.
    - {{Qact|Screen fades out to black then back in; the rogue has gone.}}
  - No, I haven't.
    - **Player:** No, I haven't. I'm just here to make sure you haven't gotten your hands on anything to eat.
    - **Rogue:** Aw, man, I keep telling you, I didn't steal the gold. Just let me out.
    - **Player:** Uh...no.

### In Varrock


#### Talking to the rogue

- **Rogue:** Welcome to my humble abode. What can I do for you?
- Select an Option
  - You mentioned a business.
    - **Player:** Up at the Rogues' Castle, you mentioned a business. What was that about?
    - **Rogue:** Ah, yes, I run...what do you call it? Something of a little jewellery business. So, if you happen to come across any unenchanted gold jewellery, I'll gladly take it off your hands for a decent sum of coins.
    - **Player:** Only gold jewellery?
    - **Rogue:** Correct. I'm not buying anything that isn't beautiful, bright gold. My...eh...clients are picky about that.
    - Select an Option
      - I have some right here.
        - **Player:** I have some right here.
        - **Rogue:** Good, good, just hand it over and I'll see what I can give for it.
      - Alright, I'll remember that.
        - **Player:** Alright, I'll remember that.
  - Nothing.
    - **Player:** Nothing.

#### Selling items


##### Using an item on the rogue that he doesn't buy

- **Rogue:** Sorry, but I don't deal with those. I only trade unenchanted gold jewellery, nothing else. Bring notes if you like, but I prefer the real deal.

##### Selling a single item

- **Rogue:** I'll give you [price] coins for that [item]. Do we have a deal?
- Select an Option
  - Yes, we do.
    - (removes=the piece of gold jewellery used on the rogue) (receives=the number of gold coins stated above)
    - **Rogue:** It was a pleasure doing business with you. Come back if you have more jewellery to sell.
  - No, we do not.
    - **Player:** No, we do not.

##### Selling multiple items


##### If there are more than 10,000 items in the stack

- **Rogue:** Whoa, that's quite a bit of jewellery you've got there! Please try to keep it in amounts smaller than 10,000. Big numbers make my head hurt.

##### If there are 10,000 or fewer items in the stack


##### If the player doesn't have 100,000,000 coins

- **Rogue:** That's a nice [item] you've got there. I'll give you [price] for the bunch. Do we have a deal?
- Select an Option
  - Yes, we do.
    - (removes=all pieces of the type of gold jewellery used on the rogue) (receives=the number of gold coins stated above)
    - **Rogue:** It was a pleasure doing business with you. Come back if you have more jewellery to sell.
  - No, we do not.
    - **Player:** No, we do not.

##### If the player has at least 100,000,000 coins

- **Rogue:** You've got enough money as it is. It usually doesn't bother me, but now you're just being absurd.
