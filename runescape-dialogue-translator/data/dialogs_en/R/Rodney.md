## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- **Rodney:** This kitchen is truly blessed by some divine force!
- **Rodney:** My knives never seem to dull and I am constantly filled with culinary inspiration!
- At tier 1, you will gain access to a range, bank and sink in the kitchen. Your knives will also be sharpened and allow you to never fail cutting through spider webs.
- At tier 2, you will gain access to a shop stocked with useful items for cooking. You will also have a reduced chance to burn food cooked within the kitchen by 3%.
- At tier 3, you will gain access to a soup creation station, benefiting from any workshop XP upgrades in your Player Owned Port. You will also gain 5% additional experience for cooking in the fort kitchen.
- **Player:** What happened to that cabbage that was here anyway?
- **Rodney:** It made a fantastic soup!

### Trade


#### With Tier 1 Kitchen

- **Rodney:** I'm afraid I don't have any surplus stock to sell off.
- Rodney's shop will unlock when you have a tier 2 kitchen.
