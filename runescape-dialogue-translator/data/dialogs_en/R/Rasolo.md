# Rasolo

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Rasolo](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARasolo)
- **Letter**: `R`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Desert Treasure

- **Rasolo:** Greetings friend. I am Rasolo, the famous merchant. Would you care to see my wares?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes
    - (opens=Rasolo the Wandering Merchant)
  - [Choice]: No
    - **Player:** No, not really.
    - **Rasolo:** As you wish. I will travel wherever the business takes me.

### After Desert Treasure

- **Rasolo:** Many thanks for returning my heirloom to me, adventurer. <br>Would you like to buy something?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes
    - (opens=Rasolo the Wandering Merchant)
  - [Choice]: No
    - **Player:** No, not really.
    - **Rasolo:** As you wish. I will travel wherever the business takes me.
  - [Choice]: I lost that ring you gave me...
    - **Player:** I lost that ring you gave me...
    - (Condition: If the player has genuinely lost the ring of visibility:)
      - (receives=ring of visibility)
      - **Rasolo:** Then by all means, take another. Only a foolish merchant would give away his only stock!
    - (Condition: If the player still has the ring of visibility:)
      - **Rasolo:** Are you sure? I don't have it anymore...
