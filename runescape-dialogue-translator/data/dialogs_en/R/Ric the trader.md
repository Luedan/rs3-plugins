# Ric the trader

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Ric the trader](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARic%20the%20trader)
- **Letter**: `R`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### During the bonus experience trading period

- **Ric the trader:** Hello there, you interested in trading in your bonus experience?
  - Who are you?
    - **Ric the trader:** I am a trader of sorts. I can take your bonus experience and offer you compensation in the form of various rare objects.
    - **Player:** Does it hurt?
    - **Ric the trader:** Not at all! I get your bonus experience and you can get experience of your choice in return - or, if you prefer, you could have some rare items.
    - **Player:** How does it work? What are you doing with the experience your take?
    - **Ric the trader:** That's not for you to know. I'm not going to be around forever, so make good use of the offer while I'm in the area!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - I would like to trade in some bonus experience.
    - *(Skill selection interface opens.)*
  - I would like to buy something.
    - *(Vic's Store interface opens.)*
  - No thanks.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*

### After the bonus experience trading period

- **Ric the trader:** Hello there, are you interested in buying items with your credits?
  - Who are you?
    - **Ric the trader:** I am a trader of sorts. I can take your bonus experience and offer you compensation in the form of various rare objects.
    - **Player:** Does it hurt?
    - **Ric the trader:** Not at all! I get your bonus experience and you can get experience of your choice in return - or, if you prefer, you could have some rare items.
    - **Player:** How does it work? What are you doing with the experience your take?
    - **Ric the trader:** That's not for you to know. I'm not going to be around forever, so make good use of the offer while I'm in the area!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - I would like to trade in some bonus experience.
    - **Ric the trader:** I am no longer trading, but you can still spend the credits you have earned.
  - I would like to buy something.
    - *(Vic's Store interface opens.)*
  - No thanks.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*

### Using an [[elemental shield]] on Ric

- **Ric the trader:** Ah, No! Anything but that!

### Using any [[prismatic fallen star]] on Ric

- **Ric the trader:** You'll have to absorb the experience from that yourself, before trading it with me!

### Using [[holy biscuits]] on Ric

- **Ric the trader:** Ah, yes! I vant! I vant a yummy biscuit!
