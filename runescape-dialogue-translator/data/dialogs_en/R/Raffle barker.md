# Raffle barker

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Raffle barker](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARaffle%20barker)
- **Letter**: `R`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Overhead lines

- *(One of the following is seen every 30 seconds:)*
  - **Raffle barker:** Balthazar Beauregard's RuneScape Raffle in Burthorpe.
  - **Raffle barker:** Check out Balthazar Beauregard's RuneScape Raffle in Burthorpe for great prizes!
  - **Raffle barker:** From the team that brought you the Greatest Show on RuneScape win prizes galore!
  - **Raffle barker:** Have you got your ticket? The prizes are huge!

### When talked to


#### During the raffle


##### Dialogue 1

- **Raffle barker:** Have you heard of Balthazar's RuneScape Raffle? Get your ticket south of the Burthorpe lodestone!
- **Player:** Ok thanks.

##### Dialogue 2

- **Raffle barker:** Roll up! Roll up! Head to Burthorpe to check out Balthazar's RuneScape Raffle!
- **Player:** Will do.

#### After the raffle

- **Raffle barker:** I'm sorry, the raffle is over now. You're too late! But you can hand in any leftover tickets to Balthazar at Burthorpe!
