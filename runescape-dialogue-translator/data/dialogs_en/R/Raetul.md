## Dialogue Transcript


### In Sophanem


#### After Icthlarin's Little Helper

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Raetul:** I see you have managed to mess things up again.
- **Player:** Why do you say that?
- **Raetul:** Well the ceremony should have been over by now, yet the plagues remain.
- **Player:** Unfortunately some other hapless adventurer was tricked in the same manner as me.
- **Raetul:** All you adventurers are alike!

#### After Contact!

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Raetul:** Well if it isn't the great adventurer Player? Ready to mess things up here again?
- **Player:** What are you talking about?
- **Raetul:** I've had these spots since the last time you tried to help. I'm wondering what's going to happen this time.
- **Player:** I fixed everything I disturbed, the spots you are suffering from are the result of the actions of another adventurer.
- **Raetul:** All you adventurers are alike!
- **Player:{{anchor|alike}}** In what way?
- **Raetul:** You're all trouble. Well I could hold it against you, but then again adventurers are my best customers. So may I interest you in any of my wares?

#### Using an acne potion on him

- **Raetul:** What are you doing?
- **Player:** I thought this acne potion might help ease your spots.
- **Raetul:** Ok let's give it a go so.
- **Raetul:** Aghhh!!! I got some in my eye. Owwww it stings.
- **Player:** Oh stop being so soft.
- **Raetul:** Well?
- **Player:** Well what?
- **Raetul:** Did it work?
- **Player:** Well...umm...no.
- **Raetul:** What? You intolerable, insufferable idiot. I've just burned out my eye for no reason? I would say get out of my sight but that's not far enough away.
- **Player:** Sorry I was just trying to help.
- **Raetul:** Scram or I'll give you something to feel sorry about!
