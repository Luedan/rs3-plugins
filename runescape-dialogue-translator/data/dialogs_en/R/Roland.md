# Roland

- **Letter**: `R`

## Dialogue Transcript


### When talked to

- **Roland:** Hey there, fancy taking a trip out to the deep sea fishing hub? Don't worry if you don't have the fishing level required to enter the guild, I know another way in!
- Select an option
  - What's there to do at the fishing hub?
    - **Roland:** Lots of fishing of course! Captain Groggy found such a great location in the deep sea, that he setup shop there. Lots of interesting sea creatures turn up now and then. The hub is also full of fish I've never seen before.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What are you doing here?
    - **Roland:** You see, my brother Goomah lives inside the fishing guild, providing access to the deep sea to all players who can gain access to the guild. I just happened to be in town to visit for a few days and thought, why not provide access to everyone? After all, I do know of a secret passage to the deep sea. Just make sure you don't tell my brother! He won't be best pleased.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Sure, let's go!
    - {{Qact|The player is transported to the Deep Sea Fishing hub.}}
    - Welcome to deep sea fishing! Feel free to explore, and when you are ready, follow the breadcrumb trail to the swarm fishing area, which you can fish at any level.
    - Don't forget, access to the area is time limited, and once the weekend is over, your access will be revoked unless you meet the 68 fishing level requirement.
