# Riki the sculptor's model

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Riki the sculptor's model](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARiki%20the%20sculptor%27s%20model)
- **Letter**: `R`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Giant Dwarf

- **Player:** Hello there, what are you doing here?
- **Riki the sculptor's model:** I... am the sculptor's associate.
- **Player:** His associate? What does that mean?
- **Riki the sculptor's model:** I... am the sculptor's associate.
- **Player:** Are you going to say anything meaningful at all?
- **Riki the sculptor's model:** I...-
- **Player:** No, apparently not.

### After The Giant Dwarf

- **Player:** I'm glad I don't have to talk to you anymore!
- **Riki the sculptor's model:** Hrm.

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Riki the sculptor's model:** Oh, I have a puzzle for you to solve.
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Riki has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Riki the sculptor's model:** That isn't right, keep trying.

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- (Condition: If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Riki has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Riki the sculptor's model:** Oh, you made it. That's a surprise.
