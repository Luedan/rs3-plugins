## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Runnynose:** Hey, you. Got any pearls?
- **Runnynose:** I like da shiny ones. *sniff*
- **Player:** What do you trade for them?
- **Runnynose:** Nothing. You just gives me.
- **Runnynose:** *sniff* Can't leave Globin' cos da totems. Scary.
- **Runnynose:** *sniff* Can't collect own pearls.
- (Condition: Without any telepearls:)
  - **Player:** Sorry, I don't.
  - **Runnynose:** Aww! Runnynose sad.
- (Condition: With at least one telepearl:)
  - **Player:** I have a telepearl.
  - **Runnynose:** Ooh, can escapes! Can I have?
  - [Menu]: Give telepearl to Runnynose?
    - [Choice]: Yes.
      - **Player:** Here you go.
      - (Condition: First time:)
        - (removes=1 x Bogolin telepearl) (receives=Divine Ichor V)
        - **Runnynose:** Thanks! You can have this. Is not shiny anyway.
      - (Condition: Afterwards:)
        - **Runnynose:** Thanks! Shiny! Can't escape anyway, just collect.
    - [Choice]: No.
      - **Runnynose:** Aww! Runnynose sad.
