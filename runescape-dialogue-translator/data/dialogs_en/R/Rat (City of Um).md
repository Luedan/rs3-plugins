## Dialogue Transcript


### Attempting to chase with a cat


#### Without catspeak

- ((Only on the first time:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Ratbusters' - Attempt to catch a ghostly rat.
  - (continue)
- **Player:** {{RSChat|Go on puss...kill that rat!}}
- {{Qact|The cat chases after the rat.}}
- **[Cat]:** {{RSChat|Meeeeeoooowww!}}
- The rat seems to phase right through your cat.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The rat seems to phase right through your cat.

#### With catspeak

- ((Only on the first time:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Ratbusters' - Attempt to catch a ghostly rat.
  - (continue)
- **Player:** Do you want to try catching that ghostly rat over there?
- **[Cat]:** Don't be ridiculous, that rat would phase right through me!

### When the "rat army" enters the City of Um at 92 Necromancy

- An army of rats have arrived across the city.

### Recruit

- **Rat:** {{RSChat|*squeak*}}
- (Condition: Not having recruited the rat before:)
  - 5 souls fly over to power the vessel.
  - [Name], the ghost rat, has been recruited into your undead rat army!
  - (continue)
- (Condition: With all the rats recruited:)
  - **Player:** Finally! My ghost rat army is complete!
