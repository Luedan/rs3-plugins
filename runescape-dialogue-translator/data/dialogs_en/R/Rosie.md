## Dialogue Transcript


### Petting Rosie in Burthorpe

- {{Qact|The player pets Rosie.}}
- (Condition: Without having received heartments and lovely gift box from Rosie:)
  - (Condition: Without at least 2 free spaces in backpack:)
    - Rosie attempts to give you Heartments and a Lovely gift box, but you don't have inventory space.
    - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
      - Rosie attempts to give you Heartments and a Lovely gift box, but you don't have inventory space.
  - (Condition: With at least 2 free spaces in backpack:)
    - (receives=250 heartments and a lovely gift box)
    - You receive 250 Heartments and a Lovely gift box from Rosie!
    - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
      - You receive 250 Heartments and a Lovely gift box from Rosie!
