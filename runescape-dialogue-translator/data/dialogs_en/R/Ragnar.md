## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Mountain Camp


#### Before Mountain Daughter

- **Player:** You don't look so well, is everything alright?
- **Ragnar:** No, outerlander. Now go away.

#### After Mountain Daughter

- **Ragnar:** It is most unusual, outerlander, that you were able to help us in this manner. But you have my thanks.
- **Ragnar:** Please, now, leave me alone for awhile while I watch the rippling of the water.

#### After Blood Runs Deep

- **Player:** I have good news. I have been to the afterlife and Asleif is there, happy and peaceful.
- **Ragnar:** Thank you, friend. This is great news.
