## Dialogue Transcript


### Before doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** You look rather out of place...
- **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** That I may do, but so do you! I suggest you speak with Brugsen Bursen or the Grand Exchange Tutor near the entrance for a lesson. Brugsen will give an interesting lesson on the Grand Exchange and the Tutor will give a smaller, plain lesson.

#### Info-logs option

- **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Not so fast! I suggest you speak with Brugsen Bursen or the Grand Exchange Tutor near the entrance for a lesson. Brugsen will give an interesting lesson on the Grand Exchange and the Tutor will give a smaller, plain lesson.

### After doing the Grand Exchange tutorial

- **Player:** Hey there.
- **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Why, hell to you too, my friend.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: You look like you've travelled a fair distance.
    - **Player:** You look like you've travelled a fair distance.
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** What gave me away?
    - **Player:** I don't mean to be rude, but the face paint and hair, for starters.
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Ah, yes. I'm from Shilo Village on Karamja, it's a style I've had since I was little.
    - **Player:** Then tell me, why are you so far from home?
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** This Grand Exchange! Isn't it marvellous! I've never seen anything like it in my life. My people were not pleased to see me break traditions to visit such a place, but I hope to make some serious profit. Then they'll see I was right!
    - **Player:** So, what are you selling?
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Logs! Of all kinds! That's my plan, at least. Nature is one thing my people understand very well.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I'm trying to find the prices for logs.
    - **Player:** I'm trying to find the prices for logs.
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Then you've come to the right person.
    - ((Guide Prices: Logs opens up.))
  - [Choice]: Sorry, I need to make tracks.
    - **Player:** Sorry, I need to make tracks.
    - **Relobo Blinyo (logs):** Okay, nice talking to you!
