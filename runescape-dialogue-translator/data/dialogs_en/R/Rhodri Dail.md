# Rhodri Dail

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Rhodri Dail](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ARhodri%20Dail)

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Rhodri Dail:** Welcome to the Max Guild! I'm always happy to see other talented individuals here. My specialty is potions, although I've got some skill with crystal singing too. Can I help you with anything?
  - Who are you?
    - **Rhodri Dail:** I am Rhodri of the Dail family. I am a ward of the Clan Meilyr, although I have spent much time with all the clans. We should always aspire to broaden our skills. How else do we achieve greatness?
    - **Player:** Are you related to Rhobert in the Tower of Voices?
    - **Rhodri Dail:** I am his brother, but he does not admit to that. He is jealous of my position here in the Max Guild. I hope one day he will discover his own talents are broader than just herblore, but for now he has ears only for the Voice.
    - *Back to previous options*
  - Can you decant things for me?
    - **Rhodri Dail:** Sure, 4-dose versions are simple enough. If you want potions decanted into 3-, 2- or 1-dose versions, I can do that too. I charge 2 coins for each normal vial and 2 coins for each juju vial I have to supply. Would you like to decant them into 1-, 2-, 3- or 4-dose potions?
    - *Enter 1, 2, 3 or 4:*
    - *Searching*
      - *If you have potions that can be decanted they will be decanted*
      - **Rhodri Dail:** There you go.
      - *If you don't have potions that can be decanted:*
      - **Rhodri Dail:** Sorry, I don't know how to decant anything that you're carrying.
    - *Ends dialogue*
  - Can you convert my flasks into normal vials for me?
    - **Rhodri Dail:** Certainly. I think I can make use of the flasks, so I'll give you the vials for free.
      - *If you have flasks to be converted:*
      - **Rhodri Dail:** There you go, that's the best I can do!
      - *If you don't have flasks to be converted:*
      - **Rhodri Dail:** Sorry, I couldn't really help you out there.
    - *Ends dialogue*
  - Can you enchant my crystals for me?
    - **Rhodri Dail:** Show me a crystal seed or crystal item, and I'll see what I can do with it.
    - *Ends dialogue*
  - Sorry, I've got to go.
    - **Rhodri Dail:** Okay, I'll be here if you need me.
    - *Ends dialogue*
