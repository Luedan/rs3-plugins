## Dialogue Transcript

### First-time dialogue

- **Ranged assistant:** Greetings. I am your assistant for the ranged performance. For savety reasons I must now explain to you the rules of the performance.
  - (continue|first time)

### Before completing your Ranged performance in the circus

- **Ranged assistant:** Do you understand the terms and conditions, and wish to continue with the Ranged performance?
- **Select an option**
  - Yes
    - Which weapon would you like to use?
      - Bow and arrow
        - (end)
      - Throwing knives
        - (end)
      - Throwing axes
        - (end)
  - No
    - **Player:** No.
  - Could you explain it to me again?
    - **Player:** Can you explain it to me again?
    - {{anchor|first time}}**Ranged assistant:** Please choose the target you wish to shoot at, either the bullseye or the popinjay, then the shooting spot from which you will attempt to hit the target. You can shoot from any shooting spot you like, regardless of your Ranged level, but you're more likely to score a hit if you have the recommended Ranged level. You may take 10 shots for the duration of your performance. 5 shots must be at the bullseye and the other 5 must be at the popinjay. Once 10 shots have been made, I will ask you to desist, and I will analyse and relay your score to you. You may also perform emotes during said performance to 'enliven' said performance. Participate at your leisure; the performance is not timed. Should you wish to terminate your performance early, speak to me. You may only do one performance at a time.
      - (Starts conversation over directly afterward with only the initial yes and no options)
  - *Only one of the following options will appear, depending on which has been toggled previously:*
    - Could you tell me how I'm doing?
      - **Ranged assistant:** Yes.
    - Could you stop telling me how I'm doing?
      - **Player:** Could you stop telling me what the audience thinks of my performance? It's distracting.
      - **Ranged assistant:** Certainly. I cannot prevent them from talking, though. I'll simply stop telling you if they think your performance is repetitive or not. Do you still want me to stop telling you?
        - Yes
          - (end)
        - No
          - **Player:** No.

### During your Ranged performance in the circus

- **Ranged assistant:** Do you wish to terminate your performance early?
- **Select an option**
  - Yes
    - *If the player scored points in their performance:*
      - Congratulations! You scored [amount] in your Ranged performance. You received [amount] experience points.
    - *If the player scored no points in their performance:*
      - **Ringmaster:** I'm sorry, but that performance just wasn't good enough for a reward. Try again next week.
      - Oh no! You didn't score anything in your Ranged performance. You received 0 experience points.
  - No
    - **Player:** No.

### After your Ranged performance in the circus

- **Ranged assistant:** You've already done your Ranged performance this week. You should speak to the ringmaster and see if there's anything else you can do.
