## Dialogue Transcript


### Applying the drenched debuff


#### First stack

- Yakamaru's waters begin to drench you!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru's waters begin to drench you!

#### Fifth stack

- Yakamaru's waters soak through you!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru's waters soak through you!

#### Tenth stack

- Yakamaru's waters conduct his full power!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru's waters conduct his full power!

### Attacking a player out of combat

- Yakamaru is displeased by your cowardice and flings dangerous projectiles at you!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru is displeased by your cowardice and flings dangerous projectiles at you!

### Player avoiding quicksand attack

- Yakamaru drops your team into quicksand! Free them before they drown!

### Player freeing a sinking adventurer

- You grab hold of [name] and quickly pull them out of the quicksand!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You grab hold of [name] and quickly pull them out of the quicksand!

### Player getting freed from quicksand

- [name] grabs you and pulls you out of the quicksand!

### Player stunning Yakamaru in the north-eastern pool

- {{Qact|Yakamaru's style changes and damage resistance is reset. Players get damaged.}}

- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - [name] has stunned Yakamaru!

### Releasing the poison

- Yakamaru releases the dangerous poison!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru releases the dangerous poison!}

### Player absorbing the poison

- You absorb the insane poison. There is no cure - stand next to Yakamaru as it dies to pass it over.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You absorb the insane poison. There is no cure - stand next to Yakamaru as it dies to pass it over.

### Avoiding getting poisoned

- No one poisoned Yakamaru so it heads to the central pool to recover.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - No one poisoned Yakamaru so it heads to the central pool to recover.

### Getting poisoned

- Yakamaru is lured by the scent of your poison!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Yakamaru is lured by the scent of your poison!

### Player attempting to resuscitate another player while someone is already resuscitating

- This player is already receiving aid.

### Cleansing poison

- As the [Jellyfish] dies, it cleanses the poisonous effect on Yakamaru!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - As the [Jellyfish] dies, it cleanses the poisonous effect on Yakamaru!

#### Having a jellyfish touch Yakamaru

- As the [Jellyfish] touches Yakamaru, it cleanses the poisonous effect!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - As the [Jellyfish] touches Yakamaru, it cleanses the poisonous effect!

  - An Image of Yakamaru is not engaged in combat and flings dangerous projectiles at the team!
