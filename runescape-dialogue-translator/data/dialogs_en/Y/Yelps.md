### During The Mighty Fall

- **Yelps:** Helloooo! I'm Yelps!
  - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Yelps:** I'm Yelps, the richest and smartest goblin that ever lived!
    - **Yelps:** It comes in handy, because I'm also the smallest goblin that ever lived.
    - **Yelps:** I'm an entrepreneur! A self-made man!
    - **Player:** Really? What do you do?
    - **Yelps:** I used to run this game, see? Lucky people would punch me in the face to win prizes!
    - **Player:** They...punched you in the face to win prizes?
    - **Yelps:** That's right!
    - **Player:** But how... Never mind.
  - **Player:** What do you do?
    - **Yelps:** Well, the game I used to run all sort of fell through.
    - **Yelps:** It turned out the treasure I was givin' away actually belonged to some human princess or something, so I had to give most of it up.
    - **Yelps:** She's still runnin' the game but...
    - **Yelps:** *shudder*
    - **Yelps:** There's no punchin'. Not even a bit!
    - **Yelps:** There are... treasure chests!
    - **Yelps:** I've been trying to reintegrate into goblin society, but it's not going so well.
  - **Player:** Why are you here?
    - **Yelps:** The high priest told me to get my best gear on and guard the camp! Here I am!
      - *Before finding all the human infiltrators*
        - **Player:** You're not taking part?
        - **Yelps:** Not on yer life! I'm no warrior!
      - *After finding all the human infiltrators*
        - **Player:** You do realise you're the goblin champion, don't you? You're fighting me in the first round.
        - **Yelps:** Y-you what? That can't be right.
  - **Player:** I'm done here.
