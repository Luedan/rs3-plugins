### Dialogue after fully cleansing the Nexus


#### After fully cleansing the Nexus:

- **Ysondria:** Thank you again, Player. I was worried this day would not come, but you have stopped the corruption.
- **Ysondria:** There is some residual energy in the sack. Allow me to release it back to you.
- (removes=charmed sack) (receives=Prayer experience)
- **Ysondria:** I shall be forever grateful for your aid in releasing this land.
- **Ysondria:** The effects of the purification can already be seen. I believe that the memory of Augrum is honoured by the work completed here.
- **Ysondria:** Is there any other help that I can give you?
- [Menu]: Select an Option {{anchor|standard}}
  - [Choice]: How can I train my spiritual skills now?
    - **Ysondria:** Traditionally, ceremonial burial is used to honour the dead. To do this, you need to bury the bones of those that have been killed.
    - **Ysondria:** As I understand it, the more powerful that the creature was when it was alive, the greater the experience you receive from burying the bones.
    - **Ysondria:** If you are devoted to training your spiritual abilities, you can create a gilded altar in your home to boost this process.
    - **Ysondria:** I have also heard of something called the Ectofuntus, which grants increased experience from bones, but I do not know what this is. All I do know is that it is located near to Port Phasmatys, far to the north-east.
    - **Ysondria:** Is there any other help that I can give you?
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Who is Augrum?
    - **Ysondria:** Augrum was my mentor. He gave his life creating those relics as a means to release the land from its inevitable decay.
    - **Ysondria:** He was a very brave and kind-hearted man, and I am grateful for your help in realising his intent.
    - **Ysondria:** Is there any other help that I can give you?
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Will the area stay cleansed?
    - **Ysondria:** I believe that it will, through the hard work that you have done here. I will not allow that evil to come back.
    - **Ysondria:** Is there any other help that I can give you?
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Why are you still here?
    - **Ysondria:** I feel close to Augrum here, and I want to ensure that everyone knows what he did, and what he sacrificed in order to free this land from that corruption.
    - **Ysondria:** I also wish to spread the word about all of the people who joined the fight and stood against the horror, using their faith and inner strength to save the land.
    - **Ysondria:** Is there any other help that I can give you?
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Ysondria:** Your aid will always be remembered.
