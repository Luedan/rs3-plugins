## Dialogue Transcript


### Talking to


#### During the event


##### First time

- **Yinteng:** Greetings!
- **Yinteng:** I am Yinteng and I am in charge of the Zodiac Festival!
- Select an option
  - *(Only after asking something else, and if not asked last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - **Yinteng:** I am Yinteng and I am in charge of the Zodiac Festival!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What do I have to do?
      - **Yinteng:** Okay, so here's the deal, I need Zodiac Talismans.
      - **Yinteng:** They are literally everywhere so you're bound to find them while you're out and about doing your usual activities.
      - **Yinteng:** But if you want a little extra there are plenty of tasks which need to be done.
      - A new task will appear in the Events Panel every day and will last 3 days before disappearing.
      - **Yinteng:** Whenever you gather enough Zodiac Talismans you will be able to trade them in for some fantastic rewards!
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What rewards are there?
      - **Yinteng:** Oh, you're in for a treat!
      - **Yinteng:** Not only do we have the glorious Zodiac Shield, we also have this adorable little lion pet.
      - **Yinteng:** Just look at him! Who's a good boy? You're a good boy, yes you are! You're a good boy!
      - **Player:** Uh, Yinteng?
      - **Yinteng:** Huh? Oh, sorry, what were we talking about again?
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - Goodbye.
    - **Yinteng:** Take care!

##### After the first time

- *(If there are zodiac talismans to hand in:)*
  - *(Dialogue from handing in zodiac talismans occurs, then continues below.)*
- **Yinteng:** What can I do for you today?
- Select an option
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - Who are you?
      - *(As on first time.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What do I have to do?
      - *(As on first time.)*
  - *(Only if not asked last:)*
    - What rewards are there?
      - *(As on first time.)*
  - Goodbye.
    - *(As on first time.)*

#### After the event

- **Yinteng:** The Zodiac Festival was a great success. I hope you enjoyed it!
