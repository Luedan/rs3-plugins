### After Sheep Herder and before Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello again sir.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Well hello again adventurer! How are you today?
- **Player:** Fine thank you. And yourself?
- **Councillor Halgrive:** MUCH better now that I don't have to worry about those horribly discoloured sheep spreading the plague.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Although we still have plenty to worry about!
- **Player:** Like what?
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Well, you never know what will happen. Not so long ago two gods turned up in Lumbridge and started fighting.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** I'd hate to think of that happening here! Have you seen how they left the castle?

##### During the Battle of Lumbridge (historical)

- **Player:** Hello again sir.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Well hello again adventurer! How are you today?
- **Player:** Fine thank you. And yourself?
- **Councillor Halgrive:** MUCH better now that I don't have to worry about those horribly discoloured sheep spreading the plague.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Although we still have plenty to worry about!
- **Player:** Like what?
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Well, there's fighting going on in Lumbridge - Saradomin and Zamorak are at war again!
- **Councillor Halgrive:** I'd hate to think what would happen if the fighting spreads to here!
- **Councillor Halgrive:** It's bad enough what has happened there! The castle is a complete mess!

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** Hello, is there any news about how our sheep keep getting infected with the plague?
- **Player:** Not that I know of, sorry.
- **Councillor Halgrive:** I'd sure like to know why we can't contain it. Please raise the issue next time you see the Head Mourner.
- **Player:** I'll keep it in mind.

### After Plague's End

- **Councillor Halgrive:**  All is well, adventurer! It's good to have the city running smoothly again after all the disruptions the fake plague caused.
