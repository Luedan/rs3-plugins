## Dialogue Transcript


### In Menaphos

- **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** Can I help you with something?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** Rashida's the name - Akhomet's cousin. She's military. I'm civilian.
    - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** I'm the challenge mistress in charge of assigning challenges and handing out rewards. They're all here on my list...
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Tell me about Daily Challenges.
    - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** A Daily Challenge is a job to complete: anything from forging swords to baking potatoes. We assign you three challenges per day.
    - You can view all Daily Challenges on the Challenges tab of the Adventures interface. If you've completed a Daily Challenge, you can also claim your reward from there, including any weekly reward chests you've unlocked.
    - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** To complete a Daily Challenge, you don't have to give me the items you gather or produce. Keep those for yourself, and use them or sell them!
    - If you complete a Combat Challenge, you'll get an XP lamp that can be used in any combat skill. If you complete any other challenge, you'll get XP in that skill directly. You'll also receive a Treasure Hunter key.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I want to toggle my active Daily Challenges.
    - ((Maxed challenge interface opens.))
  - [Choice]: I've completed a Daily Challenge.
    - (Condition: Without any challenges to turn in:)
      - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** It doesn't look like you've completed any Daily Challenges. Come back when you've completed one.
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: With challenges to turn in:)
      - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** You've completed that one. Well done! Here's your reward. ''(Repeats if there is more than one completed challenge)
      - **Challenge Mistress Rashida:** Looks like that's everything!
      - (initial)

### Toggle Challenges

- (A "Select Challenge Skills" interface opens with the following content:)
  - Select your maxed skills to toggle whether or not you will receive Daily Challenges for them.
  - (The skills are shown as icons with the skills toggled off with a red cross over them.)
  - (A checkbox with label "Block challenges in all combat skills" is shown.)
    - [Choice]: CONFIRM
      - (end)
