## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Before the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Captain Daerkin:** Hello old chap.
- **Player:** What are you doing here? Shouldn't you be looking after your glider?
- **Captain Daerkin:** I'm pretty much retired these days old fellow. My test piloting days are over. I'm just relaxing here and enjoying the primal clash between man and man.
- **Player:** You're watching the duels then. Are you going to challenge someone yourself?
- **Captain Daerkin:** I do find the duels entertaining to watch, but I suspect that actually being involved would be a lot less fun for me. I'm a lover, not a fighter!
- **Player:** Errm, I suppose you are.

#### After the destruction of Duel Arena

- **Captain Daerkin:** Hello old chap.
- **Player:** What are you doing here? Shouldn't you be looking after your glider?
- **Captain Daerkin:** I'm pretty much retired these days, old fellow. My test-piloting days are over. I'm just relaxing here and enjoying the warm weather.

### Gnome Restaurant delivery

- **Captain Daerkin:** Excellent. Just what I need whilst watching the duels.
