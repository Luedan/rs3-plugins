## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Grand Tree (Ta Quir Priw)


#### Before The Grand Tree quest

- **Captain Errdo:** Hello, human. Welcome to Gnome Air.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's Gnome Air?
    - **Player:** What's Gnome Air?
    - **Captain Errdo:** Gnome Air is the finest airline in Gielinor!
    - **Captain Errdo:** Well, it's the only real airline in Gielinor.
    - **Captain Errdo:** Ha!
    - **Player:** What do you mean by 'real airline'?
    - **Captain Errdo:** Well there's a dodgy magic carpet operation in the desert. I doubt it will ever take off. Ha!
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Where can you take me?
    - **Player:** Where can you take me?
    - **Captain Errdo:** Glough has ordered that I only take gnomes.
  - [Choice]: How much for one-way to Varrock?
    - **Player:** How much for a one-way to Varrock?
    - **Captain Errdo:** I can't take you anywhere.
    - **Player:** How come?
    - **Captain Errdo:** I would, but Glough has told me not to take humans.
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Player:** Never mind.

#### After The Grand Tree quest

- ((Gnome gilder map interface opens.))

### In Karamja (Gandius)

- **Captain Errdo:** I'm afraid the glider's not going anywhere.

### At the Digsite (Lemanto Andra)

- **Captain Errdo:** Ah, how embarrassing.
- **Player:** What happened?
- **Captain Errdo:** A bit of a technical hitch with the landing gear. I won't be able to fly you back, sorry.
