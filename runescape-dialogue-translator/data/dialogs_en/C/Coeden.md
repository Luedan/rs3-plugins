## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Coeden:** Hello...human. Welcome...to my store
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Tell me about yourself.
    - **Coeden:** Oh...not much to tell.
    - **Coeden:** I...am Coeden. I...sell seeds.
    - **Coeden: **Crwys...are my friends.
    - **Coeden:** Take a look...at my seeds...for sale. I also sell...saplings.
    - (opens=Coeden's Seed Store)
  - [Choice]: Show me your store.
    - (opens=Coeden's Seed Store)

### Persuade (requires Elite Tirannwn achievements)


#### If not claimed for the week

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Why do you sell your offspring to random strangers?
    - **Coeden:** They go...willingly.
    - **Player:** But that's like chopping off a limb and giving it to me! You can't possibly have a use for the gold.
    - **Coeden: ** Gold...is shiny,...but I willingly...give of myself...for a helper...such as...you.
    - (receives=98 assorted logs)
    - **Player:** Wow, thanks!
  - [Choice]: I think I have earned some logs.
    - **Coeden:** Such...impudence...from one who was...so helpful.
    - **Player:** Sorry, may I please have my logs?
    - (receives=98 assorted logs)
    - **Player:** Thank you!
  - [Choice]: Skip the chat - bring on the flames.
    - **Player:** This is for selling your offspring to strangers!
    - {{Qact|Coeden is set on fire}}
    - (receives=98 assorted logs)
    - **Coeden:** That...stings. Here...I give of myself...in penance.

#### If already claimed for the week

- **Coeden:** You...already had your logs...return in...[tomorrow/[number] days.]
