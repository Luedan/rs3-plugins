## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Carnillean Rising, after Hazeel Cult


#### If sided with the Carnilleans

- **Player:** Hello Ceril.
- **Ceril Carnillean:** Well, hello again adventurer! It's good to see you again! If it weren't for you, the treacherous Jones would have poisoned my family by now! We are in your debt.
- **Player:** Don't worry about it, a good deed is its own reward. And that 2,000 gold didn't hurt, either.

#### If sided with the cultists

- **Player:** Hello Ceril.
- **Ceril Carnillean:** Oooooh, I may be wrong, but ever since I asked for your help, things around here have gone from bad to worse. I think you'd better keep out of my way. And, for the LAST time, it's SIR Ceril. SIR! It's not that hard to blooming remember.
