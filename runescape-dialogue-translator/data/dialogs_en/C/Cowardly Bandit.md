- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Feud

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Cowardly Bandit:** What do you want?
- **Player:** Well nothing really, I'm new to town and wanted to get to know the locals.
- **Cowardly Bandit:** I'm too busy for chitchat. Come back if you have some business to discuss with us.

### After The Feud

- **Cowardly Bandit:** You dog! You killed our champion and for that I hope you die a slow and painful death.
- **Player:** What was that? Are you looking for a fight.
- **Cowardly Bandit:** Me? No, um no, I was um just complimenting your ..... um..... don't kill me please?
- **Player:** I wouldn't waste my breath.
