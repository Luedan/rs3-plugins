## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue


#### Without the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Charley:** Arr? Be ye wantin' te go on account with our gang o' fillibusters?
- **Charley:** The powder monkey be takin' a caulk after gettin' rowdy on bumboo, so there be plenty of room for ye.
- **Player:** Riiiiight...
- **Player:** I'll just be over here if you need me.

#### With the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Charley:** I got fish, you got gold?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - **Player:** Yes.
    - (opens='Two Feet' Charley's Fish Shop)
  - [Choice]: Yes, but I don't want your fish.
    - **Player:** Yes, but I don't want your fish.
    - **Charley:** Then what are ye doin' in a fish shop? Looking fer work?
    - **Player:** Possibly, do you have any quests?
    - **Charley:** I dunno, I haven't gone through the last catch yet. What sort of fish is it?
    - **Player:** A quest isn't a type of fish!
    - **Charley:** Then I don't have any, and yer wastin' my time!
  - [Choice]: What happened to your legs?
    - **Player:** What happened to your legs?
    - **Charley:** Ye wanna know what happened to my legs?
    - **Charley:** Yer too much of a lilly-livered, hat wearin' landlubber to know what happened to my legs!
    - **Player:** No I'm not! I've seen some freaky stuff! I can take it!
    - **Charley:** All right, [lad/lass], since yer so insistent, I'll tell ye.
    - **Charley:** See, I was clingin' onto a barrel, me ship havin' just had an encounter with this albatross.
    - **Charley:** The sea was trashin' and wild, but not so wild that I didn't see the fins of some sharks closin' in on me.
    - **Charley:** I managed to yank a sliver of wood from the barrel just as one of them grabbed me from below, but I slipped down the things throat by about two feet before I managed te kill it.
    - **Player:** How did you survive?
    - **Charley:** A passin' ship saw the sharks and knew there would be survivors in the water. They sent a longboat and picked me up, but not before the sharks had taken off my legs.
    - **Charley:** And that [lad/lass] is why they call me two feet Charley, because they found me jammed two feet down a shark's throat.
    - **Player:** I think I'm gonna be sick...
    - **Charley:** I knew ye couldn't handle the truth!
