#### Before completing Cat's Bane, without doogle leaves in backpack

- **Player:** You fight in the Rat Pits?
- **Catbot 9000:** Lose fightsss.<br>Replace partsss.
- **Player:** You replace parts of yourself with clockwork?

- **Player:** What are stink leaves?
- **Catbot 9000:** Doogle.<br>Xuan has.<br>I want.
- **Player:** So, if I get you some doogle leaves from Xuan, you'll leave?
- **Catbot 9000:** Yes.<br>Run.<br>Go home.
