## Dialogue Transcript


### Claiming the baby troll pet

- *(If the Baby Troll hasn't been unlocked yet:)*
  - **Corporal Keymans:** Look what I found! Poor little thing fell from the sky into some manure.
  - **Baby Troll:** I big and scary troll!
  - **Baby Troll:** Grrr!
  - **Corporal Keymans:** I cleaned him off, but I don't know what to do with him.
  - **Baby Troll:** Dada.
  - (continue)
- *(If the Baby Troll has been unlocked and lost:)*
  - **Corporal Keymans:** Hello there. You seem to have lost your troll. You going to take it back?
  - (continue)
- Select an option
  - I could look after him.
    - **Baby Troll:** I hungry!
    - (receives=Baby Troll)
    - Your reward has been added to your backpack: {{Colour|#ffffff|Baby troll}}.
    - You have adopted a baby troll pet! To have him follow you, open your backpack and drop him.
    - You can feed the baby troll by using an item on him from your backpack. Watch out! If he likes how it tastes, you won't get it back!
    - You can rename your baby troll: the baby troll inherits the name of the last item you fed to him.
  - Bad luck! I don't want him.
    - **Corporal Keymans:** Thanks a lot! Well, if you ever want a baby troll for a pet, come and see me.
  - You could drop him in a well.
    - **Corporal Keymans:** I couldn't do that! If you ever want a baby troll for a pet, come and see me.
