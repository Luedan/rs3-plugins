##### After Cook's Assistant and before Recipe for Disaster, or after Recipe for Disaster but on free-to-play worlds

- {{anchor|hellofriend}}**Cook:** Hello, friend, how is the adventuring going?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm getting strong and mighty.
    - **Player:** I'm getting strong and mighty. Grr.
    - **Cook:** Glad to hear it.
  - [Choice]: I keep on dying.
    - **Player:** I keep on dying.
    - **Cook:** Ah, well, at least you keep coming back to life too!
  - [Choice]: Can I use your range?
    - **Player:** Can I use your range?
    - **Cook:** Go ahead! It's a very good range; it's better than most other ranges.
    - **Cook:** It's called the Cook-o-Matic 25 and it uses a combination of state-of-the-art temperature regulation and magic.
    - **Player:** Will it mean my food will burn less often?
    - **Cook:** As long as the food is fairly easy to cook in the first place!
    - (Condition: Without the cook-o-matic manual in backpack:)
      - **Cook:** Here, take this manual. It should tell you everything you need to know about this range.
      - [The cook hands you a manual.]
      - (receives=Cook-o-matic manual)
    - (Condition: With the cook-o-matic manual in backpack:)
      - **Cook:** The manual you have in your inventory should tell you more.
    - **Player:** Thanks!

##### Claiming the post-quest rewards (if Cook's Assistant was completed before 15 December 2009)

- (Condition: If the player does not have backpack space:)
  - **Player:** Great, but I need to free up some space in my inventory first. I'll be right back.
- (Condition: If the player has backpack space:)
  - The cook hands you 500 coins and 20 sardines.
  - (receives=500 coins and 20 sardines)
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Cook's Delights' - Claim the Cook's extra rewards after 'Cook's Assistant'.
  - **Player:** Thank you!
