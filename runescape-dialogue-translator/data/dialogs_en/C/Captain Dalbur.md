#### Talk-to

- **Captain Dalbur:** Hello, human. Welcome to Gnome Air.

  - [Choice]: What's Gnome Air?

    - **Captain Dalbur:** Ali Morrisane has set up a magic carpet network, like our gliders.
    - **Captain Dalbur:** If he ever gets it set up outside of the desert, we gnomes won't be happy with him. Especially not King Narnode.
