## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Sea Slug

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Col. O'Niall:** Hello. What can I do for you?
- **Player:** Oh, I'm just wondering what you're doing.
- **Col. O'Niall:** A spot of fishing.
- **Player:** That doesn't look much like a fishing rod.
- **Col. O'Niall:** That my friend, depends on what you're fishing for.
- **Player:** And what would that be?
- **Col. O'Niall:** A little of this, a little of that; the usual things.
- **Player:** Have you caught much?
- **Col. O'Niall:** The odd bite here and there. Hmm.
- **Player:** What?
- **Col. O'Niall:** You look like a capable lad. Tell you what, when you've got a bit more experience under your belt, get yourself over to Falador.
- **Player:** What's in Falador?
- **Col. O'Niall:** An old friend of mine. You'll find him sitting on a bench in Falador Park. See what he can do for you.
- **Player:** What's his name? Who should I say sent me?
- **Col. O'Niall:** None of that matters if you can find him and if you're ready my name isn't necessary.
- **Player:** Oh right. I'll get going then. Goodbye.
- **Col. O'Niall:** Goodbye and good luck.

### After Wanted!, before The Slug Menace

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Col. O'Niall:** Ah hello, Player. Good to see you.
- **Player:** Um, how do you know my name?
- **Col. O'Niall:** By reputation. I heard about what you did with Solus Dellagar; good job.
- **Player:** But but but, that was classified!<br />How can you possibly know that?
- **Col. O'Niall:** I have my contacts.
- **Player:** You're one of the Black Knights, aren't you?
- **Col. O'Niall:** Don't be silly, boy.
- **Col. O'Niall:** If I was a Black Knight, do you really think I'd be just sitting here?
- **Player:** Well, I don't know.
- **Col. O'Niall:** I'd be looting your gear before you landed in Lumbridge my boy.
- **Player:** Hmm. Who are you, really?
- **Col. O'Niall:** Oh, I'm sure you'll find out eventually.
- **Col. O'Niall:** Let's just say that for the moment that information is beyond your means.
- **Player:** You're very cryptic, aren't you?
- **Col. O'Niall:** I need to be, it's how I've stayed alive this long.
- **Player:** But...
- **Col. O'Niall:** That's enough for now.<br />I'm sure we'll speak again sometime soon.
- **Player:** But...
- **Col. O'Niall:** Goodbye, Player.
- **Player:** Sigh, see you soon.
