## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Cook:** What do you want? I'm busy!
- '''What would you like to say?
  - Can you sell me any food?
    - **Player:** Can you sell me any food?
    - **Cook:** I suppose I could sell you some cabbage, if you're willing to pay for it. Cabbage is good for you.
    - '''What would you like to say?
      - Alright, I'll buy a cabbage.
        - **Player:** Alright, I'll buy a cabbage.
        - ''If the player has enough money
          - (receives=cabbage) (removes=one coin)
          - **Player:** Thanks, Cook.
          - **Cook:** It's a deal. Now, make sure you eat it all up. Cabbage is good for you.
        - ''If the player does not have enough money
          - You can not afford that.
          - **Cook:** I said 1 coins! You haven't got 1 coins!
          - **Player:** Sorry, I'll come back another day.
      - No thanks, I don't like cabbage.
        - **Player:** No thanks, I don't like cabbage.
        - **Cook:** Bah! People these days only appreciate junk food.
  - Can you give me any free food?
    - **Player:** Can you give me any free food?
    - **Cook:** Can you give me any free money?
    - **Player:** Why should I give you free money?
    - **Cook:** Why should I give you free food?
    - **Player:** Oh, forget it.
  - I don't want anything from this horrible kitchen.
    - **Player:** I don't want anything from this horrible kitchen.
    - **Cook:** How dare you? I put a lot of effort into cleaning this kitchen. My daily sweat and elbow-grease keep this kitchen clean!
    - **Player:** Ewww!
    - **Cook:** Oh, just leave me alone.
