#### Variant 1

- **Maude:** So, why do they call it a 'murder of crows' anyway? I've never murdered anyone - it's defamatory.
- **Flo:** What about the dead person they found you with last month?
- **Maude:** I found 'em that way. I was just there for food. I'm not gonna let a perfectly good eyeball go to waste; what do you take me for, a monster?
- **Flo:** Of course not, there's good eating on those. Maybe it should be a 'defile of crows'?
- **Maude:** Still sounds a bit suspect. What about a 'disturbance'?
- **Flo:** I'm not sure that's any better. 'An assault'?
- **Maude:** Still in crime territory. Let's also rule out: 'beating', 'altercation' and anything else that suggests a fight.
- **Flo:** What about 'gang' or 'mob' then?
- **Maude:** Mob is for meerkats...and kangaroos for some reason. I don't see a likeness. Also, still too crime-related.
- **Flo:** 'An unkindness'? That's not a crime
- **Maude:** I think that's what ravens go by.
- **Maude:** Ooh, I've got it! We should be an 'insult of crows'!
- **Flo:** I like it. Not a crime, and that is something we do. Nice one.
