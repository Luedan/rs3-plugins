## Dialogue Transcript


### In Witchaven


#### Before Sea Slug

- (see|Sea Slug#Concerned Caroline)

#### Between Sea Slug and The Slug Menace

- **Player:** Hello again.
- **Caroline:** Hello traveler, how are you?
- **Player:** Not bad thanks, yourself?
- **Caroline:** I'm good. Busy as always looking after Kent and Kennith, but no complaints.

#### After The Slug Menace

- **Player:** Hello Caroline.
- **Caroline:** Brave Player, have you any news about my son and his father?
- **Player:** I'm working on it now Caroline.
- **Caroline:** Please bring them back safe and sound.

#### After Kennith's Concerns

- **Player:** Hello again, Caroline. Are you all rested after your ordeal?
- **Caroline:** Yes, I'm feeling much better, thank you. Kennith is doing well out on the platform too.
- **Player:** I'm glad to hear it. I hope he's behaving himself!
- **Caroline:** Oh yes, Bailey has him at work trying to clear the platform of the sea slugs. He's even started writing letters to Kimberly!
- **Player:** Really? How has she reacted to that?
- **Caroline:** I don't think she's quite ready to forgive him yet, but I don't think Kennith is ready to give up either. To tell you the truth, I think he's smitten with her! Who'd have thought it!
- **Player:** Yes, that's quite a revelation. Well, I hope it works out for him. I might go out to the Fishing Platform to visit him sometime soon.
- **Caroline:** Oh, I think he'd like that - he's always talking about you!
- **Player:** I'll do my best.

#### After Salt in the Wound

- **Player:** Hello again, Caroline. Have you seen Kennith lately?
- **Caroline:** Oh, they grow up so fast!
- **Player:** Some faster than others it seems.

#### Treasure Trails

- (Condition: Only the first time:)
  - **Caroline:** Excuse me, can you help me with this question?
  - **Player:** I'll do my best.
  - **Caroline:** Great! I wrote it down for you.
  - (continue)
- (receives=challenge scroll (medium))
- [Caroline has given you a challenge scroll!]

##### When talked to again

- **Caroline:** Please enter the answer to the question.
- Enter amount:<br>[Input field]
- ((When given the wrong answer:))
  - (Condition: If the answer wasn't 11 (before Kennith's Concerns), or 0 (after Kennith's Concerns):)
    - **Caroline:** Are you sure? That sounds a little off to me.
  - (Condition: If the answer was 11 (after Kennith's Concerns):)
    - **Caroline:** Maybe you were right once, but I think the number has dropped recently.
- (Condition: When given the correct answer:)
  - (removes=the clue scroll (medium) and challenge scroll (medium))
  - (Condition: If the trail continues:)
    - (receives=a new clue scroll (medium))
    - [Caroline has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (Condition: If the trail ends:)
    - (receives=reward casket (medium))
    - [You've found a casket!]
  - (Condition: Before Kennith's Concerns:)
    - **Caroline:** Thank you so much!
  - (Condition: After Kennith's Concerns:)
    - **Caroline:** An up-to-date count! Thank you so much!
