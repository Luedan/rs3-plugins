##### During a Christmas event

- **Player:** Hi there Cait!
- **Cait:** Hi Player! How can I help?
- [Menu]: Select an Option|anchor=Cait-options
  - [Choice]: What are you up to?
    - **Player:** What are you up to now that the workshop is in production?
    - **Cait:** Currently I'm working on the shift schedule for our next week of toy-making. After that I need to do an inventory count.
    - **Player:** That doesn't sound particularly thrilling.
    - **Cait:** Not everything about Christmas is, I'm afraid!
    - (previous|Cait-options)
  - [Choice]: What can you tell me about pixies?
    - **Player:** What can you tell me about your kind? I don't really know much about them, other than your history of working with Diango.
    - **Cait:** Well, if I had to generalise... We're hardworking, easily excitable, and love long walks in the forest!
    - **Player:** How come I don't see any around Gielinor?
    - **Cait:** We don't go to Gielinor very often, and when we do, we prefer to hide from outsiders. Hence your need for my magical thingymabob.
    - **Cait:** Our ancestors are originally from Cheanannais, but our main home is actually here on Renmark, in the Enchanted Valley.
    - **Player:** Oh! So the Land of Snow is close to home?
    - **Cait:** Closer than Gielinor, that's for sure. Though long distances aren't really a problem when you have fairy rings - one moment you're at home, the next you're here, skiing down a hill alongside yetis!
    - **Player:** I'm not sure if that sounds terrifying or exhilarating.
    - (previous|Cait-options)
  - [Choice]: Can I change the pixie's uniforms?
    - **Player:** Can I change the pixie's uniforms?
    - **Cait:** Sure! Which colour would you like them to wear?
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Red and black.|cond=If the current colours are not red and black
        - {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. The pixies have all changed into red and black outfits.}}
        - The pixies quickly change into a dashing new uniform.
        - (previous2|Cait-options)
      - [Choice]: Green and red.|cond=If the current colours are not green and red
        - {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. The pixies have all changed into green and red outfits.}}
        - The pixies quickly change into a dashing new uniform.
        - (previous2|Cait-options)
      - [Choice]: Candy cane.|cond=If the current colours are not candy cane
        - {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. The pixies have all changed into candy cane outfits.}}
        - The pixies quickly change into a dashing new uniform.
        - (previous2|Cait-options)
      - [Choice]: Nevermind.
        - **Player:** Nevermind.
        - (previous2|Cait-options)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Bye for now.
    - **Cait:** Happy holidays!
