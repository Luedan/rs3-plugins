## Dialogue Transcript


### Before completing the Barbarian Assault tutorial


#### Talking to him

- **Player:** Hello?
- **Commander Connad:** Sorry soldier, you don't have permission to speak with me yet. Go and talk to the Captain.

#### Get rewards option

- You can't claim any rewards until you have completed the tutorial. Go and see Captain Cain.

#### Reset wave option

- (above|Talking to him)

### After completing the Barbarian Assault tutorial

- **Player:** I believe you might be able to reward me for my progress in this arena?
- **Commander Connad:** Well, that depends upon your skill levels for each role. What's your name, soldier?
- **Player:** Player, Sir!
- **Commander Connad:** Okay. What exactly do you want to know, Player?
- Can I trade some of my points?
  - **Player:** Can I trade some of my points?
  - **Commander Connad:** Of course.
  - {{qact|Barbarian Assault Rewards interface opens}}
- What points do I have so far, sir?
  - **Player:** What points do I have so far, sir?
  - **Commander Connad:** Let me check... You have: *x* Attacker Honour Points, *x* Defender Honour Points, *x* Collector Honour Points, and *x* Healer Honour Points.
- Could I have a 'Queen Help' book?
  - **Player:** Could I have a Queen Help book?
  - **Commander Connad:** By all means.
- Could I change my current wave?
  - **Player:** Could I change my current wave?
  - **Commander Connad:** I can change it down to wave 1 and you're currently on wave *x*. I'll give you a ticket for use on the wave  *x* door, so you can change your wave back to that room.
  - Yes, reset my wave to one.
    - **Player:** Yes, reset my wave to one.
    - **Commander Connad:** Very well.
    - {{qact|The commander hands you a ticket. Your Barbarian Assault wave is now 1.}}
  - No, I want to stay on wave *x*.
    - **Player:** No, I want to stay on wave *x*.
- That is all, sir.
  - **Player:** That is all, sir.
  - **Commander Connad:** Very well, on your way.
