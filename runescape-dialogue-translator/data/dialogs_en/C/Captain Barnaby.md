## Dialogue Transcript


### Before My Arm's Big Adventure

- **Captain Barnaby:** Do you want to go on a trip to Brimhaven?
- **Captain Barnaby:** The trip will cost you 30 coins.
- '''Select an option {{anchor|Select}}
  - [Choice]: Yes please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (Condition: If the player has enough money:)
      - You pay 30 coins and board the ship.
      - The ship arrives at Brimhaven.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
      - **Player:** Oh dear, I don't seem to have enough money.
      - You can not afford that.
      - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
        - You can not afford that.
    - (Condition: If the player has completed the Easy Karamja achievements:)
      - **Captain Barnaby:**Wait a minute, didn't you earn Karamja gloves? Thought I'd seen you helping around the island. You can go on half price.
      - You pay 15 coins and board the ship.
      - The ship arrives at Brimhaven.
  - [Choice]: No thank you.
    - **Player:** No thank you.
  - [Choice]: *(With a ring of charos (a) equipped:)* Or I could pay you nothing at all...
    - **Player:**  Or I could pay you nothing at all...
    - **Captain Barnaby:** Mmmm, nothing at all, you say...
    - **Captain Barnaby:** Yes, why not - jump aboard then.
    - You board the ship.
    - The ship arrives at Brimhaven.

### After My Arm's Big Adventure

- **Captain Barnaby:** Oh no, it's you again! Do you want to go to Brimhaven?
- **Captain Barnaby:** The trip will cost you 30 coins.
- (continuea|Select)

### Pay-fare option

- (Condition: If the player has enough money:)
  - You pay 30 coins and board the ship.
  - The ship arrives at Brimhaven.
- (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
  - You do not have enough coins to pay for the trip.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - You do not have enough coins to pay for the trip.
- (Condition: If the player has completed the Easy Karamja achievements:)
  - The Captain's grin broadens as he recognises you as having earned Karamja gloves and he let you on half price - 15 coins.
