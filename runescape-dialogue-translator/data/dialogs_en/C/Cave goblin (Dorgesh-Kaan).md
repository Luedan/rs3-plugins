## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- {{trandom}}
  - (Condition: Dialogue 1:)
    - **Cave goblin:** How can you see properly with such tiny little eyes?
  - (Condition: Dialogue 2:)
    - **Cave goblin:** Ooh! Are you Player?
    - **Player:** Yes.
    - **Cave goblin:** Everyone's talking about how you helped Zanik save the city! You're a hero!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 3:)
    - **Cave goblin:** Have you visited the market? There are visitors selling foods from all over the surface there. It's so exotic!
    - **Cave goblin:** Well, I suppose it wouldn't be exotic for you, since you're from the surface.
    - **Cave goblin:** But you should check it out anyway. Maybe you could sell some of your food. Everyone's crazy about surface food at the moment.
  - (Condition: Dialogue 4:)
    - **Cave goblin:** So, what do you think of our city, surface-dweller?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: I like it!
        - **Player:** I like it!
        - **Cave goblin:** That, surface-dweller, was the right answer.
      - [Choice]: I think it's rubbish.
        - **Player:** I think it's rubbish.
        - **Cave goblin:** Well, it beats crawling around on the surface with nothing above your head!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 5:)
    - **Cave goblin:** What's it like on the surface? It sounds scary to me, having nothing above my head.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: It's alright.
        - **Player:** It's alright.
        - **Cave goblin:** I'm still not sure it would be for me, though.
      - [Choice]: It's not all that great.
        - **Player:** It's not all that great.
        - **Cave goblin:** Never mind, you can stay down here now!
