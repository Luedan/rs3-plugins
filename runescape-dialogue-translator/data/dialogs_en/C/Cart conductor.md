# Cart conductor

- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Dwarven Mine

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Cart conductor:** I'm an employee of the Keldagrim carts. I make sure the carts in this area run on time and that people pay their fares.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Where can you take me?
    - **Player:** Where can you take me?
    - (Condition: Before gaining access to Keldagrim:)
      - **Cart conductor:** I don't think I'm allowed to take you into the city of Keldagrim, human. Perhaps, when you find another way into the city and talk to someone of significance there, you will be allowed to.
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: After gaining access to Keldagrim:)
      - **Cart conductor:** This track leads right up to Keldagrim. Only stop.
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to buy a ticket.
    - **Player:** I'd like to buy a ticket.
    - **Cart conductor:** You don't need a ticket. The Consortium has decided to grant free mine cart travel to all humans.
  - [Choice]: I have to go.
    - **Player:** I have to go.
    - **Cart conductor:** Just remember, wherever you go, you go there faster through Keldagrim carts.

### In Keldagrim


#### Near the mine carts

- (When speaking to any cart conductor near the mine cart tracks in Keldagrim. Each cart conductor starts with their own greeting which isn't unique to themselves but never changes, and the continues with the same dialogue:)
- (Condition: Talking to the green outfit near the tracks:)
  - **Cart conductor:** Tickets, tickets, get your tickets here!
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Talking to the red outfit:)
  - **Cart conductor:** Anything I can help you with?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Talking to the green outfit in waiting area:)
  - **Cart conductor:** Yes [sir/m'am]? Can I help you at all?
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What is this place?
    - **Player:** What is this place?
    - (Condition: Talking to the conductors near the tracks:)
      - **Cart conductor:** This is the Keldagrim cart-station, human.
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: Talking to the green outfit in waiting area:)
      - **Cart conductor:** You're in the main waiting area of Keldagrim cart-station.
      - (continue)
    - **Player:** Cart-station? What goes on here then?
    - **Cart conductor:** Keldagrim is the hub of all the traffic of the dwarven realm. Almost every dwarven outpost has railtracks leading to Keldagrim, connecting them for easy travel and transportation of goods.
    - **Player:** Amazing! And what powers the carts? Magic?
    - **Cart conductor:** Oh, no no, we dwarves don't use magic. All of this is powered by steam engines, developed over the past few centuries after the Consortium came to power.
    - **Player:** Thanks for the info.
  - [Choice]: Where can you take me?
    - **Player:** Where can you take me?
    - **Cart conductor:** Let's see, we can take you to the mines under Ice Mountain and to the Grand Exchange in Varrock.
    - (Condition: If the player has completed Fishing Contest:)
      - **Cart conductor:** And since you're a friend of our brothers under White Wolf Mountain, we could take you there as well.
  - [Choice]: I'd like to buy a ticket.
    - **Player:** I'd like to buy a ticket.
    - **Cart conductor:** Oh, you don't need a ticket. The Consortium has decided to grant humans free mine cart travel to and from Keldagrim.
  - [Choice]: I have to go.
    - **Player:** I have to go.
    - **Cart conductor:** Just remember, wherever you go, you go there faster through Keldagrim carts.
