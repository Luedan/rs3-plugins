## Dialogue Transcript


### In Yanille

- **Colonel Radick:** Who goes there? Friend or foe?

    - **Colonel Radick:** Ok good to hear it.
  - [Choice]: Foe!
    - **Player:** Foe!
    - **Colonel Radick:** Oh righty...
    - {{qact|Colonel Radick attacks the player.}}
  - [Choice]: Why is this town so heavily defended?
    - **Player:** Why is this town so heavily defended?
    - **Colonel Radick:** Yanille is on the southwest border of Kandarin, Beyond here you go into the Feldip hills which is major Ogre territory. Our job is to defend Yanille from the ogres.
