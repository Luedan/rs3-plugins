## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Cookie:** What can Cookie do for you?
- Select an option
  - Why are you called Cookie?
    - **Player:** Why are you called Cookie?
    - **Cookie:** I guess it has something to do with my being a cook. That, and I tend to have a lot of crumbs in my beard.
    - **Player:** That's...nice.
  - Where did your shirt go?
    - **Player:** Where did your shirt go?
    - **Cookie:** It's hot work tending to this spit all day - my shirt was getting all sweaty.
    - **Player:** So, you just took it off?
    - **Cookie:** Yup - now the sweat can run free! Plus, I can work on my tan while I'm at work now.
    - **Player:** Ewwwwwwww.
