# Chimp ice chimp

- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Desert Monkey Colony


#### While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Rug merchant:** Ook Ook! Ah! Ah Uh Ah! Ook Ah Ook!
- You cannot understand him.

#### While wearing a monkeyspeak amulet

- **Chimp ice chimp:** Chimp ice! Get your chimp ice!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Sure, I'll buy one.
    - **Player:** Sure, I'll buy one.
    - (Condition: With a chimp ice already in backpack:)
      - **Chimp ice chimp:** You already have a chimp ice. Don't be greedy.
    - (Condition: Without a chimp ice in backpack:)
      - **Chimp ice chimp:** That'll be 100 coins.
      - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
        - **Chimp ice chimp:** You don't have room to carry a chimp ice.
      - (Condition: Without enough money:)
        - You can not afford that.
        - **Chimp ice chimp:** You don't have enough.
      - (Condition: With enough money, and with space in backpack:)
        - (removes=100 coins) (receives=chimp ice)
        - (Condition: If this is a 5th, 10th, 15th, etc delivery:)
          - **Chimp ice chimp:** I'd eat that extra quick. I left that one out in the sun.
  - [Choice]: How's business?
    - **Player:** How's business?
    - **Chimp ice chimp:** Booming, my friend. Booming! Buying the rights to these treats may be the best financial move we've ever made. Would you like to buy one?
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: I've got to go.
    - **Player:** I've got to go.

#### Buy chimp ice option, without an monkeyspeak amulet

- (above|While not wearing a monkeyspeak amulet)

#### Buy chimp ice option, with an monkeyspeak amulet

- (above|sure)
