# Ceciro

- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before tier 21 of Journey to Iaia

- **Ceciro:** Hello Player. Lovely weather we're having today.

### During tier 21 of Journey to Iaia


#### First time

- **Ceciro:** Greetings Player, happy to make you aquaintance.
- **Player:** Hello Ceciro, how are you finding things on Gielinor?
- **Ceciro:** There is plenty of this world still to explore Player, with vast areas of beauty, but also areas of such destruction...
- **Ceciro:** I decided to explore the area around this town. The lair nearby is home to a dragon that once had an affinity with your friend Hannibus.
- **Ceciro:** I do not have the temperament to attempt to befriend the dragon within, but I have managed to find some remnants of our past from the outside.
- **Player:** That's good to hear, i'm glad some of your history survives.
- **Ceciro:** As am I Player however dear George the Gurh here has been dutifully helping me, but the vast wilderness nearby has him on edge.
- **Ceciro:** Could you see about calming him down? Take this redcane, a produce of Iaia, mix it with some of your native vegetables. That should help soothe dear George the Gurh.
- **Player:** Of course. I'll see what I can do.
- ((Without space in backpack:))
  - **Ceciro:** You'll be needing some redcane to calm George the Gurh. I'll give you some redcane once you have some space in your backpack.
- ((With space in backpack:))
  - (receives=redcane)
  - **Ceciro:** Much appreciated, Player. Here you go!

#### Before feeding George

- **Ceciro:** Hello Player, how is calming George the Gurh going?
- **Player:** I'm still working on it.
- ((If you have no redcane:))
  - ((Without space in backpack:))
    - **Ceciro:** You'll be needing some redcane to calm George the Gurh. I'll give you some redcane once you have some space in your backpack.
  - ((With space in backpack:))
    - (receives=redcane)
    - **Ceciro:** You'll be needing some redcane to calm George the Gurh. Here you go!
- ((If you have redcane:))
  - **Ceciro:** Not to worry. Don't forget to use that redcane to create a soothing meal for George the Gurh.

### After tier 21 of Journey to Iaia

- **Ceciro:** Thank you Player. George the Gurh should be back to his charming self shortly and we can take our leave.
- **Ceciro:** As happy as I am to find pieces of our peoples' history with this world. It is a shame to realise how manipulated our kin were by the gods and their machinations.
- **Ceciro:** But, this is our home now and we should do whatever we can to sustain it. Perhaps Olun'dai does not just apply to life on Iaia, and we can help heal the troubles of this world too, yes?
- **Player:** A very nice sentiment Ceciro. I wish you the best of luck.
