## Dialogue Transcript


### Deathbeard's Demise


#### When talked to during the event

- *(If there are phantom doubloons to hand in:)*
  - *(Dialogue from handing in phantom doubloons occurs, then continues below.)*
- **Captain Deathbeard:** Avast, me matey! Welcome to me shipwreck!
- **Player:** That's a sorry looking ship...
- **Captain Deathbeard:** Aye, she's stayin' afloat for now. But I be needin' some help gettin' her back on her sea legs.
- **Player:** What is it you need?
- **Captain Deathbeard:** Doubloons! But not any ol' coin. These ones be dead, just like me. Get 'em from everyday frolicks, and bring 'em back to me.
- **Player:** I'm on it!
  - So... you're dead?
    - **Player:** So... you're dead?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** You can't just go around asking people if they're dead, laddy/lass.
    - **Player:** Sorry. But it's not every day you meet a dead pirate captain.
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** Aye, tha' be true. I did indeed perish along with the rest of me crew. Somethin' clung to me soul, though. Somethin' dark that let me keep on goin'. I can feel th' same curse has be laid upon them, as well.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What do you need all these doubloons for?
    - **Player:** What do you need all these doubloons for?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** I plan to mend me ship, sail again to whar we perished to collect me crew 'n take on whatever it was that decided to cross us.
    - **Player:** What makes you think you'll win this time?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** Death may seem horrific, but it has its... benefits.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - What happened to your ship?
    - **Player:** What happened to your ship?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** She's seen better days, that's for sure. The sea... she hasn'a been herself of late - and no one know's 'er like me. Angry, she's been gettin', with more 'n more of what be stayin' deep risin' to th' top.
    - **Player:** You had a run in with something?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** It happened so quickly I couldn't say fer sure. One moment we're sailin' through a serene ocean. Next, we're thrown from it entirely, spit shine into th' air, me ship in ruins. I woke up on th' shore in this state, 'n not a crew member in sight.
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - How long will you be around for?
    - **Player:** How long will you be around for?
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** Not forever. This curse 'o undeath has given me a new perspective on... life?
    - **Player:** I suppose that makes sense...
    - **Captain Deathbeard:** 'O course it does! Now get ta doubloon huntin'!
    - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
  - More options...
    - You appear to be changing your appearance...
      - **Player:** You appear to be changing your appearance...
      - **Captain Deathbeard:** Aye - I'm still tryin' ta get used ta me new deathly powers. Whatever it be, it lets me change into a more ghastly version o' myself.
      - **Player:** I suppose that could come in handy.
      - **Captain Deathbeard:** I be treatin' it as a gift. I'll find a use fer it somehow.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - AAARGH!
      - **Player:** AAARGH!
      - **Captain Deathbeard:** Ye know, not all us pirates feel that's the best way to express ourselves...
      - **Player:** Oh... I didn't mean to cause offence.
      - **Captain Deathbeard:** Arghh, they never do.
      - *(Returns to the previous options.)*
    - Previous options...
      - *(Returns to the initial options.)*

#### Handing in phantom doubloons


##### Without doubloons

- You do not have anything to hand in.

##### With doubloons

- Handing in event currency will increase your progress for this event unlocking unique rewards. This will remove currency up to the amount that is required to complete the event.
- Do you wish to continue?
  - No
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
  - Yes
    - You hand in [X] Phantom doubloons. You need to hand in [Y] more to complete the event.
    - *(Dialogue from claiming rewards occurs.)*

##### With all rewards claimed

- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.

#### Claiming rewards


##### Without rewards to claim and none claimed

- **Captain Deathbeard:** You'll need to help me some more before I can offer you any rewards.

##### With rewards to claim

- **Captain Deathbeard:** There ye are me matey!
- *(If a customisation is unlocked:)*
  - Congratulations, you have unlocked [reward].
  - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If mystery boxes are obtained:)*
  - *(If the inventory is full:)*
    - You do not have enough inventory space for some of the rewards. Please make some space and try again.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - Congratulations, you have been awarded two event mystery boxes.
    - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If big mystery boxes are obtained:)*
  - *(If the inventory is full:)*
    - You do not have enough inventory space for some of the rewards. Please make some space and try again.
    - *(Continues below.)*
  - *(If there is space in inventory:)*
    - Congratulations, you have been awarded two big event mystery boxes.
    - *(Continues below.)*
- *(If Phantom Deathbeard's Outfit is unlocked:)*
  - Congratulations, you have unlocked Phantom Deathbeard's Outfit which contains the hat, doublet, breeches, feet and hook.
  - Congratulations, you have unlocked 'Deathbeard's transform' emote.
  - *(Continues below.)*
- You have unlocked [X]/9 of the rewards for the 'Deathbeard's Demise' event.

##### Without rewards to claim and some claimed

- **Captain Deathbeard:** Ye need to go doubloon huntin' some more before I can reward you.
- You have unlocked [X]/9 of the rewards for the 'Deathbeard's Demise' event.

##### With all rewards claimed

- **Captain Deathbeard:** Ye ave' claimed all of tha' rewards I have to offer.
- You have unlocked all of the rewards available for this event. You can convert any remaining currency into consumable rewards.
