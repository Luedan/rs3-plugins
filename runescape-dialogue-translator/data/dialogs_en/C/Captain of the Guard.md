# Captain of the Guard

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Captain of the Guard](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ACaptain%20of%20the%20Guard)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- *If the player is not in a clan:*
  - **Captain of the Guard:** Alf A Numerius at your service, [ma'am/sir]. It's my job to give out clan cloaks. If you'd like to know more about clans, talk to the clan scribe.
  - (continue)
- *If the player is in a clan:*
  - **Captain of the Guard:** Need something, [ma'am/sir]?
  - (continue)
- Choose an option:
  - I'd like a clan cloak.
    - *If the player is not in a clan:*
      - **Captain of the Guard:** I can't give you a cloak if you're not in a clan, ma'am. Those are the rules.
    - *If the player is in a clan:*
      - **Captain of the Guard:** Certainly. Here you are.
      - The captain gives you a cloak.
  - Goodbye.
    - **Captain of the Guard:** Goodbye, [ma'am/sir].
