# Chompy bird

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Chompy bird](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AChompy%20bird)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When a chompy bird spawns

- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Sqwark!}}

### When a chompy bird eats a [[bloated toad]]

- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Sqwark!}}
- **Swamp toad:** {{RSChat|!!Gobble!!}}
- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Gobble!}}

### When a chompy bird flies away

- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Rawk!}}

### When a chompy bird is attacked

- **Chompy bird:** {{RSChat|Screech!}}

### Plucking a chompy bird

- You start plucking the chompy bird.
- (The chompy bird carcass is replaced with a Raw chompy)

### Killing a chompy bird

- You scratch a notch on your bow for the chompy bird kill.
