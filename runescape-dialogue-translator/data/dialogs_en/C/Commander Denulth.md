  - [Choice]: How goes your fight with the trolls?
    - **Player:** How goes your fight with the trolls?
    - (Condition: Without the 15 Agility and 30 Thieving level requirement for Troll Stronghold:)
      - **Commander Denulth:** We have pinned them down thanks to the vantage point on the cliffs that you found.
      - **Commander Denulth:** They can't reach us with rocks, nor climb up to attack. We can rain arrows down on them with impunity!
      - **Commander Denulth:** Friend, we are indebted to you!
      - **Player:** Good luck!
      - (initial)
    - (Condition: With the 15 Agility and 30 Thieving level requirement for Troll Stronghold:)
      - (see|Troll Stronghold)
    - (Condition: After Troll Stronghold:)
      - **Commander Denulth:** We are busy preparing for an attack by night. Godric knows of a secret entrance to the stronghold.
      - **Commander Denulth:** Once we destroy the stronghold Burthorpe will be safe!
      - **Commander Denulth:** Friend, we are indebted to you!
      - **Player:** Good luck!
      - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Do you need any more help from me?|cond=Having not yet delivered supplies to the Ambush Commander five times:
    - (Condition: If you do not currently have supplies in your bank or backpack:)
      - **Commander Denulth:** Well, I suppose that the archers on the overlook could do with some more supplies.
      - (receives=supplies)
      - You place the heavy crate of supplies in your pack.
      - **Commander Denulth:** Here, take these supplies up to the Ambush Commander at the ambush point.
      - **Commander Denulth:** It has some supplies for the men, such as more arrows and replacement bows, as well as some things they can put together to make the ambush point better equipped.
      - **Commander Denulth:** Plus a few luxuries like cakes, and letters from friends and family. That sort of thing.
      - **Commander Denulth:** The Ambush Commander has some reward lamps. Once they get this gear he will give you some to help with your training.
      - **Player:** All right, I will drop this off immediately.
      - (previous)
    - (Condition: If you have received but not yet delivered supplies to the Ambush Commander:)
      - **Commander Denulth:** Well, if you wouldn't mind delivering those supplies for me, I may have some more to send you off with.
      - **Commander Denulth:** The Ambush Commander at the ambush point over Death Plateau has a supply of reward lamps that he will give you when he gets that gear.
      - (previous)
  - [Choice]: I thought the White Knights controlled Asgarnia?
    - **Commander Denulth:** You are right, citizen. The White Knights have taken advantage of the old and weak king. They control most of Asgarnia, including Falador, but they do not control Burthorpe!
    - **Commander Denulth:** We are the prince's elite troops! We keep Burthorpe secure!
    - **Commander Denulth:** The White Knights have overlooked us, until now! They are pouring money into their war against the Black Knights, so they are looking for an excuse to stop our funding and I'm afraid they may have found it!
    - **Commander Denulth:** If we can not destroy the troll camp on Death Plateau then the Imperial Guard will be disbanded and Burthorpe will come under control of the White Knights. We cannot let this happen!
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: See you about, Denulth!
    - **Commander Denulth:** Saradomin be with you, friend!
