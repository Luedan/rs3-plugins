## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock


#### Standard dialogue

- **Charlie the Tramp:** Spare some change, guv?
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** Charles. Charles E. Trampin' at your service. Now, about that change you were going to give me...
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What's in it for me?
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** 'What's in it for me?' Whatever happened to charity? Have you no consideration for those less fortunate than yourself?
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: *(Only before Shield of Arrav)* Sorry, I haven't got any money.
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** Thanks anyway.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: *(Only after Shield of Arrav)* Do you know where I can find the Black Arm Gang hideout?
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** Short memory, eh?
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** The ruthless and notorious Black Arm Gang have their headquarters down the alleyway to the west of us. Talk to a lady called Katrine. But don't upset her, and don't tell her I sent you. She's pretty dangerous.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: *(Only after Shield of Arrav)* I'm looking for the Phoenix Gang hideout.
    - **Charlie the Tramp:**  Yeah, I heard you talked to Baraek. It's easy to get lost on the streets of Varrock. Now listen closely...
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** To get to the Phoenix hideout, walk east from here. It's the street with the Blue Moon Inn on the corner.
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** Take the first alley leading south off of the road and you'll find the hideout at the end of that alley. It's called the VTAM corporation.
    - **Player:** VTAM. Got it.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Get a job!
    - **Charlie the Tramp:** You startin'? I hope your nose falls off!
