## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Grand Tree


#### Talk-to

- **Captain Klemfoodle:** Hello, human. Welcome to Gnome Air.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: What's Gnome Air?
    - **Player:** What's Gnome Air?
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** Gnome Air is the finest airline in Gielinor!
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** Well, it's the only real airline in Gielinor.
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** Ha!
    - **Player:** What do you mean by 'real airline'?
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** Well there's a dodgy magic carpet operation in the desert. I doubt it will ever take off. Ha!
    - (other)
  - [Choice]: Where can you take me?
    - **Player:** Where can you take me?
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** Glough has ordered that I only take gnomes.
  - [Choice]: How much for a one-way trip to Varrock?
    - **Player:** How much for a one-way to Varrock?
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** I can't take you anywhere.
    - **Player:** How come?
    - **Captain Klemfoodle:** I would, but Glough has told me not to take humans.
  - [Choice]: Never mind.
    - **Player:** Never mind.

#### Glider

- **Captain Klemfoodle:** I'm afraid that we're not allowed to take human passengers at the moment. Glough's orders.
