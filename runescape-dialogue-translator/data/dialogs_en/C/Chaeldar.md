### Assignment advice


#### Aberrant spectres

**Chaeldar:** Aberrant spectres are fetid, vile ghosts. The very smell of them will paralyse and harm you. A nosepeg will help negate their stink and their attacks are most definitely magical in nature. You'll be able to make the world more fragrant if you kill the ones lurking in the Slayer Tower near Canifis.

#### Banshees

**Chaeldar:** Banshees use a piercing scream to shock their enemies. You'll need some earmuffs to protect yourself from them. Head to the Slayer Tower near Canifis to slay them, although I have heard rumours of tougher banshees in Pollnivneach.

#### Basilisks

**Chaeldar:** Basilisks, like cockatrices, have a gaze that will paralyse and harm their prey. You'll need a mirror shield to protect you. I am sure you know that there is a Basilisk colony in the Rellekka Slayer Caves.

#### Bloodveld

**Chaeldar:** Bloodveld are strange, demonic creatures. They use their long, rasping tongue to feed on just about anything they can find. Their tongue assaults physically, so using magic attacks might be the best choice. If you can stand being licked by a half-ton demon, you should take a trip to the Slayer Tower near Canifis.

#### Blue dragons

**Chaeldar:** Blue dragons aren't as strong as other dragons, but they're still very powerful. Watch out for their fiery breath. Taverley Dungeon has a clutch of these creatures, which it can be profitable to slay.

#### Brine rats

**Chaeldar:** Brine rats can be found in caves that are near the sea. They are hairless, bad-tempered and generally unfriendly. You may have spotted them while looking for pirate treasures.

#### Bronze dragons

**Chaeldar:** Bronze dragons are the weakest of the metallic dragons, but their bronze scales are far thicker than normal bronze armour. They lay in wait for you in Brimhaven Dungeon and the Chaos Tunnels. You'll be well served by taking a defence against their breath.

#### Cave crawlers

**Chaeldar:** Cave crawlers are small and fast, often hiding in ambush. Avoid their barbed tongue or you'll get poisoned. Squish them in droves at the Rellekka Slayer Dungeon near the golden tree.

#### Cave horrors

**Chaeldar:** Cave horrors can be found in the caves under Mos Le'Harmless which favour the use of missiles or Magic. You will need a witchwood icon to fight them effectively. They drop the famous black Slayer mask, so the trip may be worth it.

#### Crawling hands

**Chaeldar:** Crawling hands are undead severed hands; fast and dexterous they claw their victims. Massacre these unnatural creations at the Slayer Tower near Canifis.

#### Dagannoth

**Chaeldar:** Dagannoth are large, sea-dwelling creatures that are very aggressive. You'll often find them in caves near sea water. I can tell that you've seen the vile creatures, so you know where to go. Remember: they attack with missiles, so protect yourself accordingly.

#### Dust devils

**Chaeldar:** Dust devils use clouds of dust, sand, ash and whatever else they can inhale to blind and disorientate their victims. They typically lurk in the smoky dungeon west of Pollnivneach.

#### Elves

**Chaeldar:** Elves are quick, agile, and vicious fighters who often favour bows and polearms. They also irritate me intensely, so slaughtering them is definitely a good thing. They live in the forests far, far to the west of human lands.

#### Fever spiders

**Chaeldar:** Fever spiders are giant spiders that carry the deadly spider fever. If you don't want to catch it, I suggest you wear Slayer gloves to fight them. I've heard they cause problems in breweries, if you want to find some.

#### Fire giants

**Chaeldar:** Like other giants, Fire giants often wield large weapons. Learn to recognise what kind of weapon it is and act accordingly. The smoke dungeon to the west of Pollnivneach has a population of these large and irritating creatures, so go smite them.

#### Fungal magi

**Chaeldar:** Fungal magi live in the upper levels of a cave north-east of the Mage Training Arena. The creatures of that dungeon are coated in toxic fungus, so you should fight them from a distance, and be prepared for their magical attacks. The fungus helps them repair physical damage, so you might prefer using magical attacks too.

#### Gargoyles

**Chaeldar:** Gargoyles are winged creatures of stone. You'll need to fight them until they are close to death before breaking them apart with a rock hammer. There is a thriving population of Gargoyles in the Slayer Tower near Canifis.

#### Grifolapines

**Chaeldar:** Grifolapines live in a cave north-east of the Mage Training Arena. The creatures of that dungeon are coated in toxic fungus, so you should fight them from a distance, and dodge when they charge at you. The fungus helps them repair physical damage, so you might prefer using magical attacks.

#### Grifolaroos

**Chaeldar:** Grifolaroos live in a cave north-east of the Mage Training Arena. The creatures of that dungeon are coated in toxic fungus, so you should fight them from a distance, although they'll try to hop over obstacles towards you. The fungus helps them repair physical damage, so you might prefer using magical attacks.

#### Grotworms

**Chaeldar:** Grotworms have recently been spotted in a cave near Rimmington. They will bite and attack with some sort of acid spit. Be careful - they are very aggressive.

#### Harpie bug swarms

**Chaeldar:** Harpie bugs are pesky critters that are hard to hit. You need a lit bug lantern to distract them with its hypnotic light. They live on Karamja, near the coast, and just south-east of the narrow stretch of land which joins the two halves of the island.

#### Infernal mages

**Chaeldar:** Infernal mages are dangerous spell users. Beware of their magic spells and go properly prepared to the Slayer Tower near Canifis.

#### Jellies

**Chaeldar:** Jellies are nasty, cube-like, gelatinous creatures which absorb everything they come across. Rellekka's caves are known to harbour these unnatural abominations. You can consider their attacks to be a form of Ranged and protect yourself with this in mind.

#### Jungle horrors

**Chaeldar:** Jungle horrors can be found all over Mos Le'Harmless. They are strong and aggressive, but you should be heroic enough to make short work of them.

#### Jungle strykewyrms

**Chaeldar:** These tricky creatures burrow beneath the jungle north west of Oo'glog. Be careful, as these poisonous jungle wyrms like to use magical attacks from a distance, while they should be weak to stab attacks.

#### Kalphites

**Chaeldar:** Kalphites are large insects that live in hives under the desert sands. South-west of Shantay Pass they swarm and make themselves a nuisance. Enter deeper into the lair if you are looking for a suitable challenge.

#### Kurasks

**Chaeldar:** Kurasks are large, brutal creatures with very thick hides. You'll need a leaf-bladed weapon, broad arrows or a magic dart to harm them. Kurask may be found in the caverns east of Rellekka. As they are so large, you may be able to kill them when they cannot reach you.

#### Lesser demons

**Chaeldar:** Lesser demons are weak to ranged attacks. Although they're relatively weak, they are still dangerous. Taverley's underworld is infested with these hellish pests.

#### Mutated zygomites

**Chaeldar:** Mutated zygomites are hard to destroy and attack with mainly magical damage. They regenerate quickly, so you will need to finish them off with fungicide. We have a bit of a problem with them here in Zanaris.

#### Shadow warriors

**Chaeldar:** Shadow warriors are dark and mysterious. They hide in the shadows, so be wary of ambushes. Apparently, a large number may be found lurking beneath the Legends' Guild.

#### Trolls

**Chaeldar:** Trolls regenerate quickly but are still vulnerable to poisons. They usually use crushing weapons. Perhaps you should head to the snowy islands where these creatures are both fierce and aggressive.

#### Turoths

**Chaeldar:** Turoths are large, vicious creatures with thick hides. You'll need a leaf-bladed weapon, broad arrows, or a magic dart to harm them. Seek them out in the Rellekka Slayer Caves.

#### Vyrewatch

**Chaeldar:** These foul creatures can be found throughout Meiyerditch and Darkmeyer in Morytania. They have a telepathic ability which enables them to dodge incoming attacks, so you'll need a weapon specially created to harm them.

#### Warped tortoises

**Chaeldar:** Warped tortoises are creatures tainted by magical waste. They are vulnerable to an elven chime.
