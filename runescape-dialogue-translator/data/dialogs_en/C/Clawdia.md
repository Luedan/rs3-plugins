# Clawdia

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Clawdia](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AClawdia)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawning

- A creature appears in the centre of the crater causing a change in the weather. Take it down to bring back summer!

### Dying during the fight

- {{Qact|The screen fades out and back in. The player appears next to Reyna with full health.}}
- You are dragged away from Clawdia's beach-ruining rampage to live for another day.

### Obtaining an item


#### With space in backpack

- (receives=item)
- You have received: [Item] x [number]

#### Without space in backpack and with space in bank

- You have received: [Item] x [number]. It has been added to your bank.

### Finishing a fight after having received rewards this hour

- You have already received rewards from killing Clawdia this hour so cannot claim any more.
- Try again when she next appears.
