- **Captain Ethar:** Greetings, recruit! Have you come for some Conquest training?

  - [Choice]: What is this place?

    - **Player:** What exactly is a Conquest?
    - **Captain Ethar:** A Conquest is the name we give to the training battles that are run here.
    - **Captain Ethar:** During a Conquest, you will command various troops in an effort to eliminate your opponent's troops.
    - **Player:** How does that help me, exactly?
    - **Captain Ethar:** You'll learn to command troops and formulate tactics: skills that might become invaluable in our continued struggle against the Void.
    - **Captain Ethar:** Of course, by taking part in a Conquest, you will also earn Commendations to trade in for rewards, as you see fit.

#### Attempting to enter the Conquest lobby without the required combat level

- **Captain Ethar:** I'm sorry, recruit, but you will need more experience of battle before you can take part in our Conquest training exercise.
- You need a Combat level of at least 50 to participate in a Conquest.
