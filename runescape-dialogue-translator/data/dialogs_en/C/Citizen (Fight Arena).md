## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Citizen:** Hello, little fluffy wuffy bunny wabbit. Where's the tea party going to be?
- **Player:** Hello?
- **Citizen:** Skippety skoppety. Yes, Mrs Badger, I'm going to make some wongleberry jam.
- **Player:** Mrs Badger? What's a wongleberry?
- **Citizen:** Don't worry, Mrs Badger, I'm sure he'll be a good rabbit. Otherwise he won't get his birthday treat.
- **Player:** I'm not sure I want to know this.
- **Citizen:** Wongleberry and buttercream trifle with sparkly sparklegums on top! Wongliscious.
- **Player:** Wongliscious indeed.
