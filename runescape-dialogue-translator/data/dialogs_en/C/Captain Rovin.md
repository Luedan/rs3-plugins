## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock


#### On free-to-play worlds

- **Captain Rovin:** What are you doing here? Only palace guards are allowed to come up here.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: I am one of the palace guards.
    - **Player:** I am one of the palace guards.
    - **Captain Rovin:** No, you're not! I know all the palace guards.
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: I'm a new recruit.
        - **Player:** I'm a new recruit.
        - **Captain Rovin:** I interview all the new recruits. I'd know if you were one of them.
        - **Player:** That blows that story out of the water then.
        - **Captain Rovin:** Get out of my sight.
      - [Choice]: I've had extensive plastic surgery.
        - **Player:** I've had extensive plastic surgery.
        - **Captain Rovin:** What sort of surgery is that? I've never heard of it. Besides, you look reasonably healthy.
        - **Captain Rovin:** Why is this relevant, anyway? You still shouldn't be here.
  - [Choice]: What about the king?
    - **Player:** What about the king? Surely you'd let him up here.
    - **Captain Rovin:** Well, yes, I suppose we'd let him up. He doesn't generally want to come up here, but, if he did want to, he could.
    - **Captain Rovin:** Anyway, you're not the king either, so get out of my sight.
  - [Choice]: Yes I know, but this is important.
    - **Player:** Yes, I know, but this is important.
    - **Captain Rovin:** Okay, I'm listening. Tell me what's so important.
    - [Menu]: Select an option
      - [Choice]: Erm, I forgot.
        - **Player:** Erm, I forgot.
        - **Captain Rovin:** Well, it can't be that important then.
        - **Player:** How do you know?
        - **Captain Rovin:** Just go away.
      - [Choice]: The castle has just received its ale delivery.
        - **Player:** The castle has just received its ale delivery.
        - **Captain Rovin:** Now that is important. However, I'm the wrong person to speak to about it: go talk to the kitchen staff.

#### On members' worlds, without the requirements for Defender of Varrock

- **Captain Rovin:** What are you doing here? Only palace guards are allowed to come up here.
- **Player:** Just exploring, really.
- **Captain Rovin:** Well go and do it somewhere else!

#### With the requirements for Defender of Varrock

- **Captain Rovin:** What are you doing here? Only palace guards are allowed to come up here.
- **Player:** Just exploring, really.
- **Captain Rovin:** Hmmm, an explorer, eh? I might have a little task for you if you're interested.
- (see|Defender of Varrock)

#### After Defender of Varrock

- **Captain Rovin:** Thank you again for your help in defeating the zombie army.
- **Player:** You're welcome. Any news on where Zemouregal and Arrav went?
- **Captain Rovin:** Nope, not yet. There's still a whole bunch of zombies in Zemouregal's old Wilderness base, but we've not seen hide nor hair of the individuals we're truly interested in apprehending.

#### After River of Blood

- **Captain Rovin:** First you helped us defeat Zemouregal's zombie army, and now you've saved all of Misthalin from invasion by Morytanian bloodsuckers. You're a veritable scourge of the undead.
- **Player:** Vampyres aren't undead. They're living creatures, originally from Vampyrium. It's a common misconception.
- **Player:** Regardless, you're welcome. Any news on where Zemouregal and Arrav went?
- **Captain Rovin:** Nope, not yet. There's still a whole bunch of zombies in Zemouregal's old Wilderness base, but we've not seen hide nor hair of the individuals we're truly interested in apprehending.
