# Cart Camel

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Cart Camel](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ACart%20Camel)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### If spoken to without a camulet

- ''Player may say any one of the following at random:
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Player:** That camel looks vicious, it'll probably eat me if I go too near!
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Player:** I wonder if I can catch something from that camel?
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - **Player:** Mmm... looks like that camel would make a nice kebab.

### If spoken to with a camulet

- ''Camel and Player may say any one of the following at random:
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Player:** That camel looks vicious, it'll probably eat me if I go too near!
    - **Camel:** Well, I have to say, I am pretty peckish, but thankfully, I don't eat humans... often.
    - *(Continues below)*
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Player:** I wonder if I can catch something from that camel?
    - **Camel:** You'll catch a stiff kick if you talk about me like that again!
    - *(Continues below)*
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - **Player:** Mmm... looks like that camel would make a nice kebab.
    - **Camel:** You and whose army mate? You and whose army?
    - *(Continues below)*
- After the cart camel responds to your insult:
  - **Player:** Oooh, sorry, didn't realise you could hear me?
  - **Camel:** Well I can, so watch what you say in future.
  - **Player:** Righto!
