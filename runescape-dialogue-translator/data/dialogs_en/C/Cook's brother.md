## Dialogue Transcript


### During 2016 Thanksgiving event


#### Talking to without having attended a previous Thanksgiving event

- {{Transcript missing}}

#### Talking to having attended a previous Thanksgiving event

- **Cook's brother:** Oooooh, what a bother! Why do these things always happen to me?
- **Player:** What's wrong?
- **Cook's brother:** Well, my brother and I thought we might introduce a new festive meal to the castle, but everything went wrong!
- **Player:** What happened?
- **Cook's brother:** We, uh, imported a new bird to serve as the main course at our Thanksgiving feast, but they had a mind of their own!
- **Player:** This sounds awfully familiar...
- **Cook's brother:** Oh it's you again! Look I understand this may be a huge coincidence but those these birds just keep escaping.
- **Player:** You should really get someone to help you with that.
- **Player:** I'm a bit... stuffed... at the moment.
- **Cook's brother:** I'm sure we can find more of those lamps for you, please we're in for a beak future!
- **Cook's brother:** Do you think you could help us?
  - Yes, I'll help.
    - **Player:** I suppose I'm not chicken, I'll help.
    - **Cook's brother:** Wonderful! I need you to track down the locations of the turkeys that escaped. These turkeys are clever, and will have disguised themselves well.
    - **Cook's brother:** There are twenty five to find, and I'll give you a book which has the last known sighting of each turkey. As you find them, their exact location will be magically communicated to me.
    - **Player:** Hold on, last time I only had twenty turkeys to find, why are there more?
    - **Cook's brother:** Let's just say these turkeys are in a fowl mood and were counting the number of chopping days until Thanksgiving.
    - **Player:** Well it's a good thing I have my trusty drumstick then!
    - **Cook's brother:** I'm sure we can find pumpkin pie, that's my favourite kind of pie to go with it also.
    - **Player:** So, do I need to do anything else?
    - **Cook's brother:** Here's the book, be careful not to lose it.
    - He hands you a book.
    - *(Dialogue terminates.)*
  - No, I'd rather not.
    - **Player:** No, I'd rather not.
    - **Cook's brother:** Oh, well. Thank you for listening, anyway.

#### Talking to when hunting for turkeys

- *(If the turkey book has been lost:)*
  - **Cook's brother:** Here's a replacement book, since you so carelessly lost the last one!
  - *(Continues below.)*
- **Cook's brother:** Have fun hunting turkeys! You've currently found [number of turkeys found].

#### Transmogrifying into a turkey

- *(If the transmogrification isn't unlocked:)*
  - **Cook's brother:** I'm afraid you can't do this yet, collect 10 turkeys for me and then you'll get to live your dream of being a turkey.

#### Claiming the medium XP lamp

- **Cook's brother:** For finding 20 turkeys you can have this old lamp I have.
- You have been given a medium XP lamp!
- *(Continues with the chat when hunting for turkeys.)*

#### Claiming the large XP lamp

- **Cook's brother:** You've found all of the turkeys!
- **Cook's brother:** I can't thank you enough!
- **Player:** I was in a fowl mood anyway, this has cheered me right up, when do we eat?
- **Cook's brother:** Well uh... we can only offer you this lamp. Sorry.
- **Player:** Well this is a sad pun-ishment.
- You have been given a large XP lamp!

#### Talking to after finishing the event

- **Cook's brother:** Thank you for finding all of the turkeys!

#### Talking to after the event

- **Cook's brother:** I hope you had a happy Thanksgiving. I'm afraid all the turkeys have returned to their homes.
