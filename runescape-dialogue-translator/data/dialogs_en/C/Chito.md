##### With crocspeak

- ((Only on first time:))
  - **Chito:** Greetings, I'm Chito.
  - **Player:** Nice to meet you! I'm Player.
  - (continue)
- ((Only after the first time:))
  - **Chito:** Hello, Player. It's good to see you on this fine, relaxing, day.
  - ((If the players has the Gators cosmetic override active:))
    - **Chito:** Wait a minu-
    - **Chito:** WHAT ARE THOSE HIDEOUS THINGS YOU'RE WEARING?
    - **Player:** Oh these things on my feet? They're my cro- I mean, Gators!
    - **Chito:** I can't believe you thought these were appropriate! The heads of my fallen brethren.
    - **Chito:** Humans have absolutely no shame.
    - **Chito:** AND, not to mention, they're not even fashionable! They're the most awfully ugly shoes I have ever seen.
    - **Chito:** So, ugly shoes, and disrespectful to the dead. Not just fashioned out of their decapitated heads, but to be walking around on them! Treading in all sorts of dirt, muck, and dung!
    - **Player:** ...
    - **Chito:** Disgusting. I refuse to engage in more conversation with you while they're in my vicinity.
    - **Player:** You know Dundee crafts them though, right?
    - **Chito:** My lifelong friend Dundee? HE crafts those monstrosities??
    - **Player:** Sorry you had to find out this way.
    - **Chito:** Our friendship is ruined.
  - (continue)
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - How are you today?
    - **Player:** How are you today?
    - **Chito:** Wonderful, thank you for asking. How could I be anything but when surrounded by a relaxing swimming pool, tasty snacks, and perfect weather for sunbathing.
    - **Player:** It is a lovely place! Maybe even better than the beach in Lumbridge Crater, dare I say...
    - **Chito:** I've tried to visit there, but the border guards always stop me. Something about frightening the locals. But I do hear it's quite fun! Sandcastles, cocktails, barbecues... What's not to like? I'm sure Dundee would enjoy the terrorbird racing too.
    - **Player:** I'll have to take you next time!
    - (previous)
  - Why don't you want to eat me?
    - **Player:** Why don't you want to eat me?
    - **Chito:** I've grown out of that. It's rather uncivilised to consume another walking, talking, sentient being like myself. Besides, there's too much to chew. Hair, bones, clothing. And don't get me started on the little 'pop' eyeballs make when you bite into them.
    - **Player:** Aaand I'm going to stop you right there.
    - (previous)
  - Why are you hanging out with Dundee?
    - **Player:** Why are you hanging out with Dundee?
    - **Chito:** Dundee is a lovely chap. He came across me some time ago, when I was just a hatchling. He feeds me a delicious variety of food, more than I could catch myself. And he tells me stories from all over Gielinor, nothing of which I could experience as a crocodile. In return, I help him with his studies. It's a nice little arrangement we have.
    - **Player:** And what about Frito, on the other side of the oasis? Do you know her?
    - **Chito:** Ah yes, Frito. Apologies for not having introduced you two formally. She's my cousin. I figured we could use some help over here, so I invited her down. I know she can be a bit... unfriendly sometimes. If you want to get on her good side, I know she's a big fan of coconuts!
    - **Player:** Noted!
    - (previous)
  - You're looking rather fancy today. (Only appears if the golden crocodile cosmetic upgrade is applied)
    - **Player:** You're looking rather fancy today.
    - **Chito:** Why thank you, Player. I feel rather fancy too!
    - **Chito:** Dundee hand-crafted this special look just for me. He really went all out! I very much appreciate it.
    - **Dundee:** Anything for you, pal!
    - (previous)
  - Farewell.
    - **Player:** Farewell.
    - **Chito:** Farewell!

- **Baby troll:** I NOT FOOD!

- **Baby troll:** *grr*
- **Baby troll:** You food.
- **Player:** Oh dear.
- **Chito:** I would pay to see that!
- **Baby troll:** ARGHHHHH
- The baby troll chomps down on Chito's leg, with all their might.
- **Chito:** ...
- **Chito:** That was... impressive! You almost drew blood.
- **Baby troll:** ...

- ((If Top of the Food Chain hasn't been completed:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Top of the Food Chain' - Feed a baby troll to Chito.

#### Cooked meat

- ((If the Feed the crocodile task has not been completed yet:))
  - **Chito:** It's better than raw, but I am used to the finer foods.
  - **Player:** Oh please, go on. I need it for this task.
  - **Chito:** Well, fine, if only to help you out. Nope. Definitely not the fine dining I am used to. Excuse me now while I look for somewhere discreet to vomit.
  - You have completed: 'Feed the crocodile' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
- ((Otherwise:))
  - **Chito:** This is better than raw, but doesn't quite have the same quality as what I'm used to.
  - **Chito:** Dundee cooks me freshly caught steak, rubbed in spices from Menaphos and sizzled to perfection on his mahogany campfire. It's quite the delicacy.
  - **Player:** Now I'm hungry!
  - ((If the Vegan Unfriendly achievement was completed:))
    - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Vegan Unfriendly' - Feed all the crocodiles some steak.

    - You can activate this via the 'Titles' tab in the 'Customisations' interface.
