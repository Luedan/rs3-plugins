## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue after The Firemaker's Curse


#### Without having completed The Temple at Senntisten, The World Wakes or Fate of the Gods


##### Below 91 Firemaking

- **Player:** What do you plan to do now?
- **Char:** I will stay here and train, until I am worthy again.
- **Player:** Train in Firemaking?
- **Char:** Of course, fool.
- **Player:** Could you train me?
- **Char:** Perhaps. I see a spark of talent in you, but you need to progress your skills further before you are worthy of my time.

##### At 91 Firemaking and above

- **Char:** Are you ready to begin?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes!
    - **Player:** Yes!
    - **Char:** Get on with it then.
    - {{qact|Char's training cave session begins.}}
  - [Choice]: Could you show me how to do it, again?
    - **Player:** Could you show me how to do it, again?
    - **Char:** As a child, I learned the basics of fire building and control, but Zaros taught me something new.
    - **Char:** You can get great worth from your fires if you create them in specific patterns, and have the right knowledge.
    - **Char:** I have constructed the grid behind me to train this skill. You will find the patterns I use in my journals.
    - {{qact|Camera pans to the grid. A square pattern is shown.}}
    - **Char:** For example, the basic square.
    - {{qact|A spiral pattern is shown.}}
    - **Char:** Or the spiral.
    - {{qact|Camera pans back to the player character.}}
    - **Char:** Of course there is one pattern, very close to my heart, that I did not write down.
    - **Char:** I will give you a set amount of time to make as many arrangements as you can, and will reward you for each arrangement made. Use this side as a base, as I did in my examples, so I can see your patterns properly.
    - **Char:** Are you ready to begin?
    - (previous)

#### With having completed The Temple at Senntisten, The World Wakes or Fate of the Gods

- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: I'd like to talk about your past, and Firemaking.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Ask about the pit.
        - (above)
      - [Choice]: cond=After The Temple at Senntisten|I'd like to talk to you about Zaros.
        - **Player:** I'd like to talk to you about Zaros.
        - (Condition: Before Fate of the Gods:)
          - **Char:** I have told you, mortal, I will not tell you any more.
          - **Player:** But when I was with Azzanadra-
          - **Char:** Azzanadra! How do you know this name? I demand you tell me!
          - **Player:** I know him! I helped him contact Zaros himself.
          - **Char:** Insolent creature! Why did you keep this from me?
          - **Player:** I didn't want to say it in front of the firemakers...
          - **Player:** And you did seem quite distracted trying to kill us.
          - **Char:** I cannot accuse you of lying.
          - **Char:** I may despise the Mahjarrat race for Zamorak's doings, but Azzanadra was always one of the exceptions. A true, loyal and brave follower of Zaros.
          - **Char:** He must think greatly of you to allow you to be present when making contact with our almighty Zaros. I suppose it cannot harm to share with you.
          - **Char:** Zaros may be gone, but only fools and those with Zamorak's overwhelming self-confidence would assume Zaros was oblivious to his future.
          - **Char:** Azzanadra and I are both restored. There are others, I'm pretty sure of it, and we will strive to put Zaros' plans in to motion.
          - **Player:** What plans? What others?
          - **Char:** Azzanadra may trust you but that does not mean I have to. I have told you enough.
        - (Condition: After Fate of the Gods:)
          - **Char:** I have told you all I wish to, mortal.
  - [Choice]: cond=After The World Wakes|I'd like to talk about Guthix's death.
    - (Condition: If the player sided with the Guthixians:)
      - **Char:**  A victorious day! And you tried to thwart us! Be gone, you may be a talented firemaker but I will not hear your Guthixian ramblings.
      - **Char:** Zaros will prevail, and you will burn in his wake!
    - (Condition: If the player sided with the Zarosians:)
      - **Char:** A victorious day! I am glad you chose the true path, Player. Zaros will prevail, and we will stand together in his glory.
      - **Player:**  I look forward to it.
      - **Char:** Let me tell you something, Player. You chose 'our' plan, but it was not the plan of all Zarosians.
      - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player sided with the Saradominists:)
      - **Char:** A victorious day! And an intriguing decision from you. I cannot say I disagree with it.
      - **Player:** You're not angry I didn't side with you, and the Zarosians?
      - **Char:** Let me tell you something, Player. You denied 'our' plan, but it was not the plan of all Zarosians.
      - (continue)
    - **Char:** It was Azzanadra, Wahisietel and Akthanakos' plan. The Mahjarrat... they wanted to keep Guthix alive.
    - **Player:** So you didn't?
    - **Char:** Of course not!
    - **Char:** To claim Guthix's death, the destruction of the Edicts, in the name of Zaros?
    - **Char:** What an honour; the greatest gift for Zaros!
    - **Char:** And then Sliske took it for himself.
    - **Char:** He left Nex and I to go along with the Mahjarrat, believing it was the only way.
    - **Char:** But he was just out for himself.
    - **Player:** But he did still kill Guthix. Allowing Zaros' return.
    - (Condition: Before Fate of the Gods:)
      - **Char:** That is true. But when Zaros returns, who do you think he will praise most? Those who turned up, or the one that killed Guthix? It should have been me.
    - (Condition: After Fate of the Gods:)
      - **Char:** It turns out I was wrong. If I had been the one to kill Guthix, I wonder if Zaros would have excommunicated me.
  - [Choice]: cond=After Fate of the Gods|I'd like to talk about Zaros's return.
    - (Condition: If Zaros was given a dark simulacrum:)
      - **Char:**  What is there to say? He is back where he belongs. My flame grows brighter once again.
      - **Player:**  What are you to him?
      - **Char:**  I am Char. No more, no less.
      - **Player:**  Well that cleared things right up, thanks.
    - (Condition: If Zaros was given a light simulacrum:)
      - **Char:**  You tried to stop him and failed - he has returned in spite of your efforts.
      - **Player:**  That's not quite correct. If not for me, he wouldn't be back at all, not even in a weakened state.
      - **Char:**  You played a dangerous game denying him. You should not need me to tell you that you are playing with fire.

### Training cave dialogue


#### Talking to Char in the middle of training

- **Char:** What are you doing? If you don't want my help you can just leave and stop wasting my time!

#### While creating patterns


##### Creating a circle or a cross

- **Char:** Good, but I'm sure you can do better.

##### Creating concentric circles

- **Char:** Good.

##### Creating a spiral

- **Char:** Very good!

##### Creating a Zaros symbol

- **Char:** Yes! Excellent!
- ((If Impressing Char hasn't been completed:))
  - Congratulations! You have completed: 'Impressing Char' - Create Char's most treasured symbol in her training cave.

#### Upon ending

- **Char:** {{RSChat|I've seen enough for now.}}
- You made [number] arrangements.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You made [number] arrangements.
