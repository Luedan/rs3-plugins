# Chris (Evil Dave's Big Day Out)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Chris (Evil Dave's Big Day Out)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AChris%20%28Evil%20Dave%27s%20Big%20Day%20Out%29)
- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### When first spoken to

- **Player:** Hi, I'd like to access my account.
- **Chris:** Today's bank is brought to you by Aubury's Rune Shop, your one stop shop for that last rune drop!
- **Chris:** Oh, it's you Dave. Did you you know that we have a special competition on today?
- **Player:** That's nice, but I just want to access my account.
- **Chris:** We're being sponsored by Aubury's Rune Shop. You could win a prize!
- **Player:** I just want to access... wait, prize? What prize?
- **Chris:** An earth rune.
- **Player:** A whole earth rune?
- **Chris:** That's right! A magnificent prize!
- **Player:** I'm in!! What do I have to do?
- **Chris:** Well Dave, all you have to do is correctly guess the number of runes in this jar.
- The banker holds up an almost entirely empty jar.
- **Chris:** As you can see, we've already given out quite a lot of prizes.
- **Chris:** Would you care to take a guess?
- **Player:** But there's only one rune in that jar.
- **Chris:** Final answer?
- **Player:** Yes. There's clearly only one rune in the jar.
- **Chris:** I'm sorry Dave, but I'm going to have to press you. Are you *quite sure*?
- **Player:** Yes. Wait, no. Yes! One rune!
- **Chris:** You win!
- **Chris:** One earth rune to you, congratulations!
- The banker removes the earth rune from the jar and hands it over to you.
- **Chris:** Aubury's Rune Shop, your one stop shop for that last rune drop!
- **Chris:** Is there anything else I can help you with today?
- **Player:** My account?
- **Chris:** Of course.

### After first speaking to

- **Player:** Hi, I'd like to access my account.
- **Chris:** Of course. Visit the Varrock Sword Shop today for a fine steal on some fine steel. They're slashing prices!
