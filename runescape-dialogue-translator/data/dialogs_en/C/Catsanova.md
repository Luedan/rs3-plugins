- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Without catspeak

- **Catsanova:** Meow, meow meow.

### With catspeak

- **Catsanova:** Ah, thank you, my friend. Your timely intervention has saved my whiskers from a most unpleasant confrontation. There I was, entertaining a beautiful young feline, when who should walk in but her father? I hid, of course, to protect my lady from  the inevitable fury of her father, but then I found myself trapped here, unable to escape. Now, thanks to you, I can return to delight the ladies with my particular...charms.
