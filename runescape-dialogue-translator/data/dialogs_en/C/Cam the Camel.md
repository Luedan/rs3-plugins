## Dialogue Transcript


### In Al Kharid


#### On free-to-play worlds

- ((The player says one of the following at random:))
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - **Player:** If I go near that camel, it'll probably bite my hand off.
    - (continue|glare)
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - **Player:** Mmm... Looks like that camel would make a nice kebab.
    - (continue|glare)
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - **Player:** I wonder if that camel has fleas...
    - (continue|glare)
- {{Qact|The camel makes a noise.}}
- ((One of the following messages plays at random:))
  - *Dialogue 1:*
    - The camel spits at you, and you jump back hurriedly.
  - *Dialogue 2:*
    - The camel turns its head and glares at you.
  - *Dialogue 3:*
    - The camel tries to stamp on your foot, but you pull it back quickly.

#### On members' worlds, without a camulet or cramulet

- [Menu]: What would you like to do?
  - [Choice]: Ask the camel about its dung.
    - **Player:** I'm sorry to bother you, but could <br>you spare me a little dung?
    - The camel didn't seem to appreciate that question.
  - [Choice]: Say something unpleasant.
    - (above|On free-to-play worlds)
  - [Choice]: Neither - I'm a polite person.
    - You decide not to be rude.

#### With a camulet or cramulet

- [Menu]: What would you like to do?
  - [Choice]: Ask the camel about its dung.
    - **Player:** I'm sorry to bother you, but could <br>you spare me a little dung?
    - **Cam the Camel:** Are you serious?
    - **Player:** Oh yes. If you'd be so kind...
    - **Cam the Camel:** Well, just you close your eyes first.<br> I'm not doing it while you're watching me!
    - {{Qact|The screen goes black momentarily.}}
    - (You close your eyes...|color=#FF0000)
    - **Cam the Camel:** I hope that's what you wanted!
    - **Player:** Ohhh yes. Lovely.
  - [Choice]: Say something unpleasant.
    - ((The player says one of the following at random:))
      - *Dialogue 1:*
        - **Player:** If I go near that camel, it'll probably bite my hand off.
        - **Cam the Camel:** What was that?
      - (continue|important)
      - *Dialogue 2:*
        - **Player:** Mmm... Looks like that camel would make a nice kebab.
        - **Cam the Camel:** What was that?
        - (continue|important)
      - *Dialogue 3:*
        - **Player:** I wonder if that camel has fleas...
        - **Cam the Camel:** What was that?
        - (continue|important)
    - **Player:** Nothing important.
    - **Cam the Camel:** The weather's really hot, don't you think?
    - **Player:** Not hot for camels, surely?
    - **Cam the Camel:** Are you joking? It's burning hot out here! Sitting by the oasis is the only way to stay cool.
    - **Player:** I thought camels carried their own water in their humps.
    - **Cam the Camel:** It's never enough. And it's always far too hot to go anywhere in the desert.
    - **Cam the Camel:** Sometimes I wish I could go travelling in cooler places, like all those adventurers I see passing through here.
    - **Player:** Well, adventuring's thirsty work as well.
    - **Cam the Camel:** Oh, that's a pity.
    - **Cam the Camel:** In that case, I'll just stay by this nice cool oasis.
  - [Choice]: Neither - I'm a polite person.
    - You decide not to be rude.

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- (Condition: Without a camulet or cramulet:)
  - **Cam the Camel:** Grrrrow!
  - (continue)
- (Condition: With a camulet or cramulet:)
  - **Cam the Camel:** Here is your puzzle box!
  - (continue)
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Cam the Camel has given you a puzzle box...somehow!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- (Condition: Without a camulet or cramulet:)
  - Cam the Camel is not impressed.
- (Condition: With a camulet or cramulet:)
  - **Cam the Camel:** That's not quite what I was looking for.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Cam the Camel has given you another clue scroll...somehow!]
  - (continue)
- (If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Without a camulet or cramulet:)
  - **Cam the Camel:** Grrronk!
- (Condition: With a camulet or cramulet:)
  - **Cam the Camel:** That's your reward!
