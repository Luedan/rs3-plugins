### At Glacor Front


#### Talk to

- ((On first time:))
  - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** Ah, Player. Fancy seeing you here.
  - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** You've a way of showing up when things become fraught.
  - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** If there's nothing urgent, though, I'll get back to trying this Arch-Glacor in illusory knots.
  - (continue)
- ((After the first time:))
  - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** Hello again!
  - (continue)
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What's your role here?
    - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** I'm here to run interference.
    - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** The Arch-Glacor has a most unpleasant attack: an icy energy beam of - frankly - ludicrous magnitude.
    - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** It's crude, but frustratingly unavoidable. And it's been killing our highly-trained warriors with impunity.
    - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** I can stop the Arch-Glacor from building up the power to use that attack, with a heady cocktail of interdicting cantrips and misdirecting illusions.
    - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** I've also been projecting Fremennik rowing songs into its mind. Very difficult to shift once they're lodged in.
  - ((Only with the Frost Cannon mechanic toggled off:))
    - Take a break. I can handle this.
      - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** Ahhh, about time! My feet are killing me, and I'm running out of irritatingly catchy tunes to hum.
      - If Charos stops channelling his spell, the Arch-Glacor will use its unavoidable attack. You will need to mitigate its damage to survive. Are you sure?
      - **SELECT AN OPTION**
        - Yes, bring it on.
          - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** Very well. Do take care not to die.
          - The 'Frost Cannon' mechanic is now active.
          - The Arch-Glacor will now use its unavoidable ice beam attack. Speak to Charos again to disable the mechanic.
        - Actually, wait!
          - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** Bother. Don't su ppose you could fetch me a sparkling water with a slice of lime, could you?
  - ((Only with the Frost Cannon mechanic toggled on:))
    - Could you start blocking that huge attack again?
      - **Charos (Frost Cannon):** No rest for the wicked, eh? Or, indeed, the devilishy handsome.
      - The 'Frost Cannon' mechanic has been disabled.
      - Charos is now preventing the Arch-Glacor from using its unavoidable ice beam. Speak to him again if you wish to re-enable the mechanic.

#### Toggle mechanic


##### With Frost Cannon toggled off

- The 'Frost Cannon' mechanic is now active.

##### With Frost Cannon toggled on

- The 'Frost Cannon' mechanic has been disabled.
