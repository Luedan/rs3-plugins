## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Family Crest

- **Caleb:** Who are you? What are you after?
- Select an option
  - Nothing, I will be on my way.
    - **Player:** Nothing, I will be on my way.
  - I see you are a chef, will you cook me anything?
    - **Player:** I see you are a chef... Could you cook me something?
    - **Caleb:** I would, but I am very busy. I am trying to increase my reknown as one of the worlds' leading chefs by preparing a special and unique fish salad.

### After Family Crest


#### With the unimbued family gauntlets

- **Caleb:** I hear you have brought the completed crest to my father. I must say, that was awfully impressive work.
- **Player:** I believe your father mentioned you would be able to improve these gauntlets for me...
- **Caleb:** Yes, that is correct. I can slightly alter these gauntlets to allow you some of my skill at preparing seafood by making them cooking gauntlets. When you wear them you will burn lobsters, sharks and swordfish a lot less. It will be a permanent modification however, so make sure this is what you want.
- Select an option
  - Yes, please do that for me.
    - **Player:** Yes, please do that for me.
    - Caleb takes the gauntlets from you, pours some herbs and seasonings on them, bakes them on his range for a short time, then hands them back to you.
    - (removes=family gauntlets) (receives=Cooking gauntlets)
  - I'll see what your brothers have to offer first.
    - **Player:** I'll see what your brothers have to offer first.
    - **Caleb:** As you wish. Let me know if you change your mind.

#### With the imbued family gauntlets or without any of the gauntlets


##### Talk-to option

- **Caleb:** I hear you have brought the completed crest to my father. I must say, that was awfully impressive work.
- **Player:** It was easy really.
- **Caleb:** Yes well... Is there anything I can help you with?
- *Choose an option:*
  - Can you change my gauntlets for me?
    - **Player:** Can you change my gauntlets for me? {{anchor|change-gauntlets}}
    - *(If the player doesn't have any of the gauntlets:)*
      - **Caleb:** You don't have any gauntlets I can imbue.
    - *(If the player currently has cooking gauntlets in inventory:)*
      - **Caleb:** Your gauntlets have already been treated with my potions. You don't have any gauntlets I can imbue.
  - *(After having the family gauntlets enchanted for the first time:)*
    - Does your family own any more gauntlets?
      - **Player:** Does your family own any more gauntlets? I really appreciate the skill and effort you and your brothers have put into those gauntlets, and to prove it, I would like to own one of each pair.
      - **Caleb:**Yes, well, my father gave me these when I was young. They are very good and I wouldn't just give them away.
      - **Player:**How much would you want for them?
      - **Caleb:**1 million.
      - Select an option
        - I would like to buy them for 1 million.
          - **Player:** I would like to buy them for 1 million.
          - **Caleb:** Yes? That's a lot of money!
          - Select an option
            - **Player:**Yes!
              - (removes=1,000,000 coins) (receives=cooking gauntlets)
            - **Player:**I suppose not.
              - (end)
        - I suppose you had better keep them.
          - **Player:** I suppose you had better keep them.
          - **Caleb:** Thank you!
  - No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
