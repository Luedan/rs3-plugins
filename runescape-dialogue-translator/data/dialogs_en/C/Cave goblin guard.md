- **Letter**: `C`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to option

- {{trandom}}
  - (Condition: Dialogue 1:)
    - **Cave goblin guard:** Have a nice day!
  - (Condition: Dialogue 2:)
    - **Cave goblin guard:** I'm keeping a eye on you, surface-dweller.
  - (Condition: Dialogue 3:)
    - **Cave goblin guard:** Surface-dweller! You will never find your way into the city of Dorgesh-Kaan!
    - **Player:** Isn't it through that big door?
    - **Cave goblin guard:** Well, yes, but you'll never find your way in!
    - (Condition: After completion of Death to the Dorgeshuun:)
      - **Player:** But I thought I was allowed into the city now.
      - **Cave goblin guard:** Oh, yes, so you are. I'd better say something different.
      - **Cave goblin guard:** *Ahem*
      - **Cave goblin guard:** Surface-dweller! You will easily find your way into the city of Dorgesh-Kaan!
      - **Cave goblin guard:** How about that?
      - **Player:** Yes, that's more appropriate.

### Attempting to open the door to the Dorgesh-Kaan before completing Death to the Dorgeshuun

- You have not yet earned the right to pass through that door.
- **Cave goblin guard:** Surface-dweller! You may not pass through that door!
