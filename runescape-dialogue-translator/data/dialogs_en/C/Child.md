# Child

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Child](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AChild)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Child:** I'm not allowed to speak with strangers.

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Child:** I'm not allowed to speak with Mourners.

### After Plague's End


#### While not wearing full Mourner gear

- **Child:** Oh! I' know you. You're the hero who freed us from the Mourners! Hurray!

#### While wearing full Mourner gear

- **Child:** Mourner! I can't believe I was scared of you. Get out!
