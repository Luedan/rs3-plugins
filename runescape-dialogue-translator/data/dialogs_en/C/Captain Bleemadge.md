# Captain Bleemadge

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Captain Bleemadge](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ACaptain%20Bleemadge)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Captain Bleemadge:** Flight ready to leave!
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Can you take me on the glider?
    - (opens=Gnome glider)
  - [Choice]: Nothing.
    - (end)

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Captain Bleemadge:** You must complete this!
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [The pilot has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Captain Bleemadge:** Please finish the puzzle for me!

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [The pilot has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Captain Bleemadge:** Well done!

### Gnome Restaurant delivery

- **Captain Bleemadge:** Aahh, the smell of home, there's nothing like it. THanks.
