# Corrupt glyph

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Corrupt glyph](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ACorrupt%20glyph)
- **Letter**: `C`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Appearing

- Some corrupt glyphs have appeared on the ritual site!
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - Some corrupt glyphs have appeared on the ritual site!

### Attempting to deactivate an incorrect corrupt glyph

- This wasn't the correct glyph.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up above the correct glyph:))
  - This wasn't the correct glyph.

### Deactivating the correct corrupt glyph

- You deactivate the corrupt glyph ([number]/3).
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - You deactivate the corrupt glyph ([number]/3).
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If this was the third corrupt glyph:)
  - You successfully deactivate all of the corrupt glyphs.
  - The following reward is added to the focus storage: [number] x [Item]

### Disentegrating without deactivating the corrupted glyphs in time

- The corrupt glyphs disintegrate into the atmosphere.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - The corrupt glyphs disintegrate into the atmosphere.
