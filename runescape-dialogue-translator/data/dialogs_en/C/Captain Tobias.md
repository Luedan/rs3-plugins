## Dialogue Transcript

{{Obsolete|Dialogue changed with 23 March update}}

### Talk-to option

- **Captain Tobias:** Do you want to go on a trip to Karamja?
- **Captain Tobias:** The trip will cost you 30 coins.
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Yes, please.
    - **Player:** Yes please.
    - (Condition: If the player does not have enough money:)
      - **Player:** Oh dear, I don't seem to have enough money.
      - You can not afford that.
      - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
        - You can not afford that.
    - ''(If you have completed the Karamja easy tasks:)
      - **Captain Tobias:** Wait a minute, didn't you earn Karamja gloves? Thought I'd seen you helping around the island. You can go on half price - 15 coins.
      - You pay 15 coins and board the ship.
      - The ship arrives at Karamja.
    - ''(Without the Karamja easy tasks:)
      - You pay 30 coins and board the ship.
      - The ship arrives at Karamja.
  - [Choice]: *(With a ring of charos (a) equipped:)* Or I could pay you nothing at all...
    - **Player:**  Or I could pay you nothing at all...
    - **Captain Tobias:** Mmmm, nothing at all, you say...
    - **Captain Tobias:** Yes, why not - jump aboard then.
    - You board the ship.
    - The ship arrives at Karamja.
  - [Choice]: No, thank you.
    - **Player:** No, thank you.

### Pay fare option

- *(If you have completed the Karamja easy tasks but do not have 15 coins:)*
  - You can not afford that.
  - You do not have enough coins to pay passage. You need 15 coins having earned the Karamja gloves.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - You can not afford that.
- ''(Without the Karamja easy tasks, and without 30 coins:)
  - You can not afford that.
  - You do not have enough money for that.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - You can not afford that.
- *(If you have completed the Karamja easy tasks:)*
  - The Captain smiles as he recognises you as having earned Karamja gloves and lets you pass for half price - 15 coins.
- ''(Without the Karamja easy tasks:)
  - You pay 30 coins and board the ship.

### Treasure Trails

- (removes=the old clue scroll (easy))
- ((If the trail continues:))
  - (receives=a new clue scroll (easy))
  - [Well done, matey! Here you go.]
  - (continue)
- ((If the trail ends:))
  - (receives=reward casket (easy))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Captain Tobias:** Well done, Player!
