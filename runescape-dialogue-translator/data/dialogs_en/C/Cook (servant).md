## Dialogue Transcript


### In Servants' Guild

- **Cook:** You're not artistocracy, but I suppose you'd do. Do you want a good cook for [number] coins?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What can you do?
    - **Player:** What can you do?
    - **Cook:** I, sir, am the finest cook in all Gielinor! I can also make good time going to the bank or the sawmill.
  - [Choice]: Tell me about your previous jobs
    - **Player:** Tell me about your previous jobs.
    - **Cook:** I used to be the cook for the old Duke of Lumbridge. Visiting dignitaries praised me for my fine banquets!
    - **Cook:** But then someone found a rule that said that only one family could hold that post.
    - **Cook:** Overnight I was fired and replaced by some fool who can't even bake a cake without help!
  - [Choice]: You're hired!
    - **Cook:** I'm not getting involved in these new houses. Hopefully soon.
