## Dialogue Transcript


### At the bridge south of Rellekka

- **Player:** Hello.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: So you fixed the bridge?
    - **Player:** So you fixed the bridge?
    - **Council workman:** Aye, that I did. 'Twas real thirsty work too. If only some kind stranger would buy us a bit of beer to sup, eh?
    - **Council workman:** What with that inn at Seers' Village so close by and all, eh?
    - The workman winks at you.
    - **Council workman:** Just make sure yer don't get me none of that non alcoholic rubbish from that poison salesman guy at the tavern!
  - [Choice]: About the Task System...
    - ((If the player has completed the easy Fremennik achievements and the rewards have not been claimed:))
      - **Player:**  I've completed all of the Easy Tasks in the Fremennik set.
      - **Council workman:** Ah, that's good to 'ear. You can wear Fremennik sea boots now - I have a pair for you.
      - (Condition: If the player does not have sufficient space in backpack:)
        - **Council workman:** Although, you'll have to come back when you've the space to take them.
      - (Condition: If the player does have sufficient space in backpack:)
        - (receives=Fremennik sea boots 1 and Antique lamp (Easy Fremennik Tasks))
        - **Council workman:** They're strange, them boots. I reckon they'll impress the Fremennik and their strange spirits. Look at them carefully, see if you can't work out what they do.
        - **Player:** Thanks!
        - (continue)
    - (Condition: If the player has lost their Fremennik sea boots 1:)
      - **Council workman:** You again? How did you get on with those boots?
      - **Player:** I lost them.
      - **Council workman:** Lost them? Well, it jus' so happens I found another pair. Take better care of 'em this time.
      - (receives=Fremennik sea boots 1)
      - (continue)
    - [Menu]: Choose an option:
      - [Choice]: Tell me about the Achievement System.
        - **Council workman:** Very well: the Achievement System is a collection of deeds you may wish to complete while adventuring around the world.
        - **Council workman:**You can earn special rewards for completing certain achievements; at the very least, each is worth a cash bounty from Explorer Jack in Lumbridge.
        - **Council workman:** Some also give items that will help complete other achievements, and many count as progress towards the set for the area they're in.
        - [Menu]: Choose an option:
          - [Choice]: Tell me about the set reward for this locality.
            - **Player:** Tell me about the set reward for this area.
            - **Council workman:**  For completing the Tasks in the Fremennik Province set you get a pair of boots.
            - **Council workman:** The more difficulty levels of the set you complete, the better the boots get - we'll tell you what they do when we give them to you.
            - You may also see what rewards a set item provides by hovering over the Rewards icon for its Exploration Achievement set in the Achievements interface.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: How do I claim these rewards?
            - **Player:** How do I claim the rewards?
            - **Council workman:** You need to complete all of the tasks in a set of a particular difficulty, then you can claim your reward.
            - **Council workman:** Some of the Fremennik set's Tasks are simple, some will require certain skill levels, and some might require quests to be started or completed.
            - **Council workman:**  To claim your Fremennik set rewards, speak to Yrsa in Rellekka, Advisor Ghrim on Miscellania, or myself.
            - (previous2)
          - [Choice]: Sorry, I was just leaving.
            - (end)
      - [Choice]: Could I buy a spare of the reward?
        - [Menu]: Choose an option:
