## Dialogue Transcript


### In Varrock

- **Curator Haig Halen:** Welcome to Varrock Museum!
- (Condition: If the player is speaking to Curator Haig Halen for the first time since achieving at least 50 kudos:)
  - {{RSFontAchievement|It Belongs in a Museum}}
  - (continue)
- [Menu]: Select an option
  - [Choice]: Have you any interesting news?
    - **Player:** Have you any interesting news?
    - **Curator Haig Halen:** Yes, we have an expedition underway at a rather interesting island to the north of Morytania. We believe that it may be of archaeological significance.
    - **Player:** Oh? That sounds interesting.
    - **Curator Haig Halen:** Indeed. We're looking for qualified archaeologists to travel there to assist the expedition.
    - **Player:** Would I qualify?
    - (Condition: If the player has less than 100 kudos:)
      - **Curator Haig Halen:** Although it's not necessarily a prerequisite, you might also want to consider helping out more here at Varrock Museum.
    - (Condition: If the player has at least 100 kudos:)
      - **Curator Haig Halen:** You've certainly done a lot to help out Varrock Museum, so we'd be silly not to ask for your expertise.
      - **Player:** Thank you. I'll look forward to it!
    - (If the player has not done the Dig Site quest)
      - **Curator Haig Halen:** Well, qualified archaeologists are our ideal, but I'm sure we'll find plenty to do for the unqualified.
      - **Curator Haig Halen:** But you may want to take your Earth Science exams at the Varrock Dig Site anyway, just for the fun of it!
      - **Player:**Okay, thanks.
  - [Choice]: Do you know where I could find any treasure?
    - **Player:** Do you know where I could find any treasure?
    - **Curator Haig Halen:** Look around you! This museum is full of treasures!
    - **Player:** No, I meant treasures for ME.
    - **Curator Haig Halen:** Any treasures this museum knows about it goes to great lengths to acquire.
  - [Choice]: Talk about the master quest cape.
    - **Player:** I notice that you have acquired a new cape.
    - **Curator Haig Halen:** Oh this? The design was donated to the museum as a symbol of the spirit of adventure and the pursuit of knowledge. Would you like to purchase one?
    - (Condition: If the player does not meet the requirements:)
      - You cannot equip this as you have not completed the Master Quest Cape achievement. Please check the Achievements tab to see what you have left to accomplish.
      - **Curator Haig Halen:** On second thought, you're yet to see many things. Guess I'll hold on to it for now.
    - (Condition: If the player meets the requirements:)
      - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
        - [Choice]: Pay the 120,000.
          - **Player:** That sounds fair.
          - (removes=120,000 coins) (receives=master quest cape)
          - **Curator Haig Halen:** Thanks. Enjoy!
        - [Choice]: Don't pay.
          - **Curator Haig Halen:** Suit yourself.

### Pickpocket option

- **Player:** I have a package for you.

- (removes=research items)
- You have completed: 'Deliver artefacts to the museum' and gained progress towards the Oasis Restoration event.
