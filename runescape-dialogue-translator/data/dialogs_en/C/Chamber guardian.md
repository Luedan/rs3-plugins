# Chamber guardian

## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Mage Arena


#### Before praying at a statue

- **Player:** Hello my friend, Kolodion sent me down.
- **Chamber guardian:** Sssshhh... the gods are talking. I can hear their whispers.
- **Chamber guardian:** Can you hear them adventurer, they're calling you.
- **Player:** Erm... ok!
- **Chamber guardian:** Go chant at the statue of the god you most wish to represent in this world, you will be rewarded.
- **Chamber guardian:** Once you are done, come back to me. I shall supply you with a mage staff ready for battle.

##### Attempting to trade him before claiming the free staff

- The guardian is deep in concentration.

#### After praying at a statue, without the cape in backpack

- **Player:** Hi.
- **Chamber guardian:** Hello adventurer, have you made your choice?
- **Player:** I have.
- **Chamber guardian:** Good, good, I hope you have chosen well. Have you been rewarded with a cape from your chosen god?
- **Player:** I'm afraid I don't have it with me.
- **Chamber guardian:** Once you have your cape, come back to me, and I shall supply you with a mage staff ready for battle.

#### After praying at a statue, with the cape in backpack

- **Player:** Hi.
- **Chamber guardian:** Hello adventurer, have you made your choice?
- **Player:** I have.
- **Chamber guardian:** Good, good, I hope you have chosen well. I will now present you with a magic staff. This, along with the cape awarded to you by your chosen god, are all the weapons and armour you will need here.
- (Condition: If the player prayed to Saradomin:)
  - (receives=Saradomin staff)
  - [The guardian hands you an ornate magic staff.]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player prayed to Guthix:)
  - (receives=Guthix staff)
  - [The guardian hands you an ornate magic staff.]
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If the player prayed to Zamorak:)
  - (receives=Zamorak staff)
  - [The guardian hands you an ornate magic staff.]
  - (continue)
- {{RSFontAchievement|Mage Arena|Defeat Kolodion at the Mage Arena and collect a god staff.}}

#### After claiming the free staff

- **Player:** Hello again.
- **Chamber guardian:** Hello adventurer, are you looking for another staff?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What do you have to offer?
    - (opens=Mage Arena Staves)
  - [Choice]: No thanks.
    - **Player:** No thanks.
    - **Chamber guardian:** Well let me know if you need one.
