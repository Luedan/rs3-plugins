## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Captain:** What do you want, civilian?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: What are you doing?
    - **Player:** What are you doing?
    - **Captain:** What does it look like I'm doing? I'm training this useless bunch of recruits!
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: Oh yes, they look very fit.
        - **Player:** Oh yes, they look very fit.
        - ''(If you haven't chosen anything previously:)
          - **Captain:** They're nothing but a useless bunch of weaklings who won't survive an hour once they're out on patrol.
          - (other)
        - *(If you previously picked the second option:)*
          - **Captain:** Have you lost your mind? Earlier you said they looked weak! Just go away and leave me alone.
      - [Choice]: Oh yes, they look very weak.
        - **Player:** Oh yes, they look very weak.
        - ''(If you haven't chosen anything previously:)
          - **Captain:** Show some respect when you talk about my men, civilian!
          - (other)
        - *(If you previously picked the first option:)*
          - **Captain:** Have you lost your mind? Earlier you said they looked fit! Just go away and leave me alone.
      - [Choice]: Can I have some armour like yours?
        - (below)
  - [Choice]: Can I have some armour like yours?
    - **Player:** Can I have some armour like yours?
    - **Captain:** Only guards of Varrock can wear this armour.
    - **Player:** Well, I could become a guard of Varrock.
    - ''(If the player has 31 Thieving or greater:)
      - **Captain:** Oh yeah?  I see you've got a Thieving level of [number]. That must give you quite a long criminal record.
      - **Player:** I could repent of my evil ways if you like.
      - (continue)
    - **Captain:** So you really want to be a guard, huh?
    - **Player:** Yes, that's right.
    - **Captain:** Does it worry you that the life expectancy of a guard is about 30 seconds?
    - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
      - [Choice]: No, that doesn't worry me.
        - **Player:** No, that doesn't worry me.
        - **Captain:** Bah! You just failed the intelligence test. Go away, civilian.
      - [Choice]: Actually, forget it.
        - **Player:** Actually, forget it.
        - **Captain:** Go away, coward.
  - [Choice]: I want nothing.
    - **Player:** I want nothing.
    - **Captain:** Lucky you. Go away.
