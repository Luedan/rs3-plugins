### Assignment advice


#### Bats

- **Vannaka:** Bats are rarely found on the ground, so you'll have to fight them while they're airborne, which won't be easy with melee. They have been spotted in the Taverley dungeon.

#### Bears

- **Vannaka:** Bears are tough creatures and fierce fighters, so watch out for their powerful claws. Bears have been spotted west of Gunnarsgrunn near the mysterious ruins. I heard a rumour of a stronger type of bear down a rift to the east of Varrock.

#### Catablepon

- **Vannaka:** Catablepon are cow-like, magical creatures that mainly live in the Stronghold of Security. Beware their weakening glare; cutting off their head could help.

#### Cave slime

- **Vannaka:** Cave slimes are the lesser cousins of jellies. Don't be fooled, though: they can still be dangerous as they're often poisonous. You'll do Lumbridge a favour if you kill the slimes in Lumbridge Swamp caves.

#### Cockroaches

- **Vannaka:** Cockroaches are resilient creatures often found in dark and damp places. They have been spotted in the Stronghold of Player Safety to the south.

#### Flesh crawlers

- **Vannaka:** Flesh crawlers are scavengers and will eat you and anyone else, given the chance. Save the weak by killing these creatures in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Frogs

- **Vannaka:** Frogs will attack and move faster than the average human. You can find frogs in the Lumbridge swamp and if you venture into the swamp caves you can find giant frogs.

#### Ghosts

- **Vannaka:** Ghosts play tricks and japes upon those entering Taverley Dungeon, so put them to rest. If you are after a bigger challenge you can find some stronger ghosts in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Grotworms

- **Vannaka:** Grotworms have recently been spotted in a cave near Rimmington. They will bite and attack with some sort of acid spit. Be careful - they are very aggressive.

#### Hill giants

- **Vannaka:** Hill giants use crude weapons or tools so prepare accordingly. There is a colony of them, deep inside the Edgeville tunnels; all good folk will approve if you clean it out.

#### Hobgoblins

- **Vannaka:** Hobgoblins are larger and stronger then their brethren. There is a peninsula, south-west of Falador, where these creatures have a hideout.

#### Ice giants

- **Vannaka:** Like other giants, ice giants often wield large weapons. Try using fire spells to melt them before they can hit you. Ice giants can be found underground to the south of Port Sarim.

#### Ice warriors

- **Vannaka:** Ice warriors are a kind of ice elemental. Thurgo the dwarf recently spotted some in the caves south of Port Sarim.

#### Icefiends

- **Vannaka:** Icefiends are beings of ice and freezing rock. They're quick and agile, so you'll want to be careful when getting close to them. Ice Mountain is infested with these pesky creatures.

#### Kalphites

- **Vannaka:** Kalphite are large insects that live in hives under the desert sands. You can best enter their hive south-west of Shantay Pass, but try not to go too deep.

#### Minotaurs

- **Vannaka:** Minotaurs are large, man-like creatures, but you'll want to be careful of their horns. I hear they have set up camp under in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Mogres

- **Vannaka:** Mogres are a type of aquatic ogre that is often mistaken for a giant mudskipper. You have to force them out of the water with a fishing explosive. You can find them on the peninsula to the south of Port Sarim.

#### Moss giants

- **Vannaka:** Like other giants, moss giants often wield large weapons. Use fire spells to burn them to cinders before they can damage you. Strangely for giants, some have been seen at the end of the Varrock sewers.

#### Scorpions

- **Vannaka:** Scorpions are almost always venomous and usually found in groups. Last I heard you can find some scorpions in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Skeletons

- **Vannaka:** The last Skeleton I killed I found earth spells took them down effectively.
- [Skeletons are undead, so Magic is your best bet against them. Skeletons can be found in Edgeville Dungeon or Varrock sewers.]

#### Spiders

- **Vannaka:** Spiders are often venomous and many varieties are camouflaged, too. I've seen plenty to the south-west of Lumbridge. If you are up for a challenge you can find stronger spiders down in the Stronghold of Security.

#### Wall beasts

- **Vannaka:** Wall beasts are really much larger creatures, but you'll only see their arms. You'll want something sharp on your head to stop them grabbing you. The geology of the caves beneath Lumbridge Swamp favours these creatures.

#### Wolves

- **Vannaka:** Wolves are pack animals, so you'll always find them in groups. Watch out for their bite, it can be nasty. There are plenty to kill on White Wolf Mountain.

#### Zombies

- **Vannaka:** Zombies are undead, so using magic against them is preferred. You should be able to find them roaming in the tunnels beneath Edgeville.
