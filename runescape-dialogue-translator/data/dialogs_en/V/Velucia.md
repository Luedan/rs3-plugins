## Dialogue Transcript


### At the Archaeology Campus


#### Before contributing the sword in the Archaeology tutorial

- **Velucia:** Hey, there! I'm Velucia. I'm a collector of artefacts for Varrock Museum! If you're interested in learning more, you should talk with Acting Guildmaster Reiniger as she's training up new archaeologists.
- **Velucia:** If you ever manage to restore an artefact, I'll be interested in looking at it.

#### After contributing the sword in the Archaeology tutorial

- **Velucia:** Thanks for helping me complete my collection, Player!
