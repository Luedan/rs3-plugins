  - (Condition: Having upgraded the ring:)

      - Vorkath's busy having a nice, long, rest.
  - (Condition: With Tiny Zemouregal summoned:)
    - **Tiny Zemouregal:** BIG DRAGON!
    - **Vorkath:** ...
    - **Tiny Zemouregal:** BIG DRAGON UP! WE FLY!
    - **Player:** I don't think Vorkath wants to do that any more.
    - **Tiny Zemouregal:** BURN IT ALL, DRAGON!
    - **Vorkath:** (Vorkath ignores him and goes back to sleep.)
  - (Condition: With Vorki summoned:)
    - **Vorki:** Look, Player! It's me!
    - **Vorkath:** ...
    - **Vorki:** It's BIG me!
    - **Vorki:** I'm going to be that big one day.
    - **Vorki:** And I'll EAT YOU, Player!
    - **Vorkath:** ...

- (Condition: If the player has The Shadow Realm and Zorgoth's Journal in their backpack:)
  - **Player:** What's the matter, Vorkath?<br/>Can't you sleep?
  - **Vorkath:** *sad dragon noises*
  - {{Qact|The player opens The Shadow Realm.}}
  - [You retrieve a book about the shadow realm from your backpack, and open it to the first page.]
  - **Player:** Ahem. 'In my research expeditions to the mainland, I have spent some time studying the sorcerers of the empty god's empire...'
  - {{Qact|Screen fades to black and then fades in.}}
  - **Player:** 'Having reached even Gielinor, I do not know what, if anything, can stop it from filling the universe entirely.'
  - **Vorkath:** *frightened dragon noises*
  - **Player:** Maybe not ideal bed-time material.<br/>Let's try another one.
  - {{Qact|The player opens Zorgoth's Journal.}}
  - **Player:** Adventure, Inc. presents 'Zorgoth's Journal', written by Zorgoth, and narrated by Player.
  - **Player:** 'I despise humans. Their population spreads across every world I touch...'
  - **Vorkath:** *angry dragon noises*
  - **Player:** ...is not what this book says!<br/>This one is about...
  - **Player:** Uh...
  - **Player:** It's about a very brave dragon, who had all sorts of adventures...
  - {{Qact|Screen fades to black and then fades in.}}
  - **Player:** '...and they all lived happily ever after!'
  - [You close the book, and observe with a smile that Vorkath is now snoring peacefully.]
  - (Condition: If first time reading the two stories to Vorkath:)
