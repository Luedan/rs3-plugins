## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Fremennik Trials

- **Volf Olafson:** Sorry, outlander, but I have things to be doing.

### Before Olaf's Quest but after The Fremennik Trials

- **Volf Olafson:** Hello there. Enjoying the view?
- **Player:** Yes I am. You have a lovely yurt.
- **Volf Olafson:** Thanks! I exercise it regularly.

### After Olaf's Quest

- **Volf Olafson:** Hello again, friend! Does my father send any word...or treasures like before?
- **Player:** Not today, but if he does, you will be the first to know.
