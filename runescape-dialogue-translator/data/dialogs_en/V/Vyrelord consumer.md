## Dialogue Transcript


### Pickpocket


#### With under 118 Thieving

- You need to be a level 118 thief to pick this Vyrelord consumer's pocket.
- ((Shown in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|You need Thieving level 118.|warning=yes}}

#### With at least 118 Thieving


##### Unsuccessfully


##### With full backpack

- You don't have enough space in your inventory to do that.
- You cannot keep pickpocketing as you have a full inventory.

##### Getting caught

- You fail to pick the Vyralady consumer's pocket.
- **Vyrelord consumer:** {{RSChat|How uncouth!}}
- {{Qact|The vyrelord consumer hits the player.}}
- You've been stunned.

##### Successfully

- ((The following repeats until interrupted, out of backpack space, or caught:))
  - You pick the Vyrelord consumer's pocket.
  - (Condition: If receiving coins:)
    - 900 coins have been added to your money pouch.
  - (Condition: If receiving other items:)
    - (receives=loot)
    - (Condition: If reaching full backpack:)
      - You cannot keep pickpocketing as you have a full inventory.
