### Starting the encounter

### Moonstone Shards attack

- **Vermyx, Brood Mother:** {{RSChat|Snaaaarl!}}
- {{Colour: Snaaaarl!|colour=#99FF99}}
- Shards of moonstone mark your feet. Prepare to move!

  - Shards of moonstone mark your feet. Prepare to move!

### Coilspawn attack

- **Vermyx, Brood Mother:** {{RSChat|Craaaaa!}}
- {{Colour: Craaaaa!|colour=#99FF99}}
- {{Qact|A coilspawn appears.}}

### Soul Rush attack

- **Vermyx, Brood Mother:** {{RSChat|Rarghh!}}
- {{Colour: Rarghh!|colour=#99FF99}}
- {{Qact|Vermyx, Brooth Mother retreats to the tunnels.}}
- Vermyx channels power from the Sanctum towards the [{{Colour] Stand in the marker to redirect it!}}
- (Condition: When standing on a marker for the first and second times:)
  - [number]/3 souls redirected.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - [number]/3 souls redirected.
- (Condition: When standing on a marker for the third time:)
  - {{Qact|Vermyx, Brood Mother reappears from the tunnels.}}
  - Vermyx's grip on the Sanctum has been loosened.
  - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
    - Vermyx's grip on the Sanctum has been loosened.
