        - [Choice]: Yes, I loaned him money, just like he asked.

          - (continue|waste)
    - [Choice]: No, I haven't seen him.
      - **Victoria:** Well, if you do meet him, he'll ask you for money, no doubt. Please don't give him any.
      - **Player:** Why not?
      - **Victoria:** Sorry to tell you this, but if you lend him money you'll never see it again. My brother is such a waste of space. I've been lending him things for years and he never gives them back.
      - **Victoria:** Yes, but it never used to be this bad. You see...
      - **Victoria:** Lachtopher used to live on the east side of the river, before it was overrun with goblins. Although he didn't have a steady job, he used to help out around farms when he needed cash.
      - **Victoria:** Then, one day, the Duke told us it was no longer safe to live on the east riverbank, so some villagers had to move across here.
      - **Victoria:** With no money for lodgings, and nowhere else to go, Lachtopher came to live with me. I've only a small house, so he sleeps downstairs on the floor.
      - **Player:** Goodness. That sounds quite uncomfortable.
      - **Victoria:** Not uncomfortable enough, it seems.
      - **Victoria:** I thought he'd only be staying for a couple of weeks, just until he'd got some money together, but he's been here for ages now.
      - **Player:** So, why not just throw him out on to the streets?
      - **Victoria:** Oh, no! I couldn't do that to my brother. Besides, my parents taught me to support and care for those in need. I'm sure that, if I try hard enough, I can change my brother's ways.
      - **Victoria:** That doesn't mean he's having any more money out of me, however. He can have a roof over his head, but that's all.
      - **Player:** Good luck with that. I don't think Lachtopher deserves a sister like you.
      - **Victoria:** Such kind words. Thank you. Remember: don't give him any money - tell him to get a job instead.
      - **Player:** Okay, I'll try to remember that.
