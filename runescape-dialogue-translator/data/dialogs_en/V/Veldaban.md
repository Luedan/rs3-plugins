##### Without having completed Fishing Contest

- **Player:** Hi there, Veldaban! Have you got any new quests for me?
- **Commander Veldaban:** Not at the moment, no, Player.
- **Player:** That's too bad, I'm starting to get a little bored.
- **Commander Veldaban:** Then perhaps you should try your hand at the Hemenster Fishing Contest.
- **Player:** Er, that's a bit of a random suggestion. Why would I go on a fishing contest?
- **Commander Veldaban:** We dwarves don't know much about fishing, but a couple of our brothers at White Wolf Mountain want to win the competition.
- **Player:** Why would they want to do that if they don't know much about fishing?
- **Commander Veldaban:** Ah, you see, the trophy is made of pure gold. And these dwarves in particular love gold.
- **Player:** Alright, sounds interesting, I'll check it out. White Wolf mountain, you said?
- **Commander Veldaban:** Yes, you can speak to a dwarf at either side of the mountain.
