#### Talk to

- *(If you're not wearing the Order of Dis robes override:)*
  - **Vala:** Welcome to the border of the Wilderness. Are you merely a traveller passing through, or are you here seeking the ultimate challenge?
  - (continue)
- *(If you are wearing the Order of Dis robes override:)*
  - **Vala:** [Cultist name]. We meet again. Are you merely passing through, or are you here seeking the ultimate challenge?
  - (continue)
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What do you mean?
    - **Player:** What do you mean?
    - **Vala:** The Wilderness is a dangerous place - filled with dragons, demons, and worse...
    - **Vala:** ...but it was once something more. A brave frontier, shaped by the hand of Lord Zamorak himself, where the strongest battled one another for glory and spoils, and the weak were prey.
    - **Player:** People fought each other?
    - **Vala:** Yes. Now... there are those of us who still follow the old ways - clashing in deadly battles where the winner takes all.
    - **Vala:** If you wish, I can mark you as a willing participant in such battles. The risks are great, yes, but the rewards...potentially boundless.
    - (previous)
  - ((Only if currently opted out of PvP:))
    - I'd like to opt in.
      - **Player:** I'd like to participate.
      - **Vala:** It would be my honour.
      - {{Colour|#FF0000|Warning:}} If you opt into PvP, you can be attacked by other players in the Wilderness! If you are killed, your belongings will drop to the ground for others to take.
      - **{{AnchorText|ARE YOU SURE YOU WANT TO OPT IN}}TO PVP?**
        - Yes, I understand the risks and wish to opt into PvP.
          - {{Colour|#FF0000|You have opted into PvP combat in the Wilderness.}}<br>You can opt out at any time through Vala.
        - No thank you.
          - (end)
  - ((Only if currently opted into PvP:))
    - I'd like to opt out.
      - **Player:** I don't want to participate anymore.
      - **Vala:** So be it. Come back anytime.
      - {{AnchorText|You have opted out}} of PvP combat in the Wilderness.<br>You can opt back in at any time through Vala.
  - I'm leaving now.
    - **Player:** I'm leaving now.
    - **Vala:** I will be here should you change your mind.
