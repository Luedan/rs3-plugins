## Dialogue Transcript


### In his cell in the Black Knights' Base


#### Before claiming the dusty key

- **Velrak the explorer:** Thank you for rescuing me! <br>It isn't very comfy in this cell.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: So...do you know anywhere good to explore?
    - **Velrak the explorer:** Well, this dungeon was quite good to explore...until I got captured, anyway. I was given a key to an inner part of this dungeon by a mysterious cloaked stranger!
    - **Velrak the explorer:** It's rather tough for me to get that far into the dungeon however. I just keep getting captured!
    - **Velrak the explorer:** Would you like to give it a go?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Yes, please!
        - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
          - **Velrak the explorer:** I'd give you the key, but your backpack is full.
        - (Condition: With space in backpack:)
          - (receives=dusty key)
          - [Velrak reaches somewhere mysterious and passes you a key.]
          - (initial)
      - [Choice]: No, it's too dangerous for me too.
        - **Velrak the explorer:** I don't blame you!
  - [Choice]: Do I get a reward for freeing you?
    - **Velrak the explorer:** Well, not really. <br>The Black Knights took all of my stuff before throwing me in here to rot!
    - (initial)

#### After claiming the dusty key

- **Player:** Are you still here?
- **Velrak the explorer:** Yes... <br>I'm still plucking up the courage to run out past those Black Knights.
- **Player:** Oh, go on. You can do it. <br>I'll watch your back.
- **Velrak the explorer:** Well, I'll try. If I make it, you'll be able to find me at the entrance to Taverley dungeon.
- {{Qact|Screen fades out and back in; Velrak has escaped.}}
- {{RSFontAchievement|Fly You Fool}}

### At the entrance to Taverley dungeon

- **Velrak the explorer:** Sunlight! The wonder of a gentle breeze caressing my skin. Oh, it is glorious.
- **Velrak the explorer:** I was trapped so long in that Kinshra dungeon, I'd forgotten how colourful and bright Taverley is. Smell that fresh air!
- **Player:** You're really not as fresh as you think. Maybe it's time for a bath.
- **Velrak the explorer:** Yes, yes. Soon. First I want to gaze upon the glistening splendour of the Taverley river a while.
- (Condition: If the player has lost the dusty key:)
  - **Player:**Hey, you know that dusty key you gave me?
  - **Velrak the explorer:** Yes?
  - **Player:**I lost it.
  - **Velrak the explorer:**And?
  - **Player:**I'm hoping you might have it.
  - **Velrak the explorer:**Why would I have it?<br> I gave the key to you.
  - **Player:**Well, sometimes when I lose things, other people find them for me.
  - **Velrak the explorer:**Wow. Do you really think other people have nothing better to do but pick up after you? Your narcissism is showing.
  - **Velrak the explorer:** It's bad enough you can't even be bothered to hold onto the gifts we give you. Do you really think the world revolves around you?
  - **Player:**Look, just check your pockets. Trust me.
  - (Condition: Without space in backpack:)
    - **Velrak the explorer:** You check your pockets. You don't even have enough room in your backpack to hold a key.
  - (Condition: With space in backpack:)
    - (receives=dusty key)
    - [Velrak pats himself down and pulls out a dusty key. He blinks in surprise and hands it to you, bewildered.]
