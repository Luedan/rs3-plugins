# Vyrewatch mine guard

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Vyrewatch mine guard](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AVyrewatch%20mine%20guard)
- **Letter**: `V`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before The Branches of Darkmeyer

- **Vyrewatch mine guard:** Get lost, blood sack. This mine's closed to the likes of you.

#### Attempting to enter the mine

- **Vyrewatch mine guard:** Where do you think you're going, blood sack? This mine is off-limits to anyone who's not supposed to be there! Only vyres, tithe-dodgers and blood-marked humans allowed. None of which are you!
- **Player:** Sorry, I'll stay out.

### Between The Branches of Darkmeyer and River of Blood


#### With the Darkmeyer outfit or House Drakan outfit

- **Vyrewatch:** This is the daeyalt mine, in case you're wondering. You're free to enter and mine should you wish.

#### Without a disguise

- **Vyrewatch:** Get lost, blood sack. This mine's closed to the likes of you.
- **Player:** What, even to Vanescula's personal tithe?
- **Vyrewatch:** Try it and see what happens.

#### Attempting to enter the mine without a disguise

- **Vyrewatch:** Where do you think you're going, blood sack?
- **Player:** Where does it look like? Into the mine, of course. I'm sure I don't need to remind you that I'm Vanescula's personal tithe...
- **Vyrewatch:** That doesn't give you free rein to go where you please!
- **Player:** Well, I suppose I could just tell Vanescula that you tithed me instead of sending me to the mines, right? I'm sure she'd understand.
- **Vyrewatch:** No, no! Really, there's no need to bother her with this. Please? By all means, go in. You'll have to mine like everyone else, though, as if you had been sent.
