# Vengeful Soul

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Vengeful Soul](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3AVengeful%20Soul)
- **Letter**: `V`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Spawning

- A vengeful soul appears nearby! Avoid it until it realises you're not the intended target of its ire.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|A vengeful soul appears nearby! Avoid it until it realises you're not the intended target of its ire.|warning=yes}}

### Catching the player

- {{Qact|The Vengeful Soul disappears.}}
- As they catch up to you, the vengeful soul loses interest and seeks to pursue another target.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|As they catch up to you, the vengeful soul loses interest and seeks to pursue another target.|warning=yes}}

### Avoiding for 15 seconds

- {{Qact|The Vengeful Soul stops chasing the player and turns into a variant that can be guided.}}
- The vengeful soul stops its pursuit. It appears to be ready to pass on.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|The vengeful soul stops its pursuit. It appears to be ready to pass on.|colour=#00FF00}}

### Guide

- {{Qact|The Vengeful Soul disappears.}}
- As you guide the vengeful soul to the underworld, they thank you by sharing some of their [Skill] knowledge.
- You gain [number] [Skill] XP.

### Failing to guide in 30.6 seconds

- {{Qact|The Vengeful Soul disappears.}}
- The lost soul disappears before you could guide it onwards.
- ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
  - {{Pop-up|The lost soul disappears before you could guide it onwards.}}
