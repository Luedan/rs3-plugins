## Dialogue Transcript


### In Morytania, north of the Ectofuntus


#### Without the requirements to start Rum Deal

- **Player:** Hello there!
- **Pirate Pete:** Mornin'.
- **Player:** Got any quests?
- **Pirate Pete:** I may have a quest, but you don't look like you'd be able to handle the kind of monsters I have problems with.

##### Quick-travel option

- **Pirate Pete:** I'm not sure where you want me to take you.
- You need to complete Rum Deal in order to quick-travel.
