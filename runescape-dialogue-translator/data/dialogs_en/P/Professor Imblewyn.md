## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Player:** I didn't realise gnomes were interested in magic.
- **Professor Imblewyn:** Gnomes are interested in everything, lad.
- **Player:** Of course.
- **Professor Imblewyn:** We have a saying, 'Curiosity killed the cat'. But cats aren't clever enough to understand what they find. Gnomes are.
- (Condition: If the player has completed A Tail of Two Cats:)
  - **Player:** Hey! Some of my best friends are cats!
  - **Professor Imblewyn:** I think that says more about you than cats.

### Manor Farm


#### Help


##### Before completing the request

- [Menu]: What would you like to do?
  - [Choice]: Ask about their request.
    - **Player:** What're you requesting today?
    - **Professor Imblewyn:** I've been working on my own farm recently and need a few items to bolster my productivity!
    - **Professor Imblewyn:** I'm trying to get some extra crops going, so I'll need : [Seed] x [number]
    - **Professor Imblewyn:** I'm also looking for a new animal, I'm currently after A [animal]
    - **Professor Imblewyn:** If you complete this, I'll give you : Cosmic rune x 50. I'm sure you'll gain some reputation with the farming guild too!
    - **Professor Imblewyn:** You'll have to be quick though!, I'm only here for [number] hours and [number] minutes. To be exact.
  - [Choice]: Give them the items & animals for their request
    - ((Without the requested items:))
      - **Professor Imblewyn:** You don't have enough items to fulfil my request. I won't take favourited animals.
      - **Professor Imblewyn:** I'm trying to get some extra crops going, so I'll need : [Seed] x [number]
      - **Professor Imblewyn:** I'm also looking for a new animal, I'm currently after A [animal]
    - ((With the requested items:))
      - (removes=the requested items) (receives=50 cosmic runes)
      - ((If a Brassica primer was rewarded:))
        - For completing this request you've received Brassica primer x 1.
        - ((Repeated in a pop-up:))
          - For completing this request you've received Brassica primer x 1.
          - (continue)
      - [You've received : Cosmic rune x 50 <br>And 2,750 farming reputation.]

##### After completing the request

- **Professor Imblewyn:** Thank you for your help!
- A new request will be available in [time]..
