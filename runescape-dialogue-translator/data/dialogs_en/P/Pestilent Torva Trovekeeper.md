## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to

- (Condition: Without having received the free Pestilent Gem shard:)
  - (Condition: Without free backpack space:)
    - **Pestilent Torva Trovekeeper:** I have a free Pestilent Gem shard for you here. But you don't have space for it!
    - (continue)
  - (Condition: With free backpack space:)
    - (receives=Pestilent Gem shard)
    - **Pestilent Torva Trovekeeper:** Hey there! Here's a free Pestilent Gem shard!
    - (continue)
- **Pestilent Torva Trovekeeper:** Can I interest you in something from the Pestilent Torva Trove?
- [You can get more Pestilent Gem shards from the Crystal Capsules Treasure Hunter Promotion.]
- (opens=Pestilent Torva Trove)
