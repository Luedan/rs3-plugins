# Plague Doctor (NPC)

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Plague Doctor (NPC)](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APlague%20Doctor%20%28NPC%29)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When first spoken to

- **Player:** What's up doc?
- **Plague Doctor:** Yes.. you'll do...
- **Player:** *gulp*
- The Plague Doctor hands you 2 stickers.
- **Player:** Thanks..?

### When spoken to normally

- **Player:** What's up doc?
- Tell me about your task list
  - **Player:** Tell me about your task list.
  - **Plague Doctor:** Yes... there's plenty of tasks you can do for me...
  - **Plague Doctor:** If you can finish the tasks on the card I'll make sure you're rewarded... appropriately.
  - **Player:** I do like being rewarded appropriately.
  - **Plague Doctor:** Yes... I'll show you how to be just like me...
  - Fill out the task list by completing tasks or using Plague Doctor Stamps! You will be able to unlock multiple Plague Doctor Outfit overrides as well as a brand new rest, teleport and walk animation!
- I've lost my task list.
  - **Player:** I've lost my task list.
  - *(If task list is in bank:)*
    - **Plague Doctor:** I do believe it's in your bank...
    - **Player:** How did you know that?
    - **Plague Doctor:** I know a lot about you... so young... so careless...
  - *(If task list is in inventory:)*
    - **Plague Doctor:** Apply this balm directly to your eyeballs... 12 times a day.
    - **Player:** Huh?
    - **Plague Doctor:** Yes... there's definitely something wrong with your eyes... the task list is in your inventory.
    - **Player:** Huh? You're right.
  - *(If task list isn't owned:)*
    - **Plague Doctor:** Yes... fine... here is another one...
    - **Player:** Thanks!
- What do I need to get for this prescription?
  - **Player:** What do I need to get for this prescription?
  - **Plague Doctor:** Yes... Have a look at my list...
  - *(During the promotion:)*
    - *(Medicine ingredients interface is shown.)*
  - *(After the promotion:)*
    - **Plague Doctor:** Not now... not now...
- I've got some ingredients for the prescription.
  - *(During the promotion:)*
    - *(If the task isn't completed:)*
      - *(If no ingredients are in inventory:)*
        - **Plague Doctor:** You haven't got anything useful to give me...
        - **Player:** Oh, okay.
        - **Plague Doctor:** It's quite alright...
        - **Plague Doctor:** There is no cure for stupidity...
      - *(If ingredients are in inventory, the corresponding ones out of the following are shown:)*
        - **Player:** I've brought you an Overload salve.
        - **Plague Doctor:** Yes...
        - **Player:** I've brought you a Divine herb patch III.
        - **Plague Doctor:** Perfect...
        - **Player:** I've brought you an empty bird's nest.
        - **Plague Doctor:** As expected...
        - **Player:** I've brought you 200 Harmonic Dust.
        - **Plague Doctor:** The most vital ingredient...
        - **Player:** I've brought you some Wallasalkibane ore
        - **Plague Doctor:** This will thicken it up...
        - **Player:** I've brought you a sack of potatoes
        - **Plague Doctor:** ...
        - *(If there are ingredients remaining:)*
          - **Plague Doctor:** Just a little bit more...
        - *(After final ingredient:)*
          - **Plague Doctor:** It is finally complete...
          - You have completed the task - 'Home Remedy'
    - *(If the task is completed:)*
      - **Plague Doctor:** The cure is complete... for now...
  - *(After the promotion:)*
    - **Plague Doctor:** Not now... not now...
- Goodbye.
  - **Player:** Goodbye.
  - **Plague Doctor:** You'll be back...
