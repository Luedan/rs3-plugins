## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Holy Grail

- **Peasant:** Woe is me! Our crops are all failing... how shall I feed myself this winter?

- **Peasant:** Oh happy day! Suddenly our crops are growing again! It'll be a bumper harvest this year!
