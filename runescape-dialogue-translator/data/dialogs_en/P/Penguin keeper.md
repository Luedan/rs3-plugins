## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

*When speaking to Penguin keeper in the Ardougne Zoo.*
- **Player:** Hello there. How are the penguins doing today?
- **Penguin keeper:** They are doing fine, thanks.
- *If the player has 30 Summoning, and doesn't have an existing penguin egg in their inventory or bank:*
  - **Player:** Hello there. How are the penguins doing today?
  - **Penguin keeper:** They are doing fine, thanks.
  - **Penguin keeper:** Actually, you might be able to help me with something - if you are interested.
  - **Player:** What do you mean?
  - *If the player has zero or three penguins unlocked in the pet interface:*
    - **Penguin keeper:** Well, you see, the penguins have been laying so many eggs recently that we can't afford to raise them all ourselves.
    - **Penguin keeper:** You seem to know a bit about raising animals - would you like to raise a penguin for us?
  - *If the player has one penguin unlocked in the pet interface:*
    - **Penguin keeper:**Well, the penguins have continued laying eggs, we simply don't have room for them all.
    - **Penguin keeper:**You seem to know your way around a penguin - would you like to raise another for us?
  - *If the player has two penguins unlocked in the pet interface:*
    - **Penguin keeper:**Well, we are now taking care of an incomprehensible amount of penguins!
    - **Penguin keeper:**It would be great if you could take care of just one more - would you like to raise a final penguin for us?
  - Select an option
    - Yes, of course.
      - **Player:** Yes, of course.
      - **Penguin keeper:** Wonderful!
        - *(If the player's inventory is full)*
        - **Penguin keeper:**Come back and see me when you have free space in your inventory. I'll be waiting.
      - **Penguin keeper:** Here you go - this egg will hatch into a baby penguin.
      - **Penguin keeper:**They aren't particularly fussy about anything, so it won't be any trouble to raise.
      - **Player:** Okay, thank you very much.
    - No thanks.
      - **Player:** No thanks.
      - **Penguin keeper:** Fair enough. The offer still stands if you change your mind.
