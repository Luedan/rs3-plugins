# Pickpocketing volunteer

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Pickpocketing volunteer](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APickpocketing%20volunteer)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### In the Thieves' Guild


#### Talking to him or her

- **Pickpocketing volunteer:** Alright there! I'm here for you to practise the ol' dippetydoodah on. If you want to know more, talk to the trainer.

#### Pickpocket


##### Failing

- **Pickpocketing volunteer:** {{RSChat|Spotted you.}}
- You attempt to pick the volunteer's pocket.
- You fail to pick the staff member's pocket.
- You've been stunned.

##### Succeeding

- You attempt to pick the volunteer's pocket.
- You retrieve a blue handkerchief and hand it back to the staff member.
- (Condition: If hanky points can still be gained for the week:)
  - [You gain a hanky point.]
- (Condition: If no more hanky points can be gained for the week:)
  - You have reached the maximum number of hanky points allowed at your level.

#### Check-points

- **Player:** How many hanky points do I have?
- **Pickpocketing volunteer:** You have collected [no hanky points yet/(number) hanky points]. You may claim the reward for up to [number] more hanky points this week.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What are all these handkerchiefs for?
    - (above)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to claim a reward for my hanky points.
    - **Player:** I'd like to claim a reward for my hanky points!
    - (Condition: With no hanky points to claim:)
      - **Pickpocketing volunteer:** You have no hanky points to claim! Try some of the guild's training exercises.
    - (Condition: With hanky points to claim:)
      - **Pickpocketing volunteer:** Sure thing! Let's see now...
      - (receives=Thieving XP)
      - [You gain [number] Thieving XP. You have 0 points remaining, and may turn in up to [number] more points this week.]
    - (Condition: Having claimed the maximum hanky points for the week:)
      - **Pickpocketing volunteer:** Sorry, you've claimed as many hanky points this week as you may at your level.
  - [Choice]: Bye for now.
    - ** Player:** Bye for now.
