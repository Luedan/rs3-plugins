## Dialogue Transcript


### November 2018


#### Overhead lines

- *(One of the following is seen occasionally:)*
  - **Parcel Pete:** It's time to give back, mail for everyone!
  - **Parcel Pete:** Parcels from the Hedge!
  - **Parcel Pete:** Post! Post! Post! There's so much post!

#### When talked to

- **Parcel Pete:** What do you want?
  - What's wrong?
    - **Player:** What's wrong?
    - **Parcel Pete:** Sorry, I'm just frustrated about this 'internship'. My brother won't listen to any of my ideas, and he's only in charge because he's older! Not to mention making me follow adventurers around like a pet...
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
  - What happened to the fanmail?
    - **Player:** What happened to the fanmail?
    - **Parcel Pete:** Ah yes, the first of my many improvements to the postal service. I simply glued all the fanmail to the parcels. Now we just have to deliver the parcels.
    - **Player:** Ah, so you just found parcels and fanmail from the same people and bundled them together. Clever!
    - **Parcel Pete:** From the same people, that would have made more sense...
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
  - Got any interesting rumours?
    - **Player:** Got any interesting rumours?
    - **Parcel Pete:** I don't read people's mail, you can't prove anything! I mean, er, I haven't heard any rumours recently.
    - **Player:** Would a few coins jog your memory?
    - **Parcel Pete:** Well if you were to hand me a few coins I might remember something...
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*
  - Anything I can help with?
    - **Player:** Anything I can help with?
    - **Parcel Pete:** Well, if you could find some bone hurting juice, I may have an idea... MWAHAHAHAHAHAHA Anyway, I have no idea where to find such a thing.
    - *(Returns to the initial options.)*

### 2021

- **Player:** Hello Pete. What are you up to?
- Parcel Pete's jawbone moves, but his voice seems to be missing.
- **Parcel Pete:** *Clack clack clack clack. Clack. Clack click clack clack. Clack click clack clack. Click click click.*
- ((The above reads "HELLO" in Morse code.))
- **Postie Pete:** Don't mind him, Player. He's had a rough year, lost his tongue in an unfortunate stamp licking accident.
- **Parcel Pete:** *Click clack. Click click click... Clack click. Click clack click clack. Click clack click clack. Clack clack. Click clack clack. Clack. Click clack. Click.*
- ((The above reads "NO ACCIDENT" in Morse code.))
- **Postie Pete:** I could use your help actually, Player, can you come and talk with me?

### 2022

- **Player:** Hello Pete. What are you up to?
- Pete whistles a tune with very inconsistent volume, pitch and tone. It's hard to listen to, but he seems content.
- **Postie Pete:** Don't mind him, Player. He's learning to whistle since losing his tongue in an unfortunate stamp licking accident last year.
- **Parcel Pete:** *Toot fwoop. toot toot toot... Fwoop toot. Toot fwoop toot fwoop. Toot fwoop toot fwoop. Fwoop fwoop. toot fwoop fwoop. Fwoop. Toot fwoop. Toot.*
- ((The above reads "NO ACCIDENT" in Morse code when the "fwoops" are interpreted as dots and "toots" as dashes.))
- **Postie Pete:** Anyway, he's on distraction duty, Player, can you come and talk with me?

### Parcels from the Dead

- **Player:** Hi Pete!
- **Parcel Pete:** *Click click click click. Click. Click clack click click. Click clack clack click.*
- ((The above reads "HELP" in Morse code.))
- **Player:** Oh dear, still having trouble speaking?
- **Parcel Pete:** *cough* Oh, sorry about that, still getting used to this new tongue.
- **Parcel Pete:** Thank goodness it's you, Player; I'm worried about my brother!
- **Parcel Pete:** Pete has taken on too much yet again, and he's working himself to the bone!
- **Player:** Ahaha, good one! They should call you Punny Pete.
- **Parcel Pete:** This isn't a laughing matter!
- **Player:** Oh, sorry. Can I help at all?
- **Parcel Pete:** I'm sure my bother would appreciate the assistance, perhaps ask him.
- **Parcel Pete:** I'm busy with my own responsibilities unfortunately, Pete has given me a new route to run!
- **Player:** Could I help with that? Where is it?
- **Parcel Pete:** Um... I can handle it on my own, don't worry.
- **Player:** I'll just see if Postie needs my help for now then. See you around!
