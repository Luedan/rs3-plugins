#### Talk-to


##### First time

- **Player:** What's going on here?
- **Pauline Polaris:** I've livid to cure, fences to fix, patches to fertilise, I'm exhausted and-
- **Player:** Hold on, hold on. Take a deep breath and start from the beginning.
- **Pauline Polaris:** Sorry, this farming is so new to me. A while ago, I was approached by the dashing and brave Lokar Searunner.
- **Player:** Dashing and brave? I might be thinking of a different guy: a pirate who ferried me to a pirate ship.
- **Pauline Polaris:** No, that sounds right. In a courageous trip to Karamja, he came across a funny chap called Papa Mambo. Lokar obtained some saplings from a vine that grows in the region and brought them to me. He wanted me to grow and enhance the vine using my magic. We did an experiment on one sapling and quickly discovered that magic enhanced the tenacious attributes of the plant.
- **Pauline Polaris:** Lokar named them 'livid'.
- **Player:** Livid?
- **Pauline Polaris:** Yes, it likely stands for something clever. But it is clearly quite livid!
- **Player:** Hmm, I might have to pay a visit to Lokar. I shudder to think what he might be using this 'livid' for.
- **Pauline Polaris:** Don't be mean: he has admirable intentions. He plans to transport what I grow to Chieftain Brundt, to help protect their village from wild beasts.
- **Player:** Against wild beasts? The livid do sound vicious, I suppose.
- **Pauline Polaris:** They definitely are. They have a mind of their own, and they carry on living even when cut from their roots. In exchange for the plants, Chieftain Brundt is kindly giving the Moon Clan some trade produce.
- **Player:** So, how can I help out on your farm?
- **Pauline Polaris:** Let me show you!
- {{Qact|Screen fade out/in. Cutscene begins.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** There are a variety of tasks you can help me out with here. You can do them in any order or just focus on individual tasks, if you want.
- **Pauline Polaris:** If you don't perform the tasks, I will eventually advance the farm on to its next stage, but don't expect any reward!
- {{Qact|Camera pans to the patches.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** First there's fertilising patches. I've buried saplings on these patches and they will need fertilising to appear.
- **Pauline Polaris:** So, cast the Fertilise Soil spell on the patch...
- {{Qact|One of the patches becomes fertilised.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** ...then the livid will grow and you'll get further knowledge in Magic and Farming.
- **Pauline Polaris:** Eventually, the livid will mature...
- {{Qact|The livid plant on the fertilised patch matures.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** ...but you will see that my plants get diseased quite often.
- {{Qact|The livid plant becomes diseased.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** It seems they are susceptible to the parasites native to this isle. Cast the Cure Plant spell on the crop and, after a little refinement...
- {{Qact|The livid plant is cured.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** ...Bingo! The livid is fine, and Magic and Farming experience are yours.
- {{Qact|Camera cuts to Pauline Polaris and the player.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** It's really tiring running this farm, so, sometimes, I'll need some help.
- {{Qact|Pauline Polaris becomes drained.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** Simply give me a few encouraging words and I'll be able to draw upon your run energy, making me right-as-rain.
- {{Qact|Pauline Polaris's energy is restored.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** In exchange, you'll receive some experience in Agility. I can recover slowly by myself, but your assistance would be much appreciated.
- {{Qact|Camera pans to the log pile.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** Once I harvest the livid, I'll teleport it over to the produce pile.
- {{Qact|The empty produce pile becomes full of produce.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** It is here that you can grab up to ten livid and bunch them together with string using the String Jewellery spell.
- {{Qact|Camera pans to the trade wagon.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** Then, place the bundled produce in the trade wagon. With this last step, you'll get experience in Magic and Crafting.
- {{Qact|Camera cuts to the log pile.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** Why is there a big pile of lumber, I hear you ask? Our fences often get into a state of disrepair. I'm not sure if it's the pesky suqah or the sea breeze, but the wood doesn't last very long here. Pick up some lunar lumber from the corner of the farm and use the Plank Make spell to turn them into fence posts.
- {{Qact|Camera pans west to broken fence.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** Then use the post on the broken fence. You'll get Magic and Construction experience for your effort upon affixing the post.
- {{Qact|The broken fence is repaired. Camera cuts to Pauline Polaris and the player.}}
- **Pauline Polaris:** So, there you have it!
