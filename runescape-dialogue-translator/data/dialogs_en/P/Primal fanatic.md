## Dialogue Transcript


### Overhead dialogue

- ((The following appears once every 18 seconds if there is a nearby player who hasn't talked to one of the fanatics:))
  - **Primal fanatic:** {{RSChat|Hey, Player. Have you heard about the Mining and Smithing update?}}

### Talk to

- (Condition: First time:)
  - **Player:** Wow, what is that you are wearing?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Afterwards:)
  - **Player:** Can you remind me what you are wearing?
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Before the game update on 12 August 2024:)
  - **Primal fanatic:** I'm wearing primal armour and equipment. You'll be able to smith it yourself in the upcoming 110 Mining and Smithing update.
  - (continue)
- (Condition: After the game update on 12 August 2024:)
  - **Primal fanatic:** I'm wearing primal armour and equipment. You can smith it yourself in the 110 Mining and Smithing update!
  - (continue)
- **Primal fanatic:** To smith it, first you'll need to mine 10 new ores across the Daemonheim peninsula, which requires 100 Mining and an appropriate pickaxe.
- **Primal fanatic:** Then with 100 Smithing, you'll be able to smelt the ore into primal bars, and smith primal armour and weapons. Want to know more?
