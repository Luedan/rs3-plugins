# Patchy

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Patchy](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APatchy)
- **Letter**: `P`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk-to


#### Without the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Player:** Hello!
- **Patchy:** Arr? Be ye wantin' te go on account with our gang o' fillibusters?
- **Patchy:** The powder monkey be takin' a caulk after gettin' rowdy on bumboo, so there be plenty of room for ye.
- **Player:** Riiiiight...
- **Player:** I'll just be over here if you need me.

#### With the little book o'piracy or having completed Rocking Out

- **Patchy:** Hello there! Can I sew ye somethin' tegether?
- **Player:** What do you mean?
- **Patchy:** Well, ye see it works like this; I can sew yer eye patches onto yer pirate hats or pirate bandanas.
- **Patchy:** Just pirate bandanas mind ye, I hate the feel of snakeskin. (Shudder)
- **Patchy:** It costs 500 gold te do it, and 600 gold te get them separated.
- **Player:** Okay, why does it cost more to get them separated than put together?
- **Patchy:** Wear and tear on scissors.
- **Patchy:** On another note, I also sew crab claw gauntlets on to pirate hooks, highwayman masks to black cavaliers, and mime masks to black berets, if ye have them with ye.
- **Patchy:** So, do ye have anything ye wants doin?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Yes
    - **Player:** Yes please!
    - **Patchy:** Now, do you want to be sewing items together or separating them?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Sew together
        - **Player:** I'd like something sewn together.
        - **Patchy:** Aye, pick out what items ye be wantin' sewn together.
        - (Performance Equipment interface opens.)
      - [Choice]: Separate
        - **Player:** I need something separated.
        - **Patchy:** Aye, aye. Just use yer item on me and I'll see if I can separate them.
  - [Choice]: No
    - **Player:** No.
    - **Patchy:** Fair enough. See you around!

### Sew or using a compatible component

- (Performance Equipment interface opens.)

### Creating an item

- (removes=components and 500 coins) (receives=combined item)
- Your items have been combined!

### Using a combined item or requesting separation through the Perfromance Equipment interface


#### Without two free slots in backpack

- **Patchy:** Ye don't seem te have enough free space few the two items. Ye might want te visit the bank.

#### With two free slots in backpack

- **Patchy:** You want me te separate these two for ye?
- [Menu]: Split this item apart? (Costs 600 coins)
  - [Choice]: Yes
    - (removes=combined item and 600 coins) (receives=component items)
  - [Choice]: No
    - **Player:** No.
    - **Patchy:** Fair enough. See you around!

### Using an incompatible item

- **Patchy:** Sorry, I can't do anythin' with that.
