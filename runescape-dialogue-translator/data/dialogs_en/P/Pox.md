# Pox

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Pox](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APox)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### When spoken to without a Catspeak amulet

- **Player:** Hi cat!
- **Pox:** Miaooow!

### When spoken to with a Catspeak amulet


#### Before or during Rat Catchers

- **Player:** Hi cat!
- **Pox:** Hello.
- **Player:** What are you doing?
- **Pox:** I think I'll go hunting soon, there's a warehouse with some rats in it nearby.
- **Player:** Happy hunting.

#### After Rat Catchers

- **Player:** Hi cat!
- **Pox:** Hello Player.
- **Player:** How are you feeling now?
- **Pox:** Still a bit below purrrr. Thank you for fetching the medicine for me.
- **Player:** No problem, I hope you get better soon.
