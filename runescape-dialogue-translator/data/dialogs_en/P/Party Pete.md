### 15th year anniversary party garden

- **Player:** Hey!
- **Party Pete:** Hi! I'm Party Pete. Welcome to the 15th Anniversary garden!
- So, what's going on here?
  - **Party Pete:** It's a party! RuneScape has hit the big 15 so I'm throwing it a birthday party nobody will forget!
  - **Player:** Even if they get too carried away with the party spirit?
  - **Party Pete:** Even then. This is a party for the ages!
- What are the plinths for?
  - *If the player has not collected any of the statues*
    - **Party Pete:** They're ready to hold statues that celebrate each of the fifteen years of RuneScape. The garden will look awesome when you have them all. They can be found in the balloon drops or by finding special portals around the world. When you fins a portal have a gander and you might have a statue! It'll be delivered directly to the correct plinth, so when you find one make sure to come back here and check it out!
  - *If the player has collected all of the statues*
    - **Party Pete:** They look great with all of your statues on them! Take some time to have a browse of them and really appreciate their splendour.
- Tell me more about the buff.
  - **Party Pete:** The party spirit is contagious. Just have some food from the tables, or have some of the cake, and it will give you the party spirit too! It's a 5% buff for all skilling that you do in the party area for the following 24 hours. Just click it again after the 24 hours are up to reactivate the bonus!
  - **Player:** It certainly seems to have worked on you!
  - **Party Pete:** Yep. Groovy isn't it?
- Are there any rewards?
  - **Party Pete:** There are lots of fantastic rewards to go alongside the celebrations! Pop 15 balloons for a snazzy extra, and 50 balloons for a present that'll go with a bang! Get the party spirit buff, from eating from the party tables, and unlock another present, this time a tasty one!
  - **Player:** That all sounds good! Anything else?
  - **Party Pete:** Collecting statues also gives you special rewards, when you have collected 4, 8, 12, and 15. Keep your eye out for special unlock messages! Also, you can get cool things from the balloons, like party hat fragments, starts, lamps and gold, and the chance to unlock a statue!
- How long does this party last?
  - **Party Pete:** Not everyone has the party stamina that I have, but we're partying hard until the end of January. The statue portals can be found until the end of the year, though, so keep hunting!
- How do the balloon drops work?
  - **Party Pete:** I've gone all out with the baloon drops for this! They are every half hour and they're at every lodestone except Prifddinas. Just look out for the announcements and confetti, and then head over and pop away! You can pop 50 balloons a day and there are great things to find inside them, like party hat fragments, stars and lamps. Also, if you pop 50 you'll get a special reward!
- Bye.
  - **Party Pete:** Catch ya later alligator!
