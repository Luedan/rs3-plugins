## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Professor Onglewip:** Greetings, human!
- [Menu]: Choose an option:
  - [Choice]: Do you live here too?
    - **Professor Onglewip:** Oh no, I come from the Gnome Stronghold. I've been sent here by King Narnode to learn about human magics.
    - **Professor Onglewip:** The stronghold's in the north-west of the continent - a long way away. You should visit us there some time. The food's great, and the company's delightful.
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: I'll try to make time for it. Sounds like a nice place.
        - **Professor Onglewip:** Well, it's full of gnomes. How much nicer could it be?
      - [Choice]: Goodbye.
        - (end)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - (end)

### Treasure Trails


#### Receiving the puzzle box

- **Professor Onglewip:** You’ll be here for the puzzle, then?
- (receives=puzzle box)
- [Professor Onglewip has given you a puzzle box!]

#### When talked to again without solving the puzzle

- **Professor Onglewip:** That is close, but it’s just not right.

#### When talked to with the puzzle solved

- (If the trail continues:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives= the new clue scroll (hard))
  - [Professor Onglewip has given you another clue scroll!]
  - (continue)
- (If the trail ends:)
  - (removes=the old clue scroll (hard) and puzzle box) (receives=reward casket (hard))
  - [You've found a casket!]
  - (continue)
- **Professor Onglewip:** Perfect; good luck!

### Gnome Restaurant delivery

- **Professor Onglewip:** Ah, now that's a familiar smell. Thank you.
