## Dialogue Transcript

- **Professor Alfredo:** Yes? Oh, another adventurer, are you? Well, just remember that I'm the supervisor here.
- **Professor Alfredo:** If you have the skills, feel free to excavate for artefacts, but please try not to damage the area.
- **Professor Alfredo:** Some of us have more important work, such as documenting the finds for the Archaeology Guild.
