## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Plague's End]]


#### Isafdar

- **Player:** Hello, please can I get past?
- **Prifddinas guard:** I'm sorry, but no humans may enter Prifddinas while those criminals are at large.
- **Player:** I'm no criminal.
- **Prifddinas guard:** Rules are rules. No one enters until the brigands are gone.
- **Player:** May I ask why you are guarding a field?
- **Prifddinas guard:** A field? Our great city of Prifddinas may be lost, but we are proud to guard the land it once occupied.
