## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Carnillean Rising

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Philipe Carnillean:** Toys! I want TOYS!
- **Player:** Sorry, I don't have any.
- **Philipe Carnillean:** Sweets! Gimme sweets!
- **Player:** Nope, sorry, I don't have any sweets either.
- **Philipe Carnillean:** I hate you! HATE YOU!

### Claiming Philipe's lamp after Carnillean Rising


#### If you have not yet claimed Philipe's lamp at the chosen location

- **Philipe Carnillean:** I was hoping to run into you again.<br>I've found something you'll appreciate.
- (Condition: Without a free backpack space:)
  - **Player:** I'll have to clear some space first.
- (Condition: With a free backpack space:)
  - [Menu]: What would you like to say?
    - [Choice]: So what have you got for me?
      - (receives=Philipe's lamp)
      - Philipe gives you a lamp.
      - **Player:** Thank you.
    - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
      - *At the Combat Training Camp*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I've come to use the training facilities. The next time I fight a big monster, you're not going to be there to distract it for me!
        - **Player:** Yes, that's true.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** My family's always had permission to train here, but it's been years since any of us bothered.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At the Mountain Camp*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I've been observing the tribe that lives here. Their society is utterly different to anything I ever saw in Ardougne.
        - **Player:** Yes, I've seen them myself.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** My fathers says these barbarian tribes are ignorant savages, but I don't think they're ignorant or savage at all.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At Shilo Village*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I heard this village had zombies that I could fight.
        - **Player:** Rashiliyia's minions are still around, yes.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** The zombies were a bit of a let-down, but it's nice to find a village with a proper hotel. I hadn't slept in a bed for ages.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At Port Phasmatys*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** All my life, I've heard stories about the monsters of the Eastern Lands. I thought I'd come and see them for myself.
        - **Player:** There are some pretty nasty things around here, yes.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I didn't realise how terrible the conditions are for people living here. There's always plenty of work for adventurers like us.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At the Polypore Dungeon*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I've been getting to know this skinweaver lady. She says she created this entire place out of fungi.
        - **Player:** It's a very strange place.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** Yeah, have you met the shopkeeper? He's the strangest thing in here! Get him to tell you about himself; you'll see what I mean.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At Dorgesh-Kaan*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** There's a goblin here who's supposed to be very wise. I wanted to meet him, and maybe learn from him.
        - **Player:** Why?
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** My father always said that goblins were dumb brutes, but, from what I've seen of this city, they're more advanced than we are.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At the Piscatoris Fishing Colony*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** This colony was being destroyed by sea trolls, until a couple of famous adventurers saved the day. I came here to learn the full story.
        - **Player:** Ahem.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** That Wise Old Man must be an amazing chap.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - *At Fight Caves entrance*
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I'm putting my combat skills to the test. It's pretty difficult, but I'll get there eventually.
        - *(Continues below)*
      - (Condition: In the City of Um:)
        - **Player:** Should I be concerned that you've managed to make it into the Underworld?
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** I'm only here for a short while! My friend Frank had a word with Harold, who was more than willing to let me visit here on the ground of education.
        - **Philipe Carnillean:** It's really opened my mind. Not all ghosts, zombies, and skeletons are nasty minions that I should jump to fight.
        - (continue)
      - (Condition: Without having rubbed Philipe's lamp from this location:)
        - (Condition: If you have yet to claim Philipe's lamp from this location:)
          - **Player:** So what have you got for me?
          - (above)
        - (Condition: If you already claimed Philipe's lamp from this location:)
          - (Condition: If you have lost it without rubbing it:)
            - **Player:** I seem to have lost the lamp you gave me.
            - (continue|I seem to have lost)
          - (Condition: If you haven't lost it without rubbing it:)
            - **Player:** Thanks for the lamp.
            - **Philipe Carnillean:** You're welcome.
      - (Condition: Having rubbed Philipe's lamp from this location:)
        - **Player:** I'll see you around.

#### If you have already received Philipe's lamp but have not yet redeemed it

  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?

  - [Choice]: Thanks for the lamp.
    - **Philipe Carnillean:** You're welcome.

    - (Condition: With free backpack space:)
      - (receives=Philipe's lamp)

  - [Choice]: What are you doing here?
    - (above)
