## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- **Professor Manglethorp:** Fascinating, fascinating. It's remarkable what these young dwarves have managed to create. Oh, hello young man.
- **Player:** Hello, what's a gnome like you doing in a place like this?
- **Professor Manglethorp:** Ah, well I have been sent by the King to talk with the dwarves here and find out more about their recent advances in technology.
- **Player:** Found anything interesting?
- **Professor Manglethorp:** The rideable carts are a very handy way to get to mining sites, and the Blast Furnace is a magnificent beast of a machine.
- **Player:** I'll have a look, thanks.

### Gnome Restaurant delivery

- **Professor Manglethorp:** Just what I need. Dwarven cuisine is a bit ... solid for my tastes.
