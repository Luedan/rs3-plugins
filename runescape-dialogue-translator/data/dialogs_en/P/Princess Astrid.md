## Dialogue Transcript


### Player Interactions


#### Greeting After [[Throne of Miscellania]], before [[Royal Trouble]]

- **Princess Astrid:** Good day, Darling

#### Responses To Emotes After Marraige

*Curtsy/Bow/Wave*
- **Princess Astrid:** Hello!
*Dance/Twirl/ Jig/ Headbang/ Goblin Bow/ Goblin Salute*
- **Princess Astrid:** Your dancing never ceases to impress me, darling!
*Jump For Joy/ Raspberry/ Glass box/ Glass wall/ Climb Rope/ Salute/ Lean/*
- **Princess Astrid:** You are silly!
*Angry/ Cry/ Panic*
- **Princess Astrid:** What's wrong darling?
*Cheer*
- **Princess Astrid:** If you want to cheer someone on, darling, I suggest you find my brother.
*Applaud*
- **Princess Astrid:** If you want to applaud someone on, darling, I suggest you find my brother.
*Beckon*
- **Princess Astrid:** I'm staying right here
*Laugh*
- **Princess Astrid:** What's so funny, darling?

### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]

- **Princess Astrid:** Good day, [Sir/Madam].
- **Player:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.

### After [[Throne of Miscellania]], before [[Royal Trouble]]

*If the player is male and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Princess Astrid:** Hello, dear.
- **Player:** Hello. Have you found a priest for our marriage yet?
- **Princess Astrid:** I'm afraid not, dear. I don't think there is one to be had on these islands for love nor money...
- **Princess Astrid:** Do not fear, though; I shall call for you as soon as one is found.
*If the player is female and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Princess Astrid:** Good day, Madam.
- **Player:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.

### After [[Royal Trouble]], before [[Blood Runs Deep]]

*If the player is male and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Princess Astrid:** I'm glad you could help father so much. He's so much happier now that he can go adventuring, and I think it was the last thing that would stop Queen Sigrid considering him for marriage. Speaking of marriage, are you any closer to finding someone to marry us? I can barely wait!
  - Soon, my dear.
    - **Player:** Soon, my dear.
    - **Princess Astrid:** Really? That's great news!
  - No luck so far.
    - **Player:** No luck so far.
    - **Princess Astrid:** That is such a shame.
*If the player is female and engaged to Prince Brand*
- **Princess Astrid:** Thank you for helping father so much, [Fremennik name].
*If the player was previously male and got engaged to Princess Astrid but is now female*
- **Princess Astrid:**I like your new look!
- **Princess Astrid:**I hear you made peace between our kingdom and Etceteria once more. Well done, [Fremennik name].
