## Dialogue Transcript

- **Pallad:** Hey! Looking to make a deposit?
- [Menu]: What would you like to say?
  - [Choice]: I'd like to access my bank account, please.
    - (opens=Bank)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my PIN settings.
    - (opens=Bank PIN)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to see my collection box.
    - (opens=Grand Exchange collection box)
  - [Choice]: I'd like to check my Lost and Found.
    - **Pallad:** You don't have anything in your Lost and Found at the moment.
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Player:** Hello, who are you?
    - **Pallad:** Pallad, I run the bank here in Heathervein.
    - **Pallad:** Just remember, if you find any gems while you're mining out here, I have first dibs.
    - **Player:** Right... I don't think that's how it works.
    - **Pallad:** Ah, who am I kidding? I just like the shiny ones, but I don't know how to mine.
    - **Player:** Isn't this a village of miners?
    - **Pallad:** I chose a different path in life - banking is in my blood!
    - **Pallad:** Though there's not much need {{Sic|for bank}} in a place like this - they mostly all just barter.
    - **Player:** I could sell you some...
    - **Pallad:** Nah, I just gotta learn how to use a pickaxe. I'm good. Thanks, though.
    - **Player:** Okay, good luck with that.
