## Dialogue Transcript


### Inside Sophanem

- **Player:** Hello.
- **Priest:** Greetings, non-believer. May the blessings of Icthlarin rest on your tired shoulders and may they set you at ease during interesting times.

### At the gates to Sophanem


#### Before Contact!

- **Player:** Can I pass through this gate?
- **Priest:** Sorry, Sophanem is under quarantine. No one may leave or enter.
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: Why is the city under quarantine?
    - **Player:** Why is the city under quarantine?
    - **Priest:** The gods have sent a plague of spots upon the city. We dare not risk it spreading further.
    - **Player:**Okay, thank you.
  - [Choice]: Okay, thank you.
    - **Player:**Okay, thank you.

#### After Contact!

- **Priest:** Good day, Player. On behalf of the town, I would like to thank you for your help.
- **Player:** It was nothing. I had some outside help.
- **Priest:** I doubt it. Nobody from outside got past us. There's no need to be modest. Don't let us keep you. I'm sure you've got plenty of other important questing to get on with.

### Using an acne potion on him

- **Priest:** Hey, hey, hey! What in the name of sweet suffering Icthlarin are you doing?
- **Player:** I have this acne potion which I thought might help ease your itching.
- **Priest:** All that you'll achieve by pouring that potion on me is to ruin my lovely robes.
- **Player:** Sorry, I was just trying to help.
- **Priest:** This plague of spots is a divine burden placed upon us, which no amount of alchemy or potion-mixing will ease.
