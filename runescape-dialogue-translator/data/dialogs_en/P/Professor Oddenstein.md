## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Ernest the Chicken

- **Professor Oddenstein:** Be careful in here, there's lots of dangerous equipment.
- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What does this machine do?
    - **Professor Oddenstein:** Nothing at the moment...it's broken. It's meant to be a transmutation machine. It has also spent time as a time travel machine, a dramatic lighting generator, and a thing for generating monsters.
  - Is this your house?
    - **Professor Oddenstein:** No, I'm just one of the tenants. It belongs to the count who lives in the basement.

### After Ernest the Chicken


#### Standard dialogue

- **CHOOSE AN OPTION:**
  - What does the portal machine do?
    - **Professor Oddenstein:** Ah, an interesting question! It creates a rift in the very fabric of space, opening a portal to some hitherto undiscovered plane that exists parallel to our own.
    - **Player:** Huh?
    - **Professor Oddenstein:** It's a teleportation machine.
  - Is this your house?
    - (above|is-this-your-house)

#### Attempting to enter the interdimensional rift without insulated boots equipped

- **Professor Oddenstein:** Err, just before you go through there...
- **Player:** What's the problem?
- **Professor Oddenstein:** That portal opens into a plane populated with some very shocking creatures. You should wear some kind of insulated armour before going there.
- *If the player has insulated boots in their inventory:*
  - **Player:** Oops, I'd better put my insulated boots on first.
- *If the player does not have insulated boots in their inventory:*
  - **SELECT AN OPTION**
    - Where can I get insulated armour from?
      - **Player:** Where can I get insulated armour from?
      - **Professor Oddenstein:** Well there were some pretty tough people here last week. Said they were Slayer Masters. They were planning on making some protective boots. You should speak to one of them.
      - **SELECT AN OPTION**
        - Thanks, I'll do that.
          - **Player:** Thanks, I'll do that.
          - **Professor Oddenstein:** No problem. See you later.
        - I don't want to run around after Slayer Masters, I'm going through.
          - **Player:** I don't want to run around after Slayer Masters, I'm going through.
          - **Professor Oddenstein:** Fair enough, just don't say I didn't warn you{{Sic}}
          - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the Killerwatt Plane.}}
    - Thanks, I think I'll stay here for a while then.
      - **Player:** Thanks, I think I'll stay here for a while then.
    - Thanks for the warning, but I'm not scared of any monster.
      - **Player:** Thanks for the warning, but I'm not scared of any monster.
      - **Professor Oddenstein:** Ok. Just don't say I didn't warn you...
      - {{Qact|The player is teleported to the Killerwatt Plane.}}
