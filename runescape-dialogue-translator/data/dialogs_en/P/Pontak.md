## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[The Fremennik Trials]]


#### Talking to him

- **Pontak:** I cannot speak to you outerlander! Talk to Brundt, the Chieftain!

#### Pickpocketing him

- **Pontak:** {{RSChat|You stay away from me outerlander!}}
- They are too suspicious of you for you to get close enough to steal from them.

### After [[Hero's Welcome]]

*When speaking to Pontak in Rellekka.*
- **Player:** Hello.
- *Pontak may greet you with any of the following at random:*
- *Greeting 1:*
  - **Pontak:** How are you, [Fremennik name]? I hope all fares well with you this day!
  - **Player:** I'm fine, thanks, how are you?
  - **Pontak:** All is well with me also!
- *Greeting 2:*
  - **Pontak:** Hello again, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. What need you of me this day? You can always rely on my assistance!
- *Greeting 3:*
  - **Pontak:** Hello. Did you want something, [Fremennik name]?
- *Greeting 4:*
  - **Pontak:** [Fremennik name], it has been too long since last we met!
- *Greeting 5:*
  - **Pontak:** Welcome back, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name]. It is always a pleasure to see you here!
- *Greeting 6:*
  - **Pontak:** Hello to you, too!
- *Greeting 7:*
  - **Pontak:** Always good to see you, [sister/brother] [Fremennik name].
  - Select an option (all greetings continue with dialogue below)
    - Can I ask you about V?
    - *Pontak may reply to you with any of the following at random:*
    - *Reply 1:*
      - **Pontak:** He died saving you, [Fremennik name]. Make sure you use his final gift wisely and live a life that we can all be proud of.
    - *Reply 2:*
      - **Pontak:** He was a great hero to our people. Why couldn't they have gone for one of the bad gods, like Zamorak or Saradomin?
    - *Reply 3:*
      - **Pontak:** Those cowards. They didn't even have the decency to face him as a warrior.
    - *Reply 4:*
      - **Pontak:** It's sad that he's gone, but he will live on as an inspiration to countless heroes across countless worlds.
    - Goodbye.
      - (end)
