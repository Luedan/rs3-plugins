## Dialogue Transcript


### Pet

  - **Pork Pie:** {{RSChat|*coo*}}

### Shoo away

- **Player:** {{RSChat|Thbbbbt!}}
- {{Qact|The player performs the Raspberry emote.}}
- **Pork Pie:** {{RSChat|Hiss!}}
- {{Qact|Pork Pie retreats from the player.}}
