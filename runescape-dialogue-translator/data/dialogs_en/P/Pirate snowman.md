# Pirate snowman

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Pirate snowman](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APirate%20snowman)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript

- **Pirate snowman:** Welcome to the Land of Snow, landlubber! Yo ho ho and a glass of sherry!
  - Merry Christmas!
    - **Player:** Merry Christmas!
    - **Pirate snowman:** Arr, ha harr! Shiver me carrot! Fifteen coals on a snowman's chest! Yo ho ho and a glass of sherry!
  - What is the Land of Snow?
    - **Player:** What is the Land of Snow?
    - **Pirate snowman:** Arr! If all the snow in this land were to melt, it would fill the Seven Seas! But it won't melt, for this be the Land of Snow! Yo ho ho and a glass of sherry!
  - Landlubber?
    - **Player:** Landlubber? When was the last time you went to sea?
    - **Pirate snowman:** I be snow, and snow be water...and the sea be water too! So, if ever there be a man o' the sea, it be me! Yo ho ho and a glass of sherry!
  - I want to go home.
    - **Player:** I want to go home!
    - **Pirate snowman:** Arr. Merry Christmas, landlubber. Yo ho ho and a glass of sherry!
    - {{Qact|You get teleported out of Land of Snow.}}
