## Dialogue Transcript


### Petting the Plague cow

*The player will say one of the following when petting the cow:*
  - **Player:** Awww, look at the poor sick cow. We'd never make a burger out of you.
  - **Player:** Don't worry cow, I'm sure somebody will find a cure for this plague soon.
