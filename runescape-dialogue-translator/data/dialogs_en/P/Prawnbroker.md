# Prawnbroker

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Prawnbroker](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APrawnbroker)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Interact

- [Menu]: Can I help you?
  - [Choice]: What can I do here?
    - **Prawnbroker: **Every time you catch a golden fish egg, you get a prawn point. You can trade prawn points for prawn perks right here! You can also get prawn points from prawn balls. You'll get a prawn perk once you collect 1, 20, 100 and 200 prawn balls in total. After handing in a prawn point, I'll give you a choice of prawn perks. These range from baitless fishing to floating shark pets. Some perks are locked behind others. You'll have to purchase all of the perks in one tier before you can access the perks in the next tier.
    - *In case of ironman:* If you have any prawn crackers you can't use, pull them with me and I'll reward you with the appropriate price.
  - [Choice]: I would like to select my perks.
    - (continue|Prawn perks)
  - [Choice]: I would like to change some settings.
    - What do you want to change?
      - Settings
      - Fishing Animation
        - I want to turn off my prestige barehanded fishing.
          - **Prawnbroker:** Fish caught using your bare hands will look like their normal selves.
        - I want to catch silver fish while barehanded fishing.
          - **Prawnbroker:** Fish caught using your bare hands will have a silver sheen.
        - I want to catch gold fish while barehanded fishing.
          - **Prawnbroker:** You now have a Midas touch while catching fish barehanded.
        - Cancel.
          - (previous)
      - Back
        - (previous)
  - I would like to claim my bonus Prawn Balls please!
    - *If the player is not yet eligible to claim bonus prawn balls:*
      - **Prawnbroker:** Come back to me when you have mastered fishing. Then I will honour your achievement with 50 prawn balls.
    - *If the player is level 99 Fishing:*
      - **Prawnbroker:** You're something of a celebrity in prawn circles. To recognise your mastery of the sea, I offer you 50 prawn balls.
      - {{qact|You receive 50 prawn balls}}
  - [Choice]: Cancel.
    - (end)

### Prawn perks


#### Using own prawnbroker


##### With prawn points

- You have <number> prawn points to spend.
- *If the player is eligible for at least a tier-1 prawn perk:*
  - Select a tier-1 prawn perk
    - Gain prawn balls more frequently.
      - You can now gain prawn balls at a greater rate.
    - Rod-fish without using fishing bait.
      - You no longer need bait while rod fishing.
    - Gain a landmark of your choice for your aquarium.
      - *If the player does not yet own all landmarks:*
        - *(Select an option interface opens)*
          - You are now able to place the [Landmark name] decoration in your aquarium!
      - *If the player does not yet own all landmarks:*
        - (receives=10 prawn balls)
        - **Prawnbroker:** You already have all the landmarks. Have some prawn balls instead.
    - Gain 10 prawn pennies.
      - (receives=10 prawn pennies)
      - You are handed 10 prawn pennies.
    - Cancel.
      - (end)
- *Upon unlocking the ability to access the next tier of Prawn Perks:*
  - (receives=golden mystery egg)
  - You are handed a golden mystery egg. You now have access to the next tier of perks.
- *If the player is eligible for a tier-3 prawn perk:*
  - Select a tier-3 prawn perk
    - Gain prawn balls more frequently.
      - You now gain prawn balls at an even greater rate!
    - Fly-fish without using feathers.
      - You can now fly fish without feathers. You can use the prawnbroker to swap between using stripy and normal feathers.
    - Prawn pennies and prawn pounds last twice as long.
      - Prawn pennies and pounds now last twice as long.
    - Gain three diving suit cosmetic overrides.
      - You can now use diving suits as a cosmetic override. They can be accessed from the Customisation interface. Or so I hear.
    - Cancel.
      - (end)
- *If the player is eligible for a tier-5 prawn perk:*
  - Select a tier-5 prawn perk
    - Gain prawn balls more frequently.
      - You will now gain prawn balls more frequently!
    - Barbarian-fish without using bait or feathers.
      - You no longer need feathers or bait while barbarian fishing!
    - Gain a great white shark pet and a great white shark for your aquarium.
      - You have unlocked the great white shark for your aquarium and as a pet! Access your pet from the Customisations interface.
    - Catch great white shark from shark fishing spots.
      - You have unlocked the ability to catch great white sharks from shark fishing spots!
    - Cancel.
      - (end)
- *First time after purchasing all perks:*
  - Congratulations! You have collected all the perks! You can now use raw great white shark to make sushi when combined with kelp or seaweed!
- *Subsequent times after purchasing all perks:*
  - You have already unlocked all available perks!

##### Without prawn points

- You do not have any prawn points to spend.
