# Prince Brand

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Prince Brand](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3APrince%20Brand)
- **Letter**: `P`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before [[Throne of Miscellania]]

- **Prince Brand:** Good day, [Sir/Madam].
- **Player:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.

### After [[Throne of Miscellania]], before [[Royal Trouble]]

*If the player is male and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Prince Brand:** Good day, [Fremennik name].
- **Player:** Good day, Prince Brand.
*If the player is female and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Prince Brand:** Good day, Madam.
- **Player:** Good day, Your Royal Highness.

### After [[Royal Trouble]], before [[Blood Runs Deep]]

*If the player is male and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Prince Brand:** Thank you for helping father so much, [Fremennik name].
*If the player is female and engaged to Princess Astrid*
- **Prince Brand:** I hear you made peace between our kingdom and Etceteria once more. Well done, madam.
*If the player is female and engaged to Prince Brand*
- **Prince Brand:** I'm glad you could help father so much. He's so much happier now that he can go adventuring, and I think it was the last thing that would stop Queen Sigrid considering him for marriage. Speaking of marriage, are you any closer to finding someone to marry us? I can barely wait!
  - [Menu]: Select an option
    - [Choice]: Soon, my dear.
      - **Player:** Soon, my dear.
      - **Prince Brand:** Really? That's great news!
    - [Choice]: No luck so far.
      - **Player:** No luck so far.
      - **Prince Brand:** That is such a shame.
*If the player was previously female and got engaged to Prince Brand but is now male*
- **Prince Brand:** I like your new look!
