# Noel

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Noel](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ANoel)
- **Letter**: `N`

## Dialogue Transcript


### Talk to


#### During the exchanging period

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: What's going on here?
    - **Player:** What's going on here?
    - **Noel:** The Petes 'ave officially handed the 'official wrapper-er of festive stuff' role to me. I'm in charge now!
    - **Noel:** 'Bout time they handed it over to me. They've made a right mess of it recently.
    - **Noel:** Speakin' of which, I just found out they've managed to lose all the Christmas wrapping paper AGAIN.
    - **Noel:** And now it's up to ol' Noel here to sort this out.
    - **Noel:** 'Ere's the rub - ain't no chance I'd find it all alone. I could do wi'v a bit of help.
    - **Noel:** I'll give ya a prezzie for every 200 Christmas wrapping paper ya find and bring back to me.
    - [You will obtain Christmas wrapping paper at regular intervals whilst playing.]
    - **Player:** Sounds good! I'll help you out.
    - **Noel:** Thank ya.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What's in the presents?
    - (below|Present info)
  - [Choice]: Exchange Christmas wrapping paper.
    - (below|Exchange paper)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Player:** Bye for now!
    - **Noel:** See ya.

#### After the exhanging period but before the end of the event

- The event is over now, but you can still trade in any paper you have left over until the end of 12th January.
- **Hand in Christmas Wrapping Paper?**
  - [Choice]: Yes.
    - (below|Exchange paper)
  - [Choice]: No.
    - **Noel:** See ya.

#### After the end of the event

- The event is not currently active, so no Christmas wrapping paper can be exchanged.

### Exchange paper


#### During the event


##### Without at least 200 Christmas wrapping paper in the backpack or currency pouch

- **Noel:** I can't give ya any presents until you av at least 200 Christmas wrapping paper for me.

##### With at least 200 Christmas wrapping paper in the backpack or currency pouch


##### Without enough free spaces in backpack

- **Noel:** Ya need at least [number] backpack spaces for yer presents.

##### With enough free spaces in backpack

- (removes=multiples of 200 Christmas wrapping paper) (receives=one present for each set of 200 paper)
- You exchange [number] Christmas wrapping paper and receive:
- (Condition: If white Christmas Presents were received:)
  - [number] x {{Colour}}
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If blue Christmas Presents were received:)
  - [number] x {{Colour}}
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If purple Christmas Presents were received:)
  - [number] x {{Colour}}
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If gold Christmas Presents were received:)
  - [number] x {{Colour}}
  - (continue)
- (Condition: If Santa's Christmas Presents were received:)
  - [number] x {{Colour}}
  - (continue)
- (pop-up)
  - {{Pop-up|You exchange [number] Christmas wrapping paper and receive:<br>[Each of the above lines that appeared]}}

#### After the event

- The event is not currently active, so no Christmas wrapping paper can be exchanged.
