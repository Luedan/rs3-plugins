# 'No fingers'

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:'No fingers'](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3A%27No%20fingers%27)
- **Letter**: `N`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before Tower of Life

- **Player:** You seem to like playing with danger, there.
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** They don't call me 'No-fingers' for nothing!
- **Player:** Well, correct me if I'm wrong, but you seem to have all your fingers.
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** It may seem that way, but I think you'll find I don't have any.
- **Player:** What?
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** These not be fingers. These 'ere be wooden fingers.
- **Player:** Wooden fingers?
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** Yep.
- **Player:** That's not possible, how could you move them?
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** Magic wood.
- **Player:** Yes, well I think I'd better be going now.

### After Tower of Life

- **Player:** Hi.
- **<nowiki/>'No fingers':** Don't suppose you've seen any boots around here? Size nine, steel toed, brown leather...
- **Player:** Nope, not at all. I'd best be going. Have a nice day. Bye!
