## Dialogue Transcript


### When a nearby player doesn't have a mining & smithing flyer

**Necronium fanatic:** color=yellow

### Talking to the necronium fanatic

- **Player:** Wow, what is that you are wearing?
- **Necronium fanatic:** I'm wearing fully upgraded necronium armour. You will be able to smith it yourself in the upcoming Mining and Smithing rework.
- **Necronium fanatic:** To smith it, first you'll need to mine some necrite ore and phasmatite, which requires 70 Mining and an appropriate pickaxe.
- **Necronium fanatic:** Then with 70 Smithing, you'll be able to smelt the ores into necronium bars, and smith necronium armour and weapons.
- **Player:** Oh wow! Do you have more information about the rework?
- *(If you have at least one inventory space:)*
  - {{Qact|You receive a mining & smithing flyer.}}
  - You're handed a flyer{{Sic}}
- *(If your inventory is full:)*
  - **Necronium fanatic:** If you've got a free space, I can give you a flyer{{Sic}}
