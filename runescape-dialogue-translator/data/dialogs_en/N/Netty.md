## Dialogue Transcript


### In Morytania


#### Before Animal Magnetism and Ghosts Ahoy

- **Player:** Hello, old woman.
- {{trandom}}
  - **Netty:** I lived here when this was all just fields, you know.
  - **Netty:** When 100 years old you reach, look like a prune you will.
  - **Netty:** I'm over 100 years old, you know.

#### After Ghosts Ahoy{{anchor|necrovarus}}

- **Player:** I'm here about Necrovarus.
- **Player:** Good news - the enchantment worked!
- **Netty:** So Necrovarus has let the ghosts go free... You have achieved many wonderful things, my young friend.

#### After Animal Magnetism{{anchor|alice}}

- **Player:** I'm here about the farmers by Port Phasmatys.
- **Player:** Hello there, just dropping in to thank you again. It all went perfectly. Alice and her husband can communicate again.
- **Netty:** I can't help but shed a tear about my own family. My sisters are so vile, I refuse to talk to them these days.
- **Player:** Sorry to hear that.
- **Netty:** Never mind; we've reunited a happy family of farmers; that's enough for me.

#### After both Ghosts Ahoy and Animal Magnetism

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: I'm here about Necrovarus.
    - (above|necrovarus)
  - [Choice]: I'm here about the farmers east of here.
    - (above|alice)

#### Using the broomstick from Swept Away on her

- **Player:** Could you enchant this broom for me?
- **Netty:** Oh, this must be Maggie's broom. I suppose I could enchant it, just this once, for old times' sake. Just one moment...
- [You receive 7,139 Magic experience.]
- **Player:** Excellent! Many thanks.
