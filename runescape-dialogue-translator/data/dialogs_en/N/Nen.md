## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue

- ((One of the following is seen randomly:))
  - If only they had trained Invention, you think to yourself. They could build a machine that would save them all this manual labour. Oh well.
  - Sweat drips down the worker's face. It is quite hot, and you wonder how they haven't yet fainted.
  - The worker glares at you, as if to wonder why you are just staring at them instead of helping them out.
  - You shock the poor worker, and cause them to nearly fall over.
