# Ninja Master

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Ninja Master](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ANinja%20Master)
- **Letter**: `N`

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before finishing the task

- **Ninja Master:** Bah! You snuck up on me there!
- **Ninja Master:** Ahem... I mean... I've been expecting you, student.
- **Player:** Well... I kind of just stumbled over here by accident.
- **Ninja Master:** Congratulations on finding me!
*If the player lacks a training card, skip to after the stamp line.*
- **Ninja Master:** I see that you have a training card there for me to sign! Let's get the menial stuff out of the way.
- The Ninja Master has stamped a task for you.
- **Ninja Master:** I have some essential wisdom for you if you would like to hear?
  - I'm ready to learn!
    - (If in Ardougne:) **Ninja master:** There is often someone around, always double check your surroundings before making a move on your target.
    - (If in Fremennik Slayer Dungeon:) **Ninja master:** These creatures come in many shapes and sizes but are all dangerous, there are many weapons at a Ninja's disposal, which will you choose?
    - (If in Gnome Stronghold:) **Ninja Master:** The Gnomes have made clever use of their surroundings to hone their agility. Always use what is around you to gain an edge.
    - (If in Ice Mountain:) **Ninja Master:** A Ninja must learn to endure all conditions that may face them, this location is perfect to teach you more about survival.
    - (If in Kharidian Desert Bandit Camp:) **Ninja Master:** These Bandits are adept with lockpicks and have many tricks, I will teach you a few of them now.
    - (If in Lumbridge Swamp:) **Ninja Master:** Objects or people are not always what they seem, always look carefully as they may offer another route to your goal.
    - (If in Mudskipper Point:) **Ninja Master:** A Ninja must be skilled in the creation and maintenance of weapons, Thurgo's anvil will help teach you more about weapons.
    - (If in Slayer Tower:) **Ninja master:** Study your enemy, learn their strengths and weaknesses so that you may defeat them in combat.
    - (If in Taverley Dungeon:) **Ninja Master:** There are many types of posion in this world and all have their uses, these creatures will aid me in teaching you more about them.
    - (If in Varrock Museum:) **Ninja Master:** Learn from the past so that you may avoid the mistakes made by others.
    - **Ninja Master:** These extra stamps should help. Use them to complete your tasks.
  - I know everything already.
    - **Ninja Master:** Oh well... Ok then. Good luck at... life.

### With task completed

- **Ninja Master:** Bah! You snuck up on me there!

- **Ninja Master:** Congratulations on finding me!

  - I'm ready to learn!
    - **Ninja Master:** Excellent! Are you ready to have your mind blown?
    - **Ninja Master:** Don't stick anything bigger than chopsticks in your ears!
    - **Ninja Master:** Wait... that doesn't sound right...
  - I know everything already.
    - **Ninja Master:** Oh well... Ok then. Good luck at... life.
