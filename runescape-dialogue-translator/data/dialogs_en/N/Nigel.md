## Dialogue Transcript

- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if not asked last:|What's going on here?
    - **Nigel:** I'm enjoying the glorious summer sunshine and barbecuing the tasty tropical trout from the rock pools.
    - [Using the barbecues gives you Cooking experience.]
    - (previous)
  - [Choice]: cond=Only if not asked last:|How are things going?
    - **Nigel:** What do you think would go better with that beauty of a creature that keeps coming out of the crater; garlic sauce or a creamy one?
    - [Menu]: Select an Option
      - [Choice]: Garlic sauce!
        - **Nigel:** I can see what you mean, it would be piquant and I imagine it would complement the flavours perfectly.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: Creamy sauce!
        - **Nigel:** Mmm, interesting choice. Just the thing to go with seafood. Or crater food come to that.
        - (initial)
      - [Choice]: You shouldn't be trying to eat it!
        - **Nigel:** Now, now, it's only in my imagination. For now.
        - (initial)
  - [Choice]: cond=Only after asking something else:|Bye.
    - **Nigel:** Enjoy your time on the beach.
