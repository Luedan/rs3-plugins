## Dialogue Transcript


### Falador and Port Sarim


#### Talk to

- **Nic the trader:** Greetings traveller...
  - Who are you?
    - **Player:** Who are you?
    - **Nic the trader:** I am Nic, the superior brother.
    - **Player:** Brother?
    - **Nic the trader:** Yes, you may have met my older brother Vic who turns up once in a while to trade his stock of rare items. Similar to my brother, I can take your bonus experience and offer you compensation in the form of various rare objects. You could say that I've acquired some of his rare items and I'm willing to trade them myself at a much better price. I've also gotten my hands on some items from Alice's dungeon that I'll happily trade... If you see anything you like, I recommend snapping it up as I can't guarantee I'll have it next time I'm trading.
    - *(Shows initial options)*
  - I would like to trade in some bonus experience.
    - *(If on a member's world:)*
      - *(Opens the credit exchange interface)*
    - *(If on a free-to-play world:)*
      - **Nic the trader:** Sorry, I can't trade here.
      - You'll need to be on a member's world to interact with the trader.
  - I would like to buy something.
    - *(Opens Nic's Store's interface)*
  - Goodbye.
    - *(Ends dialogue)*
