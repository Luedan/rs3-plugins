## Dialogue Transcript


### Standard dialogue before Plague's End

- **Player:** Hello there.
- **Nurse Sarah:** Hello my dear, how are you feeling?
- **Player:** I'm ok thanks.
- **Nurse Sarah:** Well in that case I'd better get back to work. Take care.
- **Player:** You too.

### After Plague's End

- **Nurse Sarah:** The plague itself was a fake, but the image of West Ardougne as a plague city wasn't too far from the truth. Malnutrition, poor sanitation... it's no wonder these people aren't healthy. Now that we can get in and out again, people are getting healthier, but it'll be a long time before West Ardougne is really a nice place to live.
