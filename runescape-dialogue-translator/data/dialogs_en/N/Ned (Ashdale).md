## Dialogue Transcript


### Before A Shadow over Ashdale

- **Ned:** What do you want?
- [Menu]: Select an Option
  - [Choice]: That's not very friendly.
    - **Ned:** Hmph.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: What are you doing?
    - **Ned:** I'm catching fairies with an invisible net! What's it look like I'm doing? I'm sitting here by the fire and trying to enjoy some peace and quiet.
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Who are you?
    - **Ned:** Don't play fool with me, young [miss/man]. You know full well who I am!
    - **Ned:** There's only one Ned Swarbrick on this island, and that's me. My family has been in Ashdale since the very beginning, and don't you forget it!
    - (initial)
  - [Choice]: Goodbye.
    - **Ned:** Hmph.
