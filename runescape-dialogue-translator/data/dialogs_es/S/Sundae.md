# Sundae

- **Original Wiki Page**: [Transcript:Sundae](https://runescape.wiki/w/Transcript%3ASundae)
- **Letter**: `S`
- **Language**: Español (Entidades, ítems y ubicaciones preservados en inglés)

---

## Dialogue Transcript


### Before the ceremony

- ((The following is continuously displayed until 20:00:))
  - **Sundae:** ¡Se anuncia la clasificación de {{RSChat|[number] [minute/minutes] until this {{Sic|weeks}}!}}
  - (continue)
- (Action: Los Dead Beats desaparecen del escenario 10 minutos antes de la ceremonia.)

### During the ceremony

- ((In the following the dialogue is also shown as regular overhead messages. Sundae's lines appear every 3.6 seconds, except when interrupted by a clan representatives appearing, performing emotes, or disappearing, in which case there is 5.4 seconds between the lines unless noted otherwise. The first line appears 5.4 seconds after the clan representatives.))
- (Action: Cinco representantes del clan aparecen poco después de las 20:00 horas.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|And now for the results of this week's contribution competition!}}}}
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Coming in last place we have...}}}}
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|[Captain]! Better luck next time.}}}}
- (Action: El clan representative que representa al equipo en quinto lugar realiza el gesto Cry.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Next, in fourth place...}}}}
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|[Captain]!}}}}
- (Action: El clan representative que representa al equipo en cuarto lugar realiza el gesto No.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|In third place...}}}}
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|[Captain]! Good work.}}}}
- (Action: El clan representative que representa al equipo en tercer lugar realiza el gesto Clap.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Coming in second place...}}}}
- (Action: Sundae hace una pausa de 5,4 segundos.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|[Captain]! So close!}}}}
- (Action: El clan representative que representa al equipo en segundo lugar realiza el gesto Bow. Sundae continúa después de 3,6 segundos a pesar de que clan representative está realizando un gesto.)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Which leaves us with our winner for this week...}}}}
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|[Captain]! Congratulations!}}}}
- (Action: El clan representative que representa al equipo en primer lugar realiza el gesto Cheer.)
- (Condition: If Death won:)
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Enjoy 20% increased Spooky tokens from Coffin and mausoleum thieving this week!}}}}
  - (continue)
- {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Prizes will be available momentarily from Cough.}}}}
- (Action: Los representantes del clan desaparecen.)
- (Condition: With more ceremonies remaining:)
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|Make sure you come back next week for another ceremony!}}}}
  - (continue)
- (Condition: Without more ceremonies remaining:)
  - {{Colour: {{Colour|#99FF99|That's all for our ceremonies this year, great work all of you!}}}}
  - (continue)
- (Action: Después de 3,6 segundos Sundae desaparece y los Dead Beats aparecen en el escenario.)
