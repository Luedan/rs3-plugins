### Overhead dialogue with Estrith

- *Estrith pasa regularmente por Wiglaf mientras recorre Daemonheim. Ella dirá la siguiente línea:*
- **Estrith:** {{RSChat|Soon we'll be home, eating red meat and combing innards from our hair.}}
- *Wiglaf ocasionalmente responde con una de las siguientes líneas. Es posible que no responda en absoluto:*
- **Wiglaf:** {{RSChat|Don't forget dessert: beer, mead and some more beer.}}
- **Wiglaf:** {{RSChat|Aye, you are right there. The meat here is more brown - grey if we're lucky.}}
- **Wiglaf:** {{RSChat|No red meat today, Estrith. Seagull nuggets.}}
- **Wiglaf:** {{RSChat|Why use a comb when you can use a fork? Eat what you find.}}
- **Wiglaf:** {{RSChat|Innards in your hair? I found a finger in the gruel yesterday.}}
