# Adecuación Legal (Reino Unido)

These regulations only apply if you want your plugin to be able to transfer information to a location that is outside the United Kingdom (UK) or European Economic Area (EEA), and that has not been subject to an adequacy decision from the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk) and/or the [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/adequacy-decisions_en).

As of September 2026, the countries outside of the UK or EEA that have full UK adequacy are:

* Andorra
* Argentina
* the Faroe Islands
* Gibraltar
* Guernsey
* the Isle of Man
* Israel
* Jersey
* New Zealand
* Republic of Korea (South Korea)
* Switzerland
* Uruguay

And partial adequacy that is relevant to plugins has been granted to:

* Canada
* Japan

This list is not guaranteed to be up-to-date, the full list may be viewed [here](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/adequacy-regulations/is-the-restricted-transfer-covered-by-adequacy-regulations/). Of particular note is that the USA is **not** included in this list, as its partial adequacy agreement is not relevant to plugins.

## Excepción de Adecuación

If your plugin does intend to send information to a country not listed above, then you must complete this form after submitting your plugin to Mod.io.

The password `RS3API` is required to access this form.
